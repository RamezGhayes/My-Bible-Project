## Outline in our own words
- Chapter scope: secure perseverance by setting forth the Son, absolutely and compared with angels.
- 1:1-2: law and gospel compared in revelation (manner, time, recipients, revealer). polymeros = many parts/degrees not times; polytropos many manners; palai ended with Malachi; "last days" = Hebrew idiom for last days of the Judaic church-state (Gen 49:1; Num 24:14; Isa 2:2; Mic 4:1; Hos 3:5; Kimchi); en huio keep "in". Speaker = Father (authority, love, care). Directions to preachers (5) and hearers (3). Observations: faith rests on God speaking in the penmen (2 Pet 1:20-21); gradual revelation an act of wisdom (5 reasons); revelation in Christ perfect and final (Jude 3). Christ above Moses (Maimonides' privileges examined). Heir: hon = Son as God-man (mediatorial); kleronomos (kleros lot): lordship, possession by grant, investiture (Gal 4:1; Ps 89:27); panton neuter; ethelke = investiture at resurrection, ascension, session (Matt 28:18); Baronius on Davidic crown rejected; Eph 1:10 one head. dia hou not "for whom" (against Enjedinus, Grotius): four reasons, John 1:3; worlds not new creation (against Socinians, Schlichting): six arguments; epoiesen = bara; aion/olam; plural worlds; Targum/Bereshith Rabbah on Word. Observations: Creator-Son grounds deity, equity of heirship, faith, use of creatures.
- 1:3: structure: what he is, what he does, what he enjoys. apaugasma (Wisd 7:26; not mere Hebraism); charakter = engraved stamp; hypostasis; views: divine nature (Calvin, Brentius, Estius) vs incarnate (Beza, Cameron, Socinians) - Owen: the person, agreeing with first; sun/beam analogy with caution; Shekinah/ark typology; plate on mitre. Observations: all divine perfections in the Son; God's manifestation through the Son in creation, providence, revelation, grace; come to Father through Son (John 14:9). pheron = upholding and governing (rokeb, Ezekiel's chariot); rhema = effectual power (Ps 147:15). katharismos = expiation not sanctification (against Vulgate present "faciens", drops "by himself"); Ex 29:36 LXX. ekathisen: right hand of majesty = mediatorial glory, security, majesty; Christ sits, Aaron stood; observations I-III (Satan's opposition vain, Christ's service safe, believers secure).
- 1:4: kreitton; genomenos = made/became not "declared" (Rom 1:4 used by others); timing = resurrection, ascension, session (not incarnation, baptism); name = Son. Observation: all preeminence from God's will (Phil 2:9; Acts 2:36). Angel speculation among Hebrews (Col 2:18); Metatron tradition.
- 1:5: three conditions for testimony; angels called sons generally, none told "Thou art my Son"; Ps 2 Messianic (Targum, Rashi; David a type); "this day" declarative at resurrection (Acts 13:33) rather than eternal now (Augustine), left open. 2 Sam 7:14: eis = to be; Cajetan's rejection of epistle a folly; three views: Christ only, Solomon only, Owen: both (Solomon literally/typically, Christ principally); rules of types (5); words express Father's love, not proof of natural sonship. Observations I-IV.
- 1:6: palin with legei; prototokos = privilege and heirship (against Schlichting); oikoumene; proskyneo = divine worship. Six views on bringing in (incarnation, resurrection, gospel preaching, Mede millennium, judgment, Socinian ascension): Owen = whole work of bringing forth Messiah (Mal 3:1-2). Source Ps 97:7 not Deut 32:43. Observations I-VIII (incl. Mediator is God blessed forever; saints may not worship angels).
- 1:7: pros = concerning; leitourgos = public minister (Rom 13:4); Ps 104:4; three readings (winds subject - modern Jews; mixed; spirits subject - Augustine); Owen: angels subject (scope of psalm, LXX, Targum, articles, apostle's aim); providence not nature; simile of swiftness/power or agents of winds and fire. Observations I-II.
- 1:8-9: Ps 45; kisse; le olam va ed; rhabdos euthytetos, sceptre of mercy (Esth 5:2). Solomon as subject rejected; Targum applies to Messiah; Socinian Elohim-as-office rejected; Erasmus/Grotius/Aben Ezra "God is thy throne" rejected on six grounds. Son true God, addressed as king of the church; "God, thy God" covenant relation to Mediator. Points: throne eternal; sceptre = Word and Spirit; loved righteousness = settled disposition; anointing = joyful exaltation; fellows = believers, esp. prophets and apostles.
- 1:10-12: Ps 102:25-27; kai introduces new testimony; helixeis variant; Jewish denial answered; Grotius "for thy sake" rejected; Socinian split (Crellius, Schlichting) rejected on five answers; garment folded; "the same" = I AM. Observations 1-7 (deity invites sinners; attributes comfort through Redeemer; old creation passing; Christ above creatures; heaven and earth at his disposal; immutability secures believers; stable consolation only in his eternity).
- 1:13: vehement negation; Ps 110 Messianic (Matt 22:42-44); Targum, Kimchi, Jarchi (Abraham), Trypho (Hezekiah) rejected; Adoni; speaking = decree, covenant, prophecy, investiture; enemies internal and external; footstool (Josh 10:24); conquest meritorious, exemplary, efficient; "until" and 1 Cor 15:24 reconciled in five propositions. Observations I-V.
- 1:14: leitourgika pneumata; sharath vs abad; schoolmen's assistentes/ministrantes rejected (Dan 7:10); nature, office, execution, restriction (heirs of salvation, Rom 8:17); also world government for church (Dan 10). Observations I-II, reasons 1-6.
