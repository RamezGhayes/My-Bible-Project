# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 2 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m02.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes. The page prints no chapter:verse references outside the passage headings.

## Matthew 2:1-12
Intro: identity, names and land of the wise men unknown; lessons matter more.
1. v.1-2 God has hidden servants; grace not tied to places; Melchizedek, Jethro, Job; lily among thorns.
2. v.1-3 privilege does not produce honour; "He came unto his own" (John, no ref); proverb "nearer the church, further from God".
3. v.4-6 head knowledge without heart grace; scribes answered but did not go.
4. diligence of the wise men's journey; "where there is a will there is a way".
5. v.11 faith in a helpless infant; compared to the penitent thief; we have more evidence.
Not treated: v.3 (Herod troubled, only implicitly), v.7-8, v.9-10 (star, joy), v.12, the gifts.

## Matthew 2:13-23
1. v.13 rulers seldom friendly to God's cause; exceptions Josiah, Edward VI; "not many mighty" and "put not your trust in princes" (no refs).
2. v.13-14 Christ a man of sorrows from infancy; flight by night.
3. v.19-20 Herod died; death the leveller; persecutors died, truth survived: Pharaohs, Neros, Diocletians, Charles IX of France, "Bloody Mary" of England (bracket note added identifying Mary Tudor). "The Lord lives".
4. v.23 Nazareth: obscure town; passed by Hebron, Shiloh, Gibeon, Bethel; thirty years there; poverty no sin, pride and covetousness are; humility rarest grace.
Not treated: v.15 and v.17-18 (prophecies), v.16 (massacre only in passing), v.22 (Archelaus), the "Nazarene" prophecy.
