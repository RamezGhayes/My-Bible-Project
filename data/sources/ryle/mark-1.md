## Mark 1:1-8 (printed block ends at 1:7)
1. Dignity of Christ: "Son of God" to Jewish ears = claim of deity, equality with God (John 5:18). Deity is the foundation; only a divine sufferer's death suffices for the world's sins (Rom. 9:5). "Mighty God" (Isa. 9:6).
2. Gospel fulfils Scripture: promise from Gen. 3:15; birth, life, death, resurrection, forerunner foretold. Search the OT for Christ (John 5:39).
3. Effects of John's ministry: stirred Judea and Jerusalem; "burning and shining light" (John 5:35). Popularity no proof of conversion; few of the crowds likely converted; crowded church is not a converted church.
4. John's clear doctrine: exalted Christ, spoke of Spirit-baptism. Ministers' core: Christ fully, then the Spirit's work / new birth. Self-examination: having the Spirit of Christ is the mark (Rom. 8:9).
Not treated: v.6 (dress, food).

## Mark 1:9-20
1. Voice from heaven (v.11): Father's eternal love to Son (John 3:35), approval of His mission, acceptance as Mediator and Surety of the covenant. Believers seen "in Christ", accepted in the Beloved (Cant. 4:7).
2. Christ's preaching (v.15): repent and believe, the message of faithful witnesses since Noah; Paul's summary (Acts 20:21). Human nature needs both, daily deepened. Church membership and priestly absolution save none.
3. Occupations: fishermen, not the great; "not by might nor by power" (Zech. 4:6); weak and foolish chosen (1 Cor. 1:26, 27); rapid growth shows God's hand. Poverty/labour no disgrace: Moses, Gideon, Elisha, apostles called from work; sin is the disgrace.
4. Office: "fishers of men" = drawing men from darkness to light, into the church; oldest NT name for ministry, older than bishop, elder, deacon; skill, diligence, patience of a fisherman; a minister not aiming at this has mistaken his calling.
Not treated: baptism itself / dove, temptation (12-13).

## Mark 1:21-34
1. Demons knew Christ (v.24, 34) when scribes did not; head-knowledge saves none; devils believe and tremble (James 2:19). Faith of the heart, love, obedience, personal appropriation; Luther: Christianity lies in possessive pronouns ("my Saviour").
2. Simon's household "told Him" of her (v.30): first remedy in trouble is to tell Jesus. Jacob (Gen. 32:11), Hezekiah (2 Kings 19:19), Lazarus' sisters (John 11:2). Means (doctors, lawyers) lawful, prayer first. Ps. 55:22; 1 Pet. 5:7; Phil. 4:6. Prepare before trouble.
3. Complete cure (v.31): fever gone and strength restored at once, she served. Picture of Christ with sin-sick souls: forgiveness plus renewing grace, justifies and sanctifies, new heart. Comfort for those afraid to begin lest they fall away: Christ keeps, no half-finished work, loves His own to the end, no sheep lost; grace leads to glory.
Not treated: v.22 authority, v.32-33 crowds, why demons silenced.

## Mark 1:35-39
1. Private prayer (v.35): Christ prayed at baptism (Luke 3:21), transfiguration (Luke 9:29), before choosing the twelve (Luke 6:12), on the mountain (printed "Mark 14:23", apparently for Matt. 14:23), Gethsemane (Mark 14:34). If sinless Christ prayed, how much more we. Prayerless = not Christian; little prayer = little grace, strength, peace. Private prayer the pulse of Christianity.
2. Preaching (v.38): "for this I came forth". Christ fulfilled the prophet like Moses (Deut. 18:15). Not the ceremonial office of Aaron nor the rule of David, but preaching. Honour every part of worship but none above preaching; church founded, kept and spread by preaching (awakens, guides, builds, reaches heathen). Rebukes scorners of open-air/missionary preaching and those ranking sacraments above sermons. "Despise not prophesyings" (1 Thess. 5:20) even with a plain preacher.

## Mark 1:40-45
1. Leprosy: incurable, whole body (blood, flesh, bones), loss of limbs; unclean, cut off from congregation, separate house, no touch; "as one dead" (Num. 12:12). Emblem of sin: ingrained, loathsome, incurable by man, universal (Isa. 64:6). Knowing the disease is first step to cure.
2. Christ's power: "If thou wilt..." / "I will, be clean"; immediate, total change, life from the dead. Christ able to save the worst: Manasseh, Saul, Zacchaeus. Men lost not for being too bad but for not coming.
3. Time to be silent: man disobeyed the charge, Jesus could not enter cities openly. Zeal without knowledge harms; pearls before swine (Matt. 7:6). Most Christians need spur more than bridle, yet good men can help enemies by untimely words. Pray for wisdom: "wisdom is profitable to direct" (Eccles. 10:11 as printed).
