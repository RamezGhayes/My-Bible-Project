# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 14 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m14.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references, quotations and verses treated.

## Matthew 14:1-12
Intro: a page from God's book of martyrs; Mark tells it more fully; two general lessons.
1. Power of conscience (v.1-2): Herod thinks John risen; every man has conscience (Rom. 2:15), poor guide without the Spirit but accuses/excuses; may be seared and trampled; can fill princes with fear as Felix when Paul preached; Bishop Hall: wicked man needs no tormentor but his own heart, especially for blood; God's word not bound (2 Tim. 2:9), prophets die but words live (Zech. 1:5); sin evil and bitter (Jer. 2:19); to ungodly: sin finds you out; to ministers and teachers: teach boldly, conscience may bear fruit later.
2. No reward in this world (v.6-11): John's godliness rewarded with death at a dancer's whim and a capricious tyrant; Ps. 116:15 precious death of saints; God will require martyrs' blood (Isa. 26:21); Eccles. 5:8 higher than the highest; James, Stephen, Polycarp, Huss, Ridley, Latimer; 2 Cor. 4:17 light affliction; wait for an eternal holiday.
Not treated: v.3-5 (alluded only), v.8, v.9, v.12.

## Matthew 14:13-21
Intro: all four Gospels record it.
1. Unanswerable proof of divine power (v.17-19): creation God's prerogative; no impostor would attempt it, exposed at once; store such evidence; a cold orthodox unconverted man may know this.
2. Compassion (v.14): pitied all, even those following from fashion and curiosity (John 6:26); not according to sins; Exod. 34:6; Rom. 2:4; Micah 7:18; Quesnel: abundance of pity for diseased souls; imitate.
3. Emblem of Gospel's sufficiency (v.15-21 figuratively): warns against Fathers' allegorizing where Spirit meant none; crowd = mankind lost (Isa. 53:6; Rev. 3:17); loaves = doctrine of Christ crucified as substitute; foolishness to natural man (1 Cor. 1:23); bread of God (John 6:33); fed countless; like the small cloud (1 Kings 18:44); Scripture inexhaustible.
Not treated: v.13, v.15-16, v.20 separately.

## Matthew 14:22-36
Intro: shows Christ's power and mercy and disciples' mixture of faith and unbelief.
1. Absolute dominion over creation (v.25): Doddridge: two feet walking on the sea was an Egyptian hieroglyphic for the impossible; "man of science"; Ps. 93:4; Jonah 2:3; waves His; storms only by His leave.
2. Power given to believers (v.28-29): greater miracle that weak disciple walked; John 14:12; Moses in Egypt, Daniel in Babylon, saints in Nero's household.
3. Trouble by unbelief (v.30): fear overcame faith; forgot past goodness; nearer Christ on water than in boat; eyes on enemies not Jesus.
4. Mercy to weak believers (v.31): immediate rescue; gentle rebuke; doubt shows faith small, not absent; mother not casting away infant for waywardness; Ps. 94:18; pray increase our faith; He will never leave nor forsake.
Not treated: v.22-24, 26-27, 32-36.
