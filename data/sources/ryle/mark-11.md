# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 11 (reading notes, own words)

## Mark 11:1-11
1. Publicity of the last act: Christ, who often kept quiet (Galilee, Capernaum), now enters openly amid Passover crowds and Hosanna; compared to David returning to his palace (2 Sam. 19:40). He came to die and wanted all Jerusalem (rulers, priests, elders, scribes, Jews, Greeks, Romans) to know; death a public event. Prize the cross.
2. Voluntary poverty: borrowed colt, garments as saddle; also borrowed boat, borrowed tomb. Union of divine power and human weakness. Comfort: as man He sympathizes, as God He saves.
3. Poverty no sin: distinguish poverty from sinful causes (drunkenness, extravagance, idleness, dishonesty). Honest poor honourable; Christ poor. Do duty in one's station without shame.
Not treated: details of vv.1, 11 (Bethany, looking round the temple).

## Mark 11:12-21
1. "He was hungry" (v.12): real humanity; condescension; "unsearchable riches of Christ" (Eph. 3:8); sympathy with bodily needs.
2. Fig tree with nothing but leaves (vv.13-14, 20): acted parable. Applied to the Jewish Church, every branch of the visible church, and carnal/hypocritical professors. Baptism, membership, Lord's Supper, forms do not save; fruit = repentance, faith, holiness. Fig leaves of Adam and Eve an insufficient covering.
3. Temple cleansing (vv.15, 17): irreverence offends Christ; Christian place of worship a house of prayer, not business; reverence is not superstition or Popery; leave business and money at home, no worldly heart in worship.
Not treated: v.13 "not the season of figs", vv.18-19 (leaders plot), v.21 Peter's remark as such.

## Mark 11:22-25 (v.26 not in the printed block)
1. Faith (vv.23-24): mountain-moving = proverbial; faith here = general confidence in God's power, wisdom, goodwill, fruit/companion of justifying faith, not identical with it. Prayer in faith within what is sinless and according to God's will. James 1:6; Heb. 11 (elders obtained a good report; must believe that He is); Isa. 28:16. Peace, hope, joy, patience proportional to faith.
2. Forgiving spirit (v.25): link is prayer; no prayer heard from unforgiving heart; our forgiveness tied to forgiving others; stressed in the Lord's Prayer. Self-examination: if we cannot forgive, our Christianity is in question.

## Mark 11:27-33
1. Blindness in high office (v.27): chief priests, scribes, elders, the official teachers, opposed Christ. Do not treat ordained men as infallible in any order (Episcopal, Presbyterian, Independent); popes as example. Matt. 23:9. Christ alone the infallible Priest and Bishop.
2. Envy and unbelief (v.28): could not deny miracles or fault teaching, so attacked authority. Outward call lawful and wise (Heb. 5:4, Aaron; 23rd Article of the Church of England), but not the one thing needful; inward call of the Spirit. Test: for Christ? doctrine? life? doing good?
3. Equivocation (vv.31-33): "We cannot tell" a lie to dodge truth; modern parallels (can't understand, try but no progress, no time). "I cannot" usually = "I will not". John 7:17; John 3:19.
