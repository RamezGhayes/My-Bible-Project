# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 9 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m09.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Matthew 9:1-13
1. Lord's knowledge of thoughts (v.3-4): scribes thought privately; Heb. 4:13; Rom. 2:16; be humble, thank God for cleansing blood, pray Ps. 19:14.
2. Wonderful call of Matthew (v.9): absorbed in tax booth, money; obeyed at once; nothing impossible with Christ; despair of none; Ps. 29:4.
3. Matthew's decision: Ps. 119:60 haste; no "convenient time" (Acts 24:25); wrote a book known over the earth, name better known than princes and kings; rich soon forgotten.
4. Christ's declaration of His mission (v.12-13 "not righteous but sinners"): Pharisees proud and blind; Messiah as Savior and Physician; first step a deep sense of corruption; feeling a sinner a good sign; sinners at conversion, through life, at death; debtors to Christ's blood.
Not treated: v.1-2, v.5-8 (forgiveness, authority), v.10-11 only as complaint, v.13a "mercy not sacrifice".

## Matthew 9:14-26
1. Name "bridegroom" (v.15): union with believers; loves, pays debts, supplies needs, sympathizes, bears infirmities, regards them as part of Himself; persecuting them persecutes Him; share His glory; "Lamb's wife" Rev. 19:7; faith joins; inseparable. Blessed those who believe.
2. Wise principle for young disciples (v.14-17): lack of fitness in fasting while Bridegroom present; patches, wineskins; teach gently as they can bear; no rigid conformity in "things indifferent" before repentance and faith; prayer, common sense, tact.
3. Encouragement to humblest faith (v.20-22): no words, no public confession; hidden faith commended; weak faith saves; if cannot touch garment, touch His heart; "good old writer": came trembling, went back triumphing.
4. Almighty power (v.23-25): stillness of death; only the Creator restores life; clearer view of His power gives Gospel peace; don't be cast down.
Not treated: v.18-19, v.24, v.26.

## Matthew 9:27-38
Four lessons.
1. Strong faith where least expected (v.27-28): two blind men called Him "Son of David" from report; saw what scribes missed; don't despair of anyone because of circumstances; Matt. 20:16 last first.
2. Christ's experience of disease (v.35): every sickness; sick person's friend; world avoids sick; "I stand at the door and knock" (unreferenced); happy who let Him in.
3. Tender concern for neglected souls (v.36): compassion; do we feel? millions of heathen, Muhammadans, Roman Catholics; thousands of unsaved Protestants nearby; 1 Cor. 2:16 mind of Christ.
4. Duty of prayer (v.37-38): daily, conscience; surest way to do good; money and effort useless without Christ; universities give learning, bishops ordain, congregations elect, only the Spirit makes ministers; first duty to world is prayer.
Not treated: v.29-34.
