# Ryle, Expository Thoughts: Mark 3 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark03.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27).

## Mark 3:1-12 (printed block 3:1-11)
Intro: Christ always doing good, before enemies too; example (1 Pet. 2:21).
1. Watched by enemies (v.2): on Sabbath, in synagogue, at worship (Prov. 5:14). Christians watched: dress, spending, time, conduct; failures celebrated. Holy jealousy; avoid appearance of evil; Christ, once watched, sympathises.
2. Lawful to do good on Sabbath (v.4): healing vs their murderous plotting. Real works of mercy on Sunday, no breach of 4th commandment. Not pleasure: amusements, travel, Sunday trains, steamboats, exhibitions; rob servants of rest.
3. Christ's anger (v.5): truly man, sin excepted (wonder, joy, weeping, love, anger). Lawful anger (Prov. 25:23; Eph. 4:26). Anger easily sins: Paul and Barnabas, Moses' unadvised words, Sermon on Mount on angry words. Sinless wrath rare; mix indignation with grief; better never angry than angry and sin.
NOT treated by Ryle: v.7-12 (no ryle entries there).

## Mark 3:13-21
Intro: apostles' names loved worldwide while kings forgotten (Ps. 112:6).
1. Converted before ordained: six first calls recorded (Peter, Andrew, James, John, Philip, Matthew); eleven likely converted first. "First converted, then ordained." Examine candidates; unconverted minister cannot speak from experience; parents pushing sons into ministry for income = encouraging a lie. Unconverted ministers help infidelity, please the devil.
2. Apostolic office (v.14-15): with Him, sent to preach, power to heal and cast out devils. Apostles a distinct order with no successors; literal apostolic succession does not exist (would need miracles and infallibility); still models for ministers: communion with Christ (1 John 1:3, Mary at His feet); preaching chief work, above sacraments (1 Cor. 1:17) and read forms (lampless lighthouse, silent trumpeter, sleeping watchman, painted fire); doing good, servants for Jesus' sake (2 Cor. 4:5) not lords; opposing the devil (racing, theatres, balls, gambling, drunkenness, Sabbath-breaking). Need prayers.
3. Zeal misunderstood (v.21): family said beside Himself. Jehu's anointer "mad fellow" (2 Kings 9:11); Festus and Paul. Worldly zeal praised, religious zeal called fanaticism (1 Cor. 2:14). Don't be moved; Christ knows; bear patiently, pity, pray; opposers may yet believe.
Not discussed: names of Peter/Boanerges, Judas.

## Mark 3:22-30
1. Evil of division (v.23-26): Satan not divided; division destroys strength; abuse of private judgment; weakens church, wastes energy, arms infidels, pleases devil (divide and conquer). Avoid strife, but don't confuse scrupulosity with conscience or trifles with truth. Separate from a church only when it has separated from the gospel; concede much for unity.
2. All sins forgivable (v.28): crown of the gospel. Saul, Manasseh, the Jews who crucified Christ, Peter. Blood cleanses, righteousness covers (Acts 13:39; Isa. 1:18). Receive now; cling when faint; once come, wholly forgiven.
3. Soul may be lost for ever (v.29): plain truth through Scripture; against teachers denying eternal punishment; sin infinite evil needing infinite atonement; "one plain text is worth a thousand abstruse arguments". Flee like Lot (Gen. 19:16, 17).
Not discussed: v.27 strong man.

## Mark 3:31-35
Intro: family wanted Him to stop and spare Himself; recalls "my Father's business" (Luke 2:49).
1. Perseverance: neither enemies' slanders nor friends' well-meant pleas turned Him (Luke 12:50). Answer like Nehemiah (Neh. 6:3). Take up the cross.
2. True relatives (v.35): doers of God's will. Christ loved Mary and brothers yet counts all such family. Comfort for poor, persecuted, rejected (Ps. 27:10). Warning to persecutors: attacking the King's family; "their redeemer is mighty" (Prov. 23:11).
