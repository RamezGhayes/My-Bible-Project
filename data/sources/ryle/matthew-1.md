# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 1 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m01.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27; index page is m00.htm, the preface). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Matthew 1:1-17
Intro: read Scripture reverently (inspired; judged by the light we have; pray for the Spirit's teaching); Gospels show Christ; knowing Him is eternal life. Genealogy looks unprofitable but nothing in Scripture is useless (mosses and insects).
1. v.1 God keeps His word: son of David, son of Abraham. Gen. 12:3; Isa. 11:1; 2 Tim. 2:13 (+ "not a man that He should lie", no ref). Ungodly fear, Christians comfort.
2. Corruption of human nature: godly fathers, wicked sons: Rehoboam (v.7), Joram (v.8), Amon (v.10), Jechoniah (v.11). Grace not hereditary; John 1:13; parents pray.
3. v.16 mercy of Christ: eternal God born of a woman to save sinners; none beyond His sympathy.
Not treated separately: the women in the list (not named by Ryle), v.12-15, v.17.

## Matthew 1:18-25
1. v.18 two truths: real human nature, virgin birth; mysteries to believe, not speculate; Apostles' Creed quoted.
2. v.19-20 Joseph: just, tender, avoided appearance of evil, waited, likely prayed; message from God. Isa. 28:16; Prov. 3:6.
3. v.21 Jesus = Saviour (Joshua): from guilt, power, presence, consequences of sin; who clings to sin not saved; earthly titles Great/Conqueror/Magnificent vs Saviour. John 3:17; Cant. 1:3.
4. v.23 Emmanuel: perfect God and perfect man; human traits vs divine acts in Gospels. Rom. 9:5. Application: divinity for foundation of faith, humanity for comfort in trouble.
Not treated: v.22 (fulfilment formula), v.24, v.25 (nothing on perpetual virginity).
