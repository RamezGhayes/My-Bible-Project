# Ryle, Expository Thoughts: Mark 2 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark02.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27).

## Mark 2:1-12
1. Privileges unused: Capernaum had more of Christ's presence than any city (Matt. 4:13), amazed not converted; judgment worse than Sodom (Matt. 11:23, 24). Gospel savour of life or of death; regular hearing can harden. Litany: "From hardness of heart, good Lord, deliver us."
2. Affliction a blessing: paralytic carried by four, got forgiveness as well as healing; sickness likely brought him to Christ. Bereavements become mercies, losses gains; sickness drives to Physician, Bible, prayer. Ps. 119:71. Don't murmur; every trial has a "needs-be" and a message; refuse not Him that speaks.
3. Christ alone forgives (priestly power): declared forgiveness, claimed authority of the true High Priest; scribes' question answered by Son of Man's authority on earth. No angel, man, church or minister absolves by own authority; ministers only point to the fountain and declare God's terms. Conscience demands atonement; God's holiness requires it. Christ mighty, tender, willing. Have we gone to Him for absolution?
Not commented: roof, the word "son" (v.5).

## Mark 2:13-22
1. Call of Levi (= Matthew of the first Gospel). None saved without a divine call; 17th Article (called according to God's purpose by His Spirit in due season). Sovereign but merciful; unlikely people; breaks habits; magnet and iron, south wind thawing the ground; "voice of the Lord is mighty in operation". Never despair of anyone, even the rich at the receipt of custom.
2. Physician (v.17): not merely lawgiver, king, teacher, example; an unfallen Adam might need an example, fallen sinners need healing. Skill, tenderness, experience; none too far gone. Feeling sin is the beginning; it should draw, not repel.
3. Do not mix things that differ (v.18-22): fasting unseasonable for the Bridegroom's disciples, young in grace, need gentle leading. John = Bridegroom's friend, wilderness, repentance; Jesus = Bridegroom, glad tidings, lived like other men. Applied: Galatians mixing Judaism and Christ; post-apostolic church mixing Gospel with philosophy and heathen forms, paving way to the Romish apostasy; modern serving of Christ and world. "No man can serve two masters"; "new wine into new bottles".
Not commented: Abiathar.

## Mark 2:23-28
1. Formalists magnify trifles: plucking grain called breach, while ignoring covetousness etc. Husk vs kernel: washings, fastings, dress, will-worship vs repentance, faith, holiness. Modern: saints' days, turning east at the creed, bowing at the name of Jesus. Gal. 4:10, 11. Uneasy conscience seeks ceremonies; symptom of disease, first step to apostasy.
2. Value of Scripture: Christ answered from David and the shewbread. David's record (1 Kings 15:5). Some ceremonial requirements yield to necessity. Our reason: "It is written"; sword of the Spirit; read diligently and humbly; no royal road.
3. Sabbath principle (v.27, saying only in Mark): made for Adam in Paradise, renewed at Sinai, for the whole human family; a blessing not a burden; not to harm health or block mercy. Christ did not abolish the fourth commandment; works of necessity and mercy allowed. More danger of profaning than over-strictness; national prosperity and growth in grace tied to a holy Sabbath.
