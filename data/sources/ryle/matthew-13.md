# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 13 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m13.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references, quotations and verses treated.

## Matthew 13:1-23
Intro: seven parables from nature; teachers may draw on all creation (Eccles. 12:10 acceptable words); sower seen in every congregation.
1. Preacher like sower (v.3): sow pure word, not traditions; diligent (Isa. 32:20; 2 Tim. 4:2 in season and out; Eccles. 11:4 who observes wind); cannot give life (John 6:63; 1 Cor. 3:7); hard work, pray for preachers.
2. Ways of hearing without profit: wayside (v.4, 19) careless, snatched, Ps. 135:16-17; stony (v.5-6, 20-21) feelings and resolutions fade, love of sermons no sign of grace, Ezek. 33:32; thorns (v.7, 22) cares and pleasures choke, approve Gospel but never give all; coming, attention, impressions each not enough; Jer. 17:9.
3. Fruit only evidence (v.8, 23): fruit of Spirit: repentance, faith, holiness, prayer, humility, charity, spiritual-mindedness; barren orthodoxy worthless; John 15:16; James 1:22.
Close: How do we hear? attendance no guarantee.
Not treated: v.10-17.

## Matthew 13:24-43
Intro: wheat and tares especially important now, corrects inflated expectations of missions and preaching; mustard seed and leaven purposely deferred to a later part of the Exposition.
1. Good and evil mixed in visible church till the end (v.24-30, 38-39): pure preaching (Fathers, Reformers, moderns) and strict discipline (Episcopalian, Presbyterian, Independent) can't prevent; excessive purging breaks bruised reeds; Judas; Augustine: weeds today may be wheat tomorrow; don't expect world's conversion before Christ returns; infidel objection answered: Jesus foretold it; don't hop between churches seeking perfect one.
2. Day of separation (v.41-43): angels separate without error; Baxter: don't misinterpret God's patience with ungodly; M. Henry: sanctification perfected, justification published; shine as sun; 1 John 3:1; Col. 3:4.
Not treated: v.31-35 (deferred), v.36-37 separately.

## Matthew 13:44-50
1. Convinced men give up all (v.44-46): treasure finder not seeking, merchant seeking; both sold all; Matthew; like Paul counts all loss (no ref); mark of true faith.
2. Unconverted halt because not persuaded; unwilling to risk anything = lacks grace.
3. Net (v.47-50): church mixed; separation at end; addressing congregations as if all born again "utterly unwarrantable".
4. Outward membership not enough: baptism waters on myriads never washed in water of life; bread and wine eaten by thousands who never feed on Christ by faith; am I a good fish?
Close: Baxter: plain words need belief and consideration more than exposition.

## Matthew 13:51-58
Intro: application the soul of preaching; sermon without it like letter without address.
1. Hearing needs understanding (v.51): trumpet, drum, Roman Catholic service in Latin; Spirit reaches heart through mind; ask what learned from each sermon.
2. Familiarity breeds contempt (v.54-57): carpenter's son, Mary, James, Joses, Simon, Judas; undervalue Bibles, weekly preaching; relatives, servants, neighbors of godly, parishioners of eminent ministers least moved; wrong to think we'd have believed if we'd seen Him.
3. Ruinous unbelief (v.58): decisive barrier; John 5:40; old root-sin; pray daily against pride, worldliness, unbelief; none greater than unbelief.
Not treated: v.52, v.53.
