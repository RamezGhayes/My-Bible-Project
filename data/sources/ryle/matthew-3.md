# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 3 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m03.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes.

## Matthew 3:1-12
Intro: John the forerunner; crowds (v.5); Christ called him a burning and shining light, none greater born of women. Five features of his preaching:
1. Plain about sin: v.2 repent, v.6 confessing, v.7-8 fruits; no trust in outward privileges or church membership.
2. Plain about Christ: v.3, v.11; servant vs King; send people to Christ, not to church, sacraments or ministers; union with Christ by faith.
3. Plain about the Holy Spirit (v.11): work for us must be matched by work in us; title to heaven and meetness for it.
4. Plain about the danger of the impenitent (v.7 wrath, v.12 unquenchable fire); hell as well as heaven; withholding terrors no kindness.
5. Plain about the safety of believers (v.12 wheat into barn); safe as Noah in the ark.
No chapter:verse references printed. Not treated: v.4, v.9, v.10.

## Matthew 3:13-17
Intro: first step of public ministry; Jewish priests washed at thirty; Christ the High Priest.
1. v.13-15 reverence for baptism. Two cautions: no superstitious value to the water (not a charm; baptism and grace not inseparably tied); do not dishonour it by hiding it from the congregation; pray for a blessing.
2. v.16-17 all three Persons present; "let us make man" / "let us save man".
3. v.17 voice: only earlier voice at Sinai; Saviour, Mediator, substitute, propitiation; Rom. 3:26; Eph. 1:6; believers accepted in the Beloved.
Not treated directly: v.14.
