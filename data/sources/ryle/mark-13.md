# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 13 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark13.htm (via WebFetch, 2026-11-27; outline fetch plus a second fetch for references and verses treated).
Sections registered as ryle mark-13-<a>-<b>.
Framing: prophecy of two events, destruction of Jerusalem (about forty years after the crucifixion) and the still-future second coming.

## Mark 13:1-8
1. True glory of a church is faith and godliness, not buildings (vv.1-2): Christ shows no admiration for the temple; outward show (buildings, ceremony, music) worthless without Christ preached; decent plain buildings fine (1 Cor. 14:40), no Popery in a handsome church; Parthenon, St Peter's at Rome vs. caves of early Christians. Doctrine and holy living the real ornaments.
2. Expect false doctrine and wars, not a golden age (vv.4-8): "Take heed lest any man deceive you" the key to the whole interval between advents; Jer. 28:8, 9; moderate expectations; world not converted before Christ returns; "a day of election, and not of universal conversion"; Matt. 7:14. (Rev. 1:3 also printed on the page.)

## Mark 13:9-13
1. Two kinds of trouble: from the world (v.9: councils, synagogues, governors) and from one's own family (v.12). Job 5:7; 1 Cor. 2:14; Gal. 4:29. Count the cost; Britain comparatively favoured, still expect ridicule.
2. Cordials: gospel to all nations (v.10), word not bound (2 Tim. 2:9); Spirit's help on trial (v.11): Peter, John, Paul; Huss, Luther, Latimer, Ridley, Baxter; endurance to the end saves (v.13), glory after affliction.

## Mark 13:14-23
1. Use of means lawful (vv.14-18, flight): Jacob (Gen. 32:9-13); Hezekiah (2 Chron. 32:5); Paul let down from Damascus wall; Acts 27:31. Not resting on means: Asa's sin (seeking physicians, not the Lord; no reference printed).
2. Privileges of the elect (vv.20, 22): quotes the Seventeenth Article "of our church"; justification, sanctification, glory. Cautions: election does not remove responsibility; we follow God's revealed will; assurance only via repentance and faith (Acts 17:30; 1 John 3:23): "grammar school" before "university of election"; 1 Thess. 1:4.
Not treated: v.14 abomination of desolation identity, v.19 as such, v.21, v.23.

## Mark 13:24-31
Entirely unfulfilled; second coming, may be in our day.
1. Majesty of second coming (vv.24-26): universal convulsion; 2 Pet. 3:10; first advent weakness vs. second royal judgment and reign; comfort to friends, terror to foes.
2. Gathering of elect first (v.27): Noah in the ark, Lot in Zoar; 2 Thess. 2:1.
3. Signs of the times (vv.28-29): Matt. 16:3; drying up of Turkish power, missions, revival of Popery, new infidelity, lawlessness; Rev. 16:15.
4. Certainty of Christ's words (v.31): scoffers 2 Pet. 3:3, 4; first advent fulfilled guarantees second.
Not treated: v.30 ("this generation"), per second fetch.

## Mark 13:32-37
1. Day and hour withheld (v.32): wise and merciful, keeps expectancy; early church would have been dismayed to know of centuries (Athanasius, Chrysostom, Augustine); but do not neglect prophecy: 2 Pet. 1:19; Rev. 1:3; reject dogmatic dates, encourage humble study.
2. Watch, pray, work (vv.33-37): 1 Thess. 5:6; 2 Pet. 3:14; Luke 21:34; serve in one's calling, salt and light, farming/trade/law not to be abandoned; self-examination ("Do we long for His appearing?"); holiness needed to see the Lord (unreferenced); ends on "Watch!".
