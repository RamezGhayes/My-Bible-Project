# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 5 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m05.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes.

## Matthew 5:1-12
Intro: Christ's words most precious; study the Sermon for Christian character.
v.3 humility, begin low to build high; v.4 mourning over sin (broken spirit, no ref); v.5 meek, like Lazarus in the parable, Rev. 5:10; v.6 Ps. 17:15; v.7 merciful; v.8 1 Sam. 16:7, communion with God; v.9 peacemakers, love fulfils the law; v.10 persecuted share the Master's cup. Close: Christ's principles opposed to the world's; never accept a lower standard.
Not treated: v.1-2, v.11-12 (only within the persecution point).

## Matthew 5:13-20
Intro: two themes. v.13 salt; v.14-16 light, dare to be singular. v.17 fulfilment (prophecy, ceremonial law, moral law), Isa. 42:21; bud/blade vs flower/full ear. Lessons: do not despise the OT, the Ten Commandments (Rom. 3:31; 7:22), or lower the standard of holiness (Gethsemane, Calvary).
Not treated: v.18-20.

## Matthew 5:21-37
Intro: law more spiritual than the Jews thought; three commandments. v.21-22 sixth (anger, contempt); v.27-28 seventh (thoughts, a glance); v.33-37 third (all vain and light swearing; oaths by creatures; calling God to witness allowed only on most solemn occasions). Lessons: God's holiness; man's ignorance (like the young ruler); need of Christ ("none righteous", no ref); avoid occasions of sin (guard tongue, end quarrels early, breach in a dam image, mortify members). No chapter:verse refs printed.
Not treated: v.23-26 (only the general point on ending quarrels), v.29-32 (divorce not discussed).

## Matthew 5:38-48
Intro: praised even by enemies of Christianity. v.38-39 against revenge (a matter of spirit and temper; no discussion of law or self-defence); v.40-44 universal love, second mile, "what would Christ have me do"; v.45 children of the Father, John 3:7; v.46-47 rise above the world, 1 Cor. 2:12. Lessons: small courtesies adorn the faith; the world would be happier.
Not treated: v.43 separately, v.48.
