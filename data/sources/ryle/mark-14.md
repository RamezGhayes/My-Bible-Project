# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 14 (reading notes, own words)

## Mark 14:1-9
1. God overrules the wicked (vv.1-2): "not during the feast", yet He died at the feast; they thought to silence the disciples and gave them a text for ever. Ps. 76:10; the second Psalm (unnumbered).
2. Good works misunderstood (vv.3-5): "waste"; world praises moderation, mocks wholehearted devotion; Ps. 116:12; a cold heart makes a slow hand.
3. Christ values service to Himself (vv.6, 8): "why trouble her", "a good work", "she has done what she could"; those claiming to do all they can lie like Ananias and Sapphira; serve with what we have; vindication at judgment.
Not treated explicitly: v.7, v.9.

## Mark 14:10-16
1. Judas (v.10): privileges; root = love of money; John 12:6; 1 Tim. 6:10; Balaam, Gehazi; Luke 12:15; Heb. 13:5; knowledge and membership cannot save an unconverted heart.
2. Passover timing providential (vv.12, 16): type, Gal. 3:24; four lessons: deliverance from Egypt, innocent lamb's death, blood on door-posts, feeding on the lamb. Study OT ordinances (altar, scapegoat, burnt offering, Day of Atonement) with Christ as key.
Not treated: vv.13-15 (water jar, upper room).

## Mark 14:17-25
1. Self-examination before the Lord's Supper (vv.18-19, "Is it I?"): quotes Church Catechism questions (repent truly, new life, lively faith, charity with all men); neither stay away nor come thoughtlessly.
2. Nature of the Supper (vv.22, 24): chiefly a remembrance of Christ's atoning death; bread and wine called "visible symbols"; body wounded for our transgressions; produces humility, hope, thankfulness; rejects view that His death was merely a holy example.
3. (vv.22-23, eat/drink): no benefit apart from faith; benefit regardless of state of heart = "monstrous and unscriptural figment"; for believers only; not converting or justifying; sustains life, does not impart it; Christ's body eaten only by faith; aids faith, never supersedes it. (Page does not discuss transubstantiation or sacrifice by name.)
Not treated: v.17, vv.20-21, v.25.

## Mark 14:26-31
1. Christ foreknew weakness yet chose them (vv.27-30); does not cast off for failures; Church "little better than a great hospital".
2. Disciples ignored His words (v.28, resurrection, Galilee); Hagar's well (Gen. 21:19); word to ministers who are unheeded.
3. Ignorant self-confidence (vv.29-31): Prov. 16:18; 1 Cor. 10:12; Prov. 28:26; daily prayer "Hold me up and I shall be safe" (no reference printed).

## Mark 14:32-42
1. Agony (vv.33-34): not mere fear of death; weight of man's guilt laid on Him; exceeding sinfulness of sin; do not make a mock of sin.
2. Prayer first resource (v.35): James 5:13.
3. Submission (v.36): "not what I will"; self-will causes misery; submission a better mark of grace than knowledge or feelings.
4. Best believers weak (vv.37-41, v.38): Gal. 5:17; watch and pray together; watching without praying breeds self-confidence, praying without watching fanaticism.

## Mark 14:43-51
1. Kingdom misunderstood (v.43 swords and staves, v.47): John 18:36; 2 Cor. 10:4; Zech. 4:6; Mohammedanism spread by the sword, Roman Church enforced false Christianity by persecution.
2. Scripture fulfilled (vv.48-49): Ps. 2:2; Psalm 22 and Isaiah 53 literally fulfilled (no verse refs); armed men unconscious instruments like Nebuchadnezzar and Sennacherib.
3. All fled (v.50): faith may fail; humility; charity to fallen Christians; apostles repented and became pillars; Christ sympathises with disappointments; Lam. 3:22.
Not treated: vv.44-46 details, vv.51-52 (young man).

## Mark 14:53-65
1. Folly in high places (v.53, 55): Eccles. 10:6; Son of God tried by sinners.
2. Peter following afar off, warming at the fire (v.54): drift into temptation; backsliding like a stone rolling downhill; David, Peter; "Lead us not into temptation" among most important petitions.
3. False witnesses (vv.55-59): Prov. 18:8; Ps. 120:2; expect slander; Luke 6:26; Matt. 5:11.
4. Messiahship and second coming declared (vv.61-62): Jews cannot say they were not plainly told; live expecting His return. Spitting and blows (v.65) noted.
Not treated: v.58 temple saying content, vv.63-64.

## Mark 14:66-72
1. Great saint's fall (vv.66-71): Peter's privileges (commendation, transfiguration); threefold denial with cursing; Prov. 28:14.
2. Small temptation, great fall (v.67, servant girl): Phil. 4:13; no temptation too small to guard against.
3. Backsliding brings sorrow (v.72): Lot, Samson, David, Jehoshaphat; Cranmer and "Jewell" in English church history; Prov. 14:14; Ps. 16:4; holiness brings most happiness; forgiven sin still leaves sorrow.
