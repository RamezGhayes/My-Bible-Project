# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 10 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m10.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Matthew 10:1-15
Intro: solemn chapter: first ordination and first charge to ministers, by Christ Himself.
1. Ministers not necessarily saved (v.4 Judas): Christ knew whom He chose; ordination gives no saving grace; ministers fallible; not popes or idols in Christ's place; pray for them; test teaching by Scripture.
2. Minister's great work is to do good (v.6-8): life of giving not receiving; not easy; spend himself; serve not rule (2 Cor. 1:24); misunderstanding of office caused much harm.
3. Danger of neglecting Gospel offers (v.15): more tolerable for Sodom and Gomorrah; hearers judged more severely; John 16:9; morality and church attendance not enough; real union with Christ.
Not treated: v.2-3, v.5, v.9-14 (only general "giving" for v.8).

## Matthew 10:16-23
Intro: applies to all who seek to do good, parts specific to apostles.
1. Moderate expectations (v.16-22 generally): sheep among wolves; opposition, hatred, even family; human nature worse than thought; many turned back for unrealistic hopes; great German Reformer confessed he forgot that old Adam was too strong for young Melancthon.
2. Pray for wisdom, good sense, sound mind (v.16 serpents and doves; v.23 flee): two errors, cowardly silence called prudence, and needless offense / zeal not according to knowledge; wisdom not cunning or worldly policy; David's life and Paul's ministry prove grace and common sense together; Eph. 5:15; Acts 6:3; Christ the pattern.
Not treated: v.17-22 individually (only in general), v.23b.

## Matthew 10:24-33
Intro: encouragements for discouraged workers.
1. Not above Master (v.24-25): Christ slandered, called Beelzebub; leave world alone and it leaves you alone.
2. Patience till judgment (v.26): misunderstood now; vindicated at last day; Ps. 37:6; 1 Cor. 4:5.
3. Fear God more than man (v.28): man only body; Daniel and three Hebrew children; Colonel Gardiner: I fear God, so none else I need fear.
4. Providential care (v.29-31): sparrows, hairs numbered; no chance; bodies, souls, characters in God's hands.
5. Remember meeting the Lord (v.32-33): confess openly despite mockery.
Close: every believer can do something.
Not treated: v.27.

## Matthew 10:34-42
1. Gospel not peace everywhere (v.34-36): division due to man's sin, not the Gospel; unity valuable but truth and conscience more; first coming not to set up a millennial kingdom; idle dream to expect much unity before the millennium.
2. Make up minds to trouble (v.38-39): cross, lose world's favor; crown at end, cross on the way.
3. Least service rewarded (v.41-42): God notes who helps (Lydia to Paul) or hinders (Diotrephes to John); chief butler forgot Joseph but Lord never forgets; Matt. 25:35; leave world better.
Not treated: v.37, v.40.
