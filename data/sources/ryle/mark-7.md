# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 7 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark07.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Mark 7:1-13
Intro: how low human nature can sink in religion; study often.
1. Low state of Jewish religion (v.1-5): unwashed hands, cups, pots; Israel's privileges (law, priesthood, covenants, promises; Moses, Samuel, David, prophets); fallen from Deuteronomy/Psalms to handwashing; externals cannot satisfy conscience; warning against tolerating false doctrine; some churches neither read Scripture nor preach the Gospel; contend for the faith once delivered.
2. Lip-service (v.6-7): God looks at heart; public worship with absent heart worthless; private prayer, fluent words with wandering mind; halting heartfelt prayer pleases God. (Ryle's text attributes the quotation to "Elijah", apparently a slip; printed passage has Isaiah. Not reproduced.)
3. Traditions displace Scripture (v.8-9, 13): three stages (supplement, equal, above); Christians repeated it; "history of our own church" supplies examples (church not named in so many words); adding anything as needful to salvation implies Scripture imperfect; Bible alone rule of faith; God's commands essential, man's devices possibly useful but not necessary.
Not treated: v.10-12 (Corban).

## Mark 7:14-23
1. Slowness to understand (v.14, 17-18): sin corrupts understanding; clever men miss simple truths; 1 Cor. 1:21; need the Spirit; teachable spirit; Ps. 119:64.
2. Heart the source (v.15, 20): not washing or food; companions only reveal; parents should pray for children's conversion, not just blame bad company.
3. Catalogue (v.21-23): in all hearts, restrained by fear, opinion, or grace; humility (Isa. 64:6), gratitude (blood and Spirit), watchfulness (thoughts, tongue; "lead us not into temptation").
Not treated: v.19 (foods clean), v.16.

## Mark 7:24-30
Intro: little known of the woman, facts instructive.
1. Pray for others (v.25-26): daughter could not pray; nobleman's son, centurion's servant, Jairus' daughter; James 5:16; parents, prodigals, worldly daughters; children prayed for rarely perish; pray when they won't talk.
2. Perseverance (v.27-29): discouraging reply; crumbs; dangers (cold hearts, Satan, short intercession dropped).
Applications: pray for yourself first; avoid selfish prayer, name loved ones, hardest ones; answers after 10, 15, 20 years or after death; intercession a great kindness; judgment day reveals its fruit.

## Mark 7:31-37
1. Miracle's meaning (v.32-35): Christ heals spiritually deaf and dumb, worst sinners; never despair of others or self.
2. Manner (v.33-34): reasons not given, mysterious; not tied to one means (preaching, reading, affliction, friends' counsel, providence without means); idolize none, despise none.
3. "He has done all things well" (v.37): like Caiaphas (John 11:51); looking back on conversion, forward (Shepherd makes no mistakes), resurrection (1 Cor. 13:12).
Not treated: v.31, 36.
