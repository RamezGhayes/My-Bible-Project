# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 15 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark15.htm (via WebFetch, 2026-11-27; outline fetch plus a second fetch for headings, references and verses treated).
Sections registered as ryle mark-15-<a>-<b>. Headings: 1-15, 16-32 (text printed in two blocks 16-27 and 29-32; v.28 not printed), 33-38, 39-47.

## Mark 15:1-15
1. Proof Messiah had come (v.1): rulers had lost power of life and death, sceptre departed (Gen. 49:10); Dan. 9:26; blinded rulers; wicked fulfil prophecy unawares; scoffers foretold (2 Peter 3:3).
2. Meekness (vv.2-5): silence before false charges; Heb. 12:3; second Adam silent though guiltless vs. first Adam excusing himself though guilty; Isa. 53:7; patience, guard tongue (Ps. 39:1); 1 Pet. 2:20, 21.
3. Pilate's wavering (vv.9-15): knew innocence and envy; yielded to please the crowd; rulers without principle pitiable; high places slippery; pray for rulers (1 Tim. 2:1, 2); do not envy the great (Jer. 45:5).
4. Guilt of the Jews (vv.11-14): chose murderer; Gentiles executed but guilt chiefly on the Jews who took it on themselves; do not choose Barabbas (world's friendship) over Christ.
5. Barabbas a picture of the gospel (v.15): guilty freed, innocent dies; substitution; Substitute and Surety; plea is Christ's death not our worth.

## Mark 15:16-32
Opening: love of Christ passes knowledge (Eph. 3:19; Rom. 5:8); suffered for sinners (1 Peter 3:18). Six points, each "And why? That we...":
1. Delivered to soldiers as condemned (v.16): that we be delivered from hell and presented faultless.
2. Mocked (vv.17-20: purple, thorns, reed, spitting): 1 Cor. 4:13; that we may have glory and eternal life.
3. Stripped, crucified naked (v.24): that we be clothed in His righteousness (wedding garment).
4. Death of the cross, for worst criminals: Gal. 3:13; bore the curse that we be blessed.
5. Between two thieves, numbered with transgressors (v.27): that we be reckoned innocent.
6. Mocked while dying (vv.29-32): strong consolation and assurance at our end.
Close: our debt; 2 Cor. 9:15; living sacrifice (printed "2 Cor. 5:4. Rom 12:1."); keep the cross before our minds.
Not treated: Simon of Cyrene (v.21, only in text), myrrh (v.23), title (v.26).

## Mark 15:33-38
Christ's death the moment atonement accomplished, kingdom opened.
1. Darkness (v.33): wickedness of the nation; sinfulness of sin; must have affected Pilate and soldiers; blackness of darkness for unbelievers (Jude 13); Christ's lasted three hours.
2. Rent veil (v.38): end of ceremonial law; holiest opened through Christ the High Priest (Heb. 9:8); reviving altars, sacrifices, priesthood = closing up the rent veil.
3. Cry of dereliction (v.34): mental suffering beyond understanding, bearing world's sin; mere-man/example views make the cry unintelligible; only substitution explains it.
4. Forsaken for a time yet beloved (v.34): believers may feel God's face hidden (sickness, affliction, carelessness, sovereign will); don't despair, examine, wait: Job 15:11; Job 13:15; Isa. 50:10; Ps. 42:11.
Not treated: vinegar, Elijah remark (vv.35-36), v.37 as such.

## Mark 15:39-47
1. Witnesses of real death (v.39 etc.): centurion, Galilean women, disciples who buried; excludes swoon/trance. Centurion presented as witness, not discussed as faith.
2. Honourable mention of women (vv.40-41): named women, followed and ministered; OT women as snares (before the flood, Sarah, Rebecca, Rachel, Delilah, Bathsheba, Jezebel) vs NT helpers (Elizabeth, Mary, Martha, Dorcas, Lydia, women in Romans); great work without being public teachers.
3. Joseph of Arimathea (vv.42-45): mentioned only here, waited for the kingdom, bold when disciples fled; honoured Christ in death; don't conclude no grace where unseen (1 Kings 19:18).
4. Honour on the grave (vv.46-47): face grave calmly, sting removed (1 Cor. 15:56, 57); be buried and raised with Christ now (Rom. 6:4); burial profits nothing without union by faith.
