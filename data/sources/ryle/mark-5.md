# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 5 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark05.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Mark 5:1-17
Intro: Mark gives this account more fully than the other two evangelists.
1. Possession real: not lunacy/epilepsy (not contagious, could not pass into swine; plunge has no other cause). Incredulous spirit like eastern king refusing to believe in ice. Unbelief about Satan leads to unbelief about God.
2. Satan's cruelty (v.3-5, tombs, chains, cutting himself; faint picture of hell), power (v.9 Legion, many subordinate unseen enemies), malice (v.12-13 swine; "Apollyon, the destroyer"). No jesting about the devil (condemned criminal / executioner image). Unnamed eminent Christian: no prayer complete without asking to be kept from the devil.
3. Christ's power over Satan (v.6-8, 13, 15): adjuration, command, obedience; Eccles. 8:4; twelve legions of angels.
Close: Christ triumphed on cross, will bind Satan at His second advent; Satan still rules the ungodly (Eph. 2:3); self-examination: delivered, renounced, resisting?

## Mark 5:18-20
Intro: Gospels rarely tell what became of the healed.
1. v.18 "Jesus did not let him": Lord knows best position; new converts poor judges; grace needs winter and summer; best place = humble, Bible, prayer, faith; no will of our own.
2. v.19 home first claim; family knew him under Satan; against "saints abroad", worldly at home.
Close: can we testify? Unregenerate have nothing to tell; all can speak to relatives; Andrew, Philip, Samaritan woman (John 1:41, 45; 4:29); Ps. 66:16.
Not treated separately: v.20.

## Mark 5:21-34
Intro: Christ's gentleness vs pagan gods; world full of pain.
1. v.25-26 misery of sin; Prov. 13:12 hope deferred; sin cause of all disease; hate sin.
2. v.24, 27-28 crowd thronged, one touched in faith; churchgoers from habit/fashion/curiosity.
3. v.29 immediate cure; emblem of Gospel relieving conscience; self-imposed austerities vs one touch of faith; personal application to Christ.
4. v.30-33 He knew, wanted public acknowledgment; no trumpet, but confess Christ despite ridicule.
5. v.34 faith the empty hand; begin, live, stand, overcome, peace, rest; "do I really believe?"; Christ unchanged.
Not treated: v.21-23 (Jairus' request), v.31.

## Mark 5:35-43
Intro: Christ holds keys of death.
1. v.35 rank no exemption; Jairus' wealth, physicians; Heb. 9:27; Isa. 33:24; Isa. 25:8.
2. v.36, 38, 41-42 almighty power; mourning to joy; astonishment natural.
3. Proof for dead souls (Eph. 2:1); parents pray for unconverted children.
4. Pledge of second appearing (1 Thess. 4:14; Hos. 13:14); reunion, glorious bodies; not grieve without hope; no lamb missing.
Not treated: v.37, 39-40, 43.
