# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 8 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m08.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Matthew 8:1-15
Intro: chapter has five miracles, placed right after the Sermon to prove the preacher is the Son of God; three here (leper by touch, servant by a word, fever at once).
1. Christ's power (v.2-3, 5-13, 14-15): leprosy incurable (2 Kings 5:7); healed unseen; "finger of God" (Exod. 8:19). Old saint: faith sleeps only on the pillow of Christ's omnipotence. Lean on Him.
2. Christ's mercy (v.2-3, 6, 14): leper cried, servant reported, mother-in-law seen; same heart; touched with our infirmities; Job 36:5 He despises none.
3. Precious faith (v.5-13): centurion, Gentile, nation hidden, believed when Israel blinded; praised above Israel; faith rare, gift of God, better than learning; converted heathen may enter heaven while learned are cast out. Do we have it? No salvation without it.
Quotes v.8 but does not comment on humility. Not treated: v.1, v.4, v.11-12.

## Matthew 8:16-27
1. Wisdom with professing disciples (v.19-22): scribe's offer neither accepted nor rejected; v.20 solemn reply; v.21-22 lawful-looking request, solemn answer; count the cost; some duties yield; churches harmed by admitting without warning; numbers not strength; tell of crown and daily cross. No references.
2. True faith mixed with weakness (v.23-27): v.25-26; believers forsake all yet fear in trial; Job 13:15; pray increase our faith; Christ the compassionate High Priest hears prayer of little faith.
Not treated: v.16-17, v.18, v.27.

## Matthew 8:28-34
Intro: deep, mysterious subject.
1. Devil real (v.28-29): unseen spirit, power, malice since creation till the Lord binds him; possession especially common in Christ's ministry, rarer now.
2. Devil's power limited (v.29-31): "before the time"; could not hurt one swine without leave; go to and fro only until time allowed by the Lord of lords.
3. Christ deliverer (v.28-32): bruised serpent's head; Acts 10:38; Heb. 7:25; cry to Him.
4. Worldliness of Gergesenes (v.34): two immortal souls freed, yet they cared for swine; many love money more than Christ, hinder others' salvation; remember soul, death, judgment.
Not treated: v.33 separately.
