## Mark 12:1-12
1. God's special kindness to the Jewish Church and nation (v.1): laws, land, seven nations driven out; preferred over Egypt, Assyria, Greece, Rome. Applied to Britain (not heathen like China, Hindostan) by free grace; thankfulness, humility, warning.
2. God's patience (vv.2-5): provocation and pardon; 2 Chron. 36:16; wrath only after long patience; Tyre, Babylon, Rome; Britain must bear fruit; Prov. 14:34.
3. Hardness of human nature (vv.6-8): rejected prophets, miracles, killed the Son; no innate goodness; Rom. 8:7; privileges cannot change the heart, only the Spirit; John 3:7.
4. Conscience pierced yet impenitent (v.12): they knew the parable was against them and were plotting the very deed; knowledge and conviction do not save; change of heart and will needed.
Not treated: v.9 (judgment, vineyard to others), vv.10-11 (rejected stone).

## Mark 12:13-17
1. Pharisees (superstitious formalists) and Herodians (worldly) unite against Christ (v.13); formalists and worldlings unite against the pure Gospel; expect no mercy.
2. Subtlety of the tribute question (v.14): yes offends Jews, no offends Romans; devil's hand; church/state boundary questions have troubled Christians; clashes with civil power hurt Christ's cause; prayer "Give peace in our time, O Lord" (no source named on page).
3. Wisdom of the answer (vv.15-17): saw through flattery (Prov. 26:23); their own denarius; render to Caesar / to God; true Christianity makes loyal subjects, but God first when laws conflict: the three children, Daniel.

## Mark 12:18-27
1. Unfairness of infidel arguments (vv.18-23): seven-husbands case probably invented; lessons: avoid abstruse matters, argue from plain facts; watch for dishonesty; every infidel has a conscience; many sceptics admitted never studying the Bible.
2. Error from ignorance of Scripture (v.24): Josiah's reformation after finding the law; Jewish errors in Christ's day; Dark Ages (Bible withheld); Reformation through translation. Study, spread, teach children.
3. Resurrection state (v.25): difficulties admitted; real, glorified, recognisable bodies, no hunger, weariness, death, marriage; contrast with Mahomet's sensual paradise; 2 Cor. 5:4; John 5:29.
Not treated: vv.26-27 (the bush, God of the living) per second fetch.

## Mark 12:28-34
Intro: third hard question in one day; God brings good out of evil (Judges 14:14).
1. High standard (vv.28-31): love, not mere outward obedience; unlimited to God, right measure to men; none attains it: Rom. 3:10, 20; need of Christ's atonement.
2. How far one may go and not be a disciple (vv.32-34): scribe's understanding; "not far from the kingdom", yet never said to become a disciple; rich young man; John 12:43; knowledge/education cannot make a Christian; repent, believe, be made new.

## Mark 12:35-44
1. Christ in the OT (vv.35-37): David by the Spirit in the Psalm; John 5:39; Psalms on sufferings and glory; OT as valuable as NT; boastful contempt of it leads to infidelity.
2. Hypocrisy (vv.38-40): robes, greetings, seats, widows' houses, long prayers, greater damnation; among worst sins; Job 20:5; but avoid cowardly silence; consistent life, firm confession.
3. Self-denying liberality (vv.41-44): Christ watched the giving; heart and sacrifice, not amount; Luke 12:21; meagre giving of professors; give while living, no giving after death; freely received, freely give.
Not treated: the two mites' value (v.42 detail).
