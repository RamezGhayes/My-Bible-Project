# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 10 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark10.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Mark 10:1-12
1. Perseverance as teacher (v.1): taught though most hardened; Isa. 32:20; Eccles. 11:6; duty ours, results God's; Matt. 25:21 faithful not successful.
2. Dignity of marriage (v.6-9): lax Jewish opinion, trivial divorce; original standard one man, one woman; words at marriage of Adam and Eve; one flesh; what God joined.
3. (v.10-12) divorce and remarriage, except for unfaithfulness, breaks 7th commandment (exception is Matthew's; a neutral bracket added). Marriage basis of national morality; polygamy and easy divorce foster immorality; nearer Christ's law, higher moral tone.
Advice: marry only in the Lord, after prayer; two sinners not angels; mutual sanctification (Eph. 5:25, 26); Solomon (Neh. 13:26). Lament: few invite Christ to weddings; unhappy marriages.
Not treated in detail: v.2-5.

## Mark 10:13-16
1. Church's duty to children (v.13-14): children form character early, learn sin, receive impressions, have consciences; congregations should train; churches neglecting children criticized.
2. Infant baptism: passage silent on baptism but "strong indirect argument"; passage prominent in Church of England baptismal service; quotes the Articles (baptism of young children to be retained as most agreeable with Christ's institution). Arguments: infants may be elect, washed, regenerate; members of visible church through parents (1 Cor. 7:14); circumcision; households of Lydia, the jailer, Stephanus (Acts 16:15, 33; 1 Cor. 1:16); no age limit given. Acknowledges sincere disagreement; agree to differ. Neutral bracket note added (Anglican view; Baptists differ).
Close: baptism of the Spirit more important; are you born again?
Not treated: v.15.

## Mark 10:17-27
Intro: told by Matthew, Mark, Luke.
1. Self-ignorance (v.17-20): running, kneeling, "observed from my youth"; law's searching nature; professors ignore Matt. 5; Laodicea (Rev. 3:17); pray for self-knowledge, Spirit's conviction (Rom. 7:9); ignorance of law and gospel go together.
2. Christ's love (v.21): pity and compassion; soul a noble ruin; special love for sheep, wide heart for sinners (Matt. 23:37); chief sinners may be told Christ loves them; lost because love darkness (John 3:19), will not come (John 5:40).
3. Love of money (v.22-25): went away grieved; riches dangerous; poor also; trusting wealth; contentment (Phil. 4:11).
Not treated: v.18, 26-27.

## Mark 10:28-34
1. Promise (v.29-30): hundredfold now (spiritual family), with persecutions, eternal life; encourage faint-hearted with future glory.
2. Warning (v.31): Judas (trusted with the bag) fell; Saul under Gamaliel laboured more than all; ancient churches of Asia and Africa decayed, modern England and America; believers who began well and fell; pray for humility and perseverance.
3. Foreknowledge (v.32-34): calm detailed prediction; voluntary death; covenant payment; faithful substitution; small thing to live for Him.
No printed references. Not treated: v.28.

## Mark 10:35-45
1. Ignorance (v.35-39): highest places, "we can"; expect too much present enjoyment, forget cross, misjudge strength, learn humility by failure; Acts 14:22.
2. Lowliness (v.41-44): the ten's indignation shows ambition; greatness = service; against self-esteem, rejoice in others' honour (Phil. 2:3); the Howards, Wilberforces, Martyns, Judsons, mocked yet names in heaven.
3. Ransom (v.45): atoning nature; not ordinary martyrdom; substitutionary payment of debts; full ransom; sure foundation.
Not treated: v.40, 42.

## Mark 10:46-52
1. Strong faith (v.46-47): blind yet saw Son of David; eyes of understanding open; Annas and Caiaphas never saw it; believed on report; 1 Pet. 1:8; faith begins with a venture and continues leaning on His word.
2. Perseverance (v.48): ignore critics (too soon, too late, too much); half-heartedness from not feeling sin.
3. Gratitude (v.52): followed in the way; 2 Cor. 5:14; Rom. 8:14.
Close: eyes opened? else on broad road.
Not treated: v.49-51.
