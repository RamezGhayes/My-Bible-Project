# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 12 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m12.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Three passes: outline, Scripture references and verses treated, details of Sabbath / reformation / neutrality points.

## Matthew 12:1-13
Intro: Sabbath the subject; Pharisees buried it in traditions; Christians still disagree.
1. Christ does not abolish weekly Sabbath: freed it from wrong interpretation and human additions; fourth commandment part of eternal law (decalogue), unlike sacrificial law; "Christian Sabbath" (no Lord's day/Sunday wording on page).
2. Works of real necessity and mercy allowed (v.1-5, 7, 11-12, 13): plucking grain permitted (Deut. 23:25); David, priests; mercy not sacrifice; sheep in pit; worship commands not so rigid as to break charity.
Close: necessity no excuse for idle, profane, irreverent keeping, pleasure-seeking, worldliness; Pharisees burdened it, many Christians profane it.
Not treated: v.6, 8, 9-10.

## Matthew 12:14-21
1. Desperate wickedness of heart (v.14): sinless Christ hated; heart hates God and godly; 1 John 3:13; goodness provokes it; prophets, apostles, martyrs, Huss, Jerome of Prague, Ridley, Latimer.
2. Encouraging description of Christ (v.20): bruised reed, smoking flax = weak faith, repentance, grace; life in infant as in man, fire in spark as in flame; Satan's great wrath and angels' joy at first signs; Job 36:5; Saul praying, Ananias sent (Acts 9:11); Zech. 4:10 day of small things.
Not treated: v.15-19, 21 (Isaiah named without reference).

## Matthew 12:22-37
Intro: hard passage; sin against Holy Spirit never fully explained.
1. Nothing too blasphemous for prejudiced men (v.24-26): when arguments and works can't be answered, slander; Athanasius by heretics, Luther by Romanists; bear patiently; vindicated at last.
2. Impossibility of neutrality (v.30): two parties only; half-hearted feel truth, fear to confess; way of Gamaliel and Erasmus never brought happiness or usefulness.
3. Sinfulness of sins against knowledge (v.31-32): degrees of sin; light; Heb. 6:4-7; 10:26-27; children of godly parents, servants of godly houses, members of churches; wax and clay; Pharaoh, Saul, Ahab, Judas, Julian, Francis Spira: light in head, hatred in heart; blackness of darkness.
4. Carefulness about daily words (v.34, 36-37): words show heart like water its spring; thistle-down; Burkitt: word transient physically, permanent morally; old saint: conversation with friends only made work for repentance; need Christ's righteousness (Phil. 3:9); Prov. 18:21; Col. 4:6; Ps. 39:1; 141:3; Jas. 3:2.
Not treated: v.22-23, 27-29, 33, 35.

## Matthew 12:38-50
Intro: Jonah and queen of the South real history; both Testaments stand together.
1. Amazing power of unbelief (v.38): had seen many miracles; do not want to believe; Isa. 7:9.
2. Danger of partial reformation (v.43-45): Jews (Egypt, idolatry; after Babylon no due return; repentance under John skin-deep; formalism; forty years then Rome, Jerusalem, temple, scattering); churches (revived at Reformation, settled on their lees; unbelief and formal superstition may make way for Antichrist); individuals (reform then relapse, seared, hopeless). Seek full conversion, Spirit indwelling.
3. Tender affection for disciples (v.48-50): precious; mockers persecute King's family; "whoever" encourages weak; Joseph provided for relatives; Cant. 8:8 little sister.
Not treated: v.39-42 (only in intro), v.46-47.
