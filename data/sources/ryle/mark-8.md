# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 8 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark08.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Mark 8:1-13
Intro: second feeding, before ~4000 witnesses, silences skeptics.
1. Compassion (v.2): crowd mostly unconverted, followed from curiosity; general love besides special love; Eph. 3:19; imitate: Matt. 5:44; Rom. 12:20.
2. Nothing impossible (v.4): disciples' doubt; spiritual supply (Col. 1:19), temporal providence (Isa. 33:16); He will not fail those who trust.
3. Sighed over unbelief (v.11-12): grief over others' sin mark of grace; Ps. 119:158; Ezek. 9:4; 2 Pet. 2:8 (Lot); Rom. 9:2 (Paul); self-examination; unbelief still grieves Christ.
Not treated: v.5-8 detail, v.10, 13.

## Mark 8:14-21
1. Leaven (v.15): Matthew's parallel shows doctrine; Pharisees = formalism, self-righteousness; Herod (and courtiers) = worldliness, skepticism; false doctrine inside church worse than persecution; small, subtle, truth abused; Gal. 5:9.
2. Dullness (v.16-17): new converts ignorant, don't expect perfection; line upon line (uncited); Peter and John grew; 1 Cor. 8:2; patience with young believers.
Not treated: v.19-20 basket counts.

## Mark 8:22-26
Intro: unusual means (spittle, touch, led out) unexplained; Lord not bound to means, sovereign.
1. Gradual cure (v.24 "men as trees"): picture of conversion; dim sight at first; clearer by Spirit and experience; humility about own judgment.
2. Present/future (v.25): believers see "in part" (no citation printed), like travellers by night; coming day of perfect sight; patience, watch, work, pray.
No printed references. Not treated: v.26.

## Mark 8:27-33
Intro: talk on the way, use travel time for serious subjects.
1. Opinions (v.27-28): many views, none true; many acknowledge, few know Him as God and own Saviour; Cant. 2:16.
2. Peter's confession (v.29): despite poverty and leaders' opposition; greater than Moses; bold confession though few agree; Christ will confess faithful.
3. "Must" (v.31): not compulsion nor mere example; atonement necessary; central truth of the Bible; rest on atoning death.
4. Peter's rebuke (v.32-33): ignorance, self-conceit, misguided good intention; humility, daily prayer, charity to fallen (Gal. 6:1).
Not treated: v.30.

## Mark 8:34-38
Intro: corrects mistaken ideas of the kingdom; truths for all ages.
1. Self-denial (v.34-35): salvation by grace (Eph. 2:8, 9) yet cross of doctrine and practice; Phil. 4:13; "religion which costs nothing is worth nothing".
2. Value of soul (v.36): lost by love of sin, false religion, neglect of means; no gain compensates; Esau.
3. Ashamed (v.38): shrinking from doctrines, commands, people; Prov. 29:25; pray for courage.
Not treated explicitly: v.37.
