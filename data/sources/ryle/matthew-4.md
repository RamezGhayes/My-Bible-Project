# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 4 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m04.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes.

## Matthew 4:1-11
Intro: deep and mysterious subject, plain practical lessons.
1. v.1 devil real and mighty: attacked the Son of God; sin into world, Job, David, Peter; murderer, liar, roaring lion, angel of light; watch and pray; no jesting; resist the devil as well as flesh and world.
2. v.1 temptation not strange; disciple not above master; hated evil thoughts are not sin, yielding is.
3. v.4, 7, 10 "it is written": Bible the chief weapon; read, pray over, memorize; owning a Bible not enough.
4. Christ sympathizes, Heb. 2:18; four kinds of temptation (distrust of God's care, presumption on mercy, one great secret sin for gain, misuse of Scripture), not matched one-to-one to the three temptations.
Not treated: v.2, v.11; v.5-9 details. Ryle does not say the devil quoted Scripture.

## Matthew 4:12-25
Intro: start of public ministry among a dark people.
1. v.17 began by preaching: Christ, the twelve, Paul to Timothy; churches flourish where preaching is honoured; sacraments and public prayer not above it.
2. v.17 repentance: definition (sorrow, confession, breaking off, hatred of sin); goes with faith; Acts 20:21.
3. v.18-22 humble fishermen; gospel for all; argument for the divine origin of Christianity against emperors and pagan priests.
4. v.23-24 miracles: mercy ("went about doing good"), power, emblems of spiritual healing, compassion; Christ unchanged after 1800 years.
Not treated: v.12-16 (Capernaum, Isaiah prophecy), v.25.
