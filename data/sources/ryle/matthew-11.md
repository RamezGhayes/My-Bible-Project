# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 11 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m11.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Matthew 11:1-15
1. John's message (v.2-3): not from John's doubt or unbelief; for his disciples' benefit, to hear and see Christ's evidence and dispel their jealousy; John sensed prison would end in death; Christ continues ever, unchangeable priesthood (Heb. 7:24), cleave to Him; teachers and parents near the end should point those left behind to Christ.
2. Christ's testimony to John (v.7-11, "more than a prophet", none greater): not a reed, not soft clothing; bold, self-denying, rebuked a king; above OT prophets because he saw Christ and pointed "Behold the Lamb of God".
Close: Christ honors His servants' labor even when they think it wasted.
Not treated: v.1, v.4-6, v.10, v.11b (least in kingdom), v.12-15.

## Matthew 11:16-24
Intro: addressed to Jews of His day, but shows the natural man and the peril of many now.
1. Unreasonableness of unconverted (v.16-19): children in market; John "has a devil", Jesus glutton; professing Christians object to believers' doctrine and life whatever they do; Quesnel: good men never escape the world's censure, best not to be concerned; Rom. 8:7; 1 Cor. 2:14.
2. Wickedness of willful impenitence (v.20-24): Tyre, Sidon, Sodom would have repented; regular hearers who refuse more guilty; unnamed English bishop: no aggravation of sin worse than frequent hearing of duty.
Close: not content with hearing and liking; repent, be converted, lay hold on Christ.
Not treated: v.19b, wording of v.23.

## Matthew 11:25-30
Intro: one of the most important passages in the Gospels; prays for help to see and feel it.
1. Childlike teachable mind (v.25): sovereignty a deep mystery, not for us to explain; proud miss, humble receive; pride great obstacle; Luke 1:53; Acts 9:6 first step; Luke 18:14 often repeated.
2. Greatness and majesty of Christ (v.27): mutual knowledge of Father and Son beyond us; all soul's interests in His hand: bears keys, door, Shepherd, Physician, bread of life, light, fountain, each with a response; 1 Cor. 3:22 having Christ we have all.
3. Breadth of invitation (v.28-30): all weary and burdened (sin, sorrow, anxiety, remorse); come as you are; learn of Him; gentle and lowly: Mary and Martha, Peter after fall, disciples after resurrection, Thomas tasted His meekness; yoke light vs burdens of world, sin, ceremony; 1 John 5:3; Prov. 3:17; ark of refuge like Noah's; John 6:37.
Close: have we come? keep coming daily; cling closer; come today.
Not treated: v.26 directly.
