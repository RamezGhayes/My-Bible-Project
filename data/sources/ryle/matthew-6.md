# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 6 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m06.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes.

## Matthew 6:1-8
Alms and prayer assumed. v.1-2 giving required, giving Saviour giving disciples; v.3-4 no display, left hand; v.5-6 prayerless not Christians, secret prayer, servants and poor ("necessity mother of invention"); God weighs motives, 1 Sam. 2:3.
Not treated: v.8; v.7 only indirectly.

## Matthew 6:9-15
Ten sentences. Our Father: Creator (Acts 17:28), Father of Christ reconciling (Col. 1:20-22), adoption (Rom. 8:15); vain without faith in Christ. Hallowed: John 12:28; 1 Pet. 4:11. Kingdom: grace now, glory at second coming, Jews converted and fullness of Gentiles (Rom. 11:25). Will: as angels; highest charity. Bread: manna, all bodily needs. Debts: habit of confession, John 13:10. As we forgive: only "profession", only part Christ comments on (v.14-15). Temptation: 1 Cor. 10:13. Evil: world, heart, devil, John 17:15. Doxology: praise (nothing on textual question). Close: lips and heart together.

## Matthew 6:16-24
Three subjects. v.16-18 fasting: David, Daniel, Esther, Paul and Barnabas; no direct NT command, left to discretion; do not judge others; quietly. v.17 cheerfulness, gloom no sign of godliness. v.19-21 worldliness kills tens of thousands. v.22-24 single eye; ark and Dagon; Phil. 3:13; decision the secret of happiness.
Not treated: v.19 details, v.23.

## Matthew 6:25-34
v.25 prudent provision right, corroding anxiety wrong; God who gave life and body will supply. v.26 birds; dutiful man not left in want; life in God's hand, we shall not die until our work is done. v.28-29 lilies, immortal souls. v.32 heathen vs Christian. v.33 Rom. 8:28; Ps. 84:11. v.34 today's business, grace for the cross; Ps. 37:25.
Not treated: v.30-31 separately.
