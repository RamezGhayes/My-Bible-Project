# Ryle, Expository Thoughts: Mark 4 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark04.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27).

## Mark 4:1-20 Sower
Intro: best known parable, universal and lasting, as long as congregations exist; an ancient writer: needs application not exposition.
1. Wayside (v.15): attend from form/respectability, no attention, wandering minds; Satan snatches; multitudes, many die so.
2. Stony (v.16-17): temporary feeling, joy, tears, warm talk, no root; Jonah's gourd; morning cloud, early dew (Hos. 6:4); fall at affliction; deceived by feelings.
3. Thorny (v.18-19): assent, approve, partly obey, stop short; cares, deceitfulness of riches, other lusts; most melancholy; friend of world enemy of God (James 4:4); need decided change.
4. Good ground (v.20): receive into heart and obey; fruit visible: sin hated, Christ loved, humility, spiritual-mindedness, patience, meekness, charity. Measures differ (30/60/100), kind same: repentance, faith, holiness.
Close: three ways to hear in vain, one right; fruit the only sure mark; fruitless hearer on road to hell.
NOT treated: v.10-13 (mystery, seeing not perceiving).

## Mark 4:21-25 (printed block 4:21-24)
Intro: proverbial sayings stick when sermon forgotten.
1. Impart light (v.21-22): candle on candlestick; light for others too; account at judgment, buried talent; abandon idea that only clergy teach religion; neighbours sharing a plague remedy; 1 Pet. 4:10.
2. Hearing (v.23-24): "if any man have ears" and "take heed what ye hear"; hearing a chief channel of grace (Rom. 10:17; Eph. 1:13; 1 Thess. 5:20; 2 Tim. 4:2); against undervaluing preaching for liturgy and sacraments, yet each means in proportion.
3. Diligence (v.24-25): to him that hath more given; grace God's gift but growth tied to diligence (Prov. 13:4; 19:15); Bible, private prayer, sermons, Lord's Supper; muscle by exercise; measure we mete.

## Mark 4:26-29 Seed growing secretly (only in Mark)
Intro: history of grace in a soul; don't press details (sleeping, rising); main point resemblance to husbandry.
1. Sower needed: earth yields weeds unless sown; heart barren and dead, can't quicken itself; Son of Man by Spirit, using ministers; grace from outside; despising preaching = expecting harvest unsown.
2. Beyond man's grasp (v.27): farmer can't explain growth; God gives increase (1 Cor. 3:7); why word moves one not another; John 3:8; humbles ministers, relieves anxiety: sow and leave it.
3. Gradual (v.28): nature does nothing at a bound; blade, ear, full corn; day of small things; weakest believer alive from the dead; Eccles. 9:4; don't despise weak grace or doubt for lack of Paul's faith.
4. Harvest when ripe (v.29): God takes His people when ready; Josiah, James brother of John, Edward VI died young; at resurrection a "needs-be" seen; nothing by chance, God's husbandry.

## Mark 4:30-34 Mustard seed
Intro: history and prophecy of the visible church from first advent to judgment (prev. parable = grace in heart).
1. Small at first: mustard seed proverbial (Matt. 17:20; Luke 17:6); familiar language wise (cf. v.33); manger, fishermen and publicans, crucified between thieves, forsaken, betrayed, denied; stumbling block and foolishness.
2. Growth: mustard large in east per travellers; Pentecost 3000, then 5000; Antioch, Ephesus, Philippi, Corinth, Rome; Europe, Asia Minor, N. Africa, creed of the empire; corruption acknowledged; Voltaire and Paine wrong; new branches last 50 years in America, India, Australia, Africa, China, South Seas; Hab. 2:14; Dan. 4:11; expects Satan bound, heathen Christ's inheritance.
Application: despise not day of small things (Zech. 4:10): one child a school, one conversion a church, one word an enterprise.
Not treated: v.34 private explanation.

## Mark 4:35-41 Storm
Intro: four fishermen knew storms; miracle struck them.
1. Service no exemption from storms (v.37): in path of duty yet tempest; pardon, grace, glory promised, not freedom from trouble; affliction teaches emptiness, drives to throne of grace, purifies, weans from world, makes long for heaven; will thank God for every storm.
2. Truly man (v.38): asleep on pillow after preaching all day; Heb. 4:15; knows weariness, pain.
3. Almighty as God (v.39): "Peace, be still" words of Creator; tames passions, tempers, consciences; with Christ we are safe.
4. Patient and tender (v.40): little faith, much fear, "carest thou not"; no sharp rebuke, gentle questions; Ps. 103:13; not dealt with after our sins.
Close: Christ unchanged; be patient with brethren, hopeful about ourselves; question is whether we really come, repent, believe.
Not treated: other little ships (v.36), fear after miracle (v.41).
