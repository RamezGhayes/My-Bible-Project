# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Matthew 7 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/m07.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes.

## Matthew 7:1-11
v.1 often misapplied: does not forbid unfavourable judgment in every case, decided opinions, testing spirits, reproof before we are faultless; Job 9:24. Forbids censoriousness (Pharisees); 1 Cor. 13:7. v.6 discretion: Prov. 9:8; Acts 18:6; Nabal 1 Sam. 25:17; most err by over-caution; self-examination. v.7-11 ask/seek/knock; argument from earthly parents; use the truth; secret prayer neglected; no excuse; persevere, the promise never failed.
Not treated: v.2, v.3-5 imagery.

## Matthew 7:12-20
Beginning of the close. v.12 golden rule, settles doubtful questions. v.13-14 do not follow the crowd; popular religion not saving; do not be discouraged at being few. v.15 false teachers in the church; test by Scripture, not by ordination ("clergymen may err as much as laymen"). v.20 sound doctrine and holy living; Ps. 119:105; neglect of Scripture and sloth cloaked as humility; blind lead the blind (no ref).
Not treated: v.16-19 imagery.

## Matthew 7:21-29
v.21-23 outward profession useless (baptism, privileges, knowledge, preaching, wonders) without obedience, repentance, faith, holiness; judge ourselves. v.24-27 two builders; James 1:22; what costs little is worth little. v.28-29 no such sermon before or since; John 12:48.
Not treated: v.29 (authority vs scribes).
