## Mark 16:1-8
1. Strong love (vv.1-2): early visit with spices despite weak faith; Cant. 8:6, 7; low sense of sin, low value of salvation; Luke 7:47.
2. Feared difficulties disappear (vv.3-4): the stone; believers dreading future trials; "the lion in the way is chained"; practical faith.
3. Friends of Christ need not fear angels (vv.5-6): angels at judgment gather elect, separate tares, gather wheat; live so as to be carried by angels to Abraham's bosom (Lazarus).
4. Kindness to backsliders (v.7): "and Peter"; no exceptions in grace; Micah 7:18; be generous in mercy, quick to forgive.
Not treated: v.8 (women's fear and silence).

## Mark 16:9-14
1. Abundant proof of resurrection (vv.9, 12, 14): three occasions; other NT appearances; Rom. 4:25 (atonement accepted); 1 Peter 1:3; witnesses Jew, Gentile, priests, Roman guard, women, disciples; denial needs more credulity than belief.
2. Kindness to Mary Magdalene (v.9): first, though His mother and John alive; once possessed by seven devils; Christ honours those who honour Him (last at the cross, first at the grave); penitents fully forgiven; Augustine, John Newton; 2 Cor. 5:17.
3. Weak faith of the best (vv.11, 13, 14): three instances of unbelief though told repeatedly; God brings good, their slow conviction confirms resurrection; don't be surprised at own doubts, don't expect perfect faith in others, resist unbelief; have we risen spiritually? Rom. 6:13; knowledge alone does not save.
Not treated: v.10 detail.

## Mark 16:15-18
1. Commission (v.15): whole world needs gospel; offer to every creature freely, no warrant to restrict to the elect; Rev. 22:17; strongest argument for missions home and abroad; mockers show ignorance.
2. Terms (v.16): baptism "an ordinance generally necessary to salvation, where it can be had"; not to be despised; water itself conveys no grace; many baptized never washed in Christ's blood; used reverently, intelligently, prayerfully, accompanied by special blessing, Christ seals it; faith absolutely necessary; assent and membership useless without it; unbelievers condemned, eternal hell; Deut. 32:29.
3. Promises of special help (vv.17-18): signs quoted but not discussed singly; most fulfilled in Acts; age of (physical) miracles long passed, meant for the Church's establishment; constant miracles would cease to be miracles; special help still given (Isa. 59:19); age of spiritual miracles not past: conversion and perseverance.

## Mark 16:19-20
1. Ascension and session (v.19): carries on saving work, intercedes; Heb. 7:25; wakes when we sleep, never weary, almighty, lives for ever; Forerunner preparing a place (John 14:2); Rom. 8:34.
2. Blessing on faithful workers (v.20): "worked with them", confirmed the word; growth through persecution in Acts and church history; no faithful work lost; 1 Cor. 15:58; Ps. 126:6.
Closing self-examination: Christ dwelling in hearts by faith, Spirit witnessing with our spirit (unreferenced); 1 John 5:12.
