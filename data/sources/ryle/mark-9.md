# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 9 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark09.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Mark 9:1-13
Intro: links to ch. 8 (death, self-denial); promise of seeing glory (v.1) fulfilled in transfiguration.
1. Vision of glory of Christ and His people at second coming (v.2-4): majesty, saints (Moses, Elijah) with Him, believers share honour; to the tempted and lonely; Col. 3:4.
2. Peter's "good to be here" (v.5-6): blameworthy (ignorance of suffering, forgetful of others, low view in three tabernacles), yet joy of glory; all redeemed will say it at His return.
3. Testimony to Messiahship (v.4, 7-8): Moses = law, Elijah = prophets, witness then leave Jesus alone; Father's voice + "Hear Him"; far above prophets.
Close: hear Christ as Teacher, Light, Head; not men, councils, church; John 10:27, 28.
Not treated: v.9-13.

## Mark 9:14-29
Intro: mountain of glory vs valley of conflict; conflict the rule, glory the exception.
1. Dependence (v.14-18): disciples failed; John 15:5; Cranmer and Jewell's temporary recantations; Samson shorn; daily humility.
2. "From childhood" (v.21): Satan begins early; parents teach and pray from earliest years.
3. "I believe; help my unbelief" (v.24): mixture in every believer; use weak faith, resist unbelief in prayer; known by inward conflict as well as peace.
4. Authority over Satan (v.25): spirit must obey; great chain prepared (Rev. 20:1); Rom. 16:20.
Not treated: v.19, 22-23, 26-29 (no mention of prayer/fasting anywhere on page).

## Mark 9:30-37
1. Death announced again (v.31-32): dull because expecting earthly kingdom; death central purpose; satisfaction for sin, God just and justifier; cross the focus.
2. Pride (v.33-34): commonest; from Eden (Adam and Eve); disguises as humility; hinders repentance, love; soul-ruining; humility rarest grace.
3. Greatness (v.35): last and servant of all; world rules, Christian serves; practical help.
4. Child (v.36-37): receiving Christ and Father; encouragement to workers among outcasts, fallen, neglected children; Christ notices and honours.
No printed references. Not treated: v.30 secrecy, Capernaum house.

## Mark 9:38-50
1. Toleration (v.38-40): sectarian narrowness; Num. 11:28 (Eldad and Medad), 11:29; Phil. 1:18; no persecution among Christians; rejoice whoever destroys Satan's works.
2. Hand, foot, eye (v.43, 45, 47): members instruments of temptation; Job 31:1; 1 Cor. 9:27.
3. Hell (v.43-48): mentioned three times; worm, fire; real, eternal; John in Revelation (uncited); warn others (2 Cor. 5:11) and point to mercy.
Close: "salt in yourselves" (v.50) = saving grace of the Spirit, daily watchfulness; peace, humility, love all who love Christ.
Not treated: v.41, 42, 49; v.44, 46 not in page's printed text.
