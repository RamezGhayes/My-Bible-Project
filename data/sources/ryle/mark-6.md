# Ryle, Expository Thoughts on the Gospels: Mark 6 (reading notes, own words)

Read: https://gracegems.org/Ryle/mark06.htm (WebFetch, 2026-11-27). Two passes: outline, then Scripture references and verses treated.

## Mark 6:1-6
Intro: Nazareth shows wickedness of the heart.
1. Familiarity breeds contempt (v.1-4): thirty years of Christ's presence; England's Bible and ordinances; comfort to ministers and Christians standing alone at home.
2. "The carpenter" (v.3, only in Mark): manual labour, sweat of face; poverty no sin; gambling, drunkenness, covetousness, lying disgrace; carpenter's shop rebukes idolizers of riches.
3. Unbelief (v.5-6): oldest (Eve believed devil), most ruinous (Israel 40 years out of Canaan), foolish, common; root unwillingness not lack of evidence (Paine, Voltaire); "he that believes not shall be damned" (uncited); "Lord, increase our faith".
No printed references.

## Mark 6:7-13
Intro: apostles tested before He left them.
1. Two by two (v.7): Eccles. 4:9, 10; Heb. 10:24, 25; iron sharpens iron (uncited); Paul seldom alone in Acts; missions would have done better; workers should meet and counsel.
2. Rejecting the Gospel (v.10-11): Sodom and Gomorrah more tolerable; morality does not save; what are you doing with the Gospel?
3. Repentance (v.12): simple and deep; tied to views of God, sin, Christ, holiness; all need it; ministers press it always; have you repented?
Not treated: v.8-9 (equipment), v.13 (demons, oil).

## Mark 6:14-29
Intro: Mark tells John's death most fully.
1. Truth's power on conscience (v.20): Herod feared John; Felix trembled, Agrippa almost persuaded; ministers' seed may fruit after the sower is gone.
2. One darling sin ruins (v.20): heard gladly, did many things, kept Herodias; cut off right hand (uncited); Ps. 119:128.
3. Bold rebuke (v.18): public and private, wisdom and love, results to God; Prov. 28:23.
4. Hatred of reprover (v.19): Elijah "troubler of Israel", Micaiah hated by Ahab; woe when all speak well (uncited).
5. Sin from feasting (v.21-28): birthday feast, dancing, rash promise, murder; warns against late hours, crowded entertainments, dancing.
6. Little earthly reward (v.27-28): Stephen, James; Rom. 8:18; 2 Cor. 4:17; heaven makes amends.
Not treated: v.14-16, 23, 29.

## Mark 6:30-34
1. Apostles told Him all (v.30): prayer main secret of success; Ezek. 37:9; Acts 6:4; praying ministry as well as preaching ministry.
2. Rest (v.31): care for bodies and souls; treasure in earthen vessels (uncited); withdraw for self-examination, health; Cant. 1:6.
3. Compassion (v.34): general love for all mankind, not only His sheep; same yesterday, today, forever (uncited); scribes/Pharisees blind guides; Jew, Mohammedan, Roman Catholic, heathen; missions; who cares not for others' souls likely knows not Christ.
Not treated: v.32-33.

## Mark 6:35-46
Intro: told by all four Evangelists.
1. Almighty power (v.38-44): real creative act, not illusion; manna; spread a table in wilderness (uncited); look to Jesus in need, He lives and is unchanged.
2. Prayer after the miracle (v.46): pray before and after work; enter your closet; Col. 4:12; James 4:2 (church does little because it asks little); "more in their closets".
Not treated: v.35-37, 39-40, 45.

## Mark 6:47-56
Intro: boat = believers between first and second coming.
1. Christ sees (v.47-48): toiling in rowing; came in due time; wait patiently.
2. Fear of a ghost (v.49-51): instinctive dread of unseen world; ghost stories and superstition testify to latent belief; antidote faith in and communion with unseen Saviour; "God manifest in the flesh" (uncited).
3. Bring the sick (v.53-56): bring sin-sick souls to Christ; souls dying, time short, night comes (uncited); all who touched were made whole.
Not treated: v.52. No printed references.
