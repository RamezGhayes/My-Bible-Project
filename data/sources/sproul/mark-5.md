# R. C. Sproul on Mark 5: reading notes

Read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch (full transcripts). Sermons found through the related-sermon links on each sermon page. Entries at most 3 Arabic sentences (owner rule for copyrighted authors). Outline in our own words.

## mark-gadarene-demoniac: "The Gadarene Demoniac", 2006-03-26, Mark 5:1-8 (reads 1-20)
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-gadarene-demoniac
- 1-5: unclean four ways (demons, tombs, Gentile land, pigs); worse than a leper; "subdue" is the Greek word used for taming wild beasts (word not named); compares with Job.
- 6-8: the cry may be the demons'; Matthew's "before the time" is the key; chronos vs kairos; reveals who Jesus is, like the storm.

## mark-a-fearful-deliverance: "A Fearful Deliverance", 2006-04-02, Mark 5:9-20
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-a-fearful-deliverance
- 9-10: Legion from the Roman army in the Decapolis, 5,600 soldiers, used for an innumerable host; text is about Christ's person, not a psychological message of peace.
- 11-13: authority over chaos, as at the storm; second Adam's dominion over animals; compassion for one man outweighs the pigs.
- 14-17: holiness among the unholy produces dread; Peter at the catch of fish.
- 18-20: sent home to tell of the Lord's mercy; proclaimed in the Decapolis.

## mark-jairus-daughter: "Jairus' Daughter", 2006-04-23, Mark 5:21-43
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-jairus-daughter
- 21-24: synagogue ruler a layman, not a rabbi; daughter at death's door (summary named Greek eschatos, not confirmed in quote check, so not used).
- 25-29: Lev 15; unclean like a leper; impoverished by physicians.
- 30-34: in his human nature not omniscient; Jesus the efficient cause, faith the instrumental cause; same in justification.
- 35-43: sleep a euphemism for death; command not to publicize because people would not understand; give her food.
