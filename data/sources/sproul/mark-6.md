# R. C. Sproul on Mark 6: reading notes

Read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch (full transcripts). Sermons found through the related-sermon links on each sermon page. Entries at most 3 Arabic sentences (owner rule for copyrighted authors). Outline in our own words.

## mark-jesus-nazareth: "Jesus at Nazareth", 2006-04-30, Mark 6:1-6
- tekton carpenter or stonemason; "son of Mary" implies they thought him illegitimate; real brothers and sisters, cousin view needs theological bias; skandalizo, rejected stone (Ps 118:22; 1 Pet 2:7-8).
- prophet without honor an old adage; no mighty work: not loss of power but God's judgment on the town; unbelief because the Spirit had not regenerated them.
- Not used: Muhammad Ali and personal homecoming illustrations.

## mark-sending-disciples: "The Sending of the Disciples", 2006-05-14, Mark 6:7-13
- called in order to send; apostolos = sent one; two by two as two witnesses; miracles authenticate agents of revelation (Moses).
- travel light, depend on the Father; hosts provide; one tunic = lodged in homes.
- no indifference to Christ; dust-shaking from Jews returning from pagan lands; degrees of judgment, worse than Sodom; preached repentance like John and Jesus. Textual variant in v11 noted, not discussed.

## mark-beheading-john-baptist-part-1: "The Beheading of John the Baptist (Part 1)", 2006-05-21, Mark 6:14-18
- Herod Antipas, son of Herod the Great, tetrarch; "king" a popular label; guilty conscience, resurrection means judgment; Elijah.
- marriage to Herodias, brother Philip's wife; law forbade while brother alive; cost of rebuking sin in high places; parallel with Jesus' death.

## mark-beheading-john-baptist-part-2: "The Beheading of John the Baptist (Part 2)", 2006-05-28, Mark 6:19-29
- conscience the greatest restraint on evil but can be silenced; holiness repels and fascinates.
- Belshazzar's feast (Dan 5); Salome from Josephus; erotic dance; half the kingdom a conventional phrase.
- unlawful vow should not be kept; public oath; barbarism; disciples bury John. (Transcript once says "Herod Agrippa" for the vow; not used.)

## mark-feeding-five-thousand: "Feeding of the Five Thousand", 2006-06-04, Mark 6:30-44
- compassion, shepherd-king prophecies (no refs); pastor's first office to feed the sheep.
- "you give them": do what you can, then watch God work; Moses and "Has the arm of the Lord waxed short?".
- contrast with Herod's feast; foreshadows Lord's Supper; baskets: all satisfied; rejects hoax and shared-lunch theories.

## mark-jesus-walking-water: "Jesus Walking on Water", 2006-06-11, Mark 6:45-56
- dismissal: speculative, to stop political-messiah fervor; prayer alone at crisis.
- rejects fog, illusion, sandbar; "pass by" = theophany (Ex 33; 1 Kgs 19); ego eimi echoes the divine name; fourth watch 3-6 a.m.
- hardened hearts like Pharaoh; only the Spirit changes a heart of stone.
- Gennesaret: touched the hem and were healed; awe in the Lord's presence.
