# R. C. Sproul on Mark 4: reading notes

Read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch (full transcripts). Sermons found through the related-sermon links on each sermon page. Entries at most 3 Arabic sentences (owner rule for copyrighted authors). Outline in our own words.

## mark-parable-sower: "The Parable of the Sower", 2006-02-05, Mark 4:1-20
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-parable-sower
- v3: "Listen" imperative; the obey-word builds on the verb "to hear" with a prefix (Sproul calls the prefix "hyper"; actually hypo-, so the prefix is not named in our entry): obedience is hearing that reaches the heart.
- v3-8: God the sower, the Word the seed; sowing before plowing; the Word does not return void (no reference given); harvest shows God's sovereignty.
- v15: birds = Satan.
- v16-19: spurious conversion; no one justified by a profession of faith; rejects "carnal Christian" teaching.
- v20: good soil made good by the Spirit's immediate work; regeneration precedes faith (John 6; Ephesians, the "gift of God" line = Eph 2:8).
- Not used: altar-call illustration and friend's story.
- 4:10-13 read but not expounded: not covered.

## mark-parables-kingdom: "Parables of the Kingdom", 2006-02-26, Mark 4:21-33
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-parables-kingdom
- 21-23: the lamp is Jesus; church must not hide him (entertainment, pop psychology); hidden things revealed on the last day.
- 24-25: measure = the basket used to cover the lamp (wordplay); talents.
- 26-29: kingdom grows unseen; Paul: God gives the increase; comfort for discouraged ministers.
- 30-32: "smallest" a common Jewish saying for the superlatively small, not an error; greatness of God's providence, not of the seed.
- 33-34 read only: not covered. Personal anecdotes not used.

## mark-calming-sea: "Calming the Sea", 2006-03-12, Mark 4:35-41
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-calming-sea
- 35-38: evening crossing; basin produces sudden storms; Greek mega (storm, calm, fear); Jesus asleep; disciples rebuke the Creator.
- 39: Creator commands nature, like the command for light at creation.
- 40-41: the great fear came after the storm ended; caused by Christ's holiness, not only his power.
