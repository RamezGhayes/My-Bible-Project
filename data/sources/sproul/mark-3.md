## mark-lord-sabbath@3: "Lord of the Sabbath", 2006-01-08, Mark 2:23-3:6 (3:1-6 part)
See data/sources/sproul/mark-2.md. 3:1-4: withered hand not life-threatening; man asked to risk himself; doing good lawful any day; irony since they plotted his death. 3:5-6: anger mixed with grief at hardness; plotting to kill the Lord of the Sabbath the worst Sabbath breach; do not harden the heart nor feel superior to the Pharisees.

## mark-calling-disciples: "The Calling of the Disciples", 2006-01-15, Mark 3:7-19
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-calling-disciples
- 7-12: body and soul both matter (resurrection of the body), soul first; demons try to gain power by naming him; silencing foreshadows final silencing of evil.
- 13: Jesus chose, contrary to rabbinic custom; ekklesia = called-out ones, outward and inward call; salvation individual and corporate.
- 14-15: verb mainly "make/create", same as LXX Gen 1:1 (Greek word not named); twelve tribes; "be with him"; union with Christ (eis / en).
- 16-19: names in groups of three; Judas last, linked to the Lord's Supper (1 Cor 11).
- Not used: remarks on how ministry is funded (tithe).

## mark-unpardonable-sin: "The Unpardonable Sin", 2006-01-22, Mark 3:20-30
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-unpardonable-sin
- 20-21: his own people, likely family; Festus on Paul (Acts 26:24).
- 22: Beelzebub / Beelzebul; Satan does no genuine miracles; scribes admitted miracles were real but ascribed them to Satan.
- 23-27: Jesus binds the strong man through his ministry.
- 28-30: amen; murder and adultery forgivable (David); blasphemy against the Son forgivable (cross prayer); unpardonable sin = knowingly calling Christ's Spirit-wrought work devilish after knowing the truth (Heb 10:26-29, also Heb 6); Christ will never let his people commit it; worry is evidence one has not; probably only demons commit it.
