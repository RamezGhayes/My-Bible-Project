# R. C. Sproul on Mark 2: reading notes

Read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch (full transcripts, title, date and range in page header). Index: https://learn.ligonier.org/scripture/mark-2 (three sermons). Owner rule of 27 Nov: Sproul is copyrighted, so entries are at most 3 Arabic sentences giving his main idea; illustrations and sermon order are left out. Outline below in our own words.

## mark-healing-paralytic: "Healing the Paralytic", 2005-12-18, Mark 2:1-12
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-healing-paralytic
- v5: man sought healing, Jesus addressed deeper need; "son" as speech of a superior. Suffering not equated with personal guilt.
- v6-7: only God forgives (not even Messiah); scribes understood a claim to deity.
- v8-9: "which is easier": Jesus took the hard way, since declaring forgiveness claimed divinity.
- v10: Son of Man, Daniel 7 heavenly judge receiving the kingdom; Jesus' most frequent self-designation (83 NT uses, verified).
- v11-12: forgiveness the greater gift.
- Not used (illustrations): roof construction, Luke's tiles, absolution and Luther, psychiatrist story.

## mark-new-wine-skins: "New Wine Skins", 2006-01-01, Mark 2:13-22
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-new-wine-skins
- v14: Levi (Matthew); tax farming corrupt; collectors outcasts; scandal like touching the leper.
- v15-17: Pharisees = separated ones; holiness by separation rejected; minister in the world without loving it; Pharisees the sickest.
- v18-20: law required fasting only on Day of Atonement; OT never calls Messiah bridegroom (God is groom, Israel bride); claim to be Son of God; hint of his death.
- v21-22: targets Pharisaic traditions, not OT law; real newness in Christ.

## mark-lord-sabbath: "Lord of the Sabbath", 2006-01-08, Mark 2:23-3:6
https://learn.ligonier.org/sermons/mark-lord-sabbath
Registered twice: `mark-lord-sabbath` (2:23-28) and `mark-lord-sabbath@3` (3:1-6).
- 2:23-24: rabbinic additions (Sabbath day's journey, plucking as reaping) bound consciences.
- 2:25-26: David precedent; "have you never read" a rebuke; Abiathar the leading high priest of the era, so no error.
- 2:27-28: Sabbath a gift; tradition made it a burden; Sabbath from creation; Jesus as Creator Word is its Lord: claim to deity.
- 3:1-6: man asked to risk himself; withered hand not life-threatening; doing good lawful; anger with grief at hardened hearts; plot with Herodians; warning not to harden our hearts nor feel superior to the Pharisees.
