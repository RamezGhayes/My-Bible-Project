# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 4
Read 2026-11-27 in the monergism PDF https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/OneCorinthiansCharlesHodge.pdf (WebFetch portions 100000-300000). Section title from his table of contents.
1cor-4 (4:1-21): hyperetes, oikonomoi; mysteries = revealed truths not sacraments, against Romish sacramental orders; not originators like Aristotle, Plato; fidelity to Christ and people; human day vs day of the Lord; conscience partial judge; v4 not about justification; until he come = second advent; church judges conduct (Titus 3:10) not heart; praise (2 Tim 4:8; Matt 25). v6 metaschematismos; what is written = OT, Jer 9:23-24; v7 every gift ground of gratitude, "by the grace of God I am what I am"; v8 irony like prophets against idols; Calvin on passing from repressing to mocking pride; v9 condemned (1 Cor 15:30-31; 2 Cor 1:8-9); v12 declined support (9:15); 1 Pet 2:23; rejects sacrificial-victim allusion of perikatharma. v14 noutheteo; v15 paidagogoi; Spirit efficient, ministers administrative, word instrument (Gal 2:8); v16 imitate self-denial not partisanship (11:1; 1 Thess 1:6; Eph 5:1); v18 false teachers (2 Cor 10:10); v19 dynamis = reality and efficacy (1 Thess 1:5; 2 Tim 3:5); v20 kingdom inward (Luke 17:21; Rom 14:17); v21 spirit of meekness = Holy Spirit; apostolic discipline, ch. 5.
