## 2:1-16 (outline in our own words)
- Analysis: principles of judgment applying to all, then applied to Jews (17-29). Five principles: condemning others condemns self; judgment by truth; goodness no exemption; by works; by light received. dio linked to 1:32.
- v1: general form, Jew in view; krinon = condemning; en ho = wherein.
- v2: kata aletheian with judgment (Prov 29:14; John 8:16); Jews hoped for judgment by covenant relation.
- v3: ekpheuxe; Bengel fleeing vs escaping; emphasis "thou".
- v4: chrestotes, anoche, makrothymia; kataphroneis; agnoon; agei leads; Matt 3:9; John 8:33; will involved but grace makes willing (Bengel "God leads the one willing to be led").
- v5: sklerotes, ametanoetos (Fritzsche against Grotius), thesaurizeis; en hemera = on the day (De Wette, Meyer); text note on kai.
- v6: apodosei; judgment by works not relations; reconciled with justification: works evidence and measure, here legal principle not method of salvation (2 Cor 5:10; Eph 6:8); Calvin, Bernard.
- v7: kath' hypomonen ergou agathou; accusatives depend on "seek" (against Bengel, Fritzsche apposition, Luther transposition); glory, honor, immortality glossed.
- v8: eritheia from erithos hireling (Rueckert, Tholuck, Fritzsche) = factious selfish opposition (Phil 1:16; 2:3; Jas 3:14); thymos vs orge; nominative needs verb; God not author of death (Rom 6:23).
- v9: thlipsis/stenochoria; psyche = person; proton = order or preeminence (prefers preeminence of responsibility).
- v10: glory, honor, peace = eternal life, Jew and Gentile.
- v11: prosopolepsia, Hebrew nasa panim (Deut 10:17; Eph 6:9; Jas 2:1).
- v12: anomos; apolountai; kai parallel (against causal readings); punishment proportioned (Luke 12:47-48); heathen still perish (Calvin).
- v13: akroatai (law read aloud); dikaioi/dikaiothesontai forensic; no one does it fully (Jas 1:22).
- v14: linked to v12 not as proof some Gentiles justified (Calvin); ta tou nomou poiein = do what law prescribes (not fulfill, against 3:9-20; not Beza); physei with "do" (against Bengel, Rueckert).
- v15: ergon tou nomou = the law's work collectively; syneidesis with joint force; metaxy = alternately accusing/excusing.
- v16: four connections, Hodge joins to v12 (present v15 vs future krinei); "my gospel" = gospel's announcement of judgment, not its standard; through Christ = Father's authority by the Son (Acts 17:31; John 5:22).
- Doctrine 1-10 and Remarks 1-7 (justice, privilege no refuge, goodness aims at repentance, abuse aggravates, only truly good saved, degrees, heathen judged by light, moral sense innate, Christ judge therefore divine; heart deceives in self-judgment, goodness softens or hardens, evangelical vs legal repentance, doctrine producing security in sin is false, need Christ's righteousness).

## 2:17-29
- Analysis: 17-24 principles applied to Jews (relation to God, knowledge, circumcision); 25-29 nature of circumcision.
- v17: ei de over ide (Bengel, Griesbach, Lachmann); anacoluthon, apodosis in v21; eponomaze = "art called" (against Theodoret, Bengel, Meyer); epanapaue = rest on whole Mosaic system; kauchasai right vs wrong (Mic 3:11).
- v18: diapheronta = more excellent (Phil 1:10) chosen over "things that differ"; katechoumenos orally taught.
- v19-20: guide, light, instructor, teacher; morphosis (2 Tim 3:5) not mere appearance but expression of truth (Grotius); gnosis vs aletheia (De Wette).
- v21-22: apodosis; interrogative; hierosyleis not literal temple robbing but sacrilege/irreverence toward God (Mal 3:8).
- v23-24: Isa 52:5 LXX; Ezek 36:20-23; principle applies.
- v25: gar with unstated thought; rabbinic claims circumcision saves from hell (Eisenmenger, Schoettgen); two views of circumcision: magical (rejected, Gal 6:15) vs sign and seal (value only with obedience); gegonen.
- v26: oun; dikaiomata; hypothetical not actual (Rom 3:10); logisthesetai eis.
- v27: punctuation options (Calvin, Lachmann, Fritzsche vs Meyer); ek physeos with akrobystia; dia = "with" (against Beza "through"); krinei = condemn (Matt 12:41).
- v28: phaneros external; ellipsis filled.
- v29: kryptos (1 Pet 3:4); en pneumati ou grammati: three views, Hodge: Holy Spirit as agent; hou masculine refers to Jew; Deut 10:16; 30:6; Jer 4:4; Col 2:11-12; circumcision and baptism same import.
- Doctrine 1-8 and Remarks 1-6 (visible church membership not salvation, knowledge alone does not sanctify, superior knowledge raises guilt, sins of professors most offensive, sacrament as sign and seal, vain trust in sacraments, religion of heart; Christians as Christ's epistles, decline breeds ritualism, neither over- nor under-value rites, praise of God over men).
