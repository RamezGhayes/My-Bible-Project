# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, 2 Corinthians 4
Read 2026-11-27 at https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (characters 200000-300000 of the text, covering 4:2-5:9).
2cor-4-3-6 (3-6): hidden to the lost, cause in them not the gospel; sun analogy; 1 Cor 1:18; rejection proof of moral depravity, no "good infidel"; god of this world = Satan, real control without their knowledge (2 Tim 2:26; 1 Cor 10:20); not eternal (against Manichean/Arian use); blindness precedes unbelief (Rom 1:21-28); photismos brightness emitted; image of God Heb 1:3; Col 1:15; 2:9; v5 Christ Jesus the Lord = Messiah of Moses and prophets; moral reform "raising fruit without trees"; v6 with Calvin against Luther: light in believers' hearts; twofold illumination, word and Spirit (1 Cor 2:14); Genesis creation; John 1:18; Matt 11:27; 1 John 2:23; "If Christ is God, to know him, is to know God."
2cor-4-7-12 (7-12): treasure = gospel ministry; earthen vessels = weak perishing men; disproportion of means and effects; combat image; aporeo/exaporeo; nekrosis violent death, Calvin's mortification reading noted; Rom 8:36; 1 Cor 15:31; 4:9; 2 Cor 11:23; Gal 6:17; life of Jesus includes resurrection; Gal 2:20; Rom 8:17; John 14:19; mortal flesh = weakness; rejects ironic reading of v12.
2cor-4-13-18 (13-18): spirit of faith = Holy Spirit (Rom 8:15; Eph 1:17); LXX Ps 116:10; rejects Calvin on "same"; bodily resurrection, rejects Beza's figurative reading; paristemi not judgment seat; 1 Cor 15:21-22; Eph 2:6; Col 3:3-4; Phil 3:21; 1 Thess 4:14; v15 construction, 1 Cor 3:21; v16 wasted away, inward man higher spiritual nature (Rom 7:22; Eph 3:16), day by day Hebraism; v17 real pain, momentary relative; glory/weight Hebrew link; Matt 5:12; Rom 8:17; 2 Tim 2:12; 1 Pet 1:6; v18 skopeo from skopos goal (Phil 3:14), conditional participle.
