# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 16
Read 2026-11-27 in https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/OneCorinthiansCharlesHodge.pdf (WebFetch portions 800000-913265). Section titles from his chapter overview.
1cor-16-1-9 (Concerning the Collection for the Saints at Jerusalem): poverty causes unclear, not community of goods; Acts 11:29; logia only here; authority yet voluntary (2 Cor 9:7); Rom 15:26; five grounds for Lord's day (John 20:19,26; resurrection; Rev 1:10; Acts 20:7; practice); every one; religious effect (2 Cor 9:12-14); "by him" = common treasury, not at home; letters go with "I will send"; integrity (2 Cor 8:19-20); Acts 19:21; original plan (2 Cor 1:15-16); written from Ephesus; Acts 20:2-3; Lord = Christ; door = opportunity; adversaries Diana worshippers; Judaizers at Corinth.
1cor-16-10-24 (Timothy and Apollos; exhortations and greetings; closing salutation): Timothy to learn effect (Acts 19:22; 4:17); fear of disrespect; youth (1 Tim 4:12); 2 Cor 1:1; Apollos not under Paul, party strife (1:12); watch, stand fast (settled doctrine, 2 Cor 4:2), quit like men, be strong, love; Stephanas firstfruits, Rom 16:5 reads Asia; "those who serve should be served"; carried letter (7:1); want of you; Meyer; Asia proconsular; Aquila and Priscilla (Acts 18:2,18; Rom 16:5); holy kiss (Rom 16:16; 1 Thess 5:26); own hand (Col 4:18; 2 Thess 3:17; Gal 6:11); anathema, Maran atha = the Lord comes; God manifest in flesh (2 Cor 4:3-6); love to all incl. reproved.
