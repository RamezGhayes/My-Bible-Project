# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, 2 Corinthians 7
Read 2026-11-27 at https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (characters 400000-500000).
2cor-7-1-4 (1-4): epiteleo complete (Phil 1:6); hagiosyne (1 Thess 3:13); fear of God; flesh = body, spirit = soul; Acts 15:9; Eph 5:26; Phil 2:12-13; v2 make room = 6:13; adikeo general; phtheiro moral or financial (1 Cor 3:17); pleonekteo money, collection suspicion 8:19-20; rejects reference to incestuous case; v3 not to condemn, die and live together; v4 parresia, kauchesis, Calvin "Gradatim procedit amplificando", hyperperisseuomai (Rom 5:20), martyrs' joy.
2cor-7-5-16 (5-16): no rest in Macedonia; fightings without, fears within = external and internal, rejects Calvin's church reading; tapeinoi (1 Pet 5:5; Luke 1:52; James 1:9; Ps 18:27); epipothesis, odyrmos (Matt 2:18), zelos (Gal 4:17-18); v8 regret, rejects Calvin (Matt 21:29, 32); v9 metanoia change of heart (Matt 3:8; Luke 5:32; Acts 5:31), kata theon; v10 repentance not sorrow itself, ametameleton with repentance, kosmou = unrenewed men, sorrow not naturally good (Rev 16:10-11); v11 seven effects, spoude, apologia, aganaktesis, ekdikesis, Bengel, Meyer, Olshausen, church's repentance as one body; v12 not for offender or injured (1 Cor 5:1), keeps hemon, 2:9; v13 anapepautai; v14 boast true, link to charges 1:17-18; v15 splanchna, fear and trembling = reverence (1 Cor 2:3; Eph 6:5); v16 tharro (5:6; Heb 13:6).
