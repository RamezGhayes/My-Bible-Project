# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 14
Read 2026-11-27 in https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/OneCorinthiansCharlesHodge.pdf (WebFetch portions 600000-800000). Section titles as in his text.
1cor-14-1-25 (Superiority of the gift of prophecy to that of tongues, vs. 1-25): runner (Phil 3:12,14); zeloo = 12:31; prophecy = Spirit's organ instructing; hearers ignorant of language; in the spirit = Holy Spirit; mysteries revealed (Acts 2:11); foreign languages; Calvin "sibi canit et musis", "si accedat interpretatio, jam erit prophetia"; edifies himself = understanding not suspended; v6 pairs (12:8); trumpet = intelligibility; eusemos; 9:26; voices = languages, contra Calvin; barbarian (Rom 1:14; Col 3:11); spirits = manifestations (12:7); pray that he may interpret; rejects ecstatic prayer (Acts 2:5-11); spirit = gift, unfruitful to others (Eph 5:11; Titus 3:14); Amen, Justin Martyr, Latin worship; katecheo (Gal 6:6); Matt 18:3; Isa 28 free citation of judgment; four views of sign, prefers manifestation of God's disposition; idiotai vs apistoi; Rom 10:10.
1cor-14-26-40 (Special directions as to the mode of conducting their public assemblies, vs. 26-40): each has; two or three; first be silent; paraklesis; spirits subject to prophets, rejects "one another"; "as in all churches" with v34; women silent (11:5; Acts 2:17; 21:9), subordination and propriety, aischros; v36 rebuke of independence; v37 Roman vs Protestant authority (1 John 4:6); kata taxin army image.
