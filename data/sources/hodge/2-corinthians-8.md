# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, 2 Corinthians 8
Read 2026-11-27 at https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (characters 400000-600000).
2cor-8-1-8 (1-8): grace given in them (1:22); Philippi, Thessalonica, Berea (Phil 4:15, 18); joy and poverty abounded to liberality; ploutos (Rom 2:4; Eph 1:7); haplotes; Arnold on Greece "desolation and distress"; Stanley; kata/para dynamin; authairetoi; rejects interpolation; koinonia, diakonia; first in importance; dia thelematos; v6 Titus had begun; charis = grace/disposition; logos, gnosis (1 Cor 1:5, 7); v8 "What is not spontaneous is not liberal"; 1 John 3:17; dokime.
2cor-8-9-15 (9-15): parenthesis, Christian motive; rich in glory with the Father (John 17:5), divine prerogatives; ptocheuo = incarnation, Phil 2:6; 2 Pet 1:4; John 17:22; Rom 8:17; gnome (1 Cor 7:6); expedient for their growth; "a year ago", ~six months between letters; ek tou echein; prothumia personified; euprosdektos (Rom 15:16; 1 Pet 2:5); "The disposition is what God regards"; Mark 12:42; anesis, thlipsis; isotes equality of relief, rejects agrarianism and community of goods, Acts 5:4; Gal 6:10; rules incl. 2 Thess 3:10; Exod 16:18; "superabundance of one ... necessities of others"; "Property is like manna, it will not bear hoarding."
2cor-8-16-24 (16-24): God controls inward acts without violating liberty; dependence for continuance of life; Titus's own accord; Meyer on spoudaioteros; unnamed brother: useless to conjecture (Luke, Mark, Trophimus); cheirotonetheis (Josephus, Wetstein); synekdemos (Acts 19:29); stellomenoi avoid (2 Thess 3:6); hadrotes; appearing right matters, "a foolish pride"; Rom 12:17; Prov 3:4 LXX; second brother; rejects Calvin/Beza on pepoithesei; koinonos, synergos; apostoloi = messengers (Phil 2:25); Calvin on glory of Christ; endeixis; bond among early churches.
