# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, 2 Corinthians 6
Read 2026-11-27 at https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (characters 300000-500000).
2cor-6-1-10 (1-10): "also" with the verb; workers together = cooperation with God (1 Cor 3:9); Isa 49:8; trials, graces, antitheses; chastened not killed via Ps 118:18; paradox of Christian experience; poor yet making rich, possessing all (1 Cor 3:21; Rom 8:17).
2cor-6-11-18 (11-18): mouth open = speak freely (Matt 5:2; Acts 8:32, 35; Eph 6:19); heart enlarged (1 Kgs 4:29; Ps 119:32; Isa 60:5); straitened (Ps 4:1; 118:5), lack on their side; antimisthia accusative absolute; heterozugeo, Deut 22:10; Lev 19:19 LXX; primarily heathen associations and idol feasts (1 Cor 8; 10:14-33), principle broader; not directly deciding modern marriages; metoche, koinonia (Acts 2:42; 1 Cor 1:9; 10:16); light/darkness (Luke 16:8; 1 Thess 5:5; Acts 26:18); Belial (2 Sam 23:6; 1 Cor 10:21); temple: Christ (John 2:19), church (Eph 2:21), body (1 Cor 3:16; 6:19); Lev 26:11-12; Rom 8:10-11; John 14:23; Gen 17:8; Jer 31:33; Heb 8:10; Isa 52:11-12 free citation; Ezek 20:34 LXX; 1 John 2:15; rejects 2 Sam 7:14, prefers Isa 43:6; NT liberties in quoting OT.
