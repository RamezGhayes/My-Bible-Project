# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 12
Read 2026-11-27 in the Monergism PDF https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (via fetch summaries of the text).
Analysis: 1-6 vision; 7-10 thorn and grace; 11-12 forced self-vindication; 13-18 refusal of support; 19-21 fear of judging.
v1 turns to what God did. v2 "in Christ" mark of a Christian, not conversion; harpagenta; rejects rabbinic seven heavens, third = highest heaven (Acts 8:39; 1 Thess 4:17). v3-4 doubt whether soul alone or with body, not reality; paradise = third heaven (Luke 23:43; Rev 2:7); arreta rhemata not lawful to utter. v5 boasts of "such a one." v6 judged by what is seen. v7 skolops; reports views: goad, cross, corrupt nature (Calvin); prefers bodily affliction; rejects spiritual temptation or human adversary; messenger of Satan = Satan's agency (Gal 4:14). v8-9 thrice (Matt 26); grace = God's love/Spirit's aid; power shown in weakness; episkenose dwell like shekinah in tabernacle. v10 excludes self-inflicted or deserved suffering. v11 not irony; compelled. v12 signs = insignia of office: inward gifts, external marks, holy walk; three names for miracles by purpose, effect, source (Rom 15:18-19; Heb 2:4). v13 tender irony. v14 ready to come third time; seek you not yours. v15 disinterested love. v16 charge of gain through agents. v17-18 same spirit, same steps. v19 before God, for edification. v20 vices of party strife; mixed church: penitent majority, impenitent minority (reconcile with ch 7). v21 mourn unrepentant.
