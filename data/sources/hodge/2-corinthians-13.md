# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 13
Read 2026-11-27 in the Monergism PDF https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (via fetch summaries of the text).
v1 third time confirms two earlier visits; rejects visits = witnesses; OT two-or-three-witness rule applies to discipline. v2 will not spare; church's right of excommunication; Paul's personal apostolic authority rests on office. v3 proof of Christ speaking in me; not weak among them. v4 Christ crucified in weakness, lives by God's power; Paul weak in Christ, will live with him toward them; weakness = restraint. v5 examine yourselves; faith self-manifesting; Christ indwells by the Spirit; "reprobates" = unapproved, not judicially damned. v6-7 prays they do no evil, not to prove his power; would rather appear unapproved. v8 power only for the truth (God's revealed word); limits all church power. v9 glad when weak; their perfection (restoration). v10 writes to avoid sharpness; power for edification. v11 rejoice, be perfect, comfort, one mind, peace; God of love and peace present where love and peace prevail; God's gifts and human effort cooperate. v12 holy kiss a custom, not permanent command. v13 saints set apart. v14 grace of Christ, love of Father, communion of Spirit: clear Trinitarian statement.
