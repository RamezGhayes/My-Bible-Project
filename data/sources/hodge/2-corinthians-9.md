# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 9
Read 2026-11-27 in the Monergism PDF https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (via fetch summaries of the text).
Analysis: vv. 1-5 do not let boast prove empty; vv. 6-15 give liberally and cheerfully.
v1 diakonia = alms; hagioi = Jerusalem poor (Acts 6:1; Rom 15:31; 1 Cor 16:1-3). v2 prothumia; rejects charge Paul exaggerated; erethizein stir (Col 3:21). v3 kenothe; collection begun not finished. v4 Paul takes shame on himself to spare them. v5 prokatartizo only here; eulogia = bounty; pleonexia.
v6 Prov 11:24; Gal 6:7; Luke 6:38; self-denial serves one's interest only when sincere. v7 settled willing heart; ek lupes; hilaron doten; Prov 22:9 LXX. v8 charis = earthly favour; autarkeia = competency; "he that scattereth increaseth" believed by trust in God's power (Rom 16:25; Eph 3:20; Phil 4:18). v9 Ps 112; dikaiosune = beneficence; promises as general tendencies of providence, not universal guarantees (Mark 10:30). v10 future tense, certainty; epichoregon God's office; Isa 55:10; fruits of righteousness = means of doing good. v11 haplotes generosity; thanksgiving through Paul. v12 leitourgia religious service (Phil 2:17, 30; Luke 1:23). v13 dokime proof; obedience to their confession. v14 recipients' prayer part of reward. v15 unspeakable gift = God's Son (8:9).
