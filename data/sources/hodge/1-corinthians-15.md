# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 15
Read 2026-11-27 in https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/OneCorinthiansCharlesHodge.pdf (WebFetch portions 700000-900000). Section titles as in his text.
1cor-15-1-34 (The Resurrection of Christ as securing the Resurrection of his People, vs. 1-34): objectors not Sadducees; dualism; 2 Tim 2:17-18; Acts 17:32; gnorizo; katechete perseverance; eike; first = chief; Gal 1:12; propitiation (Rom 3:23-26); huper; OT prediction (Luke 24:25-26; Acts 26:22-23; Isa 53); Ps 16:10 (Acts 13:34-37); criteria of testimony; Twelve collective; 500 in Galilee (Matt 28:10,16); asleep; James; abortion, abasement; least = rank (2 Cor 11:5; 12:11; Gal 2:6-9); 1 Tim 1:13-15; grace = Spirit's influence; Rom 7:17; kene vs mataia; Rom 1:3; John 8:21; Rom 4:25; perish not annihilation; not actually more miserable; aparche (Rom 8:23; 11:16; Col 1:18; Rev 1:5); Adam and Christ; "all" = his people; Acts 24:15; tagma, 1 Clement; end = end of world; rejects two resurrections from Rev 20; threefold kingdom; Ps 110:1; Ps 8 (Heb 2:8); subjection official not essential; against pantheism, universalism (Olshausen); v29 survey of views (preference not used); 2 Cor 4:10; beasts literal vs figurative; Menander; eknepsate; Matt 22:29.
1cor-15-35-58 (Nature of the resurrection body, vs. 35-58): seed answer; Luke 12:20; John 12:24; identity; bare grain; celestial = heavenly bodies (Galen via Wetstein); phthora/aphtharsia; psychikon/pneumatikon not ethereal; Gen 2:7; 3:22; John 5:21,26; Rom 5:14; Calvin on regeneration after birth; virgin birth objection (John 3:13); image of body; flesh and blood = body (Heb 2:14; Matt 16:17; Gal 1:16); mystery (1 Thess 4:15); not Paul's generation (2 Thess 2:2-12), inspiration vs omniscience; en atomo; last trump (Matt 24:31; Isa 27:13; 1 Thess 4:16; Ex 19:16); Isa 25:8; Hos 13:14, Vulgate, LXX; kentron (Rev 9:5-6,10); law generally (Rom 4:15; 5:12-13; 7:8-12); Rom 8:1,33-34; Heb 2:14-15; 2 Cor 11:23.
