# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 10
Read 2026-11-27 in the Monergism PDF https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (via fetch summaries of the text).
Analysis: tone change explained by audience (chs 1-9 faithful, 10-13 false teachers), rejects separate-letter theory; Paul asserts authority, warns, defends jurisdiction, refuses to boast beyond his sphere.
v1 praotes inward, epieikeia outward (Matt 11:29; Isa 42:2); tapeinos; disputes Rückert that Paul lacked courage. v2 logizomai = purpose; tolmesai; flesh = corrupt nature. v3 walk figurative; strateuomai; Calvin earthen vessel. v4 dunata to theo (Hebraic superlative, Nineveh "great to God"; Acts 7:20); ochuromata fortresses. v5 logismous, hupsoma, noema; 1 Cor 1:17-31; 3:18-20; Rom 1:21-23. v6 punish after obedience complete; excommunication or more (1 Cor 5:5; 1 Tim 1:20). v7 question/imperative/statement; Paul is Christ's as fully. v8 exousia right and power, for edification; authority from Christ alone. v9-10 bareiai, ischurai; presence = deportment, doubts physical appearance meant. v11 deeds match letters. v12 enkrinai, sunkrinai; measuring by themselves unwise. v13 kanon, metron; apostles had no fixed dioceses (Rom 15:20; Gal 2:9). v14 ephthasamen came first. v15 megalunthenai enlarged literally. v16 regions beyond. vv17-18 glory in the Lord; 1 Cor 4:3.
