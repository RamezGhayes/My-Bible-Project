# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 11
Read 2026-11-27 in the Monergism PDF https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (via fetch summaries of the text).
v1 ophelon; second clause indicative. v2 zelos theou; Paul shares God's feeling toward his bride people (Isa 54:5; 62:5; Ezek 16; Hos 2; John 3:29; Eph 5:27); hermosamen betrothed. v3 Eve created holy yet deceived (1 Tim 2:14; Rev 12:9); Genesis historical; noemata thoughts and affections; haplotes; belief shaped by heart, accountable. v4 answers request for tolerance: they bear false teachers, so bear Paul; elabete vs edexasthe (Bengel); disagrees with Chrysostom. v5 rejects ironic reading: true chiefest apostles; not inferior in gifts, labors, success (Gal 2:6-11); Reformers' argument against Petrine primacy legitimate. v6 idiotes in speech not knowledge (1 Cor 2:4-11). v7 self-humiliation, free preaching (1 Cor 9:4-15). v8 opsonion stipend. v9 katenarkesa not a dead weight (Acts 20:34; 1 Thess 2:9; Phil 4:16). v10-12 boast not silenced in Achaia; deny opponents an opening. v13-15 false apostles lacked commission, inspiration, powers; compared to false Christs (Matt 24:24); against claim that apostolic office continues in bishops; Satan as angel of light; judged by works not professions. v17 boasting not after the Lord, inspiration not undone (Bengel, Meyer). v20 katadouloi, katesthiei, lambanei, epairetai, strike (1 Kgs 22:24; Acts 23:2). v22 Hebrews, Israelites, seed: national, theocratic, spiritual privileges. v23 painful humble self-assessment. v24-25 forty less one (Deut 25:3); Philippi and Lystra only cases in Acts; shipwreck not in Acts. v26 perils chiefly from own people (Acts 9:23, 29; 13:50; 14:5, 19; 17:5, 13; 18:12). v27 fastings involuntary hunger. v28-29 daily care; sympathy with weak, burning at those who cause stumbling (1 Cor 9:22; 12:26). v31 God as witness. v32-33 ethnarch under Aretas (Acts 9:24-25); basket through window (Josh 2:15; 1 Sam 19:12); Conybeare and Howson.
