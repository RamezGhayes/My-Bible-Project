## 1:1-17 (outline in our own words)
- Analysis: 1-7 salutation, 8-17 introduction naming the two themes (method of salvation; to whom offered).
- v1: Saul/Paul name, three theories (Sergius Paulus, rejected; new name at call; Jewish/Gentile double name), combines last two. doulos = official minister (Josh 1:1; Isa 42:1; Phil 1:1). kletos: called by God, not men (Gal 1:1, 15). apostolos: immediate commission, seen risen Christ (1 Cor 9:1; Acts 1:21-22), inspired, infallible. aphorizo: set apart for a special mission (Gal 1:15; Acts 13:2).
- v2: gospel foretold in OT, proof of divine origin; "prophets" = all OT writers against Meyer (Acts 3:24). "Concerning his Son" joined to "promised".
- v3: Son = title of nature, eternal Logos (John 1; Col 1:13-17; Heb 1:2-6; Rom 8:3, 32). ginomai = born. kata sarka limits Davidic descent to humanity, implies more than man; flesh = whole human nature (against Apollinarians).
- v4: horizo = declared/demonstrated, not appointed (Sonship not an office), Vulgate "predestinatus" rejected. en dynamei = powerfully (against Theophylact/Theodoret's miracles). pneuma hagiosynes = divine nature, antithesis to flesh (not Holy Spirit as Calvin; not exalted state); 1 Tim 3:16; Heb 9:14. Resurrection as evidence, not "after" (Erasmus, Luther). Jesus/Christ/Kyrios = LXX for Jehovah.
- v5: grace and apostleship (hendiadys or two). obedience of faith: genitive options weighed, keeps "obedience which is faith/from faith". Commission universal; end = Christ's name.
- v6: kletoi = effectually called (Rom 8:28; 1 Cor 1:24).
- v7: beloved of God (Eph 2:4-5); hagioi = consecrated and purified (expiation and Spirit); Beza's punctuation; grace and peace (shalom); Christ joined with Father as source of blessing = proof of deity.
- v8: proton men unfinished enumeration; "through Jesus Christ" goes with "I thank": Christ mediator of praise (Eph 5:20; Heb 13:15).
- v9: oath appeal to God (2 Cor 1:23); latreuo religious service; "in my spirit" sincere; gospel about the Son.
- v10: ei pos submission; ede pote; euodoo = be prospered.
- v11: charisma pneumatikon broad (not only miraculous gifts); sterizo establish.
- v12: mutual encouragement; construction note (Luke 1:55).
- v13: hindered by apostolic labors, maybe the Spirit (Acts 16:6-9); fruit.
- v14: debtor to all; barbaros = foreigner (1 Cor 14:11).
- v15: to kat' eme prothymon, three readings (Calvin, Beza, "my readiness"), prefers Calvin.
- v16: not ashamed (1 Cor 1:23); power of God = power from God (Hebraism "great power" rejected); salvation; "every one" + "believeth": faith alone, universal; faith = knowledge, assent, trust, determined by biblical usage; "Jew first" temporal (John 4:22).
- v17: righteousness of God not attribute (gift, without law, not our own: Rom 5:17; 10:3; Phil 3:9); given by God (Beza) and approved by God (Luther, Calvin), joined by imputation; Pelagian, Romanist views rejected. ek pisteos eis pistin: four views (Theodoret; Calvin growth; Bengel "faith alone", approved; terminus). Hab 2:4 construction "the just by faith shall live".
- Doctrine 1-12 and Remarks 1-8 (ministry, OT gospel, Christ two natures, eternal Sonship, Christ object of prayer and mediator, gospel alone saves, justification by faith; ministers wholly given to gospel, saints set apart, virtues as gifts call for thanks, rejecting justification = rejecting gospel).

## 1:18-32
- Analysis: Gentiles cannot be justified by works: God's wrath revealed (18), knowledge leaves them inexcusable (19-20), idolatry (21-23), given up to corruption (24-31), approve others (32).
- v18: wrath = punitive justice, not passion; apokalyptetai revealed by its effects and conscience; asebeia/adikia; katechein = hinder/suppress (2 Thess 2:6-7), preferred over "possess".
- v19: gnoston = what is known (Acts 1:19; 15:18); en autois = in their minds (Beza); not only philosophers (against Grotius); Calvin: men made spectators of creation.
- v20: aorata = perfections (Theophylact over Theodoret); theiotes vs theotes; ktisis = act of creation; kathoratai; sufficient to leave inexcusable, not to save (Calvin).
- v21: dioti linked to end of v20 (against Rueckert); really knew God; emataiothesan (idols mataia); dialogismoi bad sense; heart = whole soul.
- v22: phaskontes = pretending.
- v23: glory = divine perfections; allassein en = exchanged for (Ps 106:20 LXX); all image worship idolatry.
- v24: paredoken eis akatharsian (not with en); judicial giving up, not mere permission: withdrawal of restraint; sin punished by sin (rabbinic maxim, Tholuck, De Wette); freedom remains (Meyer; Eph 4:19); reciprocal dishonor.
- v25: causal relative; truth of God = true God, lie = idol (Jer 13:25; 16:19); worshipped/served; para = passing by the Creator (Beza); eulogetos, Amen.
- v26-27: four reasons uncleanness named first; women first; recompense of their error = degradation itself.
- v28: pagans linked impiety and vice (Cicero, Silius Italicus); edokimasan / adokimos wordplay, adokimos = reprobate (against Beza, Bengel); epignosis; poiein consequence.
- v29-31: participle tied to v28, vices are consequence; Seneca; theostygeis active vs passive, active preferred with most; three words of pride; disobedience to parents serious; alpha-privative list.
- v32: dikaioma = righteous decree; death = punishment broadly; knowledge innate (cf. ch 2:14); abandonment does not remove guilt; approving others is the depth.
- Doctrine 1-10 (punitive justice essential, sin deserves punishment, God never without witness, natural religion cannot save, need of gospel for heathen, error rooted in depravity, sin punished by sin, religion basis of morality, approving sin worse, conscience never extinct). Remarks 1-8 (no excuse for sin, see God in works, reason errs apart from God, more guilt under Scripture, missions duty, security from gross sin in God, uncleanness debasing, choose company).
