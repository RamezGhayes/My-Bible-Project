# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, Commentary on the Epistle to the Ephesians (public domain), Ephesians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://www.ccel.org/ccel/h/hodge/ephesians/cache/ephesians.txt (CCEL plain text, chars 0-200000).
eph-1-sec2, Section II.--Vs. 3-14: Analysis in six parts. v3 eulogetos praise; God of redemption (John 20:17); spiritual = from Spirit; en tois epouraniois local (1:20; 2:6; 3:10; 6:12). v4 kathos source; individuals; rejects foreseen faith; federal union, covenant of redemption, Israel in Abraham (3:11); eternal (2 Thess 2:13; Matt 25:34; John 17:24; 1 Pet 1:20; 2 Tim 1:9); unto holiness (Eph 5:27; Col 1:22; Titus 2:14); three inferences. v5 sonship nature, favor, heirship (John 1:12; Gal 3:26); eudokia sovereign (Matt 11:26; Luke 10:21; Phil 2:13). v6 glory of grace; echaritosen objective (Luke 1:28; Eph 2:4, 7); in the Beloved. v7 apolutrosis ransom; = forgiveness here (Rom 8:23); riches of grace three ways (1 Cor 1:27-29). v8 wisdom with grace (1 Cor 1-2; Col 1:9); Father/Son/Spirit. v9 musterion (Rom 16:25; 1 Cor 2:7-10; Eph 3:9; Col 1:26; 1 Tim 3:16); in himself = God. v10 oikonomia plan; fullness (1 Cor 10:11; Heb 1:1; Gal 4:4); anakephalaiosasthai (Rom 13:9); five views of "all things", prefers redeemed only (Col 1:20). v11 we = Jewish Christians; eklerothemen three readings, prefers obtained inheritance (vv14, 18; Col 1:12); prothesis; counsel of his will. v12 first hoped = Jews before Gentiles (1 Cor 15:19). v13-14 word of truth = gospel (Col 1:5; Rom 1:16); sealing follows faith; seal three uses (1 John 5:10; Rom 8:16; 5:5; Rev 7:3; Eph 4:30; 2 Cor 1:21-22); Spirit of promise (Acts 1:4; Gal 3:14); arrabon (2 Cor 5:5; Rom 8:23); redemption final (Luke 21:28; Eph 4:30); peripoiesis = God's people (1 Pet 2:9; Mal 3:17; Acts 20:28).
eph-1-sec3, Section III.--Vs. 15-23: Analysis thanksgiving and three petitions. v15 faith and love; faith in the Lord; Lord applied to Christ shows deity. v17 Father of glory (rejects patristic reading; Acts 7:2; 1 Cor 2:8; Ps 24:7); Spirit of wisdom = Holy Spirit (Rom 8:15; Gal 4:6); wisdom outward/inward (1 Cor 2:6-16); apokalupsis not prophecy; epignosis (Phil 1:9; Eph 4:13). v18 kardia; whole spiritual nature (Rom 1:21; 2 Cor 4:6; Acts 26:18; Eph 5:8); klesis effectual; hope subjective; inheritance among saints (Acts 20:32; Col 1:12). v19-20 rejects Grotius; power in conversion (Col 2:12); Chrysostom, Calvin, Luther; Calvin "Stulti homines"; ischus, kratos, energeia, Calvin root-tree-fruit; resurrection type and cause (Rom 6:1-10); right hand (Heb 1:13; Matt 28:18; Phil 2:9; 1 Pet 3:22). v21-22 God-man; angels (Rom 8:38; Col 1:16; Eph 6:12); possible emanation theory; Ps 8 (1 Cor 15:27; Heb 2:8); head for church (Col 2:10; Eph 4:15-16; 5:23). v23 body constituted by indwelling Spirit; rejects officers/baptized/professors; pleroma discussion (preferred sense unclear in summary, not used); fills all in all = universe (Jer 23:24; Ps 139:7; Col 1:16-17; Heb 1:3).
