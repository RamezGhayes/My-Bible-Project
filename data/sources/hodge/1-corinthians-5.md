# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 5
Read 2026-11-27 in https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/OneCorinthiansCharlesHodge.pdf (WebFetch portion 200000-300000). Section titles as in his text.
1cor-5-1-5 (The case of the incestuous member of the church, vs. 1-5): porneia broad; stepmother (Lev 18:8); have = marriage (Matt 14:4; 1 Cor 7:2); father alive (2 Cor 7:12); Cicero; Lev 18 binding; rejects proselyte-kinship view; Chrysostom pestilence; discipline belongs to church as body; presence of knowledge, authority, power; kekrika; in the name = by Christ's authority; deliver to Satan: excommunication or miraculous bodily affliction, leans to the latter (Acts 5:1-11; 13:9-11; 1 Tim 1:20); flesh = body; 2 Cor 7:9-12.
1cor-5-6-13 (Exhortation to purity, and to fidelity in discipline, vs. 6-13): leaven diffuses; old leaven = corrupt nature; unleavened by standing; thuo vicarious (Ex 12:15); "for us" absent in oldest MSS; feast = consecrated life; poneria vs kakia; eilikrineia; lost earlier letter (Calvin, Beza); synanamignysthai (2 Thess 3:14); covetous idolatry (Eph 5:5; 1 Cor 10:14-22); not to eat = not recognize as Christians; against Romish denial of necessities; those without (Mark 4:11; Col 4:5; 1 Thess 4:12); exarate (Deut 24:7).
