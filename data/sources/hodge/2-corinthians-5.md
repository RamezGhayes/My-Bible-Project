# Reading notes: Charles Hodge, An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians, 2 Corinthians 5
Read 2026-11-27 at https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/An%20Exposition%20of%20Second%20Corinthians%20-%20Charles%20Hodge.pdf (characters 200000-400000 of the text).
2cor-5-1-8 (1-8): three views of building (heaven, resurrection body, intermediate body); rejects intermediate body (Olshausen); favors heaven itself; John 14:2; Heb 11:10; 13:14; Luke 23:43; Phil 1:22-24; Heb 12:23; rejects soul sleep and Usteri; skenos, acheiropoietos, present echomen; v2 epipothountes, Rom 8:22; v4 burden not dread, 1 Cor 15:53-54; v5 fitted us, earnest = indwelling Spirit (2 Cor 1:22; Eph 1:13-14; Rom 5:5; 8:11, 16); v6 tharrountes settled confidence, endemeo/ekdemeo, Phil 3:20; Heb 11:13; v7 eidos = thing seen, Calvin quote, Rom 8:24; v8 preference for death, Phil 1:23, Lord = Christ.
2cor-5-9-13 (9-13): philotimoumetha point of honour, Rom 15:20; living or dying Rom 14:8; 1 Thess 5:10; bema; komizo; grace vs justice, soldier's pay vs titles; blood cleanses all sin; v11 phobos reverence (Acts 9:31; Rom 3:18; Eph 5:21), peithein convince vs conciliate (Gal 1:10; Luther); v12 grounds to vindicate; false teachers' external boasts; v13 existemi loose sense (Acts 26:24; Mark 3:21), folly = self-commendation.
2cor-5-14-21 (14-21): love of Christ = His love for us (Gal 2:20); synecho; "Morality does not make us religious, but religion makes us moral"; krinantas at conversion; hyper = in place of (Rom 5:6-8; 1 Tim 2:6; Titus 2:14; Heb 2:9); all = all for whom he died; then all died by solidarity (Rom 5:12-21; 1 Cor 15:21-22; Rom 6:8; Eph 2:5-6); Rom 14:9; 1 Pet 2:24; v16 judge by externals, John 8:15; Phil 3:4; v17 union, Eph 1:19; Gal 6:15; Rom 8:9; rejects Calvin margin; Isa 43:18-19; 65:17; Rev 21:5; "cage of unclean birds"; v18 God reconciler, sacrifice propitiates not reforms, Rom 5:9-10; v19 en Christo with katallasson, kosmos mankind, reconciliation = propitiation, not imputing = forgiving (Rom 4:5; Col 2:13), word = preaching (1 Cor 1:18); v20 ambassador messenger and representative, Eph 6:20, katallagete passive, Calvin on believers' daily renewal; v21 hamartia = sin not sin-offering (three reasons, LXX), Gal 3:13; Rom 8:3; dikaiosyne forensic (Rom 1:17; 3:22; 10:3; Phil 3:9); "Imputation conveys neither pollution nor holiness"; "It is not mere pardon, but justification alone, that gives us peace with God."
