# Reading notes: John Piper, sermons on Galatians 1 (desiringgod.org)
Read 2026-11-27. Date not shown on pages.
to-deliver-us-from-the-present-evil-age, To Deliver Us from the Present Evil Age (1:1-5): series intro: cross only way to be right with God, Spirit only way to obey; apostle = one sent (John 13:16), general sense (Phil 2:25; 2 Cor 8:23) and particular (1 Cor 9:1; 15:8-9; Acts 26:16-17); foundation (Eph 2:20); authority now only through the New Testament; reading Galatians = hearing Christ; Galatia Roman province; structure grace, cross, glory ("Grace to You and Glory to God"); evil age: sin and Satan's allowed power (John 17:15; 2 Cor 4:4; Luke 4:6); deliverance begun (Col 1:13; Heb 6:5; 2 Cor 5:17); Demas (2 Tim 4:10); Rom 12:2; freedom of mind; paradox: freest submit most to apostolic authority; P. T. Forsyth quote.
this-is-not-mans-gospel, This Is Not Man's Gospel (1:11-24): apostleship and gospel stand or fall together; "not according to man" = not from man nor flattering man (6:12); Judaizers from Jerusalem claiming James, Peter, John (2:9, 12), Paul a latecomer; double defense (v1, v12); truth matters, press unbelievers to justify their worldview; vv13-14 public former life; vv15-17 reversal needs adequate cause, only divine call (Acts 9:4; 26:16-18); vv18-20 fifteen days, independent; vv21-24 Judean churches did not know him; Matt 21:23-27 from heaven or from men?
