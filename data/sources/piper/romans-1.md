## the-author-of-the-greatest-letter-ever-written (1998-04-26, 1:1)
- Why Romans now; Romans solid, old, unshakeable. Personal history: conversion about 1952 explained later by Romans (3:23; 6:23; 5:8; 10:9); John Stott Men Made New; Daniel Fuller's courses at Fuller (Rom 1-8; 9-11); 1979 sabbatical wrestling with Rom 9, call to pastor.
- 1:1 question is "whose am I", not "who am I". Slave of Christ: bought, owned, ruled (1 Cor 7:23; Gal 1:10). Contrast "second-handers" who live to please people.
- Called apostle: saw risen Christ and commissioned (1 Cor 15:7-8; Acts 26:16); unrepeatable foundation (Eph 2:20), now in written apostolic teaching.
- Set apart before birth (Gal 1:15) though persecutor (1 Tim 1:16): sovereign free grace; Rom 11:33-36. Leon Morris: God named about 160 times; Romans is about God.
- Application: oriented to pleasing Christ; doubly owned by creation and purchase.

## gods-good-news-concerning-his-son (1998-05-03, 1:1-4)
- v1: slave serving in Christ's strength (Rom 15:18; 1 Cor 15:10; 1 Pet 4:11).
- v2: gospel fulfils OT promise, not a new religion; God keeps promises. "Through" his prophets: God the speaker; Scripture holy (Eph 2:20; 1 Cor 2:13; 2 Pet 3:16).
- v3: Son existed before incarnation; unique sonship (vs Rom 8:14); fully God (Rom 9:5; Col 2:9); from David's line, messianic promises (Jer 23:5; Isa 11:10; 9:6-7; 2 Cor 1:20).
- Between v3 and v4 the Son died: "Messiahs don't die" (Luke 24:21).
- v4: Spirit raised Christ (Rom 8:11); "Spirit of holiness" possibly contrasted with unholy contact with the dead (Saul and the medium, 1 Sam 28). "Declared" can be "appointed"; "in power" modifies Son of God: shift from lowliness to power, not change in sonship. Reigns till all enemies subdued (Matt 28:18; 1 Cor 15:25-26; Phil 2:11).
- Apply: trust promises, treat Scripture as God's word, hope for visible reign.

## by-his-grace-for-his-name-through-the-obedience-of-faith (1998-05-10, 1:5)
- Grace 155 times in NT, 24 in Romans. Through Christ: grounded in his obedience and death (3:24-25; 5:18-21).
- Grace includes apostleship: empowering for a calling (12:3, 6; 15:15-16). Not owed (4:4; 11:6), received by faith (4:16), reigns (5:21). 1 Cor 15:10; Rom 15:18. Substitute your calling for "apostleship".
- Obedience of faith: obedience that comes from faith and obedience which is faith; he holds both (Leon Morris). 1 Tim 1:5; Rom 9:32; 14:23.
- For his name: God's goal (9:17; 1 Pet 4:11). Loving because calling on the name saves (10:13) and we rejoice in hope of glory (5:2). 2 Cor 4:4.

## the-called-of-christ-and-the-loved-of-god-part-1 (1998-05-17, 1:6-7)
- Identity from what God did. Small Roman church vs empire; small gathering vs world events.
- Not democracy but grace (9:15; Eph 4:18). Three claims: God calls ("called of Jesus Christ" = called into fellowship with Christ, 1 Cor 1:9); call is particular (8:28, 30); call is effectual.
- General call (Mark 4:14; Acts 17:30; Matt 22:14) vs effectual call (8:30; 1 Cor 1:23-24). 2 Cor 4:4-6 light of creation; John 3:19-20; Eph 5:14. "Called saints": holiness from the call.

## the-called-of-christ-and-the-loved-of-god-part-2 (1998-05-24, 1:6-7)
- Faith needs preaching (2 Cor 4:5) and divine light (4:6); call like Lazarus (John 11:43).
- Beloved of God: general love (Matt 5:44-45; John 3:16) vs special covenant love for the called; letter to "my beloved Noel" analogy; marriage covenant. New covenant Jer 31:31; 32:40 (will not turn away); blood of covenant Luke 22:20. Assurance: Rom 8:30-39.

## the-treasure-of-faith-in-the-life-of-the-church (1998-05-31, 1:8-15)
- Paul's mission from v5 lived in three ways. v8: thanks God, not them, for their faith; faith a gift (Eph 2:8-9), visible in obedience; tree known by fruit; faith glorifies God (4:20-21).
- v9-12: serving "in my spirit" read as hidden prayer; "by the will of God"; gift to establish them (1 Thess 3:2); mutual encouragement.
- v13: fruit = obedience of faith (6:22).

## the-gospel-in-the-church-for-the-obedience-of-faith-through-spiritual-gifts (1998-06-07, 1:8-15)
- v11-12: every believer shares grace through gifts (12:6-8; 1 Pet 4:10); small groups.
- v14: debtor: debt to people not to God by way of repaying grace (a paid gift becomes a purchase); debt to God is by theft of his glory (1:23; 3:23; Matt 6:12). Whoever got good news owes it. No qualifications (1 Cor 15:10). Apply to racism, self-righteousness, marriage.
- v15: preach gospel to believers too: it sanctifies (15:15-16; Acts 20:24; 10:17).

## not-ashamed-of-the-gospel (1998-06-14, 1:16)
- v16 thesis of letter; v15 eagerness flows from not being ashamed. Four stories of shame (lost race, father arrested, wrong clothes, actor freezes): we want to look good.
- Paul embraced shame (2 Cor 11:23-26; Rom 1:14; 1 Cor 1:22-23). Jesus despised shame for joy (Heb 12:2).
- Modern shaming: claiming truth is arrogant. Answer: speak the exclusive truth in love (John 14:6; Acts 4:12).

## the-gospel-is-the-power-of-god-unto-salvation (1998-06-21, 1:16)
- Being shamed vs being ashamed. Do not water down (1 Cor 1:18).
- Salvation here mainly final salvation, not only conversion (10:17; 1 Pet 1:23-25; Eph 2:8-9). Four reasons: other religions also convert; Paul's future usage (2 Thess 2:13; 2 Cor 7:10; 2 Tim 3:15; Heb 9:28; 1 Pet 1:5; Rom 5:9-10; 13:11); present tense "believes" = keep believing (1 Cor 15:1-2; James 2:17, 26); written to believers (1:15).
- Apply: feed daily on promises like 8:32.

## how-does-the-gospel-save-believers (Part 1, 1998-08-09, 1:16-17)
- Saved mainly from God's wrath (1:18; 2:5, 8; 5:9); gospel = God rescues us from God.
- Deliberate wrong reading "love of God revealed": love is not sentimental (5:8).
- God demands righteousness we lack and gives what he demands. Luther's discovery. Embassy bombings (Nairobi, Dar es Salaam) as surface; deathbed.
- Previews three meanings and "from faith to faith".

## how-does-the-gospel-save-believers-part-2 (1998-08-16, 1:16-17)
- Three senses of righteousness of God: vindication of God's justice in punishing substitute; right standing (imputed); moral change. All true; main sense in 1:17 is right standing.
- Reasons: Hab 2:4 quote (righteous person) needs credited righteousness; parallel 3:20-26 (revealed / manifested); overlaps with 3:25-26 and 8:3-4.
- Greek: righteousness and just same root; "believes" present participle = keeps believing. Courtroom; Luther; child explanation.

## how-does-the-gospel-save-believers-part-3 (1998-08-23, 1:16-17)
- Two concerns: displaying God (v17) and being happy in God (v16).
- Salvation future grounded in past justification: 13:11; 5:9; 8:30, 32. Imputed then imparted.
- Hab 2:4 context: Babylon as judgment (Hab 1:5-6; 1:13); how survive judgment: the righteous live by faith.
- "From faith to faith" read with 2 Cor 2:15-16 (death to death, life to life); faith as a window letting righteousness in, which awakens more faith.
- Perseverance: 1 Cor 15:1-2; Mark 13:13; Col 1:23; 1 Pet 1:5; Rom 8:13. Obey not to earn but because guilt is removed.

## the-wrath-of-god-against-ungodliness-and-unrighteousness (1998-08-30, 1:18)
- "For" ties to 17: sin is studied for the sake of gospel. Structure 1:18-3:20.
- "Is revealed" same verb and tense as in 17: present wrath now, future day of wrath later (2:5, 8-9; 5:9).
- Three present revelations of wrath: universal death (5:15-18), futility of creation (8:18-21; Gen 3:15), giving over (1:24, 26, 28).
- Wrath mixed with kindness (2:4-5; Matt 5:45; Acts 14:17); two languages: warning in pain, wooing in pleasure (Matt 11:17).
- For believers: no condemnation (8:1, 3; 1 Thess 5:9); death a gateway (1 Cor 15:55-57), suffering purification (8:28; Heb 12), sin dethroned (Rom 6).

## the-wrath-of-god-against-holding-down-the-truth (1998-09-13, 1:18)
- "Spin": everyone softens own sin, harshly judges others (2:1); universal (3:9-10, 13).
- Truth suppressed (1:19-21, 25, 28): God exists, Creator, self-sufficient; we owe thanks and must display his glory (Ps 50:23). "We get the blessings, he gets the glory."
- Why gratitude not faith here: faith responds to specific promises from special revelation (4:20).
- "In unrighteousness": suppression follows love of sin (2 Thess 2:10-12; John 3:19-21). Heart before head: moral, not mental problem; need a new heart.

## displays-of-god-remove-the-excuse-for-failed-worship (1998-09-27, 1:19-21)
- Objection: those who never heard? Answer traced backward: without excuse (v20; 2:11-16); failure to glorify and thank (v21); knowledge of God evident (v19-21; "among them"); God made it evident.
- "What has been made" = poiema, root of "poem": design and intention; wind letters in sand are not a poem.
- Science and design: Philip Johnson, Michael Behe (flagellum, irreducible complexity), Dembski; Dawkins and Crick remarks.
- Application to urban ministry in Minneapolis: common ground, everyone knows God; greatest tragedy is perishing without excuse; gospel the best urban strategy (1:16-17).

## the-first-dark-exchange-idolatry (1998-10-04, 1:21-23)
- Root problem twice: not glorifying (21) and exchanging glory (23); before the sin list.
- Futile thinking (21): Nebuchadnezzar (Dan 4:30-34); 1 Cor 3:20.
- Darkened heart: no inner light (Matt 6:22; John 8:12; 1:14; Eph 1:18; 2 Cor 4:6).
- Feels wise (22a): self-rule, Gen 3:5-6.
- Is foolish (22b-23): "likeness of an image" layers of copies; man already image of God (Gen 1:27); original for copy of copy. Incorruptible for corruptible (Isa 40:7-8): diamond for a peach, gold for rusting bolt. "Image is everything" vs glory of God is everything. Seward's folly (Alaska 1867). Phil 3:8.

## the-other-dark-exchange-homosexuality (Part 1, 1998-10-11, 1:24-28)
- Pattern three times: exchange (23, 25, 28), giving over (24, 26, 28), bodily result.
- Truth = God glorious and most desirable; lie = creature more desirable.
- "Unnatural": stock phrase in Greek ethical writing for homosexual behavior itself. Rejects reading that Paul condemns only heterosexuals acting against their nature (three reasons: "burned"; bisexual reading would make both natural; structure of passage).
- Sexual disorder one of many judgments on humanity (5:15-18; 8:20-23), not singled out. Manhood and womanhood picture Christ and church (Eph 5:31-32). 1 Cor 6:11 washed.
- Tone: conviction and compassion; church of justified sinners; goal: overcome or live joyful celibate life.
- Did not treat "due penalty" (v27) per WebFetch.

## the-other-dark-exchange-homosexuality-part-2 (1998-10-18, 1:24-28)
- Exchanges in 23, 25, 28 each followed by handing over; souls and societies disintegrate. God's glory central like the sun.
- Universality (3:9-10); Matthew Shepard murder from same root.
- Creation subjected to futility in hope (8:18-23); 2 Cor 1:8-9.
- Salvation in stages (5:9-10): forgiveness now, freedom later. 8:28. Only a justified sinner can fight sin.
- Physical/genetic origin does not make it natural (compare other inborn tendencies); a higher norm than fallen nature.
- Counsel to those with same-sex desire (identity not defined by it; faith in Christ alone; fight sin from relationship not for it; chastity; friendships) and to parents (confess own failures; love and pray). Closing: speak truth in love.

## the-perils-of-disapproving-god (1998-11-08, 1:28-32; page expounds 28-31)
- v28 literal: they did not approve to have God in their knowledge. Four steps: root (not wanting God), judgment (removal of restraints, Gen 20:6), defective mind, list of evils.
- Same Greek verb "handed over" in 1:28 and 6:17: reversal.
- List of 21 vices = samples; not every unbeliever has all; Stoics (Seneca, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius) decent. Images: river from spring, swamp, jumping off the rock of God's glory.
- Three reversals: wrath reversed by gift of righteousness (1:17), handed over to truth (6:17), renewal of mind (12:2).

## doing-and-endorsing-evil (1998-11-15, 1:28-32, esp. 32)
- Bottom of spiral: not only doing but approving others; spiritual "Kevorkians". Lust as soul-suicide (Matt 5:27-29; 1 Pet 2:11); 1997 pornography statistics.
- Three observations: all know God and some of his moral demands (18-19, 32); without excuse in treatment of others (20-21, 28, 32), being handed over does not reduce guilt; moral knowledge deeper than consciousness, suppression quick (18).
- Evangelism: nobody a blank slate; Charles Hodge quoted that even the worst sinner knows he deserves wrath; Jewish and Muslim missions. Objections (Luke 13:1-5; Job; the cross; Hurricane Mitch). Be bold.
