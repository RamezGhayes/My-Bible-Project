# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 18
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-18/ (three WebFetch passes: overview, vv1-27 detail, vv28-40 detail; where passes disagreed on who said what, we left the name out). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): Kidron carried water from the altar, red with Passover blood (Barclay, Spurgeon); John does not name Gethsemane (Matt 26:36; Mark 14:32); Luke 21:37 nights on Olivet; Morris: regular meeting place; speira = cohort of the Antonia, about 600 (Barclay); hyperetai = temple guards, Levites; torches though full Passover moon, expecting to search (Clarke); Matt 26:55; second Adam in a garden where the first failed.
A2 (4-6): knew everything, took initiative; accepted "Nazarene" (humble label); ego eimi echoes divine name in LXX Exod 3:14 (Tasker); Morris: came for a fugitive, met divine authority; fell back; Trapp: small glimpse of his glory; could have escaped; paradoxes of humility and glory (manger and star).
A3 (7-9): gives himself up, protects disciples (Clarke, Tenney); fulfils 6:39; 17:12.
A4 (10-12): only John names Peter and Malchus; two swords (Luke 22:38); right ear: probably struck from behind; Bruce: Peter's act would have hindered the mission; cup from the Father; chiliarchos = Roman commander (Tenney); bound like a criminal, bonds freely accepted (Trapp, Spurgeon).
B1 (13-14): Annas not the official high priest, father-in-law of Caiaphas; Barclay on family power (sons as high priests); Talmud critical of his house; Caiaphas' counsel (11:49-53) shows verdict decided; Morris: corrupt politician.
B2 (15-16): other disciple usually John, known to the high priest (Tenney: family or business; Trapp: fish trade); brings Peter in.
B3 (17-18): first denial at the gate (Alford: hasty, shame); cold, charcoal fire, blending in; John's verb "standing" (Trench).
B4 (19-21): questioned about disciples and teaching; Spurgeon: Jesus shielded his disciples in his answer; taught openly (Trapp: truth bold, heresy hides); Bruce: legal duty to hear witnesses first; Clarke cites Talmud (Sanhedrin 4.1): capital cases by day, guilty verdict waits to next day.
B5 (22-24): slap (Alford: foretaste of worse); "if I spoke evil, testify"; sent bound to Caiaphas.
B6 (25-27): second and third denials, relative of Malchus; rooster (13:38); Alford: faith did not fail, courage did.
C1 (28): praetorium, probably Antonia but uncertain (Morris: Philo and Josephus on Herod's palace); Passover timing: John suggests the meal still ahead, discussion of harmonisation with Synoptics; hypocrisy of ritual scruple while condemning the innocent (Trapp; Matt 23:24).
C2 (29-32): evasive "evildoer"; right of execution: Clarke (lost after Archelaus) vs Alford citing Josephus; Stephen stoned (Acts 7:54-60); crucifixion fulfils "lifted up" (3:14; 12:32-33) and curse of Deut 21:22-23 / Gal 3:13; Luke 23:2 charges.
C3 (33-35): "Are you the king of the Jews?"; Pilate had seen other would-be kings; contempt "Am I a Jew?"; Pilate's character: Boice (married into Augustus' family), Philo via Barclay (cruelty), Maclaren (weak man hiding weakness in stubbornness and violence), Luke 13:1; Herod (Luke 23:6-12).
C4 (36): kingdom not of this world; love, sacrifice, humility vs force; 1 Cor 1:23.
C5 (37-38): emphatic "I", evidence of incarnation (Alford); Tenney: asks for recognition of truth, not mercy; 1 Tim 6:13 good confession; Pilate's "what is truth" dismissive (truth = Rome's power); modern relativism; no fault.
C6 (39-40): release custom not confirmed elsewhere, Josephus records a similar release by Albinus; Matt 27:20 priests persuaded crowd; Barabbas robber (lestes), Mark 15:7 rebel and murderer, terrorist to Rome, freedom fighter to some; bar-abba "son of the father" vs the true Son; we are Barabbas, freed while the cross fell on Jesus (Spurgeon, Trapp).
Second, exhaustive read 2026-11-27 (WebFetch passes vv1-14 and vv15-27; vv28-40 from the earlier detailed pass). Added: Bruce: did not hide; Clarke: John omits the agony; slept under olive trees or caves; cohort sizes 600/1000/200 (Barclay); Luke 22:3 devil's representative; first Adam vs second Adam; v4 two reasons for initiative; v5 "he" added by translators, Trapp: name given in contempt worn as a crown; v6 Trapp ~500 men, Clarke, Alford (intended miracle?), Maclaren, list of paradoxes; v8 repeated ego eimi, no one fell: not magic; Clarke: command not request; v10 Calvin via Morris (tongue not sword), struck from behind, non-soldier, Malchus perhaps later a Christian; v12 Trapp citing Irenaeus; v13 Annas about nine years, four sons and Caiaphas (Barclay); Spurgeon on Annas' hostility; v14 logically vs morally; v17 "also" shows she knew John (Bruce), "this man" dismissive (Tasker), Trapp; v19 Spurgeon: Annas' name means mercy; v21 Talmud: no sentence on eve of Sabbath/feast; v22 officer's name withheld in mercy (Luke 23:34); v25 Luke 22:61, Peter likely saw the slap; v26 Alford: emphatic "with my own eyes".
