# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Luke 5
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/luke-5/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page arabic.enduringword.com/ar/luke-5-ar/ redirects to the general Arabic index; no Arabic Luke 5 page found.)
A1 (1-3): crowd presses to hear word of God; Gennesaret = Sea of Galilee/Tiberias; Simon honored; Morrison: "many strange pulpits" of Christ.
A2 (4-5): Jesus repays Simon's kindness; likely stayed in the boat (Spurgeon: His approving presence encourages workers). epistata only in Luke = commander/boss. Excuses Peter could have made (tired, expert, night better, nets washed). "At Your word" linked to creation and upholding the universe.
A3 (6-7): obedience, not conditions, brought the catch; Trapp: the fish preach Christ as Son of God. Partners needed: James McGready's Kentucky revival calling helpers. Clarke: pride/envy refusing to share the work hinders God's work.
A4 (8-11): Peter had seen mother-in-law healed (4:38-39) but now worships; "depart" shows sense of power and sin; better prayer "come nearer". "Do not fear": God wants love not cowering dread. Clarke: "catch" = take alive. Followed as rabbi's apprentices. Pate: "follow" Luke's technical term for discipleship, linked to "the Way" in Acts (Luke 9:23, 57-61; 18:22, 28; Acts 9:2; 19:9, 23; 22:4; 24:14, 22).
B1 (12): full of leprosy; Barclay: two forms; distance customs; only corpse more defiling; leprosy pictures sin; came without precedent, promise, invitation; doubted willingness not power; sought cleansing; Barclay quoting MacDonald on shame.
B2 (13): touched the untouchable; holiness cleanses rather than is defiled (Meyer); "I am willing": people doubt God's love more than His power.
B3 (14): silence to restrain crowds before appointed revelation (Dan 9); Lev 14 offerings (Clarke details birds, cedar, scarlet, hyssop); restored to society.
B4 (15-16): man disobeyed (Mark 1:44-45); irony: told to be silent he spoke, we told to speak are silent. Isa 35:5-6; sickness and Deut 28 covenant curses. Withdrew to pray; Barclay; Clarke: ministers need renewed strength in private prayer.
C1 (17-19): Pharisees from Galilee, Judea, Jerusalem (Mark 2:1); "separated ones"; Spurgeon: critical hearers may yet be reached. Power of the Lord present (Matt 13:58), following prayer in 5:16. Roof of thatch/tile. Spurgeon on the friends' resolve.
C2 (20-22): saw their faith (visible faith, intercessory); deepest need sin: healed legs worthless if he goes to hell; no claim the illness came from a specific sin. Leaders right that only God forgives, wrong about Jesus. Cole: divine or blasphemer. Morgan: assurance rests on God's grace.
C3 (23-26): both easy for God; visible healing proves invisible authority. Son of Man = Dan 7:13-14; Robertson: favorite self-title, messianic yet hard to attack. Tension in the room, only Jesus at peace. Amazement, glorifying God, fear.
D1 (27-28): Levi = Matthew (Matt 9:9); tax collectors traitors and extortioners; Lane: outcasts barred from testifying; Barclay: Roman monument to an honest tax collector; Wessel: sought-after jobs; fish taxed; Morris: considerable sacrifice, likely richest apostle; harder to return than fishermen.
D2 (29-32): feast for friends to meet Jesus; indirect attack via disciples; Morgan: "among men as the great Physician"; critics also sick; reasons a sick man refuses a doctor; Jesus the perfect doctor who pays the fee.
D3 (33-39): wedding = joy; Pate: rabbinic Scroll of Fasting forbade fasting on days of joy; "taken away" early hint of rejection. Garment: Geldenhuys (patch disfigures new, tears old). Wineskins: not reforming rabbinic Judaism but a new institution, the church uniting Jew and Gentile (Eph 2:16). v39: people prefer old because familiar; neither old nor new is a test; Jesus fulfils the law like acorn to oak.
