# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 15
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-15/ . Our own outline, not his text. Arabic URL arabic.enduringword.com/ar/romans-15-ar-2/ redirects to an index page.
A1 (1-2): bear up, not tolerate; counter-cultural; please neighbor for good, not people-pleasing (Phil 2:3-4); Morris: lead weak to strength; tearing down satanic strategy.
A2 (3-4): Christ pleased not Himself (Phil 2:5-11); Ps 69:9; trust Father to vindicate; Scripture for hope; reproach unsettles world.
A3 (5-6): prayer, Spirit's work; God's patience, delays not denials; patience with plan; one mind, one mouth.
A4 (7-13): receive as Christ received; Spurgeon on loving condescension; servant to circumcision; OT chain: v.9 Ps 18; v.12 Isaiah; v.13 joy, peace, hope.
B1 (14-16): confident, reminding; priestly language; Murray on word "acting as a priest".
B2 (17-19): glory in Christ's work (Morris); signs, Spirit, "fully"; Jerusalem to Illyricum; Trinity.
B3 (20-21): pioneer, not wrong to build on others; v.21 quoted (source not named on page).
C1 (22-24): Spain; Rome as western base; came as prisoner; likely reached Spain.
C2 (25-29): collection (Acts 20:1-3); spiritual/material debt.
C3 (30-33): sunagonizomai only here, Gethsemane; Jerusalem church conservative; answered unexpectedly (Acts 20:22-23; 21:10-14, 27-28; 22:22; 28:15); Chuck Smith; Spurgeon quoting Carey to Fuller (hold the rope).
