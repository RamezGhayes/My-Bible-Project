# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Mark 15
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/mark-15/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-5): Lane: early Roman courts; no right to execute (but Stephen, Acts 7:57-60); fear of politics; Roman trial procedure; charge shifted to "king of the Jews" (Luke 23:2, 5); Bruce: Pilate not serious; Lane: silence and dignity.
A2 (6-15): Pilate knew innocence (Luke 23:14); envy; Barabbas "son of the father"; Robertson: not the triumphal-entry crowd; scourging; William Edwards (JAMA 1986) medical description.
B1 (16-20): purple; Wessel: faded military cloak; mock salute; reed (Matt 27:29); spitting; Spurgeon on robe and crown; procession, Mark 8:34.
B2 (21-23): crossbeam; Simon of Cyrene; Spurgeon: Simon present when Simon Peter absent; Rufus Rom 16:13; Bruce, Morrison on "brought him"; Golgotha site debate; wine with myrrh (Prov 31:6-7, printed garbled), Lane, Cole, Spurgeon.
B3 (24-26): Ps 22:18; Lane on nakedness (Mishnah); Edwards on crucifixion physiology; Clarke on "excruciating"; Cicero, Tacitus; third vs sixth hour; Lane on title; Wiersbe on thief's hope.
B4 (27-32): numbered with transgressors; mockers; Robertson (page says Robinson) on "mocking"; Luke 23:39-43; Acts 6:7; Morgan on "he saved others".
B5 (33-37): Geldenhuys; Phlegon (also Clarke); Luke 23:45; no eclipse at full moon; Ps 22; 2 Cor 5:21, Isa 53:10, 2 Cor 5:19; Cole on sour wine; Elijah misunderstanding; "It is finished" paid in full (John 19:30); cup Luke 22:39-46, Ps 75:8, Isa 51:17, Jer 25:15; Clarke; Rom 5:8; 1 Cor 1:18; Col 2:14.
B6 (38-41): veil torn from above; centurion, John 12:32; women.
B7 (42-47): Joseph; Geldenhuys on quiet disciples; Spurgeon on courage; Lane on risk; Cole on centurion; John 19:31-36; borrowed tomb.
