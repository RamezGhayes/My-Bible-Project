# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Galatians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/galatians-1/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page not found by search.)
A1 (1-2): authorship undisputed; "Declaration of Independence" of Christian liberty; Luther "married to it"; Leon Morris: passionate letter; dated about AD 50 before Acts 15; Wuest: apostle = appointed representative with credentials; not from men nor through man; Robertson: bluntness answers charge he was not of the Twelve; Luther: holy pride; Galatia a region, Gauls settled 3rd c. BC; Morris: north (Pessinus, Ancyra, Tavium) vs south (Pisidian Antioch, Iconium, Lystra, Derbe), debate unimportant; Acts 13:13-14:23; 16:6; 18:23.
A2 (3-5): grace (Greek) and peace (Jewish); Paul uses "grace" 100+ times; Luther: these two words comprise Christianity; gave Himself (John 3:16); Calvin: atonement and righteousness only in Christ; Morris: cross in first sentence; deliver from power of evil age, not yet presence; salvation's purpose God's glory.
B1 (6): marvel; no thanksgiving (Rom 1:8-15; 1 Cor 1:4-9; Phil 1:3-11; Col 1:3-8; 1 Thess 1:2-10); Lightfoot: only such letter; turning from a Person.
B2 (7): illegitimate, troubling, distortion; "different" vs "another" two Greek words; troublers possibly sincere; Luther: devil as angel of light; gospel offends pride, wisdom, knowledge; 1 Cor 15:1-4.
B3 (8-9): even we or angel; accursed, background Deut 27 covenant curses; repetition; love for souls, sinking rescue ship.
C1 (10): God his audience; rivals built message on pleasing men; Morris on popularity; bondservant = slave, Duncan via Morris.
C2 (11-12): wordplay on gospel (Morris); revelation not speculation (2 Pet 1:3); most hear from others (Rom 10:14-15), Paul directly (Acts 9:1-9, 20-22); Luther: taught by Christ, Ananias confirmed.
C3 (13-24): testimony; persecuted (Acts 8:1-3; 9:1-2); new-revelation seekers deceived (Joseph Smith); called by grace before birth, Calvin: secret election; before conversion his actions, after God's; aphorizo related to "Pharisee" (Phil 3:5); revelation to (v12) and in (v16), Cole via Morris; Gentile-hater sent to Gentiles; Arabia near Damascus; three years; "to see" Peter = visit famous cities (Chrysostom via Lightfoot); Judean churches glorified God; content in obscurity; enough contact to show agreement, not dependence.
