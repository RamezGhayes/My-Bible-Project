# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Mark 13
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/mark-13/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): Herod's expansion of Zerubbabel/Ezra temple (Ezra 6:15); huge stones; Josephus: gold and marble like snow; fulfilled AD 70 (fire, stones dismantled for gold); literal fulfillment -> expect literal fulfillment of the rest.
A2 (3-4): four disciples on Olivet across Kidron; two questions; Luke 21 parallel; concern for Israel.
B1 (5-8): false messiahs, wars etc. = beginning of birth pains, not the end; Morgan: Lord did not foresee gradual end of conflict or peace by gospel alone.
B2 (9-13): councils, floggings, rulers; gospel to all nations first; Spirit gives words; Robertson: no excuse for lazy preacher; modern persecution examples (Barrett stats, Open Doors); hupomeno = remain under.
C1 (14a): abomination from Daniel (11:31; 12:11 printed as 11:12); Antiochus, Barclay (pig, Zeus statue); Caligula plan AD 40; Wuest, Lane on the phrase; Matt 24:15; 2 Thess 2:3-4; Irenaeus: Antichrist 3.5 years in Jerusalem temple; 1948, Temple Institute; F.F. Bruce on Josephus.
C2 (14b-18): flee; believers fled c. AD 66 but tribulation still future; 1 Thess 4:16-18, Luke 21:36, Rev 3:10, Rev 12:13-17.
C3 (19-23): unprecedented distress; days shortened for elect; Brzezinski casualty figures; Black Death; application: expectancy purifies, urgency, boldness, detachment.
C4 (24-27): cosmic signs; creation groans (Rom 8:22); prophets of day of the Lord (refs garbled in Arabic page); elect = tribulation believers.
C5 (28-31): fig tree (Song 2:11-13); Lane on fig tree; Dan 12:11 timeline; "this generation" = generation seeing signs or Jewish people; v31 claim to deity.
C6 (32-37): Son voluntarily limited knowledge; two errors (indifference, date-setting); Luke 21:36; house = church, authority, each his work; Wiersbe: warning to all believers today.
Note: Guzik is futurist/pretribulational; neutral notes added in voices.
