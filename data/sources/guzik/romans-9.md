# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 9
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-9/ . Our own outline, not his text. No Arabic page found in search.
A1: intro, chapter shift (no range).
A2 (1-2): grief seems out of step with Rom 8 assurance; grief for unbelieving Israel cut off from God's love; strong oaths show seriousness.
A3 (3-5): wish to be accursed sincere; Moses Exod 32:31-32; Jesus cursed (Gal 3:13); Jews his chief opponents yet loved; Spurgeon "get love for the souls of men"; Bengel via Newell. Privileges: adoption, glory = Shekinah cloud, covenants, law, service, promises, fathers, Christ. 9:5 clear statement of deity; Morris grammar favors, many recent scholars disagree; Wuest quoting Robertson natural reading.
B1 (6-9): word not failed; Israel = "governed by God"; Chuck Smith parallel "Christian"; Isaac child of promise vs Ishmael child of flesh.
B2 (10-13): chosen before birth, not of works; ties to "heir of God's covenant of salvation"; Esau rejected re inheriting covenant; Esau blessed (Gen 33:8-16; 36); Morris "hate" = loved less (Gen 29:31, 33; Deut 21:15; Matt 6:24; Luke 14:26; John 12:25); Morris agrees with Calvin "accepted/rejected"; Spurgeon via Newell (wonder God loved Jacob); choices not capricious.
B3 (14-16): Certainly not; God never less than fair; mercy by definition not deserved; Exod 33:19; Matt 20:1-16.
B4 (17-18): raised up to show power and name; Pharaoh hardened own heart (Exod 7:13, 22; 8:15, 19, 32; 9:7, 34); God allowed natural inclination; sometimes God glorifies Himself through a man's hardness; Harrison "freedom of God's action in all cases".
B5 (19-21): objector rebuked as disrespectful; potter and clay; "If God says He chooses, and ... responsible, who are we to question Him?"
B6 (22-24): God glorifies Himself in judgment or mercy; "Paul does not say that God has prepared them for destruction"; "do an adequate job on their own"; mercy vessels prepared beforehand; Morris; mercy to Gentiles.
B7 (25-26): Hosea 2:23; 1:10; Lo-Ammi; judgment not lasting; Israel restored, sons of living God.
B8 (27-29): Isa 10:22-23 remnant from Assyria; "He will always preserve His remnant"; Morris promise not to whole nation; Isa 1:9; Bruce remnant as hope of restoration.
C1 (30-31): Gentiles attained by faith; Israel pursued law of righteousness.
C2 (32-33): responsibility on Israel; other side of the coin, not contradiction; 1 Cor 1:22-23.
