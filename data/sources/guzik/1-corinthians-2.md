# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 2
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-4): no eloquence; witness; Christ crucified; Acts 18; Barclay (Corinth prized show and false eloquence); Fee (one passion, not rejection of all knowledge); causes of fear: illness, persecution (Calvin), circumstances (Vincent); Morgan: sense of weakness secret of power; Spurgeon: speak truth boldly; Acts 26 persuasion acceptable.
A2 (5): faith on God's power; entertainment methods; what draws them holds them; faith built on wisdom lost by wisdom.
B1 (6-8): wisdom not of this age; mature believers (Eph 4:13; 1 Cor 14:20; Phil 3:15); rulers: human or demonic, Origen and Chrysostom named; Morris; Col 2:15.
B2 (9-11): loose rewording (page misprints Isa 64:4 ref); Hodge "as it is written"; not only heaven; Clarke; Spirit alone knows God's thoughts; deep things; Eph 1:3-7; 3:5.
B3 (12-13): Spirit from God; basics understood by all believers; comparing spiritual with spiritual; Clarke alternative rendering.
B4 (14-16): psuchikos, inherited from Adam, unregenerate; believers can think like natural man; spiritual judges all, not beyond criticism; Isa 40:13 applied to Jesus.
