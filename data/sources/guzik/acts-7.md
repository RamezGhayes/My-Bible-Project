# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 7
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-7/ (four fetches: outline; vv2-50 detail; headings of section 3; vv1-16 and 51-60 detail). Our own outline, not his text. Heading of C1 on the page is the verse text plus a phrase about conviction by the Spirit.
A1 (1): Caiaphas (Matt 26:57); charges 6:11-14; panoramic survey, God not confined, pattern of rejecting messengers; Lenski (refutes every charge without self-defense); Boice (old system passing, prepares ch 8); Bruce (defense of faith, not acquittal).
A2 (2-5): God of glory in Mesopotamia, God greater than temple/land; Stott: God not confined, house among his people; Boice: not from Zion but in full glory; Abraham delayed in Haran; Gal 3:7; grew in faith; no foot of land, no child; faith not tangible proof; stranger, did not worship blessings.
A3 (6-8): 400 years, judgment on oppressor; God's protection; challenge to council; circumcision covenant through Isaac, Jacob, twelve. No discussion of the 400 figure.
A4 (9-16): God with Joseph without temple; Joseph type of Jesus, the only savior; famine, second visit; 75 vs 70 (Gen 46:27): Septuagint counts five born in Egypt; Shechem tomb only land Abraham owned (Hebron issue not addressed).
B1 (17-22): new king, infants cast out; Moses "fair" = favored by God, like Jesus; mighty in words and deeds like Jesus.
B2 (23-29): came down from royal position (parallel); "did not understand"; who made you ruler: council rejected Jesus' right to rule/judge; fled to Midian.
B3 (30-34): bush before any temple; mission not only call; holy ground; seen affliction.
B4 (35-36): rejection doesn't cancel God's choice; signs 40 years; consider accepting Jesus.
B5 (37-41): prophet like Moses; church in wilderness (ekklesia) led by Moses as Jesus leads church; living oracles; golden calf, works of hands; council worshipped temple instead of God of temple; Stephen not against temple itself.
B6 (42-43): host of heaven; Amos 5:25-27 Damascus -> Babylon; Boice: southern kingdom leaders; Rom 1:24-32; Moloch, Rephan; to whom do people surrender?
B7 (44-50): tabernacle by pattern, Joshua, David; tabernacle/temple didn't stop rejection; Isa 66:1-2; people confine God to church buildings.
B8 (51-53): Ex 32:9; Jer 9:26; Deut 10:16; stiff-necked ~20 times in OT; Spurgeon: words cut like blade, bold expecting death; circumcision/nations; Phil 3:6; Matt 9:17; gospel outward, "come to me" vs "I will come to you". (LaSor remark at v60 about Jews not used.)
C1 (54): rage in heart, couldn't ignore or accept; gnashing (Matt 8:12, page prints 12:8); Gaebelein: anger long building; religious respectability vs relationship.
C2 (55-56): fullness source of courage; J.B. Phillips; caution on what he saw; standing (Matt 26:64; Col 3:1): solidarity, welcome, defend (Matt 10:32); Bruce: confessed/confesses.
C3 (57-58): hormao = pigs (Mark 5:13); Matt 26:64-66; Bruce: crucified one at right hand seemed blasphemy; outside city, disregard Roman law; Mishnah procedure; John 16:2-3; Saul young but had vote (26:10).
C4 (59-60): ending like his life; Morgan: persecution reveals martyrs; Luke 23:34; Col 1:24; Augustine on Paul; "fell asleep"; not superman but Spirit-filled.
