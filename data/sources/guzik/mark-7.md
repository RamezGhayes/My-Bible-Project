# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 7
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-7/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No readable Arabic page.)
A1 (1-5): Jerusalem delegation (cf. Mark 3:22) already decided, judged by tradition not Word. Ceremonial washing with prayer; from priestly washings (Exod 30:19; 40:12; Lane). Oral law honored above written: Rabbi Eleazer (forfeit world to come); Wiersbe from Mishna (contradicting rabbis worse than Scripture); Trapp, Rabbi Jose (unwashed eating like harlotry); Morgan (interpretations of interpretations); Lane (Jesus' refusal of oral law drew attack). Stone jars, pouring; rabbi in prison spent water on ritual. Ps 24:3-4 reasoning seems spiritual; giving tradition God's weight is the error.
A2 (6-9): hypocrite = actor in mask; lips vs heart applies to modern churchgoers. Pillar 1 of legalism: adding human commands as doctrine; matters of conscience. Pillar 2: setting aside God's command. Ironside: people defend traditions fiercely and ignore plain Scripture.
A3 (10-13): Moses: honor parents (honor remains after obedience ends); Corban vow; disobedience disguised as devotion; word made of none effect.
A4 (14-16): nothing from outside defiles: ceremonial food laws; not a denial that e.g. pornography harms; anticipates New Covenant (Acts 10:15). Barclay: among most revolutionary passages in NT.
A5 (17-23): food passes to stomach not heart. Thirteen evils; Spurgeon: sin a filth generated within; devil preached on this text. Barclay: every outward sin begins inwardly (evil thoughts first); blasphemy toward men = slander, toward God = insult.
B1 (24-26): ~50 miles north; focus on lost sheep of Israel (Matt 15:24); entered Gentile house against tradition (Barclay). Discretion; "could not be hid". Woman a model intercessor, makes daughter's need her own.
B2 (27-30): children first; "little dogs" diminutive (Barclay: took the sting out). She accepts her place, asks on her own level. Ironside: nothing appealed more than faith with humility. Clarke: nine features of her prayer. Daughter found healed.
B3 (31-37): friends intercede. Aside, fingers, spittle, sigh, Ephphatha. Lane: entered man's mental world, gained confidence. Morgan: method adapted; cost, "man of sorrows". Varied methods so power not tied to a method. Wiersbe: sigh = inward groan, prayer (Rom 8:23, 26). mogilalos only here; LXX Isa 35:5-6 (Lane): Messiah present. "He has done all things well": creation and redemption.
