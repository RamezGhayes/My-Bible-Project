# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Matthew 12
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/matthew-12/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): plucking grain allowed by Deut 23:25; the issue was the day; four rabbinic violations (reaping, threshing, winnowing, preparing food); Spurgeon on poverty of Jesus' band; Clarke on Sabbath rigor, refusing self-defence in wars; Carson: Sabbath rules like mountains hanging by a hair; Pharisees' spying the real breach; Matt 11:28-30.
A2 (3-8): three principles: need over ritual (David, 1 Sam 21:6; Spurgeon necessity vs wilful intrusion); priests work on Sabbath, doubled sacrifices (Num 28:9; Barclay); greater than temple, Lord of Sabbath = implicit deity; Hos 6:6 mercy; Poole: when two laws conflict obey the higher.
A3 (9-14): Jesus still attends synagogue (Spurgeon); Clarke: man came to worship and found mercy; question to accuse; France: not life-threatening, Mishnah Yoma 8:6; sheep in pit; Bruce: stretching and healing simultaneous; Poole: no laying on of hands; Bruce: objection to plot; Luke 6:11.
A4 (15-21): withdrawal by Father's timing, not fear; healed "all"; Isa 42:1-4 chosen servant; Matt 20:25-28; Acts 3:13, 26; 4:27, 30; does not quarrel; bruised reed, smoking flax; Eph 3:16; Gentiles' hope surprising to Jewish readers.
B1 (22-24): blind mute demoniac; Son of David messianic; Beelzebul; France: charge of sorcery persisted in later Jewish polemic; Trapp.
B2 (25-29): knew thoughts possibly word of knowledge (1 Cor 12:8); divided kingdom; Poole, Bruce (Satan evil not stupid), Spurgeon (envy; honoured the Spirit); your sons; kingdom come; strong man bound, Barclay.
B3 (30-32): no neutrality, Morgan gathering/scattering; blasphemy = full, final rejection of Spirit's witness to Jesus (John 15:26); settled attitude, not a single phrase; worried person has not committed it; Clarke: believer cannot commit it; not forgiven in this age or the next; leaders near it.
B4 (33-37): fruit reveals tree, words reveal heart; brood of vipers = children of the serpent; Trapp on idle vs evil words; Clarke: idle word conveys no grace or teaching, condemns many preachers; Rom 10:9.
C1 (38-40): insincere request; Bruce: mock respect like soldiers (Matt 27:27-31); Poole: had enough signs; sign of Jonah = resurrection, Jonah gave his life to calm wrath; Eleazar ben Azariah: part of day counts as day; Barclay: Jesus is God's sign.
C2 (41-42): Nineveh and queen of the South; Clarke: greater than Jonah in preaching length and miracles; greater than Solomon; more light, more judgment.
C3 (43-45): unclean spirit returns; applied to that generation, ruin AD 70; Clarke: possession real; emptiness danger; Spurgeon: Satan content with swept house; Poole: Satan restless when not harming; Trapp: Satan rattles chain but cannot bite one full of Jesus; filled with Spirit.
C4 (46-50): family came perhaps fearing; Spurgeon: Pharisees may have persuaded them; Mary no special privilege; brothers sons of Mary and Joseph (Carson), against perpetual virginity; John 7:5; Spurgeon: not ashamed to call them brothers; Clarke: honours believers as his mother; invitation open even to opponents.
