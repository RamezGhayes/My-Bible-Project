# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Matthew 2
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/matthew-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): Bethlehem small (Barclay: Ephrathah); Herod capable but cruel, birth before 4 BC (France); magi astrologers not kings, maybe more than three, months later (Carson: magoi loose term; Tertullian tradition from Ps 72:10-11, Isa 60:6, Ps 68:29, Isa 49:7); Clarke: Jews of exile; Tacitus, Suetonius expectation; France: magi to Nero AD 66; star = God speaking in their language, Num 24:17; traditional names not biblical; Spurgeon: stir.
A2 (3): Herod Edomite, killed family (Barclay list); Spurgeon: gifts and plots together.
A3 (4-6): chief priests political; scribes teachers (Carson); Micah 5:2; no desire to go; Trapp: shepherds.
A4 (7-8): when star appeared -> two years; Spurgeon: theology did not change Herod; magi did not promise.
A5 (9-12): star supernatural (Spurgeon pillar Ex 13:21; Clarke halo; France "came and stood"); child 6-18 months; Trapp Joseph absent; gifts to child, Meyer gold for Egypt; seek/worship/give; vs shepherds: Jews then Gentiles, humble then learned; dream warning.
B1 (13-15): urgent flight; humanity's reaction is to kill God; Joseph at night; Carson: Egypt near, outside Herod, Philo one million Jews; Poole: place/length unknown; Hos 11:1.
B2 (16-18): not in secular record but fits Herod; Carson dozens; Bruce minor in Herod's record; mourners order; Jer 31:15, Poole fulfilled twice, Bruce Rachel mother of Israel.
B3 (19-21): dream again, obedience; child first; no Egyptian magic influence.
B4 (22-23): Archelaus cruel (Bruce), removed AD 6; Poole Antipas milder; Schanz/Augustine Jerusalem; Bruce Galilee training; Nazareth obscure, John 1:46; Nazarene: Num 6 Nazirite, netser Isa 11:1 (Bruce/Jerome), theme of despised Messiah (France; Spurgeon Isa 53:2); contempt Acts 24:5; Poole.
