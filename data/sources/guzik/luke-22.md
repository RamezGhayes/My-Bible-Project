# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 22
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-22/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-6): Passover crowds (Matt 26:14-16); leaders feared people more than God; Satan entered Judas yet Judas fully responsible; motives discussed (Kerioth, Sicarii) and noble motives rejected: greed and disappointment at a non-political Messiah; Judas set his own price; Satan not all-knowing, deceived about the cross.
A2 (7-13): Peter and John; man with water jar as secret sign so the meal could happen before Judas acted; chronology tension with John 13:1; 18:28 (Morris, Clarke, different calendars); no lamb mentioned, perhaps because Jesus presented Himself as the Passover sacrifice.
A3 (14-18): eager desire before suffering; four Passover cups; cup before and after bread (22:20); not drink until kingdom: marriage supper of the Lamb (Rev 19:9).
A4 (19-20): Passover foods reinterpreted around Himself; Exod 24:8; Jer 31:31-34; bread and cup "not merely symbols" but strong images of fellowship; remember broken body and shed blood; surveys transubstantiation, Luther (insisted on "is" at Marburg), Calvin (real but spiritual presence), Zwingli (symbols); Poole: figurative; Carson: remember His death, not birth or miracles.
A5 (21-23): betrayer's hand on table (Ps 41:9); determined yet responsible; Morris: God's use of evil does not make it less evil; disciples did not suspect Judas.
Part II intro: read with John 13-16.
B1 (24-27): dispute over greatness (Matt 20:20-26; Mark 9:33-34); rulers vs servants; "as one who serves" (John 13:3-5 foot washing); Morgan, Spurgeon on greatness through service.
B2 (28-30): continued with Him in trials; table and thrones judging twelve tribes; Rev 21:14; great servants get great reward but serve for God's glory.
B3 (31-34): Satan asked to sift; Jesus prayed; faith stumbles but not dies; strengthen brothers; Peter's confidence based on feelings; threefold denial; Meyer, Pate.
B4 (35-38): purse, bag, sword now; Isa 53:12; "it is enough" ends discussion; Pate: two swords not meant as sufficient; Boniface VIII's two-swords misuse (Geldenhuys).
C1 (39-42): as usual to Mount of Olives; pray not to enter temptation; cup = God's wrath (Ps 75:8; Isa 51:17; Jer 25:15); Spurgeon; not a victim but voluntary; Adam failed in a garden, Jesus won in a garden.
C2 (43-44): angel strengthened Him; some manuscripts lack 43-44; sweat like blood, possibly medical (Galen, Clarke); Trapp: His fervent prayer covers our dull prayers.
C3 (45-46): sleeping from sorrow; rise and pray; He cares for them though they fail.
D1 (47-53): crowd incl. Roman soldiers (John 18:3, 12) and temple guard; kiss a customary greeting used as signal; right ear (John 18:10), noted by Luke the physician; healed ear, stopped violence; hour of darkness; Maclaren on church misusing the sword.
D2 (54-60): high priest's house; followed at a distance; fire; servant girl; three denials with cursing (Matt 26:74); rooster.
D3 (61-62): the Lord looked; Peter remembered and wept; faith did not fail as Jesus prayed (22:32); Liefeld on the Greek word for "look".
D4 (63-65): mocked, blindfolded, "prophesy"; human hostility to God; endured without retaliation; Pate: mockery ironically confirms He is a true prophet as Peter's denial just fulfilled His word.
D5 (66): daybreak formal council; first illegal night trial at Caiaphas' house, Annas (John 18:13); violated rules: daytime trials, no verdict on day of trial, none at Passover, independent witnesses; Barclay.
D6 (67-71): if I tell you, you will not believe; Son of Man at right hand of the power of God (Matt 26:64); "you say that I am"; no more witnesses needed; France: "power" a reverent way to avoid naming God.
