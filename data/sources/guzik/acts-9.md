# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 9 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-9/ (Arabic, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch, three per-section outlines).
A1 (1-2): last seen 8:3; Damascus ~210 km NE, ~6 days; self-righteous rage; Saul did not seek Jesus, Jesus sought him; apocryphal late-1st-c. description via Gaebelein (short, bald, bowlegged, brows, nose; at times face of angel), uncertain. Letters from high priest Caiaphas; Nov 1990 ossuary with Caiaphas name, bones of 60-year-old man. Phil 3:5-6; Gal 1:13-14. Phinehas (Num 25) as model of "zeal" stopping a plague of false religion. "The Way" first name, 5 times in Acts: faith is a way of life; large community in Damascus.
A2 (3-6): sudden, unusual, outside Damascus; 22:6 midday; 26:13 brighter than sun. Fell from terror not reverence; no horse in 9, 22, 26 (Clarke: painters). Boice: a short book spends space on one man's change. Bruce: rabbis held God no longer spoke directly, only bat kol (daughter of a voice); Saul heard the voice itself. Repeated name = strong feeling (Luke 10:41; Matt 23:37). Why persecute me: persecuting God, futility; sincere religious people can be worst persecutors. I am Jesus: Saul likely heard him / in Sanhedrin (Guzik's "likely"). Boice: proves Jesus alive and God. Two questions: who are you (Phil 3:10), what do you want me to do (personal submission); Gallup 1990 questions to God. Goads phrase and "Lord what will you" not in earliest text of 9:5-6, from 22:10; 26:14; goad = sharp stick for ox; Jesus farmer; kicking increases pain; conscience pricked since Stephen (7:57-60); "hard for you" = love. Trembling: meeting God not pleasant. Jesus appeared to Saul: 22:3-11; 26:12-18; 1 Cor 9:1; 15:8; Barnabas 9:27; Ananias 9:17. Eyes shut yet saw Jesus. Gospel from Jesus (Gal 1:11-12). Step-by-step guidance.
A3 (7-9): companions heard, saw no one; physically blind as he was spiritually blind; three days no food or drink, humiliating, overturned his ideas; dying to self before resurrection life.
B1 (10-12): Ananias ordinary disciple, not apostle/elder/deacon, so no one could say Paul's gospel came from men; "here I am" ideal; vision gentle vs Saul's confrontation. Specific details (Straight, Judas, Tarsus) for courage and confirmation. "Behold he prays": Saul never truly prayed before (mechanical, not through Jesus, not humble). Saul saw Ananias in a vision.
B2 (13-16): objection logical but advising God; reputation known; authority from chief priests. Chosen vessel: chosen before any merit; to Gentiles, kings, Israel; calling so great Ananias's doubt understandable; suffering: realistic, gave up privilege.
B3 (17-19): courage, pretenders in church; hands + "Brother Saul" = love by touch and word since blind; filled with Spirit: seems moment of new birth; spiritual + physical healing; broken in order to be filled. Lenski: not converted on the road; change began under law, gospel entered by faith in Damascus. Baptized: wanted union with Jesus and disciples; unsure who taught him (Pentecost 2:41 seen, or from Lord). Ate, strengthened: God cares for body and spirit; among disciples he had hunted. 1 Tim 1:13, 16 pattern. Applications: salvation God's work, our part response; God finds non-seekers; uses those who feel unimportant; brokenness must lead to filling.
C1 (20-22): synagogue custom let qualified man teach; Gamaliel; Son of God = deity claim (John 5:17-18; Matt 26:63-65); preaching Son = life and cross. Amazement; 2 Cor 5:17 lived before written. Increased strength; new believers good witnesses; 1 Tim 3:6 no office but service; proving Messiah from OT.
C2 (23-25): many days = Gal 1:13-18, Arabia, ~3 years; 2 Cor 11:32-33 Aretas, AD 37-39; conversion ~AD 34-36; basket: not triumphant, humble means; Boice: not his last escape, later beaten, jailed.
C3 (26-30): disciples feared (plot? long absence? want proof); Saul answered with extra love; Barnabas took him (love believes all things, cited by Guzik as 1 Cor 7:13 [=13:7]); Gal 1:18 fifteen days with Peter, James; gospel not from apostles but same; Hellenists; Caesarea, Tarsus; left Jerusalem as persecutor, left as disciple; Tarsus years (8-12) not wasted; Tarsus port, university; Williams citing Strabo 14.5.13: surpassed Athens, Alexandria; Stoic center.
C4 (31): peace not end of persecution; no record of Galilee church planting; Caiaphas replaced AD 37, Caligula same year, hostile, killed 4 years later; built up = numbers and faith; fear of Lord + comfort of Spirit; Pierson: paraklesis = Paraclete (John 16:14).
D1 (32-35): Peter goes out to cities (cf 5:16); Lydda ~55 km, Lod, airport; Aeneas 8 years; Jesus Christ heals you: Peter instrument; echoes Mark 2:10-12; Lydda and Sharon turned.
D2 (36-38): Tabitha = Dorcas = gazelle; good works, pure motives; "which she did" continual; washed, upper room; two men, unsure if before or after death.
D3 (39-42): widows show garments; believers did not expect resurrection; sent all out, knelt, Tabitha arise: Jairus's daughter (Mark 5:38-43; Luke 8:50-56); Peter once led Jesus from cross (Matt 16:22), now follows; first "saints" in Acts? (Guzik says first time; means set apart); many believed; James not spared (12:2), God's ways; revived not resurrected, will die again; restored to serve; John 5:24.
D4 (43): tanner unclean by law; tanners lived ~25 m outside village; Morgan: despised, girl could break engagement; Peter freed from tradition, stage for ch 10.
