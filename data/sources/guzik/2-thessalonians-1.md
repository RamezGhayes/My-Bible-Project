# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 2 Thessalonians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/2-thessalonians-1/ . Our own outline, not his text. Page title: رسالة تسالونيكي الثانية – الإصحاح ١ – صفات الكنيسة المُضطهَدة.
A1 (1-2): Silvanus and Timothy co-workers; Silas at Philippi (Acts 16:19-27) and Thessalonica (Acts 17:1-9); Timothy of Lystra, mother and grandmother (2 Tim 1:5; 3:15); short founding stay; Morris via Bicknell: Father and Christ one source of grace and peace.
A2 (3-4): bound to give thanks (Morris: strong wording, perhaps praise earlier overstated); rare verb for faith growing; boasting among churches over endurance; Spurgeon: attempt more than present ability; Calvin: thank God for His work in others.
B1 (5-7): endurance = evidence of God's righteous judgment; 1 Pet 4:17; "counted worthy" = reckoned, not earned; suffering as refining fire; God just to repay (Morris: also in this life); imprecatory psalms leave vengeance to God; Hiebert: a world without final justice is not God's world; rest at revelation of Jesus with angels.
B2 (8-10): flaming fire; vengeance on those who do not know God / obey gospel; essence of hell is separation from the Lord's presence, not the fire (Dan 3 youths in fire with the Lord); vengeance = full justice not vindictiveness (Morris links to "righteous" of 5-6); eternal because the glory opposed is eternal (Calvin); glorified in saints, admired (Poole, Spurgeon on wonder); testimony believed divides saved and lost; Paul persecutor turned persecuted.
B3 (11-12): prays God count them worthy of calling; fulfil desire for goodness and work of faith with power; name = person of Christ; standing in Jesus above every other standing.
