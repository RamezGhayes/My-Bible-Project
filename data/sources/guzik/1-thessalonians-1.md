# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Thessalonians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-thessalonians-1/ (Arabic page exists; English page also read for detail). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): Paul rarely alone; Silas (Acts 16-17), Timothy (Acts 16:1-3; 2 Tim 1:5; 3:15); founding visit and expulsion (Acts 17:1-9); written from Corinth, probably earliest letter; Moffatt on simple organisation; Morris on grace; Hiebert: one preposition for Father and Jesus = deity.
A2 (2): constant thanks, present tense (Hiebert); naming in prayer, plural = team prayed (Moffatt); Rom 1:9; Eph 1:16; Phlm 4.
A3 (3-4): not thanks for praise, moral or doctrinal perfection; first faith-love-hope triad (Hiebert); ergon/kopos/endurance; Barclay "beloved of God"; Spurgeon four evidences of election.
B1 (5): more than information; Thomas: dynamis singular not specifically miracles; Calvin: spiritual power of doctrine; Spirit convicts/comforts/teaches; preacher must have conviction.
B2 (6): followers of Paul and Lord (Phil 3:17; 1 Cor 11:1); Morris: affliction = pressing grapes; joy like Philippian jail.
B3 (7): imitators become examples; new believers need lived example.
B4 (8-10): sounded forth like trumpet; trading city; Clarke and Spurgeon on changed lives as argument; Hiebert/Denney: serving a god foreign to Greco-Roman religion; Spurgeon: waiting = mark of grace; wrath: Thomas = end-time judgment before Christ's kingdom (1 Thess 5:9); Trapp: tribulation or eternal judgment, flee it. Reformed note added.
