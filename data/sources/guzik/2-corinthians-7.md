# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 7
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-7/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Outline: A. Cleansing and perfecting (1); B. Personal words about Paul's relationship with the Corinthians (2-16).
A1 (1a): therefore links 6:14-18; promises 6:17-18; separation leads to fellowship.
A2 (1b): cleanse (take away) and perfect holiness (add); God's sole cleansing 1 John 1:9 vs cooperative cleansing; Clarke eyes, ears, hands; flesh vs spirit sins (tax collectors vs Pharisees); Morgan spirit defilement deadlier; Spurgeon spiritual filth; complete holiness not sinless perfection; Paul includes himself; Spurgeon sensitivity grows, claiming sinlessness is sin.
B1 (2-3): return to 6:11-13 after digression; open to world not Paul; heart problem; collection money 1 Cor 16:1-4; confront without condemning.
B2 (4-7): boldness and boasting; joy not stoicism; Morgan souls growing; 2:14-7:4 digression from 2:13; conflicts and fears; God comforts through people; 1:3; earnest desire, mourning, zeal.
B3 (8-12): severe letter between the epistles, after painful visit 2:1; regret then no regret; Trapp pleasure/sorrow; sorrow vs repentance, Peter vs Judas; Smith; repentance first word of gospel Matt 3:2; 4:17; Acts 2:38; godly sorrow vs worldly; Hodge repentance not ground but necessary part; Spurgeon with faith; Trapp contradiction; seven marks, Redpath on indignation, zeal = heat; proved by actions; Clarke.
B4 (13-16): Titus refreshed; boast proved true; affections; fear and trembling; confidence sincere; Trapp "sour and sweet"; people mattered.
