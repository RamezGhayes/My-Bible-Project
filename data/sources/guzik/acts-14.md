# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 14
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-14/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): after expulsion (13:50) resumed in synagogue, still best starting point; "so spoke" = in a way that moved to faith; same message to Jews and Greeks; persecution God's way of moving them on; Paul preferred longer stays.
A2 (2-6): not all Jews opposed; stayed long, boldly; Bruce: opposition took time to become dangerous; Clarke: no apostle worked miracles by own power; word of His grace = same salvation for Jew and Gentile; city divided; "apostles" applied to both, Paul later used title often; plot to stone; fled to Lystra (about 33 km) and Derbe; flight does not mean martyr time had come; Ramsay: Luke accurate about Lycaonia borders.
B1 (7-10): many miracles but one recorded, focus preaching; Boice: went to preach, miracles sometimes accompanied; lame from birth listened; Paul saw faith (perhaps discernment); loud voice, leapt; Bruce: faith seen in immediate obedience; picture of conversion.
B2 (11-13): Lycaonian speech; miracle did not change their concept of God; miracle draws attention but saves no one; Barnabas Zeus, Paul Hermes messenger of gods; legend of Zeus and Hermes disguised, refused except by an old couple, others destroyed, hence eagerness; Bruce: language explains why missionaries noticed late; priest of Zeus at gate, oxen, garlands, beyond hospitality.
B3 (14-18): tore clothes, Jewish response to blasphemy; like nature; turn from vanities to living Creator; can't add Jesus to idolatry; God let nations walk own ways; witness: rain, seasons, food, gladness, the very things credited to Zeus; common grace; natural revelation instead of OT quotes for pagans; barely restrained, hard to dislodge wrong ideas about God.
B4 (19-20a): opponents traveled over 160 km; fickle crowd, don't encourage hero worship; Gal 6:17; 2 Cor 11:25; speculation 2 Cor 12 vision here, uncertain; Stephen (7:58; 8:1); rose, entered city, did not flee; Timothy from Lystra (16:1) may have witnessed.
B5 (20b-21a): next day Derbe; many disciples; work continues despite persecution.
C1 (21b-22): returned to strengthen; continue in faith; many tribulations; Paul's life proof; modern believers see suffering as outside God's plan.
C2 (23): elders in every church; care for growth not only conversion; Boice: few believers, little teaching, God the true shepherd; Bruce: modern missions think appointing new converts dangerous, but they trusted the Spirit's presence; Arabic word for elders noted; church belongs to Jesus not leaders.
C3 (24-26): Pisidia, Pamphylia, Perga, Attalia, sailed; skipped Cyprus; commended to grace; work not fully finished, first of several journeys.
C4 (27-28): reported what God did, door of faith; Williams: imperfect = repeated reporting, "the church" singular; list of hardships (Elymas, Mark, expulsions, worship, stoning); what would make you abandon God's will?; Phil 3:12-14; stayed long, rest and service.
