# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 1 Corinthians 9
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/1-corinthians-9/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No Arabic page found.)
A1 (1-2): Paul, while treating "rights" over idol meat, defends his apostleship. Evidence: free, has seen the risen Lord, the Corinthians are his seal. Seeing alone not enough; commissioned on Damascus road (Acts 26:12-18). Irony that his own converts doubted.
A2 (3-6): apologia and anakrino legal terms; Paul as on trial. Right to food and drink, to lead about a believing wife; Cephas married (tension with mandatory celibacy). Paul and Barnabas worked; Corinthians thought less of them for it.
A3 (7-14): soldier, vineyard, shepherd. Scripture: Deut 25:4 humane law whose principle applies to ministers. Wiersbe: oxen can't read. Calvin warns against using this to allegorize all Scripture. Plow in hope. Spiritual sowing, material reaping. Paul did not use the right, endured all lest he hinder the gospel; example for Corinthians on idol meat. Altar servants. Lord's command (Matt 10:10; Luke 10:8). Ministers should work hard if supported; Clarke: taking support without labor is sacrilege.
B1 (15-18): Not hinting for money. Boast not in preaching (necessity, woe) but in preaching free; Greeks despised manual labor. Willing = reward; unwilling = stewardship entrusted. Reward: gospel without charge, no accusation of greed. What rights will we give up?
B2 (19-23): Free yet servant of all to win more. Adapted behavior, never message (1 Cor 1:22-23). Acts 21:23-26 purification; Acts 16:3 Timothy. Willing to offend only over the gospel itself. Poole: as Gentile, laid aside Levitical observances. Fee: how one lives among those evangelized, not adapting the message. Clarke: aim to save souls, not money or honor.
B3 (24-27): Isthmian Games at Corinth. Self-denial, athletes refuse good things; give up idol meat for incorruptible crown. Temperate = strict training (about ten months). "Discipline" = strike under the eye, black eye; "subjection" = lead about as a slave. Body not evil; rejects flagellation; body belongs to Jesus (6:20). Herald and athlete (Clarke on herald's role). Disqualified likely loss of reward, not salvation (Clarke: judges reject one who has not earned the prize).
