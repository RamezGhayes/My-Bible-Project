# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 21
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-21/ . Our own outline, not his text. Page title: "يحذر يسوع من سقوط أورشليم وعودته".
A1 (1-2): rich and widow at treasury; lepton about 1% of a day's wage (Poole); she could have kept one coin; Jesus sees how we give, cares about faith, motive, heart.
A2 (3-4): gave more from her need; cost and sacrifice measure a gift (2 Sam 24:24); God does not need our money, giving benefits the giver.
B1 (5-6): Zerubbabel/Ezra temple (Ezra 6:15), Herod's ~80-year expansion; Josephus on gold and marble; Matt 12:5 greater than temple; AD 70 destruction, stones dismantled; good things can become idols.
B2 (7): when and what sign; start of the Olivet Discourse (Matt 24:3); Spurgeon: the siege of Jerusalem a sample of what is to come.
B3 (8): false messiahs; William Miller's failed prediction; Bar Kokhba; Pate: about 6,000 died in temple following a false prophet.
B4 (9-11): wars, earthquakes, famines, signs must come first, end not immediate; occurred before AD 70 (Acts 11:28 famine, Pompeii, comet over Jerusalem); will intensify before return like birth pains (Matt 24:8); Geldenhuys on moral and eschatological character.
B5 (12-15): persecution from religious and secular authorities, turned to witness; promise of words and wisdom; Pate: Greek idea of rehearsing a defense.
B6 (16-19): betrayal by family and friends, some killed; hupomone = active steadfastness; no hair perishes eternally.
B7 (20-24a): flee when armies surround Jerusalem; Christians fled to Pella (Eusebius), few believers died; "days of vengeance" (Luke 19:41-44; Deut 28:15-68); Morris, Trapp.
B8 (24b): times of the Gentiles; Israel regained parts of Jerusalem 1967, but Temple Mount still under Gentile control, so still "trampled"; Gentile times end before the seven-year period of Dan 9. (Dispensational reading.)
B9 (25-28): cosmic signs and Son of Man in cloud; not fulfilled in AD 70; Rev 6, 8-9, 15-18; visible return to earth with His church; lift up heads.
B10 (29-33): fig tree and all trees: signs show kingdom near; "this generation" read as the Jewish people as a nation (Pate on genea); Jesus' words do not pass.
B11 (34-36): guard against dissipation, drunkenness, cares; trap; two aspects of His coming: sudden catching up of the prepared (1 Thess 4:17) and later visible return to a judged world; those who watch and pray escape the tribulation. (Pre-tribulation rapture view.)
B12 (37-38): taught daily in temple, lodged on Mount of Olives; people came early; He did not hide before His arrest.
