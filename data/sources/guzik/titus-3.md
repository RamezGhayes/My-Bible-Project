# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Titus 3
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/titus-3/ . Our own outline, not his text. Page subtitle: تذكر هذا.
A1 (1-2): "remind" present tense, ongoing; Cretan character (1:12); White: Crete vs Asia, 1 Tim 2:1-3 prays for rulers, Titus stresses obedience; submission alone could make life passive, so ready for every good work; gentleness from knowing our and others' standing in Jesus' heart.
A2 (3): former state list; sinful nature not far away; four benefits of remembering: gratitude, humility, kindness to those still there, faith that God can change others; White: we are living proof of grace.
A3 (4-8): kindness and love of God; not by works (altar calls, sinner's prayer, baptism, church attendance, giving, Bible reading are good but do not save); God's initiative; v5 washing of regeneration: often taken of baptism and cited for baptismal regeneration, but Paul does not mention baptism here; same word in Eph 5:26 (washing by the word); Guthrie: word denotes the act of washing; Hiebert: most take it of baptismal water, but if water were the means of new birth material means would be indispensable (Matt 15:1-20; Rom 2:25-29), so washing is an inner divine act and baptism its public declaration; v8 good works; Briscoe: theology is grace, ethics is gratitude.
A4 (9-11): avoid foolish questions, genealogies, contentions (cf. 1:11); "avoid" = turn and go the other way (2 Tim 2:16); Barclay: rabbis' genealogy debates, arguing easier than being kind at home; divisive man warned once or twice, then reject; self-condemned; Clarke: just avoid him, leave him to God.
B1 (12-13): Artemas or Tychicus; Nicopolis winter; Zenas the lawyer and Apollos; names show a real world of friends; White: temporary arrangement start of later episcopal government; Morgan: names show Christianity's growth; support full-time workers.
B2 (14-15): good works for urgent needs, not unfruitful (recurring theme); greetings; Morgan: closing grace extended to all with Titus.
