# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 5
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-5/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): porneia broad sense (Poole, Clarke); heads NT vice lists, clash with Greek culture; ongoing relationship; woman unbeliever; Cicero on incest; Lev 18:8; Deut 22:30.
A2 (2): church's indifference worse; mental then moral problems; Corinth's immorality, mistresses, concubines, wives; Akiba on proselyte; tolerance and love label; should have mourned.
A3 (3-5): presence in spirit = authority of letter; Matt 7:1-5 answered; Wiersbe on motives vs behavior; deliver to Satan = into world, loss of protection (Job 1:10; Luke 22:31-32); destruction of flesh not torture (Rom 6:6; Gal 5:24); Spurgeon saved from sin not in sin; Wiersbe pious policemen; 2 Thess 3:14-15; Hodge quoted; excommunication abused, people switch churches.
B1 (6): gentle with cancer; church's sin worse; leaven = old dough; Passover removal, health.
B2 (7-8): remove unrepentant; Christ Passover lamb; salvation, liberation, joy, abundance, purity; Spurgeon again; be what you are; sincerity and truth.
C1 (9): lost earlier letter; not keep company = no fellowship.
C2 (10-13): not the world's immoral; greedy, harpax, revilers; eating = friendship; believers judge outsiders more than insiders; two reasons: purity and the man's salvation.
