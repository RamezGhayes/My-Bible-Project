# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 1
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-1/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Section I (introduction to the fourth Gospel, no verse range) not registered: four Gospels as one (Origen); John written last, omits much of the Synoptics; Synoptics stress what Jesus did, John who He is; seven signs, "I am" sayings; purpose John 20:31; early papyrus of John 18; simple yet deep (Erdman, Chrysostom).
B1 (1-2): "In the beginning" echoes Gen 1:1; Word before time (Trench, Dods); Logos in Targum usage and Greek philosophy (Morris, Barclay, Bruce); Trinity: Word is God yet distinct; Luther via Dods: against Arius and Sabellius; Boice; rejects Jehovah's Witnesses "a god" (Mantey, Bruce, Feinberg, Barclay).
B2 (3-5): all made through Him so He is uncreated (Col 1:16); Clarke; zoe vs bios; light of men; "overcome" possible sense (Morris); Gen 1:2 parallel (Bruce).
B3 (6-8): John sent to witness; Dods: witness exposes blindness; not the light, perhaps against Baptist followers (Tenney; Acts 18:24-19:7); Morris on commitment of a witness.
B4 (9-11): light available to all, not universal salvation; world did not know its Maker; Morris "came home" (John 19:27); Dods: wicked tenants (Matt 21); Trapp: hidden as carpenter's son.
B5 (12-13): receiving makes children by new birth, of God; Morris: story ends in grace; Spurgeon: empty cup under a spring; Tenney: tekna; Spurgeon watch: new birth changes the works not the hands.
B6 (14): incarnation astonishing to Jews and Greeks; Alford against Docetism; Augustine via Barclay; "dwelt" = tabernacled (Ex 33; Shekinah; Clarke, Morris, Dods, Spurgeon); tabernacle functions; eyewitness glory (Morris); grace and truth (Spurgeon, Morgan).
B7 (15-18): Baptist: comes after yet before (Morris on priority); grace upon grace (Morris, Bruce; Phil 2:27); law by Moses; no one has seen God; bosom = intimacy (Clarke, Alford).
C1 (19-22): "the Jews" = religious establishment (Bruce); not Christ, not Elijah (Mal 4:5-6), not the Prophet (Deut 18:15-19).
C2 (23-28): voice (Isa 40:3); Bruce on proselyte baptism applied to Jews; unworthy to untie sandal (Bruce on rabbinic custom); Bethabara and Joshua's crossing (Trench).
C3 (29): after temptation (Bruce, Alford); sacrifice not mere teacher; lamb line: Gen 3:21; Gen 22; Ex 12; Isa 53:7; "takes away" = bears and removes (Morris); Maclaren: world's sin as one bundle; Trench: beyond Israel.
C4 (30-34): eternity; sign of the Spirit remaining; Trench, Bruce; faithful witness tells what he saw; Trench on "Son of God".
D1 (35-39): Andrew and probably the author (Alford); "what do you seek" = motives (Morris, Tenney); come and see; tenth hour an eyewitness detail.
D2 (40-42): Andrew brings others (John 6:8; 12:22); Simon renamed Cephas, rock he would become.
D3 (43-44): "Follow Me" continuous (Morris); Bethsaida (Bruce).
D4 (45-51): Nazareth prejudice; come and see; no guile vs Jacob (Morris); fig tree, rabbinic study (Trench); confession with articles (Morris); Jacob's ladder (Gen 28:12); Meyer's greater things; Son of Man (Dan 7:13-14); four ways people come to Jesus; four witnesses.
No theology note needed.
