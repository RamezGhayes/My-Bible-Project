# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Colossians 3
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/colossians-3/ (title: خلع … ارتداء). Our own outline, not his text. Section B5 runs 3:22-4:1; in our voices it is used only for 3:22-25 (the 4:1 part is noted in colossians-4.md).
A1 (1-4): practice grounded in theology; raised with Christ; Vaughan: law cannot restrain the flesh, union with Christ does (link to 2:20-23); Wright: Christ reigns (Ps 110); Morgan: "seek" = desire and affection; Clarke: love and study heavenly things; Vaughan: harmless earthly things harm when they displace heavenly; Christ our life (Phil 1:21), people say music or work is their life; Wright: at His return believers seen as they truly are.
A2 (5-7): "therefore" from union; nekrosate strong, kill not restrain (Vaughan); Clarke: indulging feeds desire; Wright: name the sins; porneia (Wright), uncleanness broader (Bruce); Morgan: three reasons covetousness destructive, church never disciplines it; wrath on sons of disobedience, degrees of judgment (Matt 23:14), sin self-destructive (Rom 1); "walked" = past lifestyle, lapses possible.
A3 (8-9): put off "all": anger, wrath, malice, slander, filthy speech, often treated as minor; Bruce: worn-out suit; speech main channel (Jas 1:26; 3:1-9); Barclay: lying by tone or silence; two priorities in 5-9; Wright: anger and lust as unwelcome guests shown out.
B1 (10-11): put on = changing clothes; renewed in knowledge, hunger for the word; Gen 1:27, image of second Adam; barriers (Greek/Jew etc.), Scythian extreme (Peake); slave and free barrier broken; Bruce: Blandina at Lyon 177, Perpetua and Felicitas at Carthage 202.
B2 (12-17): chosen ones, comfort and calling; virtues relational (Barclay); chrestotes: guarding neighbor's goods, mellow old wine, "my yoke is easy" (Matt 11:30); humility parent of meekness and patience; forgive as Christ (Matt 18:21-35), ten ways God's forgiveness exceeds ours; Spurgeon: grudge-keeping forgiveness lame; love bond of perfection, outer garment (Clarke), virtues expressions of love (Vaughan); Meyer: peace as umpire; Wright: communal peace; word dwell richly, Clarke Shekinah; psalms, hymns, songs varied spontaneous worship (Peake); all in the name of Jesus.
B3 (18-19): hypotasso military "rank under", order not worth (Wiersbe); Wright: Gal 3:28 equality does not erase roles; middle voice voluntary (Vaughan); rejects absolute submission and submission only when husband seems "in the Lord"; exceptions: demand to sin, incapacitated husband, violence, adultery; Bruce: Stoic "fitting nature" vs "fitting in the Lord"; agapao sacrificial (Vaughan), Eph 5:25; not harsh even when given cause, love when not returned.
B4 (20-21): obey in all, pleasing to Lord; honor continues after leaving home; order of submission linked to Son and Father; 1 Sam 15:23; fathers not provoke: harsh commands, fault-finding (Peake), impossible demands (Bruce); Wright on "fathers"; discouraged children lose heart.
B5 (22-4:1): obey not with eyeservice, sincerity, fearing the Lord; as to the Lord, reward of inheritance; Wright: slave inheriting a paradox, "serve the true master, Christ"; Vaughan: about half of Rome's city people slaves, incl. professionals; v25 wrongdoer repaid, no partiality, believers who wrong others more responsible; Clarke; Eph 6:9; 4:1 justice and equality harder than kindness; Christians among strongest advocates of abolition, Paul's teaching undermined the institution.
