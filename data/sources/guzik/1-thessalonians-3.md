# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Thessalonians 3
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-thessalonians-3/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): sent Timothy, left alone in Athens; Hiebert/Morris: servant = practical service; strengthen then encourage; Barclay help not inspect; Trapp fawning dog; suffering not sign of God's anger; Morgan appointed suffering; Heb 2:10; 5:8.
A2 (4): forewarned; rocky ground (Matt 13); shepherd prepares people.
A3 (5): tempter; Hiebert: weakness in Corinth (Acts 18:5); Job; labour in vain; hurting people need help.
B1 (6): good news of faith, love, remembrance, longing; Calvin: faith and love sum of godliness.
B2 (7-9): comforted in distress; 1 Cor 2:3; Spurgeon: preacher's joy, chariot without wheels; spiritual not material welfare.
B3 (10): night and day; Morris strongest emphatic; complete what lacks; Poole presence vs letter.
C1 (11): prayer as wish (Hiebert); Thomas: singular verb, unity of Godhead (John 10:30); apostolic teaching preserved in Scripture (Eph 2:20; Rev 21:14).
C2 (12): grow in love; John 13:35; 1 John 4:20; Matt 5:46-47; Paul's example.
C3 (13): holiness defined; heart first (Matt 23:27); coming as motive; Morris: saints = heavenly beings and departed faithful.
