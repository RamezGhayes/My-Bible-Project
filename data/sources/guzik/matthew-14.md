# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Matthew 14
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/matthew-14/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): tetrarch (Barclay); guilt and superstition; Trapp: haunted by John; Origen via Barclay: Jesus and John looked alike; Caligula refused king title, exile.
A2 (3-12): John's rebuke ongoing (France); Clarke on Herodias' history; Trapp: Herodias ruled Herod as Jezebel ruled Ahab; dance indecent (Barclay, Bruce); Spurgeon: rash oath no excuse; Plumptre via Carson: weak men fear looking weak; Bruce, Clarke on head; Spurgeon: body buried, message lives; fear of man over God.
A3 (13): withdrawal not cowardice, Father's timing; Trapp: crowd's eagerness shames our sloth.
B1 (14-16): compassion (Spurgeon on word; Mark 6:34); Barclay: solitude interrupted; Carson: should have remembered Cana (John 2:1-11); Spurgeon sermon "they need not depart"; only Jesus satisfies.
B2 (17-19): boy's loaves (John 6:9); Jewish blessing; works through disciples; Spurgeon: royal banquet.
B3 (20-21): twelve baskets, no waste; Barclay generosity plus stewardship; 15-20,000 people; manna, messianic banquet.
C1 (22-24): avoid king-making (John 6:14-15); prayer; Trapp: secret prayer feeds soul.
C2 (25-27): fourth watch; ghost; Mark 6:47-52; Trapp: Christ prays for his people in danger; fear exaggerates danger.
C3 (28-33): Carson "if" = "since"; Bruce; Spurgeon hand in hand, one Greek word, doubt without evidence; Clarke: danger was little faith; France: doubt = divided; little faith still real faith; Poole: first clear public confession.
C4 (34-36): France: Gennesaret, back in Antipas' territory; John 6:21; Carson: Pharisees and Essenes avoided contact; touch as point of faith; Acts 19:11-12; 5:15.
