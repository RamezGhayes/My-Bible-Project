# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 6
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-6/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-4): crowd followed for healings (Morris: repeated watching); only John notes Passover, wilderness provision; Bruce: hill east of the lake. A2 (5-7): test of Philip (Bruce: from Bethsaida); Philip's numbers-only answer, should trust like Moses/Elisha; Maclaren, Dods. A3 (8-9): Andrew; barley food of the poor (Clarke, Talmud); Bruce: small salted fish; Taylor: small things in the right hands.
B1 (10): sit on grass, Ps 23 shepherd, host; Spurgeon on order. B2 (11): thanks, miracle in Jesus' hands, disciples distribute; Spurgeon, Tenney, Trench: multiplication quietly in the breaking; Trapp. B3 (12-13): gather fragments; disciples share in the work; Prov 11:24; Trench, Tenney on pieces and baskets.
C1 (14): the prophet (Deut 18:15); loving Jesus only for blessings. C2 (15): Bruce: Antipas under Rome; Boice, Morris, Trench: refused earthly kingdom, withdrew to pray.
D1 (16-17): Bruce: Mark's "compelled"; fishermen. D2 (18): sudden storms; Tenney: low elevation, cold air. D3 (19): three or four miles; Matthew's fourth watch; Mark: he saw them; Barclay, Maclaren. D4 (20): Bruce: "It is I" may echo divine self-naming but here simply "it is I"; Matt 14. D5 (21): Trench: immediate arrival seems miraculous; authority after refusing crown.
E1 (22-24): crowd crosses to Capernaum (Dods, Bruce). E2 (25-27): sought him for bread; Maclaren; Bruce: Son of Man non-political; seal = baptism (Bruce, Alford, Clarke). E3 (28-29): faith first, obedience from trust (Maclaren, Alford). E4 (30-33): manna; true bread from Father (Alford, Trench).
E5 (34-40): coming = trusting (Spurgeon, Morris); Bruce: first "I am" with predicate; v37 Clarke: suppliant welcomed in a noble house; Morris: "all" general; Trapp: comfort of "not cast out"; vv39-40 none lost, raised; Trench: seeing the Son and the bronze serpent.
E6 (41-46): murmuring; Tenney: "came down from heaven" repeated; v44 Father's initiative, drawing not forced; Alford citing Augustine: drawing moves the will by love; Chrysostom: drawing does not cancel our response; page states divine drawing does not mean grace cannot be resisted; Barclay: Jer 31:3; Dods: gentle sense (John 12:32); encourages evangelism; v45 Isa 54:13; Spurgeon; Dods: hearing doesn't guarantee coming.
E7 (47-51): manna vs living bread; some read as Lord's Supper; Bruce, Morris: speaks of the truth the ordinance conveys rather than the rite; Trapp criticizes early sacramental reading (infant communion); Augustine "believe and you have eaten"; Morris, Clarke: substitutionary death; Barclay.
E8 (52-59): deliberate misunderstanding (cannibalism); Spurgeon critiques transubstantiation; metaphor for faith in sacrificial death; Trench, Morris, Morgan, Dods, Spurgeon (eating = receiving); synagogue in Capernaum.
F1 (60-64): Barclay: hard to accept; no softening; v62 warning of his glory; Tenney: Spirit not eating gives life; Jesus knew betrayer. F2 (65-66): carnal motives; Tenney: "from this time" maybe "because of this"; Trench; Spurgeon. F3 (67-69): Bruce: question expects no; Peter's confession. F4 (70-71): Bruce: diabolos accuser; Alford; Boice: false disciples look like true; Trench, Tenney: Kerioth.
Theology note added on E6 (v44: Guzik/Alford/Chrysostom say the drawing can be resisted; Reformed effectual calling noted).
