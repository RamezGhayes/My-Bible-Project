# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 8
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-8/ (two fetches: outline + detail). Our own outline, not his text. The page does not discuss Deut 23:1 / Isa 56 or v37 manuscripts.
A1 (1): suneudokeo = approve with pleasure; Phil 3:6; 1 Cor 15:9; Acts 26:11; Boice: two words for scatter (ash vs seed); Acts 1:8; Ecuador 1956 missionaries.
A2 (2): mourning for executed restricted; horrified many Jews; not all Jews enemies.
A3 (3-4): Stott: ravage = army/beast; Boice: imperfect = repeated raids; Stott: "preaching" = sharing good news; Spurgeon: ointment not in jar.
B1 (5-8): Philip one of seven, evangelist (21:8); Assyrian conquest, mixing; LaSor: prejudice to hatred; Luke 9:51-56; John 4; Luke 10:25-37; joy from scattered believers' suffering and signs; Spurgeon: preach Christ not doctrine (doctrine the chair); don't adjust message.
B2 (9-13): magic tied to error/drugs, demonic; magus (Matt 2:1); Williams citing Ramsay; Williams: no deficiency in faith; Simon's faith credible at first, later acts test it.
B3 (14-17): Matt 16:19 keys; 1 Tim 4:14; 2 Tim 1:6; 1 Cor 12:7-10; four views, leans to fourth (regenerate, Spirit at faith, gifts later by hands); seek fuller filling.
B4 (18-19): Spirit as purchasable force; simony; Morgan: power for personal ends.
B5 (20-23): Phillips' rendering; Isa 55:1; Boice: John 13:8, Peter out of God's will; evidence for Simon's profession (8:13, 18); Morgan: near faith, moral, unconverted; Boice: outward religion not proof; repent, pray; Morgan on pride.
B6 (24-25): partial conviction; Boice: shift responsibility; pastor can't repent for you; tradition: false teacher, maybe repented; mission success.
C1 (26-28): desert road; Boice; MacArthur: two roads; Morgan; Pierson; Candace title; Ethiopia larger, Queen of Sheba; Hughes: noble man seeking; found the Word.
C2 (29-31): boldness with officials; reading aloud; Boice on Philip's question; teaching role; bee vs butterfly.
C3 (32-35): Isa 53:7-8 atoning death; views of Servant; all Scripture points to Christ.
C4 (36-38): v37 presented as text without textual discussion; Hughes: baptismal invitation like Pentecost; immersion not sprinkling; faith from whole heart, devil knows truth.
C5 (39-40): John 6:15-21; 1 Thess 4:15-18; joy shows faith in God not Philip; Coptic tradition; only "evangelist" (21:8).
