# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Mark 16
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/mark-16/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-6): women at dawn; Lane: spices mask decay; no expectation of resurrection; guards (Matt 27:65-66); angel rolled stone (Matt 28:2); body passes barriers (John 20:19); resurrection vs revival (1 Kgs 17:17-24; John 11:38-44); glorified body; Robertson: "Nazarene crucified" a crown; Spurgeon: one eyewitness worth twenty; Rom 1:4; 1 Thess 4:14; Heb 7:25; Morgan vs Gnostic/Platonic view of body.
A2 (7-8): invitation of grace; promises kept (John 21:1); "and Peter", Spurgeon; silence = did not talk on the way (Mark 16:11; Luke 24:9).
B1 (9-20 intro, no verse heading): case against (two oldest Greek mss, Eusebius, Jerome, alternative endings, a third of vocabulary differs, most modern commentators reject); case for (Papias, Justin, Irenaeus, Hippolytus, Carthage council, Acts of Pilate, Apostolic Constitutions; majority of mss; some chapter/verse numbers garbled in Arabic page); unlikely Mark ended at v8; Robertson: text cut or last sheet torn; original ending may be lost.
C1 (9-11): Mary Magdalene, seven demons, John 20:11-18; women's testimony not valued; disbelief.
C2 (12-13): Emmaus, Luke 24:13-27; disbelief again.
C3 (14-18): rebuke; commission a command; Ironside: missions required; Spurgeon: Wellington story; Robertson: baptism absent in condemnation clause; baptism still obedience; Trapp on unbelief (Heb 10:26; John 3:19; Gal 3:23); signs = protection in mission (Acts 28:1-6), not tests by poison or snakes.
C4 (19-20): ascension for Spirit's ministry (John 14:2-3; Rom 8:34; Eph 4:8); Poole: sitting vs angels serving; go out; Spurgeon: not only hearing sermons; signs follow believers.
