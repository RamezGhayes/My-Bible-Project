A1 (1-2): another chance to reach Israel; "brothers" equal footing (Barclay); clear conscience not sinlessness; 1 Cor 4:4; Bruce; Ananias's order illegal (Hughes); Josephus on Ananias's corruption and later violent death (Bruce).
A2 (3-5): whitewashed wall accurate; Deut 25:1-2; Longenecker: prophetic; apology, Ex 22:28; eyesight (Gal 4:14-15; 6:11) or sarcasm.
A3 (6): reads divide, Pharisee, hope of resurrection; substantially true.
A4 (7-9): Sadducees deny, Pharisees affirm; rivals united against Jesus (Matt 16:1; John 11:47-53); scribes defend, Gamaliel model (5:38-39); Pharisees took Scripture seriously, added traditions.
A5 (10): tactic rescued him but not fruitful witness; Paul later admitted (24:20-21) approach not sound.
A6 (11): low point, self-blame; "take courage" (five NT uses, all from Jesus); Matt 28:20; Rom 8:28; Rom 9:1-3; 19:21; Ps 37:4; 9:15; presence in hardship; fruit God's responsibility, witness Paul's.

B2 (16-22): nephew; providence, fulfils 23:11; prisoner without crime.
B3 (23-24): ~470 soldiers; faithfulness to promise.

B6 (34-35): Cilicia; Herod's palace; 9:15 partly fulfilled; trust in uncontrollable circumstances.
