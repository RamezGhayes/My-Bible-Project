# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 1 Corinthians 8
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/1-corinthians-8/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No Arabic page found.)
A1 (1-3): Chs. 8-10 on idol meat: cheaper temple meat in market, eating as a guest, dining in temple restaurants. Paul starts with principle: knowledge vs love; love is the goal. Knowledge puffs up (bubble), love builds (building). He who thinks he knows does not yet know as he ought; being known by God matters more.
A2 (4-6): Idol is nothing; one God; "so-called gods". Judges called gods (Ps 82:6; John 10:34; Exod 21:6; 22:8-9) by office; Satan god of this age (2 Cor 4:4) because regarded so. Many gods, unknown god (Acts 17:23). Jesus not separated from God; kurios renders Yahweh in Greek OT (quotes Leon Morris). Clarke: idols represent figments of fancy. Corinthian inference: if idols nothing, eating/dining in temples nothing.
B1 (7): Not all have knowledge; some eat as to a real idol. Weak conscience = wrongly informed, actually overactive; acting against it defiles. Being right doctrinally does not outrank love for God's family.
B2 (8): Food does not commend us to God. Acts 15:29 a temporary arrangement for gospel among Jews, not contradicted. Error: treating liberty stance (movies, drinking, music) as maturity.
B3 (9-13): Liberty can be a stumbling block; weak brother "emboldened" (built up) to sin, perishes, Christ died for him; sinning against brother = against Christ. Paul would forgo meat forever. Weak = weak in knowledge. Not a tool for legalists: Gal 2, Paul rebuked Peter. Poole: knowledge given to save others, absurd to use to harm. Clarke: "sour godliness" of critics.
