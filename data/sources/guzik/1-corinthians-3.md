# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 3
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-3/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): brothers yet not spiritual; carnal Christian a contradiction?; still brothers, babes; carnality partial (Rom 7); sarkinos (2 Cor 3:3) vs sarkikos; Redpath: carnal believer travels third class.
A2 (2): milk because they overrated maturity; difference of maturity not content; no two gospels; fast-food appetite for human wisdom.
A3 (3-4): envy, strife, divisions prove carnality; relational problems reveal God-relationship problem; walking according to man; Fee: continuing so is worldly, not an option.
B1 (5-7): servants through whom they believed; planter/waterer, God gives growth; faithfulness not results; frustration; Maclaren: why strive when both are nothing; tense remarks (loose, not used).
B2 (8-9): one team; reward by labor; Hodge hidden faithful laborers; Morgan: hard work; fellow workers; field and building.
B3 (10-15): wise builder by grace (Acts 18); Christ only foundation; materials tested by fire; reward or loss, saved as through fire; rejects purgatory; 2 Cor 5:10; Rev 4:10-11; Moody: weigh converts; leaders first, then all servants; Chronicles temple materials (refs misprinted, not used).
B4 (16-17): church is God's temple, Spirit dwells; reality not mere metaphor; naos vs hieron; destroyer judged; 1 Cor 6:19-20.
C1 (18-20): irony, become fool to be wise; God catches wise in craftiness; thoughts futile (sources not named on page); accept being called foolish; agree with God's verdict.
C2 (21-23): no boasting in men; all things yours incl. death; Acts 12 Peter's release; freedom and responsibility.
