# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Thessalonians 4
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-thessalonians-4/ (fetched twice; second pass for the verse 17 arguments). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): "finally" = transition (Hiebert); abound more; no ceiling; commands with military authority (Morris); bought with price (1 Cor 6:20).
A2 (3-6a): sanctification = separation from Roman sexual culture; porneia (Hiebert, Thomas); Heb 13:4; vessel = body most likely, some say wife; wrongs others, Lev 18 (Morris).
A3 (6b-8): four reasons: Lord avenges; called to holiness (1 Cor 6:9-11, 15-20); rejecting = rejecting God; Spirit given.
B1 (9-10): taught by God; increase more.
B2 (11): quiet = peace, contentment; entertainment as substitute religion; mind own affairs = no meddling (Bruce, Clarke); manual labour; Clarke idleness shameful.
B3 (12): Hiebert "properly"; lack nothing = maturity (3:10).
C1 (13): four "do not be ignorant"; sleep euphemism, pagan despair vs Christian rest (Ps 4:8); Hiebert: not soul sleep (Phil 1:23).
C2 (14): Morris on Jesus' real death; catacomb "in peace" vs pagan epitaphs; 1782 gravestone near Dublin (Tallaght).
C3 (15-16): word of the Lord = revelation from Christ; shout (Morris); trumpet = 1 Cor 15:52, distinguished from Matt 24:31 and Rev 11:15 (Hiebert); dead rise first (Morris, Calvin).
C4 (17): "rapture" from Vulgate; Morris: sudden seizure; meeting = welcoming dignitary; four views; holds PRE-TRIBULATION (before seven-year period, Matt 24:21) as biblically correct: (1) 1:10 imminent waiting, (2) 4:13-18 dead and living on equal footing, (3) 2 Thessalonians comfort/rest to persecuted (page reference garbled); views should not break fellowship; Spurgeon "with the Lord". Reformed note added.
C5 (18): comfort by giving it (Hiebert); comfort only makes sense if rapture rescues from imminent danger; Adam Clarke: judgment-seat would not comfort. Reformed note added.
