# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 12
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-12/ (Arabic page exists; title "الاتجاه القلبي لأتباع يسوع"). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): crowds trampling; spoken to disciples first; hypocrisy grows with success/popularity; Trapp: leaven spreads, puffs up, spoils; aim high but stay honest; 1985 evangelist who denounced sexual sin, later exposed and re-arrested.
A2 (4-5): be ready for persecution (Luke 11:53-54); Gehenna = Valley of Hinnom, Molech, refuse dump (2 Chr 28:3; Jer 7:30-31; 2 Kgs 23:10); Clarke: one life to lose, one soul to save; Rowland Taylor martyrdom 1555 (Hadleigh, family farewell, cheerful as to a feast).
A3 (6-7): Matt 10:29 two for a penny, five for two (fifth free); hairs counted; Taylor's farewell letter on God's care.
A4 (8-10): public confession/denial before angels; word against Son of Man forgivable (moment of weakness); blasphemy of Spirit = persistent rejection of Spirit's witness to Jesus (John 15:26); not too big a sin but refusal of God's terms; seekers not guilty; can repent.
A5 (11-12): synagogues, rulers, authorities; Barclay: early believers feared failing to defend faith more than pain; Trapp: Alice Driver silenced the doctors; apologeomai -> apologetics; no excuse for neglecting study/preparation.
B1 (13-15): inheritance request; Barclay: elder takes two-thirds; Col 3:5 greed idolatry; Clarke: riches bring pride, laziness, luxury; Morgan: dividing between greedy men starts a quarrel; Trapp dog under table.
B2 (16-21): condemned for ignoring eternity, not for wealth; "soul required" = life owed to God; Morrison: anxious as a beggar; Geldenhuys: left poorer than poorest beggar; Wesley lived on fixed sum and gave the rest; fear riches more than poverty; 1 Tim 6:9, 17-19; Rev 3:17-18.
B3 (22-23): greed and anxiety linked; studies on chronic stress (immune T cells, brain, heart).
B4 (24-28): ravens, lilies, grass; cannot add a cubit; God's care includes rain; Spurgeon rebukes "little faith".
B5 (29-31): nations seek these; Father knows; kingdom a settled priority not list item; Trapp: Greek word for worry like a meteor hanging in air; Spurgeon: how differ from world if we worry like it.
B6 (32-34): little flock; flock with shepherd better than big flock with hireling; sell and give alms; Pate: generosity shows real change and loyalty; earthly interests = earthly commitment.
C1 (35-40): girded, lamps lit (Ps 119:105); servants awaiting master from wedding; Pate: master reverses roles and serves; Geldenhuys: ties to warnings about heavenly focus; thief image. No rapture scheme here.
C2 (41-48): for all, leaders more responsible; "my master delays"; some use delay instead of rescuing others; Poole: ignorant punished less than knowing disobedient.
C3 (49-53): fire = judgment (Barclay: Jews linked fire with judgment) or Spirit's power after the cross or gospel spread; baptism = immersion in suffering; Heb 12:2; families divided; Barclay: Rome hated Christianity for splitting families.
C4 (54-56): rebuke for reading weather but not times; lists end-time "stage-setting" signs (Israel 1948, temple rebuilding, European confederation, leader, false religion, economic system; Matt 24:15; 2 Thess 2; Rev 13), but says none guarantees imminence. Theology note added in voices (futurist/dispensational reading).
C5 (57-59): settle with God now through Christ before judgment; Barclay: lepton smallest coin; hell eternal (Matt 25:46; 2 Thess 1:9; Rev 14:11; Mark 9:48).
