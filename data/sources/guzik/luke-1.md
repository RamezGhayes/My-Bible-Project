# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 1
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-1/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): earlier accounts (maybe Mark, Matthew), some not inspired; Carsten Thiede on early Matthew fragments; Luke companion of Paul (Acts 16:10-11; 2 Tim 4:11; Phm 24), physician (Col 4:14), only Gentile NT writer; eyewitnesses incl. apostles, maybe Mary.
A2 (3-4): investigated carefully; "in order", most comprehensive; emphases: Gentiles, women/children/marginalized, prayer, Spirit, joy, "good news"; Theophilus probably Roman official, previously instructed.
B1 (5-7): Herod the Great, Idumean from Esau (Gen 25-27); Abijah division (1 Chr 23-24); righteous but childless.
B2 (8-10): lot, once in a lifetime; golden altar (Exod 30:7-8); incense = prayer (Ps 141:2; Rev 8:5); Zechariah likely prayed for Israel's deliverance.
B3 (11-17): angel by altar, fear; John: joy, great, no wine (maybe Nazirite, Num 6), Spirit from womb, Elijah (Mal 4:5-6; Matt 11:11-14; 17:12; 1 Kgs 18:20-40); Calvin: Spirit not limited by age.
B4 (18-20): sign request; doubt as self-protection after disappointment; Gabriel stands before God; muteness; promise sure (Gen 18:14).
B5 (21-23): crowd waits; gestures; would have pronounced Num 6:24-26.
B6 (24-25): natural conception, credits Lord, reproach removed; five months seclusion.
C1 (26-27): Nazareth obscure; betrothal binding; Pate on name Mary/Miriam; John's birth prepares greater birth.
C2 (28-29): "favored", troubled, humility; believers share grace (Eph 1:6); grace given to Mary, she needed a Savior (Luke 1:47).
C3 (30-33): Jesus great, Son of Most High, David's throne (2 Sam 7:12-16); echo Isa 7:14; Spurgeon on Jesus' greatness.
C4 (34-37): question of faith vs Zechariah; overshadow = cloud (Exod 40:34; Matt 17:5; Luke 9:34); Morris excludes crude physical idea; Pate on conception; holy, Son of God (John 5:18); Elizabeth as sign (Gen 18:14); Calvin: works strengthen faith.
C5 (38): Lord's servant; Meyer on humble acceptance despite risk; Christ in believers; Spurgeon on spiritual nearness.
D1 (39-41): ~160 km; Elizabeth could understand; leap; Trapp: Christ's presence gave John joy.
D2 (42-45): mother of my Lord; blessed who believed; faith receives promises.
D3 (46-56): Hannah's song (1 Sam 1-2), 12+ OT allusions; Trapp (Scripture library; great hands not small things); Liefeld (Savior vs Immaculate Conception); Morgan (honor yet sinner); Spurgeon (sang before birth); Barclay (sorrow of cross).
E1 (57-66): faithfulness; rejoicing fulfils 1:14; Barclay on birth customs; naming John; Morgan: doubt to obedience, second chance.
E2 (67-80): Spirit-filled, ends ~400 years silence; horn of salvation in David's house = Jesus; Abrahamic covenant; John's role; Trapp (prophets one voice); Morgan (deeper than he understood); wilderness.
