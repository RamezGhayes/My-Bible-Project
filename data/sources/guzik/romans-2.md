# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Romans 2
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/romans-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): from open sinners to the moralist; Pharisee and tax collector parable (ref printed garbled, not used); Seneca; Bruce on Seneca and Nero's murder of Agrippina; Murray judging reveals knowledge of God's justice; "according to truth"; Trapp barbed arrows; Lenski strip moralists; Wuest quoting Denney (not used).
A2 (4-5): kindness past, forbearance present, patience future mercy; Spurgeon daylight, bed, bread, Bible call to repent; God leads not drives; Cain, Judas; Morris repentance vs remorse; Poole treasuring judgment like wealth; Lenski wrath like growing burden.
A3 (6-10): judged by works, eternal life by works hypothetical, all factious; Greek for wrath (boiling) and anger (swelling); Jew first (Rom 1:16).
B1 (11-13): partiality = receiving face; rabbis' double standard; possession vs keeping; Morris judged for sinning.
B2 (14-16): law of conscience; "law to themselves"; conscience corrupted, restored in Jesus; Newell moral awareness; Morris work of the law written, not law; Clarke nations judged by use of word; Spurgeon "my gospel" soldier courage; judgment through Christ distinctively Christian; Calvin ignorance no excuse.
B3 (17-20): boasting in possession of law.
B4 (21-24): Jesus exposed law-righteousness (Matt 5:19-48); motives; Trapp hypocrites, Cardinal Cremensis anecdote; Morris robbing pagan temples.
B5 (25-29): circumcision; Barclay Abraham at gate of Gehenna; Egyptians, Ishmael; label on box; Deut 10:16; Morris citing Manson "if"; those who never heard; Matt 28:19.
Closing: Newell's seven principles of judgment (2:2, 5, 6, 11, 13, 16, 17-29).
