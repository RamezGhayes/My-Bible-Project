# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 8
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-8/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No readable Arabic page.)
A1 (1-4): compassion; dilemma put to disciples so they recall past faithfulness.
A2 (5-10): seven loaves, this time their own food. Trapp: a full feast, a good meal. Jesus creates, disciples distribute. Fish mentioned later: Ironside, perhaps withheld by doubting disciples till bread multiplied; give all to Him. Seven baskets: abundance. Critics call it a doublet; mature believers can still act in unbelief.
B1 (11-12): sign from heaven like Elijah's fire (1 Kings 18:38); "testing" = tempting as Satan did. Bruce: sigh physical, cause spiritual. Pride ("big leagues"). Miracles display mercy, not convince hardened unbelief; presuming more signs would make faith.
B2 (13-15): leaven = old dough starter; sin and pride spread. Barclay: Jews used leaven like "original sin". Wrong view of kingdom: Herod political, Pharisees religious domination.
B3 (16-21): disciples think of bread; could have understood; remember five loaves/twelve baskets and seven/seven: past faithfulness a promise for present; rebuke from love.
B4 (22-26): Clarke: spittle may have separated gummed lids, miracle needed for sight. Only gradual healing; pictures disciples' gradual healing of spiritual blindness. Sent home, not into town.
C1 (27-30): what men say leads to personal question; John, Elijah, prophet: complimentary but wrong; national reformers, political hopes. Wessel (Expositor's): "anointed" special meaning late OT, ideal king. Silence.
C2 (31-32a): Isa 53:3-12; "must": man's sin and God's love. Openly; Wessel: suffering Messiah unthinkable; Barclay on conqueror expectation.
C3 (32b-33): Peter loving but used by Satan; Matt 16:17-19 sequence; overconfident in hearing God; unaware for Satan as for God; things of men; illustrates leaven (8:15).
C4 (34): crowd and disciples; cross = execution, death row. Wessel: not an irritation. Trapp citing Luther: every Christian a "Crucian". Wiersbe: self-denial vs denying self.
C5 (35-38): seed must die; whole world vs soul; refused Satan's offer (Luke 4:5-8). Clarke: if Christ came rich, many hypocrites would follow. Ashamed: He ashamed at coming with angels; Christian life a slave rebellion against world, flesh, devil. 9:1 Lane: transfiguration.
