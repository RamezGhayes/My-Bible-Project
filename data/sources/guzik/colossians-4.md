# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Colossians 4
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/colossians-4/ (title: حياة الصلاة والشهادة الشخصية والتحيات الختامية). Our own outline, not his text.
4:1 is not on this page: Guzik treats it in the Colossians 3 page, section هـ (3:22-4:1) (justice and equality harder than kindness; masters too have a Master). Since sections are registered per chapter, there is no Guzik entry for 4:1 in voices/4.mjs (same as the precedent in Mark 9:1).
A1 (2-4): Paul supports the church by prayer; "devote" from a root meaning strength, earnest (Vaughan); Spurgeon: use every means to move God's hand; watchful = alert, sleepless (Barclay), prayer can be drowsy; Spurgeon: Puritans whose fast days crowded out thanks, a separate day for praise proposed; Wright: ask, wait, then thank; open door (Acts 14:27; 1 Cor 16:9; 2 Cor 2:12); Robertson: Paul never satisfied with his own preaching.
A2 (5-6): walk wisely toward outsiders, accusations to be refuted by conduct (Bruce); salt = wit in classical writing (Peake); grace and salt = tact (Robertson); Barclay: Christianity mistaken for dullness; Peake: meet real needs; 4:2-6 private prayer and public witness; doctrine effective only when practiced.
B1 (7-9): Tychicus messenger since Epaphras could not soon return; Acts 20:4; carried letter to Ephesus and Colossae (Bruce); Onesimus runaway, now "faithful and beloved brother", "one of you"; see Philemon.
B2 (10-11): Aristarchus of Thessalonica, fellow prisoner (Acts 19:29; 27:2), Ramsay: made himself Paul's slave to travel; Mark, Barnabas's cousin, conflict over, grace made Paul tender; Bruce: only here called cousin, Acts incomplete history; Jesus Justus only named; three Jewish co-workers a comfort; Clarke: Peter apparently not in Rome then.
B3 (12-13): "servant of Christ" used of another only here (and Phil 1:1 of Paul and Timothy); labored in prayer to point of pain (Vaughan); prayed they stand mature and assured; concern because he saw the danger of the heresy.
B4 (14): only place Luke called physician, medical detail in Luke-Acts; maybe in Rome with his finished works; Demas no commendation, later deserts (Phlm 24; 2 Tim 4:10), Barclay: faded zeal; Paul in chains still had fellowship.
B5 (15): Laodicea (Rev 3); Nymphas man or woman, manuscripts vary, Wright: unsettled and unimportant; house churches, Bruce: part of wider church; Barclay: buildings from third century.
B6 (16): letters read aloud and exchanged, how NT collection formed; Wright: apostolic authority; lost Laodicean letter, Scripture complete as God chose; Barclay: maybe Ephesians; Jerome on spurious Latin Laodiceans, Clarke rejects it.
B7 (17): Archippus (Phlm 2), maybe Philemon's son or Laodicean leader, unconfirmed; message through the church, encouragement not rebuke (Bruce, Clarke); Dyke: say it to him, not behind his back; principles: ministry given by God, received in the Lord, can be left unfinished, must be finished, encourage others; Trapp.
B8 (18): own hand; "remember my chains" loneliness, likely execution; Robertson: chains clanking as he took the pen; Barclay: not pity but authority; grace the fitting close for the apostle of grace against secret-knowledge and works heresy.
