# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 11
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-11/ . Our own outline, not his text. No Arabic page found in search.
A1 (1a): follows 9:1-29 and 9:30-10:21; is restoration impossible? Certainly not.
A2 (1b): Paul himself Israelite, Benjamin; look first to own life for evidence.
A3 (2-5): remnant, Elijah and 7000 (no ref given); Harrison Paul's kinship with Elijah; Morris not numbers but God's plan; God works through small groups.
A4 (6-10): grace and works exclusive; elect obtained, rest hardened; Isa 29 stupor; Ps 69 table snare; Morris stupor = deadness; Barclay feasting off guard.
B1 (11a): stumble vs fall; Ps 69.
B2 (11b-14): through fall salvation to Gentiles (Acts 13:46; 18:5-6; 28:25-28); rejection did not cause but gave opportunity; Morris regret that Gentiles treated Jews with hatred.
B3 (15-21): life from dead; firstfruit probably first Jewish Christians, many say patriarchs; olive tree, root Israel; Morris grafting wild shoots; Talmud Ruth godly shoot; stand by faith.
B4 (22-24): goodness and severity; continue = abiding (John 15:1-8); Murray perseverance; regrafting; Morris Gentile pride.
C1 (25-27): wise in own opinion; in part = temporary; Chuck Smith Jews will realize blindness, Kingdom Age; after fullness God turns focus to Israel; not spiritual Israel; Harrison reports Calvin's view (whole redeemed) and objects; Bruce same sense v25/26; rejects Church as New Israel (Gen 13:15; 17:7-8); Gal 3:7; not every Jew (Bruce "Israel as a whole"); saved through Jesus; necessary for Christ's return (Matt 23:39; Zech 12:10-11); Deliverer from Zion (Isaiah, no ref).
C2 (28-29): enemies/beloved; irrevocable gifts; reassurance for believers.
C3 (30-32): mercy through disobedience; shut up Jew and Gentile as prisoners, mercy via Christ.
C4 (33-36): depth; no one could design plan; Clarke on confident decrees; Isa 40:13; Job 41:11; Meyer monosyllables; of/through/to Him; Eph 1:6.
