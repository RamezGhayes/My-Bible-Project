# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 14
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-14/ . Our own outline, not his text. Arabic URL arabic.enduringword.com/ar/romans-14-ar/ redirects to an index page, so English used.
A1 (1-2): receive weak, not for disputes; weak vs rebellious; causes of weakness; meat (1 Cor 8 / Jewish food laws); weak = stricter, legalism, lack of love.
A2 (3-4): neither despise nor judge; God received both; servant of own Master; God able to make him stand; rise above differences.
A3 (5-6): days show wider principle; fully persuaded; to the Lord with thanks; conscience no excuse for plain sin.
A4 (7-9): lives interconnected; Lord of dead and living.
A5 (10-12): judge vs despise both wrong; judgment seat; Isa 45:23; Smith: bema like Olympic judge's seat, rewards not salvation.
A6 (13): Sermon on Mount; admonition still valid (Rom 15:14; 2 Tim 4:2); two ways to stumble.
B1 (14-15): nothing unclean in itself; Trapp quote; Christ died for him, give up a steak.
B2 (16-18): good evil spoken of; kingdom righteousness peace joy.
B3 (19-21): edify; sincere stumbling vs catering to legalism (circumcision refusal).
B4 (22-23): faith before God; approve what God challenges; whatever not of faith is sin.
