# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 5
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-5/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page arabic.enduringword.com/ar/mark-05-ar/ redirects to a generic index; not readable.)
A1 (1-8): fullest biblical portrait of possession (Luke 8:27: long duration, naked); tombs, supernatural strength, self-harm; man came to Jesus. Demons use full title "Son of the Most High God" as a superstitious name-power attack (Lane: attempt to gain control, not homage). Right theology, wrong heart (James 2:19). Demons hate the image of God; disarmed at cross (Col 2:15) yet can deceive and intimidate.
A2 (9-13): "Legion" reveals scale, intimidation tactic; Jesus needs no name, avoid superstition in spiritual warfare; Lane on name. Abyss (Luke 8:31). Trapp: idleness is another hell to the devil. Spurgeon: Satan would rather harm pigs than do nothing. Poole: loves mischief. Clarke: demons cannot even enter swine without permission, so do not fear them; loss of property shows temporal riches little to God. Swine drowned show destructive intent (John 10:10); full disarmament at cross (Col 2:15).
A3 (14-17): people see man clothed, seated, in right mind and are afraid; superstitions shattered; asked Jesus to leave; people push Jesus away fearing what He will do more than what Satan does.
A4 (18-20): man wants to be with Jesus (relationship). Spurgeon: known as the one once possessed. Crowd's request granted, man's godly request refused. Sent as light to Gentiles; Spurgeon: Christ assured he'd not relapse. Stedman, Barclay on Decapolis (ten cities listed). Spurgeon: "Lord" and "Jesus" same Person.
B1 (21-24): back to Jewish side. Jairus synagogue ruler (Lane: lay official arranging services). Assumed Jesus must be present; contrast centurion Luke 7:1-10; Jesus meets the faith Jairus had. Trapp: knowledge and faith differ. Clarke: four things needed for answered prayer (come to Jesus, humble, earnest, trust His power and goodness).
B2 (25-34): Lev 15:19-31 unclean; Morgan: cut off from husband, society, synagogue, temple. Luke 8:43. Clarke on rabbinic remedies. Many "doctors" of the soul. Faith mixed with superstition but object of faith matters (Robertson; Acts 5:15-16; 19:11-12). Barclay on tassels (Matt 9:20; Num 15:38-40). Jesus not made unclean; she made clean, picture of our sin brought to Him. Spurgeon: not every contact saves; Augustine via Morgan "flesh presses, faith touches". Reasons Jesus called her out (assurance, public, faith not garment, not stolen blessing, encourage Jairus, "Daughter"). Morrison: seemed cruel, was kind, "I've found a friend". Tell Jesus whole truth. God seems slow to the sufferer (Jairus).
B3 (35-36): news of death; "Do not be afraid; only believe": stop fearing, keep believing; His word outweighs circumstances.
B4 (37-43): Peter, James, John. "Not dead but sleeping": higher reality. Robertson: imperfect "kept laughing". Lane: hired mourners. Unbelievers put out. Talitha cumi; God calls things that are not as though they were (Rom 4:17). Contrast list Jairus vs woman (twelve years joy vs suffering, important vs nobody, rich vs poor, open vs secret, delay vs immediate, private vs public).
