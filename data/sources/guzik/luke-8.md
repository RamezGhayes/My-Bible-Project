# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 8
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-8/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): second Galilee tour (cf. 4:42-44); women healed; Jesus' attitude to women unlike religious teachers; Morris: all Jesus' enemies in the Gospels were men; Mary Magdalene's sinful past assumed, not stated; Barclay on Joanna (Herod's steward's wife); witnesses of resurrection (24:10); Pate: diakonia linked to later deacons.
A2 (4-8): boat (Matt 13:1-3; Mark 4:1-2); parable = earthly story with heavenly meaning (France, Poole); Barclay: don't over-allegorize; better called "parable of the soils"; hundredfold; "ears to hear" a warning.
A3 (9-10): disciples didn't grasp at once; secrets given to disciples; parables a door, uninterested stay outside; Geldenhuys: parables avoided adding guilt before the cross; farmer, politician, journalist, salesman misreading; Isa 6:9.
A4 (11-15): seed = word (1 Cor 3:6; 1 Pet 1:23); Spurgeon: preacher is given the seed; path: Satan takes word (2 Cor 4:4), distraction during preaching; rock: quick joy, no root; Spurgeon: lacking the Spirit's dew, signs (doctrine without experience, faith without repentance); thorns: cares, riches, pleasures (6:49); good soil: keep, patience; difference in soil not seed or sower; Spurgeon: the word will judge the hearer.
B1 (16-17): lamp on stand; responsible to share truth through open doors; directly or by bringing others to hear.
B2 (18): how you hear; 6:49; Spurgeon's list: attentively, in faith, faithfully, reverently, seriously, with feeling, gratefully; spiritual momentum grows or declines; Laodicea (Rev 3:14-22).
B3 (19-21): family can't reach him; brothers not yet believing (John 7:5; Mark 3:21); Jesus had brothers and sisters (Carson; Pate citing Fitzmyer) against perpetual virginity; true family hear and do; prayer and fasting not enough without obedience; Poole on centrality of preaching.
C1 (22-23): "let us go over" a promise; Barclay: lake about 21 by 12 km; Wiersbe: Jonah disobedient vs disciples obedient; Bruce: sleep of the exhausted; sudden windstorm (Mark 4:37-40).
C2 (24-25): Morgan: "we perish" includes Jesus; rebukes wind; Clarke: Satan may have stirred the storm; fear = failure to trust promises, not the storm itself; Ps 89:8-9.
D1 (26-29): Gentile region east; possessed long, naked, tombs, strong, self-harm, chains (Mark 5:2-5); Jesus approaches him and he comes to Jesus; correct theology without saving faith (Jas 2:19); Trapp: demon driven like a horse or galley.
D2 (30-33): Legion, many not a name, figurative (about 6,000); abyss (Rev 9:11); pigs (John 10:10; Col 2:15); Clarke: not without God's permission; Spurgeon: Satan delights in harm.
D3 (34-37): herdsmen report; healed man sitting, clothed, sane; fear partly from broken superstitions; ask Jesus to leave; Spurgeon: a city praying against its own blessing.
D4 (38-39): asks to go; sent home to tell what God did; witness in Gentile area; staying near Jesus not needed for protection; Trapp: error of depending on Christ's bodily presence; proclaims through city.
E1 (40-42): crowd welcomes; Jairus; Lane: synagogue ruler a lay administrator; contrast 7:1-10; Morrison: Jairus's faith hidden until crisis; crowd presses.
E2 (43-44): twelve years; physicians; unclean (Lev 15:19-31), isolated; touched kraspedon (Pate, tassels); immediate; she did not defile him, she was made whole.
E3 (45-48): who touched; Peter; power gone out; Mark 5:32; public confession confirmed healing, removed stigma, showed faith not cloth healed; Spurgeon: not mere contact but faith; Meyer: near Christ without touching; "daughter".
E4 (49-50): daughter dead; "do not fear, only believe"; fear and faith cannot coexist.
E5 (51-56): Peter, James, John (Mark 5:37); not dead but sleeping, laughed at; "Child, arise"; spirit returned; food; tell no one; Rom 4:17; contrast public prominent Jairus with poor anonymous woman.
