# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 17
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-17/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): stumbling blocks unavoidable, woe to the cause; word linked to bait-trap; used positively elsewhere (Rom 9:33; 1 Cor 1:23; Gal 5:11); love keeps from causing others to stumble (Rom 14:13; 16:17; 1 John 2:10); God wants us instruments of love not judgment.
A2 (3-4): rebuke in love then forgive the repentant; no fixed number; not our place to judge sincerity of repentance; reconciliation possible without full resolution (Gal 5:22; Eph 4:2, 15).
A3 (5-6): "increase our faith" shows forgiving is hard (Clarke); quality not quantity of faith; mustard seed uproots deep bitterness; Geldenhuys: sycamine roots deep and strong; Spurgeon: the eye does not look at itself, faith looks to Christ.
A4 (7-10): servant earns no special thanks for duty; God never our debtor, service a small return for grace; Spurgeon: our work a speck beside the ark, mature saints count themselves less than nothing; Pate: Pirkei Avot 2:8 do not praise yourself for learning Torah; Matt 25:21, 23.
B1 (11-14): ten lepers stood far off (Clarke; Lev 13:46; Num 5:2); Pate: border between Samaria and Galilee; Barclay: shared misery dissolves Jew/Samaritan barrier; sent to priests before healing, cleansed as they went; Morrison: only condition obedience; Clarke: God honours faith that acts before it sees.
B2 (15-19): only the Samaritan returned; Spurgeon: religious duty common, heartfelt thanks rare; Morgan: the question shows how Jesus values thanks; Matthew Henry's diary after a robbery (reasons for thanks); bodily healing not the same as salvation, the Samaritan healed and saved by faith.
C1 (20-21): hostile question; Geldenhuys: "observation" = hostile watching; kingdom "among/within" because the King is there; Morgan: they ask when while the King stands among them; Spurgeon: they did not recognise the Son of Man.
C2 (22-24): disciples will long for one day of the Son of Man; Feinberg: over 64 false messiahs in Israel's history; modern examples (Koresh, Jones, Moon, Schneerson); coming visible like lightning, not secret.
C3 (25): must first suffer and be rejected; glory through the cross; followers walk the same path (Mark 9:35; Matt 20:26).
C4 (26-30): Noah and Lot: ordinary life until sudden judgment (Gen 7; 19; 1 Pet 3:20); Pate: "revealed" root of apocalypse; Matt 24:21; Rev 6:15-16.
C5 (31-33): do not go back for goods; Pate: "remember" = learn the lesson, Lot's wife lagged, disobeyed, looked back; Spurgeon: the look showed rejection of God's command; Trapp: hard to leave the world like a dog leaving a fat morsel.
C6 (34-36): one taken one left; Guzik links to the catching up (1 Thess 4:16-18), i.e. rapture reading; Pate compares Noah/Lot where some taken to safety; Pate: v36 not in earliest Greek manuscripts, likely from Matt 24:40.
C7 (37): where the body is the vultures gather (Matt 24:28); Barclay: it happens when conditions are fulfilled; Guzik says present conditions seem ready.
Theology note added on C6 (rapture reading) in voices.
