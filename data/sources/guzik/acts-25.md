# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 25
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-25/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): Festus succeeds Felix, goes to Jerusalem; leaders renew charges, ask for transfer, plan ambush; Felix corrupt, Festus effective; Caesarea years protective; religion without God produces deceit and murder.
A2 (4-6a): Festus refuses Jerusalem trial; God's providence whatever Festus's motive.
A3 (6b-8): many grave charges, none proved; Paul denies offence against law, temple, Caesar; false accusation common for God's people; Christ defends believers.
A4 (9-12): Festus wants Jewish favor; Paul appeals to Caesar; wise, avoided ambush; citizen's right; Nero not yet hostile to Christians.
B1 (13-14a): Agrippa II and Bernice; Agrippa's background, knowledge of Judaism, some influence over temple; dynasty hostile to Jesus and followers.
B2 (14b-22): Festus summarizes; charges religious, about a dead Jesus whom Paul says lives; no crime; Paul preached death and resurrection; Agrippa's curiosity another chance to witness.
B3 (23): pomp; Paul the most important person present though unrecognized; later generations judge differently.
B4 (24-27): Festus: crowds wanted death, nothing deserving it; needs charges for emperor; innocence so clear he cannot define accusation.
