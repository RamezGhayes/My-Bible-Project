# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Titus 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/titus-1/ . Our own outline, not his text. Page heading: رسالة تيطس – الإصحَاح ١ (إرسالية تيطس). Some cross-reference numbers on the page are reversed (e.g. "تيطس ٥:١").
A1 (1): letter convention; Clarke: no other hint of earlier Pauline visit to Crete, so after Acts; doulos = lowest slave; letter for all Cretan believers.
A2 (2): eternal life = God's life now and completed later; hope on promise of God who cannot lie; Barclay: gospel offers life, not mainly doctrine or rules.
A3 (3): preaching how God makes His word clear in history; favorable conditions (Greek language, Roman roads, Pax Romana); entrusted to Paul and all believers.
A4 (4): Titus not named in Acts (2 Cor 2:13; 7:6; 8:23; 12:18); true son, partner, model; Spurgeon: trusted with hard tasks incl. collection; White: common faith (Jude 3); Calvin: Father and Son share the work of salvation.
B1 (5): left in Crete to set in order, appoint elders in every city; elders appointed by apostolic authority, not vote or self-promotion; rejects Calvin's suggestion that Titus only approved congregational choices; White: number not fixed, a system across the island; elders = bishops (1:7); qualification is spiritual maturity, not gifts, training, eloquence, money; list a standard and self-exam, not demand for perfection.
B2 (6-8): blameless = no charge sticks; husband of one wife = faithful to one wife, not a demand to marry; believing children = rules house well (1 Tim 3:4-5, 12; White: Titus stresses obedience more); asotia (Luke 15:13); negatives; positives; Wiersbe: sensible not humorless; Matthew Henry: who cannot govern himself cannot govern the church.
C1 (9a): hold to faithful word; grounded in Scripture not programs; learned from others; Luther: preacher both soldier and shepherd.
C2 (9b): exhort and refute; correct by sound teaching, not domineering; without biblical basis do not rebuke.
D1 (10-11): rebellious, empty talkers, deceivers, of the circumcision (1 Tim 4:1); upset households for gain; Barclay: silence by argument not persecution; three things not to teach.
D2 (12-14): Cretan prophet (Epimenides per White); testimony true; rebuke sharply; Jewish myths and human commands.
D3 (15-16): to the pure all things pure; Wiersbe: against Judaizing food laws; profess, deny by works; adokimos = counterfeit coin, rejected stone.
