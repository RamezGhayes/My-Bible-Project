# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 12
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-12/ . Our own outline, not his text. No Arabic page found in search.
A1 (1): beseech = appeal to will; therefore, Morris doctrine first; nine mercies from chs 1-11; Harrison pagans sacrifice to get mercy; Newell mercies persuade will; bodies = whole person, Greeks devalued body; 1 Cor 6:20; living = stays on altar; Lev 1:10; Deut 15:21; burnt offering; logikos "of the word" 1 Pet 2:2; greater mercies, greater sacrifice. Opens with J. B. Phillips translation.
A2 (2): world system pattern; battle is mind; not feelings or doing but truth; metamorphoo Mark 9:2-3; 2 Cor 3:18; four steps; knowing vs proving will needs Spirit.
B1 (3): truthful self-view not self-degradation; gift not maturity; measure of faith dealt by God.
B2 (4-5): one body; unity vs individuality; Christ common ground.
B3 (6-8): charismata apparently coined by Paul; 1 Cor 12:11; prophecy forth-telling, may or may not predict; "the faith" article: accepted doctrine or hearers' faith; warns about flippant "Thus says the Lord"; ministry practical; teaching + exhortation (fat sheep / burnout); mercy cheerful supernatural gift.
C1 (9-13): Paul knew Sermon on the Mount; love without hypocrisy; abhor/cling; good manners; fervent = boiling; hope heavenly reward; Morris patience active, tribulation serious; hospitality = love of strangers; "given" = pursue, same word as persecute v14.
C2 (14): bless persecutors; Matt 5:46; John 16:2; persecution from inside church.
C3 (15-21): rejoice/weep, be considerate; humility; Matt 5:38-45; visible good; Murray "if possible" peace not always possible; vengeance God's ("it is written", no ref); coals: conviction or lending coals; overcome enemies by making friends.
