# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 5
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-5/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text. Headings on the page print verse ranges reversed (RTL); normalized here.
A1 (1-2): couple wanted Barnabas-like praise (4:36-37); nosphizomai = embezzle, echoes Achan (Josh 7:21), Titus 2:10; Bruce compares to Achan; Calvin's list: disobedience, theft, pride, unbelief, distorting a holy command, hypocrisy; love of money and reputation.
A2 (3-4): Satan filled heart; lied to the Spirit = to God; property his, sin = false claim; word of knowledge (1 Cor 12:8); Stott: secret sins privately, public publicly; pride a threat, spiritual image vs real life.
A3 (5-6): Peter pronounced no sentence (Morgan: no curse, God's direct judgment); 1 John 5:16-17; 1 Cor 11:27-32; discipline does not cancel salvation (Boice); don't presume time to repent; inner sin harms more than outer opposition; Orr: revival brings judgment/conviction first.
A4 (7-9): three hours; same lie; "test the Spirit of the Lord"; equal partner, agreement with sinful plan is sin.
A5 (10-11): buried beside husband; ekklesia first in Acts, Bruce: new and old; Sapphira = beautiful (Aramaic), Ananias = God is gracious; Joshua: people judged Achan, here God directly, so church must not execute such judgments.
B1 (12): prayer of 4:30; unity a greater sign than miracles; Solomon's Portico open covered area of Second Temple.
B2 (13-14): purity earned respect; joined "the Lord" not merely church; purity filters shallow commitment.
B3 (15-16): text does not say shadow healed; Luke 8:44; Stott: episkiazo used twice in Luke for God's presence; Marshall: belief in magical shadows/garments common; LaSor: physician Luke not credulous; Acts 1:8 vs leaving only at 8:1.
C1 (17-18): after 4:5-22; Boice: Luke alternates inner life and outer conflict; success threatens institutions.
C2 (19-20): Heb 1:14; 13:2; Hughes: irony, Sadducees deny angels; God does not always deliver; apostles' martyrdom traditions; Spurgeon: angel did not preach.
C3 (21-23): immediate obedience; prison locked, empty; obedience before certainty.
C4 (24-26): no appeal to crowd, trust in God; leaders fear opinion more than God.
C5 (27-28): charge is praise for gospel's reach; "this man" avoids name; 4:17-20; opposition shows influence.
D1 (29-32): explanation not plea; xylon, Deut 21:22-23; Longenecker; Calvin: rulers above God are mere men; submit but God first; gospel summary.
D2 (33): no refutation so violence; Williams: as if sawn through.
D3 (34-39): Rabban higher than Rabbi; Mishnah praise of Gamaliel; Josephus on Theudas/Judas, possible date confusion; Stott: Gamaliel's principle unreliable; success not the measure; time weaker test than eternity.
D4 (40-42): 39 lashes; Marshall; Hughes: Gamaliel saved them but beating painful; Spurgeon: boldness for Christ; suffering for the Name an honor.
