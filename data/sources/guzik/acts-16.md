# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 16
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-16/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text. The page repeats some sub-letters (two "ب"); section letters normalized in the register.
A1 (1-2): Timothy, believing Jewish mother, Greek father, good report; Lystra background (14:8-21); first journey ~5 years earlier (Barclay).
A2 (3-5): circumcised for local Jews, not for salvation; contrast Titus (Gal 2:3-5); uncircumcised Timothy seen as apostate Jew (Bruce); good Christian not bad Jew (Longenecker); Timothy fills gap after Barnabas/Mark, God provides replacements; decrees delivered; churches strengthened; faith in Christ grows churches more than methods.
A3 (6-8): Asia = Roman province (western Turkey); how forbidden unclear (prophecy, inner conviction, circumstances); Troas not first choice; yielding plans; closed doors guide; Livingstone, Carey, Judson illustrations (not used).
A4 (9-10): vision, immediate obedience, first mission to Europe; God's plan bigger; "we" = Luke joins at Troas, maybe as physician.
B1 (11-12): Samothrace, Neapolis, Philippi; favorable winds 2 days vs 5 (20:6); battle 42 BC; retired soldiers; Roman pride.
B2 (13-15): no synagogue (ten men needed); riverside prayer; Lydia purple seller from Thyatira (Rev 2:18-29), worshiper; first European convert; Lord opened heart, God must open (John 6:44); pray for opened hearts; hospitality.
B3 (16-17): Python spirit (Apollo, nearby shrine); demons insight not omniscience; spirits exist today.
B4 (18): Paul didn't need demonic endorsement, like Jesus refusing it; freed at once.
B5 (19-24): money motive; Jewish identity fueled hostility (Luke Gentile, Timothy half-Jewish); Roman citizenship; 2 Cor 11:23; stocks; heart in heaven eases legs (Tertullian, not used).
B6 (25): joy from within in any circumstance; blessed rather than cursed.
B7 (26-29): supernatural quake; jailer would die for escapes; stayed out of love; jailer trembles; may have beaten them.
B8 (30-32): pistis = trust, reliance, commitment; no counseling or lecture, simple answer; cheap-grace concern; "you and your household" a promise, household not automatically saved: they heard and believed.
B9 (33-34): washed wounds = repentance shown; baptized that night with household; suicide to joy.
B10 (35-36): quake aimed at jailer's salvation; release order.
B11 (37-39): why revealed citizenship late: Spirit's leading; rights secondary to obedience; proof of citizenship, false claim capital; magistrates sweep under rug.
B12 (40): Lydia's house, encouraged, left; Lydia gently, jailer by shock, both families changed; "they" = Luke stayed.
