# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 1
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-1/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic URL arabic.enduringword.com/2corinthians-1-ar/ redirected to an index page.)
A1 (1-2): apostle by will of God despite low regard; saints = set apart, not super-spiritual; all Achaia = circulation; grace and peace in all 13 letters; Father by adoption, not as Jesus is Son.
A2 (3-4): paraklesis = strengthening (Latin fortis); Spurgeon on Paul praising amid enemies; Father as Paraclete, Spirit (John 14:16, 26; 15:26; 16:7), Son (1 John 2:1; Heb 2:18; Luke 2:25); comfort to comfort others; pride blocks comfort; Clarke (comforts to be distributed, minister by study not experience poorly equipped); Trapp on Knox preaching before death.
A3 (5-7): sufferings listed 2 Cor 11:23-28 = sufferings of Christ; Trapp (hottest days, heaviest dew); 1 Pet 4:15-16; Hodge (Christian suffering, not pain in general); John 16:33; Heb 2:18; thlipsis = crushing pressure; hupomone active endurance (marathon runner); Calvin (not submissive until laid low by God's hand); Acts 14:22; 1 Thess 3:3; Phil 1:29; Rom 5:3.
A4 (8-11): five suggestions (1 Cor 15:32; 2 Cor 11:24; Acts 19:23-41; Acts 20:19 / 1 Cor 16:9; recurring illness), illness plausible, present tenses; sentence in ourselves; God who raises dead, Phil 3:10; delivered/does/will; intercession; Clarke (apostle needed prayers); Bernard ("persons" = faces, posture of prayer).
B1 (12-14): conscience boast; simplicity, godly sincerity; Corinthians cynical (1 Cor 16:5); Barclay (no hidden actions, motives, meanings).
B2 (15-17): double visit, second benefit; reconstruction of events, painful visit, insult (2:5-10; 7:12), severe letter by Titus, collection (8:6, 16-17; 8:10-11; 9:5), Titus in Macedonia (7:5-7), written fall AD 56; escort custom.
B3 (18): denies charge; Poole (as God true to promises, Paul taught to be true).
B4 (19-22): Jesus the reliable yes; all promises yes, amen to glory; message shapes messenger; anointed shared, only else 1 John 2:20, 27, links prophets priests kings; sealed ownership, protection; guarantee down payment (lay-away illustration); Spurgeon (ill-treatment produced this verse).
B5 (23-24): oath; Matt 5:33-37; Heb 6:13; spared them; no dominion over faith; fellow workers for joy; Clarke (Scripture alone rule of faith; no council has dominion).
