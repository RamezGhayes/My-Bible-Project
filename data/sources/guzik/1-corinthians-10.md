# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 1 Corinthians 10
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/1-corinthians-10/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No Arabic page found.)
A1 (1-5): Continues chs. 8-9. Privileges do not guarantee approval. Cloud (Exod 13:21-22), sea (Exod 14:21-31), baptized into Moses foreshadows baptism (Rom 6:3-4), manna and water (Exod 16:35; 17:6) foreshadow the Lord's table (11:23-26). Rock was Christ, present and providing. Most died; only Joshua and Caleb. Clarke: Corinthians may have thought sacraments secured them even at idol feasts.
A2 (6-10): Warnings: lusting, idolatry (golden calf, Exod 32:1-6; "sat down to eat ... rose up to play", Exod 32:6; Cole on Exodus: "play" sexual, drunken orgies), immorality (Num 25:1-3, 9; cf. 6:18-20), testing Christ (serpents, Num 21:4-9), murmuring. Self-focus, like insisting on rights; meat a symptom. 23,000 vs 24,000: several explanations; Hodge mocks critics who seize on it.
A3 (11-13): Written for us on whom ends of the ages come. He who thinks he stands. Clarke: stands only while depending on God. Temptation common to man; God limits (Job 1:6-12; Luke 22:31); way of escape, Barclay: mountain pass for a surrounded army, not necessarily easy. Must take the way; relief by sin not the escape; being tempted not sin.
B1 (14): Article before idolatry: the idolatry of temples. May buy temple meat for home; avoid temple dining.
B2 (15-22): Judge as wise. Cup of blessing (Passover), koinonia communion with blood and body of Christ; shared meals mean fellowship. Israel's altar eaters partners of the altar. Idol nothing but sacrifices to demons. Cannot drink cup of Lord and of demons. Provoke the Lord to jealousy. Intent does not cancel. Supper compared to pagan meals as meal, not as sacrifice (against Roman Catholic Mass reading). Papyrus invitation by Chairemon to dine at table of the lord Serapis. Answers two ideas: idols unreal so nothing matters; Lord's table makes safe.
C1 (23-24): Lawful not equal helpful or edifying. Not only what harm to me, but what good for others. Seek neighbor's good.
C2 (25-30): Market meat, ask nothing; food not contaminated; it lost religious character. Ps 24:1, used as Jewish mealtime blessing. Dinner with unbelievers fine; if warned, abstain for the other's conscience (8:7). Not prohibition of socializing, but of temple feast. "Liberty within the limits of love."
C3 (31-33): All to God's glory. Give no offense = occasion to stumble into sin; not about offending legalists (Gal 5:11-12). Seek the profit of many that they may be saved; low conduct shows little care for the lost.
