# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 10 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-10/ (Arabic, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch, two per-verse outlines).
A1 (1-2): Caesarea Roman coastal city, governor's seat; Pilate inscription stone; nationalist prejudice against Roman officer; Lenski: 32 Italian cohorts of volunteers, loyal. Devout with household, alms, prayer; Hughes: knew Roman gods, came to see them false, monotheist; God-fearers: love Israel's God, not circumcised; Jews could not share homes/meals; real relationship with God though outside Jewish life.
A2 (3-6): ninth hour 3 pm prayer time (10:30); vision in mind but so real (shining clothes 10:30); addressed by name; fear = proper reverence; obeyed sending for unknown Peter; angel sent, but man preaches; Lenski: angels may connect people with God's servant, not more.
A3 (7-8): faith spread to servants and soldier.
B1 (9-10): God speaks to two, confirmation, two or three witnesses; Morgan: two men 50 km apart arranged by God while cities busy; roof as terrace; hunger distraction used; trance.
B2 (11-16): sheet, clean and unclean; God rarely audible, inner voice; Lovett: God needs no sound waves; command against kosher; "No, Lord" unreasonable but human; Peter often said no (Matt 16:22; John 13:8); contrast Cornelius "What is it, Lord?"; Spurgeon: let God's fire consume the red lines of the strict; God uses available not perfect; what God cleansed; OT purification of defiled holy things; Peter thought only food; three times emphasis; Boice: knew God was saying something, not what.
B3 (17-20): understood over time; Spirit spoke (vs voice 13, 15); God didn't say they were Gentiles; "for I have sent them" what Peter needed.
B4 (21-23): surprised at two servants and soldier, saw they were not Jews; God widening heart; invitation could not be refused; lodged them = against tradition; Boice: unthinkable for zealous Jew; broke tradition not Word; deeper reading of OT; Morgan: angels guarded house; Joppa brothers as witnesses (Boice: anticipated opposition); Gaebelein: Jonah also from Joppa with message to Gentiles, fled; Peter vs Jonah.
C1 (24-26): Cornelius waiting in faith; gathered; fell and worshiped, understandable but wrong; Peter refused; all worship of men/angels rejected (Rev 19:10), Jesus accepted worship (Matt 2:8; 8:2; 14:33; 15:25; 28:9) so more than man; Peter statue foot-kissing in St Peter's inappropriate; mutual honor (Rom 12:10); Stott: neither god nor despised.
C2 (27-29): entering Gentile house proof of change; Stott: chapter is about Peter's conversion as much as Cornelius's; vision about people, yet food principle stands: Mark 7:18-19; Rom 14:14; Col 2:16-17; Bruce: Gentiles' food habits made them unclean for strict Jews; came without objection because of vision.
C3 (30-33): praying to draw near, seeking Messiah (Guzik suggests); fasting; prayer heard and alms remembered though not yet born again; preacher's dream audience prepared by Spirit; Boice: both preacher and hearer prepared.
C4 (34-43): no partiality, foundation; Deut 10:17; 2 Chr 19:7; Barclay on Jewish customs toward Gentiles and Gentile contempt for Jews (omitted per project rule); Christianity first to cross ethnic barriers; v35 not accepted without faith in Jesus but God not far from them; God sees heart not skin, wealth, nationality; Lord of all = deity, Lord of Jews and Gentiles; summary of Jesus' life (baptized, anointed, did good, healed, God with him), eyewitnesses, tree, raised, Boice on eating and drinking proving resurrection; commanded to preach; Judge of living and dead, Spurgeon: Peter did not hesitate on judgment; all prophets; "whoever believes" to all; same sermon for all audiences.
C5 (44-48): saved while hearing and believing, born again; Spurgeon wished Spirit would interrupt us so; salvation not raising hand but trusting heart; Peter let Spirit interrupt; Gentiles before came as proselytes; circumcision believers astonished: same way, same degree; filling two senses: indwelling and special empowering; tongues to magnify God, audience God (1 Cor 14:2); unique, confirming same blessing as Pentecost; Boice: Gentiles on same level as apostles; can anyone forbid water; baptism = community's acceptance; OT promise Isa 60:1-3; Gen 12:1-3; John 10:16; 12:32; Matt 8:5-13; good people need Jesus.
