# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 16
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-16/ . Our own outline, not his text. Arabic link found in search (enduringword.com/ar/romans-16-ar/) returned only the Arabic landing page.
A1 (1-2): Phoebe likely letter-bearer; letters of recommendation (needs, deceivers); name from Apollo title "bright one"; diakonos, possibly a female deacon; helper of many.
A2 (3-5a): Priscilla and Aquila (Acts 18:2,18,26); Spurgeon contrasts Ananias and Sapphira; house churches.
A3 (5b-16): Epaenetus, Mary, Andronicus and Junia ("apostles" in broader sense, evidence not strong), Amplias catacomb inscription, Aristobulus household (Spurgeon: he unconverted), Rufus (Mark 15:21?), "chosen" = eminent; Nereus; holy kiss (Luke 7:45), Clement of Alexandria on abuse.
A4: Morris: real ordinary people; Spurgeon: rank and file, honor by degree of labor; women named; Newell on ministry vs authority; ~13 names in imperial inscriptions (Phil 4:22).
B1 (17-20): note and avoid dividers/deceivers; Morris: Paul's own hand; Newell; truth without unity / unity without truth; belly, smooth words, simple; crusaders; wise to good; God crushes Satan, Rev 20:1-3.
B2 (21-24): Timothy, Lucius, Jason, Sosipater, Tertius (only named secretary), Gaius host, Erastus treasurer, Quartus.
B3 (25-27): able to establish; mystery = full plan of redemption revealed in Christ; obedience of faith; doxology; theme = greatness and glory of God's plan.
