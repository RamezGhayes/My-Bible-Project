# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 14
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-14/ . Our own outline, not his text. Guzik is continuationist; neutral notes added (WCF 1.1).
A1 (1): love then gifts; prophecy not kerusso; Farnell via Kistemaker.
A2 (2-3): tongues to God; Pentecost praise (Acts 2:11; 10:46); interpretation = prayer/praise; not sarcasm; in the spirit; prophecy edification, exhortation (coach), comfort.
A3 (4-5): edifies himself = nature of gift; Paul values tongues; private devotion; prophecy greater in assembly.
A4 (6): revelation, knowledge, prophecy, doctrine (Acts 15:35; 28:31; a third ref printed ambiguously, omitted).
A5 (7-9): instruments, trumpet; child at piano; speak into air.
A6 (10-11): languages God's gift; not human invention; one original language.
A7 (12-14): zeal to edify; pray to interpret; mind unfruitful (Matt 22:37); cannot blaspheme unaware (1 Cor 12:3; Luke 11:11-13); spirits subject (14:32).
A8 (15-19): spirit and mind; Amen; Clarke on Amen, Jews; five words.
A9 (20-25): childish; Isa 28:11-12 Assyrians; Clarke blessing to curse; sign difficulty, JB Phillips scribal error view; obscure by clear; secrets of heart.
B1 (26): house churches; Spurgeon anecdote; Eph 4:12; Spurgeon spiritual luxury.
B2 (27-28): two or three, interpreter; error remains error; self-control; not order of the dead.
B3 (29-33): two or three prophets; judge (1 John 4:1; Gal 1:8; Eph 2:20; 2 Cor 13:1); Tom Stipe; Tertullian sister; first be silent; not confusion.
B4 (34-35): women judging prophecy; 1 Cor 11:1-16; laleo; Redpath.
B5 (36-38): word came to you; spiritual acknowledge; ignorant.
B6 (39-40): desire prophesy, forbid not tongues; order; Clarke; Spurgeon Methodists; Luke 19:39.
