# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 16
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-16/ (via WebFetch, detailed per-verse outline requested). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-4): warned so they would not stumble; skandalizo = an unexpected trap (Tasker); Bruce: synagogue prayers included a curse on the Nazarenes; v2 Saul before conversion; latreia = priestly temple service (Barclay); most 20th-century martyrs killed by atheist communist states, historic persecution often from other religions/sects; v3 ignorance of Father and Son; v4 not said "from the beginning" because he was with them (Tasker, Dods).
A2 (5-7): no one now asks "where are you going" though Peter (13:36) and Thomas (14:5) had; Morris: Peter's question was about themselves; sorrow filled their hearts; "it is to your advantage": spiritual presence with every believer (Matt 18:20), Scripture completed, walk by faith not sight (2 Cor 5:16), Jesus enthroned, disciples changed after Pentecost; Alford, Dods: Spirit's presence more blessed than bodily presence.
A3 (8-11): sin (truth about man), righteousness (truth about God), judgment (the inevitable result); elegcho wider than rebuke: expose, refute, convince (Bruce); Trapp: frees the world of its illusions; Alford: conscience knows right and wrong but the Spirit makes it effective; Tenney: guilt and helplessness until man sees his need of God; warning not to resist the Spirit esp. in revival; Bruce: Spirit defense counsel for believers, prosecutor toward the world; story of an officer in the British revival 1860-61; v9 basic sin is unbelief (Tenney: total rejection of God; Morris: self at the center); Spurgeon: Spirit turns the wicked holy; v10 ascension proves he did the Father's will, though he was accused of sorcery, demon possession, law-breaking, gluttony, drunkenness, illegitimate birth; Tenney: righteousness known in the law, fully shown in the incarnate Son; v11 ruler judged; a step between conviction and sentence: Spirit shows Christ's righteousness able to pay the debt; Dods: clinging to the world like a lost cause or a sinking ship.
A4 (12-15): "many things" points to the NT; answer to those who accept Jesus but reject Paul (end of certain Jewish rites, Gentiles without becoming Jews not spelled out by Jesus); "all truth" fulfilled in the inspired NT, Spirit still guides in agreement with Scripture; Tasker: truth about Jesus' person, words, works; Dods: "things to come" = the new order; v14 Spirit shows Christ; visions or dreams that do not point to Jesus are not this work; Alford: witness to Christ only; v15 Clarke: blasphemy unless Christ equal with God.
B1 (16-18): disciples did not grasp the cross was hours away; Trench: interval between death and resurrection a time of lost sight, then seen with their eyes; Tasker: different verb in their question, RSV more exact; Tenney: verb form = continuing discussion; Godet via Morris: if he founds a kingdom why leave, if not why return.
B2 (19-22): Dods: he noticed their questioning, needed preparing; weeping at the cross, sorrow turned to joy by God's power; Spurgeon: apostolic letters after Pentecost show no grief over the cross; woman in labor, joy tied to the prior pain; Clarke: resurrection confirmed him, lasting joy; Spurgeon: soldier returning with medals does not regret the battle.
B3 (23-27): prayer changes; access more open; Morris: atonement revolutionizes relation to God and prayer; Clarke: Christ mediator; Tasker: "figures" include "a little while" and the labor image; v26 he does not say he will ask the Father for them because the Father himself loves them; v27 their love for Jesus is evidence of the Father's love, not its cause (pulse and heart image); Barclay: Jesus did not change God's heart but told them God is love.
B4 (28-32): four-part summary (came from the Father, came into the world, leaves the world, goes to the Father); Morgan: sums up the Son's atoning work; Trench: four pillars of faith; vv29-30 faith sincere but hasty and overconfident (Morgan: he knew their hearts better); v31 Tasker: may be a statement, faith real but soon shaken; Morgan: he did not say "I told you"; v32 Spurgeon: near him in ease, failed in trouble; he stood alone to accomplish his purpose; Abraham and Isaac "went both of them together" (Gen 22) as picture of Father not separated from Son.
B5 (33): peace offered not only promised; peace with God (Rom 5:1) and with others (Eph 2:14); no illusion of ease, Trapp: world like the Strait of Magellan with contrary winds; nikao used about 22 times in John's letters and Revelation, only here in the Gospels (Dods); Boice: overcame in life, death, resurrection; Morris: bold claim hours before the cross; Maclaren: the world overcomes a man when it stands between him and God.
Theology note: 16:8-11. The Arabic page treats conviction as a work that brings guilt and awareness of need (Tenney) and can lead to salvation, with Bruce's prosecutor image; we added a short neutral note contrasting with MacArthur's forensic emphasis.
Second, exhaustive read 2026-11-27 (two WebFetch passes, vv1-15 and vv16-33) after the owner asked for deeper entries. Added details: disciples forced out, not leaving (v1); Acts 8:1-3; 22:4-5; 26:9-11 (v2); v7 list: Matt 18:20 presence, gospel preserved in writing, walk by faith, understanding from his reign, disciples transformed (wise, surrendered, bold, generous); "I tell you the truth" asks full trust; v8 before/after conviction ("no one is perfect" vs rebellious sinner); revival story credited to J. Edwin Orr's work on the second evangelical awakening; v9 Spurgeon jewel in the Savior's crown; v10 accusations list; v12 examples: Jewish traditions fulfilled in Christ no longer binding, Gentiles admitted as equals; v20 list of sorrows (loss of relationship, humiliation of their Lord, enemies' apparent victory, collapse of hopes), cross not a setback; v22 joy made them willing to suffer; v25-27 cross reveals God's love, does not create it (Barclay, Morris); v29-31 Bruce: faith real but tested; v32 easier to believe in the upper room than in the garden; v33 Judas plotting at that moment; Maclaren on beauty in conflict.
