# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic), Mark 3
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/mark-3/ . Our own outline, not his text.
1A (1-6): critics expected him to heal, so admitted his power, but watched to accuse; watched with critical eyes, knew about him without knowing him; one of the few places his anger is mentioned, at hardness of heart; God asks for obedience then does what we cannot (stretch out your hand); Clarke: the man obeyed instead of arguing; Wiersbe: Herodians a political party supporting Herod; plot to kill.
1B (7-12): crowds from many regions, drawn more by miracles than teaching; small boat; spirits confess Son of God, he forbids; Lane: belief that knowing a name gave power over a person.
1C (13-15): after a night of prayer (Luke 6:12); first purpose "to be with him", then sent to preach with authority; Morris: disciple vs student, shaped by relationship; Clarke: preach, heal souls, fight Satan.
1D (16-19): heavenly fame over earthly fame (Rev 21:14); brothers, partners, political opposites (Matthew the tax collector, Simon the Zealot); Boanerges; Cananaean = zealot; Geldenhuys: Judas perhaps the only Judean; Wiersbe: names in pairs because sent two by two; why choose Judas: not ignorance, not lack of options.
2A (20-21): no time to eat; family came to seize him thinking him out of his mind; reasons for their worry (left trade, threats, fame, power, odd disciples, no meals); Cole: Matt 10:36 echoes his bitter experience; brothers did not believe until after the resurrection (John 7:3-5).
2B (22): Lane: scribes perhaps an official Sanhedrin delegation; accusation admits his power; Clarke: Beelzebul linked to the fly-god of Ekron, turned into an insult.
2C (23-27): divided kingdom; Satan the strong man bound first; the risen Christ binds the strong man; believers fully freed.
2D (28-30): all sins forgiven except blasphemy against the Spirit; they credited the Spirit's work to Satan; Stedman: he would not warn them if they had already committed it; it is persistent rejection of the Spirit's witness to Christ, not a sin too big for God, but a heart that does not want forgiveness; Ironside: the warning is not to torment honest seekers.
2E (31-35): who are my mother and brothers; doing God's will; contrast with leaders under judgment; some manuscripts add sisters; perpetual virginity contrary to Scripture in his view.
