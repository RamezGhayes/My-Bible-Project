# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 13
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-13/ . Our own outline, not his text. No Arabic page for Romans found.
A1 (1-2): link to Rom 12 (no personal vengeance, but state may punish); "every soul" incl. Christians vs zealots; God appoints rulers, even to ripen a nation for judgment; Paul under Rome; Newell quote (Jesus under Pilate, Paul under Nero, neither reviled authority); limits: Acts 4:19; govt restrains sin.
A2 (3-4): Christians best citizens; "minister" = servant, not divine; govt failing to punish evil comes under judgment; sword = capital punishment (Roman beheading; crucifixion for lowest).
A3 (5-7): conscience sake, obedience never blind; taxes support restraint of evil; Matt 22:21; rebellion question: support most legitimate govt; democracy shares responsibility.
B1 (8-10): love permanent debt (Leon Morris quote); not ban on borrowing (Matt 5:42) but warnings (Prov 22:7); Matt 22:36-40; neighbor = real people; Spurgeon: family a "greater self"; religion without love fails.
B2 (11-14): urgency, return of Jesus; Christian activity while asleep; cast off before put on (Spurgeon: rags of sin off before robe of Christ); licentiousness, lust defined; armor of light = Christ (Morris quote); no provision for flesh; Augustine's conversion via this text (brief).
