# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 3
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-3/ . Our own outline, not his text.

A2 (2b-3): word in wilderness (1:80); forgiveness = freedom (4:18); repentance = change of mind and direction; Jews baptized like proselytes; Pate: cleansing; Christian baptism Rom 6:3-4.
A3 (4-6): Isa 40:3-5; 1:76-77; problem in themselves not Romans; all flesh; Pate: messianic age near.
B1 (7-9): brood of vipers; reliance on Abraham; fruit, love (Gal 5:22; 1 Cor 13:1-3); axe = judgment near.
B2 (10-14): sharing, integrity (Mic 6:8); tax-farming; Trapp on greedy publicans; soldiers: honesty in job.
B3 (15-18): is he Christ?; sandal strap; Spurgeon on valuing Christ; Spirit baptism (Acts refs, not used); fire purifies; separating Peter/Judas, two thieves.
B4 (19-20): Herodias; Pate: Lev 18:16; 20:21; Barclay via Josephus: Herod feared revolt.
C1 (21a): identifies with sinners, leads to cross.
C2 (21b-22): praying (Luke only); Trinity; Meyer: church anointed at Pentecost; Ps 2:7; Isa 42:1; blessing and power for believers.
D1 (23a): thirty, priests (Num 4:2-3).
D2 (23b-38): Mary's line vs Matthew Joseph's legal line; Josephus public records; Hillel; to Adam, all humanity; translator anecdote.
