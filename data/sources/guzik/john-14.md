# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 14
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-14/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): reason for trouble (betrayal, denial, departure); stop being troubled (Tenney); Spurgeon: Jesus not pleased by worry; uniqueness of call to believe in him (Maclaren, Meyer); both verbs imperative (Morris, Alford).
A2 (2-4): Morrison: Jesus' calm vs Socrates; "mansions" = permanent dwelling, Tasker rejects "stations" view (Westcott, Temple, Origen), links to 14:23; Morris: John mentions second coming less but includes it; Tasker links to 1 Thess 4:15-17; Morgan on invisible presence. No use of the term rapture; return to take his own at the end, permanent home, to be where he is.
A3 (5-6): Thomas; spiritual not spatial language (Tasker); Spurgeon on honest questions; Bruce defends exclusivity; Dods: the way is a person; Exod 20:2-3; Isa 41:21-29; 1 Kgs 18.
A4 (7-8): knowing the Son = knowing the Father; full revelation of God's love at the cross and resurrection; Philip's request.
A5 (9-11): who has seen me has seen the Father (Morris: no mere man could say it; Clarke); Tenney; words and works from the indwelling Father (John 5:19; 8:28); Exod 34:5-9; Trapp on ministers.
B1 (12-14): greater works by the indwelling Spirit (Bruce), greater in extent not kind (Tasker), Boice "greater things", Pentecost and Acts (Morris); prayer in his name (Barclay, Trench: unity of mind and purpose).
B2 (15-17): love shown in obedience (Spurgeon: from love not fear); "another" = of the same kind (Tenney); Paraclete as advocate (Trench; 1 John 2:1); Trapp: Comforter vs accuser; Alford: Wycliffe; abiding forever (Morris).
B3 (18-21): not orphans (Clarke: disciples as the teacher's children; Spurgeon contrast); coming in stages (Bruce); because I live (Spurgeon); commands become natural (Morris).
B4 (22-24): Judas = Thaddaeus (Tasker; Luke 6:16; Acts 1:13); Alford on "not Iscariot"; Maclaren: Christ's words one whole; Father and Son make their home with the obedient (Bruce).
C1 (25-27): Spirit as Jesus' official representative (Tenney); "Holy" (Morris); shalom greeting (Alford, Bruce); Morgan: peace from an untroubled heart; Barclay: not absence of trouble.
C2 (28-29): Father greater in position (incarnation) not in being; Dods: the remark itself strong evidence of deity.
C3 (30-31): Satan has no foothold (Alford); Jesus not driven to death by the devil's schemes (Dods); "arise, let us go": discourse continued on the way (Morris, Alford); Morrison.
Theology note: on 14:2-3 Guzik reads the promise as the second coming with 1 Thess 4; he does not use the term rapture, so we attribute no rapture position to him.
