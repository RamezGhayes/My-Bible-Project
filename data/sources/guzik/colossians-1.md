# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Colossians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/colossians-1/ (title: عظمة يسوع المسيح). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): Paul writes from Roman prison c. AD 63, prompted by Epaphras; likely never visited Colossae; Timothy companion/scribe not co-author (Clarke); all believers saints, "brothers" perhaps the faithful against the error; mixed heresy (Jewish practice + early Gnostic ideas); ancient joiners vs modern pick-and-choose syncretism, cure = knowing Jesus better; dyed cloth, Laodicea, Hierapolis, earthquake c. AD 60 (Clarke/Eusebius); Vaughan on apostle; Bruce on grace.
A2 (3): constant prayer for people he never met, full of thanks.
A3 (4-8): faith, love, hope triad as lived reality; real faith produces love; Epaphras faithful servant (servant not superior); "all the world" legitimate hyperbole (Robertson); gospel as traveler (Clarke).
B1 (9-11): filled with knowledge of God's will, first duty to know God; Spurgeon: ignorance brings disorder and pain; walk worthy flows from knowing God; echo of John 15; Spurgeon: good works include small acts; strengthened for patience and longsuffering with joy.
B2 (12-14): Father qualified us, inheritance gift not wages; rescued from power of darkness (lethargy, deception, discouragement), believers not Satan's slaves though tempted (Spurgeon); transferred like conquered peoples moved (Barclay); "Son of His love" Hebrew idiom; redemption = ransom, warns against pushing the metaphor (to whom paid) and against superstitious use of "the blood"; forgiveness = sins sent away.
B3 (15-20): possibly early hymn, unprovable; eikon = likeness and manifestation; invisible = unknowable in full, Philo on image/Logos; prototokos: priority and preeminence, not created (Lightfoot); creator: comet tails, Jupiter rings, sun scale, insects, chromosome; Robertson: universe depends on Christ more than on gravity; thrones etc. = angels exalted by false teachers, created and subject; Arius; Clarke: before all = God; head = source; pleroma = full divine attributes (Vincent, Robertson vs Gnostic emanations); "dwell" = permanent residence, Father's pleasure (Isa 53:10); fullness in Jesus not church, priests, rituals; v20 not universalism (stated, not developed); blood not magic charm: soldiers spattered at scourging not saved; real substitutionary death.
B4 (21-23): alienated = transfer of ownership; standing changed, not just forgiveness; reconciliation God's work from the cross; God as Judge (justification) and Friend (reconciliation), keep both; real body of flesh; presented holy, image of priest inspecting sacrifice; wanting salvation includes wanting holiness; "if you continue": continuing in truth of the gospel; conduct matters but does not save; Bruce: continuance the test of reality, saints persevere to the end. (Does not discuss loss of salvation explicitly.)
C1 (24): sufferings for others' good; "afflictions" never used of the cross; Christ's sufferings in ministry continuing in His people (Robertson: no merit in Paul's suffering; Lane: not atonement); Paul suffered for the body, not ascetic self-focus.
C2 (25-26): steward by God's appointment; mystery = revealed truth not puzzle; Jews and Gentiles one body, Gentiles same privileges (Clarke); not explicit in OT.
C3 (27): Christ in Gentile believers; Robertson: climax of wonder; deus absconditus / deus revelatus; hope of glory from Christ in us, not works or maturity.
C4 (28-29): preached Christ not self, opinions or stories; everyone; warning shapes mind and heart; mature every person, not dependent on Paul; Vaughan: false teachers' elite, rejected; labor in God's energy, athletic striving (Wright), against lazy waiting.
