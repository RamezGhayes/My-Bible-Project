# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 12
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-12/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): "gifts" supplied by translators, lit. spiritual things; Clarke on gracious endowments; three "do not be ignorant" (Rom 11:25; 1 Cor 12:1; 1 Thess 4:13); pagan past (mute idols) distorts; test: does it glorify the real Jesus (John 15:26; 16:14).
A2 (4-6): gifts, ministries (offices of Eph 4), workings (energemata, root of "energy"); Trinitarian order Spirit, Lord, God; accept God's variety.
A3 (7-11): manifestation for common good; Spirit always present (John 14:16) yet may manifest; wisdom (Stephen Acts 7; Paul Acts 23); knowledge (Matt 17:24-27; Spurgeon's conversion as example; true statement does not prove source is God); faith (Peter Matt 14:22-33; George Muller); healings (Clarke: not permanently resident, Paul could not heal Timothy); miracles (dynameis, Spirit overrides natural laws, pilot illustration); prophecy not mere preaching; discernment (Acts 8:18-23; 16:16-18; 2 Cor 11:14; 1 Kgs 22:21-23; Matt 16:23; 1 John 4:1-3; 2 Thess 2:9-10; Jude 4; 2 Pet 2:1-2); tongues as prayer language to God (14:2, 14-15, 18-19, 23, 27-28), not all, not proof of filling; interpretation.
B1 (excursus): has God stopped giving some gifts? continuation: Mark 16:17-18; Acts 2:33, 39; 1 Cor 14:12; no cessation text; responds to Heb 2:3-4 three-eras argument (Exod 7:11-12; Deut 13:1-3; 2 Thess 2:9-10); miracles mainly meet needs; names MacArthur (The Charismatics) and Alva McClain as cessationists; cites Clement, Ignatius, Justin, Irenaeus, Tertullian; cessation idea from mid-4th century; refers 13:8 to ch.13 notes; gifts not criterion for leadership (1 Tim 3:1-13; Titus 1:5-9).
C1 (12-14): unity a fact not a goal; v.13 identity, immersion into Christ (Rom 6:3-5); diversity serves one purpose; Jew/Greek, slave/free.
C2 (15-20): foot and ear cannot exclude themselves; nor exclude others; God placed members as He willed.
C3 (21-26): weaker parts necessary; Clarke on vital organs; Spurgeon: be workers not drones (bees not hornets); self-serving part like cancer; theology grounds practice; suffer and rejoice together.
C4 (27-31): order apostles, prophets, teachers; foundational (Eph 2:20); prophets judged by Deut 18:20-22; helps: Clarke (Talmud Levites helpers of priests), Spurgeon; Bunyan (Help at the Slough of Despond); old preacher on Bartholomew always named with another; not all speak in tongues; covet best gifts within God's plan; more excellent way: gifts vessels, love the content; Trapp: barrels.
