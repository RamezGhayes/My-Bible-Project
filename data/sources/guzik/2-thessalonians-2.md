# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 2 Thessalonians 2
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/2-thessalonians-2/ . Our own outline, not his text. Page title: رسالة تسالونيكي الثانية – الإصحاح ٢ – مجيء ذلك اليوم.
A1 (1-2): questions from 1 Thess 4:16-18 about the rapture; coming and gathering two aspects of one great event (Morris; Granville Sharp) but separated in time: rapture, then return with the church to judge (contrasts Matt 24:37-42 vs 24:21; 1 Thess 4:16-17 vs Rev 19:11-21; Matt 24:36 vs Dan 12:11); best MSS "day of the Lord" = judgment / Great Tribulation; they were shaken only because Paul taught they would be spared; verb = "has arrived" not "is near" (Alford, Morris; Rom 8:38; 1 Cor 3:22); false prophecy, word, or forged letter.
A2 (3): apostasy (definite article, specific final rebellion), church apostasy and world revolt both possible (1 Tim 4:1-3; 2 Tim 3:1-5; 4:3-4); rejects reading apostasy as the rapture/departure (Acts 21:21 negative); revival can coexist (Rev 7:9-14); man of sin an individual (Dan 9:26; 8:23; 11:36-45; John 5:43; Clarke: early church); rejects Protestant papacy identification (Calvin held it) as too narrow; spirit of antichrist 1 John 2; Hiebert: embodiment of sin; son of perdition = destined to ruin (Moffatt).
A3 (4): opposes, exalts himself, claims worship (Luke 4:8; Rev 13:1-6, 14-15; Matt 24:15, 21); literal temple in Jerusalem following early Christians, Irenaeus on three and a half years; Caligula's statue (Moffatt); naos = holy of holies (Morris); Dan 9:27; 11:31; 12:11 (Clarke: Paul echoes Daniel); satanic counterfeit of Jesus, parallels (coming, revelation, gospel, worship, signs).
A4 (5-8): Paul taught this in short stay (Acts 17:1-10); restrainer = Holy Spirit; Spirit not leaving earth in tribulation (Rev 7; 14), "removed from the middle" not withdrawn; Thomas: special indwelling of believers ends at rapture as began at Pentecost (presented as one view); mystery of lawlessness hidden, counterfeit piety (Poole), old evil intensified; destroyed by breath of His mouth (Isa 11:4).
A5 (9-12): coming by Satan's working, false signs (Rev 13:13-17); miracles alone prove nothing; Moffatt: satanic messiah, parody; did not receive love of truth; strong delusion; the lie = man can be God; Morris: their rebellion becomes their punishment.
B1 (13-14): thanks; chosen from beginning through sanctification and belief; election and holiness together, claim of election without holiness shaky; Spurgeon: salvation without sanctification a curse; glory (1 John 3:2).
B2 (15): four reasons to stand (persecution 1:4, judgment 1:8, deception 2:9, glory 2:14); traditions = apostolic teaching now in NT, not human traditions (Matt 15:2-3; Col 2:8).
B3 (16-17): past faithfulness grounds for asking more (Spurgeon); comfort and establish; Spurgeon: church established by God rests on His purpose.
