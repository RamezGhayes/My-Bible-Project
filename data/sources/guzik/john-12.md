# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 12
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-12/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): last week; supper likely celebrating Lazarus; host possibly Simon the leper (Matt 26:6; Mark 14:3); Tasker (not Luke 7's Simon); Dods.
A2 (3): pound of costly nard; humility and boldness, loosened hair; Morris; Barclay (pistikos); Spurgeon; Luke 10:39; John 11:32.
A3 (4-6): Judas' objection, 300 denarii about a year's wages; thief with money box (Tasker, Dods on bastazo); hidden sin; Maclaren, Trench, Trapp, Morgan, Bruce, Morris; Matt 26:14-16.
A4 (7-8): Jesus defends Mary, kept for burial; poor always (Bruce, Maclaren, Morris); Mark 14:9.
A5 (9-11): crowds to see Lazarus; chief priests mostly Sadducees denying resurrection, plot against Lazarus (Trapp, Dods, Spurgeon, Clarke).
B1 (12-16): palms and Maccabean nationalism (1 Macc 13:51; 2 Macc 10:7; Bruce); Hosanna, Ps 118:25-26; lambs (Boice, Josephus); colt = peace, Zech 9:9 (Tasker, Morris, Tenney); disciples understood later.
B2 (17-19): crowd because of Lazarus; "the world has gone after him" (Bruce, Tasker, Morris); John 11:50.
C1 (20-23): Greeks to Philip; Clarke; Bruce (temple court of Gentiles; Mark 11:15-17; Isa 56:7); hour has come (contrast John 2:4; 7:30; 8:20); Alford citing Stier.
C2 (24-26): grain of wheat; losing life; serving = following (Dods on diakonos); Bruce, Maclaren, Spurgeon, Morris.
C3 (27-28a): troubled soul; came for this hour; glorify your name (Clarke, Morris, Trench).
C4 (28b-30): voice from heaven, thunder or angel, for their sake; baptism and transfiguration voices; Clarke, Alford, Trench.
C5 (31-33): world judged, ruler (Satan) cast out (John 14:30; 16:11; 2 Cor 4:4; Eph 2:2; Col 2:14-15); lifted up = cross and exaltation (Dods: cross as throne); Tasker: "if" = "when"; Maclaren: cross the magnet; Spurgeon: "all" excludes no class of people, rich or poor.
C6 (34-36): Messiah remains forever (Isa 9:7; Ezek 37:25; Dan 7:14); Son of Man (Dods); sons of light (Tasker); Gen 6:3.
C7 (37-41): unbelief despite signs; Isa 53:1; Isa 6:10 hardening as judgment following refusal; Bruce: some did believe (12:42); Morris: they chose evil, not against their will; Isaiah saw Christ's glory (Isa 6).
C8 (42-43): rulers' weak faith, fear of synagogue expulsion, love of human praise (Barclay, Trench, Trapp).
C9 (44-50): final appeal; believing Son = believing Father (Clarke); light; came to save not judge; judgment wrapped in love (Morris, Barclay); word judges at last day; Dods.
