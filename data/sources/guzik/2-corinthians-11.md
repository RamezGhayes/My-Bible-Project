# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 11
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-11/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): "folly" because reluctant; they do bear with him.
A2 (2-4): godly jealousy = love for their purity (Exod 20:5); Redpath: God jealous for us, not of us; friend of the bridegroom (John 3:29), Clarke on his role; unfaithfulness in betrothal = adultery; Satan's subtle deception of Eve as half-truth (Gen 3:1-5), Tennyson; scorn of Paul's weakness = attack on nature of Jesus (Phil 2:6-8); "another Jesus" a super Jesus without suffering; Gal 1:8-9; apostle = sent, they merely "came"; church's tolerance the problem.
A3 (5-9): sarcastic "super-apostles," not Peter or Apollos (1 Cor 1:12); untrained by Greek rhetorical standards; actor anecdote "I know the Psalm, but you know the Shepherd"; free preaching seen as amateurish; "robbed" strong word, stripping a dead soldier's armor; Macedonian support (1 Cor 9:4-11; Phil 4:14-18).
A4 (10-15): boast = took no money; candor from love; will cut off opportunity; false apostles named; no one makes himself an apostle; Satan as angel of light, appearances deceive; Hughes on uncritical following; Clarke on Satan's forms; judged according to works.
B1 (16-21): reluctant, sarcastic; would rather speak of Christ (4:5); you bear fools gladly; bondage may be legalism or domination; "too weak for that" irony.
B2 (22-33): Hebrews, ancestry no qualification (Acts 21:39); diakonos humble servant, rivals used as title; labors (1 Cor 15:10); 39 lashes (Deut 25:3) and Roman rods; prisons, Acts records only one before this (Acts 16:20-24), Acts incomplete; stoning (Acts 14:19); perils (Luke 10:30; Acts 13:50; 14:5; 16:19; 2 Tim 4:14); daily care of churches, Redpath; who is weak; Clarke: boasts in what he suffered for Christ; Morgan: God's knowledge the secret; Damascus basket (Acts 9:23-25; Gal 1:18), contrast with Saul's earlier power, dating by Aretas.
