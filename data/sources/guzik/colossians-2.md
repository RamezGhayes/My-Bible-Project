# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Colossians 2
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/colossians-2/ (title: الرد على الهرطقة الكولوسية). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): struggle = athletic image (from 1:29), inner labor of love and prayer; reaches those who never saw him, us too.
A2 (2-3): parakalein = strengthen to face difficulty with courage (Barclay); unity by love; assurance part of the riches; mystery = Christ (Wuest); three mysteries in 1:24-2:3; treasures answer Gnostic secret knowledge (Barclay); "hidden" = stored like treasure, not locked (Vaughan).
A3 (4): persuasive speech; error attractive and wise-sounding; not yet deceived but danger seen.
A4 (5-7): absent in body, present in spirit (Bruce, 1 Cor 5:3); order and firmness = army holding ranks (Vaughan); "received" = handed-on teaching; rooted (tree) and built (building) (Clarke); Meyer: holiness by receiving Christ, not separate rules.
B1 (8): philosophy, empty deceit; verb = carry off as prey (Peake), plunder (Clarke); mix of early Gnosticism, Greek philosophy, mystery religions, Jewish mysticism, Guzik: both; Paul's answers mapped (Christ God, creator, direct reconciler; no angel worship; food/days not binding); stoicheia = basic principles or the elements (Bruce), basic religious principles shared by Jews and Greeks; "reap what you sow" logic that ignores grace.
B2 (9-10): fullness of deity bodily, Christ not half-God; Trench on theotes (essential not borrowed glory); against Docetism and Cerinthianism; complete in Him = present reality to enjoy; head over angelic powers.
B3 (11-12): Gentile Colossians spiritually circumcised without hands; putting off body of flesh like ragged clothes (Vaughan); false teachers required circumcision; Peake: historical inner conversion; OT heart circumcision (Deut 10:16; 30:6; Jer 4:4; Ezek 44:7, 9); baptism = buried going under the water, raised coming up; Paul does not equate baptism with circumcision; baptism a visible picture of sharing Christ's death and resurrection (Vaughan); rejects the argument (esp. by Reformed theologians) that infants should be baptized because infants were circumcised, as not Paul's point and ignoring Acts; power of new life not from baptism itself but through faith in God's working.
B4 (13-15): only God makes the dead alive (Ezek 36; John 3); charizomai from charis; cheirographon: charge sheet, IOU, or accusation; Spurgeon: each commandment accuses; Bruce: nailed the accusing document; Vincent: blotted out = wiped/whitewashed; title over Jesus' head (Matt 27:37), union in death (Rom 6); Wright: God nailed it, not Pilate; Rome and Jewish leaders' power turned against them; 1 Cor 2:8; powers now have only deception and fear; Roman triumph (2 Cor 2:14; Vaughan); Erdman: more than forgiveness, a triumph.
B5 (16-17): "therefore" from the triumph; Bruce: absurd to submit to defeated powers; Mosaic food laws and day rules do not apply to NT believers, shadow vs substance Christ; all foods clean (1 Tim 4:4-5), all days God's; may keep kosher or Sabbath if one wishes, but not as nearer to God and not judging others; Wright: OT rules for one ethnic people, not a multinational church. No mention of Sunday/Lord's Day.
B6 (18-19): disqualify; angel worship from false humility, too unworthy to approach God (Vaughan, Peake); boasting of visions (Barclay); puffed up (Vincent); spiritual pride and claims of special insight dangerous; holding the Head, whole body grows, not an elite.
B7 (20-23): died to the elements; religion of do's and don'ts vs positive relationship; rules perish with use (Bruce); appearance of wisdom, self-made worship, false humility, severity to body; Bruce: pride coexists with asceticism; legalism feeds the flesh rather than restraining it.
