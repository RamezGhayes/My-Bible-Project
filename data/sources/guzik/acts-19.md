# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 19
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-19/. Our outline, not his text. Ranges normalized. B3 heading printed "الواعدين" (likely for الجوالين), registered with [كذا].
A1 (1-2): disciples knew little of Jesus' work and Spirit's promise; maybe newer believers on margin; Paul noticed absence of Spirit.
A2 (3-4): John's baptism pointed to Jesus but not full faith; hard to decide if saved; "disciples" usually true followers but wider use possible.
A3 (5-7): baptized in Jesus' name, hands, tongues and prophecy; twelve; challenge to test Spirit's evident presence; Spurgeon: you will surely know. Theology note added.
B1 (8-10): three months, hardened, Tyrannus two years, all Asia.
B2 (11-12): unusual miracles; God does not endorse superstition but shows mercy to simple faith; God not Paul; not a pattern.
B3 (13-16): no personal relationship, no authority; churchgoers know only someone else's Jesus.
B4 (17-20): fear, name magnified; confession; magic books burned, 50,000 silver; word grew; get rid of occult items, destroy them.
C1 (21-22): plans; collection; Rome; Timothy and Erastus.
C2 (23-28): Demetrius appeals to wallet and civic pride; Artemis temple wonder; Paul did not blaspheme; gospel affects economy.
C3 (29-34): Gaius, Aristarchus; Paul restrained; Alexander; two hours chant; modern idols (sports, politics, wealth, entertainment).
C4 (35-41): town clerk; God's protection; ekklesia secular word for assembly.
