# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 15
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-15/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): vine as OT image of Israel (Ps 80:8-9; Isa 1; Jer 2:21; Matt 21:33-44); Jesus the true vine, identity is "in Christ"; airo can mean "lift up" or "take away" (Boice, Tenney), lifting branches off the ground; pruning (Morris, Tenney, Trapp); clean by the word (Eph 5:26). Page does not say the unfruitful branch of v2 is a lost believer.
A2 (4-5): mutual abiding (Song 6:3); abiding is a choice (Boice); fruit expected (Gal 5); Spurgeon, Trench; Bruce: Gal 2:20; Phil 4:13.
A3 (6-8): v6 not a full systematic statement; three readings offered: (1) former believers who end lost, (2) apparent disciples who never abided, like Judas, (3) disciples who waste their lives with eternal destiny not in view, like Lot; states there are no true disciples who do not abide; Alford against reading "burned" as merely used up; choice left open. Prayer tied to abiding and his words (14:13-14; 14:23-24); unanswered prayer may signal a problem; fruit = character, lasting, multiplying, glorifies the Father, seen over time (Matt 13).
A4 (9-11): Father's love for the Son as pattern (eternal, personal, unchanging); abide by obedience (15:14); joy not mere happiness, full through obedience (Carson, Morris, Alford, Clarke).
B1 (12-15): love as he loved (Dods, Barclay, Tenney); Clarke: Jerome repeating the command; laying down life; friends not servants because he revealed what he heard; friendship conditioned on obedience (Spurgeon, Clarke).
B2 (16-17): he chose them (Meyer, Morris); "go" = ordinary faithful living, not only missions (Alford); lasting fruit and prayer; back to mutual love.
C1 (18-20): world hated him first; Tacitus, Suetonius; Bruce on pagan accusations; Morris; Acts 9:4; servant-master (Tasker, Tenney).
C2 (21-25): ignorance of the Father; Trench, Tenney; Maclaren: words and works as revelation; Ps 69:4; 35:19; 1 Pet 4:14-16.
C3 (26-27): Spirit of truth testifies (14:26; 16:14); disciples testify, with him from the beginning (Luke 1:2); Tasker, Morris, Alford; mentions filioque debate.
Theology note: 15:2 and 15:6. Guzik lists a loss-of-salvation reading among three without choosing; we report this with a neutral note.
