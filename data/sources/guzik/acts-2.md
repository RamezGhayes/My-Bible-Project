# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 2 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-2/ (Arabic, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch outline).
A1 (1-4a): Pentecost = Feast of Weeks/firstfruits, 50 days after Passover (Lev 23:15-22; Num 28:26), linked by Jewish tradition to giving of Law; Hughes: good travel season; ~10 days after ascension, Jer 42:7; disciples' prior experience (Luke 10; John 14-15; John 20:19-23); lessons: promise worth waiting for, God's timing, to a group, not earned by formula; ruach/pneuma = wind/breath (Gen 1:2; 2:7; Ezek 37:9-10); fire = purification (Matt 3:11), acceptable sacrifice (Rom 12:1); Pierson on "sat/rested"; Spirit now on each individual; wind and fire temporary, filling is the gift.
A2 (4b-13): other tongues = real languages not learned; Jews from every nation; Galileans thought uneducated (Longenecker); Stott: reversal of Babel; some mocked "new wine"; many had been in Jerusalem at Passover. Tongues: two views (sign for unbelievers, 1 Cor 14:21-22; personal gift for prayer still given, 1 Cor 14:2); Guzik leans to the second, values private prayer in tongues (1 Cor 14:14-15); same phrase heterais glossais as Corinth; content = praise of God's greatness, not preaching; Peter preached in common language; maybe most spoke languages no one present knew; not gibberish; order with interpretation (1 Cor 14:27-28); not all (1 Cor 12:30); not main proof of filling; warns against seeking tongues as proof of spirituality.
B1 (14-15): Peter with eleven, herald not rabbi; unprepared yet from real walk; 2:40 more words; 3rd hour 9 am, Adam Clarke: Jews did not eat or drink before third hour prayer; Stott: self-control fruit of Spirit; "spiritual drunkenness" no biblical basis.
B2 (16-21): partial fulfillment of Joel 2:28-32; last days = Messianic age spanning both comings; Pierson on "this is what was spoken"; OT Spirit on some, now all believers; Hughes: no OT saint promised constant presence; v21 invitation to all; Prov 18:10; Word does not quench Spirit.
B3 (22-24): attested by miracles they saw; plan of God yet responsibility, lawless hands; contrast with denial (Matt 26:69-75); "pangs" = birth pangs, Bruce citing Bertram: grave like womb; started with what audience knew.
B4 (25-33): Ps 16:8-11 about Messiah since David died; Holy One bore wrath yet holy (2 Cor 5:21); without resurrection no proof sin defeated; witnesses main evidence; v33 outpouring evidence of exaltation; Luke 24:44-45.
B5 (34-36): Ps 110:1 quoted ~25 times in NT; shows Messiah's deity; Bruce: Yahweh texts applied to Jesus; Lord of Joel v21 is Jesus.
C1 (37): cut to heart = Spirit's conviction; Peter's sword (John 18:10) vs sword of Spirit; in revival seekers come to preacher.
C2 (38-40): repentance = change mind and direction, first word of gospel (Matt 3:2; 4:17); Spurgeon on broken heart; baptism public expression of faith; Jews baptized Gentile proselytes; Longenecker: sign, not essential for salvation; not magic; v39 promise to you, children, far off, all called; Peter did not call unbelievers or minors to baptism; promise for all who repent and believe in later generations; Spurgeon: all peoples; infant baptism not discussed; crooked generation.
C3 (41): 3000, church 120 to ~3120; pools near temple; 1990 California baptism anecdote.
D1 (42): Longenecker on "continued steadfastly"; four: apostles' teaching, koinonia (sharing; list), breaking bread = Lord's Supper, prayers (Boice: article = formal prayers); Boice: model not perfect.
D2 (43): fear = God's power changing hearts; wonders through apostles.
D3 (44-45): not communism: voluntary, temporary, imperfect; Jerusalem church later needed help; hospitality custom at feasts; Jesus above possessions.
D4 (46-47): temple daily, homes; with one accord; Lord added daily; favor with all people.
