# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 13
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-13/ (Arabic page exists; read in two halves). Our own outline, not his text. No Greek word studies on this page. Psalm refs printed reversed on the page (16:10, 2:7).
A1 (1): after 11:27-30 and 12:25 Barnabas, Saul, Mark in Antioch; five leaders; Niger = dark/black, probably from Africa, possibly the Simon who carried the cross (Luke 23:26), only possible; Manaen raised with Herod the tetrarch (who beheaded John, Luke 23:7-12), contrast.
A2 (2): ministering to the Lord first duty (Rom 12:1); Williams: word used for priestly temple service; fasting = burden, likely prayer for the world, God sends the burdened; not "I'm burdened, you go"; Spirit spoke maybe via prophet or inner burden; Morgan doubts audible voice, don't force experiences; set apart = separate, say no to distractions; Barnabas and Saul the most gifted; work (Eph 2:10; Acts 9:15-16), serious not comfortable; "now" no delay.
A3 (3): fasting and prayer = dependence; laying on hands = commissioning for a new sphere; deliberate sending with support, unlike "accidental evangelists" (8:4); Boice: mission from Latin mitto; no committees, surveys, marketing.
B1 (4): sent by the Spirit more important than church; no eternal fruit otherwise; Seleucia port of Antioch, some believers probably; sailed to Cyprus.
B2 (5): Barnabas from Cyprus (4:36); synagogue open, learned visitor invited; John Mark (12:25), later Gospel writer, grew up in Jerusalem, eyewitness of events.
B3 (6-7): Paphos west coast, wickedness; Barclay: Venus worship; Spurgeon citing Athanasius "god of lust"; proconsul = senatorial province vs imperial; Hughes: Ramsay inscriptions suggest Sergius Paulus and family Christian; unexpected door.
B4 (8-12): Elymas translation of name; Bar-Jesus = son of Jesus, Luke may avoid repeating; Spurgeon kite image: opposition goes with success; Saul Jewish name (first king), Paul Roman "little"; Lenski: home name and Greek/Roman name; Spirit-filled rebuke; blindness fitting (spiritual = physical); Paul's own blindness (9:9); believed astonished at teaching not miracle; harshness justified, eternal destiny at stake; Morgan: harshest words for those who hinder others; don't drag others down.
B5 (13): "Paul and his company", leadership shift; Perga in Pamphylia; Mark leaves: homesick, fear of mountain dangers, resentment at leadership change, Paul's illness (Gal 4:13); later dispute 15:36-41.
C1 (14-15): Pisidian Antioch about 220 km inland, region Galatia (Galatians); Boice: ridge about 3,600 ft; some think malaria (Gal 4:13), moved to highlands; synagogue order: prayer, Law, Prophets, invitation.
C2 (16-23): Israelites and God-fearers; survey God's acts (chose, exalted, bore 40 yrs, destroyed seven nations, judges 450 yrs, Saul 40 yrs, David, seed, Jesus); God's plan in history, Jesus its goal, we share it.
C3 (24-29): John prepared hearts; untying sandals a degrading task teachers did not ask of disciples; John saw Jesus as Lord before whom all give account; v26-27 rulers did not recognize though prophets read every Sabbath, fulfilled them by condemning; v28 no cause, Pilate; v29 tree = Deut 21:22-23 curse, Gal 3:13.
C4 (30-37): "But God"; witnesses from Galilee; Ps 2:7 proof of Sonship; sure mercies of David; Ps 16:10; David saw corruption; Boice: Christianity is announcement of what God did, not philosophy/ethics; don't miss Paul's focus on historical events.
C5 (38-41): forgiveness, justification from what law could not; cannot justify ourselves; Eph 2:8-9; Galatians written a few months later; warning from prophets (Habakkuk); resembles Peter's Pentecost sermon and Stephen's (Acts 7), gospel same ~15 years later; Boice: God judges sin unless Christ's work atones.
D1 (42-43): Gentiles ask for next Sabbath; Jews and proselytes followed; urged to continue in grace; grace foundation not just start.
D2 (44-45): Boice: few news sources, visitors drew crowds; power of the word, Luke stresses word 4x (44, 46, 48, 49); jealousy, contradicting, blaspheming (likely over claims about Jesus); Williams: rejected salvation open to Gentiles; Poole: feared Gentiles' rise; Jesus changes relationships.
D3 (46-48): Jews first; judged themselves unworthy; Isa 49:6; v48 Gentiles rejoiced; "as many as appointed" mentioned with no weighing of views; Paul went to open hearts, prayed for Israel (Rom 10:1); share first with your own but seek the open.
D4 (49-50): church born in a week; prominent women and leaders stirred; revival draws Satan's opposition; rejoice in quick seasons.
D5 (51-52): dust = treated as pagan place; did not take it personally; on to Iconium; joy and Spirit (Eph 5:18); Clarke: martyrs in fire happier than persecutors asleep.
