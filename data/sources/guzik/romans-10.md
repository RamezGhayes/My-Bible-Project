# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 10
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-10/ . Our own outline, not his text. No Arabic page found in search.
A1 (1-3): prayer, no rejoicing at stumbling (Rom 9:32); zeal not according to knowledge = Paul before conversion (Acts 9:1-20); finds good to say of persecutors; own righteousness (Rom 3:20); moral problem, not submitted; information not enough; personal responsibility.
A2 (4-8): Christ end of law, obedience no longer basis, law still shows standard; Spurgeon: Christ did not make law milder; Moses "does ... live by them" = perfect doing (no ref given); no ascent/descent; word near = word of faith preached; received immediately.
A3 (9-13): confess = agree with what God says of Jesus (God, Messiah, only way); Barclay kurios = Emperor and God; Wuest quoting Robertson kurios LXX, "touchstone of faith"; resurrection includes cross; heart not intellect; Spurgeon rest full weight; no distinction Jew/Greek; Spurgeon "Scripture says" general sense; "whoever calls" human responsibility balancing ch 9.
A4 (14-15): chain of questions; sent by God and church; preaching normal means; beautiful feet = motion; gospel of peace not works.
B1 (16-17): Isa 53:1; Morris hearing in first century; heard but no saving faith.
B2 (18): Ps 19:4; Bruce not literal, Mediterranean, Spain (Rom 15:18-24); Clarke preached in promised land and much of empire.
B3 (19): Deut 32:21 provoke to jealousy, ignored.
B4 (20): Isa 65:1 bold; rejection foretold.
B5 (21): Isa 65:2 disobedient and contrary people.
