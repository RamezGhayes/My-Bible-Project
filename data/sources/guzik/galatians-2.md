# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Galatians 2
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/galatians-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): fourteen years after conversion (cf. 1:18-19); Barnabas respected (Acts 4:36-37; 11:22), Titus Gentile (2 Cor 2:13; 7:6; 8:23; Titus 1:4); by revelation; likely famine-relief visit (Acts 11:27-30); privately to those of reputation, sensitivity; "in vain."
A2 (3-5): Titus not compelled; Luther: Paul did not condemn circumcision but its necessity; false brethren spying on liberty; not yield for an hour; Morris: grammar difficult.
A3 (6): no partiality; added nothing; Stott: office accepted, inflated status not.
A4 (7-10): right hand of fellowship by James, Cephas, John; Gentiles vs circumcision, not rigid; Calvin: polemic on Peter's apostleship to Jews and papal succession; remember the poor, collection.
B1 (11-13): Peter knew better (Acts 10:10-16, 44-48; 11:1-18); men from James; likely love feast and communion (Morris); synesthien imperfect habit; hupokrites actor mask; Stott: cowardice before pressure group; Barnabas carried away, Wuest "turncoat," Lightfoot: may have led to split (Acts 15:39); Poole: public sore, public treatment; Trapp: "the sins of teachers are the teachers of sins."
B2 (14a): conduct denied truth of gospel; Luther: Peter's example taught law necessary; stakes high.
C1 (14b): Peter lived like Gentile; Lightfoot on "being a Jew."
C2 (15-16): dikaioo legal declaration (Morris); eis committal (Stott); threefold guarantee (Stott), Ps 143:2 (Lightfoot); Luther against "infused charity."
C3 (17-18): objection minister of sin; certainly not; rebuilding works system is transgression; Stott; Luther: Christian is one to whom God no longer imputes sin.
C4 (19-20): died to law = no longer trusting it; law not dead; crucified with Christ, Christ lives in me; sarx whole human nature (Luther); Morris faith central; Calvin "for me"; Spurgeon past tense "loved"; William Newell; Luther law accuses, Christ loved.
C5 (21): setting aside grace; Christ died in vain; Gethsemane no other way (Matt 26:39-42); Luther cost of salvation; Peter later agreed (Acts 15:6-11); good results of conflict.
