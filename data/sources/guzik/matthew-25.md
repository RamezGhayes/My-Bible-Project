# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Matthew 25
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/matthew-25/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic link arabic.enduringword.com/matthew-25-ar/ redirects to the Arabic index, as for ch 24.)
A1 (1): continues ch 24 readiness; three stages of wedding; ten = usual, Talmud ten lamps; Carson (round number, virginity incidental); France (Jesus as bridegroom bold); Matt 9:15, Isa 54:4-5, 62:5, Jer 2:2, Hos 1-3.
A2 (2-13): Bruce (prudent/imprudent); all slept; France (torches); oil = Spirit Zech 4:1-7, uses of oil, Luke 10:34; true vs false believers Rom 8:9, Eph 5:18; France (decisive rejection, Matt 7:22-23); Spurgeon (door stays shut); watch.
B1 (14-15): Bruce; France (what readiness means; 6000 denarii); Barclay (value by metal); Carson (20 years wages); talents = time, money, ability, authority; Clarke (according to capacity).
B2 (16-18): Carson (no delay); method unstated; third hid.
B3 (19-23): same reward proportionally; Spurgeon (faithfulness not brilliance; small settings; master's joy shared).
B4 (24-25): 8 given 15 returned; Spurgeon (answer for self); Bruce (grasping master); Meyer (small effort); Poole (blame God); failures: didn't think, work, try; excuses.
B5 (26-30): Poole (sloth), Bruce (timid, indolent); bankers; Spurgeon (indirect investment); Carson on interest Exod 22:25, Lev 25:35-37, Deut 23:19-20, Ps 15:5; Meyer; Trapp; sins of omission.
C1 (31-33): not parable; after Matt 24:30; distinct from Rev 20:11-15; Carson (separating at night); minority view: living after tribulation; population estimates (omitted).
C2 (34-40): kingdom prepared; deeds of kindness, faith not mentioned; brethren = Christians and Jews; Bruce (suffering people generally, Christian poor first).
C3 (41-46): indifference; France (failure to do right); fire for devil, Spurgeon; aionion: Carson, Clarke, Poole, Trapp for eternal; same word as eternal life.
