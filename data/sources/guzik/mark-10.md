# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 10
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-10/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page arabic.enduringword.com/ar/mark-10-ar/ redirects to the Arabic index.)
A1 (1-2): Matt 19:3 "for any cause"; Hillel lenient vs Shammai strict on "uncleanness" Deut 24:1; Barclay lists Hillel's trivial grounds, Akiba: a fairer woman; trap: against Moses or popular opinion.
A2 (3-9): Moses permitted, did not command; hardness of heart concession; Lane: law protected the divorced wife; back to "the beginning"; God instituted marriage; leave and be joined; Trapp: glued; yoke image; Poole: bond stronger than parent-child; Clarke: one body, two souls; divorce like amputation.
A3 (10-12): not the full teaching; Matt 19:9 exception (porneia broad); Paul adds desertion by unbeliever 1 Cor 7:15; incompatibility not a ground, may justify separation; remarriage after unbiblical divorce = adultery; whole counsel (Acts 20:27); remarriage permitted after biblical divorce; only single or married before God.
A4 (13-16): disciples rebuke, Jesus greatly displeased; Bruce: "brought" suggests dedication; Spurgeon on his mother's prayers; youth worth saving; emphatic blessing; children receive gifts without earning; childlike not childish.
B1 (17-18): "Good Teacher" never used of rabbis (Plummer cited in Geldenhuys); question invites reflection on who Jesus is, not denial of deity; wanted guide not Savior.
B2 (19-22): second-table commands; kept by common interpretation not true meaning; Sermon on Mount; Phil 3:6 Paul once blameless; Jesus loved him; one thing lacking: sell, give, follow; wealth his idol; John 6:29; leaves sorrowful.
B3 (23-27): disciples thought wealth = God's favor; most readers richer than him; riches content us with this age; child's dependence vs rich independence; Lane: camel largest animal; Morris: humorous illustration, don't soften; Zacchaeus, Joseph of Arimathea, Barnabas.
B4 (28-31): Peter typical; apostles' special honor in millennial kingdom, foundation (Eph 2:20; Rev 21:14); hundredfold not literal, with persecutions; first/last reversal; Matt 20:1-16; Spurgeon: none loses by giving up for Christ.
B5 (32-34): Jesus walking ahead, disciples amazed and afraid; first mention in Mark of Gentiles; Lane: delivery to Gentiles = contempt by own countrymen; shame; Acts 5:41; Heb 12:2; Mark 8:31.
C1 (35-37): political kingdom expected; right and left seats most prestigious (1 Kgs 2:19; Ps 110:1); from 9:33-34 dispute.
C2 (38-41): cup and baptism; Lane: baptism vocabulary = overwhelmed by disaster (Ps 42:7; 93:3; 69:2); James first martyr Acts 12:1-2; John tradition; ten indignant, same ambition.
C3 (42-45): rulers lord it; greatness = slave of all; ministry for the served; ransom for many, substitution (Lane: liberation by purchase, substitution central); church not worldly power models.
C4 (46-52): Son of David, mercy; persists despite rebukes; Spurgeon: "Cold prayers never win God's ear"; specific request; Lane: Rabboni strengthened, submission; Clarke: apply to Son of David, lose not a moment; faith persistent, knew who Jesus was, humble, clear request; follows on the road to the cross.
