# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 27
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-27/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): Julius, Augustan cohort; Luke and Aristarchus along, perhaps by Julius's kindness; grain ship from Alexandria, size (Hughes, not used).
A2 (3-8): Sidon, Paul's character earned favor, not yet condemned; contrary winds; Myra; Cnidus, Salmone, Fair Havens near Lasea.
A3 (9-10): Fast = Day of Atonement, ~October 59; Bruce on dangerous season (not used); Paul speaks from experience (three shipwrecks, 2 Cor 11:25), not necessarily as prophet.
A4 (11-12): centurion trusts pilot and owner; harbor unsuitable for winter; majority choose Phoenix; warning proved prophetic.
B1 (13-16): gentle south wind; Euroclydon; driven; Cauda; secure lifeboat, Luke maybe hauled.
B2 (17-19): undergird; fear Syrtis; lower sail; cargo, tackle overboard, last resort.
B3 (20): no sun or stars, no navigation; 276 aboard; hope lost.
B4 (21-22): "I told you so" but aim encouragement; no life lost, only ship.
B5 (23-26): angel; must stand before Caesar; God granted all; faith in God alone when no other hope; Spurgeon (not used); island unnamed, trusts without details; Paul prayed for all aboard, promise from that prayer.
B6 (27-29): fourteenth night, soundings, four anchors, wished for day.
B7 (30-32): sailors flee; needed for skill; soldiers cut boat, beginning to trust Paul.
B8 (33-38): eat; not a hair; bread, thanks, broke; resembles Lord's Supper; lighten ship.
B9 (39-41): bay, beach; aground between two seas; St Paul's Bay Malta; Bruce/Ramsay (not used).
B10 (42-44): kill prisoners (guard bears penalty); centurion saves Paul; all safe; God's word does not fail.
