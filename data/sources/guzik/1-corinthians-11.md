# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 11
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-11/ (headings, people quoted, Calvin point) with detailed notes from the parallel English page https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/1-corinthians-11/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): believers need examples; follow Paul as far as he follows Christ; 1 Tim 4:12; links v.1 more likely to ch.10.
A2 (2-3): traditions = apostolic teaching; praise partly ironic; head = authority, responsibility, accountability, not merely "source"; three headships; submission not inferiority: Son submits to Father (John 5:19; 8:28) yet fully God (John 1:1; 8:58; 10:30).
A3 (4-6): veil a public sign of being under authority; customary among Greeks, Romans, Jews (Clarke: only prostitutes unveiled); Fee: "veil" misleading, not full face covering; women may pray and prophesy while showing they are under authority; shaved head: adultery (Num 5:11-31), prostitution in Greek world; principle eternal, outworking may vary by culture.
A4 (7-10): order before the Fall (Gen 2:18, 22, 23); 1 Tim 2; Eph 5:23; Trapp, Clarke, Poole on glory of man; angels watch worship (Eph 3:10-11; 1 Cor 4:9; 1 Pet 1:12); Stott: cosmic drama; none of the three reasons culture-dependent; scope: home and church, not society; elders men; 1 Sam 15:23 rebellion.
A5 (11-12): interdependence; Barclay (subordination for fruitful partnership), Poole (all men born of women), Redpath (man ruling by stamping foot should stay single), Morgan's story of unmarried woman.
A6 (13-16): Jews would find uncovered women praying unthinkable; nature: short hair normal for men; long hair on men not inherently sin (Acts 18:18; Deut 22:5); hair a natural veil; appeal to churches' common practice.
B1 (17-19): credit for meeting (Heb 10:25); division treated theologically in 1:10-17, practically here; factions reveal the approved.
B2 (20-22): agape feast joined to Supper; rich ate ahead, poor hungry; class consciousness; "I do not praise you".
B3 (23-26): received from the Lord (Poole's options, channel uncertain); "given thanks" source of "eucharist"; Passover; unleavened bread pierced and striped; Poole: Paul says bread and cup, against transubstantiation; cup of redemption; Exod 24:8; Jer 31:33-34; Matt 26:29; Rev 19:9; views: Rome transubstantiation, Luther (Marburg, "Hoc est corpus meum", "andere geist"), Calvin real spiritual presence, Zwingli symbols ("I am the vine/door"); Guzik: not mere symbols but powerful pictures; proclaim = preach; Redpath.
B4 (27-28): reverence and self-examination, not exclusion; KJV "unworthily" misread; nobody makes himself worthy; repentant sinners run to the Savior; examine then eat.
B5 (29-32): judgment = corrective discipline (no article); "Lord's body" can mean the church family (against Roman use); some sickness and death were discipline (1 John 5:16; Acts 5); caution against applying to every death; none lost salvation.
B6 (33-34): wait for one another; eat at home; Paul will set rest in order.
