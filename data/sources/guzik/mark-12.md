# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 12
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-12/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic Mark pages on arabic.enduringword.com redirect to the Arabic index.)
A1 (1-8): tenant leasing common, disputes attested; Morris: three years' use presumed ownership; Isa 5:1-7: vineyard Israel, tenants rulers; generous owner, patient sending of servants; killing heir to gain inheritance foolish; Jesus knew He was Son and would be killed; Spurgeon: "He is God's ultimatum".
A2 (9-12): judgment, vineyard to others; Ps 118:22-28 Hosanna psalm; stone imagery: rock in wilderness (1 Cor 10:4), stumbling stone (1 Pet 2:8), stone cut without hands (Dan 2:45); leaders plot instead of repenting.
B1 (13-15a): Pharisees and Herodians unite (Mark 3:6); flattery; Trapp: "a fair glove drawn upon a foul hand"; Spurgeon: leaders shouldn't dwell on praise or criticism; tax since AD 6, Zealots refused; three Roman levies (ground, income 1%, poll tax denarius ages 12/14-65); dilemma.
B2 (15b-17): denarius of Tiberius, Pontifex Maximus; using Caesar's coin = accepting his authority; submit unless it requires disobeying God (Rom 13:6-7); Morris: citizens of heaven and earth; Roman rule reflects Israel's departure from God; coin bears Caesar's image, we bear God's, give ourselves to God; Clarke: "full of consummate wisdom"; later distorted (Luke 23:2).
C1 (18-23): Sadducees (Morris: wealthy aristocrats cooperating with Rome); levirate Deut 25:5-6; absurd scenario like angels on a pinhead; soul survives death.
C2 (24-25): Robertson: claimed superior intelligence yet ignorant of Scripture; resurrection life not extension of this life (happy hunting ground, Valhalla); Cole: marriage ceases to have sexual significance in heaven; like angels who do not reproduce.
C3 (26-27): Exod 3 burning bush, present tense, patriarchs live; Sadducees accepted Pentateuch, Jesus met them there; Cole: first God's power then Scripture.
C4 (28-34): scribe impressed; essence of law love God and neighbor; love first in time and priority, obedience flows from love; scribe: more than burnt offerings; not far from kingdom.
D1 (35-37): David by the Holy Spirit calls Messiah Lord; not a trap but asking if they know who He is; Son and Lord of David (Rev 22:16); common people heard gladly.
D2 (38-40): scribes trusted scholars yet warned against; long robes, greetings, best seats, devour widows' houses through flattery; teachers unpaid but taught gifts to teachers greatest deed; Morgan: long prayers like long letters of a husband far from his wife; greater condemnation.
D3 (41-42): motive over method; two mites = quadrans; Poole's calculation ~1% of a denarius; mite = "tiny thing"; gave both coins.
D4 (43-44): more than all combined; out of poverty, whole livelihood; God loves a cheerful giver; value measured by cost (2 Sam 24:24); God needs no money; perhaps a scribe devoured her house; don't wait to have more.
