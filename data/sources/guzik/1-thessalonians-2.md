# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Thessalonians 2
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-thessalonians-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): defence against slander (Acts 17:5-6, 13); Hiebert outside attack; Morris confidence; Barclay list of charges; "in vain" = nature not results; Philippi (Acts 16:23-24); Calvin; contest imagery (Hiebert, Morris).
A2 (3-5): religious marketplace (Neil); impurity debated; Hiebert: Athenian testing for office; God tests hearts; Trapp; Morris flattery; Thomas pleonexia; Calvin greed corrupts ministry; Clarke: preachers examine motives.
A3 (6-7): no glory from men though apostle; Poole: honour comes, not chased; nursing mother; Calvin on humility; Hiebert deny then affirm.
A4 (8-9): shared own lives; Hiebert tender longing; night and day work, before dawn; Moffatt citing Ramsay; Calvin waived right (1 Cor 9:14).
A5 (10-12): God and readers witnesses; own life as example (Phil 3:17; 1 Cor 11:1); father exhorting; worthy walk, kingdom and glory.
B1 (13): received as word of God; works in believers (Eph 1:19; Phil 2:13; Eph 2:2); Spurgeon: Irishman 40 vs 4 witnesses; Poole.
B2 (14-16): imitators of Judean churches; Morris ekklesia; Hiebert: Jewish opposition to Gentile salvation without Judaism; Morris "sins" many acts; Clarke mercy to oppressors.
B3 (17-20): bereft in face not heart; Satan hindered; recognise, trust, act; Spurgeon perseverance; Acts 20:1-5; hope/joy/crown; Clarke.
