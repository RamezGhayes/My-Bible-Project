# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 9
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-9/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page arabic.enduringword.com/ar/mark-09-ar/ redirects to the Arabic index, not readable.) No heading covers 9:1 on this page (outline begins at 9:2).
A1 (2-3): three favored (or most likely to fail) disciples; glory from within (Matt 17:2 face like sun); Wiersbe: "transfigured" = change outside coming from inside; Spurgeon: concealing glory the greater wonder; answers disciples' doubts after Mark 8:31, 34-38; cross leads to glory.
A2 (4): Moses = those who die and go to glory, Elijah = taken without dying (Jude 9; 2 Kgs 2:11; 1 Thess 4:13-18); Law and Prophets; possibly two witnesses (Rev 11:3-13); recognized, so we will know one another; spoke of His departure (Luke 9:31).
A3 (5-10): Peter wants to stay in glory, avoid the cross; Spurgeon: Peter bold, lovable; Cole: Peter chattering in terror; puts Jesus on level of Moses and Elijah; cloud = Shekinah (Exod 13:21-22; 16:10; 19:9; 24:15-18; 33:9-10; Num 11:25; 12:5; 1 Kgs 8:10-11; Ezek 10:4; Luke 1:35; Acts 1:9; Luke 21:27); Father: hear Him; silence till resurrection; Peter recalls 2 Pet 1:16-18; new birth greater miracle; Spurgeon: living near Christ better.
A4 (11-13): scribes from Mal 4:5-6; Elijah restores all things; contrast with suffering Son of Man; future Elijah at second coming perhaps Rev 11:2-13; also came in John the Baptist in role and spirit, not reincarnation.
B1 (14-18): scribes arguing; Cole: why didn't scribes try?; Morgan: disputing scribes, distracted father, afflicted boy, defeated disciples; Jewish exorcists needed demon's name, so mute spirit hard; epilepsy-like symptoms but demonic; Robertson: demon distinct from boy; disciples had authority (Mark 6:7) but failed; ranks (Eph 6:12).
B2 (19-27): faithless generation; demon's last violence; "if" is about father's faith not Jesus' power; "Lord I believe, help my unbelief": doubt, not rejection; Spurgeon: awareness of unbelief grows as faith begins; boy as dead, Jesus lifts him.
B3 (28-29): prayer and fasting do not earn power, express dependence; Wiersbe: authority effective only exercised in faith, faith grows through discipline; disappointment in self shows trust in self (Mark 3:14-15).
C1 (30-32): avoids publicity for Jerusalem; repeats Mark 8:31; disciples don't understand and fear to ask.
C2 (33-34): silence = shame; arguing who greatest, expecting earthly kingdom.
C3 (35-37): Barclay: Jesus sat as a rabbi deliberately; first must be last, Jesus embodied it; choose to be last; greatness measured by serving; Barclay: Jesus did not abolish ambition but redirected it; child as property, receiving child = receiving Jesus and the Father.
C4 (38-42): John forbids outsider; their own failure (9:18); Clarke: maybe John's disciple or one of the seventy (Luke 10:1-7); not against us is for us (Phil 1:15-18); cup of water rewarded; millstone for causing little ones to stumble; dragging weak believers into disputes.
C5 (43-48): figurative, sin from heart; Lane: not demand for self-mutilation; Mark 8:34-35; Gehenna, Hinnom, Molech; Clarke: "every one has his worm" (remorse); Trapp: child with spoon empties sea sooner; Matt 25:41; Rev 20:13-15.
C6 (49-50): fire = trials, offerings salted (Lev 2:13; Rom 12:1); or fire = Holy Spirit purifying; keep saltiness, peace with one another.
