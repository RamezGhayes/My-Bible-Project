# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Philippians 2
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/philippians-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): "therefore" links to 1:27-30, now internal conflict; four "if" clauses rhetorical; Clarke; Spurgeon Spirit physician, Christ medicine (Luke 2:25; 2 Cor 1:5; 2 Thess 2:16).
A2 (2-4): deep unity; three steps (no faction/vain glory, no conceit, humility); self-esteem not condemned outright; not only own interests; Lenski, Wuest pagan contempt for humility; Spurgeon limiting ambitions.
B1 (5): adopt mind of Christ (1 Cor 2:16); Wuest Paul selected aspects fitting Philippians.
B2 (6a): pre-incarnate existence, eternally God; huparchein (Barclay) essential unchanging; Wuest kept deity; morphe vs schema (Barclay, Lenski, Kennedy).
B3 (6b): harpagmos held onto; equality possessed by nature; Lightfoot set aside some privileges; Isa 45:23 echoed later.
B4 (7): kenosis not giving up deity, omniscience, omnipresence; Clarke rights undiminished; Poole king dressed as merchant; labon = addition not exchange; Robertson real likeness vs Gnostic.
B5 (8): obedience (Heb 5:8); humble aspects of life; curse (Deut 21:23; Gal 3:13); Robertson lowest rung; Clarke, Spurgeon; link to Paul's imprisonment.
C1 (9): highly exalted; Poole, Spurgeon Father exalted him; name = YHWH, argument for deity.
C2 (10-11): Kent: universal recognition not universal salvation; Isa 45:23; Kennedy; kurios = LXX for YHWH vs Caesar; practical purposes endurance, unity, imitation.
D1 (12): not earning salvation; active pursuit of free salvation; complete in Christ not yet realized; Muller; Spurgeon against laziness; Robertson "urges like an Arminian, prays like a Calvinist"; fear and trembling = reverent awe, maybe joyful trembling.
D2 (13): comfort; Spurgeon inner spring; increases responsibility; lazy servant (Matt 25:24-30); changes will and deed; God's good pleasure.
D3 (14-16): includes complaints against God; Deut 32:5; Spurgeon harmless sheep; lights: clarity, guidance, warning, joy, safety; hold fast and offer word of life.
D4 (17-18): drink offering (Num 15:4-5; 28:7); faith = sacrifice; martyrdom added offering; not rejoicing in death itself; echo 1:20.
E1 (19-22): Timothy soon; good news expected; genuine care vs self-seeking.
E2 (23-24): Paul also plans to come, not avoiding them.
E3 (25-26): brother, fellow worker, fellow soldier; messenger with gift (4:18); homesick, distressed.
E4 (27): near death, mercy; not clearly miraculous; sorrow upon sorrow.
E5 (28-30): receive with joy, honor; gambler word; early Christian "gamblers" caring for sick and dead; completing service.
