# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 17
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-17/ (via WebFetch). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1a): among the greatest prayers of Scripture (Solomon 1 Kgs 8, Abraham Gen 18, Moses Exod 32); longest recorded prayer of Jesus; resembles the Lord's Prayer (Matt 6:9-13) in themes; Trench: Son speaks as equal; continuing intercession (Rom 8:34; Heb 7:25); eyes lifted = normal posture (John 11:41; Mark 7:34; Ps 123:1); Spurgeon, Morris: prays from victory.
A2 (1b): the hour (2:4; 7:6, 30; 8:20; 12:23); Father/Son relationship; Carson: mission fails if sacrifice not accepted; Dods: glory = death, resurrection, enthronement; cross shame to the world, glory to God (1 Cor 1:18-25); Meyer against self-seeking prayer; Tenney: cross reveals God's justice, mercy, salvation; Spurgeon: seek blessings so God is glorified.
A3 (2-3): authority over all flesh = claim to deity (Tasker); Phil 2:5-11; Trinity in salvation; Spurgeon on general and particular aspects; eternal life = knowing God; ginosko = experiential knowledge, present tense = growing (Tasker); Tenney: life is interaction with environment; if God does not shape our lives we may not have eternal life.
A4 (4-5): glorified the Father through all of life (Luke 2:21-23; Nazareth years Matt 2:23; 13:55); work "finished" by divine foresight (Rev 13:8); Morris; v5 asks glory from the Father (dependence); preexistent glory; Isa 42:8; 48:11 shared glory implies shared deity; Tenney: eternal equality; list of glory texts in John.
B1 (6-8): about three years; name = character (Trench); believers today reveal God's character (2 Cor 3:2-3); Twelve chosen after night of prayer (Luke 6:12-16); Judas already gone (13:26-30); "yours and you gave them to me" hint of division of labor in the Trinity; generous judgment "kept your word" (Bruce: future potential); convinced of his origin; Carson; vv6-8 show election (v6) and faith (v8).
B2 (9-10): not indifference to the world; emphatic "I" (Trench); Morgan: prays for the instrument by which he will reach the world; Bruce: he is the world's Savior, their witness depends on this prayer; Clarke: high priest interceding for his people; v10 only Jesus can say the Father's things are his (Tenney); glorified in them (Col 1:27; 2 Cor 3:18); Morris; warning to leaders.
B3 (11-12): reasons they need prayer (end of training, arrest, cross, absence, dependence on the Spirit); Dods; Spurgeon: redeemed still need keeping; perseverance rests on his intercession (Rom 8:34; Heb 7:25); protection from division, error, sin, hypocrisy; textual note "in your name"/"the name you gave me" (Trench); unity like Father and Son, natural not legal (Tenney), spirit not uniformity; v12 faithful steward account (Alford, Bruce); son of perdition = Judas, Scripture fulfilled (Ps 41:9; 109:8; Acts 1:20); Alford: Christ did not lose him, he lost himself; Morris: phrase describes character (lostness) more than destiny.
B4 (13-16): Carson: they would remember he prayed for them that night; his joy rooted in relation to Father (Isa 53:3 man of sorrows yet joyful); v14 gave the Father's word (Trench; Spurgeon: did not claim to originate it); not of the world = not belonging to its system, not withdrawal; v15 not taken out: warning against isolation; Job, Moses, Elijah, Jonah asked to die and were not granted; ponerou neuter or masculine (Bruce); evil one = ruler (12:31; 14:30; 16:11); protection from apostasy, worldliness, sin, not from trials; Spurgeon's bucket illustration of sin; v16 separation not eccentricity.
B5 (17-19): hagios/sanctify = set apart for God's pleasure and work (Trench: objects, sacrifices, people); God's work not only our effort; Morris: no holiness apart from the word; Spurgeon: believing truth separates from the world; v18 sent as he was sent (Alford: remain to witness; Bruce: agents); he came not as philosopher, inventor or warrior; v19 consecrates himself to the cross (Trapp: as priests, altars, sacrifices set apart; Chrysostom via Bruce: offers himself as sacrifice).
C1 (20): all believers; Morgan: he prayed for them and for us; he expected the disciples' failure to be temporary (Tenney); Bruce: no worldly credentials yet trusted God.
C2 (21): multitudes from every nation (Rev 7:9-10); Spurgeon: hold truth, reject quarrelsome spirit; sin the great divider, holiness unites; Clarke; Bruce: drawn into divine love; siblings differ yet one family; life-based unity of Spirit (Eph 4:3); church already one, failing to live it is disobedience; Carson: he cared for the lost while praying for unity.
C3 (22): glory given (presence, word, Spirit, power, leadership, protection); 2 Cor 4:6; glory seen in humble suffering, shines at the cross (Morris); shared glory makes petty differences fade.
C4 (23): unity by love not coercion or compromise; Carson: achieved in principle, growing to completeness; the world to see the Father's love; warning against criticism justified as love, though loving correction sometimes needed; unbelief has other causes (2 Cor 4:3-4; Eph 4:17-19; Rom 1:20-21); Trapp: world finds nothing harsher than believers' cruelty to each other.
C5 (24): "I want" (thelo) = longing; fulfils 14:2-3; "where I am" true now in part (Eph 2:6; 1:3); Spurgeon: prays as though already in heaven; glory attractive; love before creation (Mic 5:2; 1 John 4:8, 16): love needs an object, so love within the Trinity.
C6 (25-26): "righteous Father" despite coming suffering; world did not know, he did, disciples knew he was sent (v8); will keep making the name known; Heb 1:3; slanders he bore (10:33; 7:20; 8:48; Matt 11:19); love joined with joy, holiness, truth, mission, unity, else it degrades; Christ in them (15:1-8).
Theology notes: 17:9 Guzik reads "not for the world" as the focus of this particular prayer, not indifference; neutral note added (Reformed reading sees particular intercession). 17:20 Guzik: all believers through the apostles' word.
Second, exhaustive read 2026-11-27 (WebFetch passes vv1-12 and vv13-26). Added: John Knox deathbed; Melanchthon; Spurgeon: poured out his soul in prayer before his blood; child runs only to a father; Boice: authority cannot be avoided; Carson: we are God's gift to Jesus; Morris on relationships; God sees believers complete; John 10:30; 14:10-11; 8:28-29; rabbis' disciples scattered at a teacher's death; Westcott-Hort textual debate; Cyril via Alford: comparison of keepings implies equality; Ahithophel as type of Judas; v13 six roots of Jesus' joy; v15 ship in the ocean image, six reasons not to be removed, young vs old temptations (Joseph, Daniel vs David, Solomon, Lot), Carson on the world's hatred as the evil one's tool; v18 not philosopher (Plato, Aristotle), inventor, warrior (Alexander, Caesar); v19 Chrysostom via Bruce (Garden prayer); v24 Spurgeon: spirit already in heaven; v25-26 slanders list; love without joy/holiness/truth/mission/unity.
