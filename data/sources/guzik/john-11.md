# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 11
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-11/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): greatest sign begins with sickness; Barclay; Clarke (Grotius on Synoptic silence); Morris; Dods (name Lazarus); sisters only state the need; Spurgeon: Jesus' love does not exempt from weakness.
A2 (4-6): not unto death but glory; Alford, Spurgeon (God speaks of outcomes); Morris (love for each); delay of two days; Maclaren (purpose in longer grief); Lightfoot via Dods (three-day tradition); parallel delays John 2 and 7.
A3 (7-10): goes to Judea despite stoning threat; twelve hours = appointed time; Barclay, Morrison, Alford.
A4 (11-15): sleep; disciples misunderstand; plainly dead; glad for their faith; Morgan; Clarke timeline; Matt 9:24; Acts 7:60.
A5 (16): Thomas the Twin; Barclay, Tasker (desperate loyalty), Dods (pessimist yet devoted).
B1 (17-22): four days; fifteen stadia; mourners (Tenney); Martha meets, Mary sits (Clarke); "if you had been here"; Morgan; Spurgeon on "even now".
B2 (23-27): Martha's last-day hope (Bruce: Pharisaic belief); I am the resurrection and the life (Alford, Spurgeon, Dods, Clarke, Morris, Trapp); Martha's confession (Boice).
B3 (28-32): "the Teacher" calls (Morris); Mary hurries (Tenney); same words as Martha (Bruce).
C1 (33-38): deeply moved: anger at death (Morris, Trench, Calvin: combatant against death); quiet tears vs loud wailing (Barclay; Greek apatheia); Tenney; Clarke (true humanity); Spurgeon; OT weepers.
C2 (39-40): remove stone; smell (Clarke); believe and see glory.
C3 (41-42): thanks as if already done (Dods); aloud for crowd; Alford; steps strengthening Martha's faith.
C4 (43-44): loud voice of authority (Trench), not magic (Morris); John 5:28; Rom 4:17 (Tenney, Trapp); unbind him: people do what they can (Spurgeon).
D1 (45): many believed.
D2 (46-48): report to Pharisees; council admits signs; fear of Romans and "our place"; Trench (ordinary Sanhedrin), Morrison, Bruce (the temple later lost).
D3 (49-52): Caiaphas "that year" (Alford); Bruce; Morris; unwitting prophecy (Trench, Trapp); John 10:16 gathering God's children.
D4 (53-54): plot; withdrawal to Ephraim not from fear but hour not come (John 7:30); Maclaren, Morgan, Trench.
D5 (55-57): purification (Dods); will he come? (Morris); priests and Pharisees united against him.
