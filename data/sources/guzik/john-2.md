# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 2
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): "third day" ties to ch. 1 (Barclay); Jesus not gloomy, His presence blesses homes (Spurgeon); Dods on joy before His hard mission; Joseph absent, probably dead (Barclay); rejects tradition that it was John's wedding and the Mormon reading.
A2 (3-5): wine running out a social shame, perhaps poverty (Morris); wine as joy (Boice); Mary perhaps expecting a messianic display; "woman" respectful yet marks changed relation (Barclay, Morris, Bruce); Tasker: Mary trusted His timing; "hour" = His death; "whatever He says" points to Him not herself; Barnhouse against harsh-son/tender-mother idea.
A3 (6-7): stone jars for purification; Jesus uses what is there; Spurgeon on honest "two or three measures"; servants share in the work, full obedience "to the brim".
A4 (8-10): tested at once; Alford: no fermentation process needed; servants knew the source, steward only the quality (Dods); contrast with refusing to turn stones to bread; rejects view that it was grape juice, wine diluted (Barclay); best kept for last (Spurgeon, Morrison), Satan reverses the order.
A5 (11-12): first of seven signs, old order to new life (Morris); Moses turned water to blood, Jesus water to wine (Ex 7:17-21; John 1:17); sign as pointer (Kittel); glory shown in mercy for a social need; disciples believed more; move to Capernaum (Matt 4:13; Bruce, Trench); brothers most likely children of Joseph and Mary (Matt 1:25; Luke 2:7; Tenney mentions cousin view).
B1 (13-17): Passover crowds, temple tax currency, profiteering (Barclay, Morris); whip made deliberately, display of authority; trade spoiled the Court of the Gentiles (Tasker, Morris); Ps 69:9; pattern: transformation then cleansing.
B2 (18-22): demand for a sign misguided, the act itself was a sign (Bruce); temple = His body; charge at trial (Matt 26:60-61; Mark 14:57-59) and mockery (Matt 27:40); church as temple (Eph 2:19-22; 1 Pet 2:5); He raises Himself, also Father and Spirit (Rom 1:4; 8:11; Gal 1:1); Tenney on puzzling sayings explained later; believed after resurrection (Ps 16:10).
B3 (23-25): faith based on signs; Jesus did not entrust Himself, knew what was in man; Morgan: admiration not enough; Luther via Dods: childlike faith that may grow; Bruce, Alford on His knowledge.
Note added in voices: Guzik and MacArthur differ on whether the Cana wine was fermented (reported, no Reformed issue).
