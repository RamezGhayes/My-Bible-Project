# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 11 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-11/ (Arabic, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch, two per-verse outlines).
A1 (1-3): news not secret; Gentiles need not become Jews; objection more to table fellowship than to God's work; eating = close relationship; six witnesses wise.
A2 (4-15): Boice: humility, no apostolic rank, careful order; God repeats story for emphasis; sheet = church, clean and unclean together (Eph 2:11-18); entering Gentile house forbidden by custom not Law; angel had stood in the house; Spirit as on us = God's approval.
A3 (16-18): Jesus' word (Mark 1:8 / Acts 1:5); discern God's direction, not persuade God; test: Jesus' words + OT promise (Isa 49:6); success alone does not prove a ministry is of God; silence then glorifying, soft hearts; acceptance then, objections took long time.
B1 (19-21): scattered spoke to Jews only; unnamed Cypriots and Cyrenians "heroes"; Antioch: Seleucus I c. 300 BC, Antioch on Orontes, 500,000+, third city of empire, ~300 mi N of Jerusalem; commerce and immorality; Bruce: Daphne temple, Artemis/Apollo, Astarte prostitution; Hughes: Juvenal, Orontes into Tiber; contrast Cornelius; hand of the Lord: can lead people to a club/church without it but not to the Lord; believe + turn = faith + repentance.
B2 (22-24): Barnabas generous (4:36-37), welcomed Saul (9:26-28); Boice: news reaches leaders; saw grace, rejoiced; grace not human rules; exhorted to remain with resolve; leader's main work encouraging; Eph 4:11-16.
B3 (25-26): seek = pursue (cites MacArthur on laborious search); left work to find Saul; Tarsus years not wasted; year of teaching; Hughes: preachers of Antioch; formal teaching + informal evangelism; other names for believers; Christian = belonging to Christ, Latin suffix, soldiers named for commander; Gaebelein: Antiochenes nicknamed, maybe insult; Boice: Ironside "Jesus man" in China; take name Christian before denominational labels; martyr Sanctus "I am a Christian" (from church history).
B4 (27-30): Agabus, how shown unknown; Bruce: poor harvests under Claudius in Rome, Greece, Egypt, Judea; church prepared; gave according to means, determined; investment not loss; Boice: first such relief act; explains name Christians.
