# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Romans 1
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/romans-1/ . Our own outline, not his text.
I1 (intro): influence on Augustine (Rom 13:13-14), Luther (Ps 31:1, Rom 1:17), Wesley; testimonies of Luther, Melanchthon, Calvin (key to all Scripture), Coleridge, Godet (cathedral), Morgan, Lenski; 2 Pet 3:15-16.
A1 (1): Paul's life (Acts, Gal 1, 2 Cor 11-12); written from Corinth, 3rd journey (Acts 20:2-3; Rom 16:1, 23), AD 53-58; substitute for a visit; Clement of Rome knew it; Bruce, Barclay on short version; doulos = total devotion (Morris), higher than royal title (Poole); "separated" and Pharisee (Clarke); "God" 153 times (Morris); Bruce citing Tyndale on key terms.
A2 (2-6): gospel promised by prophets; Christ seed of David, Son of God by resurrection (John 2:19); horizo = define, "horizon" (Clarke); "Lord" = LXX Yahweh (Morris); grace, apostleship, obedience of faith among nations.
A3 (7-15): church not founded by Paul or Peter, maybe from Pentecost (Acts 2:10); saints by calling (Spurgeon); grace (Greek) + peace (Jewish); faith famous despite pressure under Nero (Bruce); prayer (Spurgeon); mutual encouragement; debtor to Rome; "ready" examples (Spurgeon: Rom 15:1; Acts 21:13; 2 Cor 6:10; 2 Tim 4:6); Zinzendorf story; Newell on boldness; came as prisoner (Spurgeon).
A4 (16-17): Morris on importance; Seneca "sink of iniquity", Juvenal sewer; philosophers Epictetus, Epicurus, Seneca on need of salvation; dikaioo (Barclay); Lenski on Luther; Murray on righteousness of God; Harrison; Poole "by faith only"; Hab 2:4; Gal 3:1-3.
B1 (18a): wrath holy not selfish; Murray holy revulsion; Morris nothing to be saved from; not good news itself; ungodliness vs unrighteousness; suppress.
B2 (18b-23): creation shows eternal power and Godhead, without excuse; Lenski no excuse; Spurgeon knowing without glorifying useless, gratitude, meal; rejection darkens, itself judgment; eikon.
B3 (24-32): "gave them up" x3, judgment of letting sin run its course, Hos 4:17; "the lie" with article (the lie of being like God; reference printed as Gen 5:3, likely typo, not used); Corinth; "likewise" links female and male; Roman culture, Nero; sin carries its penalty; adokimos mind like failed coin; Wuest; vice list mixes respectable sins; envy crucified Jesus (Matt 27:18); Clarke on whisperers and boasters; v32 approve.
