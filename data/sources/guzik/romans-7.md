# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 7
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-7/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No readable Arabic page.)
A1 (1-3): links 6:14, 6:15-23; no article before "law" = broader than Mosaic; marriage: death ends contract.
A2 (4): extends 6:3-8 to death to law; Morris "through with the law"; belong to Christ, bear fruit.
A3 (5): law stirred sinful passions; developed in 7-14. A4 (6): law neither justifies nor sanctifies; newness of Spirit.
B1 (7a): is law sin? rejected. B2 (7b): X-ray; covet; speed limit.
B3 (8): Harrison: opportunity = military base of operations; Augustine Confessions pears; Prohibition; hotel "no fishing" signs; fault in heart; apart from law sin dead.
B4 (9): Morris: not truly alive, not yet killed by law; Lenski: false security, living on volcano.
B5 (10-12): commandment for life brought death; sin's deceptions (satisfaction, excuse, escape); John 8:32; law holy; Rom 6:2; 7:4.
C1 (13): exceedingly sinful by contrast and provocation; Spurgeon: sin hides our injury; uses sin's own name; Clarke: law alarms and awakens; Wiersbe: law a magnet not a dynamo.
C2 (14): sarkikos; prisoner illustration; carnal not = non-Christian; Luther "proof of the spiritual and wise man".
D1 (15-19): desire present, power absent; law gives rules not power; C.S. Lewis "no man knows how bad he is until he has tried to be good"; "no longer I" = owning and disowning (Wuest: saint may say it in passion, sinner should not make it principle).
D2 (20-23): two selves; new man the real Paul; real self delights in law; struggle in own strength; debate acknowledged: "In a sense this is an irrelevant question"; Christian may experience, non-Christian can only experience; Griffith Thomas via Morris: "regenerate or unregenerate".
E1 (24): wretched = exhausted by hard labor; Spurgeon corpse image, "this is just what the Christian has to do"; Calvin and Murray: whole mass of sin, Calvin "relics"; Morris: great saints bewail sinfulness; Poole: panting after deliverance; "I" ~40 times since 7:13; legalism -> denial or despair.
E2 (25): through Jesus (bridge), Lord (master); works through us not instead; law as teacher, coach, doctor.
