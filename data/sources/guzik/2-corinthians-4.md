# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 4
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-4/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Outline: A. How a more glorious covenant should be presented; B. Treasure in clay pots; C. Our light affliction.
A1 (1-2): greatness of calling keeps from losing heart; Greek "lose heart" = faint-hearted coward with bad conduct; mercy; renounced hidden things; "deceitfully" only here in NT = dilute/adulterate; Spurgeon (speak only if God sent the message; adapting truth to the age murders it); Hughes on craftiness and on higher scrutiny before God; suffering not proof of unfaithfulness.
A2 (3-4): veiled only to the perishing; Calvin sun and blind; Spurgeon lost to God, church, world, themselves; god of this age blinds minds, yet lovers of darkness responsible (John 3:19); John 12:31; 14:30; Eph 2:2; 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ps 24:1; Luke 4:5-8; Hughes apparent sovereignty, 1 Tim 1:17; Poole; Manichaeans; Augustine, Origen, Chrysostom defensive reading; Calvin Baal analogy; Morgan unbelief from refusing; "light" rare word, LXX Ps 44:3; 78:14; Hodge brightness of radiant body; Acts 9:1-19; pray.
B1 (5-6): not self, Robertson "poor and disgusting topic"; not moral reform, Hodge "fruit without trees"; bondservants; Gen 1:3 literal; Damascus (Acts 9:1-9); shine out not hoard (curtains image); face of Jesus John 14:9; 17:24.
B2 (7): treasure = gospel and glory; cheap clay pots, Kruse; no one worthy; Jesus without special packaging; power of God; risky vessels magnify God; Gideon Judg 7:20.
B3 (8-12): hard pressed = hunted; 40 men Acts 23:12; four contrasts; Paul's lived experience; dying of Jesus as spiritual reality; Phil 3:10; broken vial fragrance; death in us life in you; Morgan suffering made preaching more powerful; Poole ironic reading.
B4 (13-15): same spirit of faith, believing leads to speaking; Morgan minister who does not believe should not talk; resurrection; immediate goal and ultimate goal (thanksgiving to God's glory); two errors.
C1 (16): "therefore"; outward man perishing, inward renewed; links to 4:1, 4:7, 4:11.
C2 (17-18): 2 Cor 11:23-28 list; Harris theology forged in suffering; light and momentary vs eternity; affliction produces glory; scale image; Morgan birth through travail; Dodd in Clarke, Hebrew kabad heavy/glorious; light compared to others, deserts, Jesus, glory; Rom 8:17; suffering can embitter.
