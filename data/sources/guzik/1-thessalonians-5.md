# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Thessalonians 5
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-thessalonians-5/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): taught on short visit (Acts 17:2); Hiebert times vs seasons; Matt 16:1-3; Day of the Lord OT concept, a period fulfilled at Jesus' glorious return and earthly reign; thief, no date-setting (Matt 24:36); Trapp certain, sudden, unavoidable; two aspects of return: sudden "in the air" (4:16-17) and expected coming with saints (Zech 14:5) after global catastrophe, birth pains (Matt 24:8). Reformed note added.
A2 (4-5): sons of light, Semitic idiom (Morris); not wholly surprised; backsliders get ready.
A3 (6-8): sleep = apathy, not death of 4:13-15; Spurgeon doctor/captain/prisoner illustrations; sobriety; Festus (Acts 26:24; 2 Cor 5:13); armour (Eph 6), flexible imagery.
A4 (9-10): wrath = God's wrath; destined through Adam (Rom 5:12-19) but Christ took place; sovereignty vs "obtaining"; awake or asleep; Jesus' real death.
A5 (11): mutual encouragement; Hiebert: pastoral care work of every believer.
B1 (12-13): leaders known by service; labour, lead, admonish (Hiebert one group); Clarke/Calvin lazy ministers; Poole esteem with love; pray or find another church.
B2 (14-15): unruly = idle (4:11-12), fainthearted = grieving (4:13-17), weak = tempted; no repaying evil.
B3 (16-18): joy not circumstantial; Spurgeon; continual fellowship (Matt 6:6); thanks because God's hand rules, not fate.
B4 (19-22): quench like fire (Mark 9:48); prophecies despised due to misuse (idle, date-setters; 2 Thess 2); Bereans (Acts 17:10-12).
C1 (23-24): holoteleis; God sanctifies; trichotomy discussion (Mark 12:30; 1 Cor 7:34), Alford spirit higher than soul; spirit-soul-body hierarchy; body redeemed. Reformed (dichotomy) note added.
C2 (25-26): apostle asks prayer; Clarke; holy kiss same-sex, later regulated (Hiebert).
C3 (27-28): reasons letter read to all; Poole people's language; grace; Denney.
