# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 13
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-13/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-4): first visit 18 months (Acts 18:11), painful second, third coming; Deut 19:15 witnesses = three visits or associates; comes as judge; firm but not authoritarian; Hughes: rebelling against appointed minister is rebelling against him who appointed; rivals wanted power (11:5; 12:11), rebuke will show God's power; Christ crucified in weakness, lives by power; Harris: immature admired power over meekness.
A2 (5-10): examine yourselves, quick to judge others; "disqualified" negative of "test" word; Redpath: submit to Christ's scrutiny; Spurgeon: religion that passes private look but fails in daily life; Spurgeon: Christ in you = trusting, loving, communing; Paul not disqualified though worldly standard might judge so, like Job's friends; Hodge: authority limited to truth, applies to church decisions; made complete, half-built wall (1 Cor 1:4-7; 12:19); authority for building up.
B1 (11): "farewell" better "rejoice"; become complete; responsibility for peace; God of love and peace with those who pursue them.
B2 (12-14): holy kiss, today handshake, "holy" excludes romance; Morgan: sign of peace and agape; Hodge: not permanent command; Good Friday custom; 1250 Archbishop of York peace-board; only NT blessing naming Father, Son, Spirit together; Hodge: a Christian seeks and enjoys this grace, love, fellowship.
