# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 8
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-8/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No readable Arabic page.)
A1 (1): no condemnation, standing changed; "therefore" head/body; walk-clause likely copyist addition (Spurgeon notes not in earliest MSS); Poole: in Christ = union by faith.
A2 (2-4): law of Spirit frees from law of sin and death (guilt v1, power v2); Morris quoting Manson "right but not might"; Poole: sun on blind eye; likeness of sinful flesh, true man not sinful, substitute; fulfilled "in us"; Hooke: vine and grapes; Spurgeon: flesh present not ruling.
A3 (5-8): mind the battleground; Matt 16:23; Gal 5:24; Spurgeon: enmity itself.
A4 (9-11): every believer has Spirit; error of dividing Spirit-filled and others; Eph 5:18; John 7:37-39; tests of having the Spirit; Newell on sincere people without full assurance.
B1 (12-13): no debt to flesh; mortify by the Spirit; Gal 3:3.
B2 (14-15): led = result of sonship not condition; Abba; Bruce Roman adoption equal status, past canceled; Spurgeon Spirit leads, devil drives.
B3 (16): Spirit witnesses; two witnesses Deut 17:6.
B4 (17): heirs, joint heirs; family not doing; Luke 18:18; suffering condition for glory.
C1 (18): Paul not blind to suffering; 1 Cor 15:19; Morris "revealed, not created".
C2 (19-22): creation subjected through human sin; Isa 11:6-9; God subjected in hope; rejects "super-Christian" reading; Morris birth pangs.
C3 (23-25): firstfruits; adoption now and completed; 1 Cor 15:53; Morris soldier not dismayed.
C4 (26-27): Spirit intercedes; may include tongues (1 Cor 14:2, 14-15) not limited; tongues no proof of spirituality.
C5 (28-30): all things, together; chain foreknew..glorified (no definition given); Morris: God author of salvation beginning to end; Chuck Smith on predestination "plainly taught"; good work begun will be continued; conformity to Son, firstborn.
D1 (31): Newell weak hearts; the "if"; one with God a majority. D2 (32): greater to lesser.
D3 (33-39): God justifies; Christ died, rose, right hand, intercedes; list; Earle on nakedness; sword only item Paul not experienced (1 Cor 4:11; 15:30); four reasons more than conquerors; nothing separates.
