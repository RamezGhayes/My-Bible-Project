# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 26
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-26/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): Agrippa's family killed John the Baptist and James, no sympathy expected; Paul counts it privilege; partial fulfillment of 9:15 (before kings).
A2 (4-5): upbringing known in Jerusalem; strictest sect, Pharisee.
A3 (6-8): still faithful Jew, faith in Jesus strengthens hope of promise; Agrippa should accept God raises dead; Matt 19:26; Job 19:25-27.
A4 (9-11): persecuted, imprisoned, voted, forced blasphemy; remorse (1 Cor 15:9; 1 Tim 1:15); vote = Sanhedrin member (speculates marriage, not used); anger shows zeal not rightly related to God.
A5 (12-15): light brighter than noon sun, Hebrew voice; conversion mainly repentance of misguided religious zeal and wrong ideas about God, not moral failure.
A6 (16-18): servant and witness; witness to truth, not invent experiences or profit; open eyes, darkness to light, Satan to God, forgiveness, place among sanctified; sanctified = set apart by faith not works; Agrippa invited to the same.
A7 (19-20): not disobedient; Damascus, Jerusalem, Judea, Gentiles; repent, turn, deeds fitting; repentance and turning inseparable.
A8 (21-23): seized for Gentile gospel; help from God; witness to small and great; Moses and prophets: Messiah suffers, first to rise, light.
B1 (24-26): Festus: mad; Paul: true and reasonable, historical facts; not in a corner; 1 Cor 1:18.
B2 (27-29): do you believe prophets? forces decision; "almost" not enough; perhaps Bernice, fear of Festus, human approval; Spurgeon quotes (not used); Paul freer than nobles.
B3 (30-32): innocent; appeal cannot be withdrawn; appeal fit God's plan to bring Paul to Rome (23:11; 19:21; Rom 1:9-13).
