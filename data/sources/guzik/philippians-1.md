# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Philippians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/philippians-1/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): Roman house arrest c. AD 61 (Acts 28:30-31); church founded on 2nd journey, first in Europe (Acts 16); saints = all believers; overseers = leaders before fixed office; grace and peace from Father and Son.
A2 (3-6): thanks with joy; partnership from first day, material support (Acts 16:15, 32-34; 2 Cor 8-9); v6 confidence God completes; Spurgeon.
A3 (7-8): in my heart, partakers of grace in chains/defense; Clarke: loves as Christ loved world.
A4 (9-11): love abound with knowledge; Clarke river image; Corinthians lacked discernment (1 Cor 1:5-7); sincerity not enough, Pharisees; fruit of righteousness through Christ (John 15:4-6); Clarke on God's glory.
B1 (12-14): imprisonment advanced gospel; prison epistles; praetorium; brethren emboldened by Paul's joy.
B2 (15-18): envy/rivalry vs goodwill; not assuming worst; Tozer at length on rivalry among ministers; content matters, distorted gospel wrong regardless of motive; rejoices Christ preached.
B3 (19-20): deliverance through prayers and Spirit; Christ magnified by life or death; Paul did not dictate to God; Spurgeon.
B4 (21-26): to live Christ; contradicts soul sleep and purgatory; Spurgeon ship nearing harbor; Clarke sailor homeward; remaining more needful; later released and revisited Philippi.
C1 (27): live as citizens worthy of gospel (theme recurs); one spirit, striving together; unity.
C2 (28): not terrified; Martin: word for panicked horses; sign of destruction/salvation from God (2 Thess 2:3; 2 Pet 2:1).
C3 (29-30): believing and suffering both gifts; suffering not abandonment; word mainly persecution also hardship; Meyer: trust God in suffering; agon -> "agony".
