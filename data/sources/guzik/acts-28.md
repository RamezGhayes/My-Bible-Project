# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 28
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-28/ (Arabic page exists). Our own outline, not his text. The page labels two sections of part I with "ب"; registered as A2 (3-6) and A3 (7-10) with the page's labels kept.
A1 (1-2): unusual kindness; sailors didn't recognize island from this side; Melita = refuge.
A2 (3-6): Paul gathers sticks, servant heart; viper; faithful service no exemption from trials; murderer then god, don't trust extreme opinions; protected to reach Rome (23:11).
A3 (7-10): Publius; Boice on title (not used); maybe Malta fever; "healed" word = medical care, Luke may have treated.
B1 (11-15): three months; Syracuse (Archimedes aside), Rhegium, Puteoli; Bruce: gospel in Rome before Paul; Horton on escort custom (not used); thanked God, courage; contrast later abandonment (2 Tim 4:9-16).
B2 (16): promise fulfilled (19:21; Rom 1:15; 23:11; 27:23-25); Boice on ends of earth; Rome background; chained to soldier; Phil 1:13 guards hear gospel.
B3 (17-20): Jews first; not against people or customs; no capital charge; no accusation against nation; hope of Israel = Jesus; AD 70 approaching (not used).
B4 (21-22): no letters, Judean leaders had no case; Roman leaders commended for wanting to hear directly.
B5 (23-24): morning to evening, kingdom, Moses and prophets; some persuaded, some not; many expected political kingdom, Paul spoke of spiritual.
B6 (25-27): Isaiah 6:9-10 (heading misprints 9:6-10); glad for some, grieved for others; today people refuse because they don't want to turn and be healed.
B7 (28-29): salvation to Gentiles; gospel smell of life or death (2 Cor 2:16); Jerusalem destruction as judgment (left out of entries).
B8 (30-31): two years rented house; Onesimus; Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians written; Bruce on court delays; tradition release, rearrest, death under Nero ~66-67; Poole doxology (not used); "without hindrance" = nothing stops God's word, Acts unfinished, continues in church.
