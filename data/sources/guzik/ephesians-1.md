# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Ephesians 1
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ephesians-1/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page arabic.enduringword.com/ephesians-01-ar/ redirects to the Arabic index; English used.)
A2 (1-2): Moule on Ephesus (three years, school of Tyrannus, laboured, watched, wept); missing "in Ephesus" in some MSS, circular use; grace before peace.
B1 (3-6): Moule "praised with worshipping love"; Spurgeon little of gifts, new heart better than new coat; us = Jews and Gentiles; chosen before merit, Calvin name of Christ excludes merit; Clarke sacrifice language; holiness in love; Barclay Roman adoption; Gaebelein "Sonplace"; Stott higher dignity; charito Luke 1:28; Bruce be-graced; Chrysostom leper to youth; Clarke Law and Gospel.
B2 (7-8): Gaebelein ransom; blood = whole sacrificial payment; Spurgeon not power but blood; riches; forgiveness height of wisdom.
B3 (9-12): Barclay mystery; Clarke dispensation; summing up column; Savior or Judge; Rom 8:18-22; Foulkes sacred/secular; Bruce fullness ripe; Morgan wills, plans, works; Trapp counsel; we = Jewish believers, you = Gentiles.
B4 (13-14): sovereignty and response; sealing after belief; seal = Spirit (Gaebelein); Meyer wax, face, pressure; guarantee only of Spirit.
C1 (15-16): faith and love evidence; 1 John 4:20; John 13:34-35; Trapp; Rom 1:9; 1 Thess 1:2; Philem 4.
C2 (17): know God; Pope vs Spurgeon; Alford know thy God.
C3 (18-19a): eyes of heart; Alford heart; hope of calling; Deut 32:8-9; same power that raised Christ; pray 1:3-14 into reality.
C4 (19b-21): no power shortage; Bruce resurrection/cross; Clarke right hand; angels (Eph 3:10; 6:12); Alford king above court; Moule paradox.
C5 (22-23): Wood exaltation gift to church; 1 Cor 12:12; fullness = Christ fills church; Moule one Christ.
