# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 1 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-1/ (Arabic page, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch outline). No Greek words given on this page.
A1 (1): first account = Luke's Gospel, Luke-Acts two volumes; Acts bridges Gospels and Paul's letters (Jerusalem to Rome); Barclay retitle idea; Boice: no money, technology, faced hostility; Marshall: Septuagint style, historian of sacred history; Luke physician, Gentile, companion of Paul (Col 4:14; Phlm 24; 2 Tim 4:11); Ramsay and Sherwin-White on reliability; Calvin "treasure"; Lloyd-Jones via Stott; Theophilus options (believer, Roman official, "lover of God"), Luke 1:3 title; apologetic aim toward Rome; papyrus length (Boice); about 30 years, ends c. AD 60-61, Nero persecution 64; Pierson: study God's ongoing work.
A2 (2-3): commands through the Holy Spirit; Pierson: no disciple greater than teacher, ministry needs Spirit; many proofs, 40 days, 1 Cor 15:6; rejects Gnostic/New Age secret teachings.
B1 (4-5): wait in Jerusalem for promise of the Father: worth waiting, certain, beyond human ability, a test; Trinity together; baptize = immerse, ongoing: "are you being baptized in the Spirit?"; no exact date.
B2 (6): last gathering; question natural given Luke 22:20, Jer 23:1-8, Ezek 36:16-30, 37:21-28; Jesus did not rebuke; Stott: verb, noun, time phrase show expectation of immediate political kingdom.
B3 (7-8): times in Father's authority; wise not to reveal (zeal); did not say kingdom never restored to Israel; Son's submission to Father; maybe wanted Caesar's power; witness = result (indicative), filling not just training; Isa 43:10 and Jehovah's Witnesses; outline 1-7 / 8-12 / 13-28; emotional weight of each place.
B4 (9-11): cloud = possibly Shekinah; Luke 24:50 blessing; John 16:7; two men = angels (Morgan's Moses/Elijah view rejected); "this same Jesus"; return: bodily, visible, Mount of Olives, witnesses, blessing.
C1 (12-14): obedience without his presence; Sabbath journey ~2000 cubits; 120 incl. Mary, brothers (John 7:5; Mark 3:21) converted after resurrection; Calvin: women maybe apostles' wives; unity of former rivals (Peter, Matthew, Simon Zealot); prayer and supplication urgent; three steps: obedience, fellowship, prayer.
C2 (15-20): Peter natural spokesman, not office passed on; Scripture had to be fulfilled, mature view of evil; Matt 27:5 harmonised (rope broke, fell); Field of Blood named for betrayal money; Ps 69:25, 109:8; Doeg (1 Sam 21-22); Peter relying on Scripture.
C3 (21-23): qualifications (from John's baptism, witness of resurrection); "sanctified reasoning" not special revelation; did not wait for feeling; Spirit never contradicts the Word; apostles' role = witness; Joseph Barsabbas Justus and Matthias.
C4 (24-26): prayer to Jesus who knows hearts (Luke 6:12-13); lots maybe dice/stones, debatable; Prov 16:33; better than feelings/circumstances; rejects view that Paul should have been the twelfth; 1 Cor 15:8; Rev 21:14; lessons: obedience, unity, prayer, Word, desire for God's will, sanctified reasoning, imitating Jesus, dependence.
