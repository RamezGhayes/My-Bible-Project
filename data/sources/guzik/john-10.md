# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 10
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-10/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): context after John 9; thief climbs in; Tenney on "truly truly"; Isa 56:11; Jer 5:31; shepherd = leaders; Clarke on wrong ways of entering (ambition, flattery, self-interest).
A2 (3-6): doorkeeper; calls by name, shared village folds (Dods, Bruce, Tasker; Philip, Mary Magdalene, Thomas, Peter); leads from the front (Clarke); sheep flee strangers (Dods; anecdotes of shepherds' voices); not understood; Clarke's marks of a true servant.
A3 (7-10): door = shepherd lying across the entrance (Dods); thieves = religious leaders incl. Pharisees (Alford, Tasker); Morris present tense; Trapp: Manes and Augustine on Moses and prophets; Clarke kleptes/lestes; in and out = freedom and security (Dods; Jer 37:4; Ps 121:8; Deut 28:6); abundant life (Boice on perissos; Spurgeon).
A4 (11-15): good shepherd lays down life (Alford: model shepherd; Spurgeon); hireling flees, wolf and thief enemies (Alford); mutual knowledge (Dods); Spurgeon on hirelings tolerating error for peace.
A5 (16): other sheep = Gentile believers; Clarke (Eph 2:13-17); Dods: one flock, not one fold; Bruce: unity by nearness to Christ; Barclay: loyalty to one shepherd, not organisational uniformity; Spurgeon; Boice on Jerome's "one fold" and its use for exclusive church claims.
A6 (17-18): voluntary death and authority to take life again; Spurgeon (ordinary death pays own debt); critiques of denials of bodily resurrection and of teaching that Jesus was helpless in hell.
A7 (19-21): division; demon or not; miracles support claims; Barclay (delusion or Son of God); Trapp.
B1 (22-23): Dedication/Hanukkah, Antiochus Epiphanes, rededication; Barclay; Clarke, Trench on "winter"; Solomon's porch (Bruce: Acts 3:11; 5:12; Morris).
B2 (24-25): hostile question (Dods); Clarke: trap to charge treason before Rome; Morris present tense of unbelief; list of earlier claims; works testify (Dods: what kind of Messiah).
B3 (26-29): not his sheep; Spurgeon (unbelief shows not chosen/called); Clarke: they did not hear, not that they were unable; Tasker; v28 Morris (eternal life never ends), Bruce (united by faith cannot lose life, he keeps it), Spurgeon (salvation tied to abiding in Christ, branch image of John 15); page's own note: sheep safe in the shepherd's hand; v29 Clarke: Father greater than all.
B4 (30-33): oneness of essence not only purpose (Alford, Trench neuter "one"); rules out Sabellian and Arian readings; stones (Lev 24:16, Morris); Spurgeon; Bruce, Dods: they understood claim to deity.
B5 (34-39): Ps 82:6 judges called gods (Exod 21:6; 22:8-9); Alford "your law"; Tasker; lesser to greater; Scripture cannot be broken (Morris, Bruce); works; escape (Trench).
B6 (40-42): Perea outside Jerusalem's reach (Tenney); John did no sign but his word true (Trench, Morrison, Barclay); Morris, Spurgeon on fruit in unexpected places.
Theology note: on v28 the page cites Spurgeon tying salvation to abiding in Christ alongside Morris/Bruce on security; reported with a neutral note.
