# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 3 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-3/ (Arabic, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch outline).
A1 (1-3): example of 2:43, setup for second sermon, explains persecution; ninth hour of prayer; Morgan: prayer not sacrifice; Calvin: aim to spread gospel; Hughes: ninth hour of "It is finished" (John 19:30); Beautiful Gate: Josephus via Stott, Corinthian bronze ~30 m; lame from birth carried daily; almsgiving custom.
A2 (4-6): eye contact raised hope; expectation, low expectations cost blessings; no silver and gold, but authority (Luke 9:1-6); worse failure = no power to say rise and walk; monk and cardinal anecdote (uncertain); LaSor: not making conditions bearable but proclaiming gospel; Hughes: "Nazarene" insult used boldly.
A3 (7-10): taking hand = gift of faith (1 Cor 12:9); Morgan: Luke's medical vocabulary (feet, ankles, leaped); man stayed with apostles, used gift, praised; over 40 (4:22); Jesus likely passed him; God's timing.
B1 (11-12): clinging; Solomon's porch; wonder is not faith (Rom 10:17); man not ready to testify; not by our power or godliness; warns preachers implying special holiness; Jesus heals from heaven.
B2 (13-15): God of fathers; Servant (Isa 42; 52:13-53:12), Boice; delivered and denied, Pilate (John 18:29-19:16); Guzik: Jews not solely responsible, Romans too; our sins, look in mirror; Morgan: sermon greater miracle; Holy One (40+ OT times for Yahweh); Barabbas irony; "you" repeated; Prince of life.
B3 (16): faith in His name; Longenecker: name expresses nature; trust in name not intentions, gifts, resources, reputation, effort, intelligence.
B4 (17-18): brethren, without hatred; Peter had denied too; ignorance defines guilt, still sin; God fulfilled; Gen 50:20; Rom 8:28.
B5 (19-21): repent = change of direction, hopeful word; return: new creation (2 Cor 5:17); Boice: "flee to God", cities of refuge; blotted out = ancient ink wiped with damp sponge; times of refreshing = when Jesus returns to rule; Peter said Father would send Jesus if Israel repented as nation; Christ stays in heaven till restoration, which includes Israel's repentance (Matt 23:37-39; Rom 11:25-27); hypothetical not needed; pray for seasons of revival.
B6 (22-26): Deut 18:15-19 prophet = Jesus; destruction for refusing, third blessing escape judgment; prophets from Samuel; Abrahamic covenant blesses all families incl. Gentiles, "to you first"; fourth blessing turning from wickedness; man wanted small, God gave more; Israel expected political deliverer.
