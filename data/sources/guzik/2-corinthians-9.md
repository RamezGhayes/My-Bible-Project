# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 9
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-9/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page not found by search; language switcher links are "#".)
A1 (1-2): "ministering to the saints" = Jerusalem collection (ch. 8); diakonia same word as "relief" Acts 11:29; playful irony, letter unnecessary; boasted to Macedonians; geography Macedonia north (Philippi, Berea, Thessalonica), Achaia south, Corinth chief city; refs 8:1-8; 1 Cor 16:1-4; 3:8-9.
A2 (3-5): brothers sent so boast not empty, no embarrassment before Macedonians; prepared beforehand so given freely not under pressure; generosity is attitude more than amount; Redpath: God's open hands never withhold.
B1 (6): farmer sowing; rewards material and spiritual; Phil 4:19, 15-18; Matt 19:29 cannot out-give God; Calvin: harvest includes eternal life and earthly blessing.
B2 (7): purposes in heart; not grudging or of necessity like tax; Matt 6:21 spending shows heart; hilaros only here; Clarke: temple had compulsory and voluntary chests; Carlyle boyhood anecdote of joy in giving to a beggar; Hodge: giving under public opinion or conscience lacks cheer; Morgan: "hilarious" giving from love; Kruse: God a cheerful giver restoring his image; Acts 5:1-11.
B3 (8-9): all grace abound, material and spiritual reward; channels not reservoirs; autarkeia sufficiency/contentment 1 Tim 6:6; Barclay: inner self-sufficiency; Trapp: repeated "all" confronts covetousness; Matt 10:42; Ps 112:9; righteousness evidence not cause of standing.
B4 (10-11): God supplies seed and bread, multiplies; harvest of righteousness; wealth's purpose is generosity, result thanksgiving; Poole: prayer carries assurance; Poole: Christian life aims at God's glory and others' good; J. B. Phillips paraphrase.
B5 (12-14): four benefits: needs met, thanksgivings, proof of obedience to gospel glorifying God, recipients pray for givers; koinonia fellowship.
B6 (15): indescribable gift = Jesus and salvation (John 3:16); anekdiegetos unknown in earlier writing; gratitude best motive; Clarke: greatest gift; Spurgeon: preaching Christ never runs dry; Clarke: we remember hardships more than mercies; Poole: without grace none can give.
