# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 9
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-9/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): calm after temple conflict (Boice, Spurgeon); man treated as puzzle; Spurgeon: practical mercy; Bruce: like Job's friends; Morris on rabbinic saying; Dods: rabbinic options (pre-existence, sin in womb etc.).
A2 (3-5): not a specific sin but fallen nature (Gen 3); works of God displayed; Alford, Dods; Bruce caution; Trench; urgency of "I must work" (Spurgeon, Maclaren); light of the world.
A3 (6-7): spittle and clay, no fixed method (Tenney, Morris); Gen 2:7; saliva medicinal (Barclay, Alford); Mark 7:33; 8:23; obey and go; Siloam channel (Barclay, Trench); "Sent" = Jesus (Morris); obedience before sight (Morris, Tasker); first such healing; Ps 146:8; Isa 35:5; Trench: picture of Gentiles.
B1 (8-12): neighbours; "a man called Jesus"; Alford: Jesus absent at the healing.
B2 (13-16): Sabbath, kneading clay forbidden (Bruce); Clarke on mercy; division; Morris: minority fades.
B3 (17-18): Morris: unusual to ask commoner; prophet; Clarke: prophet may set aside Sabbath; Tasker: easier to deny.
B4 (19-23): parents; fear of expulsion (Ezra 10:8; John 12:42); Dods three-stage discipline; Morris, Alford: deflect to son.
C1 (24-25): oath formula (Josh 7:19, Alford); Morris; simple testimony; Bruce.
C2 (26-27): sarcasm; Bruce, Morris; Clarke: wisdom given.
C3 (28-34): disciples of Moses; irony; God does not hear sinners (Isa 1:15; Ps 66:18) with nuance (Tasker, Spurgeon); expelled; Trapp, Morgan, Clarke.
C4 (35-38): Jesus finds him; Tenney; worship accepted = deity (Tasker, Spurgeon); growth of understanding: man, prophet, teacher, from God, Son of God.
C5 (39-41): judgment; Tenney: dividing line; Dods, Tasker, Bruce on spiritual blindness.
