# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Romans 3
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/romans-3/ . Our own outline, not his text. Several page references have reversed digits; only unambiguous ones used.
A1 (1-2): advantage of the Jew; no partiality (2:11); oracles = written revelation; Rom 9:4 privileges; Trapp heavenly treasure.
A2 (3-4): unbelief does not void God's faithfulness; Spurgeon gospel no failure, God's word outweighs world; Clarke fault in man.
A3 (5): objection; "as a man"; Judas illustration.
A4 (6-8): God judges the world; Jews expected judgment for Gentiles; slander "do evil that good may come"; grace into license worst corruption.
B1 (9): all under sin as master; Poole power and guilt; Morris sin a tyrant.
B2 (10-18): Wiersbe X-ray head to foot; Ps 14:1-3; 5:9; 140:3; 10:7; Isa 59:7-8; Ps 36:1; Newell Adam innocent not righteous; Spurgeon people seek a god they make; spoiled fruit; Calvin fear of God a bridle.
B3 (19-20): every mouth stopped; Calvin vices of human nature; Poole use of law; fig leaves; Job 9:2; J.B. Phillips straightness shows crookedness; law condemns not saves.
C1 (21): "but now"; choris nomou placed first (Newell); gift not earned.
C2 (22): Morris faith receives gift; Newell faith vs trust; Spurgeon one remedy, blood and righteousness of Christ.
C3 (23-24): justification, redemption, propitiation images; four ways of falling short; Morris quoting Moule mine and Alps; dorean (Matt 10:8; Rev 22:17; John 15:25); Calvin repeats freely/grace; 1 Cor 6:20.
C4 (25-26): hilasterion mercy seat in LXX, Lev 16; Wuest pagan use; OT sins covered, paid at cross; Clarke justice required, mercy provided; just and justifier.
C5 (27): boasting excluded; law of faith.
C6 (28-30): Lenski on Luther sola fide; James 2:14-26; Calvin reconciles; one God for Jew and Gentile.
C7 (31): faith establishes law; Rom 4 to show it.
