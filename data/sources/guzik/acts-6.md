# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 6
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-6/ (three fetches: outline, vv1-7, vv8-15). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): Satan attacks from within; multiplying = organized church (records, meetings, collections, distribution, discipline); Hebrews (Judea, Jewish culture) vs Hellenists (diaspora, Greek culture), mutual suspicion; both believers; widows without income; temple leaders may have neglected believing widows; Stott: oversight not intent; MacArthur: large church overlooks; Satan uses honest mistakes. (Page cites "1 Tim 3:5-16" for widows, apparently garbled; not used.)
A2 (2-4): not pride but wise delegation; pastor not on trivia but not too proud; tables = practical/financial, Ogilvie: money-changers' tables; Barnhouse anecdote (it will cost you that); Calvin: pastors must do more than teach; dialogue, complainers may have suggested names; what apostles avoided (ignore, split, dismiss, committee, long debate); meeting needs invites service; reputation, Spirit and wisdom, inner character; seven = one per weekday; v4 priorities; Boice: apostles protected neither interests nor viewpoint.
A3 (5-7): pleased all = unity, not proof by popularity; Greek names = Hellenists, sensitivity; Boice: Aramaic speakers agreed Greek speakers lead; congregation nominated, apostles confirmed with prayer, final say apostles; not titled deacons here, probably first to fill 1 Tim 3:8-13 role (page prints "1 Tim 8:3-13"); deacon = servant; 1 Tim 3:13; same Greek word for distribution (6:1) and ministry of word (6:4); John 13:1-5; v7: priests believed; table servers became martyr and evangelist; Satan's two plans failed.
B1 (8-10): God worked through others besides apostles; full of faith and power; variant grace/faith, same meaning; synagogues named; Lovett: Cilician synagogue possibly Paul's; nothing says Stephen cleverer; credit to Spirit.
B2 (11-14): lies when debate lost; Luke may have learned from Saul/Cilicians; public opinion fickle (2:47; 5:26; Luke 19:35-40; 23:18-23); stand on Word; charges like Jesus' (Matt 26:59-61); what Stephen taught (5 points); distortion; Bruce: Stephen grasped Jesus' temple teaching; rejects idea apostles avoided this doctrine.
B3 (15): before highest court; face as angel: not soft image nor anger, peace and confidence, reflected glory like Moses (Ex 34:29), Marshall; life in God's hands.
