# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 4
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-4/ . Our own outline, not his text. Headings registered as on the page (note: the page itself gives overlapping ranges ٥-٩ and ٧-٩).
A1 (1-4): disciples baptized, not Jesus (Morris citing Godet: shows superiority to John); Jews avoided Samaria; origin of Samaritans, Gerizim temple destroyed c. 128 BC; John 3:22; Acts 2:41; Morris, Bruce on route.
A2 (5-9): Sychar/Shechem history: Gen 12:6-8; 33:18-20; 34; Josh 24:32; Josh 24; Alford: Sychar perhaps a derogatory name; real weariness, full humanity (Maclaren, Bruce); sixth hour = noon.
A3 (7-9): woman alone at odd hour, perhaps marginalized; Jesus broke convention (Barclay on rabbis avoiding women); paradox of Jesus asking water; Spurgeon, Morris/Godet: request for help opens conversation.
A4 (10-12): gift of God, living water, Jer 2:13; 17:13; Bruce: Samaritan expectation of a prophet bringing water (Num 24:7); her question sincere or sarcastic, Guzik leans sincere.
A5 (13-15): spring welling to eternal life; Spurgeon: drinking simple, only receiving; return to Jesus repeatedly; she wants to avoid daily trip.
A6 (16-19): husband; Spurgeon: reached through conscience; Alford: conviction of sin first work of the Spirit; she sees a prophet.
A7 (20-26): Gerizim vs Jerusalem; Jesus not drawn into debate, aims at her soul; spirit and truth not place; Morris on "you/we"; Barclay: Samaritans only Pentateuch; Dods, Alford; Alford links Acts 8; v26 self-revelation.
A8 (27-30): disciples silent (Dods, Morris); water jar left; Bruce: she sought out the neighbours she had avoided; Jesus exposed sin yet made her feel safe.
A9 (31-34): food is the Father's will; Spurgeon, Dods, Morgan; Morris: same verb as John 19:30; Matt 4:4.
A10 (35-38): fields white; Tenney: Samaritans approaching are the harvest; Spurgeon: expect blessing now; 1 Cor 3:6-8; disciples reap what John and Jesus sowed.
A11 (39-42): many believed through her word, more through his; two days in Samaria (Bruce, Trench); Clarke, Dods: Savior of the world beyond Jews.
B1 (43-46a): prophet without honour (Matt 13:57; Mark 6:4); Morris: welcome based on signs; return to Cana.
B2 (46b-48): Alford: official of Herod Antipas; c. 32 km; Morrison on the court's emptiness; signs can produce but not guarantee faith (2 Thess 2:9; Exod 19-20; 32).
B3 (49-50): Spurgeon: pleaded the son's need not his rank; man believed the word; Clarke: healing at a distance to draw fuller faith.
B4 (51-54): fever left at seventh hour; Spurgeon: walked home calmly; Alford, Tasker: household believed; Barclay: cost in Herod's court; John 20:30-31.
