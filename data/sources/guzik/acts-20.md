# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Acts 20
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-20/. Our outline, not his text. Ranges normalized.
A1 (1): leaves Ephesus with affection after ~2 years. A2 (2-5): Greece/Macedonia; plot; companions = church representatives with collection, witnesses to integrity; Illyricum (Rom 15:19), Acts not exhaustive.
B1 (6): Unleavened Bread; Troas 5 days, 7 days stay; "we" = Luke back.
B2 (7-12): first day, breaking bread, early Lord's Day worship; until midnight; Eutychus asleep (hour, heat, lamp smoke), fell dead, restored; talk till dawn.
C1 (13-17): Assos on foot; Miletus; bypass Ephesus for Pentecost; elders summoned.
C2 (18-21): humble service, tears, trials; public and house to house; repentance and faith to Jews and Greeks; model for leaders, even new believers.
C3 (22-24): bound in spirit; chains; life not precious; race with joy; Spurgeon: is the gospel worth dying for.
C4 (25): not see face again; central ministry kingdom preaching despite miracles; parallels Jesus' path.
C5 (26-27): innocent of blood, whole counsel; clear conscience; don't tailor message.
C6 (28): leadership from character; feed and lead; church God's, bought with his own blood, not leaders' property.
C7 (29): wolves from outside; Paul simply knows. C8 (30): from within, harder, self-promotion. C9 (31): watch; 3 years care: long-term, continuous, watchful, comprehensive, heartfelt.
C10 (32-35): commend to God and word; no coveting; worked; help weak; giving over receiving; saying not in Gospels.
C11 (36-38): kneel, weep, embrace; Rev 2 first love.
