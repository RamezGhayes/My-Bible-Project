# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 13
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-13/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Intro: Maclaren on John 13-17 as a window into God's heart.
A1 (1): last meeting before the cross; timing of the meal vs Passover (Bruce, Trench); "his hour" (John 2:4; 7:30; 8:20; 12:23); love "to the end" = to the utmost (Tenney).
A2 (2-3): the devil moved Judas' heart (Bruce); Jesus knew all was in his hands (John 3:35), came from and going to God; Morris, Tenney on "during supper".
A3 (4-5): rising, laying aside garments, towel; eyewitness detail (Trench, Dods); dirty feet; act pictures incarnation and cross; Morris: acted parable.
A4 (6-8): Peter's objection; "no part with me" (Trapp, Alford); Judas also washed, so the point is receiving Christ's cleansing, not the outward act (Morris).
A5 (9-11): Peter asks for full washing; humility = willingness to be served (Dods, Temple); once-for-all bath vs ongoing cleansing; Spurgeon: priests' laver; Jesus knew the betrayer.
A6 (12-14): "do you understand"; ought to wash one another's feet; Calvin via Morris: no annual ceremony commanded, but constant readiness to serve; Spurgeon critical of ceremonial foot-washing as show; Bruce on Maundy Thursday rite; concedes some practise it and may be blessed.
A7 (15-17): example; servant not greater than master; "apostle" = sent one (Bruce, Dods); Spurgeon on accepting lowly service; Eph 5:26.
B1 (18-20): Jesus knew whom he chose; Ps 41:9 fulfilled; foretold so they would believe.
B2 (21-26): troubled in spirit; the morsel as last mark of love to Judas; seating and reclining (Morris, Spurgeon, Tasker, Bruce, Maclaren); "disciple whom Jesus loved" four times in John.
B3 (27-30): Satan entered Judas after the morsel; "do it quickly"; Matt 26:25; Tasker, Bruce, Maclaren, Tenney; went out, it was night.
C1 (31-32): cross as highest glory not greatest shame; "glory" five times; Origen via Morris "humble glory"; Alford, Maclaren, Trapp.
C2 (33): "little children", tender address aware of their weakness; departure (cf. John 7:34).
C3 (34-35): "new" = fresh rather than novel; new standard "as I loved you" because of the cross; love as identifying mark; Tenney, Bruce (Tertullian), Spurgeon.
C4 (36-37, heading; v38 quoted and discussed under it): Peter's vow; loyalty resting on emotion collapses; Peter's denial impulsive vs Judas' deliberate betrayal; Clarke, Morgan, Bruce; Acts 3:14; 2 Pet 2:1. Our entry uses r 36-38 because the section treats v38.
Theology note: Guzik does not treat foot-washing as an ordinance (cites Calvin against a fixed ceremony), so no note needed beyond reporting this.
