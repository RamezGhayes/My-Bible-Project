# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 5
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-5/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-4): feast unidentified (Passover, Pentecost, Purim); pool remains north of temple; Alford present tense, not pressed; Dods: Crusader church mural; v3b-4 missing from many early manuscripts, Trench: early addition known to Tertullian; Clarke, Trapp: feast times; unusual healings (2 Kgs 4; 5; 13:20-21; Acts 5:14-16; 19:11-12); unusual not automatically divine.
A2 (5-6): 38 years; Spurgeon: crowd looked to water not Jesus; things people wait for; Alford: question prompted by listlessness; Barclay: healed beggar loses livelihood.
A3 (7-9): Alford: first-in belief; Calvin: man limited God's help; Morris on the mat; Barclay; Dods: carrying shows lasting cure; Trench: healed without the water; Sabbath.
B1 (10-13): Bruce: "the Jews" = Jerusalem leaders; Barclay on rabbinic rules; Morris; 1992 Israeli fire example; Alford: Jesus avoided attention.
B2 (14-15): warning about worse; Morris: perfect tense, complete cure; Maclaren: illness tied to sin; Morris: ingratitude; Dods/Lightfoot.
B3 (16-18): Bruce: inciting others worse; Deut 23:12-14; Philo (via Dods), Morrison, Clarke: God always working; Gen 2:2; Ps 121:3-4; Augustine vs Arians; Jehovah's Witnesses.
C1 (19-20): Morris: Son not independent; Dods; Bruce citing C.H. Dodd: apprenticed son image; Trench; Father loves the Son; greater works.
C2 (21-23): Son gives life (Clarke); Morrison; Alford, Trench: honouring Son = honouring Father.
C3 (24-27): passed from death to life (Clarke); life in himself = aseity; Maclaren; guards against modalism and Arianism.
C4 (28-30): resurrection of life/judgment; Morris: works do not save but reflect faith; Tenney; just judgment.
D1 (31-32): Deut 19:15, other witnesses. D2 (33-35): Clarke: lamp title for teachers; Barclay four points; Clarke: wanted political deliverer. D3 (36): works mostly mercy. D4 (37-38): Father's witness, prophecy, baptism (Luke 3:22). D5 (39): Morris perfunctory reading; Barclay arguments not God; Bruce "search". D6 (40-44): glory from men; 2 Thess 2; Bruce: Bar Kokhba AD 132; Spurgeon, Clarke, Dods, Tasker. D7 (45-47): Deut 18:15; Num 21:8-9; Exod 21:5-6; Ps 40:6-8; Luke 24:27; Morgan; Alford on Mosaic authorship.
No theology note needed.
