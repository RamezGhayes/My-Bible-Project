# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Philippians 4
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/philippians-4/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): "therefore" links to resurrection hope 3:21; stephanos athletic wreath; stand only "in the Lord".
A2 (2): Euodia and Syntyche dispute; Paul does not take sides; identity in Christ.
A3 (3): unnamed true companion; Clement and co-workers, book of life (Rev 20:15); possible Clement of Rome, uncertain.
B1 (4): joy throughout letter (list of refs); Spurgeon joy cures quarrels, God "makes delight a duty"; joy on God's control not optimism.
B2 (5): epieikes gentleness/patience; Clarke broad term; Jesus with adulterous woman; Lord fights (Rom 12:19); Lord near -> patience, hope He will right wrongs.
B3 (6): anxiety intrudes on God's domain, acting as head of household; prayer broader, supplication direct; God waits for us to voice; thanksgiving guards against complaining.
B4 (7): peace from / with / of God; Spurgeon calm of self-sufficient God; beyond not against reason; guard military; Clarke castle.
B5 (8): list; fruit of guarded mind; what we dwell on flows out; Rom 12:2; 2 Cor 10:5.
B6 (9): imitate Paul; God of peace with you.
C1 (10-14): renewed care via Epaphroditus; contentment learned; Clarke quick complainers; Spurgeon prosperity dizzying (Joseph); v13 sufficiency in suffering, context warning, John 15:5; did well to share.
C2 (15-18): alone supported in Macedonia (Acts 16); Thessalonica (1 Thess 2:9; 2 Thess 3:7-9); Spurgeon modest value; Clarke Paul worked; fruit to their account; Kennedy giving is sign of success; sacrificial aroma (Gen 8:21; Ex 29:18, 25, 41; Eph 5:2); 2 Cor 8:1-5.
C3 (19): needs supplied; Spurgeon limited to needs but according to riches; Martin; widow's oil (2 Kings 4:1-7); Luke 6:38.
D1 (20): doxology sincere; Amen.
D2 (21-22): saint = all believers; Caesar's household; Muller servants/staff; Clarke Nero's household.
D3 (23): grace begins and ends; closing Amen.
