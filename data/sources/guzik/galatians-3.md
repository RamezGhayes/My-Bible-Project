# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Galatians 3
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/galatians-3/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No Arabic page found by search; arabic.enduringword.com/galatians-03-ar/ redirects to the Arabic index.)
A1 (1): anoetos vs moros (Matt 7:26; 25:1-13); bewitched = evil eye, cure is not to look; billboard; Phillips; Barclay "put the evil eye on"; Spurgeon: real atonement in your soul, not bewitched; soft heart + soft head.
A2 (2-3): Spirit received by faith so growth by faith; law = earning, grace = believing and receiving; imagined Gentile told circumcision needed; Satan hinders growth.
A3 (4): suffering, Acts 14 stoning; some scholars "experiences to no purpose".
A4 (5): God supplies Spirit and miracles in response to faith; choice repeated.
B1 (6): Gen 15:6; circumcision later (Gen 17; Rom 4:9-10); accounted; rabbis thought Abraham kept law; Mehilta via Clarke; Morris (accounted, trust; James 2:19); Calvin (embraced grace, promised Mediator); Poole; Luther (faith highest worship).
B2 (7): Morris imperative; Cole "real Abrahams"; rabbis: Abraham at gates of hell; Matt 3:9; Guzik: God not finished with Israel (Gen 13:15; 17:7-8; Rom 11:25).
B3 (8-9): Scripture personified (Morris); Trapp heart and soul of God; Gen 12:3; blessing = right standing not wealth; Morris faith constant; Luther OT looked forward.
C1 (10): Deut 27:26; law not evil (Ps 1; 119:1, 97); partial obedience; Clarke on "all" missing in printed Hebrew, Jerome; Morris; Luther builder starting with roof.
C2 (11): Hab 2:4 quoted 3 times (Rom 1:17; Heb 10:38).
C3 (12): Lev 18:5; Neh 9:29; Ezek 20; Rom 10:5; intentions not enough.
C4 (13-14): redeemed = bought; ransom background; Deut 21:23; John 19:31-33; Morris; Luther (our sins became his).
C5 (15-18): brethren; singular Seed (Gen 22:18); 430 years; kecharistai perfect, from charis; Cole: Judaizers quote Moses, Paul Abraham.
D1 (19-21): two functions, restrain and provoke (Rom 7:5-8); until Seed (Matt 5:17); angels; Boice most obscure verse, promise one-sided; Luther money and eyes.
D2 (22): prison image; Morris jailer; warden; Luther despair drives to Jesus.
D3 (23-25): protective custody; paidagogos not teacher, guardian slave; Morris "custodian"; Jukes in Stott; Matt 5:17-48; Luther schoolmasters.
D4 (26-27): Matt 23; sons = closeness; baptism immersion, water does not save, still be baptized (Matt 28:19-20); put on Christ clothing.
D5 (28-29): Barclay morning prayer; Stott barriers not differences; Eph 6:5-8; 1 Tim 2; Eph 5:22-33; 1 Cor 9:19-21; Morris unity; Stott "Who am I?".
