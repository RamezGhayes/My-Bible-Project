# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 2
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-2/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): no second sorrowful visit; spare them (1:23); room to repent; Clarke (avoid visit while scandals unaddressed).
A2 (3-4): letter instead of visit; lost letter not 1 Cor; not every letter meant as Scripture; tears; Trapp (written with tears more than ink); Spurgeon (courtesy to opponents).
B1 (5-7): grieved church more than Paul; probably the 1 Cor 5 man (verbatim check: "such a man" in both; rival view of insulter rejected because "he has not grieved me"); 1 Cor 5:4-5; Morgan (comfort restores beyond judicial forgiveness); Trapp (excessive sorrow disables).
B2 (8-11): reaffirm love; test of obedience; pleonekteo (2 Cor 7:2; 12:17-18; 1 Thess 4:6); Luke 17:3; Satan's devices on sinner, church, Paul; Hodge (offenders repent when loved); Calvin (withholding comfort plays into Satan's hands); Trapp (Satan greedy merchant).
C1 (12-13): door at Troas, no rest without Titus; 7:5; 2:14-7:4 "great digression".
C2 (14): Roman triumph; incense = knowledge of God; F.B. Meyer; Barclay on procession.
C3 (15-16a): incense victory to Romans, death to captives; John 3:17-21; Clarke.
C4 (16b-17): who is sufficient; kapeleuo peddling; eilikrineia; Archibald Robertson (sufficiency from God); Poole; Barclay.
