# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 6
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-6/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Outline: A. The seriousness and character of Paul's ministry (1-10); B. Paul speaks to the Corinthians as a father (11-18).
A1 (1-2): co-worker, 5:18, 20; boy helping father mow; worker not "pew potato"; ambassador; pleads; grace in vain, 1 Cor 15:10; grace received actively; Bernard; Redpath yesterday's grace; Isa 49:8 urgency.

B1 (11-13): Eph 4:15; Corinthians as victims, 1 Cor 4:18-21; 2 Cor 1:23; restricted by own affections; loved world and self.
B2 (14-18): Deut 22:9; marriage and wider influences; Clarke wise man on mixed marriage; Rom 12:2; 1 Cor 15:33; not all association, 1 Cor 5:9-13, ship image; Belial only here, Poole; Hodge communion shares; 1 Cor 8-10; temple; 1 Cor 6:19-20; Ezek 37:26-27; Isa 52:11; separation unto God; Hodge pardon and sanctification inseparable; Redpath; Hughes free quotation; Jer 31:9; pantokrator only here and Revelation.
