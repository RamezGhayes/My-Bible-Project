# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 1 Corinthians 7
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/1-corinthians-7/ . Our own outline, not his text. (No Arabic page found.)
A1 (1-2): Paul answers their letter, not a full theology of marriage. "Touch" = sexual relations; "good not to touch" likely a Corinthian slogan Paul accepts only with reserve ("nevertheless"). Some thought abstaining even in marriage more pure. Paul tells married people to live as married; sex not the only or chief purpose of marriage (Eph 5:21-33; Col 3:18-19). Quotes Hodge (celibacy thought holier, separation seen as spirituality) and Clarke (damage to family peace by a spouse thinking himself holier than the apostle).
A2 (3-6): Each owes the other affection, more than the physical; render = give what is owed, not claim a right. Bodies mutually owned, no license for coercion. "Deprive" same word as "defraud" in 6:8. Withholding opens door to Satan; Satan pushes sex outside marriage and discourages it inside. Abstaining a concession, briefly, for fasting and prayer. Corinth had both errors (6:12-20).
A3 (7-9): Each has his own gift (same word as ch. 12), singleness or marriage. Paul probably once married: Jewish expectation, Sanhedrin vote (Acts 26:10), Phil 3:4-6. Quotes Trapp (marriage a binding command among Jews), Clarke (Gemara forbids man without wife), Calvin (false show of purity deceives; burning vs feeling heat). Forbidding marriage a doctrine of demons (1 Tim 4:1-3). No one gifted for immorality. Burning = overwhelming desire; marriage no automatic cure for lust.
B1 (10-11): Both Christians; Paul cites the Lord's teaching. Do not leave/divorce; if separated remain unmarried or be reconciled. God recognizes divorce only for immorality (Matt 19:3-9) and desertion by unbeliever (7:15). Separation may happen for abuse etc., but still married: separate, not single. Jesus permitted, not commanded, divorce for immorality (Matt 19:8). Disciples' reaction (Matt 19:10).
B2 (12-16): "To the rest" = mixed marriages; Jesus did not address this, Paul's apostolic word still authoritative. Don't divorce a willing unbeliever, despite 2 Cor 6:14 warning against marrying one. Sanctified = set apart by the Spirit through proximity, not saved. Children "holy"; quotes Morris (parent's holiness extends to child). Children born guilty in Adam. If unbeliever leaves, not under bondage. "How do you know" gives hope; conduct over words (1 Pet 3:1-6). Barclay: Christians accused of tampering with domestic relationships.
C1 (17): Walk with God where you are now; don't try to undo the past (e.g. leaving a second spouse); don't envy others.
C2 (18-20): Circumcision as example. Trapp (Jews made themselves uncircumcised fearing Antiochus), Clarke (the stretching technique). Keeping God's commandments is what matters.
C3 (21-24): Slave can please God; take freedom if possible. Lord's freedman / Christ's slave. Do not become slaves of men: Spurgeon ("Did Calvin redeem you?" Christ alone master). Morgan (marriage, sorrow, commerce can distract). Trapp: calling must be an honest course; Acts converts burned magic books; not license to stay in sinful occupation.
D1 (25-28): No command of the Lord, but judgment of one trusted. Present distress, crisis; stay in present state. Bound/loosed scribal terms. Marrying no sin but trouble in the flesh. Paul never says singleness more spiritual. Clarke: persecutions at the door, Nero.
D2 (29-31): Time short, not a mistake; readiness (Matt 24:44). Ps 39:5 frailty. sustello = contracted, furled like sails near port (Poole, Trapp). Five contrasts; live with eternity in view, not neglecting family. Morris: nothing solid or lasting in this world system.
D3 (32-35): Unmarried care for the Lord's things, married properly for spouse. Paul's aim undistracted service, not restraint. Singleness opportunity. Warns against forcing celibacy (Roman Catholic clergy) and against reluctance to ordain singles.
D4 (36-38): Father arranging daughter's marriage; "behaving improperly" = denying the right to marry. Better vs best.
D5 (39-40): Widow free to remarry, only in the Lord; happier single in Paul's judgment; not because sex evil.
