# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 8
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-8/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Textual note before section A (7:53-8:11): most early Greek manuscripts omit it; many later ones mark it; placed after Luke 21:38, after John 21:24, or after John 7:36 in some; Tasker: copyists unsure of its place; some fathers (Augustine, Ambrose named) dropped it for doctrinal reasons (fear of seeming to excuse adultery); early Christian writers cited it early in the second century (page prints "١٠٠ ق.م.", apparently an error); Trench speculation on Jerusalem relatives shaping the Gospel (John 21:2, 24); Morris: fits Jesus' character even if not part of John. Guzik treats it as a genuine event and comments on it.
A1 (7:53-8:2): Mount of Olives, temple at dawn (John 7:37).
A2 (3-5): public humiliation; Morris on malice; evidence nearly impossible (Morris, Boice), setup; man absent; Bruce on lapsed enforcement; Tasker on betrothal; trap Moses vs Rome (Matt 22:15-22).
A3 (6): woman used as object (Bruce, Barclay); theories of writing; Barclay: verb = writing a record against; stooping = humility (2 Cor 10:1).
A4 (7-8): law applied fairly; plotters; expose sin with humility (2 Sam 12); stoops again.
A5 (9): conscience; eldest first; speculative idea of writing sins; Morris on procession; Trench on "standing".
A6 (10-11): no condemnation; bore her guilt (Rom 8:1); Barclay; "sin no more" = repentance; Morris.
B1 (12): light ceremonies of Tabernacles, pillar of fire; Barclay, Alford, Morris; Spurgeon; Ps 119:105; 43:3.
B2 (13-16): self-testimony; knows origin/destination; judging by flesh; Morris, Dods.
B3 (17-18): two witnesses (Tasker); Clarke's ambassador.
B4 (19-20): "where is your father" = slur on birth; Tenney; treasury; hour not come (7:30).
B5 (21-22): die in sin; followers on earth follow to heaven; suicide jibe; Barclay.
B6 (23-24): above/below; Bruce singular/plural sin; Morris on "I am" (LXX).
B7 (25-27): hostile question (Tenney); list of hostile questions; speaks what he heard.
B8 (28-30): lifting up = cross; Bruce; always pleases the Father; many believed.
B9 (31-32): Morris: incomplete faith; abide = true disciple; Dods, Tasker; freedom via relationship.
B10 (33-36): never enslaved? history of foreign rule; Barclay/Josephus on Judas the Galilean; slave of sin (Tenney, Bruce, Maclaren); Son frees (Tasker, Maclaren).
B11 (37-41a): physical seed but seeking to kill; Gen 18; Bruce; Spurgeon on place for the word.
B12 (41b-43): "not born of fornication" hint at Jesus' birth (Tenney: virgin birth); Alford, Maclaren pre-existence; Morrison on bias.
B13 (44-47): devil murderer and liar; Cain (1 John 3:15) Alford, Dods, Cyril; Morris on sinlessness claim.
B14 (48-50): Samaritan with demon; Spurgeon: demon cannot honor God.
B15 (51-53): never see death; Alford; Spurgeon; mockery about Abraham.
B16 (54-55): Father glorifies him; Barclay.
B17 (56-59): Abraham rejoiced (Bruce, Gen 22:8); fifty years (Tenney, Barclay, Num 4:3); I AM (Exod 3:13-14; Tenney, Dods, Bruce); stones; Morris; no clear miracle in his leaving; Clarke's explanation called possibly fanciful.
