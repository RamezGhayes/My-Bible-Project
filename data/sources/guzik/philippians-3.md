# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Philippians 3
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/philippians-3/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Heading A1 is registered as it appears on the page, including the doubled word "الناموس".)
A1 (1-2): "finally" not near end (Wuest); rejoice in Lord theme; Martin Christian "Hallelujah"; Meyer against gloom; Maclaren abrupt tone; dogs = Jewish term for Gentiles turned on Judaizers; Lightfoot (via Muller) scavenger dogs; evil workers; concision = mutilation (Martin, Lev 21:5).
A2 (3-4): true circumcision: worship in Spirit, glory in Christ, no confidence in flesh; Wuest on "worship"; promoters of flesh least qualified (Col 2:23).
A3 (5-6): four inherited (Lev 12:3; Israel; Benjamin: Saul 1 Sam 9:1-2, with Judah 1 Kings 12:21, Jerusalem Judg 21:1; Hebrew of Hebrews vs Hellenized); three chosen (Pharisee = separated per Barclay, Matt 23:23; persecutor Rom 10:2; blameless by human not God's standard, Luke 18:18-23).
A4 (7): gains plural / loss singular (Wuest); Spurgeon bookkeeper; not harmful in themselves; pearl (Matt 13:45-46).
B1 (8): "count" again ~30 years later; Spurgeon still a good bargain; really lost all; skubala refuse/dog scraps; Clarke.
B2 (9): own righteousness vs God's by faith; Spurgeon renounced as quickly as sinner renounces sin.
B3 (10-11): experiential knowledge; levels of knowing a person; Spurgeon titles of Christ; resurrection power now (Wuest, Spurgeon; proof, justification, life, comfort); fellowship of sufferings (Rom 8:17); possibly near death; "attain" = longing for full bodily resurrection, not doubt of salvation.
B4 (12-14): not perfectionism; leaders projecting arrival; M'Cheyne, Brainerd, Baxter, Herbert, Fletcher, Whitefield (Spurgeon); Muller child; Spurgeon Christ's work finished, Spirit's ongoing; Spanish coin Ne Plus Ultra -> Plus Ultra; Luke 9:62; reasons Christ laid hold (Rom 6:4; 8:29; Acts 9:15-16); forgetting good and bad past; prize = high calling itself; Meyer; Clarke race; "in Christ Jesus".
B5 (15-16): mature share mindset; God will reveal; walk by what known; unity (1:27-2:2); threats outside (1:27-30) and inside (2:3), not carnal like Corinth.
C1 (17): not arrogant (1 Cor 11:1); observe others too.
C2 (18-19): enemies of cross in practice; license (1 Cor 6; Rom 6); Spurgeon three reasons for tears; belly, shame, earthly (rich fool).
C3 (20): citizenship; Barclay Roman colony; marks of heavenly citizens; Spurgeon patriotism; Savior and Lord titles of Caesar.
C4 (21): body transformed like His; resurrection body renewed earthly body; power to subdue; Meyer.
