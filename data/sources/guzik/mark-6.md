# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 6
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-6/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page not reachable; the arabic.enduringword.com pattern redirects to a generic index.)
A1 (1-3): Nazareth wonders; "carpenter" a put-down (no rabbinic training); anecdote under Julian: carpenter's son making Julian's coffin. tekton = builder (wood or stone): God the builder finishes; three lessons (raw material, time and work, hardest wood). "Son of Mary" disparaging (Lane: insulting to name by mother; Morgan: suspicion and contempt); Joseph likely dead. Brothers and sisters (Mark 3:31): Mary not perpetual virgin. Offense from over-familiarity.
A2 (4-6): rejection the prophet's lot; unbelief limited works: God works without belief but not against unbelief. Marveled at unbelief (cf. Luke 7:9); Trapp: monstrous sin that astonishes Christ. Did not withdraw; kept teaching.
A3 (7-13): pairs with authority (John 20:21). Travel light: dependence; rabbinic rule on temple. Present the message, not force; shake dust: rejecting Jewish town treated as Gentile; Sodom and Gomorrah, degrees of judgment. They went (doing not just hearing). Morgan: preaching to produce repentance; Barclay: brought, not created message. Oil (James 5:14-15): symbol of Spirit and medicine (Robertson: olive oil among best medicines).
B1 (14-16): "king" a title Rome denied; Elijah (Mal 4:5), the Prophet (Deut 18:15); guilt clouds Herod's sight of Jesus.
B2 (17-29): Lane: Herod weak more than cruel. John spared not the powerful; Trapp story of Latimer and Henry VIII. Dance (Trapp, Robertson: lewd, unusual for rank). Herodias planned it. Wessel: "exceedingly sorry" same word as Gethsemane (Mark 14:34). Trapp: Herod's later ruin (defeat by Aretas, exile to Gaul). Disciples buried body.
C1 (30-34): rest after work (John 9:4); Clarke: rest is necessary for those who labour. Disciples saw demand, Jesus saw need; sheep without shepherd; Shepherd feeds with teaching.
C2 (35-44): "You give them"; 200 denarii; start with what is at hand. Green grass, Ps 23:2; Barclay: word for rows of vegetables. Lane: followed accepted blessing form but looked up to heaven. Extravagant yet simple; catacomb art. Twelve baskets: generous, no waste.
C3 (45-46): exhausting day drove Him to prayer.
C4 (47-52): John 6:18-19; fourth watch. Augustine: came walking on the waves, He tramples life's storms. Spurgeon: little headway, God judges the effort of the heart. Peter omitted from Mark (Matt 14:28-31), perhaps humility (Robertson). Hearts hardened about the loaves.
C5 (53-56): Gennesaret, touch the hem; closes section on power over nature.
