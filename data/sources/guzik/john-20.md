# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 20
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-20/ (two WebFetch passes). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): first day, still dark; Roman guard and seal (Matt 27:62-66); Mary Magdalene, seven demons (Luke 8:2; Mark 16:9), her past does not disqualify her from being first witness; came to finish burial (Morris: Nicodemus could not complete it before Sabbath); she assumes theft; Dods: "we" fits Luke's other women.
A2 (3-4): John unnamed out of humility, outruns Peter, perhaps younger; both take the news seriously.
A3 (5-10): three verbs: blepei (v5, sees), theorei (v6, looks carefully), eiden (v8, perceives and believes); John stays out (Tenney: respect, defilement), small entrance (~1 m by 0.75 m); spices dried into a stiff shell, cloths would be cut or torn, here lie in order as if the body passed out of them (Barclay); head cloth apart; no thieves; Turin Shroud with caution (Trench; dictionary of evangelical theology; 1978 study), John mentions two cloths; Boice citing Sir Edward Clarke on evidence; Alford and Maclaren: John believed before seeing the risen Lord; resurrection meanings: Son of God (Rom 1:4), our resurrection (1 Thess 4:14), God's plan for the body (Morgan), ongoing intercession (Heb 7:25), sacrifice fully accepted.
B1 (11-13): weeping Mary sees two angels at head and feet; mercy seat cherubim (Exod 25:18-19; Clarke); Maclaren: grief made angels seem minor; Trapp: angels serve the saints; Heb 13:2.
B2 (14-16): not recognized: tears, unexpectedness, changed appearance (Mark 16:12; Alford, Dods, Morris); Tenney: wanting to carry the body shows devotion; Rabbouni = my teacher; Trench on her name; Meyer and Spurgeon: power of hearing one's name; Christ calls his own by name.
B3 (17-18): present imperative = stop clinging (Morris); Tenney: not defilement, do not hold me back; Bruce's Day-of-Atonement view not worth much time; body real and touchable; first time "brothers" (Spurgeon); Augustine on "my Father and your Father"; women's testimony not accepted in court yet chosen; Bruce on Celsus mocking it.
C1 (19): evening, locked doors for fear (Morris); Trapp: Pentecost turns fear to boldness; sudden arrival (Alford); appearances list (Matt 28:9-10; Mark 16:12-13; Luke 24:13-35; 1 Cor 15:5); peace greeting.
C2 (20-23): hands and side (Luke 24:39-40); peace before proclamation (Morgan, Boice); sent as the Father sent (17:18); breathed: Gen 2:7; Ezek 37:9, new creation, disciples "born again" (Trench, Clarke); Bruce: 7:39 now the Spirit given; Dods: same Spirit that was in him; Acts 2:38 as practice of declaring forgiveness; others beside the eleven present (Luke 24:33), so for every believer; church does not create or withhold forgiveness on its own, it declares it by God's Word and the Spirit's wisdom; Trench: declares terms of forgiveness; Morris: binding and loosing; Tenney: Spirit given for work, not ornament; four parts: assurance, commission, Spirit, authority. No mention of priests or sacramental confession.
C3 (24-25): Thomas the twin absent; Maclaren: isolation harmed him; Morris: grief; "doubting Thomas" unfair; Clarke: stubborn doubt; nails in feet implied by Luke 24:39.
C4 (26-27): eighth day, Sunday gatherings, maranatha (Bruce); rebuke in love (Maclaren); Spurgeon: Lord's gentleness to doubters.
C5 (28-29): kyrios and theos, Jesus accepts it; Dods: confession not exclamation; Tenney: a Jew would not call a man God; Pliny to Trajan (~112); Spurgeon's five aspects and preference over the Athanasian Creed; Maclaren; maybe Thomas did not touch; Boice: faith accepts what God gave; Clarke: blessing for later believers; Spurgeon's list of ways the blessing is lost (demanding voices, visions, feelings, answers, approval, others' support); doubt a stage not a destination.
C6 (30-31): seven signs listed (2:1-11; 4:46-54; 5:1-15; 6:1-14; 6:15-21; 9:1-12; 11:1-44); cf. 21:25; Son of God = shared nature (Tenney); life in his name, name = person and authority (Tasker); belief simple but not always easy.
Theology note: 20:22-23 Guzik reads the authority as the church's declaration of forgiveness by the gospel, given to all believers; close to the Reformed declarative reading; brief neutral note added about the Reformed link to the ministry of the Word and church discipline.
Second pass 2026-11-27 (owner asked for per-verse depth): page re-read per verse in halves; extra points used in entries (e.g. 19: Cicero, Tacitus, 1968 find, Nico Haas, William Edwards, Lactantius, Apuleius; 20: Morgan/Trench on peace, Maclaren on repeating words; 21: Trapp, Plummer, Bruce, Boice's invitations, exetasai, Spurgeon on empty nets, Tenney on 'you').
