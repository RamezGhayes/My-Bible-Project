# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Mark 11
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/mark-11/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic Mark pages on arabic.enduringword.com redirect to the Arabic index.)
A1 (1-6): John shows earlier visits; planned arrival; young donkey = man of peace, suffering servant-king; rabbis: Dan 7:13 vs Zech 9:9, humble if Israel unworthy; Ironside: Creator, unbroken colt subject to Him; pre-arranged "The Lord has need of it".
A2 (7-11): garments and branches costly honor; now accepts public acclaim (Luke 19:40 stones); Ps 118:19-29 scriptural praise; worship forms Hos 14:2, Ps 100:2, 134:2; ironic triumph vs Roman triumph (5,000 slain, golden chariot); looked around as inspector (Mal 3:1-3); courage, price on His head (John 11:57).
B1 (12-14): leaves promise fruit; cursed for pretense; Israel form without fruit; only destructive miracle; Morgan: object lesson, like Christmas tree or botany lesson.
B2 (15-19): exploitation of pilgrims; temple tax two days' wages; Court of the Gentiles the only place for Gentiles to pray; house of prayer for all nations; den of thieves = hideout of unrepentant; leaders plot.
B3 (20-24): withered from roots; "faith in God" objective genitive (Robertson, Wuest, Expositor's Greek NT; Rom 3:22, 26; Gal 3:26; Heb 6:2); mountain = idiom of obstacles; Wiersbe: not faith in faith or feelings; promise within God's will.
B4 (25-26): unforgiveness hinders prayer, hard heart a mountain; reconciliation first (Rom 12:18); forgiven forgive, refusal questions whether forgiven.
C1 (27-28): not seeking debate but ready; by what authority.
C2 (29-33): question about John not evasion; Robertson: "home thrust"; Barclay: evasive people twist themselves into silence, facing truth the only road with a future; Jesus declines since they are not sincere.
