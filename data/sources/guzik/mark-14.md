# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Mark 14
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/mark-14/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-2): Passover tension, Rome alert; Lane: feasts treated as one 7-day celebration; leaders feared people more than God; John 11:57.
A2 (3): Mary of Bethany (John 12:1-8), not Luke 7 woman; Pliny on perfume value; Lane: heirloom; broke neck, poured all; royal anointing; Spurgeon on quiet service.
A3 (4-9): Judas thief (John 12:6); Wiersbe: "waste" vs "son of perdition"; Spurgeon on 300 denarii estimate; Barclay kalos vs agathos; Ironside "did what she could"; burial; memorial.
A4 (10-11): motives (pride, greed, forcing hand); greed primary (Matt 26:15); God uses evil without commanding sin. (Arabic page has a reference "Mark 6:3", probably 3:6; not used.)
B1 (12-16): water-carrier; secrecy from Judas; Cole: unnamed disciples; chronology, Morris on calendars (John 18:28; 19:14); no lamb mentioned.
B2 (17-21): first Passover standing (Arabic page "Ex 11:12", i.e. Ex 12:11); reclining = rest; shame of betrayal at table; Jesus loved Judas, last chance.
B3 (22-25): Passover symbols; Ex 24:8; transubstantiation, Luther, Calvin (spiritual presence), Zwingli; more than symbol; Jer 31:31-34; Rev 19:9.
B4 (26-31): Hallel Ps 116-118; Ironside, Spurgeon on praise; Robertson: John 14-17 in upper room; Zech 13:7; Heb 12:2; Maclaren on enduring small things; 1 Cor 10:12; Robertson on Mark's vivid wording; Morgan on overconfidence.
C1 (32-36): olive press; 2 Cor 5:21; Heb 5:7-8; Abba; cup of wrath Ps 75:8, Isa 51:17, Jer 25:15; Ironside: wrath not death; Spurgeon on the cup; decision; "if possible".
C2 (37-42): "Simon"; watch and pray; battles won before crisis; Morgan: sleep while he watches; hour come.
C3 (43-52): kiss; John 18:10 Peter; worldly force vs word (Acts 2:37); Luke 22:51; Maclaren on church's clumsy sword; Barclay: four crosses; young man = Mark tradition, Robertson (upper room in his mother's house, Acts 12:12), Spurgeon.
D1 (no range): Annas and day session not in Mark; 3 Jewish + 3 Roman stages. (not registered, no verse range)
D2 (53-59): temple charge, John 2:19; Lane on penalty; illegal trial; Morgan quoting Tennyson on half-truth lies; Cole.
D3 (60-62): silence, Isa 53:7; Bruce on high priest standing; Spurgeon on failing trial; Jesus the true victor.
D4 (63-65): torn clothes; self-righteousness to cruelty; Spurgeon on bearing reproach.
D5 (66-72): following at distance, fear of man; Trapp, Clarke, Lane (formal denial), Wiersbe; Luke 24:34, John 21.
