# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word, Acts 21 (Arabic)
URL read: https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/acts-21/ (Arabic, read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch, section outline).
A1 (1-2): painful parting from Ephesian elders; Bruce on wording of tearing away.
A2 (3-4): founding of Tyre church unknown, Acts gives snapshots; disciples urged through the Spirit; mentions view that warning was human interpretation.
A3 (5-6): continued convinced of God's will; kneeling and praying on shore distinctive.
A4 (7): believers in nearly every city.
A5 (8-9): Philip one of the Seven (8:40); four prophesying daughters did not speak of Paul's suffering, Spirit chose another messenger; Bruce on daughters.
A6 (10-14): Agabus's prophecy genuinely of the Spirit; companions' plea human; warnings to prepare not stop (19:21); obedience not defiance; do not treat human interpretation of God's will as His voice.
A7 (15-16): love for his kin (Rom 9:1-3); Mnason; arrival c. AD 57.
B1 (17-20a): warm welcome, James and elders, detailed report, glorified God.
B2 (20b-22): zealous for the Law; rumors; Paul kept the Law (18:18-21), opposed it only as means of justification (Rom 14:4-6).
B3 (23-25): vow, purification, expenses; Gentiles only the apostolic decree.
B4 (26): Clarke: rites useless for salvation not harmful; some call it compromise/hypocrisy, Guzik answers with 1 Cor 9:20; offering not atonement, Christ alone atones.
B5 (27-30): Asian Jews; Trophimus; Gentiles barred, warning inscriptions; Romans allowed execution; echo of Stephen (6:13).
B6 (31-36): Antonia; two chains; noise; "Away with him" like cry against Jesus (Luke 23:18; John 19:15).
B7 (37-39): Greek; Egyptian rebel (Josephus); Tarsus; composure; rejects Haenchen's view of fabrication.
B8 (40): Hebrew/Aramaic; opportunity to reach kin (Rom 9:1-5); parallels Paul/Jesus journey; Phil 3:10, Rom 8:29.
Left out: nothing harsh used.
