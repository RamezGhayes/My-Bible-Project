# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Romans 4
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/romans-4/ . Our own outline, not his text. Some page references have garbled digits (Gen 15:6, Gen 17:4-5, Rom 4:7); a "Galatians 3:5" on circumcision and the whole law is likely Gal 5:3 and was not used.
A1 (1-3): answers 3:31; Abraham greatest Jewish figure; boasting not before God (3:23); rabbis said Abraham kept law before given; reckoned, not perfect; righteousness positive; logizomai bookkeeping (Wuest); Calvin before God's court.
A2 (4-5): wages vs gift; charis to friends not enemies in classical use (Wuest), Calvary; works make God debtor; Murray worker vs truster; justifies the ungodly, Morris quoting Denney miracle not legal fiction; one way of salvation in both testaments.
A3 (6-8): Ps 32:1-2; David knew sin and forgiveness; Lenski sin like shadow; Poole: opponents reject imputation yet "reckon" used ten times; credited righteousness.
A4 (9-12): Gen 15:6 before Gen 17, at least 14 years; sign and seal; father of all believers; "Abraham our father" not exclusive.
A5 (13-15): heir of world before Mosaic law; Newell faith basis of blessing; law works wrath; Morris transgression crossing a line; root of sin broken trust, Adam.
B1 (16): grace/law principles, faith/works responses; Spurgeon grace and faith together, grace and merit opposite; Poole human weakness; father of us all.
B2 (17-18): Gen 17:5; supernatural act; Sarah's dead womb and Eph 2:1; Smith on God calling future realities as present; faith not passive; Spurgeon obedience is living faith; Trapp faith corrects reason and imagination.
B3 (19-22): not weak; Greek reading question; did not waver; fully persuaded; is our God too small; Calvin power accepted when no obstacle; Spurgeon prayers for faith, preachers' doubts, "according to your faith".
B4 (23-25): written for us; faith in death and resurrection; paradidomi Father handed Son to justice (Wuest); Lenski resurrection proves sufficiency; Poole v25 summary of gospel.
