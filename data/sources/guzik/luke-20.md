# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 20
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-20/ . Our own outline, not his text. Page title: "سؤال وجواب مع يسوع". Note: the page labels both subsections of part II with "أ )"; registered as B1/B2.
A1 (1-2): Jesus teaching and preaching good news in the temple, not seeking debate; challenge follows the temple cleansing (19:45-46); Pate: leaders now saw Him as an adversary.
A2 (3-8): counter-question on John's baptism; leaders weigh both answers, want to win not find truth; Geldenhuys: those who cannot recognize authority when they see it will not be persuaded by argument; Jesus declines insincere questioners; John had pointed to Jesus as Messiah.
B1 (9-16a): landlord/tenant disputes common, esp. Galilee; Isa 5:1-7 vineyard = Israel, tenants = leaders; owner's patience = God's patience and coming reckoning; Jeremias via Pate: tenants thought killing heir gives them inheritance; Morris on possession by long use; the son = Jesus who knew He would be killed.
B2 (16b-19): "God forbid" shows they understood it was aimed at them; Ps 118 rejected stone, linked to triumphal entry; 1 Cor 10:4, 1 Pet 2:8, Dan 2:45; Pate: rejection of Son tied to rejection of stone; Fitzmyer via Pate: key load-bearing stone; falling on stone = brokenness, stone falling = crushing judgment; leaders feared the crowd.
C1 (20-22): spies posing as sincere, flattery; tax dilemma; Rome required direct tax from AD 6, nationalists refused; Clarke: word for spies implies ambush.
C2 (23-26): denarius; Pate: Tiberius image and inscription; render to Caesar / God; 1 Pet 2:17; Morris: citizens of two realms; Barclay: debt of gratitude for order; France: "give" = repay a debt; Clarke: God's image in man means we belong to God; silence and marvel.
D1 (27-33): Sadducees deny resurrection; Pate: name from Zadok (Ezek 44:15); Morris: aristocratic priestly conservative party; Deut 25:5-10 levirate; question meant to make resurrection absurd; Spurgeon: probably a stock mocking story.
D2 (34-36): resurrection life a different order, no marriage; Luke 16:27-28 rich man knows family; Rev 21:22-23; 22:1-5; central joy of heaven is relationship with God; no jealousy or exclusion; Trapp's contrast with Muslim/Mormon views; "like angels", "sons of God"; Carson: angels mention struck the Sadducees.
D3 (37-40): burning bush in the Pentateuch they accepted; God of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, not of dead; implications: departed live, remain themselves, known, not lost; Spurgeon: living God of living people; scribes "well said", no more questions.
E1 (41-44): Ps 110:1, how is Messiah David's son and Lord; tests their understanding; Rev 22:16 root and offspring of David.
E2 (45-47): warning to disciples in crowd's hearing; love of status; devour widows' houses, long prayers for show, greater judgment; scribes taught honoring and paying teachers was paramount; Morgan: long prayers can show distance from God; God values reality over appearance.
