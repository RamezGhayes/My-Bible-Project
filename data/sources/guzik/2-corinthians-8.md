# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 8
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-8/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Outline: A. Examples and encouragement (1-9); B. Practical words of advice regarding giving (10-24).
A1 (1-5): grace = opportunity and willingness; Macedonia north, Achaia south; Philippi, Thessalonica, Berea; Rome took wealth; collection 1 Cor 16:1-4; according to and beyond ability; widow Luke 21:1-4; Trapp richer mind; Hughes sacrifice not quantity; begging to give; gave themselves first.
A2 (6-8): Titus to complete; intentions vs action; ironic list; charis used for giving, four points; Redpath; not by commandment, compulsory giving = taxation; 1 John 3:17-18; Matt 6:21; not a contest; Calvin rich owe large tribute.
A3 (9): grace = giving; preexistent deity; Ps 45:8; Hughes inwardly rich; not exaggerate poverty, Matt 8:20; benefits list; become rich.
B1 (10-12): follow through; Barclay; Trapp "mouth-mercy"; what you have, overspending no excuse; willing mind; tithing: not commanded in NT, Luke 11:42, regular/planned/proportional/private (1 Cor 16:1-4), generous (2 Cor 9), 10% benchmark, Luke 16:11.
B2 (13-15): not burden, rejects "give till it hurts"; equality not socialism; Calvin; Hodge on communism and neglect; roles may reverse; Exod 16:18; Hodge "Property is like manna"; Calvin hoarded wealth rots.
B3 (16-24): Titus's care; unnamed brother, candidates Luke, Barnabas, Silas, Timothy, identity doesn't matter; avoid blame; second brother; partner; messengers, glory of Christ; three reasons.
