# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 12
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-12/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-6): reluctant boasting; super apostles boasted of visions; list of NT visions (Luke 1; Matt 17:9; Acts 7:55-56; 9:10; 10; 16:9-10; 18:9-11; 27:23-25; Rev 1:1); visions subjective, benefit mainly recipient; third person "a man in Christ," first person from v. 7; fourteen years, silence; body or not, God knows; third heaven = beyond atmosphere and stars, God's dwelling (Isa 6:1; Rev 4:1-2), Clarke on three heavens; paradise from Persian royal garden, rejects Origen's earthly paradise; heard not saw, unlike modern heaven-visit stories; Morgan suspicious of people who talk of visions; Clarke: speculation ridiculous; restraint.
A2 (7): thorn to prevent pride; everyone saw thorn, none knew vision; skolops tent stake, LXX of something troubling (Kruse); given by God, messenger of Satan; buffet; Spurgeon: he reckoned his trial a gift; Redpath; Paul takes problem to the Wonderful Counselor.
A3 (8): Phil 4:6; three times, Hebrew idiom for persistence (Morgan); Gethsemane three times (Mark 14:39-41); Trapp: "not gifts, but graces prevail in prayer"; no masochist; physical, mental, spiritual dimensions; identity unknown: proposals incl. Tertullian earache/headache, Ramsay malarial fever; Spurgeon: each picks his own thorn; silence lets all identify.
A4 (9-10): God answered differently; grace sufficient, strengthens shoulder not removes load; Spurgeon: great tribulation brings out great strength of God; present tense; Redpath: "rather modest of the Lord to say sufficient" (little fish and ocean); Bunyan through Spurgeon; dependence not independence; Morgan: proud Christian hinders; Maclaren; pleasure in weakness not asceticism, Hughes against courting suffering; Phil 4:13; Calvin: valleys receive rain, mountains stay dry; Jesus' weakness on cross and resurrection power; Spurgeon: without the Spirit thorns produce vice.
A5 (11-13): Corinthians should have defended him; nothing yet not behind; signs, wonders, mighty deeds; "forgive me this wrong," Trapp: pleasant irony; Clarke: duty to support ministry.
B1 (14-18): third visit (first 18 months, Acts 18:11; second painful); not burden; Clarke against ministers enriching themselves from poor; seeks them not theirs; children and parents, Corinthians still spiritual children (Phil 4:10-19); spend and be spent; "crafty" is critics' charge (Clarke); Titus and brother walked same steps.
B2 (19-21): not excuses but before God in Christ; for edification; Hughes; list of sins; Calvin: true pastor grieves over others' sins; uncleanness, fornication, lewdness; repentance not perfection.
