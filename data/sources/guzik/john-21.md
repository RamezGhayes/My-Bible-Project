# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 21
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-21/ (two WebFetch passes). Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): Sea of Tiberias, Galilee appearances (Matt 28:7, 10); two unnamed disciples = the many faithful unnamed (Morgan); going fishing: disobedience or prudence, the heart decides; Clarke: support stopped after the crucifixion; Morris: confusion before Pentecost; Morrison and Spurgeon: empty night, expect disappointment in service and keep going.
A2 (4-6): dawn, unrecognized (Dods sudden; Tenney mist or discouragement); "children" (paidia), asking for food (Trapp: householder); right side: no symbolic meaning (Morris, Plummer), the point is obeying his direction; Luke 5:1-11 parallel (Tasker); Bruce, Spurgeon: Christ directs service, depend on him.
A3 (7-8): John recognizes first, as at the tomb (20:4, 8); Peter girds his outer garment (ependutes, Dods via Theophylact), naked or lightly clad (Morris, Barclay), Trench: Eastern custom of not meeting a superior undressed; 200 cubits.
A4 (9-11): fire, fish, bread ready: servant even after resurrection; bring your fish; 153, net not torn, eyewitness fisherman (Tenney), Dods: a fisherman counts; Peter alone hauls (Bruce); allegories: Augustine (sum 1-17), letter values (Dods), Jerome (153 species), Cyril (100/50/3); Guzik: only the number is certain, warning against inventing hidden meanings.
A5 (12-14): "come and eat", Boice's list of Jesus' invitations (John 1:39; Matt 11:28-29; Mark 6:31; Matt 25:34-36); exetasai = formal questioning (Dods); Spurgeon imagines shame of Peter and Thomas; third appearance (Tasker).
B1 (15-16): full name "Simon son of Jonah" = seriousness (Morris), reminder he was not yet the rock; "more than these" = more than the others, recalling Matt 26:33 (fish reading noted and set aside); agapao vs phileo: some see little difference, many see Peter's new caution; Boice; feed lambs, shepherd sheep, verb covers whole shepherd role (Morris), charge reaches all later leaders (Clarke); flock belongs to Jesus. No claim of Petrine primacy.
B2 (17): third time Jesus uses Peter's word (Alford); three denials, three affirmations (Clarke); grieved; Jesus asks for love, not promises (Maclaren); Spurgeon: even strong servants need self-examination.
B3 (18-19): prophecy of crucifixion, bound and led (Morris); assurance he would not fail again; tradition of upside-down crucifixion (Clarke via Eusebius and others; Alford via Jerome); Justin Martyr via Trapp; "follow me" present imperative, continuous (Tenney, Morris).
B4 (20-23): Peter changes the subject; Morgan: rebuke for seeking God's will for another; Spurgeon: first one's own duty; Tasker: John at Ephesus; Barclay: Paul champion, Peter shepherd, John witness; rumor: Jesus spoke hypothetically (Tasker); John outlived the others and survived attempts on his life; how easily words are distorted.
B5 (24-25): the disciple who testified and wrote; Morris: hyperbole of joy; Guzik: Jesus' ongoing work through his people belongs to the "many other things".
Theology note: 21:15-17. Guzik gives no primacy to Peter and applies the charge to all shepherds; consistent with the Reformed reading, no note needed.
Second pass 2026-11-27 (owner asked for per-verse depth): page re-read per verse in halves; extra points used in entries (e.g. 19: Cicero, Tacitus, 1968 find, Nico Haas, William Edwards, Lactantius, Apuleius; 20: Morgan/Trench on peace, Maclaren on repeating words; 21: Trapp, Plummer, Bruce, Boice's invitations, exetasai, Spurgeon on empty nets, Tenney on 'you').
