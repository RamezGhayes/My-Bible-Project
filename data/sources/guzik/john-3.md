# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 3
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-3/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-3): Nicodemus Pharisee, ruler, sincere; came by night (fear or quiet); "we know" common opinion (Clarke); signs alone not proof (2 Thess 2:9; Rev 13:13-14); Jesus breaks assumption that descent from Abraham secures the kingdom (rabbinic Abraham at gate of Gehenna); new life not more teaching (Alford); born from above = born again (Tenney); one can wash oneself but not beget oneself; 1 Pet 1:3, 23; Jas 1:18; Titus 3:5; 2 Cor 5:17.
A2 (4): Nicodemus thought of moral reform; Dods: a Gentile might grasp it more readily; OT promises (Deut, Jer, Ezek): regathering, cleansing and renewal, Messiah's reign; Jews thought the first two fulfilled already.
A3 (5-8): v5 options: baptism (no real OT basis), natural birth (possible but obvious), word (Eph 5:26), Spirit (John 7:38-39); best: cleansing water of Ezek 36:25-28, Jesus expected Nicodemus to know it; nation-citizenship illustration; Alford: flesh = all that is natural; Morris: excludes self-righteousness; wind = Spirit's sovereignty; Spurgeon: do not grieve the Spirit.
A4 (9-13): confused because he thought the promise already his; teacher of Israel should know (Tenney); earthly vs heavenly things; Morris: only the one from heaven speaks with authority; Dods: claim to be the Word; Clarke: "ascended" idiom for knowing.
A5 (14-15): bronze serpent (Num 21:4-9) = sin judged; Christ made sin (2 Cor 5:21 idea); saved by looking (Isa 45:22); "lifted up" crucifixion and exaltation (John 12:32; Acts 2:33); Maclaren; eternal life a quality not just length; Tenney: faith receives.
A6 (16): God's love precedes faith; Jews thought God loved Israel only, Morris: love for all mankind distinctively Christian; three loves (Morrison; Eph 5:25; Gal 2:20); Alford: only begotten recalls Isaac; believing = trust and reliance; seven-part breakdown of the verse. No discussion of extent of atonement.
A7 (17-21): not sent to condemn; unbeliever already condemned; those who never heard: Rom 1-2, Bruce; loved darkness; Trapp: dirty dog on golden pavement; Dods: doing truth = living by the light one has; Clarke.
B1 (22-24): Jesus baptizing in Judea, continuing baptism of repentance (Morris); Aenon "springs" (Bruce; Tenney two sites); John not yet imprisoned.
B2 (25-26): dispute over purification; "all" hyperbole (Morris).
B3 (27-30): all is gift from heaven; not the Christ; friend of the bridegroom (Morris: Yahweh as Israel's bridegroom); joy fulfilled (Dods, Bruce); motto of every servant (Trapp, Meyer).
B4 (31-33): from above vs from earth; Jesus not merely a holy man; few receive (Spurgeon); seal that God is true (Tasker).
B5 (34-36): Spirit without measure; Father loves Son (Bruce on agapao/phileo); Dods: "the Son" implies deity; wrath steady opposition to sin (Spurgeon, Morris, Tenney); cannot take the gift and reject the Giver; four "must" statements (vv. 7, 14, 30).
No theology note needed: Guzik's v5 reading (Ezek 36) and v16 treatment do not conflict with the Reformed reading; he does not discuss the order of new birth and faith.
