# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Matthew 24
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/matthew-24/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page arabic.enduringword.com/matthew-24-ar/ redirects to the Arabic index; not readable.)
A1 (1-2): last exit from temple; Herod's work 19 BC to AD 63; AD 70 literal fulfilment, fire, melted gold; Bruce (finality, "these things"), Trapp via Josephus (10,000 workers, Titus wanted to spare temple), Clarke (stone sizes), Poole (God disregards houses of prayer turned dens of thieves). Ezra 6:15, Matt 23:16, Acts 6:13.
A2 (3): private on Mount of Olives; two/three questions assumed simultaneous; prophecy kept vague so each generation ready; Morgan (questions as one; Jesus corrects).
B1 (4-8): deception, false christs; wars etc not specific signs; birth pains; France (prevent premature excitement), Bruce (accumulation of disasters); William Miller 1844.
B2 (9-14): persecution; Clarke (affliction personified); Spurgeon (persecution exposes traitors, cold love); France (lawlessness and love); Morgan (has witness been completed?); Rev 14:6-7.
C1 (15): idolatrous desecration; holy place = temple, future rebuilt; Israel 1948; rejects Roman standards; Antiochus foreshadow; 1290 days; France, Bruce (holy land), Carson (temple). Dan 9:27, 11:31, 12:11, 2 Thess 2:3-4, Rev 13:14-15.
C2 (16-20): flee; France (refugee hardship); Christians to Pella; Carson (AD 68 tradition), Clarke via Eusebius/Epiphanius, Barclay (Jews crowded city); primarily end-time; church raptured before (1 Thess 4:16-17).
C3 (21-28): unprecedented, AD 70 does not match; days shortened; false christs; lightning; Spurgeon (sudden, visible), France (parousia official visit); carcass/eagles judgment when ripe.
C4 (29-31): Joel 2:10, Rev 6:12-14, Isa 34:4; sign: Constantine cross view, prefers visible return; France (semeion = standard LXX), Barclay (arrival of king); Bruce (not judgment of Jerusalem); Rev 19:11.
C5 (32-35): fig tree, Luke 21:29-31; Dan 12:11; fig as Israel Jer 24:1-10, Hos 9:10 but point is season; this generation = generation seeing signs, rejects disciples and AD 70; race view: Clarke for, France "embarrassingly wrong".
D1 (36): only Father knows; against date-setting; Dan 12:11 dilemma.
D2 (37-39): business as usual; Bruce, Carson; Gen 6:1-5.
D3 (40-44): taken = rapture (1 Thess 4:16-17), France (taken to be with); watch, thief; dilemmas, Zech 14:5, Barclay (seem to contradict); two-stage resolution; Trapp citing Suetonius on Julius Caesar.
E1 (45-47): faithful servant, diligence, reward.
E2 (48-51): three failures; "no hurry" lie; Bruce (cut in two = severe whipping).
