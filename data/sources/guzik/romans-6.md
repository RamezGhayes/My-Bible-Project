# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), Romans 6
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/romans-6/ . Our own outline, not his text. (Arabic page not reachable: arabic.enduringword.com Romans links redirect to the Arabic index.)
A1 (1): question from 5:20; Rasputin example; grace treated as dangerous; "continue" present active = habit.
A2 (2): "Certainly not" = perish the thought; died to sin; Eph 2:1 dead in vs dead to sin.
A3 (3-4): baptizo = immerse/overwhelm; union with death and burial; Matt 3:11; Acts 1:5; Mark 10:39; Bruce: baptism not optional; baptism illustrates, does not produce; newness of life.
A4 (5-10): Morris: graft; John 15; Phil 3:10-11; old man = self patterned after Adam, crucified by God; Lenski: believers did not do this themselves; Eph 4:22, 24; Col 3:9-10; body of sin; Spurgeon: sin an interloper with no throne; flesh = screen; Gal 5:24; Ezek 36:26-27; Spartacus film.
A5 (11-12): reckon = accounting term; reckon old man dead rather than battle him; Moody anecdote of freed slave woman unsure of freedom.
A6 (13-14): instruments = weapons; consecration Ex 29:20; David's hands and eyes; Spurgeon: test, promise, encouragement; Rom 5:21; Bruce: grace as excuse shows not under grace; 1 John 3:6, 9; Spurgeon: "grace that does not change my life will not save my soul"; Raynald and Edward anecdote.
B1 (15): aorist = single act, occasional sin (Wuest).
B2 (16-17): slave of whom you obey; no neutral; obey from the heart; form of doctrine = mold (Clarke).
B3 (18): change of master; Wuest definition of slave; not sinless perfection (1 John 1:8); Wuest: need not yield.
B4 (19-23): human analogy for weakness of flesh; lawlessness to lawlessness, righteousness to holiness; roots of tree; fruit shame and death; holiness and everlasting life; wages vs gift; Clarke: sinners toil for wages.
