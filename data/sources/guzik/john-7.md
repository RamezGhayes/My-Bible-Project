# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), John 7
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/john-7/ . Our own outline, not his text. The page covers 7:1-52 only; 7:53 is handled on the john-8 page (section A1 there).
A1 (1-2): stayed in Galilee by the Father's timing, not fear; Feast of Booths a week in autumn; Bruce: booths of branches on courtyards/roofs (Lev 23:40-43).
A2 (3-5): brothers are real brothers; Clarke rejects perpetual virginity; unbelief (Mark 3:21; Matt 12:46-47; 13:55-56); later in upper room (Acts 1:14); Dods, Morris, Alford.
A3 (6-9): Barclay on kairos; world does not hate them; Dods, Bruce, Tasker.
A4 (10-13): went up secretly, not with caravans; crowds divided (good/deceiver); fear; Alford, Dods, Tenney, Bruce.
B1 (14-18): taught openly; untrained (Acts 22:3); teaching from the Father; willing to do God's will -> know; seeks sender's glory; Alford, Dods, Clarke, Tenney, Trench.
B2 (19-24): none keep the law; "you have a demon"; circumcision on Sabbath argument (John 5:16); righteous judgment; Trapp, Dods, Morgan. (Page prints a cross-reference as Lev 3:12, apparently an error; not used.)
B3 (25-29): Jerusalemites puzzled; Messiah of unknown origin (Mal 3:1); they know him but not the Father; Barclay, Morris, Bruce.
B4 (30-36): hour not come; many believed; go to the Greeks misunderstanding; Maclaren, Alford, Bruce.
B5 (37-39): greatest invitation; water ritual; Isa 12:3; Zech 13:1; 14:8; Morris on "belly"; Spurgeon: drinking = simple faith; Spirit not yet given; Morgan, Tasker, Dods, Alford.
C1 (40-43): division; prophet/Christ/Bethlehem objection; no neutrality (Matt 10:34-36); Clarke, Trench, Tenney, Alford, Boice, Spurgeon.
C2 (44-49): no one laid hands; officers' report; Pharisees scorn the crowd; Bruce cites Hillel; Tenney, Barclay.
C3 (50-52): Nicodemus; mocked as Galilean; Jonah from Gath-hepher (2 Kgs 14:25); Tasker, Alford.
