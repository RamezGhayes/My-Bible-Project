# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), 1 Corinthians 1
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/1-corinthians-1/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1): ancient letter form; Paul's history with Corinth (Acts 18, ~18 months); lost earlier letter (1 Cor 5:9); Chloe's report; apostolic authority asserted; apostolos = sent envoy; Robertson: "called" apostle, equal to the Twelve; Sosthenes maybe scribe (Acts 18:17).
A2 (2): church of God vs worldly city; ekklesia: Bruce (LXX for Israel), Mare (secular assembly, now God's); sanctified/saints by God's promise not performance; Corinth: Fee (like NY/LA/Las Vegas), Morris (alert, prosperous, corrupt), Vincent (diolkos), Barclay (Korinthiazomai); Morgan: church fails by absorbing spirit of age.
A3 (3): Lenski grace source of peace; Morris Kyrios = Yahweh in LXX; Christos = Messiah; Jesus = the Lord saves.
A4 (4-9): thanks despite coming rebuke; rich in speech, knowledge, gifts; real gifts yet carnal; Spurgeon: gifts worthless unless consecrated; God faithful to keep blameless; Jesus named 11 times in 4-9.
B1 (10): appeal as brothers; Calvin: skillful surgeon; unity of mind not uniformity; schismata = tears; Barclay: katartismenoi medical term for setting bone.
B2 (11-13): Chloe (Clarke); factions probably not formal parties; boasting in leader = boasting in self; diversity fine, division sin; Spurgeon: different servants different truths, Christ whole truth; Poole: honor servants, not party heads; Quaker anecdote.
B3 (14-17): Crispus (Acts 18:8), Gaius (Rom 16:23); baptism not basis of salvation; Robertson; Hodge (unbiblical to make necessary, disobedience to neglect); not wisdom of speech; Bruce on Athens speech (Acts 17).
C1 (18): cross shameful execution; preach the cross not morality etc.; continuous tense perishing/being saved.
C2 (19-21): Isa 29:14 (page misprints ref); Isa 55:8-9; Mare on "disputer"; sophia; Einstein anecdote (5% knowledge).
C3 (22-25): Christ vs crucified paradox; Deut 21:23; Cicero on cross; Alexamenos graffito; Fee on Jewish/Greek expectations; Spurgeon: adapting truth kills it; church sign "We preach Christ crucified" overgrown to "We"; Acts 9.
C4 (26-29): not many wise; God breaks pride, not glorifying ignorance; Calvin: levels all; Alford citing Olshausen: emperor lays crown before cross.
C5 (30-31): wisdom, righteousness (imputed), sanctification, redemption (slave market); Jer 9:23-24.
