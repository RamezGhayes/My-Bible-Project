# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (Arabic page), Luke 7
Read 2026-11-26 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/ar/luke-7/ . Our own outline, not his text.
A1 (1-5): after the sermon on the plain (6:20-49), Capernaum (Matt 4:13); unusual care for a slave; sends Jewish elders, feeling unworthy; elders: "worthy", loves nation, built synagogue; Pate: likely a God-fearer, uncircumcised.
A2 (6-8): friends sent near the house; Pate: Mishnah Oholot 18:7 on Gentile dwellings; elders say worthy, he says unworthy; Spurgeon: high esteem from others and low view of himself together; military analogy, a word suffices; Geldenhuys: believed the word works even in Christ's absence.
A3 (9-10): Jesus marvels at faith (also at unbelief, Mark 6:6); "Israel" = covenant people; servant found well.
B1 (11-13): Nain (Pate: Jezreel plain, about six miles SW of Nazareth); widow's loss economic and social; Geldenhuys: hired mourners and musicians; compassion, "do not weep"; Pate: Luke's "the Lord" affirms deity.
B2 (14-17): touched the bier; Rom 4:17; also Jairus's daughter (8:41-56) and Lazarus (John 11); a return to mortal life, not the final resurrection; Pate: Moody anecdote, Jesus broke up every funeral he attended; great prophet.
C1 (18-19): John 1:35-40; John knew (1:29-36) but Jesus didn't fit political deliverer expectation; possible confusion of Messiah and the Prophet (Deut 18:15); Geldenhuys: prison suffering made it puzzling.
C2 (20-23): healings that hour; Isa 35:5-6; 26:19; 61:1; power through humble personal ministry; Morgan: quiet patient faithfulness hardest lesson; skandalizo; Pate: Israel stumbled; Meyer: blessed who does not stumble over him.
C3 (24-28): rhetorical contrasts; more than a prophet; Mal 3:1; John steadfast, disciplined, servant, messenger, greatest of OT; least in kingdom greater (new covenant texts); Spurgeon: least in kingdom on higher ground; Trapp: gospel ministers honored in heaven.
C4 (29-30): people and tax collectors justified God; Pharisees rejected God's purpose; hard toward John, hard toward Jesus.
C5 (31-35): children in marketplace; Clarke: children's game; Maclaren: unwelcome message makes any messenger unwelcome; friend of sinners = loving and seeking them, not approving sin; wisdom justified by her children = fruit (Clarke).
D1 (36-38): reclining; woman likely a prostitute; not Mary Magdalene; distinct from Mary of Bethany (John 12:3); tears, hair, kisses, perfume; Spurgeon: personal wholehearted love; Trapp: notorious sinner; Morris on the jar and reclining.
D2 (39-40): Pharisee doubts Jesus is a prophet; awkward silence; Morrison: when philosophers fail the Lord has a word.
D3 (41-43): 500 and 50 denarii; Trapp: Simon too a debtor; Spurgeon: all owe God, some more.
D4 (44-47): no water, kiss, oil vs her acts; Simon judged her while failing basic courtesy; her love evidence of forgiveness probably granted earlier.
D5 (48-50): guests ask who forgives sins; faith saved her, go in peace; forgiveness received by humble faith-filled love; greater than healing or raising the dead, forgiven forever.
