# Reading notes: David Guzik, Enduring Word (English page), 2 Corinthians 5
Read 2026-11-27 at https://enduringword.com/bible-commentary/2-corinthians-5/ . Our own outline, not his text.
Outline: A. The Christian's destiny (1-11); B. Paul defends and describes his ministry (12-21).
A1 (1): link to 4:17-18; we know by revelation; body a tent, katalyo = striking a tent; resurrection saves the body; Trapp "banquet" of faith, Latimer via Trapp; Spurgeon tent taken down, righteous rise in glory; John 14:2.
A2 (2-4): groaning; earnest desire for heaven, Spurgeon against lukewarmness; not disembodied, clothed; body not evil, incarnation; Greek view of body as prison; 1 Cor 15:54; Spurgeon quoting Bunyan.
A3 (5-8): God prepares us, stonemason image; arrhabon down payment, modern Greek engagement ring; Spurgeon "bud of heaven", make a coat by faith, no long purgatory; Col 3:2; against soul sleep and purgatory; Rev 6:9-11; 1 Thess 4:16; uncertainty about interim body.
A4 (9-10): please God present or absent; opportunities only in this life; Maclaren report to headquarters; bema step/raised platform; Hodge reverence and fear; works and motives not salvation; 1 Cor 3:12-15; 13:1-3; Heb 6:10; Harris reward not destiny; distinct from Rev 20:11-15.
A5 (11): terror of the Lord, delivered from it; message "come to Jesus", not manipulation; persuade; God knows heart.
B1 (12): not boasting, irony; answer to those who glory in appearance; 1 Sam 16:7.
B2 (13-15): beside ourselves; Mark 3:21; John 10:20; Clarke on Festus; Poole; love of Christ = His love for Paul; Spurgeon Jacob and Rachel; one died for all: can save all who come, not universalism, "all" likely the saved given "then all died", Rom 6:1-6; 1 John 2:2; Rev 4:11; Calvin "died for us that we might die to ourselves".
B3 (16): links 4:18; 5:1; 5:7; 5:12; speculation Paul saw Jesus; Morgan; Hughes; disciples weak till Pentecost; John 16:7.
B4 (17): anyone; changed not only forgiven; Spurgeon radical renewal and harder than making a world; Harris; Poole; Clarke not mended but new made; not perfection; Eph 4:22-24; Rev 21:5.
B5 (18-19): all of God; God initiated though innocent; Father laid guilt and wrath on Son; cup Luke 22:39-46; Ps 75:8; Isa 51:17; Jer 25:15; Isa 53:10; Robertson on cry of abandonment, Matt 27:46; Mark 15:34; not imputing because imputed to Jesus; excused sin would make cross unjust.
B6 (20): ambassador; "you" added by translators; we are the party in the wrong; Spurgeon yield to the nailed hands.
B7 (21): sinlessness foreign to Jewish thought, Eccl 8:5; John 8:46; not a sinner but sin, treated as sinner; Spurgeon treated as guilty, vindicated law; Kruse mystery; Trinity cooperating; exchange; Harris share God's righteousness, Phil 3:9; Spurgeon better than Adam's; Poole imputed righteousness.
