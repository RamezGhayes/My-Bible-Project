# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 15 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles and ranges from the titled gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-15-resources ("Acts 15:1-12 Is Salvation by Law or Grace? Part 1" ... "15:25-35 Part 5", "15:36-16:10 Evangelism the Right Way", with gty slugs). Pages show no date; dates omitted. 1750, 1754, 1755 robots-blocked at /library/sermons-library/, read at /resources/sermons/<code>; 1751-1753 read at library URL. 1755 spans 15:36-16:10: registered on ch 15 (36-41) and as "1755@16" (16:1-10). The pages themselves show little or no title; one fetch summary of 1753 gave a different title, not used (preceptaustin title kept).
1750 (1-12): possible titles "The Church in Conflict"/"Law or Grace?" (his words); Judaizers self-appointed, no Jerusalem backing, maybe followed Paul's route; threat of two churches; dissension = back-and-forth debate; Phoenicia/Samaria joy, not legalistic; received = formal welcome; gave God glory; Pharisee believers rigid, tied to temple (his link to AD 70 is opinion); private then public sessions; Peter's four proofs: past revelation (Cornelius, no circumcision), Spirit given = proof (Rom 8:9; Gal 3:14; 4:6; Eph 1:13; 1 Cor 6:19; Acts 19:1-7), hearts purified by faith (Eph 1:7), law a yoke no one bore, testing God (Matt 23:2-4; 11:28-30); v11 grace; v12 miracles confirm only true doctrine (13:38-39; Mark 16:20; Heb 2:3-4); works fruit not cause (Eph 2:8-10); Gal 2:16, 21; 3:11; 5:1, 6; 6:15; adding to grace oldest heresy (2 Pet 2:1); James leading elder.
1751 (13-18): Amos 9:11-12 loose LXX with interpretive comment; Gentiles saved as Gentiles, no proselyte requirement; v16 Israel not set aside, included so Jews wouldn't think Gentiles replaced them; hopos "in order that"; Zech 8:23 Jews as messengers leading Gentiles; sequence: church called out, Christ returns, Israel restored, Gentiles saved (he admits not in commentaries, not dogmatic); v18 plans known from beginning; Israel and church distinct; Isa 11:10; 60:5; 66:23; Zech 8:20-23.
1752 (19-21): second issue: how Christians treat one another; "trouble" = throwing obstacles/rocks in path; four requests: idol meat (1 Cor 8), fornication (moral + tied to pagan worship), strangled, blood (dietary, Jewish separation); for fellowship and witness, not salvation; ceremonial aspects not binding now (Acts 10), idolatry and immorality remain moral; liberty not a platform for offense; weaker brother (Rom 14; 15:1-3; 1 Cor 8); one law: love (Rom 13:8; Gal 5:13-16; 6:2; John 13:34; James 1:25; 2:8, 12); love by walking in Spirit and Word.
1753 (22-24): six reasons for grace (Peter's four, miracles, prophecy); decision pleased all; Spirit doesn't contradict Himself, so unanimity; his elders act only unanimously; envoys confirm letter; "chief men" hegeomai = leaders, plural elders not one ruler (Heb 13:7); "greeting" elsewhere only James 1:1, so James wrote letter; "brethren to brethren" unity; v24 "troubled" stronger, terrify (John 14:1, 27; Luke 24:37); "subverting" = plunder/bankrupt; Gal 1:6-7; 5:10; God loves law, hates legalism; OT obey then blessed, NT blessed so walk worthy; legalism = NT obedience with OT attitude, self-glory; Isa 1:11-18.
1754 (25-35): any addition to grace separates from Christ (Gal 5:2-4); Galatians no thanksgiving (Gal 1:6-9; Phil 1:15-18); heteros vs allos; metatithemi deserter; anathema; tarasso, anaskeuazo; best MSS lack "keep the law" in v24; "seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us" = Spirit-led unity; necessary things light, not new law; "hazarded lives" for the Name = Christ's honor (5:41; Rom 1:5; 3 John 7; Ps 115:1; Matt 10:39); plethos whole church; consolation (paraklesis, 2 Thess 2:16) grace comforts, law brings fear; celebration (Luke 2:10 vs Sinai); confirmation episterizo, prophets teaching, only grace builds (20:32; 2 Pet 3:18); continuation v35; v34 lacking in best MSS; Satan's attack only clarified doctrine.
1755 (15:36-16:10): passion for regions beyond (2 Cor 4-5); priority discipleship, revisit converts, one reproducing disciple; Barnabas imperfect "determined" = kept insisting; apostanta = service deserter not doctrinal apostate; paroxusmos (1 Cor 13:5); departed asunder elsewhere only Rev 6:14; sides with Paul though Scripture doesn't settle it: apostolic authority, church commended Paul and Silas only, Mark maybe better off with Barnabas; later reconciliation (1 Cor 9:6; 2 Tim 4:11); confirming (Acts 18:23). 16:1-3 Timothy: Jewish mother, Greek father, good report in Lystra and Iconium, laying on hands (1 Tim 4:14; 2 Tim 1:6), age ~16-25, converted through Lois and Eunice (2 Tim 1:5) not Paul; circumcised for Jews' sake, expediency, not salvation; Titus not (Gentile, would mean legalism); 4-5 decrees delivered, churches grew; 6-8 closed doors = guidance, keep moving; 9-10 vision, God revealed Macedonia when they could go no further; evangelism: passion, priority, personnel, precautions, presentation, place; Galatia province, Cilician Gates.
