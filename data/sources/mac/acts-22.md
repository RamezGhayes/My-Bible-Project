# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 22 (Grace to You, 1972-73 Acts series)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles and ranges from the titled gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-22-resources (link text + matching gty URL slugs). No dates on the pages; omitted.
1786, Paul's Arrest, Part 3 (22:1-15; slug pauls-arrest-part-3-positive-testimony-in-negative-situation; no title on page). Read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/1786. Page also reviews 21:27-40. Witness in hostile settings (Daniel, Peter, Stephen, Ignatius); ministry costlier with time. Principles: accept as God-ordained, seize opportunity, common ground, tell what God did. 22:1-5 Jewish, Gamaliel, zeal, defends motives, not anti-Jewish. 6-15 companions saw light, heard sound, did not understand; God's sovereignty in conversion; v16 deferred. (Remark that worshipping crowd turned violent left out.)
1787, Paul's Arrest, Part 4 (22:15-30; slug pauls-arrest-part-4-the-attitude-of-paul; no title on page). /resources/sermons/1787 (bare and with slug) and non-www /library robots-blocked; read at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/1787. Reviews 21:27-22:30. v16: rejects baptismal regeneration; "calling on the name" modifies "wash away sins"; Rom 10:9-13; 1 Cor 1:13-17; baptism outward symbol; Eph 2:8-9. v17-21 trance (Acts 10; 2 Cor 12); Paul argued with God, was wrong; circumstances not a guide to God's will. v22-23 Gentile equality; cloaks and dust as preparation for stoning. v24-29 flagellum; no martyr complex; protected the soldiers; warns against masochism/self-pity. Attitude: love for the lost (Rom 9-11).
1788, Paul's Clear Conscience (22:30-23:3; slug pauls-clear-conscience; page heading confirms title). Read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/1788. Registered as 1788 (22:30) and 1788@23 (23:1-3). v30: Lysias frees Paul; council met in Antonia, not Hall of Hewn Stones; fifth time Sanhedrin weighs Christ (Jesus, Peter and John, apostles, Stephen). Rest in mac/acts-23.md.
Theology note added: 22:16 baptism (MacArthur symbol view vs Reformed sign and seal, WCF 28.1, 28.5).
