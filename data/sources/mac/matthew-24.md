# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 24 (Grace to You)
Codes and titles from gty.org sermon pages and search results. Dates for 2372-2374 from Matthew Vol. 17 series page (2374 listed there as 24:43-51 but the sermon expounds 36-51); Vol. 16 series page robots-disallowed, so dates for 2366-2371 omitted.
2366, The Signs of Christ's Coming, Part 1 (24:1-3): Matt 23:38-39 context; Ezek 9:3, 10:4, 11:23; Jewish eschatology from Schurer, 2 Baruch, 4 Ezra, Sibylline Oracles, Philo, Talmud; Zech 14, Isa 9, 11:1-2, Jer 23, 30, Ezek 40-48; Luke 19:11, Acts 1:6; parousia = presence; end of age Matt 13:39-49, 28:20; Josephus, Tacitus; from v4 nothing on Jerusalem's destruction.
2367, The Signs of Christ's Coming, Part 2 (overview of 24, focus 4-14 and deception): Isa 61:1-2, Luke 4:16-21 two advents; futurist indicators v8, 13, 14, 15, 21, 29, 32-34; 1 Thess 5:1-3; Dan 8:23-25, 9:24-27, 11:36-38, 12:1-2; Rev 6, 12:9, 13; 2 Thess 2:3-9; Josephus on Titus.
2368, The Signs of Christ's Coming, Part 3 (24:4-14): six birth pains: deception, dissension (Dan 11:40-45, Zech 14:1-2, Hag 2:22, Rev 6:2-4, 9:13-19, 16:13-16), devastation (Rev 6, 8, 9, 16), desecration (Mark 13:9, Rev 6:9-11, 7:9-14, 13:7), defection (2 Tim 3:1-5, 1 John 2:19, John 8:31, Heb 3:12, Rev 2:10), declaration (Rev 14:6-7); Edersheim on labor pains of Messiah; 2 Pet 3:11. Read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/2368.
2369, The Abomination of Desolation (24:15, with 21): Isa 10:20-22, Jer 30:5-9, Dan 12:1, Zech 13:8-9, 14:2; holy place = temple Acts 21:28; Antiochus type, Maccabees; Dan 9:24-27, 11:31, 12:11-12; Rev 13; 2 Thess 2:4,9; Ezek 38 political application (Lance Lambert, Polishchuk; omitted from voices); rejects AD 70.
2370, Warnings of Coming Peril (24:16-28): near prophecy validates far; Hos 13:16; Sabbath limit; Zech 13:8-9; Rev 12:6,13-14; Dan 7:25, Rev 11:2, 13:5; days shortened maybe literally (Rev 6:12-14, 8:12, 16:10); elect Israel and believers, Rom 9-11; John 10:27; Rev 1:7, 19; 2 Thess 1; four warnings.
2371, The Sign of the Son of Man (24:29-31): page robots-disallowed at the library URL; read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/2371 (full sermon page text). Acts 1:9-11, Titus 2:11-13; Heb 1:3; Luke 21:25-26; Velikovsky; Isa 13:6-13, 34:1-8, Joel 2, Hag 2:6-7, Rev 6:12-13; Chrysostom, Cyril, Origen cross view rejected; sign = Son of Man Himself; Matt 17, 2 Pet 1:16; clouds Dan 7:13, Ps 104:3, Isa 19:1, Matt 26:64; Zech 14:1-7; Zech 12:10, 13:1, Rom 11; angels, trumpet.
2372, Could Jesus Come Today? (24:32-35): manthano; harvest = judgment Matt 3:10-12, 13; Luke 21:31; six views of "this generation"; Judg 9, Hos 9:10, Jer 24:2, Joel 1:6-7; Matt 5:18, John 10:35; pretrib arguments.
2373, Ready or Not, Part 1 (24:36-42): Mark 13:32, Matt 25:13; kenosis restricted use, John 15:15, Matt 28:18, Acts 1:7; Gen 6, 7:11-16, 2 Pet 2:5, 3:3-6, Matt 16:1-3; taken = judgment, left = enter kingdom, Luke 17:34, Matt 25:31-46; Mal 3:16-17; 2 Pet 3:8-13.
2374, Ready or Not, Part 2 (24:36-51): Cullmann already/not yet; Heb 9:28, 1 John 3:2, 2 Cor 5:11; thief, watches, digging through; 2 Pet 3:10, Rev 3:3, 1 Thess 5:2; Luke 12:35-40; stewardship; Rev 3:21; evil servant = unregenerate; dichotomeo, Exod 29:17 LXX; Barclay three apprentice demons "no hurry"; Rom 13.
