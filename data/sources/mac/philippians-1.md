# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Philippians 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles/ranges: gty sermon links with titles at https://www.preceptaustin.org/philippians_1_resources ; 50-7 to 50-10 also confirmed by gty product page https://www.gty.org/products/audio-series/206 ("To Live Is Christ"). No dates shown on any page read, so date "". The gty sermon pages themselves show no title (only what the speaker says in the transcript).
50-1, The Epistle of Joy (1:1-2) [title: Precept Austin + speaker says he entitled it so]: joy vs happiness; theology of joy (Ps 16:11; John 15:11; Gal 5:22; 1 Pet 1:8); doulos (Ex 21:5); saints = set apart; Philippi colony (libertas, immunitas, ius italicum); Barclay "stubbornly Roman"; Paul Rees; Acts 16; dated c. AD 64.
50-2, Elements of Joy, Part 1 (1:3-5) [title Precept Austin only]: five elements recollection, intercession, participation, anticipation, affection; this covers first three; deesis, koinonia (Rom 15:26; 2 Cor 8:4; 9:13); Lydia and jailer; Rees, Kelly, Hendriksen.
50-3, Elements of Joy, Part 2 (1:6) [Precept Austin only]: Ps 42-43 vs Paul; peitho; enarchomai (only of salvation); epiteleo; sovereign salvation, perseverance (John 6:37; 10:28; Rom 8:29-39; Jude 24); day of the Lord vs day of Christ; Meyer canvas; Hendriksen.
50-4, Elements of Joy, Part 3 (1:7-8) [Precept Austin only] (read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/50-4; resources URL robots-blocked): joy of affection; phroneo; dikaios; splanchna; apologia, bebaiosis legal terms; God delights (Luke 15:7, 10; Heb 11:6; Ps 147:11); Lloyd-Jones talk to yourself; joy-stealers list.
50-5, Growth in Godliness, Part 1 (1:9) [Precept Austin "Growth in Godliness 1"; speaker calls subject "Essentials for Growth in Godliness"]: prayer reveals heart; five essentials love, excellence, integrity, good works, glory; this covers love; agape of will; epignosis; aisthesis; Hendriksen; Lynch study; orphans illustration.
50-6, Growth in Godliness, Part 2 (1:10-11) [Precept Austin only]: dokimazo (testing metals/coins); diaphero; eilikrines (sunlight; cracked jar with wax); karpos through Christ (John 15; Eph 2:10; Jas 3:18); doxa; judgment seat (2 Cor 5:9-10); Trueblood "cut-flower Christians"; Susanna Wesley.
50-7, Joy in Spite of Trouble (1:12-14): unit 12-26 joy of ministry, v18 keynote; only sin justifies losing joy; prokope; halusis; praetorian guard; Gen 50:20; 2 Tim 2:9; F.B. Meyer on guards; Bunyan; church in Communist China.
50-8, Joy in Spite of Detractors (1:15-18) (library URL; resources URL blocked): eritheia; thlipsis; motive matters, power in content; distinct from other gospel (Gal 1:6; 2 Cor 11:4); keimai, apologia; God vindicates; Manton, Simeon, Eadie.
50-9, Joy in Spite of Death (1:19-21): five sources of confidence: precepts (Job 13:16 LXX; soteria range of meaning), prayers (Rom 15:30; Eph 6:18-19), provision of Spirit (epichoregia), promise (apokaradokia; not ashamed), plan of God; Christ center and circumference; fill in the blank; Hamlet; Hezzy conversion.
50-10, Joy in Spite of the Flesh (1:22-26) (library URL): synechomai; analuo (camp, ship, prisoner); 2 Cor 5:8; spirit with Christ at once, body sleeps till resurrection; rejects soul sleep and purgatory; Judson in Burma; meno/parameno; prokope.
50-11, Conduct Worthy of the Church (1:27-30) [Precept Austin only] (library URL): "only"; politeuesthe citizenship (Phil 3:20; Heb 12:22-23); four marks: stand firm (stekete), one spirit/mind, striving together (sunathleo; Jude 3), suffering as grace gift; basketball player; Postman.
