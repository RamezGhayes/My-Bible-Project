# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Our own outline, not his text.
41-12 Lord of the Sabbath Part 2 (3:1-6): withered right hand, not urgent, Jesus chose the Sabbath to confront the system; Scripture forbids work (Gen 2:3; Exod 20:8-11; Exod 31) not helping the sick, rabbis allowed aid only near death; called the man forward unasked; question do good or harm, save or kill; Isa 1:11-17; 58:6-13; trap, silence; anger with grief at hardness; the command involved no work; Pharisees with Herodians, enemies united by threat to power; Lord of the Sabbath = deity; road to the cross.
41-13 Mark's Sweeping Summary of Jesus (3:7-12): a still photo gathering the evidence; wise withdrawal, hour not come; crowds from Jewish and Gentile regions, Idumea (Gen 36); boat = ordinary good sense; "afflictions" = scourges, illness seen as God's judgment; healing a creative act (John 1:3; Rom 1:4); demons know and tremble (Jas 2:19); refuses hell's endorsement; old covenant kept God distant (Day of Atonement; 2 Cor 3; Heb 10); Lion and Lamb (Rev 5); only human confession at the cross from a Roman soldier.
41-14 Twelve Ordinary Men (3:13-19), from search-result extracts of the sermon page: the twelve were ordinary, a motley group, perhaps seven fishermen; the question why twelve: the choice parallels his other public acts such as cleansing the temple in Jerusalem, a deliberate act against Israel's leaders.
41-15 Liar, Lunatic, or Lord? (3:20-35): story within a story; C. S. Lewis's trilemma found in the passage: family (lunatic), scribes (liar, from Satan); crowd became an obstacle; "beside himself"; thirty years among them with no sign of madness; brothers did not believe (John 7:5); true family = doing God's will, not less love for Mary; family later believed (Acts 1:14; James and Jude slaves of Christ); Luke 11:27-28; Matt 7:21.
41-16 The Unforgivable Sin (3:20-35): scribes admit supernatural power, insanity cannot explain miracles so they say Satan; Beelzebul from Baal-zebub of Ekron (2 Kgs 1); divided kingdom absurd; binding the strong man: only God is stronger than Satan; blasphemy = knowingly attributing the Spirit's work to Satan; Jesus ministered by the Spirit's power in his incarnation; only with full revelation and final rejection (apostasy; Heb 2:3; 6:4-6; 10:26-29); those afraid they committed it usually have not; Paul a forgiven blasphemer (1 Tim 1:12-13); Matt 12:32; Mic 7:18 who is a pardoning God like you.
