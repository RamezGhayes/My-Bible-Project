# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Galatians 4 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles and ranges from the precept austin list https://www.preceptaustin.org/galatians-4-resources (gty links) and gty series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/48v3/galatians-vol-03 (48-27 date 2018-01-21, range 4:21-5:1). Other dates not shown.
48-19, The Adoption of the Believer (4:1-11 per list; expounds 1-7): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-19. nepios; Jewish, Athenian (Apaturia, ephebos), Roman (Liberalia) coming of age; paidagogos 3:24; stoicheia things in a row; Col 2:8, 20-23; Rom 1; fullness of time (exile, canon, Greek, roads); John 1:1, 14; Luke 1:32, 35; Rom 8:3; active and passive obedience; regeneration and adoption; John 8; John 1:12; 1 John 3:1; Rom 8:17; Eph 1:3-5; Wesley faith of a slave; Luther.
48-21, The Inheritance You Can't Lose (list says 4:7-11; page expounds 4:1-11, registered 1-11): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-21. Roman adoption (adults, witnesses, senate, Julius Caesar, Augustus, Tiberius; four results; cannot be disowned; patria potestas); dia v7; know = covenant intimacy (Gen 4:1; Matt 1:25; Amos 3:2); John 6:44; reverse conversion; Exod 14:10-12; 16:3; 17:3; calendar temporary (Col 2:16-17); 1 John 2:19; Rom 8:14-18, 28-39; anti prosperity gospel; Isa 11:2.
48-22, Paul's Plea: Cling to the Gospel! (4:12-18; page reads through 20, defers 19): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-22. Appeal, remembrance, warning, desire; strong verb beg; Acts 13-14; malaria and eyes (6:11); illness as judgment (Job 4, 8, 11; Acts 28:3-6); despise = regard as nothing; loathe = spit out; blessing = happiness; Deut 32:10; eagerly seek; sarcasm v18; 2 Pet 2:1; Matt 23:37; Hos 11; 1 Thess 2:7, 9-11.
48-24, The Right Motive in Sanctification (4:19-20): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-24. Pastor's labor for sanctification; Col 1:27; Rom 8:10-11, 28-29; 1 John 3:2; definition of sanctification; metamorphoo 2 Cor 3:17-18; Exod 33; anti legalism and antinomianism; Sinclair Ferguson, The Whole Christ; perplexed. (Page's morphoo gloss was garbled by the summary; not used.)
48-27, A Perfect Portrait of Saving Faith, Part 2 (4:21-5:1; here 4:21-31), 2018-01-21: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-27. Illustration, interpretation, exhortation; Ishmael 14 years older; figurative not allegory; sustoichei; Heb 12:18-22; Isa 54:1 exiles; John 3:3; Gen 21:8-9; Pharisees, Acts 15:1-5; no ecumenism; John 8:34, 36; free from condemnation and ceremonial law. Code also used for Galatians 5 (5:1) as 48-27@5.
Not readable (robots): 48-20 The Christian's Assurance of Salvation (4:1-11), 48-23, 48-25, 48-26.
