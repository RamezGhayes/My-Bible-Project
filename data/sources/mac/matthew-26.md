# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 26 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, ranges and dates from Matthew Vol. 17 and Vol. 18 series pages (gty.org/sermons/series/7017, 7018). 2382 The Last Passover, Part 1 (26:17-19, 1984-11-18) could not be fetched (robots-disallowed at every URL form found), so left out; block 17-19 has no mac voice.
2381, Preparing For Christ's Death (26:1-16): fourth passion prediction; God's timing, Matt 2, Luke 4, John 5-11; John 10:18, 19:11; Passover lamb 1 Cor 5:7, 1 Pet 1:19-20, Isa 53; Josephus 256,500 lambs; Luke 22:22, Acts 2:23; Annas temple bazaars; John 12:1-8, Luke 10:39,42; John 12:6; Exod 21:32; Luke 22:6; three responses to cross.
2383, The Last Passover, Part 2 (26:20-30): Thursday/Friday Galilean/Judean reckoning; meal order; Luke 22:24-27; John 13; Ps 55, 41:9, 2 Sam 16 Ahithophel; Acts 2:23; Heb 10; John 13:23-27; Hendriksen "wholesome self-distrust"; Edersheim (left seat honor); Luke 22:19, 1 Cor 11:24; rejects transubstantiation, John 6; Exod 24:8; Heb 9:22; cup of blessing 1 Cor 10:16,21; Hallel 113-118.
2384, Restoring Deserting Disciples (26:31-35): Zech 13:7; Prov 29:25; Matt 28:9-10, Rom 6:4; John 13:36-38, Luke 22:31-34; Prov 3:5; five contrasts; Heb 11:17-19; 2 Cor 12:9; John 21, 15:5; Acts 5:40-41; 2 Tim 1:7-12, Rom 1:16, Mark 8:38, Heb 11:24-26. Read at the library URL.
2385, The Son in Sorrow, Part 1 (26:36-39): read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/2385 (library URL robots-disallowed). John 18:2; three disciples; Ps 42, Isa 53; cup Ps 75:8, Isa 51:17, Jer 49:12; Heb 4:15; Barclay (oasis of love); Edersheim (disarmed death).
2386, The Son in Sorrow, Part 2 (26:40-46): Luke 9:32; Matt 6:13; Rom 7:15-25, Gal 5, 1 Pet 5:8; Luke 22:43-44, Heb 5; Isa 53:3-12; John 12:23-27; Luke 22:53, John 14:30; military "rise let us go"; Spurgeon 1885 sermon; sequences of disaster/victory.
2387, The Traitor's Kiss, Part 1 (26:47-50a): read at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/2387. Crowd, speira; unjust, mindless, cowardly, profane; kiss, kataphileo; Prov 27:6, Ps 2; hetairos vs philos John 15; greed (Gen 25), deceit (Matt 13), hypocrisy.
2388, The Traitor's Kiss, Part 2 (26:50b-56): Peter/Malchus named only in John; healing; fatal (Gen 9:6, Rom 13:4), foolish (2 Kings 19), fulfilment (Ps 41:9, 55, 22, Isa 53, Zech 11); Luke 22:38; 2 Cor 10:4; Crusades; Mark 14:51-52; defective disciples; John 18:4-6, 36; Luke 22:53.
2389, The Illegal, Unjust Trials of Jesus, Part 1 (26:57, 59-61): read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/2389. Illegalities list; Deut 16:18-20, 17:7, 19:16-19, Num 35; John 18:12-24; Mark 14:56-57; John 2:19; 1 Pet 2:23; Joseph Salvador via Simon Greenleaf.
2390, The Illegal, Unjust Trials of Jesus, Part 2 (26:62-68): read at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/2390/. Silence; Lev 5:1, 1 Kings 22:16, Lev 24:16; Maimonides; Mark 14:62, Dan 7:13-14; Lev 21:10; John 10:37-39, 19:7; Mishnah delay; Num 12:14; Luke 22:65; Gen 50; Acts 2.
2391, The Restoration of a Sinning Saint (26:58, 69-75): Zech 13:7; Ps 1:1; John 18:10, 15-16, 26; Luke 22:58,59,61,32; five steps of fall, 1 Cor 10:12; John 21; Exod 34, Mic 7:18, 1 John 1:7,9; 1 Kings 19; Judas remorse.
