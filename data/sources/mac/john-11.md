# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 11 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 5" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V5/john-vol-5).
43-57, A Death for the Glory of God (11:1-16), 2014-09-07: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-57. Seventh and last public sign; J.C. Ryle; purposes: proof of deity, strengthening faith, precipitating the cross; four days vs Jairus' daughter and Nain; Lazarus "whom God helps"; two Bethanys (1:28; 10:40); phileo v3, agapao v5, Jesus' human friendship; three kinds of sickness (natural, discipline 1 Cor 11, judgment Acts 12); not ending in death; delay; twelve hours: God fixes each life's span, so act boldly; sleep as tender metaphor; glad for their faith; Thomas a courageous pessimist (Luke 9:23).
43-58, I Am the Resurrection and the Life, Part 1 (11:17-36), 2014-09-14: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-58. Death not the end (Eccl 8:8; Job 14; Ps 90), all raised (John 5:28-29); four days: decay described, no swoon; mourning customs, large crowd of witnesses; church's character in caring for grieving; Martha (Luke 10): "if you had been here" shows limited faith; v22; last-day hope (Job 19:25-27; Dan 12:2); I am, Jesus is life itself (John 1:1-4; 14:6); "do you believe?"; confession full Christology; Mary echoes; deeply moved range of emotions (Heb 4:15); mourners' sustained wailing vs Jesus' sudden tears; grief over death, sin, unbelief, judgment; apatheia; Isa 53.
43-59, I Am the Resurrection and the Life, Part 2 (11:37-46), 2014-09-21: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-59. Covers vv37-46. Signs prove deity (John 5:36; 10:25, 37); perplexity v37, Father's timing (John 13:7; Job 33:13); v38 strong agitation word; people roll stone, only God raises; Martha's focus on corpse; glory = manifest nature, here as life; prayer of thanks, no petition, aloud for crowd; contrast cross cry; loud cry by name, only Lazarus called; bound; unbind him: believers help; Rev 1:18; vv45-46 two responses.
43-60, I Am the Resurrection and the Life, Part 3 (11:45-57), 2014-09-28: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-60. Three responses: believers (v45; saving vs superficial faith, John 2:23-25; 8:30-31; 1:12), plotters (vv46-53), indifferent (vv55-57). Council did not deny signs; Roman fear a pretext, Jesus never led revolt; Caiaphas self-serving but God overruled his words into a statement of substitution, for the nation and the scattered children of God (Gen 50:20; Prov 16:1; Balaam); plot unanimous; Ephraim about 12 miles north (2 Chr 13); crowd curious but indifferent, Hosanna then "crucify" (Matt 11:20-24; Luke 11:23).
