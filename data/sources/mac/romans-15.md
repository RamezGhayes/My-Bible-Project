# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 15 (Grace to You)
Codes, ranges, dates from https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7350/romans-vol-10 and https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7351/romans-vol-11 . Not read: 45-116 (15:22-24, no fetchable URL found).
45-112, Pleasing One Another for the Sake of Christ (15:1-7): unity of Father, Son, Spirit (Ps 133; Jer 32:38-39; Ezek 37; John 17; Acts 2, 4; Eph 4:3-6); opheilo, bastazo (Gal 6:2); 1 Cor 10:23-24; Absalom 2 Sam 15; Ps 69:9 messianic; John 4:34, 8:29, Luke 22:42; hypomone, paraklesis; 2 Tim 3:16-17; Ps 130:5; deity in v.6 (John 5:17-18); six motives; doctrinal error still corrected.
45-113, Rejoicing with One Another in the Plan of God (15:8-13): v.7 proslambano illustrated (Acts 18:26, Philem 12,17, Luke 15:2, John 6:37); minister = servant sent to Jews (Matt 5:17); Jews praise truth, Gentiles mercy; Ps 18:49/2 Sam 22:50, Deut 32:43, Ps 117:1, Isa 11:1,10; v.13 summary prayer; "abound" overflow.
45-114, In Defense of Boldness, Part 1 (15:14-16): boldness defended; goodness (Gal 5:22), knowledge, noutheteo; against Freud/Rogers/Jung reliance; reminding (2 Pet 1); 2 Cor 5:14, 1 Cor 9:16; leitourgos (Luke 1:21-23); Gentiles the offering sanctified by Spirit; all believers priests, Christ sole mediator (1 Pet 2:5,9; Rev 1:6; 1 Tim 2:5).
45-115, In Defense of Boldness, Part 2 (15:17-21): boast in Christ (1 Cor 1:31; Gal 6:14); five preacher virtues; 2 Cor 12:11-12, Mark 16:20, 2 Tim 4:7; "fully" geographic, Illyricum ~1400 miles; Isa 52:15 LXX, Servant of Isa 52-53; 2 Cor 10:13-16.
45-117, The Ministry in the Will of God, Part 2 (15:25-29): recap hindered (1 Thess 2:18; Jer 10:23; Prov 16:9); priority over dream; Jerusalem poverty (Acts 6, 8, 11-12); Gal 2:7-10; koinonia (2 Cor 8:4, 9:14); John 4:22, Isa 2:3; leitourgeo (2 Cor 9:12-13); sealed fruit; blessing rooted in obedience.
45-118, The Ministry in the Will of God, Part 3 (15:30-33): prayer 8th principle; sunagonizomai (Col 2:1, 4:12, Jas 5:16, Isa 64:7, Gen 32); rhuomai, plots (Acts 9, 20, 21; Agabus); diakonia acceptance; joy not martyrdom (Ignatius); theology weakening prayer is heresy; God of peace (Rom 5:1), titles in ch.15.
