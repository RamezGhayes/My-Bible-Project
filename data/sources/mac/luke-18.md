# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 18 (Grace to You)

42-227, Persistent Prayer for the Lord's Return (18:1-8): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-227. Purpose stated up front; audience disciples, context 17:22-37 second coming; egkakeo lose heart; "eschatological prayer" (21:36; 11:2); judge: entrepomai shameless; adikia; 2 Chr 19:4-7; Amos 5:10-12; Edersheim: "robber judges" wordplay; Talmud judges for a meal; widow powerless (Exod 22:22-24; Deut 24:17); ekdikeson me vindicate; hupopiazo boxing; eis telos; lesser to greater; elect cry day and night (Rev 6:10); makrothumei God holds back wrath (2 Pet 3:9, 15; Rom 2:4); T.W. Manson: king stationed army far off to give rebels time; en tachei suddenly; v8b will He find persistent, longing faith; Maranatha. Rapture and kingdom sequence mentioned.
42-228, Who Can Be Right with God? Part 1 (18:9-14; expounds 9-12): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-228. How can a man be right with God (Job 9; 25:4; Ps 143:2); two religions: human achievement vs divine accomplishment; Gen 15:6; Isa 53:11; v9 broad audience; Luke 16:14-15; Phil 3; Matt 5:20; Luther and Rom 1:17; exoutheneo "count as nothing"; Kenneth Bailey; v10 anabaino, 9am/3pm sacrifices; Pharisee stands near holy place to be seen (Matt 6:5); prays "to himself", five "I"s; harpax, adikos, moichos; Monday/Thursday fasts, only Day of Atonement commanded (Lev 16:31); tithes herbs (Matt 23:23).
42-229, Who Can Be Right with God? Part 2 (18:9-14): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-229. Pharisee's soliloquy asks nothing; Amos 4:4-5; v13 makrothen; eyes not lifted; beating breast rare for men (Luke 23:48); heart source of evil (Mark 7:21; Jer 17:9); "the sinner" (1 Tim 1:15); hilaskomai = be propitiated, only here and Heb 2:17, plea to apply atonement; both believed same God and sacrifices, difference is repentance; Mormon leaders anecdote; v14 dedikaiomenos perfect passive, declared righteous permanently; instant gift, no works; Paul builds on it; exalt/humble about salvation.
42-230, Children and the Kingdom of God, Part 1 (18:15-17): robots-blocked; not read, not registered.
42-231, Children and the Kingdom of God, Part 2 (18:15-17): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-231. Ezek 16:4-6, 20-21 God calls children "Mine"; context 18:9-14, children achieve nothing; brephe infants; disciples rebuke (Mark 10:14 indignant); toiouton "such as these"; children sinful but not yet morally responsible; 1 Cor 7:14 sanctified home; Deut 1:39; Jonah 4:11; 2 Sam 12:23 David "I shall go to him"; 1 Kgs 14:13; R.A. Webb; Calvin, Hodge, Warfield on infants dying saved by grace; v17 receive like a child, sovereign grace; parents teach, model, love (2 Tim 3:15).
42-232, The Impossibility of Salvation, Part 1 (18:18-27; expounds 18-19): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-232. Real encounter; archon perhaps synagogue ruler; young (Matt 19:20), ran and knelt (Mark 10:17); agathos; found no rabbi called "good teacher"; eternal life = quality, knowing God (John 17:3); v19 forces implication: if I am good, obey Me; law must work before grace; ideal seeker who still fails; critique of easy decisions; airplane anecdote.
42-233, The Impossibility of Salvation, Part 2 (18:18-27): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-233. "Do" reveals works system; law a mirror not ladder; Jesus cites second table; "one thing you lack" a test of total submission, selling estate would cut him from family; Phil 3 Paul counted gains loss (skubalon); Matt 13:44-46; Luke 14; Rom 3, 7, 10.
42-234, The Impossibility of Salvation, Part 3 (18:24-30): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-234. Willingness not literal command for all (Luke 14:33); worldly sorrow (2 Cor 7); wealth seen as blessing (Job's friends, Tobit, Sirach, Talmud almsgiving); Rabbi Nachman elephant; rejects kamilos "rope" emendation and "needle gate"; Mark 10:24 all; Matt 7:13-14; Jer 13:23; only God regenerates (John 1:12-13; 3:3-8; 6:44; Eph 2:8-9); Peter: true disciples; Judas excluded; Matt 19:28 regeneration = millennial kingdom (Acts 3:19-21).
42-235, Approaching the Cross with Determined Anticipation, Part 1 (18:31-34): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-235. Cross purposeful; route via Perea, Jericho, ascent; Schurer: no crucified Messiah in Jewish expectation; Mark 10:32 amazed and afraid; written by prophets; sacrifice from Gen 3, 4, 22, Exod 12, Levitical system; earlier hints (5:35; 12:50; 13:33; 17:25); three predictions (9:22, 44; 18:31); Luke adds Gentiles; Ps 22; Isa 53; Zech 12:10; omniscience examples; priestly hierarchy, scribes (Josephus).
42-236, Approaching the Cross with Determined Anticipation, Part 2 (18:31-34): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-236. Five points: plan, predictions, proportions of suffering, power over it, perception. God primary cause (Acts 2:23; 4:27-28); paradidomi; Bultmann answered; sufferings: disloyalty (Ps 41:9), rejection (incl. Father, Ps 22:1), humiliation, injustice (Isa 53:7), injury (scourging, crucifixion); third day (Ps 16:10; John 2:19); v34 said three ways (9:45); no category for crucified Messiah (1 Cor 1:18-23; Deut 21:23); Alexamenos graffito; Luke 24:25-27, 44-46.
42-237, A Blind Beggar Receives Saving Sight (18:35-43): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-237. Last public miracle in Luke before the passion; Jericho city of palms (Josephus: Antony gave it to Cleopatra); approaching vs leaving reconciled (vicinity, or turned back); blindness linked to sin (John 9); two blind men in Matthew, Bartimaeus in Mark; "Son of David" messianic (2 Sam 7:12-14; Luke 1:32); cried louder; Mark 10:50 threw off cloak; King serving outcast; kyrie; v42 sozo, faith saves (5:20; 7:50; 8:48; 17:19); followed = obedience, glorified = worship; crowd's shallow praise; "who are your people?"
Theology notes: none needed in the mac entries (eschatological framework of 42-227 and the millennial reading of Matt 19:28 in 42-234 not used in entries). Guzik B2 entry carries a note on Spurgeon's "blessed, not baptized" remark vs Reformed infant baptism.
