# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 5 (Grace to You)
42-61, Characteristics of Jesus' Divinity, Part 1 (5:1-6): page robots-blocked; not used.
42-62, Characteristics of Jesus' Divinity, Part 2 (5:1-11; 2000-12-17 from series page Luke Vol. 06): five divine attributes: truth (vv1,3 when Jesus speaks God speaks; teaching central, Luke 4:40-41), omniscience (v4 knows the fish; John 1:47-48; 2:24-25; 6:64; 16:30; Matt 24:36 voluntary restriction), omnipotence (vv6-7; Neh 9:6; Ps 62:11; Isa 50:2), holiness (v8 Peter's conviction; Gen 18:27; Job 42:6; Isaiah's vision; Judg 13; Ezek 1; Exod 20:19; Rev 1; kurios as worship), mercy (vv10-11; Exod 33; Jesus moves toward the sinner; zogreo catch alive; fear that clings vs fear that runs, Deut 10:12-13; 13:4). "Followed" technical term for discipleship.
42-63, A Lesson from a Leper (5:12-16; date not shown): full of leprosy = advanced; five marks of approach (desperation, reverence, urgency, humility, faith); Lev 13:46; Josephus: lepers treated as dead; rabbis' distance rules. Touch broke the prohibition (Lev 5:3 cited); creative miracle; Mark 1:41 compassion. v14 Lev 14 eight-day ritual as picture of cleansing from sin; reasons: certification, witness to priests, time from crowds. v15 Mark 1:45. v16 prayer (Heb 4:15; Luke's prayer notes chs 3, 6, 9, 11). Dr Paul Brand on nerve damage. Leprosy as judgment: Uzziah (2 Chr 26), Naaman; Luke 4:27.
42-64, A Saving Faith in a Paralyzed Man (5:17-20; date not shown): forgiveness heart of the gospel; Gen 3 garments, Exod 34:6-7, Neh 9:17, Ps 103:12, Mic 7:18-19, Isa 43:25; 55:7. v17 orchestrated delegation; power of the Lord: Jesus acts through the Spirit (Luke 3:22; 4:1, 14, 18; Acts 10:38). v18 four bearers; stigma (John 9:2-3). v19 outside stairs, tiles (keramos). v20 "their faith"; "Friend, your sins are forgiven" perfect tense = permanent; Luke 18:9-14. Pharisees from "separate", oral tradition (Matt 15; 23), about 6,000.
42-65, Only God Forgives Sin, Part 1 (series page: 5:21-26, 2001-02-18; text read recaps 17-20 and expounds through v23): forgiveness not by proxy, faith and repentance personal; v21 their theology right: only God forgives; blasphemy Lev 24:10-23. v22 epiginosko; only God knows hearts (1 Sam 16:7; 1 Kgs 8:39; Jer 17:10; Ps 139; John 2:25; Luke 2:35). Justification: Rom 4:5; 3:26; 2 Cor 5:21; Prov 17:15; Ps 32 / Rom 4:6-8. Maclaren: pardon flows from God's offended heart. v23 question left for next sermon.
42-66, Only God Forgives Sin, Part 2: robots-blocked, no extracts; not used (so no mac entry for vv25-26).
42-67, Jesus Came to Call and to Save Sinners (5:27-32; date not shown): v27 "noticed" = gazed intently; Levi a small-time collector; v28 decisive break then continuing pattern; v29 evangelistic banquet; v30 self-righteous grumbling; v31 physician analogy, Matt 9:13 / Hos 6:6, Exod 15:26 the Lord who heals; v32 sums up the incarnation. Luke 3:12-13; 18:9-14; 1 Tim 1:15; James 4:6; Isa 6:5. J.C. Ryle: no true Christian goes to heaven alone.
42-68, The Uniqueness of the Gospel (5:33-39; date not shown): v33 Luke: Pharisees ask; Monday/Thursday fasts (Luke 18:12); only commanded fast Day of Atonement (Lev 16:29, 31); voluntary grief fasts (Esth 4; Isa 58; Joel 1:13-14; Dan 10:2-3); Matt 6:16-18. v34 weddings, Megillat Taanit. v35 "taken away" sudden violent seizure; first explicit hint of His death in Luke; Zech 13:7; Isa 53:8. v36 old garment = Jewish religious system, not OT law which the gospel fulfils. v37-38 new wine needs new skins; Gal 5:4; Luke 22:20. v39 settled religion breeds contentment and resistance to the gospel.
