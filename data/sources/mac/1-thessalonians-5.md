# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Thessalonians 5 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles from the titled gty links at https://www.preceptaustin.org/1_thessalonians_5_resources (Precept Austin); gty transcripts confirm "The Day of the Lord" Parts 2 and 3 (52-20, 52-21), "Night People/Day People" (52-22) and "A Call for Discernment, Part 3" (52-34). Other titles rest on Precept Austin only. Dates unknown. Ranges: where the gty page shows a scripture range it is used (52-19, 52-20, 52-21 = 5:1-3; 52-22 = 5:4-11); Precept Austin gives 5:1-2, 5:3, 5:3, 5:4-8a.
52-19, The Day of the Lord, Part 1, https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-19 : chronos/kairos; timing hidden (Matt 24:36; Acts 1:7); every generation expectant; thief (Matt 24:50; Luke 12:39-40); Day = technical term for final judgment on the wicked; near/far prophetic days (Joel, Amos, Zephaniah); rapture (comfort) vs Day (trouble the comfortable); covers "its coming" only.
52-20, The Day of the Lord, Part 2, https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-20 : Felix (Acts 24:25); wrath already taught (1:10; 2:16; 4:6; 2 Thess 1:5-10); rapture signless, Day has precursors: Elijah (Mal 4:5), apostasy and man of sin (2 Thess 2:3-4), valley of decision (Joel 3), cosmic signs (Matt 24:29); rapture before seventieth week, abomination at midpoint, Day toward end; "peace and safety" false prophets (Jer 6:14; 8:11; Ezek 13; Mic 3:5). PRE-TRIB stated. Reformed note added.
52-21, The Day of the Lord, Part 3, https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-21 : three views of history (Berkhof, Marsh, Hoekema, Camus); olethros = ruin not annihilation; ekpheugo; birth pangs = Matt 24 and seals (Rev 6); church raptured before; 2 Pet 3. Dispensational framework; note added.
52-22, Night People/Day People, Part 1, https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-22 : Paul's answers in order (4:13-18; 5:1-3; 5:4-11); mental and moral darkness; "sons of" idiom (Judg 19:22; Acts 4:36); gregoreo; sober; sleep = indifference; Hendriksen; destiny briefly (9-10); believers never in Day of the Lord. Note added on v9.
52-23, Night People/Day People, Part 2 (5:8b-11): NOT USED, robots-disallowed.
52-24, The Shepherd's Responsibility (5:12), 52-25, The Sheep's Responsibility (5:13), 80-270: NOT USED, robots-disallowed. No mac entries for 5:12-13.
52-26, Dealing with Problem People (5:14-15), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-26 : Beecher, Morrison; against church-growth marketing; David and Saul's armour; five kinds: wayward (ataktos; Moffatt "loafers"; noutheteo; A.T. Robertson), worried (oligopsychos; Aristotle; Gandhi), weak (faith/morals; accountability log), wearisome (Matt 18:21-22), wicked (Rom 12:17-21).
52-27, Rejoicing Always (5:16), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-27 : pantote emphasis; joy = fruit of Spirit, "feeling on top of a fact"; ten reasons, ten hindrances; feelings governed by truth; Job, David, Stephen, Paul, Jesus.
52-28, Praying Unceasingly (5:17), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-28 : prayer like breathing; Luke 11 and 18 parables by contrast; Matt 6:7; Gethsemane; Acts; proseuchomai; Coleridge; ten motives.
52-29, Giving Thanks in Everything (5:18), and 80-267: NOT USED, robots-disallowed. No mac entry for 5:18.
52-30, Not Quenching the Holy Spirit (5:19), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-30 : rejects charismatic-abuse reading; quench/grieve/resist/blaspheme; charismatic mysticism, psychology, pragmatism; five works of the Spirit; John Owen; Our Sufficiency in Christ.
52-31, Responding to God's Word (5:20), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-31 : illustrations (oil, Haifa donkeys, blind French girl); propheteia = what God said; new revelation (apostolic era) vs reiteration; prophecy as Scripture (2 Pet 1:19-21; Rev 1:3); character and benefits of Scripture; Luther, Kierkegaard. Cessationist view noted in entry.
52-32, A Call for Discernment, Part 1, and 52-33, Part 2 (5:21-22): NOT USED, robots-disallowed.
52-34, A Call for Discernment, Part 3 (5:21-22), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-34 : Trojan horse; Satan mixes truth and error; "touch not my anointed"; six causes of poor discernment; Screwtape; bin / diakrino; six steps (Prov 2:3; 1 Kgs 3:9; 1 Cor 12:10; Heb 5:14; John 16:13; Acts 17:11); Phil 1:9-11.
52-35, A Prayer for Complete Sanctification (5:23-24), and 52-36, A Plea to Pray for Pastors (5:25-28): NOT USED, robots-disallowed. No mac entries for 5:23-28.
