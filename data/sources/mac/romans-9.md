# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 9 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles, dates from series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7347/romans-vol-07 and preceptaustin romans_9_resources. Other sermon pages robots-blocked.
45-71, Is Israel's Unbelief Inconsistent with God's Plan? Part 1 (9:6-13; Dec 4, 1983): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/45-71. Four units promise/person/prophets/prerequisite; Jewish belief all Israel saved by birth; word = covenants of v4 (Jer 32:42; Isa 55:11); nation elected to privilege, individuals to salvation; remnant Rom 11:4; Heb 11:4; John 1:47; 8:39; Gal 3:6, 9, 29; Rom 2:28-29; Ishmael Gen 21:13, Keturah; "called" sovereign; Isaac pictures redeemed (Gen 18:10, 14; Gal 4); Esau Heb 12:16, Jacob deception; two nations, Edom (Num 20:18; Amos 1:11; Obad 10; 1 Chr 5:1); v11 all chosen before birth, Lamb's book, against man-centered theology; effectual call (1 Cor 1:9; Gal 1:15 etc.); v13 Mal 1:2-3 primarily nations, ~1000 years later, rejects "loved less"; God hates evil Ps 5:5; 11:5; Mal 1:4.
45-72, Is Israel's Unbelief Inconsistent with God's Plan? Part 2 (9:14-18; Dec 11, 1983): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/45-72. "God forbid" strongest negative; Gen 18:25; Ps 119:137; Jer 9:23-24 etc.; Job's wife, Rabbi Kushner; Exod 33:19 golden calf context (Exod 33:13-19); mercy act vs compassion feeling; v16 Ishmael willed, Esau ran (Gen 27), Heb 11:20 by faith; Moses mercy vs Pharaoh justice; v17 Exod 9:16, Amenhotep II, raise up = bring to prominence (Num 24:19; Hab 1:6); Exod 15; Josh 2:9-11; Ps 105, 106, 136; v18 skleruno, Exod 4:21, God hardens ~10 times, Pharaoh himself several; Judas; John 6:37, 44; Josh 11:18-20; Rom 11:7; 1 Thess 5:9.
