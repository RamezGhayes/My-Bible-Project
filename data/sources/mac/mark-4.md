# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 4 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Our own outline, not his text.
41-17 A Survey of the Soils (4:1-20), from search-result extracts of the sermon page: call to listen repeated through Mark 4; really the parable of the soils.
41-18 A Diagnosis of the Soils, Part 1 (4:1-20), from search-result extracts: roadside and stony soils; 30/60/100 points to the future.
41-19 A Diagnosis of the Soils, Part 2 (4:1-20): parables as judgment on fixed unbelief (Isa 6:9-10; Matt 13 adds Ps 78:2); sower = every believer; seed = gospel of Christ (Luke 24; 1 Cor 2:2); roadside: hard hearts, Nazareth (Luke 4); rocky: emotional false converts, Edwards on affections, John 6:61-71; thorny: double-minded (rich young ruler, Judas, Demas), against prosperity gospel, Matt 6:33; good soil prepared by God (Deut 30:6; Ps 51:10; Prov 20:9; new covenant; Heb 10:22); 30/60/100 far above normal yield, not a ranking (Matt 20); every believer bears fruit (John 15; Eph 2:10; 1 Cor 1:26-30).
41-20 How to Listen to the Lord (4:21-34): lamp (Ps 119:105); hidden to be revealed; ears to hear; take care how you hear; measure (Matt 13:12; Luke 8:18); seed growing secretly = God's work not technique; mustard seed with Ezek 17, Dan 4, birds = nations; explained privately; listen obediently, appreciatively, dependently, confidently.
41-21 Calming the Storm (4:35-41): fully God and man; asleep on a cushion; divine appointment toward the Gerasene; sudden squalls; "don't you care"; Ps 10; Ps 44; rebuked the wind, great calm; Creator and Sustainer (John 1; Heb 1; Col 1; 1 Cor 8); Ps 107:23-30; Ps 65; Ps 89; fear of the holy (Luke 5; Gen 18; Isa 6; Rev 1); Matt 14 confession.
