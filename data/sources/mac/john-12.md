# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 12 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 6" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V6/john-vol-6).
43-61, One Savior, Five Reactions (12:1-11), 2014-11-09: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-61. Christ divides (Matt 10:34); chs 12-21 one week; meal at Simon the (ex-)leper's house (Matt 26; Mark 14); five reactions: Martha's service (diakoneo; Luke 10 rebuke about priority; Matt 20:26-28; Luke 22:27), Mary's lavish humble love (nard, hair loosened, burial), Judas' hypocritical self-interest (thief; 300 denarii vs 30 silver; John 6:70; 17:12; Acts 1:25), crowd's hollow curiosity (v9), leaders' hostile plot to kill Lazarus (never denied the miracle). Poor always: priority of worshipping Jesus present. Remnant believes.
43-62, Triumph and Tears (12:12-17), 2014-11-16: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-62. Hour has come, Jesus sets the timing (contrast 6:15; 8:20); two crowds; colt = humility, contrast Rev 19; Zech 9:9; palms (Maccabean welcome; Rev 7:9); Hosanna = save now, Ps 118:25-26, Hallel; Daniel 9 seventy weeks; v16 understood after glorification (Luke 24; Acts 2); fickle crowd (John 19:15); Luke 19:41-44 tears over Jerusalem.
43-63, Transitioning to the Cross (12:17-26), 2014-11-23: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-63. Shallow testimony of crowd (2:24; 6:66); v19 unwitting prophecy that the world will come; Greeks as God-fearers (Acts 8), first sign of Gentile harvest (Hag 2:7; John 10:16; Matt 8:11); hour, Son of Man (Dan 7), glory through death; grain of wheat: no harvest without his death (Luke 24:25-27); vv25-26 turning from self and sin, false converts; serving = following, honour from Father; "if anyone" open invitation; God not finished with Israel (Rom 11).
43-64, The Perplexities of the Cross (12:27-34), 2014-11-30: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-64. Three enigmas: Son's anguish (tarasso; not fear of pain but of wrath and bearing sin, Luke 22:42-44; refuses "save me"; Matt 26:53; voluntary John 10:17); Father's answer (glorified in life and Lazarus, will glorify in death and resurrection; third voice after baptism and transfiguration; for disciples; thunder as God's voice, crowd missed it); cross as accomplishment (v31 world judged, Satan dethroned, Heb 2:14; v32 lifted up = crucifixion, not preachers lifting him; "all" = all peoples, Jews, Gentiles, every nation, the elect through history; no link made to 6:44); v34 Dan 7; Isa 9 vs suffering Messiah (Isa 53; Zech 12:10).
43-65, When God's Patience Runs Out, Part 1 (12:35-43), 2014-12-07: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-65. God slow to anger but patience finite (Noah, 1 Pet 3:20; Rom 2; 2 Chr 36:16); final call vv35-36, walk = believe, sons of light; hiding as judicial act (Luke 21:38); v37 signs never denied (11:47; 15:24); v38 Isa 53:1 as Israel's future confession (Zech 12:10); "would not" then "could not"; Isa 6:9-10 judicial hardening, remnant (Isa 6:13), Pharaoh (Exod 7); God planned it: cross and Gentile mission, Rom 11 (partial hardening, "all Israel will be saved"); v41 Isaiah saw pre-incarnate Christ; 2 Cor 6:2.
43-66, When God's Patience Runs Out, Part 2 (12:37-50), 2014-12-14: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-66. "Day the light went out"; patience examples incl. 40 years before AD 70 and Jewish converts at Pentecost; stubborn will (v37, ongoing; Matt 23:37) and sovereign judicial will (vv38-41); vv42-43 insufficient faith: facts without humble submission (Jas 2:19; Jas 4:6-10); vv44-50: believing Son = believing Father (5:23); light; came to save now, will return as judge; word judges at last day (courtroom image, Rev 20); Father's command is eternal life; John 8:42.
Blocked/untitled: none.
Theology notes: v32 "all" read as all kinds/peoples (Reformed reading); no note needed beyond that.
