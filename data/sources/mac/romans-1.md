# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 1 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, ranges, dates from series pages Romans Vol. 01 and Vol. 02 (gty.org/sermons/series/7341, 7342).
45-01, Romans: The Man and the Message (1:1): Augustine, Luther, Wesley; Paul's background Tarsus, Gamaliel, persecutor (Acts 8:1-3, Phil 3:5); Damascus (Acts 9); apostle to Gentiles (Acts 13:1-3); purposes (Rom 1:11, 15; 15:28-32); 57 OT quotes, mostly Isaiah; quotes Sanday, Godet, Chrysostom, Barnhouse etc.
45-02, The Preacher of the Good News (1:1): robots-blocked; not used.
45-03, The Promise and the Person of the Good News (1:2-4): robots-blocked on several tries; not used.
45-04, The Provision, Proclamation, Privilege, and Purpose of the Good News (1:4b-7): robots-blocked; not used.
45-05, Marks of True Spiritual Service, Part 1 (1:8-10a): thankful spirit, Acts 20:2-3; latreuo; ceaseless prayer 1 Thess 5:17, Eph 6:18; Rom 12:1; willing spirit Isa 6:8.
45-06, Marks of True Spiritual Service, Part 2 (1:10b-12): robots-blocked; not used.
45-07, Marks of True Spiritual Service, Part 3 (1:13-16a): fruitful (Gal 5:22-23; Rom 6:22; 16:5); debtor, "barbarian" from bar-bar; eager (Acts 20:22; 2 Cor 12:15; 1 Cor 9:16-17); bold; Jeffrey Wilson, Basil, Garrison.
45-08, The Gospel of Christ (1:16-17): power, salvation, faith, righteousness; dunamis; Jer 13:23; 1 Cor 1:18, 23; Eph 2:8-9; Haldane on Jew first; Celsus; 2 Cor 5:21; Phil 3:8-9; Hab 2:4; John G. Paton.
45-09, The Wrath of God (1:18): orge settled; apokalypto present; asebeia, adikia; Ps 7:11; Hab 1:13; John 3:36; Eph 2:1-3; Barnhouse dam.
45-10, Reasons for the Wrath of God, Part 1 (1:19-20): gnostos; natural revelation; Ps 19; Ps 94:9; Acts 14:15-17; 17:23-27; John 1:9; Strong, Hodge, Tertullian, Helen Keller, Jastrow.
45-11, Reasons for the Wrath of God, Part 2 (1:21): revelation, rejection, rationalization, religion; glory thread Gen 3:8, Exod 33-34, 1 Kgs 8:10, Ezek 8, John 1:14; Acts 12:21-23; Torrey.
45-12, Reasons for the Wrath of God, Part 3 (1:22): moraino; Col 2:8; Isa 29:14, 1 Cor 1:19; Lloyd-Jones; Dan 4; Josh 7:19.
45-13, Reasons for the Wrath of God, Part 4 (1:23): robots-blocked; not used.
45-14, Abandoned by God, Part 1 (1:24-25): paradidomi (Matt 4:12; 5:25; 18:34); akatharsia; Mark 7:21-23; Jer 17:9; Prov 4:23; Gen 6:3; Ps 81:11-12; Hos 4:17; C.S. Lewis.
45-15, Abandoned by God, Part 2 (1:26-32): robots-blocked; not used.
