# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 10 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Dates mostly not shown on fetched pages (series pages robots-blocked).
41-48, The Truth About Divorce, Part 1 (10:1-9): English pages robots-blocked; read the official Grace to You Spanish transcript at www.gty.org/sermons/print/GAV-41-48/la-verdad-acerca-del-divorcio-1a-parte (title confirmed by search results; date not shown). Mal 2:16 God hates divorce; Mal 2:1-16 priests; Neh 13:23-29; Hos 4:9 like people like priest; end of Galilean ministry, Perea; trap (Mark 3:6; John the Baptist and Herod Mark 6:17-18); Jesus to Scripture not tradition; concession to hard hearts; four reasons: design Gen 1:27, union Gen 2:24 (cleave = glue; kiddushin), one flesh (children), God's work (Gen 1:28; 2:18); Lev 20:10; Matt 5:28; curse Gen 3:16 with Gen 4:7 explains conflict; Hillel trivial grounds.

Perea under Antipas; sklerokardia; Mark omits exception, supplied from Matt 19:9; Matt 5:32; Deut 24:1-4 describes, only command v4; "indecency" not adultery (Deut 23:13-14); adultery death penalty Lev 20; OT divorce for adultery Ezra 10, Isa 50:1, Jer 3:6-8, Matt 1:19; Hosea and Gomer restoration with repentance; remarriage after biblical grounds permitted; Matt 19:10-12 singleness gift; Prov 19:14; Eph 5.

Webb on infant salvation; receive like child; kateulogei.
41-51, The Blasphemy of the Rich Young Ruler (10:17-22; 2010-12-05 shown on page): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-51. Eager, humble but loose "good"; goodness absolute; second table of law; law as tutor (Gal 3:24; Rom 7:7); depth of law Matt 5:20-48; "one thing": exposes idol, first table Exod 20:3, Deut 6:5; Jesus gave no prayer or decision, law before gospel; Rom 3:10-18; John 6:28; 1 John 5:20; Phil 3.
41-52, God's Perspective on Spiritual Wealth (10:23-31): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-52 (date not shown). Psychological reasons secondary (Matt 6:21; 1 Tim 6:10, 17; Luke 12:16-21; 16:19-31); Jewish wealth = blessing, almsgiving atones (Tobit, Sirach 3, Talmud); camel idiom (Talmud elephant), rejects kamilos and Needle Gate; impossibility not difficulty; Jer 13:23; Luke 1:37; regeneration a divine miracle (John 1:12-13; 3:3-7); Luke 18:13; Eph 2:8-9; Jim Elliot quote; hundredfold in church family (Acts 2); age to come millennial (Matt 19:28; Acts 3:21); first/last = equality (Matt 20:1-16).
