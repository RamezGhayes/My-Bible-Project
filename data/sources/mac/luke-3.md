42-35, Setting the Stage for Jesus, Part 1 (3:1; 1999-12-05; library/sermons-library/42-35): seven rulers and priests; political and religious setting matters more than date; Gentile rule, tetrarchs, corrupt priesthood; Pilate inscription at Caesarea (1961); Lysanias inscriptions; Tiberius co-regency from c. AD 11 so John c. AD 26 (Luke 3:23; John 2:20); v2 rhema and prophetic call formula (Gen 15:1; 1 Sam 15:10; 2 Sam 7:4; Jer, Ezek, Hos ...), 400+ years of silence; Luke 1:13-17, 80.
42-36, Setting the Stage for Jesus, Part 2 (3:2-3; 1999-12-12): five Gentile rulers; Annas/Caiaphas appointed under Roman influence, Annas real power (John 18), temple exploitation; wilderness around Jordan apart from establishment (Matt 3:5; Luke 1:80; John 1:28; 3:23; 10:40; 1 Cor 1:26); baptism of repentance for forgiveness as the nation's need; Gal 4; John 8; Matt 22.
42-37, Setting the Stage for Jesus, Part 3 (3:3; 1999-12-26; resources/sermons/42-37): forgiveness, not moral improvement, is the need; Jews cured of idolatry but self-righteous (Matt 5; Luke 18; Rom 2:17-24, 28-29; Matt 23:13, 28); OT forgiveness: Jer 31:31, 34; Ezek 16:60, 63; Mic 7; Ps 32; 51; 103:12; Lev 16:20-22; Isa 55:6-7; Ezek 18:30-32; repentance = radical recognition of sin and helplessness.
42-38, Setting the Stage for Jesus, Part 4 (3:3; 2000-01-02): repentance not a meritorious work, granted by God, inseparable from faith (Acts 5:31; 11:18; 20:21; 26:19-20; 2 Tim 2:25; 1 Thess 1:9); godly vs worldly sorrow (2 Cor 7:9-11); John's baptism modelled on proselyte baptism, did not itself forgive, said Jews were no better than Gentiles (Matt 3:5-6); many baptized later rejected Jesus; Luke 5:32; 13:3, 5; 2 Chr 7:14; Isa 1:16-18; Jonah 3:10.
42-39, A Voice Crying in the Wilderness, Part 1 (3:4-6; date not found): Isaiah 40 context, comfort after judgment, God's purpose for Israel salvation; Isa 40:1-11, 28-31; 41:8-13; 49:14-16; 51:1-4; 54:4-8; 55:6-8; 59; Rom 11.
42-40, A Voice Crying in the Wilderness, Part 2 (3:4-6; date not found): herald preparing road for a king; leveling = deep repentance (hidden sin, pride, deceit, obstacles such as money, lust, unbelief); LXX adds "salvation" where Hebrew has glory; dispensational: literal earthly kingdom promised (2 Sam 7; Ps 2; Isa 2; 11; 35; Jer 23; Ezek 34; Dan 2; Zech 12-14; Rev 20), Israel's rejection postponed not cancelled the kingdom (Matt 11; Rom 11). Neutral note added in the voice entry.
42-41, True Repentance: God's Highway to the Heart, Part 1 (3:7-8; 2000-01-30): John's sermon as sample and model; marks 1-4: reflect on sin (vv4-6), recognize wrath (Mal 3:1-5; 4:1; Isa 2; Zeph 1), reject ritual (Matt 5-7; Phil 3), renounce ancestry (Isa 51:1-2; John 8:37-39; Rom 2; 4; 9; Gal 3; Ezek 18); vipers: Gen 3; Matt 3:7; 12:34; 23:33; John 8:44; Rev 12.
42-42, True Repentance: God's Highway to the Heart, Part 2 (3:8-14; 2000-02-06): fruit (Acts 26:19-20; Isa 1; Ezek 33; 36:25-27); v9 axe already laid, individual and national (AD 70; Zeph 1; 3:8; Rom 2:5-8; Eph 2:10); vv10-11 sharing (Lev 19:18; Isa 58:7-8; Mic 6:8; Jas 1:27; Phil 2:4; 1 John 3:17); vv12-13 Zacchaeus; v14 soldiers probably Jewish under Antipas (revised view); six marks summary.
42-43, True Repentance: God's Highway to the Heart, Part 3 (3:15-17; 2000-02-13; title/range from gty PDF): repentance central (Acts 17:30; Luke 24:47; 2 Pet 3:9); critique of gospel definitions omitting repentance; mark 6 receive true Messiah; expected one (Matt 11:3; Luke 7:20); John 1:6-8; 3:30; Spirit and fire: only God gives the Spirit and judges, deity of Christ; Ezek 36; Acts 4:12.
42-44, True Repentance: God's Highway to the Heart, Part 4 (3:15-17; date not found): winnowing = complete separation, unquenchable fire = eternal punishment (Ps 1:4; Jer 15:7; Job 20:26; Isa 34:8-10; 66:24; 2 Thess 1:7); fire as judgment (Isa 29:6; Zeph 1:18; 3:8; Mal 3:1-2; 4:1; Dan 7:10); shallow repentance (Acts 8 Simon; Rev 3 Sardis; Titus 1:16; Matt 7; 25; 2 Tim 2:19); Joel 2:28-29; Acts 2:38.
42-45, The Legacy of John the Baptist (3:18-20; date not found): preaching, personalizing, persecution; gospel = forgiveness for those who repent and believe; repeated rebuke of Antipas (Idumean); Herodias; Luke places imprisonment out of sequence; Machaerus (Josephus); John 3:22-36; Matt 4:12; 14:1-12; Mark 6:14-29; Luke 9:7-9; 13:31-32.
42-46, The Messiah's Divine Confirmation, Part 1 (3:21-22; date not found): c. AD 26; baptized with the crowds; only Luke notes prayer; "fulfil all righteousness" (Matt 3:13-15), imputation (2 Cor 5:21; Phil 3:9); Spirit's descent = official anointing, not first indwelling (Isa 42:1; 61:1; Luke 4:21; Acts 10:38); Trinity against modalism; John 1:31-33; Isa 64:1.
42-47, The Messiah's Divine Confirmation, Part 2 (3:21-22; date not found): heaven opened, Spirit remains (John 1:32-33); Father's voice (Ps 2:7; John 5:37; 8:18); Spirit without measure (John 3:34); Jesus set aside independent use of deity and worked by the Spirit (Luke 4:1, 14); Matt 12:22-32; Heb 9:14; Rom 1:4; 8:11; Owen, Smeaton cited.
42-48, The Messiah's Royal Lineage (3:23-38; date not found): public genealogical records until AD 70; age thirty (Gen 41:46; 2 Sam 5:4; Num 4; Ezek 1:1); "supposed" son of Joseph; Luke = Mary's line through Heli and Nathan, Matthew = Joseph's legal line through Solomon; Jeconiah curse (Jer 22:24-30) avoided; Zerubbabel/Shealtiel overlap (levirate, adoption; 1 Chr 3:17-19); 77 names; Adam son of God by creation; Gal 3:16.
