1726, The Faith That Does Not Save (8:9-24): wheat and tares (Matt 13:24-43); magos, mageuon; bewitched = astonish/dupe/control; pride; Justin Martyr statue; Gnosticism; kingdom of God broad; name = all he is; believing, obeying, continuing (Hebrews); Simon's motives; John 2:23-25; Judas; three purposes of Peter/John; transitional, rival temples, unity (John 17); Acts 10; 11:15-17; 15; 19; doctrine from Epistles (Rom 8:9; 1 Cor 12:12-13; 6:19-20); commodity, black-arts dealers; simony; four wrong doctrines (self, salvation, Spirit, sin) with Ps 10:4; Prov 16:5, 18; James 4:6, 10; Matt 18:3-4; 2 Cor 5:17; pride examples; Muslim "two Gods"; "to hell with you and your money" idiom; kakia; metanoeo; Naaman/Gehazi (2 Kgs 5); Ott/Trent rejected; Gal 6:3; James 2; v24 fear not repentance; 2 Cor 7:9-10; baptized man who later made pornographic films; son Matt, daughter Marcy; leaders can't uproot tares.
1727, The Faith That Does Save (8:25-40): Acts 1:8 stages; first Gentile convert; Eusebius, Abyssinian church; preparation/presentation/response; angel = messenger; 1 Cor 2:14; 2 Cor 4:3-4; John 14:17; Eph 2:1; John 6:44; Gaza destroyed c. 36 BC, ~60 miles; submissive Philip, ran; 2 Tim 2:20-21; Acts 4:31; Ethiopia = Africa south of Egypt; Candace dynastic title, king divine; Deut 23:1; 1,200 miles; proselyte or God-fearer; 2 Chr 19:3; 30:18-19; Ps 119:2; Hos 6:3; Jer 29:13; John 7:17; Simon contrast; Isaiah 53; Acts 17:11; John 5:39, 46; Littleton and West; guide = John 16:13 word; Ps 25:9; start where person is; 1 Cor 9:22-23; 1 Pet 3:15; JW and John 1:1; Servant = Messiah (not Israel/Isaiah/Jeremiah); John 15:26-27; Trumbull; substitution; taught baptism; faith, confession, rejoicing; baptism not necessary for salvation, public confession; immersion (Presbyterian joke); v37 not in earliest MSS, 2nd-c. addition; catching away = miracle, base for Ethiopia; Azotus to Caesarea via Ekron, Jamnia, Lydda, Joppa, Antipatris; Moody/Sunday/Graham; airplane, Dave Hocking.
