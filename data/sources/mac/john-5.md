# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 5 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26 at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>. The gty series page "John, Vol. 03" (43V3) is robots-blocked, and monergism refused; Precept Austin John pages have no gty links. Dates not found (left ""). Sources for titles/ranges per sermon below.
43-25, The Damning Power of False Religion (5:1-16): page shows no title; title from gty URL slug /sermons/43-25/the-damning-power-of-false-religion (search result) and YouTube "The Damning Power of False Religion (John 5:1-16)"; sermonaudio GTY TV "…Part 2" has Bible text John 5:1-16. Points: feast unidentified (Deut 16:16); Bethesda = house of mercy, Sheep Gate (Neh 3:1); v3b-4 later scribal addition, superstition, Papyrus Ebers; omniscience and compassion; three commands; healing not dependent on faith; Sabbath chosen deliberately (Jer 17:21; Mark 2:27); contempt of leaders; compassion/contempt/complacency; v14 this illness tied to sin, not all (John 9:2-3), warning of eternal judgment; v15 ingratitude; dioko continuous persecution.
43-26, The Most Startling Claim Ever Made, Part 1 (5:17-20): title shown on gty page; range from sermonaudio GTY TV page (Bible text John 5:17-20). Points: God never ceases working; rabbinic Sabbath debates; Gen 2:2; Isa 40:28; Heb 1; equality understood (5:18; 7:1, 19, 25; 8:58); v19 Son does nothing of himself, impeccability; v20 Father's love for the Son basis of redemption, believers a love gift (Col 1:19; 2:3, 9); Einstein illustration.
43-27 (The Most Startling Claim Ever Made, Part 2, 5:21-24 per YouTube title and gty slug): robots-blocked at both /library/ and /sermons/; not read, not registered. So 5:21-24 has no MacArthur entry.
43-28, The Dead Will Hear Christ (5:25-29): title and "John chapter 5 verses 25 to 29" shown on gty page; YouTube title same range. Points: hour now = spiritual resurrection (Eph 2:1; Matt 8:22), effectual hearing; v26 against modalism (Athanasius); Son of Man (Dan 7) judges with human experience and divine knowledge; all raised bodily, hell bodily; sequence: rapture, tribulation, millennium resurrections, Great White Throne (Rev 20); works = evidence not means (Matt 7).
43-29, Testimony to the Deity of Christ, Part 1 (5:30-37): title shown on gty page; range from sermonaudio GTY TV page, which links gty 43-29 and gives Bible text John 5:30-37. Points: 17-47 one discourse; v30 non posse peccare; v31 legal two-witness standard (John 8:12-14); Baptist as luchnos; works (John 10:25, 37; 15:24); voice of Father; v40 unwillingness central; unbelief not from lack of evidence.
43-30, Testimony to the Deity of Christ, Part 2 (5:37-40): title shown on gty page (page oddly also says the transcript is of "The Dead Will Hear Christ", but content is 5:31-47); range from sermonaudio GTY TV "Testimony to the Deity of Christ, Part 2", Bible text John 5:37-40, aired 2014-10-12 (TV date, not used). Points: Scripture the greatest witness; "search" indicative, strong tracking word; perfect "has testified"; Helen Keller image; 2 Tim 3:16; Luke 16:29-31; Luke 24; OT Christ texts; Exod 34:6-7; 1 John 5:9-12.
43-31, Testimony to the Deity of Christ, Part 3 (5:41-47): title shown on gty page; range rests only on the sermon's own content (it expounds 5:41-47); no second source found. Points: glory from one another; phylacteries vs Shema love (Deut 6); v43 false messiahs, Antichrist (Matt 24); salvation all of God, condemnation the sinner's (John 3:19); Moses = whole OT; Luke 16:31; Isa 53:2-3.
