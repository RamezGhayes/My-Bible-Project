# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 23 (Grace to You, 1972-73 Acts series)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles and ranges from the titled gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-22-resources (1788) and https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-23-resources (1789, 1790) (link text + matching gty URL slugs). No dates on pages; omitted.
1788@23, Paul's Clear Conscience (22:30-23:3; page heading confirms). Read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/1788. v1 confidence, "men and brethren" not rulers; conscience teaching: weak, defiled, evil, seared; sincerity does not justify (1 Cor 4:4); purified by Spirit after conversion (Rom 9:1; 2 Cor 1:12). v2 full blow; Ananias corrupt, pro-Roman. v3 whitewashed wall (Ezek 13); human outburst, not Christ's silent submission (1 Pet 2:23), yet Christ's righteous anger; Jewish law protected accused; Ananias later murdered. (Thesis line on "Jewish opposition" left out.)
1789, Confrontation and Consolation (23:4-11; slug confrontation-and-consolation; no title on page). Read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/1789. Outline: confrontation (v1), conflict (2-5), conquest (6-10), consolation (11). v3 angry, sinful outburst; v4 office God-ordained though holder corrupt; v5 public confession praised; reasons for not recognizing (disorder, no robes, eyesight). v9 last phrase doubtful in best manuscripts. v11 consolation, commendation, confidence; grace where failure is; confess immediately.
1790, Providential Protection (23:12-35; slug providential-protection; no title on page). /resources/sermons/1790 robots-blocked; read at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/1790. Ps 56; God not named, Esther; miracle vs providence. Plot (12-15): oath invoking God. Discovery (16-22): nephew; secrecy two reasons. Foiled (23-30): ~470, 9 p.m., providence "first class"; letter self-flattering; innocence under Roman law; parallel Pilate. Farewell (31-35): Antipatris ~35 miles, Cilicia, Herod's palace. God faithful (promise kept in one day) and caring; Phil 4:6; Heb 13:6; Luke 12; 1 Sam 30:6. Left out: remarks attributing hostility to Satan through religious pride and on corruption of Israel's leadership (harsh).
Theology/interpretation note added at 23:10 (Guzik judges the Pharisee tactic unfruitful; MacArthur calls it conquest).
