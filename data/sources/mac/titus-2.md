# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Titus 2 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes from the gty links (title slugs) at https://www.preceptaustin.org/titus_2_resources ; ranges from the link texts there (re-checked 2026-11-27). Precept Austin link texts differ slightly for 56-14/56-15 ("Character of a Healthy Church-Young Woman", 56-14 given as 2:4-5 and 56-15 as 2:3-5) and 56-22 ("The Authority of the Preacher"); titles follow the gty URL slugs. Ranges of 56-14 (2:3-5) and 56-15 (2:4-5) follow the page contents (56-14 opens with the older women's teaching of v3; 56-15 expounds the v4-5 terms), i.e. the Precept Austin ranges swapped. The gty pages show no title or date, and no allowed source gives dates, so dates are empty.
URL forms: several pages are robots-disallowed in one form and readable in another; the url registered is the one actually read. Not read: 90-99, 80-182, 90-350 (other series, listed by Precept Austin).
56-12, Sound Doctrine Backed by Sound Living (2:1): ch2 = character of a healthy church; "sound" (hugiaino, hygiene) 9 times in Pastorals, 5 in Titus; false teachers of 1:10-16; laleo present = keep talking; prepo = fitting; adokimos (rejected stone); doctrine and duty never separated (Rom 12:1; 1 Pet 1:16); Ukraine churches infected by error from 1920s, error a communicable disease; three hina clauses: word not blasphemed (v5; Rom 2:24; Ezek 36; 2 Sam 12), opponent ashamed (v8; 1 Pet 2:12), adorn (v10, kosmeo, cosmetic); Heine "show me your redeemed life"; quality over size; holiness the evangelistic strategy.
56-13, God's Plan for Older Men and Older Women (2:2-3): aging society statistics; Eccl 12 decline imagery; positive aging (Lev 19:32; Job 12:12; Ps 71:17-18; 92:12-15); presbutes (Phlm 9; Luke 1:18); rebuke as a father (1 Tim 5:1); temperate, dignified (semnos), sensible; sound in faith, love (Gal 6:2), perseverance (2 Tim 4:7); older women as mothers (1 Tim 5:2; Phil 4:2-3; Anna Luke 2:36-38); reverent = priest-like (hieroprepes, only here); not diabolos (slanderer, Satan's title); not enslaved to wine; teachers of good, informally, at home (1 Tim 2:12); Wesley at 86; Moses at 80.
56-14, God's Plan for Younger Women, Part 1 (2:3-5): older women teach by mouth and life, non-negotiable list; then long polemic: modern feminism as repackaged Gnosticism (quotes Steinem, Cronin, 1971 declaration, Sanger, MacLaine, Gilder, Peter Jones); Gnostic myths (evil matter, demiurge, Eve as savior, Sophia, androgyny); Eve "original feminist" (Gen 3); Rom 1:26; calls church to wake up.
56-15, God's Plan for Younger Women, Part 2 (2:4-5): kalodidaskalos = lived pattern; sophronizo = train in self-control; philandros (love by will; Eph 5:25; Phil 2); philoteknos, motherhood highest calling (1 Tim 2:15), singleness and barrenness exceptions; sophron; hagnos (1 Pet 3:3; 1 Tim 2:9-10); oikourgos = home her sphere, not prison (Prov 31; Prov 7:11); kind; subject (Eph 5:22; 1 Cor 11:3; Gen 3:16 desire to dominate; Gal 3:28 equality not erasing order); widows 1 Tim 5, rescued exposed infants; Spurgeon's tribute to his wife; Vivian Gornick quote; outside work a decision for wife, husband, Lord.
56-16, God's Plan for Younger Men (2:6-8): exhortation, example, effect; parakaleo; sophroneo (1:8; 2:2, 4, 5, 12); young = under about sixty; "in all things" ends v6; dangers of youth (laziness, early freedom, decadent culture: "familiarity with vice produces attachment", godless education, immaturity); Titus a young man models it; tupos = die, mark (John 20:25; Phil 3:17; 1 Cor 11:1; Matt 23:3); 1 Tim 4:12; kalos; aphthoria = living the teaching without corruption; semnotes; logos = speech (Eph 4:29; Col 4:6), hugies; antikeimenos; phaulos; shame aimed at repentance; "us"; Gordon Dalbey; 1 Cor 16:13.
56-17, God's Plan for Employees (2:9-10): Otis Chandler lunch; personal witness over mass media; three hina clauses; godly character the greatest evangelistic strategy; slavery: text neither endorses revolt nor addresses the institution, but how a servant shows God's power; properly regulated like employer-employee (refers to his Ephesians 6 commentary); Col 3:22-25; Eph 6:5-7; 1 Tim 6:1-2; five qualities: submission (hupotasso, military rank; 1 Pet 2:18), excellence (euarestos, elsewhere of pleasing God), not argumentative (antilego), honesty (nosphizo, Acts 5:2-3), loyalty (pistis, endeiknumi); adorn (kosmeo, jewels); David Koresh as blasphemy of Jesus' name; "not by what we say but by what we are".
56-18, Saving Grace, Part 1 (2:11): fallen "stars" vs true stars (Dan 12:3; Phil 2:15); Titus 2 character not achievement; God as Savior in Titus; four aspects of saving grace, first: deliverance from penalty (Rom 6:23; John 8:24); against losing salvation; grace = unmerited favor; epiphaneia; incarnation (Erich Sauer); Simeon; "for all men" (NASB) not "to all men": not universal reach, gospel offered to all (1 Tim 2:3-6; John 3:16; 2 Pet 3:9); Shedd: atonement offered to man as sinner, not as elect or non-elect; condemnation from unbelief (John 3:18; 16:8-9); atonement sufficient for all, efficient for believers; particular texts (Matt 1:21; John 10; 17; Eph 5:25); harmony left to God.
56-19, Saving Grace, Part 2 (2:11): saving God (Isa 45:21; 43:11); soteria = rescue; grace = Christ incarnate (3:4); faith the sinner's only issue (John 3:14-17; 1:29); 2 Cor 5:19-20; 1 Tim 2:5-6; 1 John 2:2; 1 Tim 4:10 malista; R. L. Dabney: Christ's sacrifice bought temporal reprieve and sincere offer for the race; Day of Atonement and exodus analogies; Rom 2:4; one indivisible expiation sufficient for all, applied to believers; Calvin on 1 John 2:2 "sufficient for all"; "many" (Isa 53:11; Matt 20:28), "sheep" (John 10:11, 15, 26), John 17:9, "us" in Titus 2:14, church (Eph 5:25); mysteries admitted; men perish by unbelief, salvation credited to God.
56-20, Saving Grace, Part 3 (2:11-14): John Brown (Scottish) on atonement: mercy addressed to men as sinners not as elect; four aspects: penalty, power, presence, possession; v12 paideuo (pedagogy), asebeia, worldly lusts, sophronos, dikaios, eusebos, "present age"; Rom 6:14; 1 John 3:6-9; 2 Cor 5:17; "reprograms the computer"; lordship salvation, his books The Gospel According to Jesus and Faith Works; "fire insurance" converts questioned; v13 eager waiting, epiphaneia; deity: one article governs God and Savior, singular pronoun in v14, "great" used of Christ, Father does not come at second coming (Rom 9:5; Heb 1:3); v14 owned by sin before (Rom 6:16-17), possession permanent (John 10:28-29; Rom 8:35-39), losing salvation would mean insufficient price.
56-21, Saving Grace, Part 4 (2:11-14): God a saving God unlike religions of appeasement (Mic 7:18); OT and NT texts on God as Savior; v11 penalty; v12 power, justification and sanctification inseparable (Rom 7); v13 presence removed at glorification (1 John 3:2); v14 gave Himself, substitution (Mark 10:45; Gal 1:4; 1 Tim 2:6), lutroo = release on payment (1 Pet 1:18; Acts 20:28); people for His possession (Deut 26:18; 1 Pet 2:9; Eph 2:10); display His glory (Matt 5:16); self-examination.
56-22, The Preacher's Authority (2:15): could not get past the verse; epitage elsewhere "command"; speak (hearing), exhort (believing), reprove (obeying); periphroneo = think around, evade; Jesus' authority from the Father not tradition (Matt 7:29; John 7:16; 12:48-49); four false sources: personal power (word-faith, commanding demons; Acts 19:13-16), church power (Roman magisterium, Orthodox councils, cults; Mark 7:8: church under the Word), reason (1 Cor 2:14), experience; Packer "the Bible is the real preacher"; James Denney; Barclay; 1 Cor 14:24-25; reasons authoritative preaching is rare; causes of anti-authority culture.
