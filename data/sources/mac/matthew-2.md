# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 2 (Grace to You)
Codes/dates from https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7001/matthew-vol-01 .
2182, Who Were the Wise Men? (2:1), 1978-02-05: magi hereditary priestly tribe of Medes (Herodotus), kingmakers, Esther 1:13, 19; Jer 39:13; Dan 2:10; 4:7, 9; 5:11 Daniel master of magi; Simon Magus Acts 8, Elymas Acts 13; Parthian politics; Gentiles first to recognize the King, John 1:11; Vincent on traditions.
2183, Fools and Wise Men, Part 1 (2:1-3): Bethlehem house of bread, Ephrathah, Rachel, Ruth, David (1 Sam 16-17, 20; 2 Sam 23); timing after purification; star theories rejected, star = glory of the Lord (Luke 2:9; Ex 14:19-20; Matt 24:29-30; Num 24:17; Rev 1:16); kokab; Suetonius, Tacitus, Josephus, Virgil expectation; Gal 4:4; Herod's history and murders; Gaebelein; John 1:11.
2184, Fools and Wise Men, Part 2 (2:4-12): three responses hostility/indifference/worship; Micah 5:2 shepherd-king; proskuneo; priests and scribes structure (Acts 6:7); John 5:39; 7:40-42; gifts gold king, frankincense God (Ex 30; Lev 2), myrrh death (John 19); gifts funded Egypt; worship before service.
2185, The King Fulfills Prophecy, Part 1 (2:13-15): four places Bethlehem, Egypt, Ramah, Nazareth; Micah 5:2 with Micah 2-3 background; dreams; anachoreo, pheugo; Egypt background (Philo); Satan behind Herod, Rev 12; apocryphal stories, Celsus via Origen, Talmud rejected; Hos 11:1 historical, Israel type of Christ, Ex 4:22, Deut 32, Isaiah servant, Jonah/Matt 12:40; Fairbairn on types; Gaebelein.
2186, The King Fulfills Prophecy, Part 2 (2:16-23): two years and under; start of Israel's sorrows; Jer 31:15 in consolation chapters 30-33, v16 return, Jer 33 branch; Ramah border 1 Kings 15:17; Rachel Gen 30:1, tomb near Bethlehem; Josephus Herod's death; Archelaus 3000 killed, eagle incident; Nazareth Luke 2:4; Nazarene: no exact text, netser weak, maybe unrecorded prophecy like Enoch (Jude 14), despised Messiah Ps 22, 69, Isa 49, 53, Dan 9:26; Acts 24:5; Jerome synagogue curse; 1 Pet 1:10-11.
