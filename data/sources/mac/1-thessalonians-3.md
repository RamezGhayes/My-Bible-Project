# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Thessalonians 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles from the titled gty links at https://www.preceptaustin.org/1_thessalonians_3_resources (Precept Austin), confirmed by the transcript wording for 52-11 ("The Pastor's Heart") and 52-12 ("A Pastoral Prayer"). Dates unknown.
52-10, The Pastor's Heart, Part 1 (3:1-4 per Precept Austin): NOT USED, robots-disallowed.
52-11, The Pastor's Heart, Part 2, https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-11 . Precept Austin gives 3:5-10, but the transcript read treats 3:1-10 (with 2:17-20) as one sweep, so registered as 3:1-10. Seven marks: affection (3:1, 5), unselfishness (synergos), compassion (sterizo, parakaleo; Heb 4:15), protectiveness (ho peirazon, kopos; three works of Satan; Russian congregation), delight (euangelizo, four points, steko; Luke 15), gratitude (antapodounai; manure pile; Heb 13:17), intercession (hyperekperissou, katartisai: set bone, mend nets). Charles Jefferson, Jeremy Taylor, Calvin.
52-12, A Pastoral Prayer (3:11-13), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-12 : optative wish-prayers (5:23; 2 Thess 3:5, 16); Spurgeon; autos + singular verb = unity of Father and Son (John 10:30); faith grows with knowledge, not "believe harder" (Mark 9:24); love: truth -> faith -> love; one another / all men; 2 Thess 1:3 as answer; hearts established, blameless (Lev 19:2); Dr. Thomas on love; 1 John 3:3; bema (2 Cor 5); says rapture followed by time of rewards, to be described in ch 4; prefers "all His saints" = believers but allows other reading. Reformed note added on rapture.
