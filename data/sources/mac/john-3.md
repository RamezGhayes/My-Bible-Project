# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from gty series pages "John, Vol. 1" (43V1) and "John, Vol. 2" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V2/john-vol-2).
43-11, Jesus Teaches the Teacher (3:1-3), 2013-01-13: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-11. Elements of salvation; election first, regeneration first in time; birth happens to us; Nicodemus Pharisee, Sanhedrin (John 7); Pharisee minutiae; Phil 3:4-6; Matt 23; context 2:23-25; "by night" no symbolism pressed; hypocrite's fear; "we know" testimony from an opponent but not saving faith; Isa 42:3; Jesus answers the heart; four senses of kingdom; anothen "from above" (3:31; 19:11); John 1:12-13.
43-12, The Blueprint for Being Born Again (3:3), 2013-01-20: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-12. 2:23-25 non-saving belief; anothen; regeneration gives new life, nature, mind; monergistic (Jas 1:18); ordo: election, then regeneration; regeneration, justification etc. one instantaneous work, with faith and repentance present; says faith and repentance do not produce regeneration, regeneration comes first; occurs at hearing the Word (Jas 1:18; 1 Pet 1:23; Rom 10:14-17); no steps to be born again, sinner can ask God for life; helkuo (John 6:44); Lazarus illustration; irresistible grace.
43-13, God's Role in Regeneration (3:1-10), 2013-01-27: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-13. Sinner's worry, Savior's word, Spirit's work; v4 Nicodemus understood and was stunned; v5 rejects "water breaking" and baptism (Christian baptism from Acts 2, Nicodemus could not know it); water and Spirit = new covenant cleansing (Ezek 36:25-27; 37:1-14; 11:19; Jer 31:31-33; Ps 51); v6 sarx, depravity (Gen 6:5; 8:21; Job 14:4; Ps 51:5; Jer 17:9; Rom 3); v7 statement of fact not command; v8 wind invisible, uncontrollable (John 5:21); v10 ignorance inexcusable; later Nicodemus (7:50-51; 19:39).
43-14, The Divine Summons (3:1-10), 2013-02-03: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-14. Mostly a theological appendix: regeneration as God's work alone; Lazarus, believers "a race of Lazaruses"; Rom 8:28-30 golden chain; kaleo, ekklesia; external call (Matt 22:14) vs effectual internal call through the epistles; life activates the will, producing repentance and faith; no one saved against his will (Ps 110:3); Edwards on the will; Cambridge Declaration; Newton.
43-15, Twin Truths: God's Sovereignty and Man's Responsibility (3:11-21), 2013-02-17: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-15. Shift from regeneration to faith (sola fide; Eph 2:8-9; Hab 2:4); plural "you"; Luther: gospel in miniature; "whoever believes"; sovereignty and responsibility as parallel truths not reconciled by us (Matt 11:27-28; John 6:37-44; Acts 2:23; 4:27-28; Rom 9-11). He does not define "world" or address extent of atonement. vv19-21 deferred.
43-16, Simply Believe (3:11-14), 2013-02-24: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-16. Outline: confrontation of unbelief (11-12), commendation of belief (13-18), condemnation (19-21); "we" firsthand knowledge (8:38); unbelief breeds ignorance (1 Cor 2:14); Templeton; no one ascended, against heaven-tour books; Son of Man (Dan 7); bronze serpent (Num 21) an illustration, look and live; lifted up = cross and exaltation (Ps 22; Isa 53).
43-17, Belief, Judgment, and Eternal Life (3:15-21): title and range from series page, but page robots-blocked in all forms tried (/library/sermons-library/43-17, with slug, /resources/sermons/43-17, gty.org/resources/sermons/43-17). Not registered, no entries.
43-18, Humility Is the First Law of Ministry (3:22-30), 2013-03-17: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-18. Judean ministry up to ~6 months (4:35); Jesus' baptism one of repentance; Aenon near Salim, John moves away (humility); v24 chronology vs Synoptics; jealous disciples avoid naming Jesus; v27 ministry a gift (John 15:5; 1 Cor 4:7; 1 Tim 1:12-13); v28; best man (shoshbin); v30 first law of ministry (1 Cor 2:2; 2 Cor 4:5); stars fading at sunrise; Ryle; against priestly hierarchy (1 Tim 2:5).
43-19, Jesus Is Superior to John (3:31-36), 2013-03-24: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-19. Five reasons: heavenly origin (ge vs kosmos), firsthand knowledge (5:30; 8:26; Heb 1:1-2; Mark 13:32 voluntary limitation), confirms God true (1 John 5:10; John 5:23), Spirit without measure (Col 2:9), all things given (John 5:19-23; Eph 1:20-23); v36 belief is a command, unbelief = disobedience; John's death (Matt 14).
Blocked: 43-17 (3:15-21).
Theology notes: none needed; MacArthur's regeneration-before-faith and v5 Ezekiel 36 reading match the Reformed reading; he does not address extent of atonement in 3:16 in the sermons read.
