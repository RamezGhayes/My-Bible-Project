# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 8 (Grace to You)
Codes/titles/ranges from gty pages, series https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7345/romans-vol-05 (45-54 to 45-57 with dates) and the gty sermon links listed at https://www.preceptaustin.org/romans_8_resources (ranges for 45-58 to 45-68). Vol. 06 series page robots-disallowed, so dates for 45-58 onward unknown and omitted.
45-54, Free from Judgment, Part 1 (8:1-2), 1983-03-27: children of wrath; katakrima = penalty executed; Matt 18 debt; Heb 10:14; 1 John 2:1-2; "therefore" = justification chs 1-7; three senses of in Christ (1 Cor 15:22; 12:13; 1 John 1:3); Gal 2:20; I -> us; law of sin and death = indwelling principle (7:21); Spirit of life = Holy Spirit (John 3:5); freedom from guilt/penalty, power an ongoing struggle; Hodge; Luther "blessed exchange".
45-55, Free from Judgment, Part 2 (8:3): not fetched (context budget).
45-56, Free from Judgment, Part 3 (8:3-4), 1983-04-17: reality/reason/route/result; God condemned sin; likeness = similar not exact; for sin = sin offering; John 6:51; Heb 10:1, 4; Isa 53:4-6, 11; v4 = sanctification, moral righteousness, not imputed; Augustine grace given that law be fulfilled; Haldane Father as judge against Son; Chantry; Nommensen and the Batak; peripateo = bent of life; Gal 5:24-25; Titus 2:11-12; 1 Pet 1:15.
45-57, The Spirit Takes Us from Sin to Righteousness (8:1-11), 1983-04-24: gospel the law of the Spirit of life; Edwards; Augustine; Ezek 36:26-27; two categories; kata = nature; phroneo/phronema = bent (Phil 2:5; 3:19); corpse illustration; David Brown; 1 Cor 2:14; flesh remains till glorification; oikei; 2 Cor 13:5; Spirit of God/Christ (Trinity); spirit alive because of Christ's righteousness; bodily resurrection (Rom 1:4).
45-58, The Spirit Empowers Us for Victory (8:12-13): debtors not to flesh (Gal 6:8); mortify by Spirit; dunamis; pleroo = dominated (Eph 5:18; Col 3:16); 2 Cor 10:3-5; Matt 5:29-30; Owen; Baxter.
45-59, The Spirit Confirms Our Adoption (8:14-16): led = evidence; Abba (Mark 14:36); "cry" loud; Roman adoption (patria potestas, mancipatio, vindicatio; four results); Barclay seven witnesses; Mephibosheth (2 Sam 9); Ps 143:10; Isa 30:21; 2 Tim 1:7; Heb 2:15; 1 John 3:18-24; Donne; Billy Bray.
45-60, The Gain of Glory (8:17-18): heirs (Gal 3:26; Matt 25:34; Eph 1:3, 11; John 17:22); ei = since; eiper; pathema (Heb 2:10); logizomai; 2 Cor 4:17; v30 no salvation without glorification; Lloyd-Jones.
45-61, Creation's Groans for Glory (8:19-22): personification (Isa 35:1; 55:12); mataiotes; Gen 3:17-19; Matt 19:28; Acts 3:21; Isa 65:17; 2 Pet 3:10-13; Rev 21:1-4; Lloyd-Jones spring; Haldane leprosy; Jewish apocalyptic (Baruch, Sibylline).
45-62, Believers' Groans for Glory (8:23-25): stenazo; soul redeemed, body not (Rom 7:24; 2 Cor 5:4; Phil 3:20-21); Jesus sighed (Mark 7:34), wept (John 11); Acts 7:34; Ps 38:9; hope (Heb 6:17-19; 1 Thess 5:8; 1 Pet 1:13).
45-63, The Spirit's Groans for Glory (8:26-27): three groans; infirmities = whole fallen condition; intercession = rescue; Spirit Himself groans, not tongues; inter-Trinitarian; 1 Sam 16:7; Heb 4:13; Luke 22:31-32; Heb 7:25; Thomas Watson.
45-64, The Promise of Security, Part 1 (8:28): not fetchable (URL not reachable).
45-65, The Promise of Security, Part 2 (8:28): all things incl. sin overruled; good = glorification/conformity; lovers of God (John 14:21; Ex 20:6; Deut 7:9; Neh 1:5); effectual call (Matt 22:14; Rom 9:11; 2 Tim 1:9; Eph 1:4; Deut 7:6-7; Rom 11:29); "we know" from Scripture; Lloyd-Jones; Richard Wolf.
45-66, The Ultimate Security of Our Salvation (8:29-30): foreknowledge = "a predetermined, foreordained, foreseen love relationship, born in the eternal purpose of God"; Wuest/Granville Sharp (Acts 2:23); proorizo (Eph 1:5, 11); effectual call; glorified same tense; conformed (Phil 3:21; 2 Cor 3:18; 1 Cor 15:49); prototokos preeminence (Col 1:18); Toplady; Lloyd-Jones.
45-67, The Hymn of Security, Part 1 (8:31-34): ei = since; Ps 27:1-3; 46:1-2; Gen 15:1; Gen 22:12, 16; Isa 53:10; charizomai (2 Cor 2:7, 10); accuser (Job 1:9-11; Zech 3:1-2; Rev 12:10); election (John 10:29; Luke 18:7); fourfold protection (Rom 4:25; Ps 110:1; Isa 53:12; Heb 7:24-25); Marcus Loane; Wesley.
45-68, The Hymn of Security, Part 2 (8:35-39): NOT USED, robots-disallowed in every URL form tried (incl. Spanish); no extracts. Hence no mac entries for blocks 35-37 and 38-39.
