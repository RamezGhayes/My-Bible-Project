# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 7 (Grace to You)
Dates from series page www.gty.org/sermons/series/41V3/mark-vol-3.
41-32, Scripture-Twisting Tradition (7:1-13): after peak popularity (Mark 6; John 6) conflict grows; officials from Jerusalem. v1-4 ceremonial not hygienic; "fist" washing; cups and pots: tradition not Scripture; Ezra origin of scribes; Rabbi Jehuda Mishnah ~200 AD; rabbi claims rinsing secures eternal life; Akiba used water for rinsing not drinking; Hillel via Talmud scribal words weightier. v5 charge is breaking tradition not law. v6-8 Isa 29:13 (LXX); lips vs heart; Deut 6:5; Isa 1:10-17; Prov 21:27; John 4 spirit and truth. v9 experts at setting aside God's command. v10-12 Exod 20:12; 21:17; Corban; Num 30:2. v13 tradition invalidates Word. Matt 23; Phil 3; Mark 12:28-34.
41-33, The Inside Story on Defilement (7:14-23): v14 crowd recalled (Matt 15:10). v15 nothing outside defiles. v16 absent from better manuscripts. v17 Matt 15:12-14 Pharisees offended, blind guides. v18 food to stomach not heart. v19 end of dietary laws (Acts 10; 1 Tim 4; Col 2:20-22; Heb 9:10; 6:1-2; 10:22); Levitical defilements were symbols of heart cleansing. v20 Isa 6:5. v21 Jer 17:9; Rom 3:10; Ps 14:3; James 1:14-15; 1 Sam 16:7; Prov 4:23; Matt 23:27. v22 plural acts then singular attitudes; porneia vs moicheia; evil eye. v23 no ritual cleanses; Ezek 36:25-27; John 3; Titus 3:5 new heart. Zimbardo (environmental evil) contrasted; Waller.
41-34, Food from the Master's Table (7:24-30): every URL form robots-blocked; no extracts. Not used.
41-35, To Speak or Not to Speak (7:31-37): v31 trip through Gentile region a months-long private training of Twelve; Matt 15:29-31; Mark 6 green grass vs Mark 8 ground. v32 deaf stigma; touch compassion (Mark 1:41; 5:41); mogilalos only here. v33 aside, sign language: Jesus shows He understands. v34 look to heaven = source; sigh = compassion. v35 orthos: instant language never learned, no therapy. v36 silence: message incomplete without cross (Mark 1:44; 5:43; 8:26; contrast 5:19-20; Luke 9:18-22). v37 huperperissos only here; "all things well" echoes Gen 1 "good", Creator (John 1:1-3). Isa 35 (vv. 1-8): mogilalos in LXX; kingdom where Jew and Gentile healed.
