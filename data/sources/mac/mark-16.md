# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 16 (Grace to You)
41-84, Amazement at the Empty Tomb (16:1-8): title from search result; page (gty.org/resources/sermons/41-84) shows no title/date. Women, spices, stone; guard and seal, bribery (Matt 27:60-66; 28:2-15); one young man vs two angels, Deut 19:15; differences = independent inspiration; predictions Mark 8:31, 9:31, 10:33-34, 14:28; 1 Cor 15:1-20; Mark ends at v8, abrupt but climactic; 9-20 deferred to evening message.
SC12-1, Confidence in God's Word, as It Is Written (16:8-20 argument): fetched at gty.org/sermons/SC12-1/... ; page shows no title or date (speaker says the series ended June 5, 2011 and this is a postscript; a Master's University article says he preached a sermon of this title in 2012). Manuscript transmission (P52, Bodmer, Chester Beatty, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus; counts; patristic quotations); Eusebius, Jerome; Justin, Tatian, Irenaeus (quotes 16:19); internal: transition, Mary Magdalene reintroduced, Galilee vs Jerusalem, ~18 non-Markan words, "Lord Jesus", Peter absent, snakes/poison in no Gospel, summary of Luke 8:1-3, John 20:18, Luke 24, Matt 28:19, Acts 1:8 etc.; v8 intentional ending (amazement theme); rejects lost-ending and other theories; A. T. Robertson 99.9%.
GAP: 41-85 "The Fitting End to Mark's Gospel" (Mark series sermon on the ending, presumably 16:9-20) exists on gty.org but every URL was robots-blocked and search gave no extract text, so it is not registered or used. SC12-1 may be the same message preached at the Shepherds' Conference; coordinator may prefer to verify.
