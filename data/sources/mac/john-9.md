# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 9 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 5" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V5/john-vol-5).
43-49, Blind for the Glory of God (9:1-12), 2014-05-04: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-49. (WebFetch reported the speaker naming the message "Unbelief Investigates a Miracle", which is the series-page title of 43-50; we use the series page.) Darkness, light, sight, darkness; OT healings rare (2 Kgs 5; 20; Num 21), explosion in Jesus' ministry (Acts 2:22; Isa 42:7); creative miracles; no childhood miracles; beggar = spiritual blindness, sovereign grace initiates; rabbinic prenatal sin, Exod 20:5 vs Ezek 18; some sickness from persistent sin but not assumed; "we must work", day = life, night = death (Eph 5:16); spittle reason unknown; obedience perhaps first stirring of regeneration; Siloam, Hezekiah tunnel, John 7:37, Christ the true Siloam; vv8-12.
43-50, When Unbelief Investigates a Miracle (9:13-34), 2014-05-11: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-50. Unbelief inimical, intractable, irrational, insolent; synagogue/church split; Sabbath rules (Maimonides on saliva); two syllogisms; blind deemed cursed; parents lying; excommunication grades (Talmud); Josh 7:19; repeated question admits miracle; sarcasm; Moses; four stages of conflict; only God draws (John 6:44), sinner responsible.
43-51, The Hopelessness of the Stubbornly Blind (9:35-41), 2014-05-18: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-51. Blindness metaphor (Isa 43:8; 2 Cor 4:4); three blindnesses: natural, satanic, judicial (Isa 6; John 12:39-40; Rom 11:8); sight: divine initiative (Luke 19:10), Son of Man (Dan 7:13-14), prepared faith (Lydia), kyrie sir vs Lord, worship as mark, assurance not from a prayer; blindness: judgment as present division (Luke 2:34; John 3:18), stubbornness, rejecting sight, doom.
Blocked/untitled: none.
Theology notes: none needed.
