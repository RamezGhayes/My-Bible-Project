1747 (1-10): qualifications for every job; failed psych screening for Parks and Recreation job; eight qualities shown by action; gifts, boldness, power; Iconium ~100 mi SE of Antioch, Roman colony (as he says), frontier; Lystra ~17-18 mi, colony of Augustus; Paul targeted big cities; region's dangers; v1 houtos "so" = spoke in Spirit's energy; Jews first: love, ready audience, OT; belief alone not proof, perseverance; v2 unbelieving = disobedient; v3 chronos long time (month to three years); parresiazomenos boldly; "therefore" oun his favorite word, opposition led to more ministry; Acts 4:29; 8:3-4; 1 Thess 2:2; Matt 10:23; 12:30; 10:34; v4 apostles plural, Barnabas = messenger sense (Phil 2:25), diakonos analogy; v5 horme rush (19:29), hybrisai abuse, stoning Jewish penalty for blasphemy; v6 flight wisdom not cowardice; v7 preached at once; v8 known town figure, never walked; v9 imperfect heard = kept listening; atenisas fixed gaze; v10 loud voice; miracles apostolic confirming (2 Cor 12:12; Heb 2:3-4); Acts 1:8; Eph 3:19-20; Rom 12:6-8; 1 Cor 12; 1 Pet 4:10; gifts given at salvation, channels not talents; leadership gifts; miracles/healing temporary, Scripture the test; confess sin promptly keeps channel clear; missionary at home first; Acts of Paul description of Paul, uncertain.
1748 (10-21): Galatia region; book The Body of Christ; review gifts (21, 25 preaching; 21-22 teaching; 22 exhortation; 23 administration; miracles v3, healing v10 apostolic); 1 Pet 4:10; Rom 12; boldness v3; power: Eph 6:10; 1 Pet 4:11; Acts 1; Eph 3:16, 20; Acts 8:19-20; 2 Tim 1:7; engine without ignition; remedial Christianity; humility 11-18: Lystra frontier colony; Lycaonian not understood; legend Philemon and Baucis (others destroyed; couple temple guardians, trees); Jupiter, Mercury; Satan tries exaltation after persecution, pride more dangerous; tore clothes vs high priest's false outrage (Matt 26:63-65); John 7:18; like passions = weakness, mortality; mataion vanities = empty, "no things"; euangelizo; natural revelation Rom 1:18-28; Acts 17:30; Rom 2:13-15; providence witness; barely restrained; John the Baptist (John 1:6, 15; 3:29-30; Matt 11:11); humility: credit God for success, own failure; 1 Pet 5; James 4:6, 10; Matt 18:4; Minneapolis music group stepping stone to nightclubs, ended fast; persistence 19-21: Gentiles didn't usually stone, Jewish opponents drove it; fickle crowd; Timothy, Lois, Eunice (16:1); rising a miracle; Derbe ~30 mi, first city without riot; Eph 5:15-16 redeem (middle voice, buy up for own advantage); Col 4:5; Ps 90:12; chronos vs kairos; Melanchthon recorded wasted time; Phil 4:11-13.
1749 (19-28): eight qualities summary; Great Commission; every Christian witnesses (Acts 1:8); Livingstone, Carey, Booth; Chapin; church sleeps, individuals rise; preaching at home vs traveling "sugar stick" messages; Eph 3:16-20; humility: John 7:18; Isa 51:1 hole of the pit; Prov humility before honor; 1 Cor 15:9-10; v19-20 was Paul dead?
