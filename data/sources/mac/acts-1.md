# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 1 (Grace to You)
Codes: 1972 Acts exposition uses 4-digit codes 1700 ff. (not 44-xx). Titles and ranges: gty URL title slugs and link texts on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-1-commentary (re-checked 2026-11-27: 1700 "Acts 1:1-5 ...Unfinished Work--1", 1701 "Acts 1:6-11 ...--2", 1702 "Acts 1:12-26 ...--3", 1236 "Acts 1:1-11 How to Finish Christ's Work"). Range of 1700 kept as 1:1-11 because the speaker on the gty page says "we will consider down through verse 11 as one unit" (he gets as far as v8). gty pages show no title or date; no allowed source gives dates, so dates are empty. Read 2026-11-27.
Note: 44-xx codes exist (44-1, 44-9, 44-10, 44-11, 44-15) but are a later separate Acts series whose gty pages show no title (44-1 is an overview of Acts 1-4, 44-10 covers 2:14-36); not registered.
1700, Resources for Finishing Our Lord's Unfinished Work, Part 1 (1:1-11), (title from preceptaustin slug; no date). Read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/1700. Intro: 60-63 AD, 30 years, 1:8 outline, six panels ending with growth refrains (6:7; 9:31; 12:24; 16:5; 19:20); Luke loyal citizens aim, Sergius Paulus, town clerk, Festus; Theophilus = friend/beloved of God, "most excellent" also of Felix, Festus; second-century tradition: official in Antioch. 1-2: "began", redemption finished (John 17:4; 19:30 tetelestai), evangelism ongoing; content and training (2 Tim 2:15; Heb 5; Hosea); live what you teach (Matt 23:2-3), M'Cheyne, Spurgeon; Jesus ministered by Spirit (Heb 9:14), chose apostles (John 15:16). 3: appeared only to his own, many infallible proofs (1 Cor 15:5-8), John 20-21, 1 Pet 1:8; kingdom (millennial and mystery form). 4-5: promise of Father = Spirit (Luke 24:49; 11:13; Acts 2:33; John 14-16); OT Spirit came and went; with you then in you (John 14:17); baptism of Spirit once at salvation, many fillings; Rom 8:9; car without engine. 8: dunamis, dynamite; filled = controlled (Eph 5:18) = Word dwelling richly (Col 3:16); Eph 3:20; Alka-Seltzer.
1701, Part 2 (1:6-11), (preceptaustin slug and range; no date). Read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/1701. Six provisions: message, manifestation, might, mystery, mission, motive. Stolen body theory refuted; kingdom three senses, mediatorial history (Gen 9:6; Rom 13; Gen 10). Might: dunamis, passive Spirit baptism (1 Cor 12:13; Rom 8:9), yield (Eph 5:17-18). Mystery 6-7: affirms future kingdom for Israel against amillennial view; OT did not reveal church age; Ezek 36; Joel 2; Luke 19:13; Deut 29:29; 1 Thess 5:1-2; date-setter illustration. Mission 8: martyres = witnesses -> martyrs; tell what seen and heard (1 John 1:1; 2 Pet 1:16); courtroom testimony; shepherd council parable. Motive 9-11: glorified body returns same; Father Divine nail prints; rewards (2 Cor 5:9-10; Rev 22:12); goat and carrot; grandfather poem.
1702, Part 3 (1:12-26), (preceptaustin slug and range; no date). www page robots-blocked; read at https://gty.org/library/sermons-library/1702. Proper men; Christ chose Matthias through Peter; Paul apostle of different order; last OT-era act. 12-14: obedience; Mount of Olives height, Bethany side (Luke 24:50); Sabbath journey 2000 cubits from camp arrangement; upper rooms; Judas son of James = Thaddaeus; women named; brothers (John 7:5; 1 Cor 15:7); prayed with Mary not to her (Mark 3:31-35); prayer did not cause Pentecost, promise of Father. 15-20: 120, Spurgeon anecdote; v16 definition of inspiration; Judas never saved (John 6:64, 70); remorse not repentance (Matt 27:3-10); fall after hanging; Aceldama Aramaic; Ps 69:25; 109:8 episkope = overseership; God's plan without causing sin, Caiaphas (John 11). 21-23: qualifications; nothing else known of the two men. 24-26: prayer to Lord knowing hearts; "his own place" = hell chosen; lots not gambling, Prov 16:33, last OT-style act. (Polemical aside linking Marian devotion to Babylonian cults left out of entries.)
1236, How to Finish Christ's Work (1:1-11), date unknown. Title and range only from preceptaustin slug (single source); gty page shows no title. Read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/1236. Same six-point outline; St Paul's Cathedral workmen; tetelestai; Theophilus = friend of God; time limited (1 Pet 1:17; Job 14:14; Ps 89:47; Eph 5:16 redeem = buy up); 1 Cor 2:9-13; Acts 17:11 Bereans; 2 Pet 1:16-20; John 20:19-29 Thomas; passive "shall be baptized"; 1 Cor 12:13; "none of your business" on dates, Mark 13:32-37; martures; motivation from judgment seat (2 Cor 5:9-11; 2 Tim 4:6-8; Col 4:17).
