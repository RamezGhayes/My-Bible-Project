# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Colossians 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles and ranges from the titled gty links at https://preceptaustin.org/colossians_3_resources and colossians_4_resources (MacArthur sections), checked against the gty pages (https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>). Dates not shown, left empty (2145 mentions an election and 1976, not recorded as a date).
2145, Living the Risen Life (3:1-4); title from preceptaustin only (page shows none): to reach the world, first leave it (John 8:23; 17:15; Gal 1:4); inside lives in heaven, outside here; M'Cheyne; heavenly position (Eph 1:3; 2:6; Phil 3:20); four R's: reminder ("if" = since; bios vs zoe), responsibility (zeteo seek continually, phroneo set mind; not escapism, the Lord Himself; Matt 6:33), resource (Christ at right hand), reason (dead to world, life hidden: union, concealment, security; appear with Him in glory); Lightfoot; Ptolemy vs Copernicus; Hudson Taylor at Brighton.
2146, Spiritual Suicide (3:5-9); title from preceptaustin only (page shows none): positional death worked out daily (1 Cor 15:31; Luke 9:23); aorist imperative = decisive; not physical self-mortification (nail belt, Origen); Rom 8:13, deeds of body; Rom 6:11-13 sequence; members = metonym; list moves from act to heart: porneia, uncleanness (Matt 5:28), passion and evil desire (Lightfoot), pleonexia = always more, bowl with a hole; covetousness = self-worship root of all sin (money -> theft, power -> tyranny, person -> sexual sin); priest who never heard it confessed; sex and idolatry together (Num 25; 1 Kgs 14-15; 2 Kgs 23:7); cure = contentment (Phil 4:11); v6 wrath (Rom 1:18), chastening for believers (Heb 12); v7 former life (Eph 2:1-3; 1 Cor 6:9-11); v8 put off dirty clothes, beggar back in rags, white baptismal robes; orge smoldering, thumos blaze like burning straw; slander of image-bearers (Jas 3:9), racial slur in park; sapros (Eph 4:29); lying, Satan father of lies (John 8:44); method: starve sin, crowd it out with the Word (3:16).
2147, Putting on the New Man, Part 1 (3:9-11); title from page alt text and preceptaustin: dress for identity (Downey landowner jailed as vagrant); old man = unregenerate Adamic self, crucified (Rom 6:6); new man created (2 Cor 5:17); not two selves fighting, conflict with the flesh; Lenski: old man cannot be converted, only replaced; Thomas Goodwin: two men, Adam and Christ (Rom 5:12-17); Rom 7:1-4; renewed unto epignosis toward image of Creator (2 Cor 4:16; Rom 8:29; 2 Cor 3:18 implied; 1 John 3:2); Hendriksen: waters deepening; growth by obeying what you know; v11 barriers erased: Jews and Gentile dirt, Scythians (Herodotus, Josephus, Tertullian), Aristotle slave = living tool, Onesimus, Perpetua and Felicitas; "just a Christian".
2148, Putting on the New Man, Part 2 (3:12-17); title on page and preceptaustin: enduo = wrap; splagchna, compassion new vs ancients abandoning sick; kindness (Titus 3:4); humility unknown as virtue to Greeks; meekness = suffer injury rather than inflict (Matt 11:29), not Milquetoast; makrothumia (Barclay; 1 Tim 1:16); chosen, holy, beloved once Israel's titles; forgive as Christ (Eph 4:32; Matt 18:22); love the belt (Rom 13:9-10); brabeuo umpire, peace; enoikeo = be at home; heed, handle, hide, hold forth the Word; parallel Eph 5:18; psalms, hymns, spiritual songs, to the Lord; Calvin on grace in singing; v17 all in His name (Rom 13:14); Chrysostom ark animals; A. J. Gordon autumn leaves; Roman corpse punishment; Guy King "Jesus man"; Jerome K. Jerome.
2149, The New Man Makes a New Home (3:18-4:1); title from preceptaustin only (on the page the speaker sums up the chapter as the new man making a new home); registered as 3:18-25 because a record cannot span two chapters, so 4:1 is not covered by MacArthur: people cannot get along (Waldo Beach: anomie, anonymity, alienation); wives: hypotasso voluntary, distinct from "obey" used for children and slaves, idios = own, not inferiority (Christ submitted to Father), not absolute (Acts 5:29); rejects cultural-conditioning dismissals; husbands: present tense keep loving, pikraino = bitter/exasperate, love as self-sacrifice, Isaac and Rebekah (Gen 24:67), Hendricks "sergeants with biblical clubs"; children: tekna under parental authority, keep obeying, not into sin (Acts 5:29); fathers = parents (Heb 11:23), list of ways parents provoke (overprotection, favoritism, depreciation, no praise, no affection, unmet needs, no standards, harsh criticism (Ginott), neglect (David and Absalom), over-discipline); slaves -> employees: no eyeservice, heartily as to the Lord, inheritance (R. G. Lee "Pay Day Some Day"), no partiality, faith no excuse; masters just and fair; Christian social reform (Wesley, Wilberforce, Elizabeth Fry, Fliedner, Shaftesbury, Carey).
