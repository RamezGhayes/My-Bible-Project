# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 5 (Grace to You)
Series page: https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7344/romans-vol-04 (codes, titles, ranges, dates).
45-40, The Security of Salvation, Part 1 (5:1-2a), 1982-09-19: six links of security; peace objective, God at war with sinners (Rom 1:18; Ps 7:11; Rom 8:7-8); Col 1:20-22; intercession (1 John 1:9; Heb 8:12; Eph 2:14); Hodge "sweet quiet of the soul" = result; prosagoge = harbor; histemi = stand fixed; Ex 19, Nadab/Abihu, Korah, veil Matt 27:51; Heb 4:16; 10:19-22; grace where sin abounds (5:20); Jude 24; Pink; 2 Tim 1:12; Luke 22:31-32; Rom 8:31-39; Heb 10:14; 1 John 2:19; Bonar hymn.
45-41, The Security of Salvation, Part 2 (5:2b-5a), 1982-09-26: cannot lose salvation; Isa 32:17; Gal 5:4 addressed to legalists; hope of glory Rom 8:28-30; kauchaomai; arrabon Eph 1:13-14; glory = full expression of God's character (2 Cor 3:18; Phil 3:20-21; Col 3:4); Heb 3:6, 14 perseverance is evidence; thlipsis (pressing olives), hupomone, dokime; James 1:12; 1 Thess 3:3; Moses' face.
45-42, The Security of Salvation, Part 3 (5:5b-11), 1982-10-03: God's faithfulness (Ps 36:5 etc.); possession of love, ekkechytai lavish (John 7); Rom 8:14-15; 2 Cor 1:22; huper (John 11; Gal 3:13); object-oriented vs divine love; synistemi; Hodge "constancy of God's love"; deliverance 9-10 (Eph 2:3; Heb 7:25; John 14:19); Trapp; joy in God (Gal 5:22); quenching the Spirit dims assurance.
45-43, Adam and the Reign of Death (5:12-14), 1982-10-31: "one" x11, "reign" x5; antithetical analogy; devil sinned first (1 John 3:8); sin = principle; death a penalty (Gen 2:17; Ezek 18:4; Rom 6:23; Heb 9:27); aorist "all sinned" in loins of Adam; "the sin of Adam legally and effectively was our sin"; infants (Ps 51:5; 58:3; Job 14:4); corporate personality, Levi in Abraham (Heb 7:9-10), Achan (Josh 7); Augustine seminal transmission; Adam to Moses no written law yet death reigned; tupos; historical Adam needed.
45-44, Christ and the Reign of Life (5:15-21), 1982-11-07: five contrasts; paraptoma, charisma; Calvin: grace more powerful to save than Adam to ruin; John Murray; reign in life (1 Pet 2:9; John 10:28-29; 2 Tim 1:10); v18 resumes v12, rejects universalism, 1 Cor 15:22-23; v19 constitutional sinner/righteous status, Phil 2:8, Isa 53:11 "many"; law exposes and stimulates sin, F.F. Bruce; huper super-abounds; "through Jesus Christ" vv1,9,10,11,15,17,21; Acts 4:12.
