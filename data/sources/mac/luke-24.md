# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 24 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, verse ranges and dates from the gty series page Luke Vol. 25 (www.gty.org/sermons/series/42v25/luke-vol-25), which lists 42-289 to 42-298; Precept Austin Luke 24 page also links 42-291 and 42-292 with the same titles. Transcript pages show no title (42-291 page showed an unrelated sidebar heading "Losing Your Life to Save it", ignored).
42-291, An Empty Tomb with an Angelic Explanation (24:1-12), 2008-11-09: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-291 (content covers vv1-7). Resurrection foundational (Rom 10:9-10); five points common to the Gospels; first day (Matt 12:40); dawn harmonised by perspective; Mary Magdalene's early visit (John 20:1-2); guard, seal, bribe (Matt 27:62-66; 28:11-15); "Lord Jesus" (Acts 2:36); two angels; mild rebuke; passive "raised"; two proofs: empty tomb and revelation; rejection a matter of hardened hearts.
42-292, Witnessing Women and Doubting Disciples (24:1-12), 2008-11-16: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-292. Resurrection the vindication (1 Cor 15:14-20); empty tomb, angels, women, apostles' unbelief; lēros nonsense; "the eleven" title; grave robbers would not leave linens; Peter thaumazō; John believed (John 20:3-10); feelings, facts, faith (Rom 10:9).
42-293, Christ: The Living Expositor, Part 1 (24:13-32), 2008-11-23: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-293. Need, source, result of understanding; ekratounto divine passive; skuthrōpos; krima legal; political redemption hope; "all" the prophets; Deut 18:18-22; Isa 53; Acts 26; David Gooding quote; Mary mistook him for gardener.
42-294, Christ: The Living Expositor, Part 2 (24:13-32), 2008-11-30: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-294. anoētos; edei; OT survey (Gen 3:15; 4; 6-8; 22; Lev 17:11; Exod 12; 17; Ps 22; 69; Isa 50; 52-53; Zech 9; 11; 12); passives prevented/opened; "urged" strong; burning hearts (Martyn, Brainerd, Wesley); Apollos; Acts 8. (Sermon also narrates v33; entries kept within 13-32.)
42-295, The Living Christ Dispels All Doubt (24:33-43), 2008-12-07: robots-blocked in library, resources, Resources and sermons URL forms; not registered, no voices (vv33-43 have Guzik only).
42-296, The Great Commission: Proclaiming Forgiveness to the World, Part 1 (24:44-49), 2008-12-14: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-296. Luke's Great Commission; biblical foundation (Law, Prophets, Psalms; Acts sermons); historical accomplishment, critique of Barth's Heilsgeschichte; 1 Cor 15:3-8; transformational provision, forgiveness thread in Luke (1:77; 3:3; 5:20; 7:47-48; 19:10; 23:34); church is a mission.
42-297, The Great Commission: Proclaiming Forgiveness to the World, Part 2 (24:44-49), 2008-12-14: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-297. Forgiveness includes regeneration, justification, adoption, sanctification; repentance God-granted (2 Tim 2:25); in his name (Acts 4:12); all nations (Isa 49:6; Mal 1:11; Acts 10; 15); human witnesses, martus; promise of the Father = Spirit, clothed with power; S. D. Gordon climbing illustration about baggage.
42-298, The Significance of the Ascension (24:50-53), 2008-12-21: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-298. Condescension to ascension; blessing a pledge; Bethany on Mount of Olives, Zech 14:4, Acts 1:11; "worshipped" first used by Luke here, affirms deity; joy, temple bookends; ten significances (finished work, glory as God-man, coronation, Spirit John 16:7, place John 14:1-3, evangelism, headship, triumph over Satan, gifts Eph 4:8, intercession Rom 8:34; Heb 10:11-12).
Blocked: 42-295 (24:33-43).
Theology notes: none needed.
