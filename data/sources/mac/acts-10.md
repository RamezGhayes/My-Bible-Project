1734, Part 1 (10:1-20). Acts 1:8; Peter keys (Matt 16); Samaritans despised, bread; tanner (9:43); Gentile dust shaken off (Matt 10:14); Gentile milk, bread, oil, utensils unclean; Gentile mockery of circumcision, Sabbath; Eph 2:11-19; Gal 3:28. Caesarea built by Herod, given by Augustus, garrison, procurators' seat, mostly Gentile, ~30 mi N of Joppa; cohort 600, legion 6000 in ten cohorts; centurion 100; Josephus five cohorts at Caesarea; centurions good men in NT. Three levels: Gentiles, God-fearers, proselytes; God-fearers reached first by Paul. v1 God chooses receiver (John 6:37, 44; Eph 1; Acts 13:48; 15:14; John 10:16; Rom 10:20), election and volition not reconciled. v2 God responds to willing heart: John 7:17; Jer 29:13; Matt 7:7; light -> more light; laos = Jews. v3 ninth hour 3 pm; prayer; great things in prayer. v4 memorial; God sees heart; honest religion still needs truth. v5-6 specific; obedience of faith Rom 1; Rom 10:9-10; why Peter go: Peter's faith, Gentile house. v7-8 immediate obedience; don't demand proof; mou martures (1:8). Summary of five receiver principles. v9 God chooses messenger; sincere bigot; Gen 12; Isa 49:1; Jer 1:5; Luke 1; John 15:16. v10 ekstasis suspension of senses; hunger; Cornelius fasting (10:30). v11-12 sheet tarp; Lev 11 (camel, badger, hare arnebeth, pig, scaleless); Lev 20:25 separation reason; dietary laws mainly separated at meals, hygiene minor. v13-16 "common" = defiled; three times; specific meaning: food laws abolished (Rom 14; Mark 7:14-23; 1 Tim 4:1-4); general: clean/unclean = Jew/Gentile; sheet = church from heaven, mixed, taken back at Rapture. v17-20 perplexity, timing; Kenny Porter policeman tract; Spirit: go doubting nothing; Acts 18:9-11. Cornelius not saved yet (11:14).
1735, Part 2 (10:21-33). Eph 2:11-16 theology of ch 10; temple courts, Court of Gentiles, Trophimus (Acts 21), Heb 10:19-20; mutual hatred (John 18:35; Acts 16:20-21); African communion of former enemy tribes (Ngoni, Nsenga, Tumbuka). Sovereign call review (Isa 65:24; Acts 16:14; Luke 24:45; John 6:45; Eph 2:4-8; 2 Cor 4:4; 1 Cor 12:3). Submissive will: v21 readiness; v22 righteous, God-fearer, well reported by Jews; three classes; v23 exenisen = entertain as guests (Heb 13:2), Gentile soldiers in tanner's house; six brethren (11:12; 10:45), no command, God leads through desires (Ps 37:4; Phil 2:13), his joining Grace Church; v24 relatives, friends, whole household; v25-26 worship refused, "I also am a man"; Acts 14:11-15; contrasts Catholic primacy (Ott), Isa 42:8, Eph 1:22, Col 1:18, 2 Cor 12:11; love for Catholics who know Christ; v27-28 athemitos taboo, OT did not forbid, rabbis added; seven days defilement (rabbinic rationale omitted); John 18:28; v29 without objection, Ps 119:60; v30-33 four days fasting, immediately, "present before God to hear all"; obedience validates faith: John 8:30-31; 14:15, 21, 23; 15:10, 14; 1 John 2:4, 19; James 2:18-20; Matt 7:21; Matt 21:28-32; Luke 9:57-62; bondslave; God leads today through desires and circumstances.
1736, Part 3 (10:34-35). "opened his mouth" colloquial weighty; one Greek word for respecter = rank people; "perceive" linear, still grasping, slow but getting it; shatters prejudice both sides; Deut 10:17; 2 Chr 19:7; Amos 9; Mic 6:8; Rom 2:11; 3:29; 10:12; James 2:1-9 partiality sin, God cannot sin; Israel chosen as witness not favorite; church to love poor and marginal; condescension (Black minister's remark). v35 beyond Peter's grasp (Matt 16:16-17); fear = reverence attitude, work righteousness = right action; James 2. Not saved by deeds; not yet saved (11:14); accepted = God regards one who responds to light, gives more; Rom 2:12-15; Gen 18:25; Matt 8:5-12; Hos 8:13; John 7:17; Rom 1:18; 3. No one in heaven apart from faith in Christ; God reveals atonement to one living up to light "whether by name or not", he does not know how. Six-point frame: sovereign call, submissive will, simple presentation, spiritual power, symbolic confession, sweet fellowship. Prodigal: reconciliation to Father first; Ps 119 spirit of obedience; birth analogy.
1737, Part 4 (10:36-43). Resurrection greatest event (Mark 10:45; John 2:19; 11:25; 14:19); Easter way of life; same message to all groups. v36 word = gospel, peace with God from war (2 Cor 5:18-19; Rom 5:1), no salvation outside Christ; Lord of all = God in flesh (Ex 20:3; Phil 2:9-11). v37 Judea from Galilee after John. v38 anointed = set apart as Messiah (Luke 4:18; Isa 61:1; Matt 3:17); went about doing good; sickness ultimately tied to curse via sin (Job 1-2; 2 Cor 12:7); "oppressed" = tyrannized, elsewhere only James 2; Nicodemus (John 3:2). v39 eyewitnesses; irony doing good yet slain; Roman soldiers, Jewish leaders' trial; Hosanna = save now, political (John 12:12-17); John 12:24. v40 God reversed verdict; deniers of bodily resurrection; 1 Cor 15:14-19. v41 ate and drank = physical body; died to pay, rose to give life; Matt 27:62-66 sealed tomb. (Easter-custom etymology omitted.) v42 Judge of living and dead (2 Tim 4:1): "if not Deliverer, Judge". v43 prophets; whosoever believes, remission, no ethnic/social barrier (Rom 10:9; John 3:16); shipwreck father illustration.
1738, Part 5 (10:44-48). Tom Sawyer/Huck gang; Palm Springs club; circumcision party (must pass through Judaism); Jesus smashing fences; Matt 28:19; Gal 3:26-28; Eph 2-3 one new man; Gen 12 blessing. Framework recap; contrast Acts 2 and 4: results depend on call and will. v44 Spirit fell while speaking; saved at belief though "believed" not stated; 11:14-15; not delayed; Gentile Pentecost sets pattern. Spirit at salvation: Ezek 36:26-27; Zech 4:6; John 14:17; 7:37-39; Acts 1:8; John 16:7; Rom 8:9; 1 Cor 12:3; 6:19; 2 Cor 6:16; Jude 19; Eph 2:22; Acts 11:17 gift not wage. Functions: witness, prayer (Rom 8:26-27), seal/security (Eph 1:13; Dan 6:17; Matt 27:66), authenticity (1 Kings 21; Rom 8:14-16), arrabon down payment/engagement (Eph 1:14), teaching (John 16:13; 1 John 2:27). Samaria delay (8:15-17) for apostles to witness equality; not normal. v45 circumcision astonished. v46 tongues sign for Jews; three times in Acts, always groups; never single seeking individual. v47-48 forbid water; baptism public symbol of death, burial, resurrection, promptly after salvation, unbaptized believer disobedient; six brothers likely baptized (corroboration). Stay days: fellowship, newborn; Lydia (16:14-15). Household believed and baptized. Heb 4:2 mixed with faith; 2:41; 13:48. Henry George "for men simply as men"; friend of sinners; assurance by indwelling Spirit.
