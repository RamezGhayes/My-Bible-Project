# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 13 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Series pages robots-blocked; dates mostly not shown.
42-180, Supernatural Lessons from a Natural Disaster (13:1-5): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-180. Third interruption of discourse (12:13, 41); Passover setting; Pilate prefect c.26-36 cruel; maybe grabbed altar horns like Adonijah; dokeo; Job's friends (Job 4:7; 22:4-5); John 9; Herod Acts 12:21-23 individual judgment; Siloam tower by Pilate's aqueduct; apollumi eternal; repentance = change mind about sin + embrace Christ (Luke 24:46-47; Acts 11:18; Rom 2:4; 2 Pet 3:9); Spurgeon 1861 sermon after train and tunnel disasters; Larry King after 9/11; Russian believers "I repented".
42-181, Living on Borrowed Time (13:6-9): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-181. Why does God let us live (Rom 6:23; Ezek 18:4; Ps 51:5); patience as common grace (Exod 34:6-7; Num 14:18; Ps 86:15; 103:8); Jonah 4:1-2; Rom 2:4-5; 1 Tim 1:16; Gen 6:3 with 1 Pet 3:20; Hos 4:17; 9:12; 2 Pet 3:9, 15; fig tree blessing (Deut 8:8; Mic 4:4; Zech 3:10); skapso, kopria; v9 conditionals; Luke 3:9; Israel primary (Rom 9:4-5; Luke 19:41-44; 20:9-16; 21:20-24; Matt 21:18-19); Isa 55:6-7; Ps 32:6; John 8:21, 24.
42-182, Christ Creates Conflict in the Synagogue (13:10-17): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-182. Three conflicts: error (v10; Luke 4:43; John 8:44), Satan (11-13; astheneia, no faith mentioned, divine passive perfect "released", Acts 10:38, John 9), hypocrites (14-17; archisynagogos, aganakteo; Mishnah allowed mercy and watering animals; Matt 12; Deut 5:13-15; Matt 23:4; lesser to greater; Luke 19 son of Abraham; kataischuno; fickle crowd). Woman pictures sinner freed by sovereign Lord. Mentions Luther and indulgences.
42-183, The Increasing Influence of the Kingdom (13:18-21): read at www.gty.org/sermons/42-183/... Mustard = external growth (good); birds = nations benefiting without sharing life (Dan 4:10-14; Ezek 31:2-6; 17:22-23; John 15); OT kingdom texts Ps 2; 72; Isa 54; Mic 4:1-4; 5:2-4; Matt 13:32 lachanon; leaven = internal positive influence, explicitly rejects "leaven always evil" (contrast Luke 12:1; 1 Cor 5; Gal 5:9); about 50 lb flour for ~100 people; not by politics; Matt 16:18.
42-184, Are Just a Few Being Saved? Part 1 (13:22-24): read at www.gty.org/sermons/42-184/... Journey 9:51-19:27; honest questioner (Luke 12:8-9); entering = saved; Finney's anxious bench; Jewish expectation (Isa 45:17; 56:6-7; 60:21), rabbinic exclusions; remnant (1 Pet 3:20; Isa 55:6); four words exertion/desperation/relation/perception; agonizomai (1 Tim 6:12; 2 Tim 4:7; 1 Cor 9:25-27); Matt 7:13-14; Luke 9:23-26, 57-62; Luke 3:4-6 with Isa 40:3-5; Phil 2:12-13; Jas 4:6; A. W. Pink 1937 quote.
42-185, Are Just a Few Being Saved? Part 2 (13:22-30; April 10, 2005 shown): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-185. Desperation: door shut (Gen 6:3, ark; Luke 12:16-21; Matt 25:1-13; Rom 1:24-28; 2 Cor 6:2); relation: "I do not know you" (Matt 7:21-23; Gal 2:20; Phil 1:21); v27 from Ps 6:8; perception: weeping and gnashing (Matt 8:11-12 etc.); Ps 107:2-3 four directions; Rom 1:16; Gal 3:28.
42-186, Who Really Killed Jesus? (13:31-33): read at www.gty.org/sermons/42-186/... Pharisees not kind, aimed to push Him to Judea; Perea; alopex contempt, petty not lion; Exod 22:28 and Acts 23 yet prophets rebuked rulers; third day idiom for completion; John 10:17-18; Herod Antipas history; Luke 23:8-11; prophets killed in Jerusalem (2 Kgs 21; 2 Chr 24:20-21; Jer 26:20-23; Heb 11:37); human guilt plus God's plan (Acts 2:23; 4:27-28; Isa 53:10; 1 Cor 2:8); condemns anti-Semitism.
42-189, Divine Compassion for Those Deserving Condemnation, Part 3 (13:34-35): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-189. Compassion, condemnation, "until" as hope; God's faithfulness central (1 Cor 10:13; Titus 1:2; Heb 10:23; Josh 21:43-45); Abrahamic covenant unilateral (Gen 15; Heb 6:13, 17); 2 Sam 7; John 1:11; Rom 11:1-5, 25-29; rejects replacement theology; 1 Sam 12:22; Ps 89:30-37; Jer 30:10-11; Ezek 16.
42-190, Divine Compassion for Those Deserving Condemnation, Part 4 (13:34-35): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-190. Five-part study of these verses; past (Deut 7:6-9; 4:37; 9:4-5), present desolation since rejection, future "until" (Ps 118:26; Isa 43:1, 11; Matt 23:37-39); Gentiles grafted in should be humble.
Not read: 42-187, 42-188 (pages robots-blocked), 42-191.
