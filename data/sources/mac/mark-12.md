# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 12 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles from gty.org search results; dates not shown on fetched transcript pages.
41-60, The Rejected Cornerstone (12:1-12): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-60. Wednesday of Passion Week; Jesus foresaw death (Mark 8:31; 9:31; 10:32-34, 45); owner God, vineyard Israel, tenants religious leaders (Isa 5:1-2, 7); servants prophets: Jeremiah, Zechariah (2 Chr 24), Micaiah (1 Kgs 22), John the Baptist; Justin Martyr: Isaiah sawn (Heb 11:37); beloved son = Christ (baptism voice); cast out unburied (John 1:11); judgment AD 70, stewardship to apostles (Matt 21:41, 43); Ps 118:22-23 implies resurrection (Acts 4:11; 1 Pet 2:6-8; Rom 9:32-33; Eph 2:20); Luke 20:16, 18.
41-61, The Pathology of a Religious Hypocrite (12:13-17): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-61. Hunting verb "trap" unique in NT; Pharisees + Herodians unlikely alliance; aim: Roman arrest; flattery to force answer; kensos poll tax denarius; Judas of Galilee AD 6 (Acts 5:37); AD 66 revolt; Jesus knows hearts (John 2:25); denarius idolatrous (Exod 20), Tiberius son of divine Augustus, Pontifex Maximus; government God-ordained common grace (Rom 13:1-7; 1 Tim 2:1-2; 1 Pet 2:13-17; Dan 4); obey God rather than men (Acts 5:29); no sacral society until millennial kingdom, rejects postmillennialism; image of God on man; Luke 23:1-2; Matt 12:30.
41-62, Biblical Ignorance in High Places (12:18-27): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-62. Sadducees dominated Sanhedrin/temple; "polished scoffing"; levirate Deut 25:5-10, Gen 38, Ruth; planao, ignorance willful; Jude 13; no marriage in resurrection (Luke 20:34-36), contrasts Mormon and Islamic views; Exod 3:6 present tense (Gen 26:24; 28:13); OT resurrection hope Ps 16:9-10; 49:15; 73:24; Job; Isa 26:19; Dan 12:2; Hos 6:1-2; Ezek 37; Josephus on Sadducees; John 5:25-29; John 11; 1 Cor 15:35-52; Phil 3:20-21.
41-63, Loving God (12:28-34): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-63. Third wave of questions (Matt 22:34; Ps 2:2; Acts 4:23-30); scribe more honest; trap: elevate Himself above Moses; Shema Deut 6:4-5; agapao love of will; heart (Prov 4:23), soul (Matt 26:38), mind, strength; unregenerate God-haters (Deut 7:9); Deut 30:6 circumcised heart; Lev 19:18 not self-love; first four commands God, six neighbor; Matt 5:43-48; scribe's words in temple court implicitly indict sacrificial religion; "near" not enough; 1 Cor 16:22; Rabbi Chalafta on Moses.
41-64, Son of David, Lord of All (12:35-37): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-64. Final conversation with leaders, final invitation after 12:34; David "in the Holy Spirit"; Ps 110:1 central, right hand = shared divine authority; Ps 110 recognized messianic, later rabbis reassigned it (Abraham, Melchizedek, Maccabeus); Son of David texts 2 Sam 7:12-13; Ps 89; Amos 9:11; Mic 5:2; Ezek 37:24-27; Matt 1, Luke 3 genealogies; Acts 2; 1 Cor 15; Heb; Phil 2; Rom 10:9; crowd enjoyed listening, tragic not repentance.
41-65, Religion and Its Victims (12:38-44, plus 13:1-2): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/41-65. Final public words; scribes experts in law; long robes, enlarged tassels (Num 15; Matt 9:20); titles (Matt 23); devour widows' houses by fees, estates, pledges; long prayers (Matt 6); greater condemnation (Heb 10:29); 25-30 OT texts on widows Exod 22 to Mal 3; James pure religion; treasury in Court of Women, 13 trumpet receptacles; widow a victim of the system, text says nothing of her motives, not a lesson on giving but judgment illustration; leads to 13:1-2 temple's destruction.
