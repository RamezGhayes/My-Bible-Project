# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 17 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles: 1759 from preceptaustin acts-17 link text + gty slug men-who-turned-the-world-upside-down-part-1; 1760, 1761 from the title shown on the gty page; 1762-1764 from the title the speaker gives in the transcript ("Getting to Know the Unknown God"; 1764 calls itself the third message on the passage; part numbers for 1762/1763 not confirmed, so not used). Dates: none confirmed on gty/preceptaustin, so omitted. Registered ranges follow what each transcript actually treats. Entries shortened to max 3 sentences (owner rule 27 Nov).
1759, Men Who Turned the World Upside Down, Part 1 (preceptaustin: 17:1-3, 10-11; transcript treats 1-12): straight from Philippi; distances Amphipolis/Apollonia; Livingstone; v6 wave-makers Elijah (1 Ki 18), Jer 38:2-4, Amos 7:10, Paul Acts 21:28-30; 22:22; 24:5; 28:22; message not personality; dialegomai imperfect = dialogue; courage from love for Israel (Rom 1:16; 10:1); OT proof Isa 53, Ps 22, Ps 16; Christ = Messiah title; Matt 16:21-22; Luke 24:25-27; peitho v4; salvation mental first; Thessalonica founded 315 BC, ~200,000, capital, Egnatian Way; Berea backwater (Cicero); pisteuo v12; noble = open-minded; anakrino judicial; John 5:39, 46; Luke 16:29-31; courage: trust (Ps 27; Eph 6:10), confession, thanks ahead (Acts 28:15); content: confess, study (2 Tim 2:15), personalize (Rom 12:2), share.
1760, The Men Who Turned the World Upside Down (page: Acts 17:1-17): converts, conflict, concern; peitho passive vs pisteuo active; marketplace loafers; jealousy of Gentiles on equal terms; system cannot tolerate holiness; idlers (2 Thess 3:11); treason charge like Jesus' trial; rulers' bond keeps Paul away; Bereans vs "concordance cripples"; Tom Paine; Cleveland couple; "stirred" like wind shaking; 1 Thess 2:14-16; Paul low, lonely; v16 provoked by God's glory denied; Petronius easier to find god than man; Rom 1:5; John 15:16 fruit; Bunyan.
1761, God's Man Confronts Satan's City (17:16-34): Paul alone, weakness; Paul's background; Athens golden age, Eye of Greece, Pausanias six volumes vs Paul's one remark; Renan "ugly little Jew"; paroxyno (cf. 15:39); Rev 3:17; bricklayer/architect; dielegeto; "whoever happened by"; agora description; committees; spermologos = seed-picker; Epicureans (chance, death ends, remote gods, pleasure), Stoics (pantheism, fatalism, cycles); John 7:15; 1 Cor 1:18; 3:18-20; Jesus + Anastasia; Areopagus as court (~30, murder, gods); Col 2:8; 1 Tim 6:20; 2 Tim 2:16-17; mockers (Acts 2:13; 26:8, 24; Jude); procrastination (24:25; Heb 3); kollao = glue; Dionysius court member, Damaris; modern Athens; motives: lostness, glory, compulsion; Moses Ex 32, Ps 69:9, Jer 20:9, Elijah; Henry Martyn.
1762, Getting to Know the Unknown God (transcript runs from v16 to v24): Spurgeon 1855; church drifting from knowing God; Jesus/Spirit point to Father; John 17:3 kind of life; Areopagus = court that condemned Socrates; deisidaimon; step 1 God is (Heb 11:6); atheism as moral motive (Ps 14; nabal), Einstein, agnostic; cause/effect and design (Heb 3:4; Ps 94:9-10); altar story Epimenides; Augustine; Creator vs Epicurean/Stoic; Ruler; not in temples (1 Ki 8:27); no images of God (Calvin, Hodge cited).
1763, Getting to Know the Unknown God (page passage 17:22-29; transcript treats 22-26): sophisticated idolatry; everyone worships; God-shaped vacuum; five-watt light, Rom 1:20; God's jealousy (Ex 20:3-5; 34:14); Tasker marital fidelity; Jer 9:23-24; God is (Deut 6:4; Mark 12:28-34; Isa 44:6-8); Creator Job 26, Ps 33:6; Ruler Heb 1:3, Isa 40:12-22; Giver v25 (Job 22:2; Ps 50:9-12; Rom 11:36; Jas 1:17; 1 Tim 6:17), life/breath/all = birth, sustenance, everything; Controller v26: one blood vs Greeks/barbarians, times = seasons and eras, bounds = location and extent.
1764, Getting to Know the Unknown God, third message (transcript treats 27-34; gty /library URL robots-blocked, read at /resources/sermons/1764): those who think they know God and don't (John 8:54-55; Rom 2:17-21; Titus 1:16; 2 Tim 3:5; Wailing Wall) vs those who know they don't; v27 purpose clause, optative "if haply", not far, art exhibit; Jer 29:13; Ps 145:18-19; John 3:19; v28 poets meant Zeus; v29 creature cannot make Creator; v30 agnoia ~ unknown, "overlooked" only here = no special judgment, consequential judgment still (Ezek 18:4; Gal 6:7; Rom 1:18); v31 John 5:22-27; Jude 14; 2 Thess 1:7-8; resurrection = credentials; does not think God sends people to hell, John 1:9, but salvation only through Christ (John 14:6; Acts 4:12); vv32-34 Paul left to see who'd follow; Matt 11:27; 1 John 5:20; John 17:3. Does not name Dionysius/Damaris.
