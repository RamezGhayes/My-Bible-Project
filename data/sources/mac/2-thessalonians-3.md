# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 2 Thessalonians 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles, ranges and dates from https://www.monergism.com/1-2-thessalonians-mp3-series-2 (no codes); codes from the gty links at https://preceptaustin.org/2_thessalonians_commentaries (53-14 to 53-17 with title slugs in the URLs, e.g. 53-16_Work--A-Noble-Christian-Duty-Part-3). The 53-14 and 53-16 pages head their text "Work: A Christian Duty".
Blocked: https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/53-14, 53-16, 53-17 robots-disallowed; the same sermons were read at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/<code>.
Not used: 80-201 "The Marks of a Faithful Congregation" and 80-281 "A Church to Be Commended" (3:1-5, listed by Precept Austin) were not read.
53-13, The Pastor's Desire for His People (3:1-5), 1992-05-30. Title from monergism only (Precept Austin wrongly links 53-13 as "Coming Man of Sin, Part 6"); the speaker on the page proposes "What the Pastor Desires from His People". Read at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/53-13: mutual duties of pastor and people (Heb 13:17; 1 Thess 5:12-13); loipos = finally/for the rest; pray present tense continual, peri = surround; strongest apostle asks weakest saints; word "run" (trecho; Ps 147:15) and glorified = received and honored (Acts 13:44-49; Col 4:2-3); rhuomai rescue; "perverse" = out of place, rare for a person; not all have faith; Lord faithful vs faithless men (1 Cor 10:13; Jude 24); sterizo; the evil one = Satan; confidence in the Lord not in human nature; commands from God, even gospel call (Matt 28:20); "direct" = make straight path (1 Thess 3:11); love of God ambiguous (Lightfoot); steadfastness of Christ; four desires: prayerful, trusting, obedient, growing.
53-14, Work: A Noble Christian Duty, Part 1 (3:6), 1992-07-05: views of work; Gen 3:19; Prov 24 sluggard; Talmudic prayer of scribes; Eusebius two tiers of piety, monasticism; Luther and Calvin rejected sacred/secular split (Luther: preaching and washing dishes); causes of idleness (Jewish elitism, Greek disdain for manual labor, end-times urgency, laziness) none valid; help needy, not able-bodied idle; command with Christ's authority; "keep aloof" = furling sails; ataktos = out of rank; tradition = apostolic teaching (1 Thess 2:13); third step of Matt 18:15-17; 1 Thess 4:10-12; Eph 6:5-8; Col 3:22-24; Jesus carpenter ~20 years; no premium on indolence.
53-15, Work: A Noble Christian Duty, Part 2 (3:7-9), 1992-07-12: warped work ethic; Ecclesiastes cynicism and corrective (2:24; 3:13); work a gift (five reasons), a command (Ex 20:9); God and Jesus work (John 5:17); work before the fall (Gen 2:15; "gardener" became "farmer"); causes at Thessalonica; six incentives: disfellowship, example; mimeisthai; ataktos; eat bread idiom; kopos, mochthos; Jason (Acts 17:7); tentmaker (Acts 18); right to support (1 Cor 9; Deut 25:4; 1 Tim 5:17; Gal 6:6) waived to model work (2 Cor 11:7; Acts 20).
53-16, Work: A Noble Christian Duty, Part 3 (3:10-15), 1992-07-19: v10 maxim, repeated rule, no excuse; real distress differs (homeless, alcohol); John 6 crowd wanted endless provision; v11 ergazomenous / periergazomenous wordplay, busybodies (1 Tim 5:13); v12 parakaleo command and appeal; quietly, own bread; daily work a platform for witness; v13 kalos (Milligan) = noble as seen by others, do not let idlers make you indifferent to the truly poor (Luke 14:12-14; Acts 20:35; Gal 2:10); v14 take note, shame = turn inward; v15 not enemy, brother (contrast 1 Cor 5; Matt 18; Prov 27:6; Gal 6:1); six motivations: disfellowship, example, survival, harmony, shame, love.
53-17, Peace in Every Circumstance (3:16-18), 1992-07-26: stormy letter ends calm; four prayers in the letter; needs: peace, strength, truth, grace; optative; worldly peace fragile; peace divine (Rom 15:33; Eph 2:14; Gal 5:22), a gift (Ps 85:8; John 20; Isa 57:19-21), Thomas Watson on sinner's seeming peace; not dependent on circumstances (John 14:27); "Lord with you" = presence for power, not mere omnipresence (Ps 139; Matt 28:20; Acts 1:8; 2 Cor 12:9-10); own hand authenticates against forgery of 2:2 (Gal 6:11; Phlm 19); truth (John 17:17; 3 John 4); saving and enabling grace (Titus 2:11; 2 Cor 12:9; 2 Pet 3:18); "all of Him and yet it takes all of me".
