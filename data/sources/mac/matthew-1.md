# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 1 (Grace to You)
Codes and dates from series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7001/matthew-vol-01 .
2180, The Gracious King (1:1-17), 1978-01-15: King revealed/rejected/returning; genealogies for land, priesthood, throne (Num 26; 35; Ezra 2:62; Rom 11:1; Josephus); Jesus = "Jehovah saves", Christos = prophet priest king; David before Abraham, 2 Sam 7; slanders (Matt 13:54-57; John 7:27, 40-41; 8:41, 48); sins of Abraham and David; four "outcast" women (Gen 38; Josh 2; Gen 19:30-38; Deut 23:3; 2 Sam 11-12); three eras; Jer 22:30 Jeconiah curse, virgin birth solution, Joseph legal / Mary blood via Nathan (Luke 3:23; 4:22); v16 "husband of Mary", feminine "of whom"; Mary a sinner needing Saviour (Luke 1:28, 46-47; Mark 3:31-35; Luke 11:27-28), critique of Roman Catholic Marian dogma; friend of sinners Matt 9:13.
