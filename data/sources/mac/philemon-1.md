# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Philemon (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes and titles from the gty links at https://www.preceptaustin.org/philemon-resources (series "Forgiveness: How to Restore Broken Relationships", 1991); titles and ranges also in the Grace to You broadcast series on SermonAudio, https://vault-beta.sermonaudio.com/series/20015 (broadcast dates 2011 and 2015 only, not preaching dates). Preaching dates not shown on any page read, so left empty (57-4 mentions the 50th anniversary of Pearl Harbor, i.e. late 1991). The gty pages show no title.

57-1, A Living Lesson on Forgiveness (1-3): forgiveness theme (Eph 4:32; Col 3:13; Luke 15; Matt 18); "prisoner" not "apostle", personal appeal; Christ put him in prison (Phil 4:22; Eph 4:1); only prison letter to an individual; Timothy companion not co-author; agapetos; sunergos; Philemon wealthy Colossian, house church (Dura-Europos c. 232 first known building); likely converted in Paul's Ephesus years (Acts 19); Apphia "sister", wife; Archippus "fellow soldier", son and minister (Col 4:17); letter read to church for accountability; grace means, peace result; God and Jesus joined = deity; slavery: NT never calls for abolition, uses slavery as picture, instructs both sides (Eph 6; Col 4; 1 Tim 6:1-2; 1 Pet 2:18), gospel removes abuses, revolt would have been crushed, gospel changed hearts and slavery died where it spread.
57-2, The Characteristics of One Who Forgives (4-7): culture of vengeance (Dirty Harry, Rambo; Susan Forward "Toxic Parents"); four results of unforgiveness (prison of past, bitterness, open door to Satan Eph 4:26-27; 2 Cor 2:10-11, hindered fellowship); 75 word pictures; outline: 4-7 character, 8-18 action, 19-25 motives; praise nourishes virtue; faith present tense; chiasm of v5; agape love of choice (Gal 5:6; 1 John 3:14); koinonia = belonging, Onesimus now belongs as brother; epignosis experiential (Swiss Alps photo vs skiing); eis Christon, devotion to Christ's glory forgives; splanchna; "refreshed" military rest; Philemon no office, a businessman; poem of father who struck his son.
57-3, The Actions of One Who Forgives (8-18): appeal to love not command (Rom 13; Matt 22); presbutes aged beyond years; prisoner of Christ; "begot" in chains; repentance proved by returning (Matt 3); useless/useful wordplay, slave nicknames; splanchna; free consent; Col 3:22-23; providence (Gen 50:20; Rom 8:28; Ps 76:10): "you lost a slave, you gained a brother"; not abolishing slavery, servant and brother; koinonos; restitution (Num 5:6-8), likely stole; Paul as substitute like Christ; three elements: reception, restoration, restitution; Matt 18; Ps 51.
57-4, The Motives of One Who Forgives (19-25): own hand IOU (Col 4:18); motives: unpayable debt (Philemon owes Paul himself; his own debts to parents, wife...), blessing others (v20 onaimen wordplay; Phil 2:2; church unity), obedience (v21; Matt 6; 18; Luke 17:3-4; "more than I say": warm welcome, ministry, not explicitly emancipation), accountability to leaders (v22 visit; 1 Cor 4:18-21; prayer "nerves that move the muscles of omnipotence"; Jas 5), fellowship (v23-24 Epaphras Col 1:7; 4:12; Mark; Aristarchus; Demas 2 Tim 4:10; Luke), grace (v25); Thomas More; Onesimus of Ephesus possible; Mitsuo Fuchida, Peggy Covell, Jacob DeShazer, Luke 23:34.
