Titles, verse ranges and dates from the Grace to You series page "John, Vol. 2" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V2/john-vol-2), which lists code, title, passage and date together; 43-20/21/22 also confirmed (title, passage, date) by gracechurch.org series 746 "Messiah: The Living Water". 43-20, Messiah: The Living Water, Part 1 (4:1-15), 2013-04-21: left Judea to avoid premature clash (7:30; 8:20); disciples baptized, validity not tied to baptizer; "had to" pass = divine appointment; Samaritan history (2 Kgs 17, 722 BC, Gerizim, Nehemiah); Sychar between Ebal and Gerizim (Deut 28); kopiao weariness, humanity; noon visit to avoid scorn; principles: unexpected condescension (J.C. Ryle "spiritual aggression"; Jonah warning), unsolicited mercy (dorean, Jer 2:13; Ps 36:9; Isa 55:1; John 7:37-38; Jas 4:2), unparalleled blessings (eternal security); woman's sarcasm.
43-21, Messiah: The Living Water, Part 2 (4:16-26), 2013-04-28: worship synonymous with salvation; unhesitating conviction (sin confronted before faith); theoreo; Gerizim vs Jerusalem; end of place-based worship, veil, AD 70; v26 "I who speak to you am" = divine name; John 2:23-25; Luke 18 publican.
43-22, Messiah: The Living Water, Part 3 (4:27-42), 2013-05-05: thesis: v42 "Savior of the world"; providence, priority, prophecy, proclamation; disciples' silence = trust; water jar, eagerness to tell = mark of true salvation; food = saving sinners; Luke 15; fields white = prophecy; sowers Moses, prophets, maybe Baptist, the woman; Ps 126:6; testimony leads to Christ; Israel means not end; Acts 4:12.
43-23, Contemplating Unbelief (4:43-45), 2013-05-26: John the gospel of belief; divine side (new birth) and human side (faith); survey of "believe" texts; v44 linked to Nazareth; Galileans received him because of Jerusalem; levels of unbelief (ready disciples, Samaritans needing information, Galileans needing signs); Matt 11:20-24.
43-24, Saving Faith in a Herodian Household (4:46-54), 2013-06-09: official tied to Herod Antipas; desperation drives to Jesus; v48 rebuke of demand for signs; v49 fearful partial faith (Mark 9:24); v50 from works to words; time-reckoning debate unimportant; v53 faith in his person, household; faith = assurance on objective Word; gift yet command; Heb 11:1; Heb 6.
