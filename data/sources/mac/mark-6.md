# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 6 (Grace to You)
Dates from the series page www.gty.org/sermons/series/41V3/mark-vol-3.
41-26, Amazing Unbelief (6:1-6): Capernaum left under judgment (Matt 11:20-24); Nazareth ~500 people; disciples learn from rejection before their mission. Astonished at wisdom and works; answer obvious (God); Edersheim on synagogue speakers. tekton put-down; "son of Mary" possible illegitimacy slur (John 8:41); brothers James, Jude (epistles); skandalizo. Prophet without honor (Luke 4:16-30 earlier visit; Mark 3:21; John 7:5). v5 problem of purpose not power: miracles attest truth. v6 thaumazo; culpability (John 5:39; Matt 23:37; John 3:36). OT unbelief examples (Eve, flood, wilderness, Zarephath widow and Naaman). Four marks: obscures the obvious, elevates the irrelevant, assaults the messenger, spurns the supernatural.
41-27, Ordinary Men, Extraordinary Calling, Part 1 (6:6-9): Jesus' solitary ministry, now delegated; Galilee time short. Apostle = sent one; four phases of calling. Pairs: support, two witnesses. Twelve common men = judgment on apostate religious elite, twelve tribes (Luke 22:29-30; 1 Cor 1). Signs of apostle (2 Cor 12:12; Heb 2:4). No provisions: training in dependence, situational (Luke 22:35-36); Exod 12:11 new exodus hint. Never charge for gospel (Matt 10:8; 1 Pet 5; 1 Tim 6:10; but 1 Cor 9; Gal 6:6). Lessons: proclaim salvation, manifest compassion, live dependently.
41-28, Ordinary Men, Extraordinary Calling, Part 2 (6:10-13, 30-32): stay in one worthy house (Matt 10:11-13), contentment, discernment. Rejection expected; dust = treat as Gentile/unclean, testimony of judgment (Matt 10:14-15; Acts 18:5-6). Preach repentance from self-righteous religion (Acts 2-4 sermons). Authority over demons and disease validates message; oil = symbol of God's presence, OT anointing of kings and priests (James 5:14; Luke 10:34). v30 apostles, accountability (Heb 13:17). v31 rest; Heb 4:15; kingdom doesn't depend on one servant.
41-29, The Murder of the Greatest Prophet (6:14-29): Herod hears because the Twelve multiplied ministry (Luke 9:7-9). Elijah expectation from Malachi; emphatic "I" beheaded: guilt and terror; Luke 13:31-32 fox. Arrest (John 3:22-24; Luke 3:19-20); unlawful marriage (Lev 18:16; 20:21). Herodias's grudge; Herod protects John, perplexed yet hears gladly. Lewd banquet with Galilean elite, Herodians (Mark 3:6). Rash oath; no real authority to give a kingdom. Pride over repentance. Burial; Matt 14:12-13 Jesus withdraws, preview of cross. Luke 23:6-12 Herod meets Jesus, silence. Matt 23:29-37 killing the prophets. Broadus: Fulvia and Cicero's head; Jerome report on Herodias (uncertain). Josephus on Herod the Great's death.
41-30, The Creator Provides (6:30-44): Jehovah-Jireh. Rest (Heb 4:15; Mark 4:38). Bethsaida (Luke 9:10). Crowds curious (John 6:2); He welcomed and taught kingdom (Luke 9:11). Sheep without shepherd (Num 27:17; 1 Kings 22:17; 2 Chr 18:16; Ezek 34:5); splagchnizomai. Common grace. Philip 200 denarii (John 6:5-7), Andrew and boy (John 6:8-9); 2 Kings 4 parallel. Ordered groups: sovereign command makes order. Thanks, not magic (1 Tim 4); uncursed food (Gen 3 contrast). chortazo gorged. Twelve baskets. 5,000 men plus women and children; only miracle besides resurrection in all four Gospels; Passover (John 6:4); try to make Him king (John 6:14-15; Deut 18:15); bread of life (John 6:26-35); many leave (John 6:66); woe on Bethsaida (Matt 11:21).
41-31, Jesus Walks on Water (6:45-56): Intercessor, Protector, Compassionate Lord. v45 compelled (Matt 14:22) to remove disciples from revolutionary crowd (John 6:14-15). v46 High Priest interceding (Heb 2:17-18; Luke 22:32; John 17). v48 sees them by omniscience; Job 9:8 treads the waves; "pass by" = come alongside. v49 phantasma; v50 tarasso; "Take courage, it is I"; Psalms of refuge. v51 existemi; Job 26:14; Ps 77:19; Hab 3:15. v52 no insight from loaves, hardened hearts. Matt 14:28-33 Peter; worship; Mark omits from Peter's humility; Ps 94:18. v53-56 Gennesaret; John 6:21; bread of life sermon (John 6:22-59, 44, 51); common grace; temporary healing not the goal, eternal life is.
