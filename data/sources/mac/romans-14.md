# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 14 (Grace to You)
Codes, ranges, dates from https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7350/romans-vol-10 . Not read: 45-108 (14:1), 45-110 (14:13-15, robots-disallowed), so block 13-15 has no mac entry.
45-109, Receiving One Another with Understanding, Part 2 (14:1-12): four thrusts of 14:1-15:13; Matt 18:6-10; weak/strong defined, not Judaizers; proslambano, krino, exoutheneo, oiketes, idios, kyrieuo; reasons: God received him, Lord sustains (Jude 24, 1 Pet 1:5), Lord sovereign, Lord alone judge; days (Gal 4:9-10, Col 2); 1 Tim 4:2, 4:4; textual note on v.6; v.8 "greatest injunction to holy living"; Lord/God interchange = deity; Isa 45:23, Phil 2; 1 Cor 3, 4; 2 Cor 5:10.
45-111, Building Up One Another Without Offending, Part 2 (14:16-23): recap of 13-15 (stumble, grieve, destroy; 1 Tim 4:2, 1:5); principle 4 witness (Rom 2:24, 1 Pet 2:15-16), agathos; kingdom; dokimos (Rom 12:1-2, Titus 2:5,10); dioko (2 Cor 13:11, Eph 4:3, 1 Cor 14:12); principle 5 work of God (Eph 2:10, 1 Cor 8:13), present imperative; wine non-moral, he abstains; principle 6 don't flaunt; v.23; 1 Cor 10:31-33.
