# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 19 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Sermon pages show no title or date (43-110 shows only a note naming "I and the Father Are One, Part 2" about an audio/video mismatch, not used as title).
Titles, ranges, dates: 43-105 to 43-108 from gty series page "John, Vol. 9" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V9/john-vol-9). 43-109 and 43-110 titles and ranges from gty links on Precept Austin (https://www.preceptaustin.org/john_19_resources) with matching gty URL slugs; dates unknown (series pages 43V10 and 43V11 robots-blocked), registered with empty date.
43-105@19, Jesus Appears Before Pilate, Part 3 (series page: 18:25-19:7), 2016-05-22: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-105. Registered here only for 19:1-7 (key 43-105@19); 18:28-40 part belongs to the John 18 worker. 19:1 scourging: thongs tipped with metal or bone, flesh torn, could kill; Deut 25:3 limit of 40 for Jews, none under Rome; Roman citizens exempt (Acts 22); 19:2-3 soldiers' mock-king game, Philo on boys mocking a fool, grooves of such games he saw at Fort Antonia; four reasons for cruelty: carnal mind hostile (Rom 8:7), depravity (Rom 3), Satan's hour (Luke 22:53), God permitted it as Jesus bore sin's punishment; 19:4-6 ecce homo, fifth or sixth declaration of innocence, chief priests and temple police lead the cry; 19:7 Lev 24:16 blasphemy; Matt 26:63-66; seven charges across the Gospels (temple, evildoer, perverting nation, forbidding tribute, stirring people, claiming kingship, blasphemy); irony: last charge true; no neutrality (Matt 12:30); Heb 6:6. Omitted: his remarks on national guilt (harsh-statement rule).
43-106, Jesus Appears Before Pilate, Part 4 (19:1-15), 2016-06-05: read at .../43-106. Opens with John Wall (1650), "None But Christ": Christ's whole life a crucifixion (womb, manger, poverty offering, circumcision, temptation, rejection, Gethsemane); scourging no fixed limit, men in shifts; ecce homo = "is this enough?"; v8 phobeo = terrified, root of phobia; pagan belief in gods visiting as men (Acts 14:11; 28:6); v9 silence: Pilate hardened past response like Pharaoh, Gen 6:3; v10 bluster; v11 "he who delivered me" = Caiaphas and the Sanhedrin, not Judas; degrees of guilt; v12 charge shifts from blasphemy to sedition, Pilate's fragile standing; v13 bema at Gabbatha; v15 "no king but Caesar" the greatest blasphemy, Lord their king (Isa 33:22); Barabbas = son of the father; question: what will you do with Jesus.
43-107, Prophecies Fulfilled at Calvary (19:16-30), 2016-08-21: read at .../43-107. Transcript covers 16-25. Theology not physical detail; Pilate declared him innocent six times; "handed over" technical term, Rom 8:32 God handed over his Son; human instrument, divine cause; v17 "went out" vs "led out" in Synoptics, sheep led (Isa 53:7), straight from sentence to death (Isa 53:8), carried cross himself (estimate 200-300 lb), Simon later; Isaac (Gen 22:6); outside the camp (Exod 29; Lev 4; 16; Heb 13:11-12); Golgotha, Latin calvaria; hill near Damascus Gate and Garden Tomb; v18 Jews stoned (Beth-has-sekilah); "lifted up" (3:14; 8:28; 12:32-33; Num 21); 18:31-32; Ps 22 details; Isa 53:12; about 330 prophecies fulfilled (citing Liddon); v23-24 four soldiers, four garments (sandals, belt, headpiece, cloak), seamless tunic like the high priest's, pontifex = bridge builder; Ps 22:18; soldiers fulfil Scripture unknowingly; first of four marks of glory.
43-108, The Sign at Calvary (19:16-30), 2016-08-28: read at .../43-108. Transcript covers 16-27. Four marks of glory: Scripture, sign, sympathy, supremacy; went willingly (Mark 15:20; Luke 23:26; 10:18); Isaac had a substitute, Christ none; Pilate offered them to stone him; Isa 53:9, 12; criminals' bodies to Gehenna, the city dump; v19-22 sign: no crime to write, sarcastic revenge on the leaders who blackmailed him (19:12); "Nazarene" a jab (1:46); Hebrew religion, Greek culture, Latin power; Passover pilgrims; Pilate spoke truth like Caiaphas (11:50); John 12:13; 18:36-37; Acts 3:17-19 ignorance and offer of forgiveness; Phil 2 every knee; sign like a tract for the thief (Luke 23:42); seamless tunic = high priest (Exod 28:31-32); v25-27 five at the cross amid danger; John never names Mary; Acts 1:14 last mention; rejects sinlessness, perpetual virginity, assumption, co-redemptrix, intercessor; Mary called God her Savior (Luke 1:47); Luke 2:35; silent grief; "Woman" not disrespect, a changed relationship; sister = Salome (Matt 27:56; Mark 15:40); Clopas = Alphaeus; Magdalene not a prostitute (Luke 8:2); Mary = Miriam, "bitterness".
43-109, Sympathy and Supremacy at Calvary (19:16-30), date unknown: read at .../43-109. Recaps 16-24; sympathy: four women and John; courage to stand by a condemned man; Mary's low profile, no role in redemption beyond bearing Christ; Joseph likely dead, brothers unbelieving (7:5); John once a son of thunder (Luke 9:54), became apostle of love, "the disciple whom Jesus loved" (13:23; 20:2; 21:7); "Woman" a term of dignity; family ties changed with his ministry (2:4; Mark 3:31-35; 2 Cor 5:16); sympathy through John 13-17; supremacy: omniscience, "I thirst" fulfils Ps 69:21; refused gall sedative, accepted sour wine; hyssop and Passover (Exod 12); tetelestai = completed accomplishment; died early, still strong, shouting, by his will (10:17-18); "bowed" same verb elsewhere for laying head (pillowed); Luke 23:46; thief (Luke 23:39-43): both mocked first, one feared God, confessed, asked to be remembered; Spurgeon: last companion on earth, first at heaven's gate; Rom 3; Luke 23:34; centurion (Luke 23:47; Matt 27:54); crowds beat breasts; Acts 2:32-41.
43-110, Jesus, the Death Conqueror (Precept Austin: 19:31-20:10; registered as 19:31-42 since the transcript covers only ch. 19), date unknown: read at .../43-110. Theme: Jesus controlled his death, body, burial; death cafés illustration; Larry King after 9/11; Job 18:14; Heb 2:15; Eccl 8:8; Heb 9:27; death stalked him (Matt 2; Luke 4; 5:16-18; 7:1; 8:59; 10:31; 11:53); 10:18; 11:25; 14:19; 2 Tim 1:10; 1 Cor 15:53-57; tetelestai; timeline 9 am (Mark 15:25), darkness noon-3 as wrath, died ~3 pm; v31 preparation, high Sabbath with Passover; Deut 21:22-23 and hypocrisy; crucifragium, mallet on legs, suffocation; v33 executioners' testimony vs swoon theory; Mark 15:44; v34 cautious ruptured-heart view (Stuart Bergsma; Ps 69:20-21), not dogmatic; v35 eyewitness (1 John 1:1); v36 Ps 34:20; Exod 12:46; Num 9:12; 1 Cor 5:7; v37 Zech 12:10 future mourning; Isa 53:5; v38-42 Romans left bodies, leaders planned no burial; Isa 53:9; Joseph rich Sanhedrin member, secret (12:42), "gathered courage" (Mark 15:43); new tomb (Matt 27:60); Nicodemus ~100 lb, a king's quantity; Jews did not embalm; spices show honoring a dead king, not plotting theft; part of a day counted as a day; no condemnation; Heb 3:1.
