1739, The First Gentile Church (11:1-30). Outline: groundwork (10:1-11:18), genesis (19-21), growth (22-26), generosity (27-30). Repetition of Cornelius story = importance (scroll ~35 ft). Danger: Christianity a Jewish sect; prejudice, nationalism. v1 news before Peter, verdict before hearing. v2 circumcision party (15:5), "contended" with doubts, imperfect = prolonged; circumcision vestibule. v3 entering and eating taboo. v4 in order, facts not authority. v5-10 sheet = church; unclean animals = Gentiles; end of diet laws; three times = stubbornness. v11-12 nothing doubting; six + Peter = seven witnesses (Egyptian/Roman law claim, his own). v13-14 household prepared, Peter instrument. v15-17 Spirit gift, same gift, condition faith in Christ; withstand God. v18 repentance unto life; Jonah would not say it; catalyst verse; ~7-year delay: apostolic doctrine (2:42), instruments mature, prejudice broken. Principles: recite facts, test experience by witnesses and Scripture, beware "God told me". v19 Phoenicia coast, Tyre, Sidon, ships to Cyprus; 8:4 resumed; nationalistic view. v20 Greeks = pagans; Barnabas from Cyprus; rose among weeds; unnamed = faceless commitment. v21 hand of Lord = power with blessing (Ex 14:31; Ezra 7:9; 8:18; Neh 2:18); belief must turn; mere assent. v22 Barnabas beloved (Acts 4; 9); right man for task: love + gifts, Cypriot. v23 exhort = encourage; purpose of heart = fixed resolve; 13:43; 14:22; John 15; week-old Christian wanting to quit; cling to Word, Spirit teaches through it (1 John 2). v24 agathos good to others; ochlos hikanos huge crowd; righteousness testimony to others, faith to self, Spirit fullness to God. v25 seek with difficulty; Saul preaching in Cilicia, hard to find. v26 year teaching; church's purpose teaching (6:4; 2:42; 13:1), not entertaining; Christian = Christ party, derisive (cf Caesariani; 26:28; 1 Pet 4:16); Eusebius: Sanctus. v27 prophets foundational (Eph 2:20; 1 Cor 14); office ceased, gift of preaching continues. v28 Claudius 41-54, famines 45-46 (Tacitus, Josephus, Eusebius, Cassius). v29 according to ability; proportionate; Gentiles giving to Jews = love; against token giving. v30 elders; sent best men with money.
