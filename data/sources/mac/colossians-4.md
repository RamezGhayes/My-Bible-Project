# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Colossians 4 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles and ranges from the titled gty links at https://preceptaustin.org/colossians_4_resources (MacArthur section), checked against the gty pages (https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>). Dates not shown, left empty.
4:1: covered in 2149 (The New Man Makes a New Home, 3:18-4:1), registered under chapter 3 only, so no MacArthur entry for 4:1 here. What it says on 4:1: masters give what is just and fair, knowing they have a Master in heaven ("do unto others").
2150, The Speech of the New Man, Part 1 (4:2); title from page header and preceptaustin: 3:5-4:6 framework, speech the theme of 4:2-6 (Matt 12:34; Eph 4:24-29); Bias returning the tongue; James 3; marks of unredeemed mouth (Rom 3:13-14) and redeemed mouth; four kinds of speech: prayer, proclamation, performance, perfection; proskartereo = persevere courageously (Kittel); Eph 6:18; Luke 18:1-8 unjust judge; Luke 11:5-8 friend at midnight; Jacob at Peniel; Virginia Stem Owens "not a tame lion", Jacques Ellul prayer as combat; balance persistence with accepting God's answer; watch = alert, specific requests (Matt 26:40-41; 1 Pet 4:7), notebook of answers; thanksgiving five times in Colossians: presence, provision, pardon, promise of victory.
2151, The Speech of the New Man, Part 2 (4:2-6); title on page and preceptaustin: new accent of new man; prayer = God-consciousness plus persistence (Nehemiah 1 and 2:4); proclamation: "door for the Word", "as I ought" (1 Cor 9:16), Paul's Roman house arrest advanced gospel (Acts 28:30-31; Phil 1:13; 4:22), church's first recorded prayer for boldness (Acts 4:29-31); mystery of Christ; warns against experience-, ego-, expedience-centered evangelism; Tex Watson letter; performance: walk before talk, wisdom (Prov 9:10; Jas 1:5), redeem kairos (Ps 90:12); perfection: speech with grace (Luke 4:22), salt stings, preserves, and is wit (Plutarch) (Eph 4:29); Ps 12:4 vs 141:3; whiskey bottle smell.
2152, With a Little Help from My Friends (4:7-18); title from preceptaustin; on the page no heading, speaker uses the same phrase: group photograph of co-workers; partners (Ex 17; Eccl 4:9-12); Tychicus servant's heart (diakonos, sundoulos of Christ; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12; 2 Tim 4:12), carried Colossians, Philemon, Ephesians (circular, blank addressee); Onesimus sinful past (Philemon), Ignatius may imply he later became a pastor (possible only); Aristarchus sympathetic heart, "fellow prisoner" = war captive, chose to share; Mark surprising future (Acts 13:13; 15:37-39; Phlm 24; 1 Pet 5:13; 2 Tim 4:11); Jesus Justus, paregoria (paregoric), only three Jewish co-workers (Acts 28:24-28); Epaphras single passion, agonizomai in prayer, "zeal" = pain/intense concern, teleios mature; Luke specialized talent, physician; Demas sad future (2 Tim 4:10), like Judas; Nymphas house church; letter from Laodicea probably Ephesians (conjecture); Archippus: fulfill ministry, challenge to every believer; signature, chains, grace.
