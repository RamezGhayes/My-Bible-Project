# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 13 (Grace to You)
Codes and titles from gty.org pages and search results; dates not shown, omitted. 2297 and 2298 read from gty study-guide print pages (fetched page text, not extracts).
2297, Kingdom Parables, Part 1 (13:1-3): King depicted and denied (chs 1-12); Toussaint "separated the fruit from the tree"; postponement, parenthesis (dispensational); universal vs mediatorial kingdom (McClain); Ps 29:10; 103:19; 1 Chr 29:11; true and false (Rom 9:6; 2:28-29; Matt 8:12; John 15); David/Absalom illustration; boat; parables start, 1 Cor 14:21.
2298, Kingdom Parables, Part 2 (overview 13:1-50, focus 10-17, 34-35): parabole/mashal; four values of parables; seven parables summarized; mystery; v12 talents Matt 25:28, Rom 9:4-5; parables as judgment, Isa 6:9-10; vv16-17 1 Pet 1:10-11, 1 Cor 2:10, Ps 119:18; Ps 78:2 Asaph; plan on schedule.
2299, The Responses to the Gospel (13:3-23): farming details, yield 7.5-fold average; sower = Son of Man (13:37), seed = Word (Luke 8:11); result depends on heart; fruit not foliage; 2 Cor 4:4; 2 Tim 3:12; William Arnot; Ezek 36:26; 1 Pet 5:10; 1 Tim 6; fruit Ps 1, John 15, Eph 2:10; world, flesh, devil.
2300, The Kingdom and the World (13:24-43): disciples expected immediate purge (Matt 3:10-12; Isa 11:3-4; Ezek 20:33-38; Acts 1:6); darnel; Roman law; field = world not church; Son of Man Dan 7:13; John 8:44; 1 John 5:19; angels reap (2 Thess 1:7-9); fire Mark 9, Matt 25, Dan 12:2-3; Inquisition and Crusades warning; John 17; Col 1:13; Barclay quoted.
2301, The Power and Influence of Christ's Kingdom, Part 1 (13:31-32): Lord Kelvin; mustard smallest of garden herbs (Shinners); small start, Luke 17:20-21; worldwide end Ps 72:8-11, Isa 54:2, Rev 11:15; birds = nations sheltered, Dan 4, Ezek 31, 1 Cor 7:14.
2302, The Power and Influence of Christ's Kingdom, Part 2 (13:31-33): Whittier; Micah 4:1-5; Zech 8:18-23; John 18:33-37; Rom 14:17; leaven not evil here (structure, text); Luke 12:1, Gal 5:9, 1 Cor 5, Lev 23; Gen 18:6; Arnot; Acts 17:6; growth statistics.
2303, Entering the Kingdom (13:44-46): Thomas Guthrie; buried treasure, Josephus; rabbinic finder law; pearls, Talmud, Pliny; six principles: priceless, hidden (1 Cor 2:14), personal (Rom 9:6), joy, finder vs seeker (Paul, Samaritan, Spurgeon Isa 45:22; eunuch, Lydia), transaction Isa 55:1, Luke 14:28-32, Phil 3:4-8.
2304, The Furnace of Fire (13:47-52): hell theme, Matt 23:33; sagene dragnet, Lenski; separation vs coexistence; angels; hell: torment, body and soul, degrees, endless (Luke 16; Matt 10:28; 11:22-24; Luke 12:47-48; Matt 25:46); Gerstner, Bunyan; vv51-52 trained scribes; 2 Cor 5:11.
2305, The Power of Unbelief, Part 1 (13:53-58): Capernaum cursed (11:23); eight responses chs 13-16 mapped to soils; Luke 4:16-30 earlier visit; five marks of teaching; amazement not faith; unbelief blurs obvious; John 3:2, 3:19-20, 10:37-38; Edersheim.
2306, The Power of Unbelief, Part 2 (13:53-58): tekton; James Acts 15; brothers show Mary had children; four lessons: blurs obvious, builds irrelevant (John 7:15; Acts 4:13), blinds (1 Cor 1:23; Deut 32:20), blocks supernatural (Matt 7:6; 12:38-39); OT and NT examples; Heb 3:12; John 9.
