# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 18 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Dates not confirmed on gty/preceptaustin pages, omitted. Entries max 3 sentences (owner rule).
1765, The Gospel in Vanity Fair (gty page shows no title; the transcript itself uses "the Gospel in Vanity Fair" for Paul in Corinth, and 1767's gty slug is the-gospel-in-vanity-fair-part-3; single-source title, flag). Treats 18:1-8: Corinth (isthmus, Aphrodite, "corinthianize"), Paul weak (1 Cor 2:3); encouragements: companionship (Aquila, Priscilla, probably believers already; Suetonius "Chrestus"), skenopoioi = leather workers; work ethic, support after proven faithfulness (1 Cor 9; 1 Tim 5:17); dialegomai/peitho imperfect; Silas/Timothy with Philippian gift, "devoted to the Word" (NASB); opposition organized, shaking garments, blood on heads, responsibility; Titus Justus = Gaius?; Crispus, hearing-believing-baptized (Rom 10:17).
1766: read at /library/sermons-library/1766 and /resources/sermons/1766; covers 18:1-10 (companionship, converts, God's presence, election paradox); NO title on page and speaker gives none: NOT registered, no entries.
1767, The Gospel in Vanity Fair, Part 3 (title from gty slug on preceptaustin acts-18 page). Transcript treats 18:1-18, focus 9-17: Paul near quitting; three promises (presence emphatic, protection 2 Tim 4:16-18, "much people" = elect, 2 Tim 1:9; Rom 9; 2 Thess 2:13; with responsibility); 18 months; Gallio brother of Seneca; religio licita; words and names; Sosthenes beaten, who beat him uncertain; later "brother" (1 Cor 1:1); God uses indifferent rulers (Cyrus).
1768, From Judaism to Jesus (speaker's title in transcript). Treats 18:18-23: Nazirite vow ~30 days, gratitude; Priscilla/Aquila house church (1 Cor 16:19); feast clause textually disputed; up/down Jerusalem; Acts transitional, doctrine from epistles (Rom 8:9); Hebrews theology of shift; New Covenant lifelong devotion vs periodic vows; transformation takes time; lay believers strategic.
1769, From Judaism to Jesus (speaker's title; continuation). Treats 18:24-28: Alexandria; logios eloquent; dunatos; katecheo = oral instruction, not revelation (Gal 1:11); fervent = human spirit (Rom 12:11); akribos exactness; knew only John's baptism, pre-cross; Apollos an OT believer/John's disciple, saved between Ephesus and Corinth (his inference); corrected kindly; Achaia letter; refuted publicly. Theology note added (many Reformed see Apollos as a true believer).
