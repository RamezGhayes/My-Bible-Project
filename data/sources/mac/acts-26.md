1797 (26:2-19): motives: Festus wants charge, Agrippa curious, Paul sees opportunity; v2-3 sincere courtesy, Agrippa informed; v4-5 double superlative, strictest sect; v6-8 hope of Israel, Messiah and resurrection; twelve tribes, rejects British-Israelism; Matt 28:11-15 willful rejection; v9-11 vote = pebble; honest confession; v12-15 goads, ox, resisting Christ a losing battle; v16-18 minister and witness, apostle office needed direct appointment and seeing risen Christ, not continuing; saved out of world, sent back (against monasticism); light not "more light"; everyone serves Satan or God; Eph 4:18; Col 1:12; Eph 2:8-9; v19 obedience; 2 Cor 5:17-20; 6:1-2; evangelism command for all (2 Tim 4:5).
1798 (26:1-32): year of evangelism; Spirit-filled life reproductive; Paul never defensive; v18 pattern: open eyes (conviction), darkness to light, Satan to God (conversion), forgiveness, inheritance, by faith (Eph 2:8-9; Rom 10:9-10); God's sovereignty in salvation and calling; v19 sovereignty and responsibility (Gal 1:16); Moses, Ezekiel; marks of obedience (1 Pet 1:14; Rom 6:16; Acts 5:29; Heb 11:8; John 14:15); v20 works prove repentance (James 2); v21 equal salvation to Gentiles; v22 help from God (2 Tim 3:11; 4:17-18); v23 Ps 22, Isa 53, Ps 16; protos = preeminence; v24 John 10:20; 1 Cor 1; v25 sophrosune sound mind; v26 not in a corner, accountability; v27 trap: yes means Jesus is Messiah; v28 "in short time trying to convert me?" not near-conversion; v29 love, freedom without chains; v30-31 not sedition; v32 cowardice; hindrances: popularity, pride, immorality, unbelief, indifference; faithfulness not results.
