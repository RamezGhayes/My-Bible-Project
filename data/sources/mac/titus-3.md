56-23, The Christian's Responsibility in a Pagan Society, Part 1 (3:1-2), 1993-07-18: "remind" present imperative; vv1-8 four things to remember: duty (1-2), former condition (3), salvation (4-7), mission (8); submit whatever ruler's character (Caesar to Hitler); Matt 22:15-22 render to Caesar, Jesus paid tax; Rom 13:1-7; submission evangelistic not political; only exception when state commands sin (Acts 4:18-20; 5:40-42); 1 Pet 2:12-17; ready for every good deed = "aggressive goodness" (Gal 6:10; contrast 1:16); v2 blasphemeo = slander, confront sin without insulting sinners; amachos = not fighting, no political agenda, not our home; epieikes "sweet reasonableness"; prautes power under control (Matt 5:5; 2 Tim 2:25); for all men (1 Tim 2:1-6; 4:10; Titus 2:11); America like pagan Rome; mission evangelism not moral legislation, change from inside out.
56-25, He Saved Us (3:4-7), 1993-08-15: sozo (Matt 8:25; Acts 2:47); Youth for Christ rally, kept the word "saved"; v3 depravity (anoetos; Rom 1:18-32; Eph 2:1-3; 4:17-19); Savior twice in each chapter; goal heirs (Rom 8:17; 1 Pet 1:4); sevenfold operation: kindness (chrestotes; Luke 6:35; Rom 2:4; Calvin: God finds nothing in us to love), love (philanthropia; Acts 28:2; Lam 3:22; Luke 15:20), mercy (addresses misery, grace addresses guilt; Phil 3 skubalon; 1 Tim 1:12-13), regeneration (palingenesia = new birth, called a washing of the dead corpse; John 3; agent the Word, Jas 1:18; 1 Pet 1:23; no mention of baptism or water), the Spirit (radical renewal; 2 Cor 5:17; Acts 8 Simon; poured richly), the Son (Acts 2; eternal covenant), grace (justified in broad sense; Calvin quoted on extending beyond imputation); believers not smug toward pagan culture; Spurgeon on the footsteps of mercy ordained.
56-26, The Last Word on Relationships (3:9-15), 1993-08-22: Titus "condensed epistle", key 2:10; three relationships (ch1 God and leaders, ch2 one another, ch3 world); four groups: false teachers (shun: turn your back; foolish = moron; 1 Tim 6:3-5; 2 Tim 2:14-23; genealogies allegorized, Jubilees, Haggada, Irenaeus, Eusebius; seminary professor would not argue with error), factious people (hairetikos from "choose", Gal 5:20; Lenski; noutheteo first and second warning, Matt 18:15-17; 2 Thess 3:14; Rom 16:17-18; restorative; unity John 13:35; v11 ekstrepho turned inside out, present "sinning", autokatakritos), fellow servants (Artemas, Tychicus Acts 20; Col 4:7; Eph 6:21; Nicopolis founded by Octavian after Actium, Zahn; Zenas only Christian lawyer named; Apollos Acts 18; help "diligently"), faithful friends (good deeds 3:1, 8; 2:14; Matt 5:16; love; grace with you all).
