# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Galatians 5 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles, ranges and dates from gty series pages https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/48v3/galatians-vol-03 and https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/48v4/galatians-vol-04.
48-27@5, A Perfect Portrait of Saving Faith, Part 2 (4:21-5:1; here 5:1), 2018-01-21: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-27. 5:1 stand firm; freedom from moral law's condemnation and ceremonial law; John 8:36. Code also used for Galatians 4 (21-31).
48-28, The Dangers of a Distorted Gospel (5:1-6), 2018-02-04: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-28. Acts 15:1, 5, 10; enecho; behold; no hybrid; marturomai; severed; fallen out of realm of grace, warning to unconverted (Heb 6:4-6); you to we; paideuo (Titus 2:11-12; Heb 12:5-11); Rom 11:6; James 2:10; John 8:36.
48-29, The Wickedness of Gospel Distorters (5:7-12), 2018-02-11: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-29. Race; obey the truth = believe (Rom 2:8; 6:17; 16:26; 2 Thess 1:8; 1 Pet 1:22; Acts 6:7); effectual call (Rom 8); ecumenism; leaven (Matt 16:6, 12; 1 Cor 5:6; 2 Tim 2:17); disturbing; 2 Pet 2:1, 3; Jude 11-15; Timothy Acts 16; Stephen Acts 6:13-14; Chrysostom; Gal 6:12; Cybele, Phrygia.
48-31, How Christian Freedom Works (5:13-16), 2018-03-11: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-31. Opportunity military; freed from curse, penalty, shadows, not moral content (Col 2:16; Acts 10); douleuo; agape (Phil 2); Lev 19:18; Rom 13:8-10; Rom 5:5; daknete, analothete; seven questions (1 Cor 6:12; 8:9-13; 9:19; 10:23-33; Heb 12:1; 1 John 2:6); abstains from alcohol; Exod 20:2-6; John 15:14; Spurgeon holiness voluntary; John 8:34, 36.
48-33, The Believer's Lifelong Battle for Holiness (5:16-26; expounds 16-18), 2018-04-22: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-33. Step by step; Eph 5:18; Col 3:16; 2 Cor 3:18; 1 John 2:6; Rom 7:14-25; Rom 6:12; Heb 12:1; 1 John 1:8-10; James 3:2; Prov 20:9; law contributes nothing to sanctification (ceremonies); Luther, Warfield on Wesley, Finney, Ryle, Thomas Watson.
48-37, The Fruit of the Spirit, Part 2 (5:16-26; focus 22-23, peace), 2018-05-20: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-37. Bouquet; hagios 200+ times; saints (1 Cor 1:2; 1 Pet 2:9); Eph 5:18 wine in pagan temples; John 14:1, 27; Rom 14:17; 15:13; Mark 4:35-41; peacemaker (Rom 12:18; James 3:17-18; 1 Thess 5:13); Phil 4:5-7, 9; Col 3:15.
48-40, Restoring the Sinning Brother (5:26-6:6; here 5:26), 2018-10-07: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-40. 5:26 three prohibitions; kenodoxos; challenging = combat word; two groups; 5:13-15; 1 Thess 5:14; two walks; Rom 7:14-24. Code also used for Galatians 6 (1-6) as 48-40@6.
Not readable (robots): 48-30, 48-32, 48-34, 48-35, 48-36, 48-38, 48-39. No MacArthur page read covers 5:24-26 in detail.
