# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 1" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V1/john-vol-1), which lists code, title, passage and date. Sermon pages themselves show no title.
43-1, Declaring and Defending the Deity of Christ (John 1, registered 1:1-51), 2012-10-14: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-1. Introduction: Christ the theme of Scripture (John 5; Rev 19:10); love for Christ and the Word; true vs false Christ (2 John 7-11; 1 John 4); early heresies; John 20:31 purpose, apologetic and evangelistic.
43-2, Jesus: The Divine Word (1:1-5), 2012-10-21: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-2. ~90% unique; purpose 20:31; logos for Greeks and Jews (Heb 1:1-2); preexistence (imperfect "was", Ex 3; John 8:58); coexistence, against Sabellianism (Prov 8:27-30); self-existence, Creator uncreated (1 Cor 8:6; Heb 1:3); zoe; light; katalambano, darkness cannot overpower (Luke 22:53; Col 1:13).
43-3, Jesus: The Divine Light (1:6-13), 2012-11-04: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-3. Light makes visible; John sent (Isa 40; Mal; Luke 1; Matt 11:11); martureo courtroom witness; minister not priest/coach/therapist; luchnos vs phos (John 5:35); alethinos genuine; Ryle sun analogy; rejection shows depravity (Jer 17:9); receive = believe; notitia, assensus, fiducia; v13 not heritage, effort or religious system; only God gives new birth (Ezek 36-37; Jer 31).
43-4, Jesus: Glory, Grace, and God (1:14-18), 2012-11-11: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-4 (the /resources/ and gty.org/library forms were robots-blocked). sarx physical humanity (Rom 1:3); skenoo; hypostatic union; glory (Ex 33:18-23; 34:6-7; 40; 1 Kgs 8:11; transfiguration, 2 Pet 1); grace and truth together; v15 two witnesses (Deut 19:15); charin anti charitos (2 Cor 12:9; Heb 4:16); egeneto; OT saints saved by grace (Gen 6:8); kolpos; exegeomai.
43-5, The First Testimony Concerning Jesus, Part 1 (1:19-37), 2012-11-18: read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/43-5 (slug URL blocked). Three days: He is here / Behold Him / Follow Him; "the Jews" = hostile establishment; Elijah (Mal 4:5), the Prophet (Deut 18; Acts 3:22-23; 7:37); voice (Isa 40:3-5), level pride; proselyte baptism; sandal = lowest slave; Bethany beyond Jordan; humility of the faithful preacher.
43-6, The First Testimony Concerning Jesus, Part 2 (1:19-37), 2012-11-25: read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/43-6. Credentials of John; Elijah harmonized with Matt 17 (spirit and power now, literal return before second coming); voice prepares hearts; delegation left without excuse; Lamb (Gen 22; Ex 12; Isa 53), shock of a lamb for a king; oida; Spirit remaining; later doubt (Matt 11); following = staying with Him. Sermon continues to v42, but entries kept inside 19-37.
43-7, The Disciples' Testimony Concerning Jesus (1:38-51), 2012-12-02: read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/43-7. Unnamed disciple = John; tenth hour eyewitness; Messiah/Christos; Petros vs petra (Matt 16:18; Eph 2:20); "follow Me" continuous; Bethsaida; Nazareth and Joseph, virgin birth; Nathanael = Bartholomew from Cana (John 21:2); alethos (Rom 2); omniscience at the fig tree, Gill's Syriac tradition doubted; "Son of God" Hebraism for nature; Gen 28:10-17; Dan 7:13-14; seeking sinner, seeking Savior (John 15:16), seeking saint (Rom 10:14).
