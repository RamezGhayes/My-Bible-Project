# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 2 Thessalonians 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. The gty pages (https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>) give the transcript but no title or date. Titles and ranges from the Precept Austin link texts (verse range + title + gty code) at https://preceptaustin.org/2_thessalonians_commentaries (re-checked 2026-11-27) and https://www.preceptaustin.org/2-thessalonians-1-resources; no allowed source gives dates, so dates are empty; 53-5 and 53-6 titles also in gty URL slugs, 53-5 title also on its page. Range of 53-5 is 1:11 on Precept Austin.
53-1, A Church to Be Proud Of (1:1-5): churches boast of buildings, size, etc.; five marks: genuine conversion ("in" God, v1; Father and Son one source implies deity; Sardis, Laodicea Rev 3), growing faith (opheilomen = duty; huperauxanei; persecution destroys false faith, not true: Matt 13:20-21; Luke 22:32; 1 Pet 1:6-7; criticizes "forgive God" teaching), growing love (sacrificial; John 13:34-35), persevering hope (v4 emphatic "we ourselves", Prov 27:2; diogmoi, thlipseis; pistis = faithfulness), kingdom attitude (v5 endeigma = evidence, krisis; declared worthy, not earned; Matt 6:33; Leon Morris paraphrased: suffering shows God is with them). Background: Silas, Timothy (Acts 15-17); no apostolic title; Thessalonica on Egnatian Way, AD 50; three problems of letter (persecution, confusion on return perhaps forged letter, idleness).
53-2, The Vengeance of the Lord Jesus, Part 1 (1:6-10): return climax of history; parousia (for believers, arrival of one known) vs apokalupsis (unveiling to the world, v7); Christ hidden now, veiled at first coming; false messiahs; "from heaven" (Acts 1:9-11; Heb 8:1; Matt 24:30); "angels of His power" (Ps 68:17; Mark 8:38); flaming fire = Shekinah glory (Ex 3:2; Ex 19), distinct from judgment fire (2 Pet 3:10); Paul ascribes to Jesus what OT ascribes to God (deity); relief (anesis, loosening a bowstring) and retribution; God's vengeance = settled action of perfect holiness (Isa 59:17; 66:15; Rom 3:5), human vengeance forbidden (Matt 5:38-48); defends imprecatory psalms against Wenham's report and Wansey; Jesus too speaks of judgment (Matt 3:12; John 5:22-30).
53-3, The Vengeance of the Lord Jesus, Part 2 (1:6-10): reasons the return is certain; two purposes; Matt 13:40-42; 24:30-31; retribution: why (just; apodidomi; Isa 45:20-25; Rom 1:18; Luke 13:1-5), who (persecutors, Gen 12:3, Matt 18:6; those who do not know God, relational knowledge John 17:3, Matt 7:23; gospel is a command, Acts 17:30-31, Heb 10:26-31; religion as damning illusion), how (thlipsis 2 Cor 7:5; aionios = endless; olethros = ruin not annihilation; Mark 9; Matt 25; Luke 16; separation from presence; eternal loneliness); Spencer Nielsen newsletter illustration.
53-4, The Vengeance of the Lord Jesus, Part 3 (1:6-10): relief = rest: salvation rest (Matt 11:28-29; Heb 4:9), millennial rest (Acts 3:19-21; Ps 2; literal thousand-year kingdom), eternal rest (Rev 14:13); relief just because of substitution (Rom 3), Tozer on justice; reward proportional to suffering (Matt 20; Rom 8:17-18; 2 Cor 4:17; 2 Tim 3:12); professing believer who never suffers may not be a Christian; hotan (whenever); glorified in saints, transparent glass (1 John 3:2; Phil 3:21); "belief for relief, unbelief for retribution".
53-5, Praying for the Right Things, Part 1 (1:11): shallow prayers (Jas 4:3); three wishes; Chekhov "The Bet"; three requests: worthiness, fulfilment, power; prayer as resource; Acts 6:4 shepherd prays and teaches; sovereignty and responsibility both (Job 42:2; Prov 16:33; Isa 46:9-11; 1 Sam 12:19-23; Rom 9-10; Luke 22:31-32; Isa 38); T. C. Hammond; future hope produces holy life now (2 Pet 3:8-14; 1 John 3:3); Pony Express.
53-6, Praying for the Right Things, Part 2 (1:11-12): cargo cults vs health-and-wealth; prayer as continual condition, lava image; calling = effectual (Rom 8:30; 11:29; 1 Thess 2:12), distinct from Matt 22:14; positional and practical worthiness (Eph 4:1; Phil 1:27; Col 1:10); good desires by God's definition (Ps 37:4; 145:16); work of faith with power (Jas 2; Eph 3:16); "name" an OT title of God, so Jesus is God (Isa 42:8); hypocrisy as excuse; reciprocity; Barclay on teacher's glory; grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ supports deity.
