# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 2 (Grace to You)
Titles/ranges/dates: monergism list https://www.monergism.com/book-acts-mp3-series-0 (1972 series, ascending, no codes). Codes matched by reading each gty page and checking its passage against the list (1700-1702 slugs confirmed on preceptaustin; the Acts 2 codes not confirmed by a titled link: single source for titles). Read 2026-11-27.
BLOCKED (robots, www and non-www): 1703 (= The Baptism of the Holy Spirit, Part 1, 2:1-4, 1972-05-21 by list order), 1704 (Part 2, 2:5-13, 1972-05-28), 1707 (Peter's Sermon: Exalting Christ, Part 2, 2:24-36, 1972-06-18). So no MacArthur entries for 2:1-13 and 2:24-36. His view on tongues therefore not reported here.
1705, Peter's Sermon: Explaining Pentecost (2:14-21), 1972-06-04. Read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1705 (passage 2:14-21 matches). Priority of preaching (Acts 4:2; 8:5; Luke 4:16-19; 2 Tim 4); kerusso, kerugma, didache; Broadus; Stott Preacher's Portrait; Whitefield. 14: standing with eleven, Matthias completes twelve; lifted voice; authority "thus says the Lord"; Aramaic; crowd est. 100,000-200,000; Peter who denied. 15: third hour, Jews did not eat or drink before it on feast days. 16: this is that, Joel 2:28-32. 17: last days = Messianic times began with Christ (1 Pet 1:20; Heb 1:2; 1 John 2:18; Heb 9:26); prophets saw no gap (Isa 9:6; Dan 9), church a mystery (Eph 3); Feinberg "prefillment"; 17-18 visions belong to millennial kingdom, skeptical of vision claims. 19-20: signs of judgment (Rev 6, 8, 9, 14, 16; Matt 24:29), "terrible" day; darkness at cross preliminary. 21: call on name. Sermon structure; kerugma six features.
1706, Peter's Sermon: Exalting Christ, Part 1 (2:22-23), 1972-06-11. Read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1706. Life (22): men of Israel; Nazareth despised (John 1:46); apodeiknumi = exhibit, prove, appoint (1 Cor 4:9; Acts 25:7; 2 Thess 2:4); dunameis, wonders, semeia (road sign); John 5; 10:37-39; 11:47-48; 14:10-11; 15:22-25; Nicodemus; rejection from love of sin not lack of evidence. Death (23): delivered, determinate counsel, foreknowledge = foreordination (1 Pet 1:20; Rom 11:2; 1 Pet 1:2), reckless driver illustration; instigators and executioners; sovereignty and responsibility (John 19:11; Acts 4:26-28; Luke 22:22); conviction of greatest sin (John 16:8-9). Outline of 24-36 only as headings.
1708, Peter's Sermon: The Appeal and Results (2:37-42), 1972-07-02. Read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1708. False answers (legalists etc.). 37: pricked (only NT use, pierce suddenly); grief, guilt, fear (footstool v35), helplessness; Acts 9:6; 16:30; Zech 12:10; 13:1; Heb 4:12. 38: metanoeo 180 turn, aorist; dread sin itself; baptism public break, Ryrie; eis = "because of" (Matt 12:41), not in order to; dorea free gift, Spirit at salvation (1 Cor 12:13; Acts 10; John 14); rich ruler barrier (Luke 18). 39: "you" hearers, far off = Gentiles (Eph 2:11-13); divine and human side (v21); did not expand on "your children". 40: many words; "save yourselves" passive; crooked generation, AD 70. 41: 3000 gladly received, baptized same day. 42: continued (John 8:30-31; 6:66); Guayaquil anecdote. Baptism follows salvation promptly.
1709, The First Fellowship (2:42-47), 1972-07-09. www page read (non-www blocked). Content, character, consequences. Saved church (John 15; 8:31 alethos; 1 John 2:19; 1 Thess 1; Rev 2:14-16 Balaam, Num 22-25; 2 Cor 6:14); studying (didache; 2 Tim 2:2, 15; 1 Pet 2:2; Rom 12:2; Hos 4:6); fellowship positional and experiential (1 John 1:3-4), ekklesia, Heb 10:24-25, breaking bread = Lord's Table (1 Cor 10:16-17), love feast; praying (John 14:13-14). Awe (Luke 7:16); miracles (Acts 3; 5:12-16; 9) to confirm word (Heb 2:3-4), public sign miracles ceased; sharing: imperfect tense of sold/parted, as needs arose, not commune, pilgrims stayed, homes kept (v46), Acts 5 lie; 2 Cor 8:14-15; 1 John 3:16-18; 1 Pet 4:9; joy, singleness of heart. Attractive (John 13:35; 17:21), Aristides apology; growing: Lord adds.
