# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 28 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes and titles from the gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-28-resources ("The Last Lap", "The Story That Never Ends"; slugs the-last-lap, the-story-that-never-ends); link text has no verse range, ranges from the sermon pages (1801: 28:1-16; 1802: 28:17-31). 1801 robots-blocked at /library/sermons-library/, read at /resources/sermons/1801. 1802 read at library URL; its opening calls the message "The Story That Has No End" while preceptaustin and the slug say "The Story That Never Ends" (registered with the latter). No dates on pages (1802 mentions February 1975 but no date shown); dates omitted.
1801 (1-16): God fulfills desires of those who fear Him (Ps 37:4; 145:19); v1-2 Malta = refuge, Luke may play on it; barbarous = non-Greek-speaking; hospitality (1 Pet 4:9; Heb 13:2; Rom 12:13); law written on hearts (Rom 2:14-15); v3 servant leader (Mark 10:45; John 13:14-15); v3-6 "fastened" medical word, truly venomous; Dike Justice, pagan moral sense (Rom 1:18-19); Mark 16:18, Luke 10:19 signs for apostolic era, not snake-handling; Rom 16:20; called god, contrast 14; v7-10 Publius title confirmed by inscriptions; prayer then hands, God the source; Paul likely preached; tradition Publius first pastor; honors suggest believers; v11 Castor and Pollux, only identification; v12 Syracuse tradition; v14 brethren at Puteoli; v15 Forum of Appius, thanked God, courage (perhaps discouraged); Romans written ~3 years earlier; v16 own house, soldier; six blessings on faithfulness; Prov 28:20.
1802 (17-31): v17 Jew first (Rom 1:16), 7-12 synagogues; no animosity (Rom 9:1-3; 10:1); v18-19 innocent, nothing to accuse nation of; v20 hope of Israel = Messiah and resurrection (23:6; 24:15; 26:6-7; Isa 26:19; Dan 12:2), chain (Eph 6:20); v21-22 no letters, perhaps first ship, no case; v23 kingdom of God = God's rule, narrowed to Jesus Messiah; law and prophets; v24 imperfect: kept believing / kept disbelieving; division (14:4; 17:4-5); v25-27 Isa 6:9-10 also in Matt 13, John 12; John 12:37, 39 (judicial hardening, harsh framing left out); Rom 9-11 rejection not permanent, olive tree warns Gentiles against pride, Israel grafted back; v28 turn to Gentiles (13:46-47; 15:14-18); v30-31 open ending deliberate, church continues the story; likely released after two years (his conviction, not certain); Colossians, Philemon, Ephesians, Philippians; evangelism: anywhere, lovingly, biblically, boldly, to all, Christ the fulfillment, division expected.
