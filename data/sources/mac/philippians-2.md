# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Philippians 2 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles/ranges from gty sermon links with titles at https://www.preceptaustin.org/philippians_2_resources . No dates shown on any page read (date ""). gty pages show no title header; where the speaker names the title it is noted.
50-12, The Motives for Spiritual Unity (2:1-2) [Precept Austin; speaker speaks of "the formula for spiritual unity"]: healthy church yet discord concern; motives/marks/means; first-class conditionals; paraklesis, paramythion, koinonia, splanchna, oiktirmos; ingratitude to Christ and Spirit; John 13:35; 17; Eph 4:3; Barclay danger of healthy church; marbles vs magnet.
50-13, The Marks of Spiritual Unity (2:2) [Precept Austin only]: attitude problem; sin against Christ, Spirit, leaders; phroneo; sumpsuchos hapax; Trinity unity; flesh vs Spirit; mind of Christ via Word and Spirit (Col 3:16; Eph 5:18).
50-14, The Means to Spiritual Unity (2:3-5) [Precept Austin only]: five principles; eritheia (Gal 5:20; Aristotle); kenodoxia; tapeinophrosune; hegeomai, 1 Tim 1:15; Alexander Whyte; others' interests; Christ model.
50-15, The Model of Spiritual Unity (2:5-8) [Precept Austin only]: primarily ethical; morphe nature, isos; emptied = set aside some privileges: glory (John 17:4-5), independent authority (Heb 5:8; John 5:30), some use of divine attributes (Matt 24:36), riches (2 Cor 8:9), favorable relation to Father (Matt 27:46; 2 Cor 5:21); did not cease to be God; real servant (Luke 22:27); Calcutta slum man.
50-16, Humiliation of Christ (2:5-8) [Precept Austin link text only]: theological; descent; morphe/schema; isos; kenoo; deity texts (John 1; 5:18; 8:58; 20:28; Col 1:15; Heb 1:3); emptied of glory, independent authority, free exercise of attributes, riches; not emptied of deity, "never ceased to be God", not exchange; C.S. Lewis Miracles; borrowed boat, animal, tomb.
50-17, Exaltation of Christ, Part 1 (2:9) [Precept Austin only]: resurrection, ascension, coronation, intercession; humility->exaltation principle; echarisato; huper; name = Lord (kurios) = sovereign rule; God alone makes Him Lord; Hendriksen, Flavel, Tozer.
50-18, Exaltation of Christ, Part 2 (2:10-11) [Precept Austin only]: gospel incomplete without exaltation; hina; exomologeo; glossa; Isa 45:23; compelled submission (2 Thess 1:7; Matt 25:41); John 5:23; Henry Martyn; hospital crucifixes.
50-19, God at Work in You, Part 1 (2:12) [Precept Austin only]: quietism vs pietism; not work for/at/up; mining (Strabo, Spain); perseverance (Col 1:23; Heb 3:14); "all of God and all of me"; rejects Hannah Smith potter analogy.
50-20, God at Work in You, Part 2 (2:12) [Precept Austin only]: "so then" from 5-11; present imperative; "your own" emphatic; five points; fear and trembling = godly awe, Wardlaw "self-distrust"; Isa 66:2; C.T. Studd candle.
50-21, God at Work in You, Part 3 (2:13) [Precept Austin only]: personal God vs Brahman; energeo, thelo, eudokia; person, power, presence, purpose, pleasure; holy discontent/aspiration/resolve/work; mystery; rejects quietism and pietism; Griffith Thomas, T.C. Hammond; Hezekiah.
50-22, Stop Complaining, Part 1 (2:14) [Precept Austin + speaker: "I titled the message, Stop Complaining"]: goggusmos emotional, dialogismos intellectual; Adam; wilderness (Ex 14-17; Num 11-17; 1 Cor 10:10-11); Lam 3:39; complaint aimed at God.
50-23, Stop Complaining, Part 2 (2:15-16) [Precept Austin + page presents it as second message "Stop Complaining"] (resources URL; library URL blocked): three reasons; Lasch narcissism; amemptos, akeraios, amomos; skolios, diestrammene (Deut 32:5); holding forth = offering; pastor's sake (Heb 13:17; 3 John 4); Meyer, Heine.
50-24, Model Spiritual Servants: Paul (2:17-18) [Precept Austin + page mentions series "Model Spiritual Servants"]: sacrificial rejoicer; drink offering (2 Kings 16:13; Jer 7:18; Hos 9:4); spendomai present = ongoing life; first-class "since"; leitourgia; Meyer "no heart pangs, no spiritual seed"; intimacy with Christ (Phil 3:10; 4:13).
50-25, Model Spiritual Servants: Timothy (2:19-24) [Precept Austin + page]: single-minded sympathizer; Brooks example; isopsuchos; dokime; merimnao; teknon; sovereignty over plans; Christ vs Paul as models; Demas; Calvin quoted.
50-26, Model Spiritual Servants: Epaphroditus (2:25-30) [Precept Austin + page]: loving gambler; godly, servant, courageous, loyal; adelphos, sunergos, sustratiotes, apostolos (church messenger), leitourgos; gambling verb; Carthage plague AD 252, Cyprian, parabalani; Jim Elliot. (The fetch summary also had an unclear remark on astheneo; not used.)
Other gty sermons on Phil 2 listed by Precept Austin (2003, 80-205, 90-280, 80-59) are outside the 50- series; not read.
