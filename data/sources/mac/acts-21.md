# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 21 (Grace to You, 1972-73 Acts series)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles and ranges from the titled gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-21-resources (link text + matching gty URL slugs). No dates shown on the pages; dates omitted. All readable at https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/<code> (non-www and /library forms for 1781 robots-blocked).
1781, The Courage of Conviction, Part 1 (21:1-6; slug the-courage-of-conviction-part-1; page heading "The Courage of Conviction"). Four marks of courage: knows purpose, cannot be diverted, pays any price, affects others; this one covers first two. Purpose: deliver collection, unify church. v4 "through the Spirit" = exercise of prophecy gift to be tested, not divine prohibition; Paul obedient (sensitive to Spirit, pure motives, warnings = preparation, clear conscience later). v5 kneeling on beach. Without goals, little need for courage.
1782, The Courage of Conviction, Part 2 (21:7-9; slug ...-part-2-singlehearted-purpose; page heading "The Courage of Conviction"). Page actually reviews 20:22-24 and 21:1-15: levels of commitment (incomplete: Demas; insincere: Peter, Ananias and Sapphira; intermittent: Ephesus), God's standard Luke 9:23; John Mark contrast; Ptolemais one day; Philip faithful in small, once Saul's target, hosting Paul = transformation; daughters' gift revelatory (speculative), Papias; courage is trust.
1783, The Courage of Conviction, Part 3 (21:10-16; slug ...-part-3-paying-any-price; no title on page). Agabus NT prophet, practical revelation; object lessons like OT prophets (1 Kgs 11, Isa 20, Jer 13, Ezek 4-5). v12-13 loved ones can deter from God's will; settled readiness; plans for Rome and Spain; suffering endured with sound doctrine of God (Heb 12; Rom 8:28); worry (Matt 6). v14 Lord's Prayer, Gethsemane. v15 courage contagious; Moody illustration (not used).
1784, Paul's Arrest, Part 1 (21:17-26; slug ...-part-1-the-measure-of-humility; no title on page). Mnason; Pentecost arrival; welcome; elders replace apostles; Paul credits God = humility. v20 tens of thousands, genuine believers. v21 rumors false (he attributes them to Judaizers and Satan: left out). v22-26 method adjustment on non-essential, not compromise; 1 Cor 9; disagrees with G. Campbell Morgan. Humility: before God, before church authority, under persecution.
1785, Paul's Arrest, Part 2 (21:27-40; slug ...-part-2-positive-testimony-in-negative-situation; page heading "How to Give a Positive Testimony in a Negative Situation"). First of six defenses; imprisonment ordained by Christ; Trophimus assumption; mobs at Stephen's and Jesus' trials; two chains fulfil Agabus; Egyptian; accept situation from God, use it to witness (1 Peter).
Left out: Satan/Judaizers remark (harsh), Moody and football illustrations.
