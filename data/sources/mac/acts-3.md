1710, A Miracle to Confirm the Word (3:1-10; page treats 1-11), 1972-08-20. Read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1710 (speaker says it is 1972). Sign gifts confirm word (2 Cor 12:12; Heb 2:3-4; 2:43); apostolic healing ceased with apostles (Eph 2:20); counterfeit healings (Matt 7; 2 Thess 2), Unger, examples; Isa 53:5 spiritual; pray "if God wills". 1: Peter and John; "went up" imperfect = habit; three prayer hours (Dan 6:10; Ps 55:17); not yet broken with temple (Luke 24:53; 2:46). 2: lame from birth, imperfect; over 40 (4:22); beggars' places (Luke 16; Mark 10), temple gate best; Josephus: gate 75 ft, gold and Corinthian brass, 20 men to shut. 3: asked mercy, got grace. 4: fastened gaze, same word as 1:10. 6: name = character, authority, power; representative. 7: medical terms; instant creation (John 11:43; 5:8). Four marks: unexpected (Eph 3:20), in Christ's name, instant, complete. 8-10: joy, praise (worship from joy), testimony (4:16; Isa 35:6 lame leap as hart). 11: Solomon's porch (John 10).
