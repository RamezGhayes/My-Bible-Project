# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 19 (Grace to You)

1770, From Judaism to Jesus (page shows no title; speaker presents it as the third part of the study on the transition from Judaism to Jesus begun in 1768/1769, which carry that title; single-source title, flag). Treats 19:1-7: disciples of John, not yet Christians; mathetes = learner; "received the Spirit when you believed" = at faith, no second blessing; not re-baptism; laying on of hands not a special rite; last such case, transitional sign; every believer receives Spirit at salvation (Rom 8:9; 1 Cor 6:19; 12:13); Acts 8, 10 transitional; doctrine from epistles.
1771, Exorcism: True or False? Part 1 (preceptaustin acts-19 link text + gty slug). Treats 19:8-10: boldly, reasoning, persuading (imperfect); kingdom = Christ; hardening gradual; "the Way" exclusive; Tyrannus, variant 5th-10th hour; two years, all Asia, Rev 2-3 churches; Word saturation.
1772, Exorcism: True or False? Part 2 (preceptaustin link text + slug). Treats 19:11-20: miracles authenticate before NT; soudaria sweat cloths; Luke distinguishes disease/demons; exorkistes only here; frauds using name as formula; demon knew Jesus; fear, name magnified; confession of spells; burning imperfect; value; "word grew" key; authority not ritual; critiques modern deliverance; believers: confession, holiness, Word.
