# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 16 (Grace to You)

2318, The Church that Christ Builds, Part 1 (16:13-20): certainty, intimacy (Acts 20:28), continuity; ekklesia/qahal, Acts 7:38, Heb 12:22-23; Acts 2:47, 13:48, Acts 9; Rome vs Protestant positions summarized.
