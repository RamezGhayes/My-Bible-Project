# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 7 (Grace to You)
Series page: https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7345/romans-vol-05 (codes, titles, ranges, dates).
45-50, Dead to the Law (7:1-6), 1983-02-13: ch7 answers 6:14; third fruit = liberty; exalts law (Ps 19:7-10; Ps 119; Isa 42:21); v1 anarthrous law; dead driver; marriage analogy not allegory, not divorce teaching (1 Cor 7:39); first husband = law, new = risen Christ (Rom 6:9); "become dead" aorist passive; body of Christ = crucifixion (2 Cor 5:21; Gal 3:13; 2:19-20); fruit (Gal 5:22-23); Hodge; flesh physical vs ethical (John 1:14; Rom 8:4-9); passions energized; v6 delivered, douleuo (Wuest), kainos; rabbis Rabbah, Judah.
45-51A, Sin and the Law, Part 1 (7:7-13), 1983-02-20: transcript only covers v7: law cannot save/sanctify/condemn believers but convicts; 613 commandments; Deut 27:26; Gal 3:10; James 2:10; me genoito; moral law; Rom 3:20; 4:15; 5:13; "I" = Paul's own pre-conversion conviction (Phil 3:5-8; Gal 1:13-14; Rom 10:3); epithumia, tenth commandment internal.
45-51B, Sin and the Law, Part 2 (7:7-13), 1983-02-27: Hodge law "arouses sin"; Matt 5 inward; Matt 22:37-39; aphorme military base; Gal 3:21; Bruce sin the villain; Murray; Bunyan Interpreter's dusty room; v9 alive = complacent; Mark 2:17; Rom 5:6; v10 law for life, Rom 8:4; v11 deceit (Eph 4:22; Heb 3:13); v12 holy, just, good; Ps 19:7-11; v13; Luke 18:13; Gal 3:19-24 tutor; M'Cheyne; Ps 119:11.
45-52, The Believer and Indwelling Sin, Part 1 (7:14-17), 1983-03-06: "I" = mature regenerate Paul ("Paul at the very heights of his Christian perception"; "Don't tell me this man is not a Christian"); reasons: 7:22, 7:25, Rom 8:7, 3:10-18, aorist vs present tense shift, sin slew vs he fights; rejects unbeliever, low-level legalist, pre-conversion views; three laments (14, 18, 20) condition/proof/source; sarkinos; sold under sin vs Ahab (1 Kings 21) / 2 Kings 17; 1 Cor 3:1, 3; Ps 51:5; Isa 6; v15 will frustrated; v17 de ouketi, Gal 2:20; sin survives not reigns; Cranfield; Thomas Scott; Pogo; Jer 17:9; Gal 5:16-17.
45-53, The Believer and Indwelling Sin, Part 2 (7:18-25), 1983-03-13: flesh = unredeemed humanness; can do some good but not fully; de ouketi; sensitivity grows (Ps 32; 51); v21 law = principle, no eradication; inward man renewed (2 Cor 4:16; Eph 3:16); Ps 119 parallel ending 119:176; Ps 1:2; members incl. mind; only regenerate taken captive; 2 Cor 10:3-4; v24 strongest lament; Haldane; body of death = body subject to sin and death; Tarsus corpse practice; deliver = rescue comrade; glorification (Rom 8:18-25; 1 Cor 15:54-57; 2 Cor 5:4; Phil 3:12); Tennyson.
