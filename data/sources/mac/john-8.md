# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 8 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Transcript pages mostly show no title. gty series page 43V4 robots-blocked; Precept Austin John 8 page scanned (0-700k chars) for gty links.
43-44 (8:12-21): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-44. No title found, so NOT registered, no entries. Relevant for the textual question: he says 7:53-8:11 is absent from the oldest manuscripts, appears in later ones in varying places (even in Luke), compares Mark 16:9ff, says it should not be read as original or inspired text, notes it interrupts 7:52 -> 8:12, but grants it fits Jesus' character and may have circulated orally; he skips it (treated only in his study Bible notes and commentary). This could not be shown on the site because the sermon is unregistered. (Other content: treasury in Court of Women; light of the world, Isa 42; 49; Ps 27:1; illumination ceremony; two witnesses; John 12:35-43.)
43-45 (8:21-25 with v30): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-45. The speaker says "if he had titled it" it would be "How to Die in Your Sins" (hypothetical, not a title); NOT registered, no entries. (Four ways to die in sins: self-righteousness, worldliness, faithlessness, obstinacy.)
43-46, The Freedom of True Discipleship (8:31-36): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/43-46 (library form robots-blocked). Title from Precept Austin John 8 link (gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-46/the-freedom-of-true-discipleship) and a MacArthur/GTY "Featured Sermon" post republished on blogs.crossmap.com (2022-09-23) giving title, John 8:31-36 and the gty link. Date unknown. alethos mathetai; same "believers" called devil's children (v44); Matt 7:21-23; soils; 1 John 2:19; perseverance/obedience as benchmark (not perfect); faith a gift that cannot be destroyed (Eph 2:8-9); Ryle; Kafka bathtub (2 Tim 3:7); truth in Christ (John 14:6); Luke 4:16-30; slave vs son, Gal 3-4; freedom from power, penalty, presence of sin.
43-47, False Assurance of the Religious (8:37-47), 2014-04-13: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-47; title, range and date as reported on the page (one source). Two families; bluntness as mercy; Rom 1-3; Lloyd-Jones; three claims (physical seed true; spiritual seed false; children of God false); rabbinic saying on Abraham at Gehenna's gate; Justin, Trypho; Abraham's deeds = faith (Gen 15:6; Gal 3), Gen 18, Gen 22; chōreō "no place"; unbelief -> ignorance (1 Cor 2:14); devil murderer/liar (Gen 3); Emperor of All Maladies; no one convicts him of sin; tests of God's children (Matt 13:38; 1 John 3:1-10).
43-48, Jesus Antagonizes the Enemies (8:48-59): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-48. Title: speaker says on the page he titled it so (one source). Date unknown. Ten assaults in ch. 8; antagonizing as mercy; four stages of conflict; three cycles blasphemy/truth/invitation; Samaritans (2 Kgs 17); no retaliation (1 Pet 2:23); never see death = second death (Rev 20-21); Abraham's promise (Gen 12; 15; Heb 11:13); not fifty (Luke 3:23); I AM (Exod 3:14; John 1:1); stoning (Lev 24:16); invitation ends here. He also calls devout Judaism that rejected Christ satanic in this sense; not used in entries.
No MacArthur entry for 8:1-30: 7:53-8:11 not preached; 43-44, 43-45 untitled.
Theology notes: none needed; perseverance as mark of true faith (43-46) matches the Reformed reading.
