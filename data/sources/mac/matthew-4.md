# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 4 (Grace to You)
2191 date from https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7001/matthew-vol-01 . Dates for 2192-2196 not available (Matthew Vol. 02 series page robots-disallowed; sermon pages show no date), so "date" omitted for them.
2191, The Crisis of Temptation, Part 1 (listed 4:1; content covers 4:1-3a), 1978-05-14: baptism proclamation, temptation proof; victory then vulnerability (1 Kings 18-19); led/driven (Mark 1:12); peirazo test vs tempt (James 1:2, 13; Gen 50:20); last Adam garden vs wilderness (Deut 8:15; Mark 1:13; George Adam Smith); real temptation, no sin (Heb 4:15; 2:18; 2 Cor 5:21; 1 Pet 2:22); forty days, Moses, Elijah, Paul in Arabia; Satan personal; Toynbee; Luther ink bottle.
2192, The Crisis of Temptation, Part 2 (4:1-11): bread = distrust of Father's care, Deut 8:3, manna, Adam's fruit; Matt 6:33; Gethsemane; pinnacle (Josephus height), Ps 91 misused, Deut 6:16, Theudas, the Egyptian, Barclay; sensation proves nothing; Ps 2:8 kingdoms without cross; 1 John 2:15-16 mapping; angels minister; three attack areas; 1 Cor 10:13; Jerome vs Jovinian.
2193, The Light Dawns, Part 1 (4:12-17): year between 4:11 and 4:12 from John 1:19-4:54; God's timing; Isa 9:1-2; light theme Mal 4:2; John 1:5; 3:19-21; 8:12; 2 Cor 4:6; 1 Pet 2:9; Prov 4:18-19; Luke 3:19; 4:16-31.
2194, The Light Dawns, Part 2 (4:12-25 unit, focus 4:12-17): Mark 1:14; Galilee size, Josephus 204 villages; Capernaum Matt 9:1, 9; 11:23; Galilee of the Gentiles mocking, 2 Kings 15; Isa 8:19-22; 9:1-7; Isa 60:1-3; Luke 2:25-32; John 7:40-52; repentance 2 Tim 2:25; kingdom postponed outwardly; preaching with authority Matt 7:28-29.
2195, Fishing for Men (4:18-22): evangelism the church's task; phases of call (John 1; Luke 5:1-11; Mark 3:14; Matt 10:1); ordinary men (1 Cor 1:26-28; Acts 4:13; Broadus); fishing methods; disciples' weaknesses; qualities of fishers; Luke 19:10; Prov 11:30; Dan 12:3; Brainerd, Martyn, M'Cheyne; Moody lower lights.
2196, The Healing Work of Jesus (4:23-25): teaching, preaching, healing; Matt 5-7 words, 8-9 works; synagogue, Neh 8; Hendriksen; gospel of the kingdom (Col 1:13; 1 Cor 15:1-4); diseases, demoniacs, epileptics (Matt 17:15), paralytics; purposes of miracles (John 14:11; Matt 11:2-5; Isa 35:5-6; Matt 12:28; Heb 6:5); six features; Warfield; McLaren; cessation argument.
