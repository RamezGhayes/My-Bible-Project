53-7, The Coming Man of Sin, Part 1 (2:1-5), 1992-02-23: titles of the man (vv3, 8, 9); one final individual and a present spirit (1 John 2:18, 22; 4:3; 2 John 7); anti = against and instead of; historical precursors (Gen 6, Athaliah, Antiochus, Herod); three issues of letter; Dan 9:24-27 seventy weeks, 69 weeks from Artaxerxes decree 444 BC to triumphal entry AD 33, 70th week future; prince from revived Roman empire; covenant with Israel, midpoint abomination (Matt 24:15) triggers Great Tribulation and Day of the Lord; rapture before the Day of the Lord (1 Thess 4:13-18).
53-8, The Coming Man of Sin, Part 2 (2:1-5), 1992-03-01: Daniel's little horn (Dan 7:8, 21, 25-26; 8:23-25; 11:36-45); Rev 13; Antiochus; Hitler as model ("100 Hitlers"); Day of the Lord (Isa 13:9; Joel 2:31; Zeph 1:14-15; 1 Thess 5:2); episunagoge with parousia under one article = rapture (Heb 10:25); 1 Thess 1:10; 5:9; de, erotomen = plea; enesteken = has arrived; three dia phrases, forged letters (3:17 signature); shaken like a ship broken loose; their fear shows they were taught removal before it.
53-9, The Coming Man of Sin, Part 3 (2:3-5), 1992-03-08: fake-rapture prank; exapatao (Matt 24:4; Eph 4:14); don't be forgetful, imperfect "I was telling"; Day of the Lord precursors Paul could have named; apostasia = deliberate abandonment (Josh 22:22 LXX); rejects general/church-age readings, "the" apostasy a specific event = the man's self-exaltation in the temple ("mother of all apostasies"); anomia reading; revealed = already exists hidden; rejects Antiochus, Caligula, Nero, Satan, principle; son of perdition as Judas (John 17:12); Zech 11:16-17; revived Rome; literal Jerusalem temple at midpoint of 70th week; 2 Thess 2 does not itself settle rapture timing; relies on 1 Thess 4-5; Rev 3:10.
53-10, The Coming Man of Sin, Part 4 (2:6-10), 1992-04-12: restrainer neuter then masculine = force exercised by a person = Holy Spirit (Gen 6:3; Acts 7:51; John 16); rejects gospel, Jewish state, church, government, Rome, Michael; restraint lifted at midpoint, not tied to rapture; Spirit not leaving the world; Antichrist only at God's time, after all elect born (Gal 4:4; Rom 11); criticizes postmillennial hastening; mysterion (Rom 16:25); breath of mouth (Isa 11:4; Ps 33:6); katargeo; epiphaneia of parousia; Rev 19-20; counterfeit Christ; energeia, dunamis, semeion, teras (Acts 2:22; Heb 2:4); pseudos = effect, real supernatural acts leading to lies; Matt 24:24; Hendriksen quoted.
53-11, The Coming Man of Sin, Part 5 (2:10-12), 1992-04-26: 1992 date-setting brochure; five admonitions; perishing lack love not information, moral not intellectual (John 3:19; 5:39-40; 8:24); "love of the truth" unique phrase, maybe Christ; deluding influence = God's judicial act, Satan instrument (1 Kings 22:23; Exod 9:12; Isa 6:9-10; Rom 1:24-28; Gen 6); in damnation God responds to human choice, in salvation man responds to divine choice; giving over.
53-12, The Coming Man of Sin, Part 6 (2:13-17), 1992-05-24: picketers "Jesus does not forgive sin" as antichrist spirit (2 John 7); final Antichrist (Rev 13); don't be insecure: pastor who said believer with unconfessed sin goes to hell; critiques Arminian perfectionism; golden chain: loved (uninfluenced love, Eph 1:3-4), chosen from beginning (Rev 13:8; 17:8; John 15:16; Phil 1:6), transformed (sanctification by Spirit, faith in truth, Rom 10; Acts 16:31), called (efficacious, irresistible grace, 1 Thess 2:13), glorified (v14; Rom 8); v15 paradosis = apostolic teaching now in Scripture vs Matt 15; Col 2:8 (1 Cor 11:2; 2 Tim 1:14); vv16-17 "Himself" singular for Christ and Father = deity and equality; sterizo; believer responds but God energizes.
