# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 13 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 6" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V6/john-vol-6); each sermon page also shows the same title.
43-67, The Savior's Love for His Own (13:1), 2015-01-11: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-67. Thursday of Passion Week, Passover setting (1 Cor 5:7); John slows the narrative; corrupt temple vs the "holy place" of Jesus with his own; ch. 13-16 promises, ch. 17 high-priestly prayer; love for "his own" described with many qualities; list of gifts in 13-17; Rom 8; 1 John 4:19.
43-68, The Humble Love of Christ (13:1-17), 2015-01-18: read at .../43-68. Love stated (v1), spurned (v2, Judas, John 6:70), shown (3-11), summoned (12-17); disciples arguing over greatness (Luke 22:24); humility and love proportionate; "no part" = salvation only by Christ's cleansing; bath = once-for-all salvation, feet = daily cleansing by confession (1 John 1:9); Judas excepted; v17 "since you know... if you do"; Phil 2. Does not present foot-washing as a church ordinance; stresses the principle of humble practical service.
43-69, Unmasking the Traitor (13:18-30), 2015-01-25: read at .../43-69. Treason anticipated (18-19, Ps 41:9), announced (20-21), twelve astonished (22-25), traitor addressed (26-30); sovereignty without fatalism, Judas acted willingly; commission not cancelled by a hypocrite; morsel as honour; Satan's full takeover after a gradual descent; night as picture of eternal darkness; Ps 55; Zech 11; John 17:12; Gen 50:20.
43-70, Traits of a True Believer, Part 1 (13:31-38), 2015-02-01: read at .../43-70. Glory of the cross for Son and Father, then exaltation; "little children", cannot follow now (7:34; 8:21); why the command is "new" (rank quarrels, Christ's example, Spirit's new capacity); believer known by love not symbols; love for God's glory, for brothers, loyalty, humility, love for enemies; 2 Cor 13:5.
43-71, Traits of a True Believer, Part 2 (13:31-38), 2015-02-08: read at .../43-71. Regenerate heart (Ezek 36; John 3; Titus 3); love for the Lord's glory; love that serves, sanctifies (Matt 18), suffers (2 Cor 11-12); personal loyalty; assurance (1 John 3:18-19); Peter: self-ignorance, boasted too much, prayed too little, acted too fast, followed too far; restored in Galilee (John 21), steady afterward by the Spirit.
Blocked/untitled: none.
Theology notes: none needed (no ordinance view taken).
