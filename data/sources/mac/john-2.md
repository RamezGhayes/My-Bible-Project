# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 2 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 1" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V1/john-vol-1).
43-8, The Beginning of Miracles (2:1-11), 2012-12-09: read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-8 (/resources/ form robots-blocked). Start of public ministry, signs (20:30-31; 21:25); Cana ~9 miles from Nazareth, Nathanael from Cana; marriage common grace, not a sacrament (against Roman Catholic view); betrothal customs; wine diluted 3-10 parts water; Mary went to the wisest person she knew; Joseph probably dead (19:26-27); "woman" courteous, end of Mary's authority (Matt 12:46-50; Luke 11:27-28); "hour" = cross; v5 not a basis for Marian intercession; six jars 20-30 gallons each, for washing (Mark 7:3-4); filled to brim, servants as witnesses; architriklinos; created from nothing, instant; calls it pure unfermented wine bypassing the curse; three responses (2:11; 12:37; 12:42-43).
43-9, Zeal for My Father's House (2:12-17), 2012-12-16: read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/43-9. Capernaum, brothers did not yet believe (John 7; Matt 11); Passover (Ex 12; 23), Josephus ~250,000 animals; Court of the Gentiles, rejected animals, 10-12% exchange, "bazaars of Annas"; temple police, Antonia; expulsion a miracle of authority, no one hurt, no precedent for Inquisition; holy anger, preview of judgment (Isa 1:11-17; Ps 51:16-19); Ps 69:9, David type of Christ; two cleansings (also Matt 21); Lord's Table self-judgment (1 Pet 4:17; 1 Cor 11:27-31).
43-10, Deity on Display (registered 2:18-25), 2012-12-30: read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/43-10. gty series page lists 2:18-22, but the sermon itself expounds 2:23-25 at the end, so registered 18-25. Three confrontations in 2:12-25; focus on omniscience; sign demand (Matt 12:38); v19 future fact, first prediction of death and resurrection (Matt 12:39-40); Trinity raises Christ (Rom 1; 1 Cor 15); 46 years, Josephus; v22 Emmaus (Luke 24); pattern of misunderstanding (John 3; 6:51; 7:33-35; 8:51-53; Matt 26:60); God knows the future (Isa 41:21-22; Ps 139); vv23-25 same verb pisteuo: they believed, He did not entrust Himself; non-saving faith (Jas 2:19; Matt 7:21-23; John 8:31-32); 1 John 3:20; Peter in John 21.
