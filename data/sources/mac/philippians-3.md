# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Philippians 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles/ranges from gty sermon links with titles at https://www.preceptaustin.org/philippians_3_resources , plus what the gty pages show. No dates shown (date "").
50-27, The Distinctive Qualities of the True Christian, Part 1 (3:1-3) [title: gty page shows "The Distinctive Qualities of the True Christian"; part number from Precept Austin "Qualities of True Christian-Pt1"] (resources URL; library URL blocked): religious vs righteous; circumcision symbol of inner cleansing (Gen 17; Josh 5; Rom 2:25-29); heart circumcision; Talmud; Judaizers (Gal 1:7); divine accomplishment vs human achievement (Eph 2:8-9); Jehovah's Witnesses; India.
50-28, ... Part 2 (3:1-3) [same sources]: blepo x3; okneros; kuon vs kunarion; Talmud Gentiles dogs; peritome/katatome; three marks as objective tests; Acts 15:1-10; Gal 5:2-3, 12; counterfeit watches; Gardiner Spring; Meyer.
50-29, ... Part 3 (3:1-3, focus 2-3) [same sources]: gospel plus self-examination (Matt 7:21-23; 2 Cor 13:5); five things that do not verify salvation; Simon Magus, rich young ruler, Judas, ten virgins; kauchaomai.
50-30, ... Part 4 (3:1-3, focus 3) [part number from Precept Austin only; page shows no title] (resources URL; library URL blocked): latreuo; John 4; Matthew Mead The Almost Christian Discovered (1661); Meyer flesh = self-effort; natural conviction vs spiritual repentance; treasury of merit.
50-31, Religious Credits That Don't Impress God (3:4-8; content 4-7) [title as stated by the speaker on the gty page; Precept Austin link text is "Religious Credentials", range 3:4-8]: kerdos/zemia; hegeomai; skubalon; seven credentials; Matt 13:44-46; Matt 19; Benjamin (Judg 19-20; 1 Sam 9; Mordecai); Meyer; rejects household salvation and infant baptism.
50-32, Surpassing Value of Knowing Christ (3:8-11) [Precept Austin only]: inner account of conversion vs Acts 9; exchange (Matt 16:25-26); gnosis vs Gnostic; yada (Amos 3:2; John 17:3); skubalon, Isa 64:6; v9 total dependence, God accepts Christ in our place, He became sin so we receive His righteousness (word "imputation" not used); v10 Rom 6, Eph 3:20; v11 ek = out-resurrection, rapture and believers' resurrection distinct from others at end of millennium; "attain" humility; five gains identification, justification, sanctification, participation, glorification; Meyer.
50-33, Reaching for the Prize (3:12-16) [Precept Austin + gty URL slug "reaching-for-the-prize"] (library URL; slug URL blocked): perfection a goal; six principles; dioko, epekteinomai; 1 Cor 9:24-27; Heb 12:1-2; answering Gnostic/Judaizer perfection claims; rejects Wesleyan second work/sinless perfection; v15 "perfect" possibly sarcastic toward Judaizers; Kierkegaard; Alpine grave "He died climbing".
50-34, Following Godly Examples (3:17-21; this message v17) [Precept Austin only]: Christlikeness goal of sanctification; Scripture + Spirit; summimetes, skopeo/skopos; leaders as models; mountain guide vs helicopter; no sinless perfection or eradication.
50-35, Fleeing from Enemies (3:18-19) [Precept Austin + gty URL slug; page names series "Reaching for the Prize", Philippians Vol. 03] (resources URL without slug; slug and library URLs blocked): goal conformity not need-satisfaction; Tony Walter; Tozer "faith looks out instead of in"; two groups Judaizers and Gnostic-style libertines; telos; koilia; Acts 20:31; Bunyan muckraker.
50-36, Focusing on Expectations (3:20-21) [gty page header + Precept Austin + URL slug] (resources URL without slug): third element; politeuma hapax, registered colony; apekdechomai Second Coming; metaschematizo, summorphos (Rom 8:29; 1 John 3:2); intermediate state; Phocas; Sorokin.
Other gty sermons listed by Precept Austin for Phil 3 (90-65, 90-66) are outside the 50- series; not read.
