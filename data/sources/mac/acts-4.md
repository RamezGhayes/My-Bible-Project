Intro: 10,000-20,000 believers; John 15-16; subtle persecution of ego and acceptance; 2 Tim 3:12; Phil 1:29-30; not masochism; James 1:2-4, 12; 1 Pet 2:20-21; 4:12-16; 5:10; Rom 8:35-37; 2 Cor 12:9-10; Phil 2:17; Col 1:24 fill up afflictions; Mark 13:13; Gal 6:17. Principle 4 obey God (14-22): blindness of sin (John 3:19; 8:44-47); Luke 16 miracles do not convince; 15-17 private meeting, public image; 18 verb for speak rare, speak aloud publicly; 19-20 Rom 13:1, 1 Pet 2 limits; Dan 6; Ex 1:17-22; Acts 5:29; 1 Cor 9:16; 21-22 released for crowd; Chrysostom before Arcadius. Principle 5 bind together (23), cf 32-35. Principle 6 bless the Lord (24-28): despotes = absolute ruler; Creator; Ps 2:1-2 fulfilled in Herod, Pilate, Gentiles, Israel; v28 predetermined; Ps 76:10; Joseph Gen 50:20. Principle 7 boldness (29-31): not escape or revenge, "not an out but an in"; servants = bondslaves; place shaken, filled, spoke boldly; 32 one heart.
