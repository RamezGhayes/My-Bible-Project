# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 10 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26 with WebFetch. The gty transcript pages show no title or date; titles and ranges confirmed from the MacArthur sermon links on preceptaustin.org/luke-10-commentary (title + verse range + gty code). No dates found, so dates are empty.
42-134, Attitudes of Effective Evangelism (10:1-4; library/sermons-library/42-134): seventy are genuine disciples, not apostles; attitudes: compassion, prayer, urgency, vigilance, trust; harvest = coming judgment (Joel 3:12-13; Matt 13:30, 39-42; Rev 14:14-20); sovereignty does not lessen evangelism; Num 11; Eccl 4:9-12; Deut 19:15; Mark 6:7; Matt 9:35-38; Matt 10:16-22; 1 Cor 1:26-29; Luke 22:35-36; people saved by the gospel, not friendship. He notes healing and apostolic roles were unique to that era.
42-135, Responses to the Kingdom: Peace or Punishment (10:5-11): peace = core of message; "son of peace" Hebrew idiom (Barnabas "son of encouragement"), prepared heart like Simeon; returning peace = withdrawing the offer (Matt 10:13; 7:6); contentment vs false itinerants; worker's support (1 Tim 5:17-18; 1 Cor 9; Gal 6:6); food laws (Mark 7; Acts 10); healing delegated, perhaps temporary, authenticating messengers (Heb 2:3-4), today validated by Scripture; kingdom near = King has come, entry by submission; dust = public protest, final appeal (Luke 9:5; Matt 10:14).
42-136, A Warning to the Indifferent (10:12-16): "that day" = Great White Throne (Rev 20:11-15); degrees of punishment (Luke 12:47-48; Heb 10:26-29); Sodom; Tyre and Sidon (Ezek 28; Isa 23; Amos 1:9; Joel 3:6); Capernaum's indifference as damning as hostility; rejecting messengers = rejecting Christ and Father (Matt 10:40; John 5:22-27; 1 John 2:23); a warning should close an evangelistic conversation.
42-137, The Joys of a True Disciple (10:17-20): three reasons for joy: power over Satan's kingdom (Col 1:13; 1 John 5:19; Gen 3:15), protection (serpents/scorpions = demons; Rev 9; 12:9; Rom 16:20; 1 John 4:4; 2 Thess 3:3), preservation (book of life: Exod 32:32-33; Ps 69:28; Isa 4:3; Dan 12:1; Rev 3:5; 13:8; citizen registers); four views of Satan's fall (Isa 14:12; Luke 4; John 12:31; Rev 20), imperfect tense = ongoing watching during the mission.
42-138, What Makes Jesus Rejoice? Part 1 (10:21): only place Luke records Jesus rejoicing; agalliao = overjoyed; "Father" = shared essence (John 5:18); "Lord of heaven and earth" = sovereign; eudokia, mission not a failure; wisdom irrelevant to salvation, pride vs humility (1 Cor 1; 2:6, 9, 14; Rom 1:22); nepios = infants (Isa 57:15; 66:2; Prov 22:4); God determines to whom truth is revealed.

42-140, What Makes Jesus Rejoice? Part 3 (preceptaustin places the link under 10:25-29, but the transcript itself reads and expounds 10:21-24, so registered as 10:21-24): three sources of joy: Father's sovereign pleasure, election (Isa 43:10-11; 46:8-11; Dan 4:35; John 6:44-45; Rom 9:14-23; 2 Cor 4:4-6; Phil 2:13); Son's authority (John 14:6; Rev 3:7-8); disciples' privilege by the Spirit (Matt 13:11, 16-17; 1 Pet 1:10-12; Heb 11:39-40; Luke 2:25-32).

42-144, The Good Samaritan (10:30-37; resources/sermons/42-144, library form robots-blocked): story treated as fictional parable, not allegory of Christ; priest and Levite know the law (Lev 19:34; Exod 23:4-5); Samaritans' hostility (Ezra 4-6; Neh 2-4; John 8:48); wine antiseptic, oil soothing; open-ended payment; "go and do likewise" indicts fallen humanity, no one can love so (Lev 18:5); repent and seek mercy (Luke 18:13); Josh 18:17 Adummim. Short neutral note added that many read the command also as practical instruction.
42-145, The Christian's Priority (10:38-42): Bethany under two miles from Jerusalem (John 11-12); Martha = "mistress" in Aramaic, probably eldest; Mary's posture unusual for a woman; warns against building a contemplative/monastic model from Mary; hospitality commanded (Rom 12:13; 1 Pet 4:10; Titus 2:4-5; 1 Tim 5:10); Martha's problem priorities, not service; "one thing" = hearing the Lord's word (Ps 27:4; Phil 3:13; Deut 8:3; Luke 6:46-47; 8:20-21; 11:28).
