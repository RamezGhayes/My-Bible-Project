# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 17 (Grace to You)

Titles, ranges, dates: 43-93 to 43-96 from "John, Vol. 8" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V8/john-vol-8); 43-97 to 43-100 from "John, Vol. 9" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V9/john-vol-9). No sermon page shows a title; titles rest on the series pages only. 43-98's series range is "John 17" (registered 17:1-26).
43-93, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 1 (17:1-5; page focuses on v1), 2015-12-06: "Holy of Holies" of Scripture; Matt 6 a pattern for disciples (Jesus never asked forgiveness), John 17 his real prayer; Melanchthon's last lecture, John Knox deathbed; dependence of the incarnate Son (Acts 6:4); Jesus' prayer life (Mark 1; Luke 6; 9; Matt 11:25); shows present heavenly intercession; three parts 1-5, 6-19, 20-26; "table talk" (Luther); eyes lifted vs tax collector (Luke 18); Handley Moule on "Father"; hour (John 2; 12), sovereign timetable (Isa 53; Acts 2); Heb 12:2; Trinitarian glory (5:23; 7:18; 11:4; 12:23-28; 13:31-32); application: depend in prayer, security.
43-94, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 2 (17:1-5), 2015-12-13: Hebrews: always lives to intercede, advocate; crossing Kidron with sacrificial blood toward Gethsemane; not selfish; zoe defined in v3 as relational knowledge, quality not just duration; authority over all flesh (Matt 28:18; Eph 1:21-22; Phil 2:9-11); "given me" seven times (6:37, 44); ginomai in 1:14 (became) vs en in 1:1 (was); logos for Greeks and Jews; preexistence, coexistence, self-existence (Gen 1:1; Exod 3:14; 8:58); work = substitutionary atonement (Matt 20:28; 1 John 3:5, 8; 19:30); eternal life grows to full knowledge (1 Cor 13:12); Lord's Table.
43-95, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 3 (17:6-10), 2016-01-10: intercession as fuller expression of atonement (Heb 7:25; Rom 5:8-10 "much more"); name = identity, nature, attributes, works, will (1:14, 18; 14:9; Heb 1:3; Ps 20:7); theirs before conversion, given out of the world (Acts 13:48; 18:9-10; Eph 1:4-6, 11; Col 3:12; 2 Thess 2:13; Rev 13:8; 17:8; 20:15); world = anti-God system; kept your word = obedient faith (3:36; 6:68); four verbs (know, receive, understand, believe) vs false faith (2:23-25; 6:66); "sent" about 30 times; model for ministry; v9 particular intercession: prays for the elect, not the world (6:37-44; Rom 8:28-39); v10 Luther: only Christ can say all God's is mine; glorified in believers (10:28-29; 1 Pet 4:11); eis telos (13:1) = to the uttermost; Aaron and Melchizedek.
43-96, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 4 (17:11-19; page focuses 11-12), 2016-01-17: tabernacle, high priest once a year with blood and incense; ephod onyx stones with tribes' names on shoulders (Exod 28); Christ carries his people; love eis telos; Trinity eternal love vs solitary deity (1 John 4:8; Heb 4:14-16; Rom 8); world system under Satan (15:18-20; 16:2); Holy Father vs unholy sinners; phulasso = guard against outside threat (Luke 11 strong man; Acts 28); 10:27-29; 1 Pet 1:3-5; arrest scene in ch 18; unity = sharing Trinitarian life (Gal 2:20); son of perdition: never a son of God, apoleia = destruction, ruin (Matt 7:13); 6:70; 13:18 (Ps 41:9); Matt 26:24; honored place at the meal yet responsible; prophecy not determinism; loss part of the plan.
43-97, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 5 (17:11-19; page covers 1-16 thematically), 2016-01-24: Puritans; Day of Atonement, high priest never sat; Christ sat down (Heb 8:1; 10:14); sympathizes (Heb 4:14-16); hold fast and draw near (Heb 4:14, 16; 10:22-23, 38-39); prayed-for: the eleven and future believers (500 in Galilee, 120 in Jerusalem); "if I could lose it, I would"; four petitions: security, unity, joy (rooted in future promise, Heb 12:2; 16:20-24), immunity from the evil one (vv14-16); Satan powerful but defeated (Job; Luke 22:31-32; 2 Cor 12; Rom 16:20; 1 Pet 5:8-10; Heb 2:14; 1 John 3:8); false believers exposed (Luke 22:3; Acts 5).
43-98, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 6 (John 17; main focus 11-19), 2016-01-31: security texts (Ps 37:23, 28; Isa 43:1; Jer 32:38-40; John 5:24; 6:37, 39; 10:28-29; Rom 8:1, 30, 35; Phil 1:6; 2 Tim 1:12; 1 Pet 1:4-5; Jude 24); Father's purpose, Spirit as arrabon (down payment, Eph 1), Son's intercession; sanctify three times = separation from sin; three stages (penalty, power, presence); means: the Word (2 Cor 3:18; Ps 119:11; 1 Pet 2:2); v18 holiness and mission (Matt 5:16; Phil 2:15; 1 Pet 2:9); v19 positionally imputed (2 Cor 5:21), practically model (4:34; 5:19, 30; 6:38; 8:28-29), empowered by the Spirit (Isa 11:2; 61:1; Luke 4:18; Acts 10:38); walk as he walked (1 John 2:6; Col 2:6); dawn brighter (Prov 4:18); babies craving milk.
43-99, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 7 (17:20-23), 2016-02-07: third "I do not ask" (vv9, 15, 20); prays for believers across history, not the world; word = apostles' preaching from Pentecost and NT writings (Jude 17; Rom 1:16; Rom 10; Acts 16:14 Lydia); faith: object Christ, time all history, power Spirit, instrument apostolic word; unity not mainly church harmony but shared eternal life (not all believers alive at once); Trinity needed for love (contrast with Islam); Eph 1 "in Christ"; 1 John 1:3; Phil 2:15; "God joined to man so man joined to God"; v22 glory as man (1:14; 2 Cor 4:4) shared (2 Cor 3:18; Isa 42:8; 48:11), believers reflectors; v23 Father loves them as the Son, wrath spent at Calvary (Heb 12:6; Rom 8; 1 John 4:19; John 21; 15:9-10); sin more ugly "in Christ" (1 Cor 6:15-20).
43-100, The Lord's Greatest Prayer, Part 8 (17:24-26), 2016-02-14: glorification = unity in heaven; heaven is a person (Ps 16:11; Phil 1:23; Col 3:1); weak anticipation; believers as the Father's gift to the Son (Eph 1:3-4; 1 John 3:1-2), cheap item vs gift from one who loves you; glory veiled then glimpsed (1:14; Matt 17; 2 Cor 4), fully seen (Rev 21-22 Lamb the lamp; Moses in the rock); examine affections; v25 righteous Father; v26 heaven = all glory and all love; Heb 7:25; Rom 5; Samuel Rutherford (d. 1661), visit to Anwoth, Anne Cousin's hymn (1854), John Owen on glory in the face of Christ.

Theology notes: 17:9 MacArthur teaches particular intercession for the elect; consistent with the Reformed reading, no note needed. 17:20 he says Jesus prays for all who will believe, not for the world.
