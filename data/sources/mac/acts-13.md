1741 (1-2): Acts gives standards by example, Ephesians by doctrine; shift Peter to Paul, Jewish to Jew-Gentile church (John 4:21; Eph 2:14); Antioch model: strong in Word, then send; review 11:21-30 (salvation, teaching, "Christians", famine gift); Spirit-controlled church (11:24; 13:9; 13:2, 4); 1 Cor 12:7-13 car without engine; Gal 3:3; 2 Pet 1:21; obedience needs knowledge of Word; critique of numbers/entertainment/liturgy, "incessant gospel"; five leaders diverse (racial, social); prophets with apostles (Eph 2:20), office ended; teachers continue (he as pastor-teacher); Barnabas, Saul apostles in secondary sense; Hos 4:9 like people like priest; Acts 6:3; 1 Tim 3; Titus 1; James 3:1; Niger maybe Simon of Cyrene (Mark 15:21), Lucius maybe Rom 16:21; Manaen foster-brother of Herod Antipas; leitourgeo = public office, liturgy; Acts 6:4; fasting with prayer, partial/total, Luke 5:33-35 expected not commanded, Matt 6:16-18; priesthood sacrifices 1 Pet 2:5; Heb 13:15-16; Rom 12:1; Eph 5:22; Col 3:23; Mal 1:13-14 best offering; 2 Tim 2:15; prepares sermons for God's evaluation.
1742 (2-13): call = assignment, not elevation; "Separate Me": ministry owed to Spirit; waiting not inventing programs; God picks prepared experienced men, critique of inexperienced graduates, his own ordination; laying on hands = identification and support, not conferring Spirit (Num 8 Levites; Moses-Joshua); "sent away" = release (Lazarus loosed, John 11). v4 Spirit sent, church released; Seleucia port 16 mi; Cyprus 30-50 x 110 mi, Salamis, Paphos; McCartney: start of Christian odyssey above Columbus, Magellan etc.; sent by Spirit guarantee; started small; Mark helper = support ministries. v5 Salamis largest city, many Jews, synagogues, Paul's custom; Satan resists every advance; word not void. v6 110 mi; Paphos Roman seat, Venus with sexual rites, "pit of sin"; Bar-Jesus = son of salvation. v7 prudent = intelligent, seeker, hired medium; 1877 inscription with name Sergius Paulus; magos (Matt 2 magi, Persian astronomers, astronomy to astrology), demonic not tricks; medium a contact; Simon (Acts 8) and slave girl (Acts 16) pattern; Jeane Dixon; Antichrist and false prophet; control rulers control land; Tiberius mediums; Rom 13 caveat; "wickedness in high places". v8 Elymas maybe Arabic root wise/powerful; heaven vs hell; Screwtape; occult rising since ~1938; girl in church and "astral projection"; rationalism leaves vacuum; Eph 6 armor; 2 Tim 3:8 Jannes, Jambres; 3:13 goes from goao low chants; seekers sincere. v9 Paul = little, Gentile name, new role; Spirit-filled moment by moment; set eyes; James 4:7 resist; not in flesh. v10 dolos from fishhook bait; mischief = easy wickedness; son of devil vs Bar-Jesus; enemy of righteousness; perverting right ways = definition of occult. v11 hand of Lord; for a season, not permanent; Luke's medical terms; Paul's own blindness; hope (own speculation) Bar-Jesus later saved; demons can't handle Spirit-filled. v12 doctrine not miracle astonished; contrast with crowds; confessed lordship, saved. v13 Mark leaves: resentment at Paul leading, attachment to Barnabas, Taurus fears (torrents, robbers; 2 Cor 11:26), romance faded on first term; 15:36-39 split; 2 Tim 4:11 reconciled; South American mission compound empty from internal strife; report enemy to commander.
1743 (13-23): preaching church's greatest need; Lloyd-Jones three reasons for decline (authority of Scripture lost, reaction to domineering pulpits, counseling/programs/entertainment substitutes); Acts 6:1-4; three expansions each with Spirit-filled sermon (Peter, Stephen, Paul); v13 Mark (three reasons); Pamphylia, Pisidia, Galatia, Galatians a circular letter; 100 mi Taurus trek, robbers, rivers Cestrus and Eurymedon, Alexander; 2 Cor 11; malaria at Perga (Gal 4:13; Ramsay); synagogue: ready audience, Scripture-knowing, love for Israel (Rom 10:1; 11:1); service: Shema (Deut 6:4-9), prayers, Law reading (cycle), Prophets (haftarah), invitation; v16 gesture, Israelites and God-fearers, called preacher commands attention; God the subject: chose, exalted, bore, destroyed, divided, gave, raised; v17 high arm (Ex 6:1, 6); v18 variant: "suffered/tolerated" vs "nourished/bore like a father", prefers latter (Deut 1:31); v19 seven nations (Deut 7:1), lot; v20 450 yrs, judges deliverers to Samuel; v21 king demanded, Saul of Benjamin 40 yrs; v22 removed for Amalek disobedience (1 Sam 15); after God's heart = one who repents (1 Sam 13:13-14), not sinless; sovereignty with free will, disobedience can't thwart; v23 David's seed (2 Sam 7), Micah Bethlehem; name Jesus climax; history not cyclical (Stoics, Sartre, Huntley-Brinkley); purpose fellowship and glory; cross center, second coming completes; OT saints saved by faith looking forward.
1744 (23-37): Bible record of Christ, OT coming, NT came; three-part sermon; v23 God can't break promises (Num 23:19; Isa 46:9-10; Jer 33:15-17), name surprise, seed through Mary and legally Joseph; v24 forerunner, "Baptist" not a denomination, John's baptism preparatory unlike Christian (Rom 6), repentance precedes faith, critique of "try Jesus"; Isa 40:3; Mal 3:1; herald means Messiah imminent; v25 humility, camel hair, locusts; denied being Messiah; sandals lowest slave's task; Matt 11:11; lower you go higher God raises; John 1 witness not light; Lamb of God; John 3:30; v26 Jews and God-fearers, "to you/us" better manuscripts; outline Savior (23), offered (26), by faith (39); v27 two questions; rulers did not know, blinded by sin and hypocrisy; read prophets every Sabbath; people handle Bibles without understanding; John 5:39; Matt 22:29; Luke 24; cults twist; ignorance of written word = of living Word; v28 no cause, Pilate repeatedly; foreknown; Isa 53:3; John 7:5, 48; Ps 69:4; John 15:25; Jesus couldn't engineer fulfillment; v29 list: Ps 109:25/Matt 27:39; Ps 22:17/Luke 23:35; Ps 22:18/John 19:23-24; Ps 69:21/Matt 27:34; Ps 22:1/Matt 27:46; Ps 31:5/Luke 23:46; Ps 34:20/John 19:33; Ps 22:14/John 19:34; Zech 12:10; Isa 53:7 led; Gen 22:6 Isaac wood; Heb 13 outside gate; Jews stoned, crucifixion foreign; serpent lifted (John 3; 12:32); God never a victim; v30 "But God" keynote, reverses verdict, Rom 1:4; v31 1 Cor 15 500 witnesses; v32-33 Ps 2:7 incarnation and resurrection, claims as God and Son substantiated; v34 Isa 55:3 sure mercies, dead Messiah fulfills nothing, unconditional "I will give"; v35-37 Ps 16:8-10, David decayed, no Jew thought David rose; summary; apps: judge future by past faithfulness, pray for Israel.
1745 (38-41): three Jewish concerns: history, Messiah, sin; v38 best news; forgiveness under law real but temporary; 2 Sam 24 David's census (sensitive, deserving, ask God to punish); national confession ("Israel ch 9", likely Neh 9); Neh 1:6; Ps 38:3-4; 41:4; 51; Ex 32:33; Ex 34:7; Lev 26 sevenfold; Ps 32; 103; Isa 55:6-7; Mic 7:18; Lev 5:5-6 confession plus offering, only past sin; law exposes; Heb 9:6-9 parabole figure, conscience never clear; 9:14; 10:1-2, 4, 12, 14; Acts 10 Peter preached forgiveness; modern looseness vs Jewish sensitivity; v39 "all" twice; justified = declared righteous; Rom 3:21-28; Gal 2:16; 3:11, 23-24 schoolmaster; 1 Cor 1:30 in Christ; Col 2 debt nailed; "all that believe" includes Gentiles, uproar; servant/broken-house illustration; prescription bottle (sacrifices vs Lord's table); v40 beware, invitation with warning; v41 Hab 1:5 Chaldeans, Babylon; judgment hardest doctrine, Santa Claus God; Johnny Carson/Billy Graham TV exchange (as MacArthur reported it; not used); caller upset over class on hell; worm and fire; fool rejects Christ.
1746 (42-52): gospel always upheaval (Jerusalem, Samaria, Damascus, Cyprus); v42 asked to repeat = highest compliment; "the Jews ... the Gentiles" not in original, "they" = synagogue hearers; v43 followed; "continue" implies profession; can't know they were saved; perseverance evidence (1 John 2:19; John 8:31); no premature assurance; Jews tempted back to law (Gal 5:4, 7; Heb 10:38-39); young Jewish convert under family pressure; v44 whole city, Gentiles brought friends; v45 envy, same word as 5:17; prejudice not intellect, love of sin; imperfect = continual contradicting; v46 bolder; Jew first (Rom 1:16); self-judgment (John 3:18); Mona Lisa and symphony critics judge themselves; v47 Isa 49:6; Simeon Luke 2; v48 tetagmenoi from tasso, papyri: enroll/list; Lamb's book of life (Rev 13:8; 17:8), election before foundation; sovereignty and responsibility, God credited for salvation, man responsible for rejection; v49 word stressed v44, 46, 48, 49; city turned by Scripture not programs; v50 devout women and chief men; 2 Tim 3:11; 2 Cor 11; v51 Luke 10, Gentile soil defiled, treated as heathen; v52 contrast; gospel divides, no watered-down toleration; Acts 17:11; Heb 3:7-8; no neutral ground.
