# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 16 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 8" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V8/john-vol-8). None of the sermon pages show a title, so all titles rest on the series page only.
43-88, The Benefit of Christ's Departure (series: 16:1-11; page text 15:18-16:7), 2015-10-25: evening moves from love to hatred; skandalizo = trap with bait stick, warned so not ambushed; earlier warnings Matt 5; 10; Luke 9; disciples expected political kingdom; synagogue expulsion (9:22; 12:42) = loss of family, livelihood, community, a spiritual leprosy; latreia = priestly altar service; Saul (Acts 26:9; Gal 1:13); martyr history (apostles, Rome, Reformation era, 1997 NYT article, ~100 million persecuted today); persecution mostly from religion, persecutors think they serve God; 1 Pet 4:12-13; 14:29; why not earlier: hostility fell on him while present; 2 Cor marks, Rev 12:17; v5 no one asks where he goes: sorrow self-focused (13:36; 14:5); Luke 24:50-53 later joy; v7 Spirit as reward of finished work (Acts 2:33); Spirit's work: inspires Scripture, regenerates, sanctifies (2 Cor 3:18), empowers witness; honor the Spirit.
43-89, The Holy Spirit: God's Prosecutor (16:8-11), 2015-11-01: allos = another of same kind; elegcho = legal term, indict by evidence, prove guilty, a verdict not a feeling; Luke 3:19; 1 Tim 5:20; Jas 2:9; Rev 3:19; 1 Cor 14:24; Eph 5:11; 2 Tim 4:2; Titus 1:9, 13; 2:15; inward saving work real and necessary (John 3; 2 Cor 4; 2 Tim 2:25) but "not the point here"; prophetic prosecutors: Enoch (Jude 14), Moses, prophets (Ezek 11), John the Baptist, Jesus (3:19; 5:45; 7:7; 8:34-46; 9:39; 15:22-24; Matt 23), all persecuted; disciples take up the role; sin = unbelief; righteousness = ascension shows only his righteousness gives access, imputed (Phil 3); judgment = Satan judged (Gen 3:15; Col 2:15; Heb 2:14; Rev 20), so lesser sinners will not escape; Acts 2 cut to the heart, 3000; preachers as prosecutors, bad news before good news.
43-90, The Authenticator of Scripture (16:12-15), 2015-11-08: setting on the walk to Gethsemane; "cannot bear" bastazo = lift a heavy stone (10:31); many things = the coming NT; Jesus affirmed OT (Matt 5:18; 10:35) and pre-authenticates NT; need, extent ("all the truth", article = completeness; Deut 4:2; 12:32; Rev 22:18-19), source (Spirit speaks what he hears, as the Son, 5:19-30), purpose (glorify Christ, doxazo); 1 Cor 2:6-16 (spiritual words); disciples understood only after resurrection (2:19-22; 12:12-16; Luke 18:31-34; 24); Iron Curtain believers with pencil-copied chapters; seminary from ~70 countries; study word by word; do not force Christ into every OT text; Apollos (Acts 18:28).
43-91, From Sorrow to Joy (16:16-22; page text to v24), 2015-11-22: Christ sympathetic High Priest; hopelessness deepest suffering (Prov 13:12; Job 17; Ps 42:5; Jer 29:11; Lam 3:21-23); disciples expected conquest of Rome; Mark 9 afraid to ask; sorrow "filled" = pushed out all other feeling; "a little while" used often (7; 12; 13); rejects resurrection-only or second-coming-only readings; refers to ascension and coming of the Spirit, Christ present through the Spirit (14:17-18; Rom 8:9), a Trinitarian statement; amen amen ~25 times in John; world rejoices at death; Mary at tomb (20:11), Emmaus; cross from grief to joy (1 Thess 1:6; Rom 14:17); childbirth and Gen 3 curse; union (Gal 2:20; 1 Cor 6:17); joy no one takes (10:28-29).
43-92, The Hope That Overcomes the World (16:23-33), 2015-11-29: needs: love, someone to trust, hope = love, faith, hope (1 Cor 13:13); peace = inner calm, joy = exuberance; vv23-24 ask the Father in his name; paroimia = veiled speech (Hebrew mashal); the hour = Pentecost, NT after Gospels has no parables; veil torn, direct access; v27 Father's love phileo = warm affection, he likes them though he knows all; love via loving the Son (14:21, 23); contrast with access only through Mary (Ludwig Ott); v28 whole gospel; vv29-30 Calvin: a faint scent of truth refreshed them; 16:19 omniscience; v31 statement preferred; scattering (Zech 13:7; Matt 26:56), faith real but weak; 2 Tim 2:13; don't overestimate your strength; regathered, Pentecost; v33 world = satanic system; thlipsis = pressure, crushing (1 Thess 3:3; 1 Pet 5:9); tharseite only imperative, only Jesus uses it, command backed by authority; overcome past tense, already secured (1 John 5:4-5; 1 Cor 15:57-58).
Blocked: none. Untitled sermon pages: all five (titles from series page).
Theology note: 16:8-11 MacArthur reads elegcho as forensic verdict on the world; he affirms the Spirit's inward saving work as real but secondary to this text. Reported as given.
