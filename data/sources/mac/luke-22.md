# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 22 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates for 42-267 to 42-276 confirmed 2026-11-27 on the gty Luke Vol. 23 series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/42v23/luke-vol-23 (42-267 2008-01-13, 42-268 2008-01-20, 42-269 2008-02-03, 42-270 2008-02-17, 42-271 2008-02-24, 42-272 2008-03-02, 42-273 2008-03-09, 42-274 2008-03-16, 42-275 2008-04-06, 42-276 2008-04-20; registered). 42-277 is on Vol. 24 (robots-blocked) and Precept Austin's Luke 22 page does not link it: its title "Part 2" is inferred from 42-276 being Part 1 on the same range and the 42-277 page saying "We looked at it last week, we'll look at it again this morning"; no date. resources/sermons URLs robots-blocked for several codes; read via gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code> or www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>.
42-267, Sinister Players in the Sovereign Plan of Redemption (22:1-6): cross was God's plan and timing ("the hour" in John); leaders feared riot; Sadducee politics/money vs scribes' theology; Caiaphas (John 11:47-53); warns against calling Jews "Christ-killers" (Gen 12:3); Satan entered Judas, "God's devil" within limits (Job 1-2; Luke 22:31, 53), may have hoped for a riot to stop the cross; "the bigger your devil the smaller your God"; Judas from Kerioth, thief (John 12:6), prophecies (Ps 41:9; 55:12-14; Zech 11:12-13); thirty pieces = price of a slave (Exod 21:32).
42-268, God's Preparation for the Final Passover (22:7-13; 2008-01-20 on page): death purposeful (Mark 10:45; 1 Cor 5:7); OT sacrifices point to it (Isa 53:10); Christ controls the schedule; Peter and John (two men to bring a lamb); man with water jar unusual; secrecy hides the place from Judas; Passover elements (four cups and Exod 6:6-7, bitter herbs, charoseth, salt water); Galilean vs Judean day reckoning explains Thursday meal and Friday lambs (John 18:28); Lord's Table replaces the Exodus memorial.
42-269, The Final Passover, the First Communion (22:14-20; read via www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-269): v14 hour = sunset, reclining, order of the meal; v15 emphatic desire; v16 strongest negative, last Passover until a future millennial Passover (Ezek 45:21; Rev 20); v17 cup of blessing (1 Cor 10:16); v18 looks to future kingdom and redeemed Israel; v19 bread of affliction (Deut 16:3); "this is" = symbol and remembrance; rejects transubstantiation and consubstantiation; body intact, bread in His hand, like "I am the door"; breaking = unity, no bone broken (John 19:36); v20 new covenant in blood (Exod 24; Jer 31:34; Ezek 36; 2 Cor 5:21); end of ceremonial system; 1 Cor 11:26 looks back and forward.
42-270, Table Talk on Trouble and Triumph, Part 1 (22:21-30; page heading "Table Talks on Trouble and Triumph"): God triumphs over trouble; v21-23 betrayer at the table (Ps 41:9; John 13:21-30), Judas the model hypocrite; v22 determined (Acts 2:23; 4:27-28) yet "woe": sovereignty and responsibility both held; v24 philoneikia, love of strife (Luke 9:46-48); v25-27 rulers vs servant, foot washing (John 13); v28 stood with Me (contrast John 6); v29-30 kingdom covenanted (diatithemi), future and postponed, earthly, Israel-centered; apostles rule the tribes (Matt 19:28); Matthias replaces Judas.
42-271, Table Talk on Trouble and Triumph, Part 2 (22:31-34): God's sovereignty over evil (Isa 45:7; Lam 3:37-38; Amos 3:6); "you" plural, all apostles; sift = winnowing; Satan is God's Satan (Job 1-2; Zech 3; 2 Cor 12); intercession always effective (Rom 8:34; John 17); "fail" linked to eclipse: saving faith cannot be extinguished (1 John 2:19); "Simon, Simon" compassion; Peter's overconfidence; restoration (John 21) and "strengthen your brothers".
42-272, Table Talk on Trouble and Triumph, Part 3 (22:35-38): earlier mission without purse (Luke 9:3; 10:4); "but now" hostile world; sword figurative for readiness, not attack; v37 Isa 53:12 God treated Christ as a sinner; "it is enough" = resignation, protection is God not weapons; Peter's sword rebuked (Matt 26:52), no retaliation in Acts; world's hatred (John 15:18-16:4); resources: Spirit, prayer, Christ's victory (John 16:33; 2 Cor 2:14).
42-273, Four Features of Triumphant Prayer (22:39-46): Man of Sorrows (Heb 5:7); Satan's hour (22:53); Christ tempted against holy impulses, recoiling from sin-bearing; four principles: anticipation (pray before temptation), affliction (knelt, fell on face), submission ("not My will", 1 John 5:14-15; John 12:27; 18:11), restoration (angel strengthens, v43); v44 agonia, hematidrosis; cup = wrath (Matt 20:22); disciples asleep from sorrow.
42-274, A Traitorous Kiss for the Triumphant Savior (22:47-53): crowd incl. Roman cohort from Antonia, torches and weapons (John 18:3); Judas' kiss as signal, repeated (Mark 14:45), "friend" = hetairos (Matt 26:50; Prov 27:6); "I AM" and the crowd falls (John 18:4-8); Peter cuts Malchus' ear (John 18:10), Jesus heals it, only instant healing of a wound; three reasons against the sword (Matt 26:52-54): fatal, foolish (twelve legions), contrary to Scripture; John 18:36 no war for the kingdom; v52-53 treated as insurrectionist, hour of darkness under God's plan; disciples flee (Zech 13:7); restrained biblical portrait of Judas vs later legends.
42-275, The Danger of Spiritual Over-Confidence (22:54-62): 1 Cor 10:12; four stages: impudent (v33), insistent (Matt 26:33-35), indolent (sleeping, prayed too little), impulsive (sword); Prov 16:18; house of Annas and Caiaphas; three denials woven from all Gospels; the Lord's look of love and sorrow; root: self-confidence, prayerlessness, independence, compromise; bitter weeping vs Judas' despair; restoration (John 21:15-17); 1 Pet 1:6-7; 2 Pet 3:17.
42-276, The Sinless Savior Before the Sinister Sanhedrin, Part 1 (22:63-71): morning session a staged repeat of the night decision; Israel's justice laws (Deut 16:18-19; 19:16-19), Sanhedrin of 71, two or three witnesses; Annas (John 18:12-24) and Caiaphas (Matt 26:57-68) phases, false witnesses; v67-68 council as prosecutor and judge; v69 right hand (Matt 26:64); v70-71 self-confession could not convict under their own law; silence as judgment; 1 Pet 2:23.
42-277, The Sinless Savior before the Sinister Sanhedrin, Part 2 (22:63-71): restrained wording; v63-65 mocking (Matt 26:67-68; Mark 14:65), foretold (Luke 18:31-33); in the line of rejected prophets (Luke 4:24; 11:47-51; 13:34; 20); dawn session; Ps 110:1; Dan 7:13-14; violated rules: night, feast day, self-incrimination (Maimonides), unanimous verdict; torn garments (Lev 21:10; 24:16); John 11:47-53; those who judge Him will face Him as Judge; Hugh Martin (1875).
