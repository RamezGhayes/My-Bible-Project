# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 3 (Grace to You)

F. Moore, Maimonides.

Campbell Morgan; sandals, John 13; winnowing, granary heaven, chaff hell, Ps 1; fire texts (Gen 19:24; Num 16:35; Deut 4:24; 32:22; Jer 4:4; Mal 3:1-3; 4:1; Mark 9:43-48); Bunyan; Eric Sauer threefold repentance, Ps 51, 32; Luke 15:18; 23:41-42; 2 Tim 2:25; false confessions (Ex 9:27; Josh 7:20; 1 Sam 15:24; Judas); axe, AD 70; Isa 11:3-4; 61:1-2; Acts 17:30.
2190, The Commissioning of the King (3:13-17), 1978-05-07: baptism/anointing/word; rejects Gospel of the Hebrews and Gnostic view (Matt 1:23); John kept preventing; fulfil all righteousness (Matt 17:24-27), identification with sinners (Isa 53:11-12; 2 Cor 5:21; Luke 12:50); J. R. Miller cross's shadow; immersion argument (Aquinas); heavens opened literally; Spirit anoints humanity (Ps 45:7; Isa 61:1; Acts 10:38); dove and sacrifice; Ps 2; approval of hidden 30 years; Trinity; throne is a cross; unique, not a model.
