# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 15 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates: 43-79 to 43-84 from the gty series page "John, Vol. 7" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V7/john-vol-7); 43-85 to 43-87 from "John, Vol. 8" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V8/john-vol-8). 43-79 and 43-80 pages show the same title; the pages of 43-81 to 43-87 show no sermon title, so their titles rest on the series pages only.
43-79, I Am the True Vine (15:1-6), 2015-07-19: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-79. "I am" = deity (Exod 3); Israel the fruitless vine (Isa 5; Ps 80; Ezek 15), Christ the true vine; two kinds of branches both outwardly attached; fruitless branch = Judas and false professors in every age (Matt 7:21); explicitly rejects loss of salvation (John 10:27-29; 6:37-39); does not use "lift up"; pruning: trials the handle, the Word the blade (Heb 4:12), for more fruit; fruit is evidence not cause.
43-80, The Benefits of Abiding in Christ, Part 1 (15:4-11), 2015-08-16: read at .../43-80. Meno = remain, not mystical; benefits: salvation/assurance, Trinitarian indwelling, fruit (introduced); Judas; superficial followers (John 2; 6; 8; 12); perseverance; trials prove faith (1 Pet 1); 1 John 2:19.
43-81, The Benefits of Abiding in Christ, Part 2 (15:4-11), 2015-08-23: read at .../43-81. Fruit lists (repentance, Spirit's fruit, praise, giving, speech, conduct, converts); fruit increases; v6 warning against defection; prayer v7; glory v8; love v9-10; joy v11; against "let go and let God" passivity (Col 1:29).
43-82, The Benefits of Abiding in Christ, Part 3 (15:4-11), 2015-08-30: read at .../43-82. Six benefits: salvation, sanctification, answered prayer (aligned with his will; no claim for unbelievers, except prayer for salvation), assurance (2 Pet 1), love kept through obedience, joy; Judas as false disciple; warnings of v2 and v6 as serious as promises.
43-83, Slaves and Friends of Jesus, Part 1 (15:12-17), 2015-09-06: read at .../43-83. Paradox: friendship with commands; trusted royal slaves; doulos = slave (ownership), kurios/despotes; Lordship and self-denial; Phil 2; Luke 17:7-10 unworthy slaves.
43-84, Slaves and Friends of Jesus, Part 2 (15:12-17), 2015-09-13: read at .../43-84. Greatest love (atonement and example); friends by obedience; no secrets; v16 election (Eph 1; John 6:37, 44; 1 Cor 1:26-31; Rom 9); appointed (tithemi) to bear fruit, preview of Great Commission; lasting fruit esp. converts; prayer as provision for the mission.
43-85, Why the World Hates Christians, Part 1 (15:17-25), 2015-10-04: read at .../43-85. Mainly vv17-20: love one another because the world won't; "if" = "since"; kosmos = evil system under Satan; reason 1: not of the world, chosen out, the world's conscience; reason 2 introduced: hated Christ first (Matt 10:24); history of persecution.
43-86, Why the World Hates Christians, Part 2 (15:17-25), 2015-10-11: read at .../43-86. Four reasons: not of the world (1 John 3:11-13; John 3:19-20), hated Christ (v20), do not know God (v21, Rom 1), Scripture predicted it (v25, Ps 69:4; Acts 2:23; 4:27-28); vv22-24 no excuse, rejecting the final revelation (Heb 1:1-2; 10:29).
43-87, The Christian's Confidence from God's Promises (15:26-27), 2015-10-18: read at .../43-87. Witness to the world, of the Son, from the Father, through the Spirit, by believers; Spirit proceeds from and is sent by the Father; v27 first the apostles (NT), then all believers; Acts 1:8.
Blocked: none.
Theology notes: 15:2 and 15:6. MacArthur reads the cut-off and burned branches as false professors like Judas and rejects loss of salvation; consistent with the Reformed reading, no note needed.
