# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Galatians 3 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles, ranges from gty.org series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/48v1/galatians-vol-01 (48-11, 48-12) and the precept austin list https://www.preceptaustin.org/galatians-3-resources (gty links with ranges). Sermon pages give no dates; dates left out.
90-32, The Ministries of the Holy Spirit, Part 2 (3:1-5; series Whatever Happened to the Holy Spirit?): read at gty.org/sermons/90-32. Topical; v3 focus; Joseph Carroll key in pocket; Ps 23; Col 2:10; Eph 1:3; 2 Pet 1:3; anti pragmatism/psychology; Abba; John 15:26; 16:14; 1 Cor 12:3; 2 Cor 3:18; Exod 34; Ezek 36:27; Acts 13; 15:28; Rom 8:14; Ps 143:10; 119:35, 133; 51:6; Augustine, Spurgeon, Hudson Taylor, A. W. Pink.
48-13, Works or Grace?, Part 2 (3:10-14, expounds 10-12): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-13. katara vs eulogia; Balaam, Shimei; Matt 10:28; Deut 27-28 Shechem, Gerizim/Ebal tribes; Jer 11; 17:5-7; imprecatory psalms (C. S. Lewis, R. M. Benson); Ezek 33:11; triple curse; Gal 1; Matt 5:48; James 2:10; Hab 2:4; Rom 3:20; Lev 18:5; Rom 4:14-15; Pew survey 52%.
48-14, Works or Grace?, Part 3 (3:10-14): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-14. Curse on all men; Ezek 18; Ps 51; Nahum 1:3; Prov 17:15; 24:24; 2 Cor 5:21; curse on one man; propitiation; Deut 21:23 background (blasphemer stoned then hung); Passover; Isa 53; John 10:11; Acts 2; 4; objective, final, efficacious; Heb 7, 9, 10; criticizes Mass; God's glory (Ps 106:8; Isa 48:11; Ezek 36:22; Eph 1:12, 14); two purposes of v14; 1 Cor 16:22.
48-15, The Big Picture of Salvation (3:15-18 per list; page treats 3:6-24 overview): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-15. Gentile churches; Reformation 500 years; Mosaic covenant addition not replacement; ceremonial temporary, moral law remains; veil torn; Gen 15 God alone passes; Jer 31:31-34; Ps 110:4; Heb 7:11-12; Matt 5:17-18; Rom 10:4; 2 Cor 3. Only 15-18 used.
48-16, God's Eternal Covenant of Promise (3:15-18): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-16. diatheke; lesser to greater rabbinic; blood covenants Gen 15; singular seed (Gen 4:25; 21:13; 1 Sam 1:11; 2 Sam 7:12; Gen 3:15); active/passive obedience imputed; 430 (Exod 12:40); progressive revelation; kecharistai; Noah, Abraham, David, new covenant (Gen 9; 17:19; 1 Chr 16:16; Isa 55:3; Ezek 37; Jer 31; 32:38-40; Heb 12:24; 13:20); Sinai bilateral; Heb 13:10-14.
48-17, The Inferiority of the Law (3:15-22): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/48-17. Three reasons: purpose (Rom 7; Ps 51; Rom 4:15; Heb 8), mediators (Exod 19; Deut 33; Ps 68:17; Acts 7:38, 53; Heb 2:2; Gen 18), accomplishment (me genoito; 2 Cor 3:6); 2 Cor 1:20; Acts 13:32-39; Rom 15:8; Heb 6:12-20; Luther on law and grace.
Not readable (robots): 48-11 Bewitched by False Doctrine (3:1-5, 2017-08-20), 48-12 Works or Grace?, Part 1 (3:6-14, 2017-08-27), 48-18 Deliverance from Bondage to the Law (3:23-29). Not used.
