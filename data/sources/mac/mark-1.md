# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Our own outline, not his text.

41-2 The Herald of the New King (1:1-8): euaggelion = announcement of a ruler's arrival; Isa 40:9; 52:7 (God returns to reign over Zion); Augustus inscription (9 BC) calls his birthday the beginning of good news; Mark presents a rival King. Jesus = Yahweh saves (Matt 1:21); Christ = Anointed King; Son of God = same nature as the Father. v2 Mal 3:1 "before me" so the coming King is Yahweh himself; herald custom. v3 Isa 40:3 near (return from Babylon) and far (Messiah; "the Lord" = Jesus). v4 baptism of repentance pointed to forgiveness, did not itself forgive; a Jew baptized admitted he was no better than a Gentile. v5 national movement but often shallow (Lane: wilderness recalls the exodus); about 120 in the upper room. v6 Elijah's dress (2 Kgs 1:8); false prophets copied it (Zech 13:4); Luke 1:15-17; Mal 4:5; Matt 11:14; locusts allowed (Lev 11:22). v7 untying sandals the lowest slave's task; John 3:30. v8 Spirit baptism = regeneration (Titus 3), not a second blessing; only God gives the Spirit, so Jesus is God. Five truths: promise, person, prophet, preparation, preeminence.
41-3 The Significance of Jesus' Baptism (1:9-11): coronation of the King; Galilee of the Gentiles (Isa 9); obscure Nazareth; all four Gospels record it (critics cannot explain an invention so awkward); Matt 3:15 fulfill all righteousness = perfect obedience counted to believers (active), identification with sinners pointing to the cross (passive); rejects other explanations. v10 immersion; schizo = tear, same verb as the veil (15:38); breaking of silence; Isa 64:1; Testament of Levi; visible descent, not a vision; dove = manner of descent; Isa 11:1-2; 32:15; 42:1; John 3:34 without measure; Owen and Smeaton: the Spirit as the one through whom the divine Son acted in his human nature (Heb 9:14; Rom 1:4). v11 Ps 2:7 + Isa 42:1; no prophet called God's Son (Heb 1:3); "beloved" echoes Gen 22; "well pleased" = sinless; Trinitarian witness. Mark 11:27-33 Jesus points back to John's baptism as the public ground of his authority.
41-4 Jesus' Strategy for Effective Ministry (1:12-20): baptism = coronation; authority over Satan, sin and sinners. v12 "drove" = strong verb, the Father's will (Jas 1: God tempts no one). Alone, unlike Adam in Eden with Eve (Gen 3); beasts and the curse (Deut 8:15); forty days like Moses (Exod 34) and Elijah (1 Kgs 19). Satan tempted him to abandon humiliation, i.e. the cross, not his kingship. Heb 4:15; Luke 22:28, 53. Gen 3:15; 1 John 3:8. Angels served food; sign of the Father's approval. vv14-15 after John's arrest; Galilee away from corrupt Jerusalem; gospel of God (Rom 1:1); time fulfilled (Gal 4:4; Eph 1:10); Luke 4 / Isa 61; kingdom present because the King is present (spiritual now, millennial, eternal); repent = turn from sin, believe = trust the person and work of Christ (Acts 20:21); rejects social or political gospel. vv16-20 already met (John 1:35-42); successful businessmen; "follow me" unusual for a rabbi (Mark 8:34); Jer 16:16 fishers in judgment, here salvation; sovereign call and enabling; Zebedee with hired men; Levi (2:14); John 15:16; 2 Tim 2:2. Three principles: sanctity of soul, simplicity of subject, selectivity of successors.
41-5 Cosmic Authority (1:21-28): the King must overpower the ruler of this world. Capernaum; synagogue from the exile; scribes and Ezra (Ezra 7; Neh 8); scribes quoted rabbis, Jesus spoke with his own authority. Unclean spirit = demon, fallen angel; "us" speaks for all demons; demons know him while leaders, crowds and disciples do not (until 8:29). No formula or dialogue; silenced so they could not be his publicity; Luke 4:35 man not harmed. Crowd amazed, demons terrified; amazement is not saving faith: be amazed and also fear, and run to him.
41-6 Kingdom Power (1:29-39): fever left at once and she served with full strength; after sunset when the Sabbath ended; "many" Hebraic idiom = all; demons silenced; before dawn prayer: the dependent Son praying to the Father; "for this I came" = preaching is the priority, miracles authenticate the message; Isa 53:4 (Matt 8:17); Isa 61 (Luke 4); Gen 3 curse on body and soul; few healings before Christ, so his ministry was unique. Proof, power, priority.
41-7 Jesus Trades Places with a Leper (1:40-45): leprosy made a man unclean and cut off (Lev 13); kneeling worship, "if you will": sure of his power, unsure of his will. Moved with compassion; touched him though the law forbids (Lev 5:3); he cannot be defiled; "I will, be cleansed"; instant and complete restoration; Scripture speaks of cleansing lepers. Sternly warned (snorting verb); show yourself to the priest (Lev 14) as a testimony to the priests; disobedience spread the news; Jesus cannot enter towns, stays in desert places: he trades places with the outcast, pointing to the cross. Also 2 Sam 3:29; 2 Chr 26 (Uzziah).
