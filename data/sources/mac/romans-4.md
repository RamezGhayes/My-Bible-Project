# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 4 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, ranges, dates from series pages Romans Vol. 03 (45-34) and Vol. 04 (gty.org/sermons/series/7343, 7344).
45-34, Abraham--Justified by Faith, Part 1 (4:1-3): robots-blocked on two tries; not used.
45-35, Abraham--Justified by Faith, Part 2 (4:3): by faith not works, grace not law, divine power not effort; faith channel not basis; FAITH acrostic (facts, agreement, internalization, trust, hope); Gen 11:31-32; 12:1-8; 15:6; Josh 24:2; friend of God 2 Chr 20, Isa 41; Heb 11:13; 1 Cor 15:3-8.
45-36, Abraham--Justified by Faith, Part 3 (4:4-8): ergazomai; Rom 11:6; Gerstner deeds categories; Matt 20:1-16; faith empty hand; 1 Tim 1:15; Job 9:20; Isa 64:6; Ps 32:1-2; anomia; Ps 103:12; Lev 16:21-22; Ps 130:3-4; Exod 34:6-7; Isa 53:10; John 1:29; Heb 9-10; Pharisees; Felix Jardeo illustration.
45-37, Abraham--Justified by Grace, Part 1 (4:9-12): Gen 15:6 before Gen 17 (14+ years); Ishmael circumcised at 13 (Gen 17:25); sign and seal; three roles; Deut 30:6; Jer 4:3-4; father of circumcised who walk in steps (single file); Rom 2:25-29; Phil 3:2-4; Gal 5; Ludwig Ott, Aquinas on sacraments; rabbis on circumcision saving from hell.
45-38, Abraham--Justified by Grace, Part 2 (4:13-17): heir of the world (land, people, blessing, Redeemer); Gen 12:1-3; 13:16; 15:18-21; Gen 15 Abraham asleep; anarthrous nomos; Rom 7:7; 8:17; Gal 3:16-18, 29; charizomai perfect (Gal 3:18); John 8:56; Bunyan Holy War.
45-39, Salvation by Divine Power--Not Human Effort (4:18-25): hope vs faith; Gen 15:5; 18:11; katanoeo; diakrino; Ps 57:7; Heb 11:17-19; Jas 1:3; Rom 15:4; Ps 78:5; paradidomi judicial; paraptoma; Gal 4:21-31; Barnhouse on Abram's name; Calvin faith always conquers; Hodge resurrection essential.
