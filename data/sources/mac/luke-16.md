# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 16 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Sermon pages show no title or date. Titles and ranges from the gty links with titles on preceptaustin.org/luke-16-commentary. "date" left empty.
42-206, Investing Earthly Finances with an Eternal Focus (16:1-13; text runs to v14): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-206. A third of ~40 parables on money; steward high-status; "squandering" as Luke 15:13; fired at once; "What shall I do?" (Luke 3:10-14; 12:17; Acts 2:37); private discounts: oil ~1.5 years' wages, wheat ~2 years; debtors think master approved; praised for shrewdness not wickedness; sons of light (John 12:36; Eph 5:8; 1 Thess 5:5); Jas 4:14; retirement illustration; v9 friends through gospel giving, lesser to greater (Matt 6:19-21; Luke 6:38); vv10-12 character not circumstance, widow, Hag 2:8, Ps 104:24, Matt 25, 2 Cor 4:18, Enron; v13 Calvin and A.W. Pink quotes, Matt 6:24; v14 Pharisees scoff.

42-208 (title not confirmed; 16:9-14 and giving: tithe system, 2 Cor 8-9): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-208, title not confirmed, NOT registered, no voices.
42-209, Why False Teachers Mock the Truth, Part 1 (16:14-15): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-209. Hostility progression (Luke 7, 11, 14; 15:2); scoff = turn up nose, Luke 23:35; Judas; 1 Tim 6:5; 1 Tim 3:3; Titus 1:7; 1 Pet 5:2; Jer 6:13-14; korban; self-justification = false religion (Matt 6; Gal 6:12; Rom 1:17; 10:2-3); "managed God down"; Matt 23:13-15; four motives; John 3:19-20; 1 John 2:3-4.
42-210, Why False Teachers Mock the Truth, Part 2 (16:14-16): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-210. Mocking messengers = mocking God (Luke 13:34; Gal 6:7; Ps 2:4; 2 Kgs 2:23-24; Jer 20:7-10; Ps 22:7; Isa 53:3); God knows heart (1 Sam 16:7; Jer 17:10); "detestable" like a stench; Nicodemus; v16 law and prophets until John, bridge between eras (Luke 1:67-79; 3:4-6; Matt 11:11; John 1:29; 3:30); kingdom only through Christ (John 14:6; Acts 4:12); John 5:39.
42-211, Why False Teachers Mock the Truth, Part 3 (16:16-18; text reviews 14-15): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-211. Forcing in = costly struggle, self-denial, repentance (Luke 9:23-24; 13:3, 5; 14:26-33); keraia, verbal inspiration (John 10:35; Gal 3:16; Luke 20:41-44); ceremonial laws set aside (Acts 10), promises and moral standards remain; no category for grace; v18 Deut 24:1-4 limited provision; adultery capital (Lev 20:10; Deut 22:22); Hillel trivial reasons; porneia exception (Matt 5:31-32; 19:3-9); Jer 3; eight marks of false religion.
42-212, A Testimony of One Surprised to be in Hell, Part 1 (16:19-31; text covers 14-22): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-212. Hope as common grace; religious people surprised in hell (Luke 13:24-28; Matt 25); purple from murex, Lydia (Acts 16:14), fine linen; Luke 12:19; ptochos; "thrown down" (Matt 8:6) suggests paralysis; Lazarus = God helped; Dives; crumbs (Matt 15:26-27); dogs scavengers; Luke 10 priest and Levite; argues it is a parable ("a certain man", Luke 10:30; 14:16; 15:11; 19:12); great reversal.
42-213, A Testimony of One Surprised to be in Hell, Part 2 (16:19-31): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-213. Hell neglected; aionios for both; death; angels (Matt 18:10; Heb 1:14); Pharisaic wealth theology, Job's friends; Abraham's side (Gen 15:6; Rom 4:3; Jas 2:23; Luke 13:28-29; John 13:23); Hades = hell (Ps 139:8; Amos 9:2; Luke 10:15; Acts 2:27, 31); Gehenna; rejects soul sleep, annihilation, purgatory; "have mercy"; Isa 66:24; Luke 6:25; drop of water; privilege no protection; fixed chasm, punitive not remedial; vv27-31 warn, sufficiency of Scripture; 2 Thess 1; Heb 10.
42-214, A Testimony of One Surprised to be in Hell, Part 3 (16:19-31): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-214. Rabbi on TV; contrasts; Gen 13:2; hell punitive; Mark 9:48; Ps 51:3; Jude 7; Matt 8:12; Rev 14:10-11; 20:10; Flavel (conscience the whip); Edwards; five rejected reasons (stock, substance, secularism, gross sin, lack of charity; Eph 2:8-10); people perish for not hearing Scripture.
42-215, A Testimony of One Surprised to be in Hell, Part 4 (16:19-31): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-215. Funerals; C.S. Lewis, Lutzer; Matt 10:28; Heb 9:27; Mark 9:48; Dan 12:2; apollumi = ruin not cease (Matt 9:17; Luke 15:4; Thayer), against annihilationism; reasons rejected; nine OT gospel beliefs (God's nature, repentance, sovereign grace, forgiveness, faith, imputed righteousness, substitutionary atonement, Messiah, self-abandonment) with texts.
