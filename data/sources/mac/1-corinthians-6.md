# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Corinthians 6 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, dates from the series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7133/1-corinthians-vol-02 . Read 2026-11-26.
1825, Forbidden Lawsuits (6:1-8): Christians suing Christians is sin; they forgot the rank of the church and the Christian attitude. Athens litigation background (private and public arbitrators, juries of 201 to thousands; litigation as entertainment). Jews settled disputes in the synagogue, pagan courts thought an insult to God's law. "Unjust" = unjustified. Saints judge the world (Dan 7:22; Rev 3:21; 2:26-27; Matt 19; Luke 22), angels (2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6), takes both fallen angels judged and good angels ruled. kriterion = tribunal. Reads v4 as a command. v7 "defect"; Matt 5:38-42; Matt 18:21-35; Plato: the good man chooses to suffer wrong rather than do it; God on my team, vengeance his. v8 summary: revenge, gain, fraud. Application: take disputes to elders; court only in narrow cases (protecting marriage, rescuing children). Anecdotes (Sam Ericsson, attorney elder; his own car accident settlement).
1826, Forgive Because You're Forgiven (6:9-11): third point, relation to the world: unrighteous don't inherit the kingdom; believers are a different sphere. Vice list applied today; malakos only here in NT, role exchange, Deut 22:5; homosexuals, Sodom, 1 Tim 1:10; Nero, Sporus, Otho, Socrates, Plato's Symposium; love while calling it sin. Not teaching loss of salvation; describes unregenerate. "Such were" past tense; new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Gal 6:15; Eph 2:10). Three "but"s. Washed = regeneration (Titus 3:5; John 3), new divine life; justified = declared righteous, standing; sanctified = new capacity for holy living, pattern broken though believers still sin. In the name of the Lord Jesus, by the Spirit. Forgiveness: Eph 4:31-32; Col 3:12-13; Matt 18:21-22; Luke 17:3-4; Matt 5:43-48; Paul used legal rights only for the gospel (Acts 16:35-39; 22:24-26; 25:10-12). Mel Trotter story.
