# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 5 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes/titles/ranges: Acts series uses 17xx codes (not 44-). Titles from the sermon page itself (1716) and the titled gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-5-resources (1717-1719, link text and gty slug "the-early-church-pattern-for-evangelism-part-N"). Dates not shown anywhere reached; omitted.
1716, The Sins of the Saints (page covers 4:32-5:11; registered as ch 5, v 1-11 because entries are in ch 5; its 4:32-37 part not used here): Satan attacks from within, first sin in church; Cromwell "warts and all"; but = contrast with Barnabas; names Ananias/Sapphira ironic; Sapphira knew; not sin to keep money, giving voluntary; lie + hypocrisy (desire for spiritual status + money); Chafer "secret sin on earth is open scandal in heaven"; Matt 6:1-18; Luke 18:9-14; pleroo = full control, same as 4:31; Satan cannot possess true believer (1 John 4:4) but deceit opens door; "why" puts blame on Ananias; Spirit is God (vv3-4), not impersonal force; all believer's sin is against Spirit (temple); death judicial, shock of conscience; Edward I's glare and the dean; 1 Cor 11:30; 1 John 5:16; James 1:18-21; 1 Pet 4:17; buried outside city; Sapphira expected praise, second lie; test the Spirit = thinking God deceivable; "when the church gets pure, the world gets saved"; church not a social club; keep vows incl. financial.
1717, The Early Church Pattern for Evangelism, Part 1 (5:12-16): keys purity, power; sign gifts for apostolic era ("signs of an apostle"; Heb 2); signs point, wonders awe; one accord = unity restored; Solomon's porch elevated colonnade (Acts 3); no one dared join = fear from judgment, only committed; magnified = high regard; cheap membership; discipline Matt 18:15-17, 1 Tim 5:20, 1 Cor 5:1-5; cancer illustration; M'Cheyne; Ironside undertakers; two words for beds (rich/poor); oriental shadow belief; text doesn't say shadow healed; Luke distinguishes illness/demons; miracles temporary, new birth the great miracle; lying wonders 2 Thess 2.
1718, The Early Church Pattern for Evangelism, Part 2 (5:17-32): persecution key; hairesis -> heresy; zelos = jealousy; Sadducees wealthy, liberal, pro-Rome, deny resurrection and angels; 2 Tim 3:12; 1 Pet 2:20; 3:17; 4:14; Matt 5; self-inflicted suffering vs persecution; Acts 16 parallel; angel defies Sadducees; "words of this life" = Christianity is life, Christ circumference (John 14:6; 11:25; Phil 2:16); obedience over strategy; Sanhedrin + gerousia; perplexed = wit's end; "behold" strong; no violence from fear; nonresistance opens doors; two indictments (4:18; Matt 27:25); 4:19; persistence key; diacheirizomai = kill with own hands; Deut 21:23; Acts 2:23, 36; 3:15; 4:10-11; archegos = prince/pioneer/author, swimmer with rope; forgiveness requires repentance; Spirit witness (John 14:26; Matt 10:19); Paul Acts 25:11; 28:23, 30; Phil 1:21; cork illustration.
1719, The Early Church Pattern for Evangelism, Part 3 (5:33-42): influence for life or death; productivity key; dieprionto = sawn through; conviction mental/doctrinal; John 16:8-11; Sadducees control council, Pharisees popular; Rabban, seven teachers, Gamaliel first, grandson of Hillel, teacher of Paul (22:3); Theudas 400, Josephus' later Theudas, identity uncertain; Judas of Galilee census under Quirinius c. AD 6, Zealots from his movement; Gamaliel's principle dangerous, success not proof; movements that succeed without God; test is Scripture; Matt 12:30; 2 Cor 6:2; Moody and Chicago fire; 39 stripes Deut 25, Mishnah posts, thongs, 1/3 front 2/3 back; Gal 6:17; Col 1:24; persecution targets Christ; daily persistence, 6:1; Prov 28:1 lion; full revelation today.
Not used: 44-19 and 44-20 (another, later set of Acts 5 sermons on gty; 44-20 opened but showed no title, so not registered).
