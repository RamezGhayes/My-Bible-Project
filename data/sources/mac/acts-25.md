# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 25 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes and titles from the titled gty links on https://www.preceptaustin.org/acts-25-resources ("Acts 25:1-7 Paul's Before Festus- 1", "Acts 25:7-12 ... 2 -The Power of a Dedicated Life", "Acts 25:13-26:3 Paul's Before Agrippa- 1--Evaluating the Accusations") with matching gty slugs (paul-before-festus-part-1, paul-before-festus-part-2-the-power-of-a-dedicated-life, paul-before-agrippa-part-1). No title or date on the sermon pages; dates omitted. 1795 registered 1-12 because the sermon itself covers 25:1-12. 1796 spans into ch. 26, registered also as 1796@26 (26:1-3). Not used: 1243, 1257, 80-197 (other series).
1794 (1-7), read at /library/sermons-library/1794: hidden principles under the narrative; Felix recalled 59; Festus better (Josephus); v3 sought favor not justice, ambush; religious system hates truth (John 15:18-25; 2 Cor 11); sin enslaves, hatred kept two years (Rom 6:16; Cain, Esau, Saul, Haman); v4-5 dunatoi = influential men; providence stopped ambush because God promised Rome (Gen 45:7-8; John 19:10-11; Acts 2:23; Dan 4); v6 promptness; v7 charges of 24:5-6 false.
1795 (1-12), read at library URL: ten principles mined; false religion persecutes true (2 Pet 2:1-2), against ecumenism; doulos slavery of sin (John 8:30-36); providence (Lam 3:37-38); persecution false and for Christ (Matt 5:11); no proof; v8 blameless life (1 Pet 3:14-16); Christianity cleared of sedition (Acts 16, 18); submit to government even bad (Rom 13:1-6; 1 Pet 2:12); v9-11 courage from confidence in God; not a martyr-seeker, used legitimate means; appeal legal; Acts 23:11; v12; one dedicated life affects the world.
1796 (25:13-26:3), read at library URL: narrative, Paul's example of evangelism; Agrippa II last Herod, Bernice symbol of his sin; Roman law accusers face accused; no charge sticks; Festus sees resurrection dispute, the core issue; Augustus = sebastos, revered; v22 imperfect, long-standing wish; v23 phantasia pomp, chained Paul dominates; 26:1 answer (1 Pet 3:15; 2 Tim 4:2); turn every conversation to Christ (Niemoller illustration, not used); 26:2-3 courtesy; testimony as offense not defense; reads 26:28 as "are you trying to convert me?".
