# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 10 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates from the gty series page "John, Vol. 5" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V5/john-vol-5). Sermon pages themselves show no date or formal title.
43-52, I Am the Door (10:1-10), 2014-07-27: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-52. John 9-10 one scene; paroimia; Judean shepherding, Ezek 34 (one shepherd "my servant David" = Messiah); fold at night, sheep passing under the rod (Ezek 20:37); doorkeeper; names; thieves = Pharisees/scribes, all false teachers, also Zech 11:15; fold = Israel/Judaism, not church or heaven; leading out; door = only way of salvation; perissos abundant life (salvation, provision, protection, Word as pasture); election, effectual call, sheep do not follow strangers (Matt 24:24); Rom 8; 1 Pet 5 application.
43-53, I Am the Good Shepherd (10:11-21), 2014-08-03: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-53. kalos vs agathos; real risk (Amos 3:12; 1 Sam 17; Exod 22:13); psuche whole person; huper substitution; "actual atonement" for the sheep known and given by the Father (Matt 1:21; Isa 53:8); hireling mercenary (Zech 11), applied to leaders and false teachers (Acts 20; 1 Pet 5); ginosko intimate love (Gen 4:1; Matt 7:23); other sheep = Gentiles (Isa 42:6; 49:6; Gal 3:28; Eph 2:14; John 11:52); voluntary obedience and Father's love (Phil 2); division vv19-21: irrational blasphemers and rational sceptics, both still unbelieving.
43-54, I and the Father Are One, Part 1 (10:22-42), 2014-08-10: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-54. Covers mainly vv22-26. Five scenes; Dedication/Hanukkah, Kislev 25, Antiochus, 2 Maccabees; winter; question in v24 not sincere but a trap; works testify; unbelief because men love darkness (John 3:19-21; 7:7); v30 unity of essence (John 5:17-18); Lev 24:16. (Some contemporary political asides not used.)
43-55, I and the Father Are One, Part 2 (10:22-42), 2014-08-17: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-55. Covers vv25-31. Human side: they did not believe, culpable (Matt 12); divine side: not my sheep (John 6:37-44; 17; John 3); does not fully harmonise sovereignty and responsibility (Acts 2; 4); vv27-29 security: hear/know/follow, eternal life cannot end, none snatches, the Son's hand and Father's hand one; rests on God's decree, not believer's merit (1 John 2:19; Rom 8); v30 essence (John 17; Col 2:9); v31 stones, fourth attempt, mob not legal process; world hates him for exposing its deeds (John 7:7).
43-56, I and the Father Are One, Part 3 (10:22-42), 2014-08-24: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-56. vv31-42: claim to give life = equality (John 5); kalos works; Ps 82 judges called gods as receivers of God's word, lesser to greater; v35 luo, Scripture cannot be loosened, inerrancy (Matt 5:17-19; 2 Tim 3:16; 2 Pet 1:21); works argument; escape, hour not come; Bethany beyond Jordan (John 1:28); John did no sign but testified truly, model for preaching; many believed.
Blocked/untitled: none.
Theology notes: 43-53 teaches particular ("actual") atonement for the sheep; 43-55 teaches perseverance from vv27-29. Both Reformed; reported as found, no note needed.
