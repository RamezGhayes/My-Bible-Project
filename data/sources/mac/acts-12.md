1740, The Stupidity of Fighting God (12:1-25). Four possible emphases; chose folly of fighting God (Prov 21:30); eggs on granite, rainbow in net, whiskbroom vs tide; survey of rulers who fought God. v1 vex = injure; time of Agabus famine; Herod Agrippa I vs Herod the Great, Antipas, Agrippa II; Maccabean line; Rome-educated, self-promoting; political motive for Roman favor. v2 James of inner three; Talmudic sword for leading to false gods; Matt 20:23 cup fulfilled; executioners carried out God's plan. v3 pleased the Jews; Peter jailed twice before; Unleavened Bread pilgrims. v4 quaternions = 4x4, three-hour watches, two chained, two outside; pascha = Passover (KJV "Easter"); anagagein public trial. Gamaliel 5:38-39; 23:9; Job 15; Jer 21:5; Rev 2:16. Three reasons: power can't be contested, punishment can't be avoided, purposes can't be frustrated. v5 ektenos medical term, stretched muscle, agony, Luke 22:44; 1 Pet 4:8; Acts 26:7; James 5:16; prayer first, not committee. v6-7 sleeping: John 21:18 promise, past deliverances, 1 Pet 5:7; light, angels radiate glory; smote (patasso) same as v23. v8-10 gird, sandals, garment; rejects Barclay's naturalism; vision; iron gate of itself; Bezan text seven steps (speaker); Heb 1:14. v11 came to himself. v12 synidon = seeing whole; believers need assurance God in control and that he answers prayer; Mary mother of Mark, house church, wealthy; Mark later wrote from Peter. v13-16 Rhoda (Rose); "you're crazy"; his angel; zeal without faith, his own prayers "mostly zeal". v17 kataseio beckoning; tell James and brethren; another place unnamed, maybe protection; no evidence for Rome tradition; fades except Acts 15. v18 guard pays with life, Philippian jailer (16). v19 Herod's search, guards executed; dietribe = pass time, sulk. v20 Tyre, Sidon free cities dependent on Galilee food; tariffs; Blastus chamberlain. v21 Josephus: festival for Claudius, silver robe in sun, theater built by Herod the Great. v22 voice of a god; flattery. v23 God shares glory with no one; eaten by worms; Josephus five days; punishment unavoidable. v24 word grew; purposes can't be frustrated; Ps 2:1-4; Isa 45:9 (cited as 49:5); Matt 16:18. v25 return to Antioch with John Mark; Paul dominates to ch 28; Mark restored after failure. Appeal to come to Christ.
