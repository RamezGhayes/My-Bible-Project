# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 25 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, scripture ranges and dates from the Matthew Vol. 17 series page (gty.org/sermons/series/7017/matthew-vol-17). Registered "v" follows the sermon content where it differs slightly from the listing (2379 listed 25:32a, 2380 listed 32b-46).
2375, The Fate of the Unprepared (25:1-13): sermon page robots-disallowed; read the GTY study-guide print page for this sermon (gty.org/resources/study-guides/print/2375/...). Wedding stages Matt 1:18-19; torches John 18:3; Josephus Wars VI.ix.3 ten men; professing church; 1 Sam 16:7; William Arnot; oil = saving grace, Matt 22:11, 2 Tim 3:5, Jas 2:17; Matt 13; midnight Exod 12:29-33, rabbis; 1 Thess 5:2; 2 Cor 13:5; Rom 6:23; Matt 13:45-46, Isa 55:1, Luke 6:46-49; ark door; Matt 7:22-23, Luke 13:24-28; Luke 21:34-36; Rom 13:12-14; Tennyson.
2376, The Tragedy of Wasted Opportunity, Part 1 (25:14-19): Eccl 11:1,6, Prov 10:5, Ps 69:13, Isa 55:6, 2 Cor 6:2, Jer 8:7, Ps 95, John 12:35-36; Whittier; waiting vs working; 2 Thess, 2 Pet 3, Jas 2; visible kingdom Matt 13; Achan Josh 7; Matt 13:44.
2377, The Tragedy of Wasted Opportunity, Part 2 (25:19-30): T.S. Eliot; reckon; eu; 1 John 2:28, 2 Tim 4:7-8, Heb 12:2; hard man; Proverbs sloth; tokos, 12%; Judas; Matt 13:12; Matt 22:13, 1 John 1:5; rewards as service, Luke 19:17,19.
2378, The Judgment of the Nations, Part 1 (25:31-32): Num 32:23, Ps 90:8, Prov 13:21, Isa 3:11, Rom 1:18, 2:9, Heb 9:27; John 5:22, Matt 28:18; 2 Thess 1:7-8, Rev 19:11-16, Col 3:4; Isa 9:7, Luke 1:32-33, Zech 14:4, Joel 3:11-14; survivors of tribulation; Rom 11:26.
2379, The Judgment of the Nations, Part 2 (25:31-32): sheep/goats = saved/lost; Mal 1:11, Ezek 40-48; Jude 14; premillennial digression after Erich Sauer, five arguments with refs; Papias, Justin, Irenaeus; Origen, Augustine; Bob Hope (omitted).
2380, The Judgment of the Nations, Part 3 (25:31-46): Ps 7:11, 1:5; many NT judgment texts; shepherd practice; Gen 48:13-14; v34 key: blessed of my Father, inherit, prepared, foundation, Rom 8:17, 1 Pet 1:3-4; six needs; Rom 2:6-7, 2 Pet 1:5-7, Jas 2, 1 John 3:16-17; imputed righteousness; Heb 2:11-12, 1 Cor 6:17, Gal 2:20, Matt 10:40, 18:4-5, John 13:35; Matt 7:22-23, 11:4-5; fire prepared for devil; Zech 14; same word eternal.
