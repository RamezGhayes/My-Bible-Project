# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Colossians 2 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles and ranges from the titled gty links at https://preceptaustin.org/colossians_2_resources (MacArthur section), checked against the gty pages (https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>). Dates not shown, left empty. Not used: 80-329, 80-36, 90-281 (one-off sermons, not read).
2139, Strengthen Your Heart (2:1-2); title from page image caption and preceptaustin: love for the church first qualification of a minister (Eph 5:25; Acts 20:28); Paul's daily care for churches (2 Cor 11:28); kataphileo (Acts 20); agon = athletic straining; Epaphras (4:12); love even for churches he never saw; parakaleo = strengthen; Hebrew heart = mind and will, bowels = emotions (Ps 22:14; Lam 2:11; 1 John 3:17); emotions respond to the mind (dream of falling), so fill mind with truth (Prov 4:23); flywheel illustration; parakletos = Strengthener (John 14:16); Eph 3:16-19.
2140, Paul's Burden for the Church (2:2-7); title from page header and preceptaustin: five desires as a checklist: strong hearts; united in love (positional unity 1 Cor 12:13, keep it Eph 4:3, love as bond 3:14, humility Phil 2; failures of love 1 John 3: jealousy, hatred, indifference); settled understanding (sunesis; assurance grows as truth is lived; 2 Pet 1:10; mystery = Christ's deity and sufficiency; apokryphos: treasures hidden only in Christ, open to every believer; 1 Tim 3:16); v5 taxis = rank, stereoma = solid front; walk as you received (aorist; 1 John 2:6; his father's "What would Jesus have done?"); rooted (perfect), built up (present), established (passive, by God) (Acts 20:32; Jude 20); abounding in thanksgiving.
2141, Philosophy or Christ (2:8-10); title from preceptaustin (page: speaker suggests the same, no heading): philosophy = man's search for ultimate causes without God; 1 Cor 2:9 empiricism and rationalism fail; Russell, Hobbes, Hume; letter outline, four elements of heresy (philosophy 8-15, legalism 16-17, mysticism 18-19, asceticism 20-23), Essenes possible; beware present tense (Acts 20:29-30); "spoil" = carry off as booty, kidnap (Gal 5:1); only NT use of "philosophy"; fishhook; Josephus; tradition of men (Mark 7); rudiments = ABCs, works religion; possible elemental spirits/astrology; v9 pleroma, present tense, Christ keeps humanity; v10 perfect tense complete (2 Pet 1:3-4); spiritual, moral, mental completeness; cults (Eddy, Joseph Smith, Moon) add to Christ.
2142, Complete in Christ (2:10-15); title from preceptaustin only (page shows none): hugies, instant healings picture complete salvation; three: salvation 11-12, forgiveness 13-14, victory 15; "surgical salvation" of false teachers; two Jewish views of circumcision (saves vs sign); he says infant baptism came from the first view (not developed); Abraham circumcised after faith (Rom 4:11); circumcision of heart (Deut 30:6; Jer 4:4); new nature, old body (Rom 7; 6:6); v12: "no water" here, spiritual baptism = being placed into union with Christ, water baptism a picture; rejects baptismal regeneration; energeia; v13 dead = unable to respond (Eph 2:1, 11-12); God initiates; all sins forgiven; cheirographon = signed IOU; blotted = wiped off like ink sponged from papyrus; forgiveness gracious, complete, eager, certain, unequaled, motivating; confession acknowledges, does not earn; v15 apekduomai disarmed, public triumph (Heb 2:14); 1 Pet 3:18-19 proclamation to bound demons; G. H. C. MacGregor on fear of fate.
2143, Spiritual Intimidation, Part 1 (2:16-23); title from page alt text and preceptaustin: intimidation by false doctrine; heresy (Essenes) Christ plus; legalism = spirituality tied to man-made rules or set-aside OT rules; "therefore" from 10-15; Gal 5:1; food abolished (Mark 7; Acts 10; 15; Rom 14:17), liberty but care for weak conscience (Rom 14; 1 Cor 8-10), no accommodation for false teachers; festivals (Lev 23; Num 28); Sabbath not binding on Gentile believers, undercuts Seventh-day Adventist view, Jewish believers free by conscience (Rom 14), Gentile church met on first day (Acts 20); shadow/substance: manna, Passover, Sabbath rest point to Christ; mysticism: humility, angel worship (Rev angel refuses worship), visions, defrauded of prize like a runner disqualified; v19 hold the Head; modern unverified claims; asceticism: monks, Simeon Stylites; Hudson Taylor calling vs status; Maclaren: only Christ's power subdues the flesh; Paul Sailhamer.
2144, Spiritual Intimidation, Part 2 (2:16-23 per preceptaustin; the sermon itself mainly works from 3:1-4 with 2:18-23 as background); title from page header and preceptaustin: character shown by what we seek, love, think (Matt 6:21); four false avenues (philosophy, legalism, mysticism, asceticism), v23 they only satisfy the flesh; modern intimidators; worship must follow revealed Word: Uzzah and the cart (2 Sam 6; Num 4), Saul and Agag (1 Sam 15:22-23), man of God from Judah (1 Kgs 13); Gal 1:8; remedy: concentrate on Christ (Col 3:16; 2 Cor 3:18; Eph 1:3; Phil 4:19); Barnhouse.
