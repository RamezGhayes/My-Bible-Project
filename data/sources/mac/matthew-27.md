# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 27 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, ranges and dates from Matthew Vol. 18 and Vol. 19 series pages (gty.org/sermons/series/7018, 7019). All nine sermons read from the sermon pages.
2392, The Traitor's Suicide (27:1-10): John 18:31; metamelomai vs metanoeo, 2 Cor 7:8-10; naos; Deut 21:23, 27:25; Ahithophel 2 Sam 17, Saul 1 Sam 31; price of blood; Zech 11:12-13, Jeremiah heads prophets, Luke 24:44; Acts 1:18-19 rope broke. (Sermon also cites "Deut 16:16-19" for false witnesses per fetch; omitted as uncertain.)
2393, What Shall I Do with Jesus? Part 1 (27:11-18): charges from Luke 23:2, John 18:28-38; John 18:36; silence, Isaiah sheep; Luke 23:8-15; Barabbas Mark 15, Luke 23, John 18:40; envy.
2394, What Shall I Do with Jesus? Part 2 (27:19-26): wife's dream providence; crowd; Luke 23:20-24; Deut 21:6-9; Gen 4, 9; blood on us = that crowd; Acts 5; Romans future salvation; scourging; John 19:1-5; Isa 53; Acts 2.
2395, The Wickedness of the Crucifixion, Part 1 (27:27-37): four groups; Jer 17:9; Acts 2; auxiliaries; scarlet Isa 1:18; thorns Gen 3:18; John 19:3,17; Simon, Mark 15:21, Rom 16:13; Luke 23:27-31; Heb 13:12-13; kranion; Ps 69:21, Prov 31:6, John 18:11; Ps 22:18 quote later scribal addition; three languages; Luke 19:14; Heb 6:6.
2396, The Wickedness of the Crucifixion, Part 2 (27:38-44): lestai; knowing, fickle, religious wicked; Matt 26:61,63; Ps 22:7-8; Zech 12:10; Luke 23:39-43; Acts 2, 6:7; Heb 6; David Thomas.
2397, God's Miraculous Commentary on the Cross (27:45-53): six miracles; darkness Exod 10, Isa 13, Joel 2:30-31, Amos 5:18,20, Zeph 1:14-18; ekleipo; Origen, Tertullian; Ps 22:1, Hab 1:13, 2 Cor 5:21, Gal 3:13, 1 Pet 2:24, 3:18; Luther; Malachi Elijah; voluntary death John 10; Gen 3:15, Gen 22; veil, Josephus, Heb 4:16; earthquake Exod 19:18, Ps 18:7, Heb 12:26, Rev 5; saints firstfruits 1 Cor 15:20.
2398, Responses to the Death of Christ (27:54-56): four responses; Mark 15:39, Matt 16:17, 1 Cor 12:3, Luke 23:47; anarthrous Son; Luke 23:34, John 12:32; Lenski (Longinus); women Luke 8:1-3, John 19:25-27; Morgan; Luke 23:48; Matt 26:56; 2 Cor 7:8-10; Ps 113:9 (not used); Studdert Kennedy poem.
2399, The Amazing Burial of Jesus, Part 1 (27:57-61): three witnesses; John 19:31-37; Isa 53:9; Mark 15:43, Luke 23:50-51; Nicodemus John 19:39-40; Deut 21:22-23; Matt 12:40, Talmud reckoning; women Matt 28:1-10.
2400, The Amazing Burial of Jesus, Part 2 (27:62-66): Sabbath, Gentile house; "that deceiver"; Matt 12:38-41; Mark 8:31, John 20:9; guard, seal; providence not miracle; Ps 76:10; Acts 4:27-28; Gen 50:20.
