# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 15 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Sermon pages show no date. Titles and ranges from preceptaustin.org/luke-15-commentary (gty links with titles) and the monergism Luke 15 sermon index (luke-reformed-sermons-bible-14), which lists 42-198 and 42-201 to 42-205 with titles. "date" left empty.
42-197, Heaven's Joy: Recovering the Lost (15:1-2): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-197. What makes God rejoice; communicable attributes; Deut 30:1-9; Isa 62:1-5; Jer 32:37-41; Zeph 3:14-17; John 17:13; Heb 12:2; Luke 10; pattern lost/sought/found/restored/celebrated; vv7, 10, 32; Pharisees' separation rules, Ps 1, Prov 1:15, Isa 52:11; Luke 5:29-32; 19:7; parables like political cartoons; clarity, centrality (9:51-19:27), complexity.
42-198, Recovering the Lost Sheep (15:3-7): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-198. Pharisees cast as shepherds (low status); Ps 23; Isa 40:11; Ezek 34; pooled village flocks, family shepherds (John 10); leave 99; sheep defenseless; "when" = certainty; ~70-75 lb sheep on shoulders; neighbors co-owners; hypocrisy; God by nature Savior (1 Tim 4:10; Luke 19:10); John 10:11-15; Isa 53:6; 1 Pet 2:25; Luke 18 Pharisee; Rev 5; early Christian shepherd art.
42-199, Recovering the Lost Coin (15:8-10): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-199. Drachma = day's wage, savings or dowry on cord/hair; offends Pharisees (shepherd, then woman); Luke 13:34; dirt floor, clay lamp, twig broom; women friends; angels share God's joy (1 Pet 1:12; Rev 4-5; Matt 18:10); sinner like lifeless coin; God seeks those who belong to Him by sovereign election; Christ down to cross and tomb; celebration for the Recoverer; repentance previewed.
42-200 (title not confirmed; content: repentance, Luke 15:7, 10, 18, 21, Luke 3, Acts, 2 Cor 7:9-11): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-200, but no source seen gives its title or range, so NOT registered and no voices.
42-201, The Tale of Two Sons, Part 1 (15:11-16): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-201 (www form blocked). Honor-shame village reading; tes ousias vs kleronomia; wish father dead; Deut 21:17; father's bios divided; God gives sinner over (Rom 1); sold cheap, Gentile land, loose living (v30); famine account; kollao; pigs (Lev 11:7; Deut 14:8); carob pods; sin as rebellion against relationship; "plan A" options.
42-202, The Tale of Two Sons, Part 2 (15:17-21): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-202. Heaven's joy (vv7, 10; Luke 10); repentance begins with honest assessment; "against heaven" (Ezra 9:6); hired men, Lev 19:13; rabbinic earn-back view; expected public shaming at gate; father sees, compassion (bowels), runs (stadium word), exposed legs, Kenneth Bailey; old Arabic versions avoided "ran", Van Dyck 1860 worksheets moved from "hurried" to "ran"; confession omits hired-man request (grace received); Matt 18; Matt 20.
42-203, The Tale of Two Sons, Part 3 (15:22-24): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-203 (recaps 11-21). Robe = father's own, ring = signet authority, sandals = sons not slaves; no probation; fattened calf, ~200 people; usufruct; Rom 4:5; John 6:37; Isaiah robe of righteousness; celebration honors father's grace.
42-204, The Tale of Two Sons, Part 4 (15:25-28): title/range from preceptaustin and monergism; page robots-blocked in gty.org/library and www forms; not registered, no voices (vv25-27 have no mac entry).
42-205, The Tale of Two Sons, Part 5 (15:28-32): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-205 (text treats 25-32). Matt 21:28-32; elder son not consulted; anger like Pharisees (Luke 5; 19); refusing feast = works system (Matt 23:13); father comes out and entreats (paraclete link); John 5:23; slave-like service, "never transgressed" (Luke 18; Matt 19; Phil 3); "this son of yours"; Deut 21:18-21; "child", all mine is yours; few Pharisees converted (Nicodemus, Paul); unfinished ending, Pharisees wrote it by killing the Son; Augustine on free will without grace; Luther; proverb of two sons.
