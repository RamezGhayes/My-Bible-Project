# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 5 (Grace to You)
41-22, Dominating Powers, Part 1 (5:1-20; text read covers vv. 1-8 and the abyss plea): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-22 (date not shown). Mark 1:1 and John 20:31 purpose; power over supernatural realm (Gen 3:15; 1 John 3:8; Luke 11:20-22). Gerasa vs Gadara (Matt 8:28; Luke 8:26). Tombs, unbindable, screaming, cutting: "a taste of hell"; nakedness (Luke) linked to shame (Gen 3; Rev 3:18). Satan usually hides in religion (angel of light). v6 proskuneo; demons know Jesus (Mark 1:23-24; 3:11); orthodox demons (Luke 1:32, 35). "Do not torment me": Matt 8:29 "before the time". Abyss Luke 8:31; 2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6-7; Gen 6. Satan is "God's devil", under God's purposes. Also John 12:31; 16:11; Rom 16:20; Matt 25:41; 1 John 4:4.
41-23, Dominating Powers, Part 2 (5:1-20): page robots-blocked in every URL form; no extracts beyond the title. Not used.
41-24, The Power and Pity of Jesus, Part 1 (5:21-34): English page robots-blocked; read the official Grace to You Spanish transcript at www.gty.org/sermons/print/GAV-41-24/el-poder-y-compasion-de-jesus-1a-parte (date not shown). Crowd wants power more than teaching. Jairus, respected lay official, humbles himself publicly; Jesus accessible, no intermediaries. Matt 9:18: she had already died, so Jairus believed Jesus could raise her. Compassion: Matt 12:20 (Isa 42:3); Matt 14:14; Mark 1:41; 8:2. Woman: Lev 15:19-27; Lev 12; Talmudic remedies; Luke 8:43 incurable; ceremonial uncleanness pictures sin's defilement. Tassels Num 15:37-41; Matt 23:5. "Touch" = grasp; faith in Christ's power, not magic. v30 Christ personally aware of power going out (Heb 1:3; John 2:25; Luke 19:10). v31 sunthlibo crushed. v33 holy fear before deity (Judg 13:22; Isa 6; Ezek 1; Rev 1:17; Mark 4:41). v34 "Daughter" only time; faith has saved her; peace for those at peace with God; restored socially, religiously, before God.
41-25, The Power and Pity of Jesus, Part 2 (5:35-43; 2010-02-07 from series page Mark Vol. 3): read the official Spanish transcript www.gty.org/sermons/print/GAV-41-25/el-poder-y-compasion-de-jesus-2a-parte. v35 "too late" like Martha John 11:21. v36 stop fearing, keep believing (Ps 22; Luke 8:50). v37 first time the three are singled out. v38 funeral customs, professional mourners, flutes (Matt 9:23). v39 sleep = NT view of believers' death (1 Thess 4); Peter and Dorcas in Acts. v40 mocking though they knew she was dead. v41 Talitha tender, "little lamb"; only Mark preserves Aramaic. v42 immediate, no rehabilitation; Luke: spirit returned. v43 silence commands (Mark 1:25, 34, 44; 3:12; 7:36; 8:26, 30; 9:9): avoid stampede, false messianic hopes (John 6:15), premature conflict; full message awaits the cross (1 Cor 2:2; Mark 15:39).
