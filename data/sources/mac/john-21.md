# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 21 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles and ranges from gty links on Precept Austin (https://www.preceptaustin.org/john_21_resources) with matching gty URL slugs; sermon pages show no title or date; dates unknown (registered empty).
43-115, Audacious Disobedience (21:1-14): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-115. Epilogue matching prologue; questions answered; v1 "after these things", between day 8 and day 40 (Acts 1:3); "manifested" sudden supernatural appearing; glorified body not at first recognized; lake ~12 by 7 miles, ~650 ft below sea level, Kinneret, Gennesaret, named for Tiberius; Matt 28:10; v2-3 seven, mostly fishermen, Peter and Thomas named first (grace); should have been at the appointed mountain (Matt 28:16), lake = disobedience; "I'm going fishing" = returning to old career; Peter impulsive, self-doubting; commissioned (Matt 28; 20:21) but no Spirit's power yet; Luke 5 background; v4-5 pais = generic "children", rendered as casual "guys", not the tender words of ch. 13 or "brothers" of ch. 20; disobedience strains fellowship; question exposes failure, admitting it; 14:21, 23; application to gifted believers who turn to other pursuits; v6 absurd but authoritative command, Gideon (Judg 7), Phil 2:12-13; v7-8 John recognizes, Peter dresses and jumps, eager for restoration; ~100 yards; v9-10 fire, fish, bread, John 6, provider, bring your fish (participation); v11 Peter alone drags, 153 large, ~300 lb, net not torn, concrete numbers show reality, "St. Peter's fish"; v12-13 breakfast, care for ordinary needs, real body eats, he serves them; v14 third appearance to the group; lesson: disobedience brings failure and loss of fellowship, obedience brings fruit and fellowship.
43-116, Peter, Do You Love Me? (21:15-25): read at .../43-116. Restoration mirrors the call (Matt 4:19); v15 "Simon son of John" like a parent using a full name; "these" = boats, nets, fish, old career; agapao highest will-directed love, phileo warm affection; Peter dared not claim more after his failure; Calvin on love for Christ needed to persevere in ministry; appeals to Christ's omniscience; bosko, sheep belong to Christ, under-shepherds (1 Pet 5); v16 wider charge, no more fishing; v17 Jesus takes Peter's word, grief at his love being questioned, three for three; "corrective surgery", Christ accepts imperfect love; clay pots; 1 Thess 4:9-10; what you serve shows what you love; v18-19 "truly, truly", girding = freedom vs captivity, stretched hands = crucifixion; upside-down tradition; a gift: assurance he would not deny again (Luke 22; Luke 9:23); "Follow me" the third element; v20-21 comparison habit; v22 hyperbole, mild sarcasm, mind your own call (1 Tim 4:16); v23 rumor corrected to guard Christ's integrity; John died after Patmos exile; Peter and John together in Acts; v24 personal authentication; v25 hyperbole, modern data comparison; love drives obedience (Matt 22:37; Deut 6:4-5; 10:12; 2 Cor 5:14).
Theology note: 21:15-17 MacArthur sees under-shepherds, no primacy; consistent with the Reformed reading, no note needed.
