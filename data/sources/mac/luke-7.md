# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 7 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Codes, titles and ranges from the Luke sermon list (codes 091-101) at https://lionandlambapologetics.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Luke-MacArthur.pdf ; dates not shown on the gty pages (monergism listing not reachable), left empty.
42-91, The Man Who Amazed Jesus, Part 1 (7:1-2): page robots-blocked in every form tried; no search-result URL; not used.
42-92, The Man Who Amazed Jesus, Part 2 (7:2-10; page header "The Man Who Amazed Jesus"; text also covers v1): only recorded case of Jesus amazed at faith (Mark 6:6 at unbelief); entimos "highly regarded"; pais (Matt 8:6) a boy, paralytic; centurions, Aristotle and Cato on slaves; elders (presbuteros); "worthy" = Jewish merit; loves (agapao) our nation, built synagogue; Cornelius (Acts 10:1-2); Gibbon on Roman use of religion; skullo "trouble"; unworthy from sense of sin, not Jewish purity rule (Luke 5:8; 18:13); authority reasoning; Israel indicted (Matt 8:11-12; Luke 10:15), Capernaum synagogue ruins; centurion embodies the Sermon on the Plain.
42-93, Jesus' Power over Death (7:11-17): divine purpose (Isa 46:9-11), Nain a day's walk; divine providence at the gate (Prov 16:9, 33); funeral customs; only son of a widow; compassion (splagchnizomai; Lam 3:22-23); touching the bier: purity not defiled (Num 19:11-22); power by a word, no faith required; given back to mother; "God has visited His people" after 400 years silence (Luke 1:68, 78); "great prophet" an incomplete confession; contrast Martha (John 11:27); missing the time of visitation (Luke 19:41-44).
42-94, Are You the Coming One? (7:19-20; page text spans 18-23): Wesley "Come, Thou Long Expected Jesus"; ho erchomenos a messianic title (Ps 118:26); what confessing Jesus as Lord means; "paint by numbers": OT outlines Christ (Gen 3:15, 3:21, 14, 22, Joseph, ark, Jacob's ladder, Passover, manna, rock, Day of Atonement, bronze serpent, Deut 18, Boaz, David, Job 19:25, Psalms 2, 16, 22, Isa 9, 11, 53, 61, Mic 5:2, Zech 9:9, 11:12-13, 12:10, 13:7).
42-95, Why the Believer Doubts, Part 1 (7:18-20): reports secondhand to John in prison; two witnesses (Deut 19:15); Luke calls Jesus "Lord"; doubt mixed with faith since he goes straight to Jesus; doubt is a believer's problem (disciples' little faith; Mark 9:24); causes: personal tragedy, popular misconceptions (political Messiah; health-and-wealth preaching); go to the Lord and his Word.
42-96, Why the Believer Doubts, Part 2 (7:21-23; gty page title "Believing Doubt"): Christmas Sunday, third message; v21 healings in that hour as private demonstration; v22 signs echo Isa 26:19; 35:5-6; 61:1, kingdom inaugurated; no mention of judgment, which John expected (Luke 3:7-9); causes 3-4: incomplete revelation, wrong expectations; doubters who became heroes (Abraham, Gideon, Thomas); doubt a starting point, answered by the Word; postscript Matt 14:10-13; says church age is a mystery and full promises to Abraham and David await Israel's future belief (dispensational framework; see note in voices).
42-97, The Greatest Man Who Ever Lived, Part 1 (7:24-27; text through 28): reed shaken = spineless; malakos soft clothing, courtiers; prophet with self-denial, Nazirite (Samson, Samuel, John); more than a prophet: himself prophesied (Mal 3:1; 4:5; Luke 1:17); greatness by God's standard, not worldly awards; you can't accept John and reject Jesus.
42-98, The Greatest Man Who Ever Lived, Part 2 (7:27-30): expository vs topical; John baptized Jews as if Gentile converts; v29 tax collectors and people justified God; v30 Pharisees and lawyers rejected God's purpose; John 5:35 lamp enjoyed for a while; Matt 21:32; revisionist charge "he has a demon" (v33).
42-99, The Greatest Man Who Ever Lived, Part 3 (7:28): greatness from unique privilege of introducing Messiah; least in kingdom greater: era of promise vs fulfillment (Luke 10:23-24; 13:28 John in the kingdom); NT mysteries (incarnation, Gentiles, one body, Christ in you); ministry of reconciliation (2 Cor 5:18-20).
42-100, The Parable of Brats: Style Versus Substance (7:31-35; gty page "The Parable of the Brats"): "this generation" pejorative; marketplace games of wedding and funeral; John the funeral messenger, called demonized; Son of Man eating and drinking, the Bridegroom (Luke 5:30-35); it is never the style, always the substance; wisdom vindicated by her children: the faithful remnant.
42-101, The Transformed Sinner (7:36-50): read via https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-101 ; Jesus evangelizing a Pharisee; distinct from Bethany anointing; Pharisee's invitation not sincere; dining on couches, onlookers; woman likely prostitute, not Mary Magdalene; Luther "heart water"; kissing (as Luke 15:20); Simon reasons inwardly; debt parable, forgiver absorbs the debt (Phlm 18-19); hospitality neglected (Gen 18; 29:13); perfect tense "have been forgiven": love the evidence; faith saves (Eph 2:8-9), love its fruit.
