# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 9 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Sermon dates not shown on the transcript pages that could be fetched (series pages robots-blocked), so dates left blank.
41-42, Why You Should Be Ashamed (8:38-9:1; registered here for 9:1 only): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-42. Rejects resurrection/Pentecost/church views of 9:1; fulfilment = transfiguration six days later, Peter James John the "some"; preview of kingdom; prophets pair near and final fulfilment; Dan 7 imagery for 8:38; Matt 16:28.
41-43, The Unveiled Son (9:2-8): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-43/the-unveiled-son. Lead-in Mark 8:29-38; Mt Hermon; six vs eight days (Luke counts promise day); metamorphoo = outward form; OT glory-light: Lev 9, Exod 16:7-10, 24, 33, 40:34-35, Num 14/16/20, 1 Kgs 8:11, 2 Chr 7:1, Hab; Moses and Elijah glorified but not resurrected (Dan 12:2); Peter wants to skip cross, near Feast of Tabernacles; Father's correction "listen to Him"; two or three witnesses (Deut 19:15; Matt 18:16; 2 Cor 13:1); 2 Pet 1:16-18; John 1:14; Heb 1; John 17; 2 Cor 3:18; Rev 1:16; only Jesus, path to cross.
41-44, When Does Elijah Come? (9:9-13): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-44. Prohibition, prophecies, preview. Silence commands (Mark 5:43; 7:36; 8:30); gospel incomplete without cross (1 Cor 1:23); resurrection known (Job, Ps 16, Dan 12); Mal 3:1; 4:5-6; Isa 40:3-5 herald; suffering Son of Man Dan 7:13, Ps 22, 69, Zech 12:10, Isa 53; John the Baptist in spirit and power (Luke 1:13-17; John 1:21; Matt 11:14; 17:12-13); Elijah persecuted 1 Kgs 19; future Elijah before second coming, Rev 11:5-6 possible.
41-45, All Things Possible (9:14-29): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/41-45 (other URL forms robots-blocked). Like Moses descending Sinai; faithless generation Deut 32:5, 20; disciples' earlier authority (Mark 6:7, 13); "if you can" turned back on father; all possible to him who believes; Heb 11:6; "help my unbelief" mixed faith; sympathetic high priest; Satan power of death (Heb 2); "only by prayer": prayer is how faith accesses God's power; 2 Cor 5:7; Gal 2:20; Eph 2.
41-46, The Virtue of Being Last (9:30-41): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-46/the-virtue-of-being-last. Son of Man Dan 7; paradidomi legal handing over; Judas (Matt 26:24), leaders (Matt 27:2; Acts 3:13), Pilate (Matt 27:26), God's plan (Acts 2:23); concealment a mercy (Luke 9:44-45); humility Isa 66:1-2, Mic 6:8, James 4:6, 10, Luke 14:11; child Matt 18:1-6; Phil 2:1-9; exclusivity vs 1 Cor 3, Eph 4:1-6, Phil 1; seventy Luke 10:1, 17; cup of water reward.
41-47, Radical Discipleship (9:42-50): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/41-47. Radical love (v42): union with Christ, Acts 9, Matt 25:34-40, Gen 12, Ps 105:15; ways of causing stumbling (Eph 6:4; Heb 10:24-25; Rom 14). Radical purity (43-48): hyperbole, sin internal (Mark 7:14-23; Jas 1:14-15; Matt 5:27-30); vv44, 46 absent from older MSS; Hinnom Josh 15:8, Ahaz/Manasseh 2 Kgs 16, 21, Jer 7:31, Josiah 2 Kgs 23:10; Isa 66:24. Radical sacrifice (49): salt with offerings Lev 2:13; Ezek 43:23-24; Ezra 6:9; Rom 12:1. Radical obedience (50): unmixed salt, Pliny on salt adulterated with gypsum; Matt 5:13-16; Luke 14:34-35; peace vs 9:33-34.
