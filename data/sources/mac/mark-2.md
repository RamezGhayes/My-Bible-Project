# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 2 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Our own outline, not his text.
41-8 Jesus' Authority to Forgive Sin (2:1-12): the greatest benefit of Christianity is forgiveness; crowds curious and passive, not a measure of success; scribes looking for blasphemy; friends dug through thatch, mud, tiles: faith that acts; v5 distinguishes the friends' faith for healing and the paralytic's saving faith; vv6-7 only God forgives: Jesus is God or a blasphemer, no middle ground; v8 God alone knows hearts (1 Sam 16:7; 1 Kgs 8:39; 1 Chr 28:9; Jer 17:10; Ezek 11:5); v9 visible healing proves invisible forgiveness; vv10-11 authority over sin; he delegated healing and exorcism but never forgiving sins; v12 Matt 9:8 they saw only a man, amazement short of faith. OT: Exod 34:6-7; Neh 9:17; Ps 103:12; Isa 38:17; 43:25; Prov 17:15 vs Rom 4; Lev 24 blasphemy; Dan 7:13-14 Son of Man; Ps 8:4; Ezek 2. Christ: God, sole forgiver, substitute satisfying justice; Son of Man a veiled messianic title.
41-9 The Scandal of Grace (2:13-17): 2:1-12 shows he can forgive, 2:13-17 whom he forgives: admitted sinners; Levi served Rome and Antipas, seen as traitor; immediate obedience, Jesus knew his heart; costly break; feast = revival among outcasts; table fellowship = acceptance; Pharisees' separatism (they used Ps 1:1); physician (Exod 15:26 the LORD who heals); "I came to call sinners": the self-righteous are not called; Rom 3-5 justification of the ungodly.
41-10 The Matchless Distinctiveness of the Gospel (2:18-22): gospel cannot be blended with any system; question a rebuke; John's disciples drifting toward Pharisaic observance; Pharisees fasted Monday and Thursday, never required; only required fast Day of Atonement (Lev 16:29-31); Isa 58 true fasting; rabbis forbade fasting at weddings; v20 "taken away" violent removal, first hint of his death in Mark (Isa 53:8); contrast self-righteousness/grace, ritual/relationship; patch: not patching apostate tradition but replacing it (not God's law); wineskins; Luke 5:39; Messiah not called bridegroom in OT but marriage imagery for God and Israel (Isaiah, Ezekiel).
41-11 Jesus Is Lord of the Sabbath, Part 1 (2:23-28), from search-result extracts of the sermon page: Mark already shows who he is (1:1, 3, 7-8, 11, 14, 24); Sabbath ordained by God (Gen 2:3), the command simply says rest, not detailed rules; rabbis piled up regulations; "Lord" = sovereign: he is Lord of the Sabbath because he is God, he designed the day; Son of Man from Dan 7:13; contrast Matt 11:28-30 rest and easy yoke with legalism. (41-12 extracts: Sabbath made for man's benefit; Exod 20:8-11; claiming lordship of the Sabbath = claiming to be God, backed by power over disease, demons, death, sin.)
