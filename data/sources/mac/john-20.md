43-111, Christ Is Risen, Part 1 (20:1-10): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-111. Resurrection the main event, goal of redemption, validates the cross; Rom 10:9-10; 1 Cor 15; Sunday worship; no one saw the event, a creative miracle; evidence: empty tomb, angels, eyewitnesses; Ps 16; Isa 53; Jonah; v1 Jewish days sunset to sunset, third day; "still dark" vs other Gospels, shadow of Mount of Olives; other women (Matt 27-28; Luke 23:55) bring spices; stone moved to let witnesses in, not him out (cf. 20:19); v2 she assumed theft, no plan to fake; v3-4 John reserved, Peter impulsive; v5-7 linen in place, head cloth apart; no embalming, ~100 lb spices; thieves would not unwrap; v8 John believed, content unclear, Mark 9:24; v9-10 no expectation of resurrection, undermines fabrication; guard and Roman seal (Matt 27), earthquake, bribe (Matt 28:11-15) self-contradictory, story still told decades later; Mark 16 angels, ekthambeo astonished, emphobos (Luke) terrified; two witnesses (Deut 19:15); Emmaus (Luke 24).
