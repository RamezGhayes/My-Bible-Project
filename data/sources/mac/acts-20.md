# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Acts 20 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Dates omitted (not confirmed). Entries max 3 sentences.
1774, For the Love of the Church (title on gty page; part number not shown). /library URL robots-blocked, read at /resources/sermons/1774. Announces 20:1-17, explains 1-2: uproar (Matt 27:24), embrace, philema/kataphileo; love from love for Christ (1 John 5:1); teaching = love.
1775, For the Love of the Church (title on gty page). /library blocked, read at /resources. Treats 20:1-13: affection, holy kiss refs; teaching (kerygma/didache); persistence through plot (Rom 15:30); companions = unity of Jew and Gentile via collection; "we"; Unleavened Bread, aiming at Pentecost; v7 Lord's Day not Sabbath (Rev 1:10; 1 Cor 16:2; Gal 4:10; Col 2:16); love measured by sacrifice (1 John 3:16).
1776, For the Love of the Church, Part 3 (gty slug in preceptaustin acts-20 link). Treats 20:7-17: first day linked to resurrection, Lord's Day, Sabbath not binding; Jewish day reckoning; breaking bread = love feast + Lord's Supper; teaching by dialogue; lamps, gradual sleep, truly dead, Paul embraced like Elijah/Elisha; miracle confirms authority; Assos walk; Ephesus bypassed for Pentecost. Theology note added (WCF 21.7-8 Christian Sabbath).
1777, Paul Looks at His Ministry (speaker's title in transcript). Treats 20:17-20: presbuteroi = mature; own converts; elder = overseer same office; serving as slave; humility; tears (Ps 126:6); trials, lack of opposition may mean ineffectiveness; kept back nothing (2 Tim 3:16); public + house to house teaching.
1778, Paul Looks at His Ministry, Part Two (speaker's title). Treats 20:21-24: testifying thoroughly; metanoia full reversal, not remorse (Judas); faith; bound (Rom 7:2), Holy Spirit or own spirit, both fit; Agabus; life not dear; trust in God's control.
1779, A Charge to New Testament Church Leaders (title as given on the page). Treats 20:25-28: watchman (Ezek 33:8), not loss of salvation; whole counsel; leader's holiness (1 Tim 4:16; 2 Tim 2:20-21); whole flock, local (1 Pet 5:2); presbuteros = episkopos = poimen one office, plurality, no hierarchy; poimaino, bosko (John 21); Heb 13:17; "his own blood" = God the Son, deity of Christ; Eph 5:25-27. Theology note added (teaching/ruling elders).
1780, A Charge to Church Leaders (title as given on the page). Treats 20:29-38: wolves (grievous = dangerous), diastrepho twist; inside worse; Ephesus later (1-2 Tim); nouthesia; commend to God, word of grace (Acts 6:4); inheritance; no covetousness; hands; agraphon; kneeling; affection; love for message.
