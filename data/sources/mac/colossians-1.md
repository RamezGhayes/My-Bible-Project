# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Colossians 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles and ranges from the titled gty links at https://preceptaustin.org/colossians_1_resources (MacArthur section), checked against the gty pages themselves (https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>), which open but mostly show no title or date. Dates unknown, left empty. Series: gty "The Fruitful Life" (topical series 71, Col 1:1-23, no list on page). Not used: 80-218, 1201, 80-127, 80-80, 1258 (separate one-off sermons, not read).
2130, Introduction to Colossians (1:1-2); title on page and preceptaustin: six reasons to study (science, ecumenism, authority, pragmatism, relationships, eschatology); Paul's authority "by the will of God"; Timothy companion not co-author; saints = set apart, no moral sense in itself; faithful brethren same group; Colossae in Lycus valley with Hierapolis and Laodicea, earthquakes, wool and dye, gone by AD 400; church founded through Epaphras during Paul's Ephesus years (Acts 19); heresy: philosophy/secret knowledge (Christ one emanation among many, proto-Gnostic) + legalism, ritual, asceticism, angel worship (Essene parallels); answer: Christ sufficient (2:9-10), "Christ plus nothing".
2131, The Gospel Truth (1:3-8); title from preceptaustin only (page shows none): Paul thanks, never met them; "Lord" = equality with God; euangelion = news of victory; gospel content 1 Cor 15:1-4; seven aspects: received by faith (pisteuo; epi/eis; Heb 11:1; Spurgeon rope/log), results in love (Gal 5:6; John 13), rests in hope (1 Pet 1:4; Heb 6:18-19; 1 John 3:1-2; Moses Heb 11:24-27), reaches the world (not local mystery cult; heresies local, truth universal; Rev 7:9), reproduces fruit (Acts 12:24; mustard seed), rooted in grace (Acts 11:18; 16:14; Rom 4), reported by men (Epaphras sundoulos, diakonos; Rom 10:14-15).
2132, Being Filled with the Knowledge of God's Will (1:9); title on page and preceptaustin: prayer as ministry, OT/NT intercessors; pray for the thriving too, mature believers most attacked; pray without ceasing = God-consciousness; pleroo = filled, dominated (John 16:6; Luke 6:11; Acts 4:31); epignosis = deep controlling knowledge; zeal without knowledge (Rom 10:2; Hos 4:6); Stott "Your Mind Matters"; God's will revealed in Scripture (Eph 5:17-18; 1 Thess 4:3; 5:18); sophia vs sunesis (principles vs application).
2133, The Fruit of Knowledge (1:9-11); title from preceptaustin only; on the page the speaker says the study is called "Filled with the Knowledge of God/His Will": behavior rests on absolutes; truth then duty (Rom 12:1; Eph 4:1); knowledge -> wisdom (principle) -> understanding (application); five results: walk worthy (uniform image; 1 John 2:6), fruit (Ps 1; Heb 13; Phil 4), growth by knowledge (Eph 4:13), strengthening continuous (Eph 3:16), endurance and longsuffering with joy (Dan 3; Acts 16).
2134, What Makes Christians Most Thankful (1:12-14); title on page and preceptaustin: petition and praise balance; thanks commanded (Ps 50:14; Heb 13:15; Christ gave thanks); three realities: inheritance (qualified = position by grace; Eph 2:1-3, 11-12; partaker = allotted portion, present; light = truth and purity; arrabon Eph 1:14), deliverance (aorist, rescued at new birth from Satan's jurisdiction; Luke 22:53; Rom 16:20), transference (moved to Son's kingdom; kingdom future millennial, universal, present in hearts Rom 14:17; David in exile under Absalom); redemption = forgiveness; "through His blood" absent from older MSS in v14 though the idea is elsewhere.
2135, Christ Above All (1:15-19); title on page and preceptaustin: heresy of emanations, dualism, Christ plus; image = exact replica (Heb 1:3 stamp; John 14:9); prototokos = rank and inheritance not chronology (Ps 89:27; Jacob/Esau; Rev 5 scroll); creator (dia, Heb 1:2), angels created; before all (John 8:58); holds together: nuclear "glue" (Chestnut, Darrow, Gamow), 2 Pet 3:10 luo; head = source, firstborn from dead; body: unity, diversity, mutuality; pleroma in Him alone; John Owen.
2136, Reconciled to God (1:20-23); title from preceptaustin; on the page no title, speaker calls it reconciliation to God: plan, means, aim, evidence; apokatallasso = completely reconcile; cosmic restoration (Rom 8:19-22; Eph 1:10; Isa 11, 65), against matter-evil dualism; Erich Sauer; NOT universalism: "all things" = all for whom reconciliation is possible, unbelievers and fallen angels to lake of fire (Rev 20), evil beings stripped of power and forced to bow; forensic reconciliation for the world (2 Cor 5:19) received only by believers; blood = sacrificial death, He gave up His life (John 10); Rom 5:6-10; aim: holy (God), unblameable (self), unreproveable (accusers), positional now (Rom 8:1, 33), practical later; v23: continuance is the mark of true believers, those who fall away were never saved (Luke 8:13; John 8:31; 15; 1 John 2:19; John 6:66).
2137, Paul's Ministry: Fulfilling the Word of God (1:24-25); title from the gty page caption (preceptaustin: "Paul's Ministry-- To Fulfill the Word of God"): eight aspects, four here; source God (diakonos humble; oikonomia stewardship; Damascus road; his own car accident); spirit joy (Heb 12:2; Acts 16; Aristides); suffering: thlipsis never used of atonement; rejects Roman Catholic supplement/treasury of merit; hostility aimed at Christ falls on His body, Paul takes the blows; five reasons suffering brings joy; scope: complete the whole assignment, depth over breadth (Rom 15:20; Jesus limited His ministry).
2138, Paul's Ministry: The Mystery of Christ in You (1:26-29); title from the gty page header (preceptaustin: "Paul's Ministry- Mystery of Christ in Us"): recap of source, spirit (humility: Moses, Gideon, Isaiah, Peter), suffering (potter Pusa, Harper's 1875), scope; subject = mystery revealed (Deut 29:29), Christ in you (John 14:23; Gal 2:20; Eph 3:4-6); style: kataggello proclaim, noutheteo admonish, teach; sum: every man mature (Eph 4:12; Col 4:12), Walter Lippmann rejected; strength: labor to exhaustion, agonizomai, by God's energy; Brainerd, Henry Martyn.
