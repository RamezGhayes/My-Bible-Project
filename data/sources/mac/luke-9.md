# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 9 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Dates for 42-113 to 42-120 from the Luke Vol. 10 series page (www.gty.org/sermons/series/42V10/luke-vol-10); Vol. 11 series page robots-blocked, so later dates left out.
42-113, A Profile of a Christian Messenger, Part 1 (9:1-2): first internship after ~18 months; same authority as Jesus (Luke 4:36, 40; 6:18); Matt 10:8 raise dead; herald the kingdom; sin, repentance, grace (Mark 6:12); twelve = tribes, judge Israel (Luke 22:29-30; Rev 21:14); Geldenhuys quoted on preaching God as King and repentance.
42-114, A Profile of a Christian Messenger, Part 2 (9:1-6; series lists 9:3-6): five marks: proclaims salvation, compassion, trust, contentment, discernment; Luke 22:35 shows training not permanent rule; Matt 10:8-10; Phil 4; dust: Acts 13:50-51; 18:6; Matt 10:14-15; 7:6; OT on God as Saviour/compassion (1 Tim 4:10; Ps 91; Amos 8:5-6).
42-115, Who Is This Man? (9:7-9): crowds credit Jesus not the Twelve; Mal 3:1; 4:5; guilty conscience; Luke 13:31 threat; the question traced through Luke (5:21; 7:49; 8:25; 22:67-70); John 20:31; Peter Jennings anecdote.
42-116, The Range of Jesus' Compassion (9:10-11): robots-blocked, not read, not used.
42-117, Divine Compassion in Common Grace (page passage 9:10-17; series 9:12-17): needs: rest (Mark 6:31), truth, healing (Matt 14:14), food; 2 Kgs 4:42-44; creation ex nihilo; twelve baskets; John 6:22-66 bread of life.
42-118, Life's Most Important Question (9:18-22): Ps 2; Dan 9:25-26; Matt 16:16-17; silence: political uprising + judicial hiding (John 12:34-42); Isa 53; Ps 16.
42-119, The Starting Principle of Discipleship (9:23; page header 9:23-26): against self-fulfilment gospel; Schuller, Hybels criticised; all or nothing; Luke 14:25-33; Matt 10:32-39; 7:13-14; John 12:24-25.
42-120, The Necessary Components of Saving Faith (9:23): salvation = discipleship; self-denial (Phil 3:4-8; Matt 5:3), cross-bearing (1 Cor 15:31), obedience (John 14:21); Ps 34:18; 51:17; Boice, Stott (Basic Christianity), Thomas Watson quoted.
42-121, The Gospel: Self-Love or Self-Hate? robots-blocked, not read, not used.
42-122, The Gospel in Perspective (9:23-26): principle, paradox, cost (1 John 2:15-16), warning; narrow gate Matt 7:13-14; John 10:9; 14:6; Acts 4:12; Jer 29:13; Luke 5:28.
42-123, Who's Ashamed of Whom? (9:23-26, focus 26): Jer 6:15; Phil 3:19; Heb 2:11; Rom 1:16; Dan 7:9-14; Dan 12:1-2; Rev 20:11-15.
42-124, A Glimpse of the King's Return, Part 1 (9:26-32): two comings; near prophecy verifies far; Deut 19:15; eight vs six days; Exod 34; 40; 1 Kgs 8:10-11; Deut 34:6; 2 Kgs 2:11; exodus planned; sleep of sorrow Luke 22:45.
42-125, A Glimpse of the King's Return, Part 2 (9:26-36): glory cloud Exod 13, 16, 40; 1 Kgs 8; Ezek 1; Deut 18:15; Heb 1; John 1:14; 2 Pet 1; Job 26:14.
42-126, The Significance of Faith, Part 1 (9:37-43): faith defined Heb 11:1; Heb 11 survey; mountain vs valley; two sons; Luke 9:1, 6; Mark 6:13; Matt 17:19-20.
42-127, The Significance of Faith, Part 2 (9:37-45): Deut 32:5, 20; 31:28-29; Acts 2:40; Jas 1:6-8; Mark 9:29; Dan 10.
42-128, An Addendum on Faith (9:37-43 with Heb 11): mostly Hebrews 11; registered nowhere, not used.
42-129, The Amazing Person of Christ (9:43-45): Lord's Table meditation; Acts 2:23; 3:13; Rom 8:32; Isa 53; concealment as mercy Luke 18:31-34; 24:44.
42-130, The Mark of True Greatness, Part 1: robots-blocked, not read, not used.
42-131, The Mark of True Greatness, Part 2 (9:46-50): pride's effects; Matt 18:4-10; Luke 18:15-17; Phil 2:1-9; Luke 11:23.
42-132, A Mission of Mercy (9:51-56): mercy heart of mission; Samaritans 2 Kgs 17:33; Acts 1:8; 2 Kgs 1; Luke 19:10; Acts 8:6-8; 1 Tim 1:13; Mic 7:18.
42-133, Barriers to True Discipleship (9:57-62): comfort (Matt 8:19-20; Dan 7:13), money/inheritance (Matt 13:22), family (Luke 14:26; Matt 10:37); Hesiod plough proverb; Bunyan "Mr. Facing Both Ways"; Luther 95 Theses #4.
