# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 4 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Codes, titles and ranges confirmed from the Luke sermon list (codes 042-060, with titles and ranges) at https://lionandlambapologetics.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Luke-MacArthur.pdf , plus Precept Austin (42-49 = "The Invincibility of the Messiah", Luke 4:1-2) and search-result titles on gty.org (42-50, 42-51, 42-56, 42-58). gty pages showed no dates; monergism.com listing could not be fetched (client error / proxy 403), so dates are left empty.
42-49, The Invincibility of the Messiah (4:1-2): temptation as final exam; "not a victim but a victor"; Jesus as true man yet sinless (Heb 2:17), set aside independent use of deity; temptation external only (John 14:30; Jas 1); impeccability (Luke 1:35; 2 Cor 5:21; 1 Pet 2:22; Heb 7:26), Westcott on sympathy without experience of sin; contrasts with Adam; full of Spirit (John 3:34); Mark 1:12 "drove"; wilderness Jeshimon (Deut 8:15); devil's origin (Isa 14; Ezek 28; Rev 12; John 8:44); hunger after 40 days.
42-50, The Temptation of the Messiah, Part 1 (4:3-8; page text reviews 1-2 and stops at v8): "if" presumes truth; first temptation: distrust God's love, Deut 8:3, John 4:34; second: distrust God's plan, crown without cross, Deut 6:13; Satan's partial truth; rules the evil system but God sets nations (Dan 4:17; Acts 17; Rom 13); Israel failed 40 years, Jesus passed in 40 days; Luther "the devil is God's devil"; application: "if God really loved me..."
42-51, The Temptation of the Messiah, Part 2 (4:9-13; text recaps 1-8): Christ's character as evidence for Scripture; Milton Paradise Regained; "Son of God", "Abba", equality with God; aim: make Jesus act independently of Father and Spirit; third temptation: presume on God, Ps 91 misused, Deut 6:16; temple corner over Kidron, Eusebius on James; "until an opportune time" (Luke 22:28; Matt 16:23; John 13:27); Satan's strategy: distrust love, distrust plan, presume; when temptation comes (after highs, when weak); defense by Scripture; illustration of faith-healer presumption.
42-52, From the Wilderness to Galilee: Jesus' Judean Ministry (4:14-15): small door to a great room (Edinburgh gallery); Galilean ministry to 9:50; Josephus 240 towns; place, power, popularity, priority, praise; the year in Judea recorded by John (John 1-4) omitted by Luke; news spread from Passover crowds (John 4:43-45).
42-53, The Messiah's Mission (4:14-21; title on the gty page; list title "Jesus' Return to Nazareth: Ministry in the Synagogue"): synagogue history and order (ten men, Philo "houses of instruction", Shema, readings, sermon, benediction); freedom of the synagogue for visiting teachers; Jesus stands to read Isa 61:1-2 with phrase from 58:6; stops before day of vengeance; perfect tense "fulfilled" = messianic claim; Edersheim, George Adam Smith.
42-54, Jesus' Return to Nazareth: Preaching in the Synagogue (4:16-21): theme of salvation, substitute; four pictures of the lost: poor (ptochos, beggar, spiritual bankruptcy; Luke 18:9-14), prisoners (aphesis = forgiveness), blind (by nature, judicially, by Satan; 2 Cor 4:4), oppressed (Matt 11:28-30); year of favor = present age of salvation, vengeance future.
42-55, Jesus' Return to Nazareth: Rejection by the People (4:22-30): admired the oratory, not the person; salvation only for those who confess spiritual poverty; "physician heal yourself" = demand for a sign; miracles don't produce saving faith (John 11:47); widow of Zarephath, Gentile who admitted destitution; Naaman humbled ("seven ducks in a dirty pond"); rage, Deut 13 as pretext; reason for rejection: refusing to see oneself as destitute.
42-56, Jesus' Authority over Demons, Part 1 (4:31-34): critique of TV "search for Jesus"; Luke now moves from person to power; Jesus must overpower Satan to free sinners (Col 1:13; Acts 26:18; Heb 2:14; 1 John 3:8); four things that terrify demons: preaching, purpose, purity, power; first developed: authoritative preaching of the Word; David Gooding on authoritative proclamation.
42-57, Jesus' Authority over Demons, Part 2 (4:31-37): demon panics at the gospel; Christians cannot be possessed (indwelt by Spirit); possession concentrated in Gospels and Acts; distinct from mental illness; NT terms for possession; Matt 12:43-45 empty house; Acts 19 sons of Sceva; deliverance through the gospel, not exorcism formula.
42-58, Jesus' Authority over Demons, Part 3 (4:31-37): demons fallen angels; Gen 6, Jude 6; demons hide, Christ's presence exposes them; first miracle Luke records; strong man bound; purpose (Rev 20 binding and lake of fire), person ("Holy One of God"; silencing their testimony, 4:41), power (a word, no ritual); critique of Dickason, Unger, Neil Anderson on demonized believers.
42-59, Messiah Jesus, the Great Deliverer (4:38-44; title on gty page; list title "Jesus: The Divine Deliverer, Part 1"; text expounds 38-40): signs in body, mind, soul; Peter's wife (1 Cor 9:5); fever rebuked, instant service; six marks of Jesus' healing (word/touch, instant, total, all who came, organic disease, raised dead); healing gift a temporary sign gift; critique of modern healers; sunset after Sabbath; about twenty healings from Abraham to Isaiah, none from Isaiah to Christ.
42-60, Jesus: The Divine Deliverer, Part 2 (4:38-44): three realms: natural, supernatural, eternal; Mark 8:22-25 explained; prayer for healing (Jas 5:16) answered providentially; Boice's death; his wife Patricia's accident and recovery; demons silenced (confusion, publicity, political uprising; Acts 16:16-18); minds blinded (2 Cor 10:4-5; 4:4); v43 kingdom = realm of salvation, preaching the purpose; v44 "Judea" = whole Jewish land.
