# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Romans 13 (Grace to You)
Codes/dates from series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7349/romans-vol-09 . 45-97, 45-98, 45-100, 45-102, 45-104, 45-106 not read (45-100 robots-disallowed).
45-99, The Christian's Responsibility to Government Part 3 (13:1-2): in world not of it; early critics (Celsus, Porphyry, Julian, Tacitus on Nero "hatred of the human race"), Origen, Tertullian; believers in govt service (Matt 8, Luke 19, Acts 10, 13, 16, 18; Joseph, Daniel); "no Christian nation, only Christian people"; 1 Tim 2:1-4, Titus 3:1, 1 Pet 2:12-17; exception Acts 4-5; reasons 1-3 for submission; Exod 21:23-25, Num 35:19, Deut 22:18-19, Job 31:11.
45-101, Answering Tough Questions About the Christian and Government (13:1-5): Q&A; sword = taking of life; Gen 9, lex talionis; murder vs killing; Cain (Gen 4 vs 9); war Luke 14:31; Isa 33:22; imprecatory psalms; submission of workers Eph 6:5, Col 3:22.
45-103, Paying Your Taxes, Part 2 (13:6-7): recap reasons (Korah Num 16); leitourgos (Heb 1:7,14); apodidomi, phoros, telos, phobos, time; 1 Pet 2:17; 1 Tim 5:17-18, Deut 25:4; Dan 4:17,25,32; Dan 5; Jer 22:13-17; Isa 10:1-2; Amos; Culver, Justin Martyr, Tertullian, Ottley, Epistle to Diognetus.
45-105, Love Fulfills the Law, Part 2 (13:8-10): debt recap, Ps 37:21, Lev 25:35-37, Deut 23:19-20, Neh 5, Rom 5:5; commandments in LXX Deut 5 order; Lev 19:18; Exod 20 four/six; Matt 22:37-40; Matt 5:17; James 2 royal law; 1 Cor 13.
45-107, Put on the Lord Jesus Christ, Part 2 (13:11-14): positional vs practical; wake up, throw off, put on; kairos; Titus 2:11-13; Manton; soldier image, Eph 6; euschemonos, komos, methe, koite, aselgeia, eris, zelos; Gal 5:19-21; Job 24:13-17; 1 John 3:2-3; 1 Thess 5:1-9. Date 1985-04-21 from series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7350/romans-vol-10 ; that page lists the range as 13:12-14, while the sermon page gives primary text 13:11-14 and treats v.11 (kairos), so registered as 11-14.
