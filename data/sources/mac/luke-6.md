# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 6 (Grace to You)
42-69, Jesus: The Divine Truth-Teller (6:1-5; 2001-04-08 per series page Luke Vol. 06): page robots-blocked in all URL forms tried; not used.
42-70, Divine Truth Confronts Human Tradition (series page: 6:6-11, 2001-04-22). Read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-70 and www.gty.org/sermons/42-70/divine-truth-confronts-human-tradition. The sermon opens by re-expounding 6:1-5, so registered as 1-11. vv1-5: Torah allowed eating grain from a field; Pharisees counted picking as reaping and rubbing as grinding; David and the showbread (1 Sam 21); "Lord of the Sabbath"; Jesus not a Sabbatarian but defying Judaism's traditions; Sabbath = rest (Exod 20), fulfilled in Christ (Matt 11:28; Col 2:16-17; Heb 3-4). vv6-11: Luke alone "right hand"; they wanted accusation not meaning; Jesus reads hearts (5:22; 9:47; 11:17); question exposes them; rabbis allowed healing only if life at risk; Isa 1:11-17; 58:6-7, 13; anoia = rage "without understanding"; John 5:18; 1 Maccabees Sabbath massacre; hatred of truth fits God's plan; Acts 15:5 some Pharisees believed.
42-71 to 42-83 (apostles, 6:12-19): robots-blocked or not reachable; not used.
42-84, The Character of a True Christian, Part 1 (title from gty search listing; page shows 6:20-49, this message vv20-26; date not shown): mixed crowd (John 2:23-25; 6:66; 8:31); makarioi vs ouai as authoritative decrees, two states only; ptochos beggar; Isa 61; 66:2; Luke 18:13; Deut 27-28; hunger chortazo fully filled (Ps 42:1-2; 63:1; 107:9; Luke 1:53; 13:29; 14:15 messianic banquet); weeping godly sorrow (Jas 4:9; 2 Cor 7:10; Jer 31:13); woes: comfort in full now; kingdom present (Rom 14:17) and future.
42-85 (6:20-26 incl. 22-23): page read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-85 but it shows no title or date, so not registered and not used (title not invented).
42-86, The Commands of Kingdom Love (6:27-28 in this message; date not shown): "you who hear" (1 Cor 2:14); four commands: love (attitude), do good (action), bless (speech), pray (appeal); Jewish context: Essenes hate sons of darkness, Pharisaic maxims, Zealots; OT standard: Deut 32:35; Exod 23:4-5; Job 31:29; Prov 25:21; Lev 19:18; Luke 10:25-37; Rom 12:17-20; Stephen (Acts 7) and Jesus on cross (Luke 23:34); illustration of a former pastor visiting his son's killer.
42-87 to 42-89 (6:29-45): robots-blocked or not reachable; not used.
42-90, The Ultimate Religious Decision (6:46-49; date not shown): "Lord, Lord" (kurios = Jehovah in LXX) without obedience = profanity of the sanctuary; come, hear, act; dig deep, rock; house = religious life, storm = divine judgment, rock = God, foundation = Christ (1 Cor 3:11); audience devout Jews: close is not enough; Matt 7:21-27; Rom 10:9-10; John 8:31; Jas 1:22; Acts 4:12; 2 Thess 1; Peter Kreeft's inclusivism criticised.
