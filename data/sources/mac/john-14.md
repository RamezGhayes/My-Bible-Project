# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 14 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles, ranges and dates: 43-72 from the gty series page "John, Vol. 6" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V6/john-vol-6); 43-73 to 43-78 from "John, Vol. 7" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V7/john-vol-7). Sermon pages show the same titles.
43-72, Heavenly Promises (14:1-6), 2015-02-15: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/43-72. v1 claim to deity, trust the unseen (1 Pet 1:8); Father's house = heaven, the original of the temple (Heb 9:23; Rev 21); v3 eschatological: second coming and rapture of the church (1 Thess 4; 1 Cor 15), timing unknown, also applies the "receive" wording to death (Stephen, Acts 7); v6 exclusivity, against inclusivism; Spirit's indwelling better than bodily presence (16:7).
43-73, Powerful Promises (14:7-14), 2015-04-12: read at .../43-73. Disciples knew titles but not full meaning; Philip wants a visible God; words and works as evidence; greater works = greater in extent, primarily apostles, by the Spirit (Acts 1:8); "in my name" not a formula but alignment with his will (1 John 5:14); faith over sight.
43-74, The Promise of the Holy Spirit, Part 1 (14:15-19), 2015-04-19: read at .../43-74. Promises to those who love and obey; parakletos broader than "comforter"; allos vs heteros; "with you" = Spirit in Christ's ministry, "in you" after departure; Spirit of truth, revelatory role, NT and inerrancy; heaven as relationship, a foretaste now.
43-75, The Promise of the Holy Spirit, Part 2 (14:15-24), 2015-04-26: read at .../43-75. Not orphans: Jesus comes in the Spirit; because I live (resurrection life); "in that day" from Pentecost; Judas' question reflects expectation of a visible kingdom; Trinitarian indwelling of every believer; against experiential mysticism; obedience as evidence (Luke 6:46).
43-76, The Illuminating Role of the Holy Spirit (14:24-26), 2015-05-03: read at .../43-76. Truth as God's gift; disciples understood later (2:22; 12:16); primary sense: Spirit enabling apostles to recall and write the NT (Acts 11:16; 2 Pet 1:21); secondary: illumination of every believer (1 John 2:20-27), not extra-biblical revelation; Spirit glorifies Christ.
43-77, Supernatural Peace (14:27), 2015-05-10: read at .../43-77. Shalom and eirene; peace objective (reconciliation, Rom 5:1) and subjective; "my peace" from Christ, rests on trust (Isa 26:3); world's peace false (Isa 48:22; Jer 6:14); pursued by righteousness, thankful prayer, right thinking (Phil 4).
43-78, What the Cross Meant to Christ (14:28-31), 2015-05-17: read at .../43-78. Disciples' grief self-focused; four things: person dignified (return to glory), truth documented (fulfilled prophecy), enemy defeated (Satan finds nothing in him, Gen 3:15), love demonstrated (obedience, Luke 22:42); "Father greater" per Athanasian Creed, as to manhood, Phil 2; "arise" = leaving for Gethsemane, teaching continues.
Blocked/untitled: none.
Theology note: 43-72 explicitly reads 14:3 as including the rapture of the church; reported with a neutral note.
