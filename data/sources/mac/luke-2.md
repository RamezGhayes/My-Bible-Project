# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 2 (Grace to You)
Dates from series pages Luke Vol. 02 and Vol. 03.
42-22, Jesus' Birth in Bethlehem, Part 1 (2:1-7, focus 1-3): taxation census, decree ~8 BC, birth ~6-4 BC; God directs pagan rulers (Cyrus, Nebuchadnezzar); Mic 5:2; Gal 4:4; Augustus "Savior of the world".
42-23, Jesus' Birth in Bethlehem, Part 2 (2:1-7): national setting: David's line, Bethlehem (1 Sam 16; Gen 35:19; Mic 5:2; Matt 2); kataluma = guest quarters; manger; swaddling = full humanity; condescension (Phil 2). (Firstborn/siblings remark not used.)
42-24, God, the Savior of Men (2:11): not fetched; not used.
42-25, The Announcement of Jesus' Birth, Part 1 (2:8-11): shepherds lowest class, Isa 61; sacrificial flocks; glory (Exod 40; Ezek 8-10; Exod 33); fear (Isa 6; Ezek 1; Rev 1:17); Gen 15:1; euaggelizo; all people (Isa 42:6; 49:6; 60); Savior (Matt 1:21; Luke 19:10; John 4:42; 1 John 4:14; Acts 4:12).
42-26, The Announcement of Jesus' Birth, Part 2 (2:8-14): Savior from sin; Christ king/priest/prophet (Dan 9:25-26; 1 Tim 2:5; Heb 1); Lord = Yahweh (LXX), Rom 10:9; Isa 9:6; sign; peace = reconciliation; good pleasure = God's sovereign choice; Rev 4-5.
42-27, The Announcement of Jesus' Birth, Part 3 (2:15-21): revelation, faith, action, witness, reflection, praise, obedience; wonder not salvation; Mary and 2:35; Gen 17:11-12; Lev 12.
42-28, Testifying to Jesus: Joseph & Mary (2:21-24): Gal 4:4; Num 13:16; Lev 12:1-8; Exod 13; Num 18:15-16; poor offering before Magi; Mary's need of Savior (1:46-47).
42-29, Testifying to Jesus: Simeon, Part 1 (2:25-30): robots-blocked in all forms; not used. So 25-28 and 29-30 have no mac entry.
42-30, Testifying to Jesus: Simeon, Part 2 (2:31-35): Gentiles equal; Isa 9:1-2; 42:6; 49:6; 52:10; 60; 45:25; 46:13; Acts 4:12; Jonah; first negative note; Isa 8:14; Isa 53; John 1:11; John 19:25; hearts revealed.
42-31, Testifying to Jesus: Anna (2:36-38): Hannah = grace; prophetess not new revelation; Miriam, Deborah, Huldah; Asher, 2 Chr 30; 1 Sam 1; remnant.
42-32, The Amazing Child Who Was God, Part 1 (2:39-46): Matt 2 omitted; full humanity, Mark 6:3, Heb 4:15, 5:8; Hendriksen vs apocrypha; Exod 12; 23:17; 34:22-23; Deut 16:16; Exod 12:26-27; child to boy.
42-33, The Amazing Child Who Was God, Part 2 (2:47-52): v49 crux, divine Sonship, break from earthly family (Luke 8:19-21; 11:27-28); wonder 2:18, 33; sword 2:35; Luke 9:45; fifth commandment; Heb 4:15; 5:8-9; titles progression.
42-34, Part 3 (2:49): not fetched; not used.
