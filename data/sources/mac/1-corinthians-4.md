# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Corinthians 4 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Codes, titles, ranges, dates from the series pages https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7132/1-corinthians-vol-01 and https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7133/1-corinthians-vol-02 .
1820, Examining the Servants of Christ (4:1-5), 1975-09-14: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1820/examining-the-servants-of-christ. Chs 1-2 wisdom, 3-4 leaders; judge only doctrine (Rom 16:17) and sin; hyperetes lowest oarsman (Luke 1:2; Acts 26:16; Acts 20:19); oikonomos (Gen 43-44; Isa 22; 1 Pet 4:10; Titus 1:7); mysteries, whole counsel (Acts 20:27; 2 Cor 4:2; 2 Cor 2:17; 2 Tim 2:15); stewardess and waiter; faithfulness (Num 12:7; Col 1:7; 4:7; Matt 24:45; 25:21); critique of therapy-pulpit pastor; anakrino; self-evaluation biased (2 Cor 3:18); judge nothing before time (1 Cor 3:12-15; 2 Cor 5:9-10; Rev 22:12; Rom 8:1; 1 Cor 10:31).
1821, Humility and Conceit (4:6-13), 1975-09-21: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1821/humility-and-conceit. Humility theme; Abraham, Jacob, Moses, Gideon, John Baptist, Peter, Christ; 1 Tim 5:17; puffed up like frog, present = stop; Num 11:26-29 Eldad and Medad; diakrino; Eph 2:8-9; Jas 1:17; Eph 4:8,11; pianos tuned to one standard; full (Matt 5:6), rich (Rev 3:17), reigned; ophelon; spectacle, Moffatt doomed gladiators, Mark 9:33-35; babbler Acts 17:18; no Greek/Latin word for humility; buffeted = fists; Matt 8:20; 2 Cor 11:23-27; offscouring = scraped residue.
1822, How to Be a Spiritual Father (4:14-21), 1975-10-05: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1822. Metaphors of ministry; agapetos, noutheteo; Eli (1 Sam 2-3, LXX word); paidagogos, hyperbole; ego emphatic; Jas 1:18; 1 Pet 1:23; John 3; mimetai (1 Cor 11:1; Phil 4:9); Timothy; basileia, dynamis, logos; rod or gentleness; 1 Thess 2 nursing mother; Matt 11:29; six marks (begets, loves, admonishes, example, teaches, disciplines); Ryle on simple preaching; Augustine wooden key; Stott; Hodge.
