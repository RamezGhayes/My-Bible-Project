# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 15 (Grace to You)

41-80, The Shameful Scorn of Jesus Christ (15:15-21): "handed over" technical; praetorium; parody of coronation; faded military red; Caesar greeting; Isa 50:6; Mark 10:33-34; 14:65; Philo; Simon compelled (Matt 5:41), Cyrenian Jews (Acts 2:10; 6; 11:20; 13:1); Plutarch crosspiece; Rufus Rom 16:13, sons known to Roman church.
41-81, Divine Mercy for the Blasphemers (15:22-39, mainly 22-32): Golgotha site uncertain; Prov 31:6, Ps 69:21, Matt 27:34; Ps 22:18; third hour, death at time of Passover lambs; Lev 24:16; John 19:19-22; robbers perhaps Barabbas's men; Isa 53:12; Ps 22 head-wagging; leaders later bribed soldiers; Isa 53:10; Acts 6:7; Justin; 1 Cor 1; Zech 12:10; Rev 1:7; John 19:11; Angola prison anecdote; 33-39 brief, deferred.
41-82, God Visits Calvary (15:22-41, focus 33-41): darkness = God's presence in judgment (Joel, Amos, Zephaniah; Ex 10:21-22); bore hell's wrath three hours; cry = sense of absence after wrath; doubled address (Gen 22, Ex 3...); Mal 4:5-6; Ps 69:21; not exhaustion (John 10:18; 19:30; Luke 23:46); veil, earthquake (Matt 27:51-53; Ps 18:7; Nah 1:5); centurion first Gentile convert, Luke 23:47; first human confession in Mark (1:1, 1:11, 9:7; John 20:31); women identified, Matt 10:3, 27:56; only women and angels (1:13) minister to him.
41-83, How God Buried His Son (15:42-47): Joseph secret disciple; Nicodemus (John 19:39-40); Isa 53:9; Ps 34:20; Ex 12:46; Num 9:12; Zech 12:10; Ps 69:20; Deut 21:22-23; Matt 27:57-66; Luke 23:50-56; Matt 12:40; Rev 1:18.
