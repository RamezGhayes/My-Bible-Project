# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 20 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Titles and ranges confirmed from the monergism Luke 20 listing (monergism.com/topics/.../luke-%E2%80%94-reformed-sermons-bible-19, mp3 links webmedia.gty.org/sermons/High/42-2xx.mp3) and preceptaustin.org/luke-20-commentary (links with gty slugs). No dates shown anywhere read. gty pages themselves show no title.
42-244, Rejecting the King's Authority (20:1-8; date not shown): authority = exousia vs dunamis; Jesus never sought permission from human authorities, two temple cleansings (John 2; Luke 19:45-46); Passion Week timeline (Wednesday); v1-2 ephistemi = pounce; chief priests, scribes, elders = Sanhedrin, rivals united against Him; question about cleansing/entry (John 2:18). v3-7 counter-question, rabbinic method; John's baptism as proselyte-style call; Luke 7:29-30; leaders trapped, "we don't know". v8 refusal as judgment: rejected light gets no more (Gen 6:3; Neh 9:30; Isa 63:10; Luke 23:8-9 Herod). Gospel still open (Acts 2).
42-245, The Murder of God's Son: A Prophetic Parable, Part 1 (20:9-18; text expounds 9-16): Wednesday; Matt 21:33-41; owner = God, vineyard = Israel (Isa 5:1-7; Ps 80:8-16; Jer 2:21), tenants = spiritual leaders, slaves = prophets Moses to John, son = Christ, long journey = OT history; rejected prophets (Jer 7:23-25; 25:4-11; Matt 23:29-37; Luke 11:49-51; 13:34-35; Acts 7:51-52; Heb 11:37); Justin Martyr; Talmud on land claims; leaders knew who He was but rejected Him from pride (John 12:42); v16 destroy = AD 70, end of priesthood and temple.
42-246, The Murder of God's Son: A Prophetic Parable, Part 2 (20:9-18): destruction (AD 70 under Titus, genealogies lost) and displacement: stewardship of truth passes to the apostles and the church (Matt 21:43; Luke 9-10; Matt 16:18-19; Eph 2:20; 3:2-6; 1 Cor 4:1; John 14-16 Spirit and Scripture); future restoration of Israel (Rev 7; 14; Zech 8) [dispensational]; v17-18 Ps 118:22 cornerstone, Dan 2, Acts 4:10-11, Isa 8:14-15; 28:16, Rom 9:32-33, 1 Pet 2:6-8; broken or crushed; v19 they seek arrest not repentance.
42-247, A Diagnosis of the Christ-Rejecters (20:19-26): coalition of rivals (Mark 3:6; Matt 22:15) wanting Rome to execute Him as insurrectionist; six sins: hatred, pride (Matt 21:46; 23), hypocrisy (spies pretending righteous), flattery, deception (tax trap), stubbornness (silence, then lie in Luke 23:1-2). Tax background: Judas of Galilee, AD 66 revolt; denarius with divine titles of emperor, a little idol; render: civil government legitimate (Rom 13; 1 Pet 2:13-17), limit Acts 5:29; two kingdoms, no theocratic society now; coin bears Caesar's image, you bear God's.
42-248, The Savior Silences the Sadducees (20:27-40): universal sense of afterlife (Egyptians, Greeks, Franklin's epitaph); Jewish hope of resurrection (2 Macc; Ps 16:9-11; 49:15; 17:15; Job; Dan 12:2); Sadducees deny resurrection, angels, spirits (Acts 23:8), Pentateuch only, wealthy temple controllers allied with Rome, vanished after AD 70; levirate (Deut 25:5-10; Gen 38; Ruth); Matt 22:29 ignorance of Scripture and power; marriage belongs to this age; like angels, sons of resurrection (Hebrew idiom of nature); 1 Cor 15:35ff; Phil 3:21; "counted worthy" by grace through faith; Exod 3:6 present tense, patriarchs live (Gen 26:24; 28:13); scribes "well said", Matt 22:33 astonishment, no more questions.
42-249, David's Son and LORD (20:41-44): final evangelistic appeal (Mark 12:34; Luke 19:41); "son of David" correct but incomplete (2 Sam 7:12-14; Ps 89; Amos 9:11; Mic 5:2; genealogies Matt 1, Luke 3, records lost AD 70); Ps 110:1 understood as messianic, footstool (Josh 10:24-26), spoken in the Spirit (Mark 12:36; Acts 4:25); Messiah must be God become man (John 20:28); deity of Christ essential (1 John 2:22); older brother of Luke 15 as the leaders.
42-250, Confronting Error with Condemnation, Not Conversation (20:45-47): title from preceptaustin link (gty slug confirms); false teachers warnings (Acts 20:29-30; 2 Cor 11:13-15; 2 Pet 2); Jesus turns to disciples; critique of "conversation" with error (Emerging Church); caution: beware, separate (Ps 1:1); six marks: long robes/tassels (Num 15:38-40; Matt 23:5), greetings and titles (Matt 23:7-10), chief seats, places of honor, devouring widows' houses through fees and estates (Exod 22:22; Deut 10:18; Mal 3:5; Luke 16:14), long prayers for show (Matt 6:5-6); greater condemnation (Heb 10:29-31).
