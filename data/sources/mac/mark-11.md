# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 11 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Dates mostly not shown on fetched pages.
41-56, The False Coronation of the True King (11:1-11): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/41-56 (preach date not shown). Monday Nisan 10, lamb selection day; Dan 9:24-27 483 years from Artaxerxes' decree (445 BC); colt shows omniscience; Zech 9:9 (Matt 21:4-5); coats = submission (2 Kgs 9 Jehu); Solomon's mule 1 Kgs 1 (not main point); Ps 118:26 conqueror's psalm sung for Maccabeus; crowd expects political deliverance, "mob hysteria"; John 12:12-16; palms Rev 7:9; v11 looked around = inspecting before cleansing; true coronation Phil 2:9-11, Heb, Rev 19-20.
41-57, Nothing but Leaves (11:12-21; Jan 16, 2011 shown on page): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-57. Tuesday; curse on temple, leaders, nation; temple history Gen 22, 1 Chr 21, Ezra 6:15, AD 70; hunger = humanity; leaves before figs = false profession; only destructive miracle; Luke 13:6 fig tree parable; Deut 28 curses; Matt 23; cleansing: Court of Gentiles, priests rejected animals, exchange markup, doves overpriced exploiting poor (Lev 12); John 2:13-17 earlier cleansing, Ps 69:9; Isa 56:7; Jer 7:11; 1 Kgs 8:29-30, 41-43 house of prayer for foreigners; issue is worship not social reform; Luther; John 4; Rom 10; withered from roots = rotten core; true worship only at the cross.
41-58, The Necessities for Effective Prayer (11:22-25, with 11:20-21 context and v26 note): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-58. Wednesday; Mark 13:2; five components: historical (remember: Deut, Isa 46, Ps 77, 78, 105, 143), theological (God's will: Matt 6:9-10; 1 John 5:14; Jas 4:15), spiritual (believe), practical (ask), moral (forgive); mountain hyperbole, rabbis "mountain movers"; doubt = doubting God; critique of faith movement; Jas 1:6; Matt 14:29-31; 17:20; faith empty hand; Matt 7:7-11; Jas 4:2-3; Mark 14:36; John 14:12-14; 15:7; standing posture; forgiveness for fellowship not salvation (John 13); Matt 6:14-15; 18; Eph 4:32; v26 not in early MSS, from Matt 6:15; Acts 1-2 praying church.
41-59, The Confrontation Over Authority (11:27-33): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-59. Wednesday; Luke's verb "attack"; exousia; hoped for blasphemy charge; counter-question rabbinic; John's baptism forces choice; fear of stoning (Luke 20:6); "we do not know" = failure of duty; Jesus' refusal a judgment (Hos 4:17; Matt 7:6); authority from Father (Matt 28:18; John 5:19, 27, 30; 10:18; Mark 1:22, 27; 2:10); Luke 7:28-30; John 2:18; believers speak with authority by the Word (Titus 2:15).
