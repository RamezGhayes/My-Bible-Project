# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Corinthians 11 (Grace to You)
1844, The Subordination and Equality of Women (11:2-16 announced; transcript covers 2-6): critiques Christian feminism (Gal 3:28; 1 Pet 3:1, 7); 1 Tim 2:11-14 grounded in creation and Fall, not culture; 1 Cor 14:34-37; Acts 21:8-9; Corinthian veil = married woman's submission and modesty, unveiled associated with prostitutes; Eerdman on Roman protests; Amman example; dress custom cultural, principle permanent; ordinances = tradition (2 Thess 2:15); Paul corrects little doctrine except ch.15; "I would have you know" (Col 2:1); head = rule, authority; Eph 1:22-23; 4:15; 5:23; Col 1:18; Matt 28:18; Phil 2:10-11; 1 Cor 15:25; Heb 2:8; Christ submits yet one with Father; Shakespeare quote; Jewish men covered, Maimonides; covering cultural; praying = to God, prophesying = about God to people; women not speaking in the assembly (14:34); distinction of the sexes in appearance.
1845, The Role of the Godly Woman (11:2-16; covers 7-16): man image and glory of God (Gen 1:26; Eph 4:24); Moses' veil (Exod 33; 2 Cor 3:13) misread by rabbis; woman fully in God's image (Gen 1:27) but responsive role; Gen 2:18, 21-24; exousia = symbol of authority; angels watch (Heb 1; Eph 3); 11-12 balance (Gal 3:28; 1 Tim 2:15); phusis = nature/instinct; Ken Anderson on hair growth and hormones; Aristotle quote; Roman writers, early councils, catacombs; Deut 22:5; no specific length; hats cultural; v.16 universal practice of churches.
1846, The Celebration of the Lord's Supper, Part 1 (11:17-22), 1976-05-09: Passover background; Mark 14:22-25; paraggello = command; Acts 2:41-46; 20:7, 11; love feast cultural holdover; eranos; ekklesia people not building; schismata (John 7:43; 9:16); 1 Cor 1:10; 3:1-3; dei = divine necessity; hairesis (Acts 24; Gal 5:20); dokimoi (1 Thess 2:4; Jas 1:12; 1 John 2:19); Matt 18:7; Luke 17:1-2; Titus 3:10; ouk esti + inf = impossible; 1 Cor 10:16-17; Eph 2 walls; Gal 2:10; Thessalonians model (1 Thess 1:2-3; 4:9); Heb 10.
1847, The Celebration of the Lord's Supper, Part 2 (11:23-34), 1976-05-16: revelation from the Lord, earliest written account; John 13 betrayal contrast; Passover seder (kiddush, haroset, Hallel Ps 113-118, four cups); Christ crucified at Passover; estin = represents (John 10; Matt 13); rejects transubstantiation and consubstantiation; "for you"; "broken" not in better manuscripts, John 19 no bone broken; diatheke, blood covenants; remembrance in Hebrew sense; proclamation, eschatological; ways of coming unworthily; flag analogy; self-examination; body in v.29 = physical body (church reading possible); krima vs katakrima (v.32); quotes "whom the Lord loves He chastens" (no reference given); weak, sick, sleep escalating; Acts 5; John 6:51-54; Luke 22:7.
