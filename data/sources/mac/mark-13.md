# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 13 (Grace to You)
41-66, The Grim Reality of the Last Days (13:1-13): Wednesday of Passion Week; disciples expected kingdom (Isa 9, Zech 14); structure 1-2/3-13/14-23/24ff; AD 70; Luke 19:40-44, Matt 23:38-39; parousia Matt 24:3, Acts 1:6; rejects postmillennialism and preterism; false christs (Camping etc.); long section on Islamic eschatology (not used); birth pangs intensify; persecution Jewish (2 Cor 11:24), Gentile, family; John 15:18, 16:2, 2 Tim 3:12; promises Acts 1:8, Luke 21:15; endurance proves faith, salvation by grace, 1 John 2:19. Date not shown on page.
41-67, The Future Tribulation (13:14-23): "let the reader understand" = future readers; abomination; Antiochus 167 BC type (Dan 11:31); Dan 9:27 covenant broken mid-week; rebuilt temple; rejects AD 70 fulfilment; flight, woe, winter/Sabbath; days shortened; elect not deceived, John 10:5; Rev 6, 8-9, 12, 16; pretrib rapture. Date not shown.
41-68, Coming in the Clouds (13:24-27): after the tribulation (Dan 12 1,290/1,335, ~75 days); cosmic signs, Isa 13, 24, 34, Joel 2:10, 31, 3:15, Hag 2:6, Zeph 1:14-18; sign is the Son of Man himself, not a cross (Cyril of Jerusalem, Chrysostom, Origen); Dan 7:13; visible coming Rev 1:7, Acts 1:11; 2 Thess 1:5-10; Jude 14-15; not AD 70. Date not shown.
41-69, The Final Generation, Part 1 (13:28-37 announced; mainly 28-30, 32): manthano "learn"; "you" = generation seeing signs; rejects AD 70, Jesus mistaken, Jewish race, sinners, Hal Lindsey 1948; fig imagery Jer 24:2, Hos 9:10, Joel 1:6-7, Mark 11:12-14; v32 voluntary limitation; 75 days Dan 12; pretrib arguments (Rev 3:10, John 14:1-3, 1 Thess 4). Page shows no date; it says 28-37 "this morning and tonight", and Part 2 is dated April 3, 2011, so date given as 2011-04-03 (inferred; coordinator please verify).
41-70, The Final Generation, Part 2 (13:28-37), April 3, 2011: fig tree, Luke 21:29; v31 Matt 5:18, Luke 16:17, Isa 40:8, 2 Pet 3:3-13, Gen 6-7; v32 Phil 2, John 15:15, Acts 1:7; four Roman watches, echoed in Mark 14-15; Gethsemane sleep Mark 14:34-41; Luke 21:34-36; evangelism a rescue operation; date-setters (Millerites, etc.).
