# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 21 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. gty pages show no title or date; titles and ranges taken from link texts on preceptaustin.org/luke-21-commentary (which link each code). No dates found.
42-251, Abusing the Poor (21:1-4): Wednesday; rejects usual "sacrificial giving" readings as imposed on the text; widow as victim of a corrupt system that taught alms buy merit and devoured widows (20:46-47; Corban, Mark 7:8-13); "He looked up" after the woes of Matt 23; Court of Women, 13 trumpet receptacles; penichros to ptochos; James 1:27; applications: indulgences, prosperity preachers exploiting the poor.
42-252, Jesus' Description of the Temple's Destruction (21:5-7; title per preceptaustin link text): disciples admire temple after the woes (Matt 23:37-24:1; Luke 13:34-35); question when/sign (Matt 24:3 parousia = presence); Jewish expectations from Zech 12-14 and intertestamental books (Schürer, 2 Baruch, 4 Ezra): tribulation, forerunner, final war, restored Jerusalem, "sound like dispensationalists"; disciples expected everything at once, no gap (Acts 1:6); AD 70 "a historical footnote", vv10-11 and 25-27 not fulfilled then; Israel desolate ~2000 years until Zech 12:10.
42-256, The World in Conflict and Distress (21:9-11; text treats 7-11): longest answer to a question; v8 deception (Josephus on messiahs under Felix, Gnostic gospels, Da Vinci Code), false Christianity will prevail popularly; v9 wars the mark of history, "not immediately"; v10-11 nation against nation, earthquakes, plagues/famines (loimoi/limoi), terrors and heavenly signs linked to Revelation 6-9, 16; birth pains (Matt 24:8); AD 70 at most partial; rejects postmillennialism; pre-trib rapture mentioned.
42-257, The Persecution and Endurance of Christians, Part 1 (21:12-19; read via www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-257 after other URLs were robots-blocked): fires as preview of judgment (Rev 8:7; 16:8); v12 synagogues as local courts with scourging; fulfilment in Acts (4; 5; 7; 8; 12; 16; 21-28); v13 opportunity for testimony (Phil 1:12-13); v14-15 do not rehearse, Calvin's counsel to ministers facing martyrdom (Matt 10:17-20; Luke 12:11-12); v16-17 family betrayal (John 15:18-21; 16:1-4); v18 safe in God's care even in death; Clement of Rome; 2 Tim 3:12; Foxe.
42-258, The Persecution and Endurance of Christians, Part 2 (21:12-19): Jewish persecution ~AD 30-70, Gentile persecution continuing; purpose: scattering for evangelism (Acts 1:8; 8:1) and proving faith through triumphant martyrdom; Roman motives political, religious, social, economic (Acts 16; 19); Nero, Domitian, Polycarp ("eighty and six years"), Decius, Diocletian; Pliny, Tacitus, Suetonius; Hus; estimates of martyrs; tribulation martyrs (Rev 6:9-11; 7:9-14; 13:7).
42-259, The Undying Faith of Christians Facing Death (21:18-19): v18 a proverb of safety (1 Sam 14:45; 2 Sam 14:11; Acts 27:34), spiritual not physical survival; v19 endurance; Matt 24:9-13 falling away = never regenerate (1 John 2:19); "perseverance of faith": saving faith a gift that does not fail (Eph 2:8-9; John 6:37-40; 10:27-29; 1 Cor 1:8-9; 1 Pet 1:3-7; Rom 8:35-39; Jude 24); Westminster Confession on not falling totally nor finally.
42-260, The Terrors of the Great Tribulation, Part 1 (21:20-24; read via www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-260): church age of deception, disaster, persecution as birth pains; Great Tribulation (Matt 24:21; Rev seals/trumpets/bowls; 144,000, two witnesses); rejects AD 70, Crusades, 1948/1967 as the fulfilment of v20-24; sign = end-time armies around Jerusalem after the abomination of desolation (Matt 24:15; Dan 9:27, Antichrist breaks seven-year covenant at midpoint); Antiochus IV as type (Dan 11:30-31); 2 Thess 2:3-4; Rev 13:15; Zech 12; 14.
42-261, The Terrors of the Great Tribulation, Part 2 (21:20-24): God staked His reputation on prediction (Isa 41:21-23; 44:6-7; 46:9-11); v20 final desolation, not merely AD 70; v22 days of vengeance = day of the Lord; Zech 13:8-9 (two thirds perish, one third refined); Jer 30:1-8; 3.5 years (Dan 12:7; Rev 11:2-3; 12:6, 14; 13:5); Dan 12:11-12 75 days; pre-trib rapture (Rev 3:10); Christ's feet on the Mount of Olives; millennium then new creation; 2 Pet 3:11-14 holiness.
42-262, Celestial Signs of the Coming Savior (21:25-28; preceptaustin lists it under vv25-28, text expounds 25-28): sequence vv7-24, seven-year tribulation (Dan 9) with midpoint; "after the tribulation" (Matt 24:29); cosmic "staging" (Isa 13:6-13; 24; Joel 2-3; Ezek 38:19-22; Hag 2:6-7; Zeph 1:14-18; Rev 6:12-17; 8; 16); return like ascension (Acts 1:9-11); 2 Thess 1:5-10; unrepentant still blaspheme; now is the day of salvation.
42-264, The Final Generation of the Future Judgment (21:29-33): fig tree an analogy not an allegory ("and all the trees"); "you" = believers who see the signs; rejects five views of "this generation" (the disciples; AD 70; the Jewish race; an evil generation; the 1948 generation); his view: the generation alive in the tribulation after the rapture sees it all; pre-trib arguments (Rev 1-3 vs 4-19; Rev 3:10; 1 Thess 4; populating the millennium); v33 word unfailing (Isa 40:8; Matt 5:17-18; 1 Pet 1:23-25).
42-265, The Believer's Gift to Christ (21:34-36; text also surveys 25-33): Christmas contrast; four gifts to the returning King: vigilant anticipation, spiritual separation (bareo heavy hearts, dissipation, drunkenness, cares), evangelistic occupation (judgment on all the earth), faithful continuation (pray to escape and stand); ekpheugo read as support for pre-trib rapture; Matt 24:36-51; 25; Titus 2:13; Rom 13:11-14; 1 John 2:28; 3:2-3.
42-266, God's Preparation for Christ's Cross (21:37-22:2; registered ch 21 vv37-38 because 42-267 covers 22:1-6): cross the climax of redemptive history (1 Cor 2:2); OT foreshadowings (Gen 3:21; 22; Exod 12; Num 21); Jesus foretold His death (Luke 9:22, 44); v37-38 days teaching in temple, nights on Olivet; God's sovereign timetable behind leaders, Satan, Judas, disciples; earlier failed attempts on His life (Luke 4:28-30; John 7:30, 44-46); John 10:17-18; cross as human injustice and divine justice.
NOT USED: 42-253, 42-254, 42-255 (Second Coming series messages on 21:5-36, read but no title/range confirmable from any listing reached) and 42-263 (21:25-28, read, title not confirmed). 21:8 therefore has no mac entry (42-252 range ends at v7; 42-256 at vv9-11).
