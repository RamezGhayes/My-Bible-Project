# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 6 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26 at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>. gty series page 43V3/43V4 robots-blocked. Titles shown on each gty page; confirmed by Grace Church (gracechurch.org) series pages or sermonaudio GTY TV pages as noted. Dates from gracechurch.org where found.
43-32, Jesus Feeds the Multitudes (6:1-15): title on gty page; sermonaudio GTY TV "Jesus Feeds the Multitudes, Part 1" links gty 43-32, Bible text John 6:1-15. Date not found. Points: most massive miracle, in all four Gospels; creative miracle; rejects "sharing" explanation; meta tauta generic; withdrawal to Galilee; Philip tested, 200 denarii ~ eight months' wages; barley loaves, small pickled fish (Mark 6:38); groups of 50/100 (Mark 6:40); created food; twelve baskets; Deut 18:15-19; Acts 3:20-22; refused political/welfare messiah.
43-33, True and False Disciples, Part 1: title on gty page; gracechurch.org series 740 lists Part 1 as John 6:15-29, 2013-12-01 (registered so, although the sermon also covers 6:1-21). Points: mathetes = learner; marks of false disciples: attracted by crowds, fascinated by the supernatural, earthly benefits (king by force, John 18:36; 1 Sam 4 ark), no desire for worship (Matt 14:33); 1 John 2:19; Strange Fire critique of charismatic movement.
43-34, True and False Disciples, Part 2 (6:22-27), 2013-12-08: title on gty page; gracechurch.org sermon 9709 and series 740. Points: crowd crossing to Capernaum (Matt 4:13); Jesus doesn't answer; chortazo used of animals; seal = authentication; fifth mark personal prosperity; Heb 3-4, Ps 95; prosperity and social gospel.
43-35, True and False Disciples, Part 3 (6:28-34), 2013-12-15: title on gty page; gracechurch series 740. Points: demands on God (Simon Magus, Acts 8; positive confession); only work is believing, faith a miracle preceded by regeneration; manna (Exod 16; Neh 9; Ps 78; 105; Heb 3:3); bios vs zoe; seventh mark: not satisfied in Christ.
43-36, I Am the Bread of Life (6:32-59): title and the text John 6:32-59 stated on the gty page (Christmas Sunday sermon, date not shown); sermonaudio GTY TV "I Am the Bread of Life, Part 1/2" also John 6:32-59. Points: divine provision / human appropriation; first of seven "I am"; preexistence (John 1:1, 14; 3:13; 17:5); rejects heaven-tourism books; giving, drawing, receiving, keeping, raising (John 17:2, 6, 9, 24; 10:29); election before foundation of world; preacher only a tool; universal call in v40, preach to all since we don't know the elect; theoreo; eating = believing, personal, Spirit-produced hunger; blood = metonym for death; rejects Mass (Lev 17; Deut 12).
43-37, The Pathology of False Disciples, Part 1: title on gty page; gracechurch.org series 739 lists it with passage "John 6", 2014-01-05 (registered as 6:1-71). Points: chapter unit (6:1 and 7:1); seven marks; Jesus "the first Calvinist in the New Testament"; 37-40 paradox unresolved; gogguzo murmuring, rejection to mockery; teaching internal through the gospel; Matt 13:44-46; Phil 3; critique of contemporary evangelicalism and social gospel.
43-38, The Pathology of False Disciples, Part 2: title on gty page; gracechurch series 739, "John 6", 2014-01-12 (registered as 6:1-71). Points (reading from 6:48): flesh = death on the cross, only Savior; eating/drinking = embracing atoning death, union; Lev 17; rejects the Mass as a travesty on the one sacrifice; grumbling, stumbling, leaving; eighth mark; v65 with 37, 44; Judas; Matt 11:20-24; John 8:42-47; 10:24-26.
43-39, The Pathology of False Disciples, Part 3 (6:60-71), 2014-01-19: title on gty page; gracechurch.org sermon 9716 and series 739. Points: skleros = hard, offense in content; followed for works, left over words and the cross; withdrawal final; unbelief the damning sin; sovereignty and responsibility both held; Holy One of God (Mark 1:24; Luke 1:35); Judas prototype (John 13:21-30; Ps 41:9; Zech 11-13); Heb 10:23-39 apostasy warnings.
Blocked: none for John 6 at /library/. Theology note added on 43-36 (Lord's Supper, Reformed spiritual feeding). Note: MacArthur's reading of 6:37-45 (election, effectual drawing, preservation) matches the Reformed reading; no note needed.
