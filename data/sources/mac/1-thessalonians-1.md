# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Thessalonians 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27. Titles and ranges from the titled gty links at https://www.preceptaustin.org/1_thessalonians_1_resources (only source; gty pages show no title or date). Dates unknown, omitted.
52-1, Identifying the Elect, Part 1 (1:1-2), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-1 : key verse 1:4; Niebuhr; Thessalonica history (Cassander 315 BC, Egnatian Way, capital, vice); Acts 17 three Sabbaths; Paul not "apostle" here; ekklesia; one preposition = equality of Father and Son; election by sovereign love (Deut 7:7-8; John 15:16; Acts 13:48; Rom 9); grace and peace.
52-2, Identifying the Elect, Part 2 (1:3), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-2 (page text called the series "Identifying the Elect"): three marks: production (ergon; James 2; Eph 2:10), affection (kopos, agapao; 2 Cor 5:14; Gal 5:6), continuation (hupomone; Matt 13:20-21; 1 John 2:19; Jer 32:40); assurance from present reality not past event; rock singer illustration; God supplies the virtues.
52-3, Identifying the Elect, Part 3 (1:4-10), https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/52-3 : present condition vs past conversion; ten marks: powerful preaching (v5), preachers' lives, new life (mimetai; Robert Thomas: holiness, love, suffering), joy in thlipsis (Acts 5:41), example (typos), witness (execheitai, perfect tense; 2 Cor 8), allegiance (epistrepho, douleuo; Calvin, Leon Morris), expectation (Acts 1:11; Titus 2:13). Says orge in 1:10 = eternal judgment and explicitly rejects reading v10 as a pre-tribulation rapture text. 2 Pet 1:10.
Other gty items listed by Precept Austin for ch 1 (1317A, 2018, 2019, 80-173) not read.
