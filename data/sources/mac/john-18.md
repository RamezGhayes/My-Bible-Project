# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on John 18 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-27 via WebFetch at https://www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/<code>. Titles, ranges, dates from "John, Vol. 9" (https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/43V9/john-vol-9). No page shows a title; titles rest on the series page only.
43-101, The Betrayed Christ Protects His Own (18:1-11), 2016-03-13: ch 17 prays, ch 18 acts to protect; divine resolve, power, love, righteousness; Jesus no victim; Kidron with Passover blood, a later census of about 256,000 lambs; Gethsemane = oil press; avoids Bethany, goes where Judas knows (Luke 21:37; 22:39; 7:53-8:1); David crossing Kidron betrayed by Ahithophel (2 Sam 15); speira ~600 (or maniple ~200) plus temple police, Antonia; ego eimi = divine name, men fall (Isa 11:4; 2 Thess 2:8); release of disciples fulfils 17:12, spares them a trial beyond their faith (1 Cor 10:13); Judas' kiss as hypocrisy (Matt 26:47, 4-5); Malchus healed, last miracle before the cross; Matt 26:52; Gen 9:6; cup = God's wrath; do not resist Christ.
43-102, Jesus' Unjust Trial, Peter's Shameful Denial (18:12-27), 2016-03-20: two simultaneous scenes, Christ's glory against Peter's sin he goes to pay for; chiliarchos from chilioi = commander of a thousand; cohort up to ~600, temple police up to ~200; binding: "hold him fast" (Matt 26:48), shame; Isaac bound (Gen 22:9), Ps 118:27; Calvin: bound so we are loosed; Annas power behind the office, high priest for life in Jewish view (Num 35), five sons and a grandson held the post; Caiaphas (11:47-53) unwitting prophecy; temple trade exploiting pilgrims, Talmud condemns house of Annas; Jewish law broken: no forced self-incrimination (Maimonides), presumption of innocence, no night or secret trials, witnesses required; struck (Mic 5:1), no retaliation (1 Pet 2:23); other disciple = John (20:3), access via family ties; Peter followed from afar (Mark 14:54), boast (13:37), caught off guard; charcoal fire, cold night = night trial; rooster ~3 a.m. (Matt 26:74; Mark 14:72); Luke 22:60-62 the look; Judas remorse vs Peter repentance, restoration (John 21); applications: no self-confidence, pray, avoid dangerous company, don't yield to fear, confess and be restored.
43-103, Jesus Appears Before Pilate, Part 1 (18:28-38), 2016-05-08: illegal night trial (Matt 26:66) and daylight ratification (Matt 27:1); praetorium = governor's hall, Pilate usually at Caesarea, in Jerusalem Herod's palace or Antonia; avoiding Gentile houses a rabbinic tradition, not OT law (Num 19; Mishnah); Calvin: fear outward pollution while full of malice; evasion impugns Pilate; ius gladii lost (Talmud: about 40 years before 70 AD; Coponius AD 6); Lev 24:16 stoning; Stephen; v32 crucifixion needed so 12:32 "lifted up" fulfilled; Pilate's background: Herod's kingdom divided, Archelaus removed, standards with Caesar's image, Caesarea protest, aqueduct from temple treasury, shields with Tiberius' name, fear of reports (19:12), removed AD 35, reported suicide; vv33-35 ridicule, no Roman crime; v36 kingdom not of this world (Col 1; Rev 11); v37 born (humanity) and came (preexistence, 8:58); found truth vs seeking; "what is truth" first postmodern cynicism; v38 no guilt (19:4, 6; Luke 23; 2 Cor 5:21; Heb 7:26; 1 Pet 2:22).
43-104, Jesus Appears Before Pilate, Part 2 (18:28-38; page text runs to 19:1), 2016-05-15: recap; sent to Herod between vv38-39 (Luke 23); ius gladii; responsibility for Christ's death (Acts 2:22-23; 3:13-15; 4:10; 13:28; Luke 24:20) yet God's sovereign plan behind it (Acts 2:23; 4:27-28; Isa 53:10); thousands of Jews converted (Acts 2:36-41; 4:4); explicitly rejects anti-Semitism, Christians must love Jewish people and preach to them (Gen 12:3; Rom 1:16; 11); every rejecter today shares the guilt (Heb 6:4-6); Barabbas material (lestes; bar abba) lies outside the registered range, so used only via 43-105. We left out statements that read as collective blame.
43-105, Jesus Appears Before Pilate, Part 3 (series: 18:25-19:7; registered for ch 18 as 18:25-40, the 19:1-7 part belongs to John 19 as "43-105@19" or similar), 2016-05-22: formal not-guilty verdict; fear of Tiberius and riot; Herod; Passover custom a local custom giving room to manoeuvre; offered "King of the Jews" hoping crowd prefers a harmless figure; Barabbas robber and murderer (Matt 27); priests' envy (Matt 27:18-23); Pilate's wife's dream; irony: guilty rebel freed, innocent rejected; scourging, mockery, Behold the man, blasphemy charge (19:1-7, not used here); no neutral ground; refusing to choose is a choice.
