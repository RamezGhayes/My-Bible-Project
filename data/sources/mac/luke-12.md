42-168, A Certain Cure for Hypocrisy, Part 1 (12:1-7): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-168 (resources/sermons form robots-blocked). Context Luke 11:37-54; discourse 12:1-13:9 warning of judgment to "learners"; leaven = hypocrisy (Matt 16:6; Mark 8:15; 1 Cor 15:33; Jude 23); hidden revealed (Luke 8:17; Eccl 12:14; Rom 2:16; 1 Cor 4:5); Gehenna: Ahaz, Manasseh (2 Chr 28:3; 33:6), Josiah's dump; argues against annihilationism; do not fear Satan, resist (1 Pet 5:9); sparrows cheap food; omniscience (1 Sam 14:45; Luke 21:18; Acts 27:34); Mal 3:16-17.
42-169, A Certain Cure for Hypocrisy, Part 2 (12:8-9): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-169. Trinitarian framework (honor Father, Son, Spirit); homologeo; Matt 7:21-23; John 5:23; 14:6; 1 John 4:15; Rom 10:9-10; Lord = sovereign, self-denial (Luke 9:23-25); critique of "accept Jesus" evangelism without repentance; 1 John 3:10; Matt 10:32-33; Dan 7:13-14.
42-170, A Certain Cure for Hypocrisy, Part 3 (12:10-12): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-170. Blasphemy against Son forgivable (Mark 3:28; Matt 12:31); against Spirit = final rejection of Spirit's testimony (Luke 11:15 Beelzebul); Spirit works through Scripture (Rom 10:17; 1 Pet 1:23; 2 Tim 3:15; 1 Cor 12:3; 1 John 4:1-3); vv11-12 promise of perseverance; synagogue flogging Deut 25:1-3 (39 lashes), Acts 22:19; Luke 21:12-19; 1 Pet 4:12-14; Calvin and five French prisoners; Foxe.
42-171, The Rich Fool (12:13-21): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-171. Materialist; rabbis settled disputes; firstborn (Deut 21); Christ judges spiritually (John 5) not economic matters; pleonexia, salt water (Eccl 5:10); bios vs zoe (John 10:10); greed idolatry (Rom 1:25; Eph 5:5; Col 3:5); harvest from providence; "I/my", no God/poor/widows; forgot God, others, mortality; Jas 4:13-14; Eccl 2:18-19; no U-Haul behind hearse; Matt 6:19-21; storage units; donors to ministry.
42-172, Anxiety-Free Living, Part 1 (12:22-25): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/42-172. Three commands vv22, 29, 32; part of the gospel, not managing anxiety; two masters (Matt 6:24); 1 Pet 5:7; life given for purpose (Ps 23); ravens lesser-to-greater (Job 38:41; Ps 104; 145:15; 147:9; Ps 34:10); famine from human factors; cubit ~18 inches = lifespan, God decides; be healthy for service.
42-173, Anxiety-Free Living, Part 2 (12:25-31): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-173 (text reviews from v22 and runs to v32). Worry from ignorance or distrust (sin); read Psalms; lilies, Solomon (1 Kgs 10); grass as oven fuel; "little faith" (Matt 6:30; 8:26; 14:31; 16:8); v29 not forbidding work (2 Thess 3:10); unbelievers' father the devil (John 8:44); Father "knows"; seek kingdom = enter by repentance, self-denial, faith (Matt 6:33; 13:44-46; Col 3:1-2); Pss 34, 37; Isa 33:14-16. Entries kept to vv26-31.
42-174, Anxiety-Free Living, Part 3 (12:32-34): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-174. Father gives the kingdom itself (Rom 14:17; Matt 25:34; Luke 22:29; Rom 8:17, 32; Eph 1:3; 2 Pet 1:3); little flock vulnerable yet secure; sell and give (Matt 6:19-21; 19:21; Luke 18:22); faith alone but genuine faith surrenders; heart follows treasure; young pastor with cancer illustration.
42-175, Waiting for the Master's Return, Part 1 (12:35-40, focus v40): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/42-175. Certain event / uncertain time; Son of Man (Dan 7:13-14; Mark 2:10, 28; 14:62); Acts 1:7; Matt 24:36; imminence (Titus 2:13; Jas 5:7-9; 1 Pet 4:7; 1 John 2:18, 28); 2 Pet 3:3-9; critiques full preterism (2 Tim 2:17-18), date-setters, indifference.
42-176, Waiting for the Master's Return, Part 2 (12:35-40): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/42-176. Girded loins (Exod 12:11; 1 Pet 1:13), lamps (Matt 25:1-13); wedding feasts no fixed start; master serves (Luke 22:27); watches; thief (1 Thess 5:2; 2 Pet 3:10; Rev 3:3; 16:15); signless rapture, seven-year tribulation, Armageddon, millennium (John 14:1-3; 1 Thess 4:16-18; Rev 3:10; 19-20). Theology note added (dispensational pretribulational scheme).
42-177, Reward and Punishment at Christ's Return (12:41-48): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-177. Peter's question; faithful steward set over all (Matt 24:45-47; 1 Cor 6:2-3; Mark 13:33-37); wicked servant = defiant, esp. exploiting religious leaders (Matt 24:48-51); "cut in pieces" (1 Chr 20:3; Exod 29:17); many/few lashes; knowledge increases danger (Rom 1-2); Barclay fable of three apprentice devils ("no hurry"); ten reasons Christ must return; end-time sequence.
42-178, Jesus, the Great Divider (12:49-53): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-178 (resources form blocked). Expected national peace (Ps 72; Isa 9:6; Ezek 34, 37); forfeited by rejection; fire = judgment (Isa 66:15; Luke 3:9, 16), kindled at the cross; baptism = suffering/wrath (Mark 10:38); "distressed" = pressed; perpetual Gethsemane; John 19:30; Matt 10:34-36; Mic 7:6; family excluded child; also reviews 42-48 categories; Matt 19:27-29.
