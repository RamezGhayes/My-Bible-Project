42-217, Four Hallmarks of Humility, Part 1 (17:1-4): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-217. Context 9:51-19:28, alternating disciples/Pharisees; humility theme though the word absent; four marks (restraint from offending, readiness to forgive, recognition of weakness, rejection of honor); Prov 3:34; Jas 4:6; 1 Pet 5:5; anendekton hapax; skandalon bait stick; woe; millstone, Jews disliked drowning; little ones = believers (Matt 18:6-10; Mark 9:42); Pharisees cause stumbling by false teaching and hypocrisy (Luke 7:22-23); prosechete heautois = beware (Matt 7:15); epitimao; Luke principle, Matt 18:15-17 process; minor sins covered by love vs patterns confronted; Luke 15:1-2; Luke 11:4.
42-218, Four Hallmarks of Humility, Part 2 (17:3-4): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-218/. Matt 18:22; directional sin handled by Matt 18 process; sins of falling (Gal 6:1-2, paraptoma, katartizo, prautes, baros); love covers (1 Pet 4:8; Prov 10:12; Ps 32:1); Mark 11:25-26 forgive before prayer; David (2 Sam 11-12; Ps 51); reasons to forgive: most God-like (Eph 4:32), all sin against God (Matt 18:21-35), unforgiveness brings chastening (Jas 2:13), hinders fellowship not justification, unfit worship (Matt 5:23-24), usurps God as judge (Rom 12:19), offences may perfect us (Jas 1:2).
42-219, Four Hallmarks of Humility, Part 3 (17:5-10): read at www.gty.org/resources/sermons/42-219 (title from library URL). Apostles' request sensible, admits inadequacy; prostithemi add; mustard seed small but grows; sukaminos deep roots; mountain sayings proverbial; 2 Cor 12:9-10; Eph 6:10; Col 1:29; John 14:12 greater in extent; Spirit empowers sanctification. Servant (doulos) parable with Kenneth Bailey on Middle Eastern village life; Pharisees loved honour (Matt 23:5; Luke 20:46); we are unworthy servants, God not in our debt; Luke 12:37 the Lord will serve the humble.
42-220, Ten Men Healed, One Man Saved (17:11-19): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-220. Border region, perhaps near Ephraim (John 11:54); "at a distance" true leprosy (Lev 13:45-46; Num 5:2-3; 2 Kgs 7:3); epistates; sent to priests: tests faith, upholds Moses, priests must certify; cleansed as they went; Samaritan loud voice, fell on face = worship, gave thanks; nine likely went to the temple without turning to Christ; three questions; allogenes, John 1:11; v19 sozo not iaomai/katharizo, compare 7:50 = salvation; common grace benefits without salvation; Paul Brand on leprosy.
42-221, The Invisible Kingdom of God, Part 1 (17:20-21): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-221 (WebFetch reported a heading "The Visible King and His Invisible Kingdom"; series title per Precept Austin used). Universal kingdom by creation, personal kingdom by recreation; entos = within; mustard seed and leaven (13:18-21) grow until "millennial glory"; 19:11; Schurer's nine-point Jewish eschatology; calls Jewish expectation premillennial and says that is his eschatology; Rom 11:33-36; Dan 4:34-35; Ps 2; Isa 9:6.
42-222, The Invisible Kingdom of God, Part 2 (17:20-21): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-222. Humble coronation; Joel's day of the Lord (Joel 1:15; 2; 3) shaped expectation of cosmic signs; present tense, kingdom not observable; Pharisees demanded sign (11:16); entos not en meso (Matt 23:26 vs Luke 2:46); Rom 14:17; John 3:3-5; rejects postmillennialism; apostles preached the kingdom (Acts 1:3; 8:12; 14:22; 28:23); millennial reign as consummation.
42-223, Seven Characteristics of the Coming King, Part 1 (17:22): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-223. Second coming cardinal doctrine; Luke 17 describes the nature of the judgment, Luke 21 the chronology; only saints enter kingdom; Acts 1:6-11 literal return; normal hermeneutic for prophecy; seven traits: desired, visible, delayed, unexpected, revealing, divisive, deadly; v22 epithumeo strong desire, longing for Christ's honour (Ps 69:9); delay intended (Jas 5:7). Pretribulational rapture sequence in Revelation; millennium of Rev 20.
42-224, Seven Characteristics of the Coming King, Part 2 (17:23-25): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-224. Nine reasons for the second coming (2 Sam 7; Ps 2; Zech 12-14; 2 Thess 1:7; Gen 3:15; Rev 20...); 2 Pet 3 scoffers; v23-24 visible like lightning, warns against date-setters (1948/1988), preterism and secret comings; Rev 1:7; 19:11-16; v25 genea = the nation Israel; kingdom delayed until Israel stops rejecting; rapture first, Israel converted in the tribulation (Daniel's 70th week).
42-225, Seven Characteristics of the Coming King, Part 3 (17:25-30): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-225. Acts 3 Israel must repent before the return; vv26-30 life as usual then sudden judgment, Noah 8 saved, Lot 3 saved; evil will not improve (against postmillennialism); Noah and Lot removed before judgment as picture of the rapture; tribulation warnings (two witnesses, 144,000); Luke 16:31.
42-226, Seven Characteristics of the Coming King, Part 4 (17:31-37): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/42-226. Judgment before kingdom; vv31-33 flat roof, do not go back; Lot's wife a mnemonic warning, heart attached to Sodom; J.C. Ryle: their world is in their hearts; Luke 9:23-25; 1 John 2:15; vv34-36 "taken" in judgment like the flood (Matt 24:39), "left" to enter the kingdom; v36 imported from Matt 24:40; wheat/tares, sheep/goats; v37 aetoi eagles or vultures, worldwide judgment (Jude 14).
