# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on 1 Corinthians 1 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Codes, titles, ranges, dates from the series page https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7132/1-corinthians-vol-01 (and preceptaustin 1-corinthians-1-resources). 1813, 1814, 1815 English pages robots-blocked; their content was read on gty.org's own Spanish translation print pages (GAV-1813, GAV-1814, GAV-1815) of the same sermons.
1810, Called to be Saints (1:1-3), 1975-04-13: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1810. Hagios; position vs practice; shoplifting anecdote; five reasons for apostleship (Acts 1:21-26; Acts 9; 1 Cor 15:8-10; Eph 2:20; 1 Cor 4:9-13; 1 Cor 9:1-2); apostolos as Sanhedrin envoy; apostolate ceased; Sosthenes (Acts 18:17); church of God (Acts 20:28); Corinth geography, diolkos, Isthmian games, Aphrodite; 1 Cor 6:9-11; Acts 16-18 founding; 1 John 2:15-17; lost letter 1 Cor 5:9; Heb 2:11; 10:14; Acts 26:18; charis, eirene/shalom.
1811, The Benefits of Being a Saint (1:4-9), 1975-04-20: read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/1811. Past/present/future; aorist; charis; en Christo; grace vs guilt, obligation, merit (Rom 3:10, 25-26; Rom 4); enriched (2 Pet 1:3; Col 2:10); utterance (Acts 1:8; 4:31; Eph 6:19); knowledge (Matt 11:25; 2 Cor 4:6; 1 Pet 3:15); marturion (Acts 20:21, 24); charismata (1 Cor 12:1); waiting (1 Thess 1:10; Phil 3:20); blameless positional (Heb 9:28; 1 John 3:2; 2 Thess 1:6-10); God faithful (1 Thess 5:23-24); Ambrose Bierce; Getty illustration.
1812, Splits and Quarrels in the Church (1:10-17), 1975-04-27: read at gty.org/sermons/1812. Plea, Parties, Principle, Priority; parakaleo (Philem 8-9); local unity; Matt 5:48; 2 Cor 13:11; katartizo; schisma (Matt 9:16); 1 Thess 5:12-14; Heb 13:17; Rom 15:5-6; Eph 4:3-6; Phil 2:2-5; erides; carnality (1 Cor 3); Deut 6:4; John 17:20-22; 1 Cor 6:17; 12:12, 25; Ps 133:1; inspiration = infallibility not omniscience; Acts 26:16-18; Matt 28.
1813, The Foolishness of God, Part 1 (1:18-21), 1975-05-11: read via Spanish print page GAV-1813. Philosophical allegiances; sophia logou vs stauros; moria; logos tou staurou = whole revelation (Rom 1:16); Isa 29:14 Hezekiah/Sennacherib, 185,000; Prov 14:12; Jer 8:9; Jas 3:15; Isa 19:12; 33:18; suzetetes; kerygma content; Rom 1:19-20, 25; unknown god; Lloyd-Jones; Bultmann; Peter (Matt 16:22-23; Acts 3:18; 1 Pet 2:24).
1814, The Foolishness of God, Part 2 (1:21-28), 1975-05-18: read via Spanish print page GAV-1814 (truncated near end). Jas 3:15, 17; Col 2:8-10; Ecclesiastes 1-2; Matt 7:24-27; Rom 1:16; signs (John 9; Matt 16:4); apatheia; Celsus; Deut 21:23; Isa 53; Ps 22; John 12:24; Col 1:29; irony of "foolishness of God"; Celsus mocking Christians as bats, ants, frogs, worms; John the Baptist (Matt 11:11); Matt 11:25; Sergius Paulus, Dionysius, Erastus.
1815, The Foolishness of God, Part 3 (1:29-2:5), 1975-05-25: read via Spanish print page GAV-1815. Ch.1 part: no boasting (Eph 2:8-9); election God's part, faith human response; wisdom (2 Cor 4:6; Eph 1:9, 17-18), righteousness (2 Cor 5:21; Phil 3:9), sanctification (2 Cor 3:18; Eph 2:10), redemption; Jer 9:23-24; Gal 6:13-15. Ch.2:1-5 part noted under chapter 2.
