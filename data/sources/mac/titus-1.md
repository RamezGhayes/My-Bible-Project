# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Titus 1 (Grace to You)

56-1, Commitments of a Powerful Leader, Part 1 (1:1): Titus next-to-last letter, parallels Timothy; Crete churches immature; ch1 leaders, ch2 members, ch3 witness; "condensed epistle"; formal greeting delegates apostolic authority; doulos (only time Paul uses "slave of God"; Moses Rev 15:3, Joshua, prophets Amos 3:7), apostolos dignity from sender; slave-messenger illustration; mission threefold: evangelism (faith of the elect; 2 Tim 2:10; Deut 7:6; John 15:16; Acts 13:48; election activated by faith Eph 2:8-9), edification, encouragement; simple gospel (1 Cor 2:2); "biblical but not relevant".
56-2, Commitments of a Powerful Leader, Part 2 (1:1-3): principles vs values; job applications; epignosis = full knowledge, here sanctifying (1 Tim 2:4; Titus 2:11-14; 2 Pet 1:3); truth produces godliness, justification and sanctification inseparable; false teachers known by fruit; hope = certain (Titus 2:13; 1 John 3:1-3; Rom 8:24-25); God cannot lie (1 Sam 15:29; Heb 6:18); promise before ages = eternal covenant of redemption (2 Tim 1:9; Heb 13:20; John 6:37-44; 17:24; 1 Cor 15:28); kairos; message confined to Scripture.
56-3, Commitments of a Powerful Leader, Part 3 (1:3): idios "His own word"; God's means = preaching (kerusso, kerugma); herald; many texts (1 Cor 1:21; Rom 10:13-15; 2 Tim 4:2; 1 Cor 9:16); "God our Savior" six times in Pastorals, Jesus Savior three in Titus; his car accident; against manipulation: Duane Litfin, McGuire's five steps, preacher seeks comprehension, Spirit gives yielding; altar-call manipulation; expository preaching; Walt Kaiser "junk food".
56-4, Commitments of a Powerful Leader, Part 4 (1:4): fifth commitment, God's men: Paul multiplied himself; tour of Rom 16 names (Phoebe, Priscilla and Aquila, Rufus son of Simon of Cyrene, Erastus plaque); Titus teknon gnesios (1 Tim 1:2); not in Acts; Gal 2:1-5 uncircumcised Greek, test case of grace; 2 Cor (8:23; 7:6-13; 8:6); Titus 3:12; 2 Tim 4:10; 2 Tim 2:2; grace and peace; "God our Savior" (v3) and "Christ Jesus our Savior" (v4) = one Savior, Christ's deity.
56-5, The Moral Character of a Pastor (1:5-6): standards ignored; quick restoration of fallen pastors more damaging; Crete (Homer: hundred cities; Acts 2:11; 27); epidiorthoo (straighten, orthodontics); elder = overseer = pastor = bishop, one office (Acts 20; 1 Pet 5; 1 Tim 3); appointment: Spirit calls (Acts 20:28; 13), apostolic delegates appoint with prayer and fasting (Acts 14:23), congregation usually affirms (Acts 6); anegkletos (unchargeable) and anepilemptos; Ps 15; Isa 33:14-15; Ps 101; 2 Tim 2:19-22 vessels; "one-woman man": not polygamy, not required marriage, widower may remarry (Rom 7), divorce disqualifies except biblical grounds (Matt 5; 19; 1 Cor 7:15), reputation for sexual purity; Ovid; Prov 6:27-35 adulterer's reproach not wiped away (Keil-Delitzsch; Gen 49:3-4 Reuben); sexual sin permanently disqualifies from pastorate though fellowship can be restored (1 Cor 9:27).
56-6, The Required Character for a Pastor: Family Leadership (1:6): four categories; leadership is example (Phil 3:17; 1 Pet 5:3; 1 Cor 11:1); 1 Thess 2:7-12 mother and father; home the proving ground; pista = believing, rejects "obedient but unconverted" reading; mainly older children (asotia, rebellion = adult conduct), young children under control (1 Tim 3:4); faithful father whose child rejects Christ not responsible but not qualified; single or childless not barred; 1 Cor 7:14; 1 Tim 2:15; 2 Tim 1:5; wife's faith assumed.
56-7, The Qualifications for a Pastor, Part 1: Noble Character, Part 1 (1:7): purpose for pulpit committees; seven images in 2 Tim 2; shepherd image (Luke 22:26; foot washing); Acts 14:21-23 pattern; elder/overseer/pastor interchangeable, later hierarchies human inventions; Crete problems; episkopos; oikonomos = steward of a house not his own (Acts 20:28; 1 Cor 4:2); not self-willed (2 Pet 2:10; Matt 20:25), not quick-tempered (orgilos; 2 Tim 2:24; Jas 1:20), paroinos introduced.
56-8, The Qualifications for a Pastor, Part 2: Noble Character, Part 2 (1:7-8): Richard Baxter, The Reformed Pastor ("no sermon greater than the life behind it"); paroinos = alongside wine; nephalios; ancient diluted wine; Grace Church elders affirm total abstinence (1 Cor 8; Rom 14; Lev 10:9; Prov 31:4-5; 1 Tim 5:23); plektes (Pelagius quote); aischrokerdes (1 Pet 5:2; 1 Tim 6:6-11); false teachers of 1:10-11 opposite; philoxenos (Rom 12:13; Heb 13:2; Luke 14:12-14); philagathos (Phil 4:8); sophron; dikaios; hosios (used of Christ, Heb 7:26); egkrates, accountability no substitute for inner godliness; Spurgeon Lectures to My Students.
56-9, The Qualifications for a Pastor, Part 3: Teaching Skill (1:9): exhort and refute as two-edged sword; primacy of teaching in Pastorals (1 Tim 4:6-16; 5:17; 2 Tim 2:2; 3:16-4:2); leadership grounded in Scripture, not psychology or business; didaktikos; 1 Tim 5:17 "double honor", kopiao; preaching (will) vs teaching (mind); antechomai = cling (Luke 16:13); sufficiency of Scripture; apostolic teaching (Acts 2:42), pastors not innovators; parakaleo; hugiaino; antilego = convince, not humiliate; Ezra 7:10; Apollos; John White, Stott, Spurgeon, Piper quoted.
56-10, Men Who Must Be Silenced, Part 1 (1:10-11): 1 Tim 1:3-7; tongue (Jas 3; Rom 3:19; Ezek 3:26-27); three ways to silence: deny platform (publisher of Benny Hinn), overpower error with truth (Matt 22:34), holy living (1 Pet 2:15); many false teachers (1 Tim 4:1-2; 2 Pet 2:1-3); rebellious (his own accountability), empty talkers (Shakespeare), mind-deceivers; circumcision party (Gal 2); myths; Philip Keller "predators in the pulpit"; households (2 Tim 3:6); upset families; sordid gain (1 Pet 5:2), they quit when the money stops.
