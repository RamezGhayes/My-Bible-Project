# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 1 (Grace to You)
Dates from series pages Luke Vol. 01 and Vol. 02 (gty.org/sermons/series/42v1, 42v2).
42-1, Luke: Physician and Historian (1:1-4): one sentence, classical Greek prologue like Herodotus, Thucydides, Polybius, Josephus; salvation history; not eyewitness; Acts 1:1; Acts 16, 27 "we"; Col 4:10-14; Phm 23; 2 Tim 4:10-11.
42-2, Luke: Theologian and Pastor (1:1): robots-blocked; not used.
42-3, Zacharias: The Righteous Priest (1:5-7): 400 silent years since Malachi (Mal 3:1; 4); Isa 40:3; Abijah 1 Chr 24; Exod 6:23; righteous by faith Gen 15:6, substitution Isa 53:4-6, 61:10; Ps 119:1; barrenness Gen 30, 1 Sam 1, Ps 127; divine plan not Deut 28 curse.
42-4, God Breaks His Silence (1:8-17): lot once; Heb 9:3-4; incense = prayers; fear parallels Judg 6, 13, Dan 8, 10, Matt 17, Rev 1; "fear not" Gen 15, Rev 1; John = God is gracious; Elijah and Elisha last miracle prophets ~800 years earlier.
42-5, A Faithful Promise to a Faithless Priest (1:18-25): Gen 15, Judg 6, 2 Kgs 20 compared; Acts 12; 2 Pet 1; Gabriel and Michael; Dan 8-9; unbelief forfeits participation, not plan; Lev 10, 2 Sam 6; Num 6:24-26; conception after return home; 1 Sam 1; God uses common faithful people.
42-6, The Greatness of John the Baptist (1:15-17): robots-blocked; not used.
42-7, The Greatest Child Ever Born (1:26-35) and 42-8, The Divine Announcement to Mary, Part 1 (1:26-31): robots-blocked in all forms; not used. So 26-29 has no mac entry.
42-9, The Divine Announcement to Mary, Part 2 (1:31-33): Yeshua; Isa 7:14; Matt 1:21; Luke 19:10; 1 Tim 1:15; unqualified greatness vs John's; glory = attributes (John 1:14; 12:41; Isa 6); El Elyon, Heb 1:3, John 10:30, 14:9; 2 Sam 7:12-16, Ps 2, Isa 9:6-7; Gen 32:28. (Also millennial sequence; not used.)
42-10, The Foundations of the Virgin Birth (1:34-35): robots-blocked; not used.
42-11, Fallacies about the Virgin Birth (1:34-35): method not doubt; vs Zacharias; Gen 3:15; Isa 7:14; Jer 31:22; Ps 2; Machen; 2 Pet 2:1; Jude 4.
42-12, The Virgin Birth: A Divine Miracle (1:34-38): supplication/strategy/sign/sovereignty/submission; Gen 1:2; Heb 1:3, 8; 2 Sam 7:14; Gen 18:14; Jer 32:17; Ps 115:3; Dan 4:35; doule like Hannah 1 Sam 1; Matt 1:20-25; 1 Tim 2:5; Jer 7, 44 queen of heaven.
42-13, Mary and Elizabeth (1:39-45): confirmation; Exod 18:7; Luke 1:15; Gen 25:21-23; 2 Sam 23:2; 2 Pet 1:21; mother of my Lord, not mediator; Luke 11:27; John 3:29.
42-14, Mary's Praise (1:46-55): worshiper not worshiped; Ps 34:2; Isa 45:21; 1 Sam 1:11; Gen 30:13; Ps 136:23; Ps 126:3; 111:9; 103:17; Pharaoh, Nebuchadnezzar; Ps 107; Jas 4:6; Isa 57:15; Gen 12.
42-15, The Miraculous Birth of John the Baptist (1:56-66): mercy (Ps 86; 1:50, 54); Gen 17:9-14; Lev 12:3; Ruth 4:17; "at once"; promise veracious, purpose gracious, power wondrous; Num 23:19; Josh 23:14; Titus 1:2; Rom 6:14; Ps 84:11; 1 Pet 5:10; Ps 62:11; Matt 19:26.
42-16, Zachariah's Song: Introduction (1:67-80, focus 67-69): three saving covenants; 2 Sam 7; 23:5; Jer 31; horn Ps 18:2, 132:17, Deut 33:17; visitation of grace; Exod 15; Judg 5; 1 Sam 2.
42-17, Zachariah's Song: The Davidic Covenant (1:67-80, expounds 67-77): horn = Messiah, not John (Aaronic line); 2 Sam 7; 1 Chr 17:4-15; Ps 89:30-37; 132:17; Isa 9:7; oath to Abraham; v76 forerunner; v77 New Covenant (Jer 31).
42-18 to 42-21 (New Covenant parts, Abrahamic covenant): robots-blocked in all forms tried; not used.
