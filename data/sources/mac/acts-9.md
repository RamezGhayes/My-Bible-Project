1728, The Transformed Life, Part 1 (9:1-9), www library page. Conversion told 3 times (9, 22, 26); Jew, Roman, Greek, Christian; none too low. Tarsus university (Athens, Alexandria); father Pharisee, Roman citizen; tentmaking goat hair; Gamaliel ~13; Stephen "time bomb" (6:10; 7:58), Lincoln 1831 New Orleans illustration; 8:3 wild boar; 26:9-11; Haman. v1 breathing in threats = atmosphere; mathetes learner. v2 high priest's authority over synagogues under Rome; men and women; the Way (hodos; John 14:6; Matt 7:14; Isa); Damascus 2,200 ft, ~160 mi, paradise, 150,000 incl. ~20,000 Jews; six days via Samaria revival. Five steps: contact (3: God initiates, election, natural man cannot seek; eunuch; his own car-accident conversion; don't devalue quiet conversions; companions 22:9, John 12:29; Saul saw Christ 1 Cor 15:8; 9:1; 9:17, 27; 22:14; Stephen last to see risen Christ, his prayer answered); conviction (4: repeated name in Luke = rebuke/warning: Martha, Simon, Jerusalem; Rom 1 conscience; goads, Shamgar Judg 3; union with Christ: 1 Cor 6:17; Heb 4:15; body/head; issue is response to Christ John 16:9; 15:25; 1 Cor 16:22); conversion (5: in the "white space"; who are you Lord; knew message from Stephen; repentance breaks, faith heals; Pharisees same evidence; 1 Tim 1:12-16 ignorance; critics Renan sunstroke, thunderstorm, Klausner epilepsy, Ironside sarcasm; rest, 9:31); consecration (6: phrase textually from 22:10 but biblical; Lord from start, no Savior-then-Lord: John 13:13; Acts 10:36; Rom 10:12; 1 Cor 12:5; Phil 2:11; maturity = will aligned; immediate obedience; no grand promises; crushed rose); communion (8-9: three days for mind to catch up; blinded by glory, last sight Jesus; depths take lifetime).

Seven marks, three here: faith (1-9; 1 Tim 1:12-14; Ezekiel; John 3; instantaneous; positional vs progressive); fervor in supplication (9-12: three days praying in Judas's house; prayer like breathing, newborn's cry; Ananias not of ch 5, devout, good report 22:12, maybe leader (speculative); vision to Saul because no Bible/track record; two coordinated visions; God answers even slowly); faithfulness in service (13-17: objection; "Go"; vessel of election; Rom 9; 2 Cor 4; 2 Tim 2; Gal 1:1; Rom 11:13; 15:16; suffering 2 Cor 11:23-27; Brother Saul; 22:14-15; v20 immediately, Arabia later; no "someday"; 1 Cor 4:1 stewards; saved to serve).
1730, The Transformed Life, Part 3 (9:17-19). Eph 2:1-10 (dead, but God, workmanship); positional vs practical (Col 2:10); new man not new suit. Recap marks 1-3; non-Christians serving "God" serve demons (1 Cor 10:20); tentmaking not identity. Filling (17): Eph 5:18 continuous, wind filling sails, control and power; quench (1 Thess 5:19); Acts 2:4; 4:8, 31; 6:5, 8; 7:55. No apostle's hands: Saul himself an apostle commissioned directly, unlike Samaria/Gentiles/John's disciples where apostolic presence unified church. Spirit refines (leadership, discipline 1 Cor 9:24-27, persistence Phil 3:14, convictions Acts 23, independence, boldness, crusading, long-windedness Acts 20:7-12) and replaces (hatred->love Rom 9:1-3; guilt->peace Phil 4:11-13; roughness->gentleness 1 Thess 2:7; pride->humility Acts 14:11-15, 2 Cor 12:7). Fellowship (17, 19): Brother Saul costly; no brotherhood of man (John 8:44; Matt 23:8); 1 John 3:14; 1:3-7; Heb 10:25; Ps 119:63. Baptism (18): after salvation and Spirit, so does not save; public identification; any believer may baptize, Ananias presumed; went public (19). Yield daily (1 Cor 15:31).
