# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Mark 8 (Grace to You)
Dates not shown on the pages read (series page Mark Vol. 04 robots-blocked), so left blank.
41-36, Our Compassionate Provider (8:1-10, 2010-05-16 per Vol. 3 series page): all URL forms robots-blocked, no extracts. Not used.
41-37, Spiritual Blindness, Part 1 (8:11-21; content covers 11-13, previews 14-21): read at www.gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-37/. Blindness universal, layered: nature, sin, Satan (2 Cor 4:3-4), judicial hardening. Turning point: from invitation to denunciation; focus on Twelve. v11 Pharisees and Sadducees unite; sign from heaven (Jewish belief only God does heavenly signs); Joshua's sun, Elijah's fire. v12 deep sigh stronger than 7:34; light increases, darkness deepens (John 5:36); Pharaoh; emphatic no sign; only Jonah's (Matt 12:39-40) and leaders bribed guards (Matt 28:11-15). Nicodemus (John 3:2) shows evidence enough. Voltaire, Woody Allen quips. v13 leaving them = Rom 1 giving over. OT: Ps 82:5; Deut 32:20.
41-38, Spiritual Blindness, Part 2 (8:11-21; content 14-21): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-38. Final public rejection in Galilee. v14 one loaf; lunch focus. v15 leaven = permeating corruption; Pharisees legalism, Sadducees liberalism (Matt 16), Herodians secularism; Eph 4:14; leaven positive in Matt 13:33. v17-18 truth hidden from outsiders, given to disciples (Matt 13); 1 Cor 2:14; John 3:19-20; 2 Cor 4:4. v19-20 twelve and seven baskets: memory of provision key to faith. v21 patient discipling; Matt 16:12 they understood.
41-39, Jesus' Power over Blindness (8:22-26): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-39. Bethsaida home of Peter, Andrew, Philip; last Galilean miracle, opens private training; 8:27-30 the "Continental Divide". Only Mark; parallels 7:32-37. Touch (Mark 1:41 etc.); blind seen as cursed (John 9:1-2). Only time Jesus asks what he sees. Trees walking = disciples. anablepo; diablepo, apokathistemi, emblepo: complete healing; every healing complete. Not into village; Bethsaida judged (Matt 11:20-24). OT Ps 146:8; Isa 29:18; 35:5-8. Themes: deity, kingdom, judgment, death predictions, disciples' sight in stages (Luke 24:45). Feinberg anecdote.
41-40, The Ultimate Good News/Bad News (8:27-33 presumably): all URL forms robots-blocked; no extracts. Not used.
41-41, Losing Your Life to Save It (8:34-38): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-41. Context 29-33 (no cross in disciples' kingdom view). v34 self-denial (disown self; poverty of spirit, Matt 5:3; Ps 34:18; 51:17), cross-bearing (suffering, reproach, death; 1 Cor 15:31; Gal 2:20), follow continually (akoloutheo; John 8:31; 15:14; 1 John 2:6). v35 paradox; noble sacrifices don't earn heaven. v36 bad bargain (Matt 13:44-46; rich fool). v37 soul's value shown by price Christ paid. v38 shame and judgment (2 Thess 1). Luke 14:26-27; Phil 3:4-8; Luke 18:9-14.
41-42, Why You Should Be Ashamed (8:38): read at gty.org/library/sermons-library/41-42. Shame underlies repentance; moral and religious shamelessness (Phil 3:18-19). Adulterous generation = spiritual adultery (Exod 32; Jer 6:15; Isa 57; Ezek 16; Hos 2). Only the self-ashamed are saved (Matt 5:3-6; Luke 18:13); either ashamed of self or of Christ; believers not put to shame (Rom 1:16; 9:33; Heb 2:11; 11:16). Dan 7:9-14 background: Ancient of Days, books, angels, Son of Man; Rev 20:11-15; hell eternal shame; Matt 7:23. Mark 9:1-2 transfiguration preview.
