# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Matthew 28 (Grace to You)
Codes, titles, ranges and dates from the Matthew Vol. 19 series page (gty.org/sermons/series/7019). 2401 The Resurrection of Jesus Christ, Part 1 (28:1-7, 1985-05-12) and 2402 Part 2 (28:8-10, 1985-05-19) could not be read (robots-disallowed; the study-guide chapter page had only metadata; search budget then exhausted), so left out: blocks 1-10 have no mac voice.
2403, The Lie that Proves the Resurrection (28:11-15): enemies' testimony; Matt 27:42, 27:62-66; formal Sanhedrin phrasing Matt 12:14, 22:15, 27:1,7; three-part resolution; self-contradiction; Justin Martyr; Eccl 3:11; G.B. Hardy; Venturini, Renan; Simon Greenleaf; Luke 24:11, John 20:9; 2 Cor 4:4; Rom 10:9; John 11:25, 14:19.

2405, Making Disciples of All Nations, Part 2 (28:18b-20): exousia; Dan 7:13, Acts 2:36, Phil 2:9-11; Rev 11:15, Ps 2, 1 Cor 15:27; participle "having gone"; Matt 10:5-6, Rom 1:16, Acts 1:8; mathetes John 8:31; Gen 12, Ps 96, Isa 42, 52, 66; baptism sign, not saving, Acts 2:38,41, 8:38, 16:33; one name three persons; Acts 20:27, John 14:26, Heb 5:9; ego emphatic John 14:18; sunteleia.
