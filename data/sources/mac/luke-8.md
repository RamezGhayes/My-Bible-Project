# Reading notes: John MacArthur, sermons on Luke 8 (Grace to You)
Read 2026-11-26. Codes, titles and ranges from the Luke sermon list (codes 102-112) at https://lionandlambapologetics.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Luke-MacArthur.pdf ; dates not shown on the gty pages, left empty.
42-102, The Scope of Jesus' Ministry (8:1-3): kerusso herald, euangelizo; transition to final itinerant Galilean phase (to 9:50); kingdom = sphere of salvation; Mary Magdalene not a prostitute, from Magdala; Joanna, Susanna; diakoneo; six limits: sovereignly, geographically, theologically, strategically (twelve; 2 Tim 2:2), socially, materially; "depth vs breadth"; David McKenna on self-styled messiahs.
42-103, Receptivity to the Gospel, Part 1 (8:4-15; expounds 4-12): boat, crowd; parables conceal and reveal, judgment on unbelief (Isa 6); broadcast sowing, paths, limestone, thorns, hundredfold; mystery = hidden then revealed; seed = gospel (1 Pet 1:23), no synthetic seed; roadside = hard heart, devil snatches; pray God plows hearts; keep sowing.
42-104, Receptivity to the Gospel, Part 2 (8:13; page heading "Heart Condition", expounds 4-13): heart condition the variable (Prov 4:23; Mark 7:21); rocky soil: immediate joy, no root; joy not the mark of salvation; wrong motives produce shallow response; trials expose false faith (1 John 2:19; John 8:31; 6:66-69); preach law before grace (Puritans); Finney "many half converts".
42-105, Receptivity to the Gospel, Part 3 (8:14-15; "Heart Condition", third message): "spiritual EKG"; thorns: worries, riches, pleasures (1 Tim 6:9-10; 1 John 2:15-17; Matt 19 rich young ruler; Matt 13:44-46); good soil: kalos kagathos, hold fast, hupomone; assurance from perseverance not a prayer; attitude fruit and action fruit; Ps 1.
42-106, Be Careful How You Listen (8:16-21): read via https://www.gty.org/resources/sermons/42-106 ; four marks of true hearing: evangelistic (lamp, v16), authentic (v17, Luke 12:1-3), fruitful (v18, Matt 25 talents), obedient (vv19-21; Jas 1:22; Matt 7:24); family thought him mad (Mark 3:21); Mary not a perpetual virgin (Fitzmyer cited), brothers believed later (Acts 1:14).
42-107, The Story of the Calm, Part 1 (8:22-25; expounds 22-23): redemption of people and planet; Christ's power (Heb 1:3); illustrations of creation's energy; kingdom features: Satan bound 1,000 years (Rev 20), Isa 11; 35; Ezek 40-48 (premillennial framework; see note in voices); "one of those days" = same day as parables (Mark 4:35); boat sailing; aphupnoo deep sleep: weariness and sovereignty; disciple = learner, not necessarily believer; rejects two-tier Christianity.
42-108, The Story of the Calm, Part 2 (8:22-25): lake 682 ft below sea level, winds funnel down ravines; lailaps; Thompson "The Land and the Book"; "Master, we are perishing" mixes despair and faith; Ps 65:7; 89:9; 107:23-30; instant great calm; strengthening storm not chastening (Jonah contrast); "Who then is this?" answer: God; fear before holiness (Luke 5:8).
42-109, The Maniac Who Became a Missionary, Part 1 (8:26-29; touches 30-31): Gerasa/Kersa; man naked, tombs; demon possession theology from Luke 4 series; demons orthodox (Jas 2:19); proskuneo = prostration; chains broken (Mark 5:3-5); can't medicate demons; abyss; "before the time" (Matt 8:29).
42-110, The Maniac Who Became a Missionary, Part 2 (8:30-39): 1 John 3:8; Legion, thousands (2,000 pigs); abyss (2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6); pigs show deliverance and demons' deadly intent; people not angry about pigs; clothed, sitting, in his right mind (sophroneo); "made well" (sozo); great fear of holiness; Gooding: men more at home with demons than with Christ; Jesus never returned; sent home: first Gentile missionary; "what God did" = "what Jesus did".
42-111, Jesus' Compassionate Power, Part 1 (8:40-48): accessibility, availability, interruptibility, inexhaustibility; Jairus humbled; twelve years; Talmud remedies; unclean (Lev 15:19); clutched tassel (Num 15:37-41); healing without faith sometimes, salvation never; power personal, felt going out; end of relics and magic; "daughter", peace with God; Eusebius on a statue of her.
42-112, Jesus' Compassionate Power, Part 2 (8:49-56; text recaps 40-48): delay seems disastrous; respected Jairus waits for rejected woman; "do not fear, keep believing" (Mark 5:36); hired mourners, garment-tearing rules; death redefined as sleep (John 11:11; 1 Thess 4:13-17; 2 Cor 5:8); mocking laughter like at the cross; talitha cumi; food proves real life; tell no one = worship before witness; believers need not fear death.
